M6 Chapter 22: Business Combinations — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives

  1. Define a business combination and explain the three components of control (power; exposure to variable returns; ability to use power to affect returns) in accordance with HKFRS 3 (Revised) and HKFRS 10.
  2. Apply the acquisition method: identify the acquirer, determine the acquisition date, and measure the consideration transferred (including deferred and contingent consideration) at acquisition-date fair value.
  3. Account for acquisition-related costs, distinguishing them from consideration transferred and from debt/equity issue costs under HKFRS 9 / HKAS 32.
  4. Recognise and measure identifiable assets acquired, liabilities assumed (including contingent liabilities) and any non-controlling interest (NCI), applying the recognition and measurement exceptions.
  5. Calculate goodwill or gain from a bargain purchase under both the partial goodwill and full goodwill NCI measurement options.

必背考點

A. Recognition 認確認知

1. Is it a business combination at all?（判斷入口） - Business combination = a transaction or other event in which an acquirer obtains control of one or more businesses — HKFRS 3(R) App A. - 兩大條件缺一不可：(i) a business must be acquired; (ii) the acquirer must control the business after the acquisition. - A business = an integrated set of activities and assets with at least an input and a substantive process that together significantly contribute to the ability to create outputs — HKFRS 3(R) App A, B7, B8, B12A. Outputs 並非必要（HKFRS 3(R).B9）。 - Substantive process = not minor or ancillary; an organised workforce performing a critical function 係 indicator — HKFRS 3(R).B12D。Back-office systems (accounting, billing, payroll) 一般唔算 substantive process（HKFRS 3(R).B11）。 - Control 三要素（全部同時存在）— HKFRS 3(R).7, B13; HKFRS 10.6–7: 1. Power over the investee (existing rights giving the current ability to direct relevant activities); 2. Exposure, or rights, to variable returns from involvement; 3. Ability to use power to affect the amount of returns. - Rights 必須係 substantive（currently exercisable、能 benefit 持有人），唔係 merely protective — HKFRS 10.B22–B24。Deeply out-of-the-money options 唔算 substantive（LP Knowledge Check Q3）。 - De facto control：持股 <50% 都可以有 power，如果其餘股權分散、唔會集結投票 — HKFRS 10.B38–B46。

2. Recognition principle at acquisition date — HKFRS 3(R).10, 12: - Acquirer 須 separately from goodwill 確認：all identifiable assets acquired、liabilities assumed、any NCI。 - 條件：項目須 meet the definition of an asset/liability at acquisition date，且係 business combination 嘅一部分（唔係 separate transaction，例如報酬員工未來服務嘅安排 — HKFRS 3(R).52）。 - 收購後可能確認 acquiree 從未確認嘅 intangible assets（如 internally developed brands、customer lists），只要 identifiable： - Contractual-legal criterion 或 separability criterion（capable of being separated and sold/transferred/licensed/rented/exchanged）— HKFRS 3(R).B31–B34; HKAS 38.12。 - 不考慮 acquirer 是否有意出售（intention irrelevant），只看是否 capable — HKFRS 3(R).B33。 - Assembled workforce 永遠唔可以單獨確認（無 control、無 contractual/legal right、唔 separable）→ 價值 subsumed into goodwill — HKFRS 3(R).B37–B38。

3. Recognition exceptions（LP 要求掌握嘅三個）— Exhibit 22.4:

Exception HKFRS 3 (Revised) requirement Ref
Contingent liabilities Acquiree 按 HKAS 37 唔確認，但 acquirer 須確認若屬 present obligation arising from past events 且 FV reliably measurable，以 acquisition-date FV 計量（possible obligation 則不確認） HKFRS 3(R).22–23
Leases (acquiree as lessee) 按 HKFRS 16 確認 lease liability（= PV of remaining lease payments as if a new lease）及 right-of-use asset（同額，± favourable/unfavourable terms 調整） HKFRS 3(R).28A–28B
NCA / disposal groups held for sale 按 HKFRS 5 計量 HKFRS 3(R).31

B. Measurement 計量

Acquisition method 五步驟 — HKFRS 3(R).5: 1. Identify the acquirer（付現/發股/促成合併、規模較大一方通常係 acquirer — HKFRS 3(R).6, B14–B17）； 2. Determine the acquisition date = date acquirer obtains control（一般係 closing date，可早可遲於 closing，睇 facts）— HKFRS 3(R).8–9； 3. Determine the consideration transferred； 4. Recognise & measure identifiable assets/liabilities/NCI； 5. Recognise & measure goodwill or gain on bargain purchase。

Consideration transferred — HKFRS 3(R).37（sum of acquisition-date fair values of）: - assets transferred by the acquirer; - liabilities incurred by the acquirer to former owners; - equity interests issued by the acquirer。 - Share consideration：以 acquisition date 嘅 fair value（上市股=當日報價），無論股份幾時發行 — HKFRS 3(R).37。若 acquirer 非上市而 acquiree 上市，可用 acquiree 股價計量（HKFRS 3(R).B14 方向，LP §22.3.3.1）。 - Deferred consideration：以 acquisition-date FV（即折現值）計量；unwinding of discount = finance cost in P&L (HKFRS 9)，唔屬於 business combination。 - Contingent consideration（如 earn-out）：以 acquisition-date estimated fair value 計入 consideration — HKFRS 3(R).39。日後 contingency 落實後，差額唔會回頭調整 goodwill（settlement difference 唔屬 business combination accounting）。 - Acquisition-related costs（finder's fees、due diligence、legal、valuation、internal acquisition department 成本）：expensed as incurred，唔入 consideration、唔入 goodwill、唔入 parent's separate FS 嘅 investment cost — HKFRS 3(R).59。例外：debt/equity issue costs 按 HKFRS 9 / HKAS 32 處理（share issue costs = deduction from equity，e.g. against share capital/share premium）。

Identifiable assets & liabilities：以 acquisition-date fair values 計量 — HKFRS 3(R).18（HKFRS 13 定 FV）。FV uplift on PPE、unrecognised intangibles、contingent liabilities 全部要計入 identifiable net assets。

NCI measurement options — HKFRS 3(R).19（acquisition-by-acquisition basis 逐單選擇）: - Fair value（full goodwill method）；或 - Proportionate share of the acquiree's identifiable net assets（partial goodwill method）。 - NCI at FV ≠ 股數 × 股價，亦 ≠ proportionate share of net assets（可能含 lack-of-control discount）— HKFRS 3(R).B44–B45。

Goodwill — HKFRS 3(R).32:

Goodwill = FV of consideration transferred
         + amount recognised for NCI
         (+ FV of previously held equity interest — step acquisition, out of scope)
         − FV of identifiable net assets acquired at acquisition date

Goodwill = residual asset，代表未單獨識別資產（synergies、assembled workforce、potential new contracts）嘅未來經濟利益 — HKFRS 3(R) App A。

Gain on bargain purchase — HKFRS 3(R).34–36: - 當 identifiable net assets > consideration + NCI 時，先 reassess：(1) 是否已識別晒所有 assets/liabilities；(2) 計量是否反映 acquisition date 所有可得資料； - 確認無誤後，gain 即時計入 profit or loss（acquisition date），歸屬 acquirer。

C. Disclosure / Reporting requirements 披露與報告

  • Business combination 以 equity instruments 達成 → 形成 parent–subsidiary group，parent 須編製 consolidated financial statements（assets/liabilities/equity/income/expenses/cash flows 以 single economic entity 呈列）— Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) s.379(2)；HKFRS 10.4, App A。
  • 以 net assets acquisition 達成 → 無 subsidiary，資產負債直接併入 acquirer 財報，唔叫 consolidated FS。
  • HKFRS 10 豁免：intermediate parent（符合條件）及 investment entity 可免編 consolidated FS（細節 out of scope）。
  • ⚠️ 本章 LP 未展開 HKFRS 3 嘅逐段 disclosure 清單（屬 Ch 23 consolidation 範圍），考試層面掌握上列「邊個要編 consolidated FS」即可。

易混淆概念對比

表 1：Full goodwill method vs Partial goodwill method（HKFRS 3(R).19, 32）

項目 Full goodwill method Partial goodwill method
NCI 計量 NCI at fair value NCI at proportionate share of identifiable net assets
Goodwill 包含 Acquirer + NCI 雙方應佔 goodwill 只包含 acquirer 應佔 goodwill
Goodwill 金額 較高 較低（差額 = NCI 應佔 goodwill）
政策選擇 逐單收購選擇（acquisition-by-acquisition） 同左
點樣分辨（繁中） 題目畀「FV of NCI」或「NCI at fair value」就用呢招 題目講「proportionate share of net assets」就用呢招
考試伏位 用 NCI 股數 × 股價當 FV（錯：可能有 lack-of-control discount）；FV of NCI 唔係 net assets × NCI% 計 goodwill 時忘記加 NCI；或將 NCI% 用錯母公司持股比例（要用 100% − parent%）

表 2：Deferred consideration vs Contingent consideration

項目 Deferred consideration Contingent consideration
性質 金額確定，只係遲啲找數 金額取決於未來事件（earn-out、業績目標）
Acquisition-date 計量 折現值（PV at acquisition date） Estimated fair value（反映預期達成機率）— HKFRS 3(R).39
後續處理 Unwinding of discount → finance cost in P&L (HKFRS 9)；唔影響 goodwill Contingency 落實後差額 唔調整 goodwill；與 business combination 分開處理
點樣分辨（繁中） 題目寫「payable in 12/24 months」+ 畀 discount rate → deferred 題目寫「if revenue/earning target met…」+ 畀 fair value → contingent
考試伏位 用 face value 唔折現；或將 unwinding 當 acquisition cost 用最終實付金額取代 acquisition-date FV 重計 goodwill（錯）；或將 FV 當成 expense

Phase 2 閃卡

Flashcard 1 / 共 8 張
Question

Define "business combination" and state the two features a transaction must have to be one (HKFRS 3 (Revised)).

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：兩個條件要齊——買嘅嘢要係一盤「生意」（有 input + 實質 process），而且買完你要控制到佢。淨係買部機器或者買少少股份唔夠控制權，就唔係 business combination。

Flashcard 2 / 共 8 張
Question

State the three components of control under HKFRS 10.6–7 / HKFRS 3(R).7.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
1. Power over the investee — existing rights giving the current ability to direct the relevant activities; 2. Exposure, or rights, to variable returns from involvement with the investee; 3. The ability to use that power to affect the amount of the investor's returns.

💬 白話解釋：記「權、利、用權影響利」。三個要同時存在；仲要留意係 current ability，唔係 future；direct 嘅係 relevant activities，唔係所有 operation。持股過半通常有 power，但唔係必要條件（de facto control）。
