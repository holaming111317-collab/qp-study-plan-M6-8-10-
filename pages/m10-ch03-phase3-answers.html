M10 Chapter 3: Principles of Contract and Consumer Law in Hong Kong (Part 2) — Phase 3 Answers

Q1 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): Under s.16(2) of the Sale of Goods Ordinance (Cap. 26), where the seller sells goods in the course of a business, there is an implied condition that the goods supplied are of merchantable (satisfactory) quality — subject to exceptions where defects were specifically drawn to the buyer's attention, or where the buyer examined the goods and the examination should have revealed the defects, or (on a sale by sample) where the defects would have been apparent on reasonable examination of the sample. 'Merchantable quality' is defined in s.2(5) of the SOGO by reference to fitness for common purposes, appearance and finish, and freedom from minor defects, qualified by reasonableness.

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係 pinpoint section 記憶題，模擬考試對 statutory citation 嘅要求。SOGO implied terms 一條龍：s.14 = title（賣方有權賣）、s.15 = sale by description（貨對辦）、s.16(2) = merchantable quality、s.16(3) = fitness for particular purpose、s.17(2) = sale by sample。Option A (s.14(1)) 係 seller's right to sell 嘅 implied condition；Option B (s.15(1)) 係 goods must correspond with description；Option D (s.17(2)) 係 bulk corresponds with sample。三個都係 condition，但題目問嘅係 merchantable quality，所以只有 C 啱。

Rule Check: Sale of Goods Ordinance (Cap. 26) s.16(2); definition of merchantable quality at s.2(5)

Common Trap（繁中）: 考生最容易亂咗 s.15 同 s.16 — 記住 15 係「description 對辦」（例如標籤寫咩就要係咩），16(2) 先係品質。另外注意 s.16(2) 只適用於 seller in the course of a business 賣貨；私人賣二手嘢唔受呢條管。

Q2 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): The contract was discharged by frustration. A contract is frustrated where a supervening event, outside the control of the parties and without fault, renders performance impossible, illegal, or radically different from what was contemplated — including where the common purpose or foundation of the contract is destroyed. Here, viewing the fireworks display was the express common foundation of the hire contract (it was written into the contract itself), and its unexpected cancellation destroyed that foundation. Applying Krell v Henry (1903) 2 KB 740, the contract is frustrated and automatically discharged, regardless of the parties' intentions, so Mei Ling is released from her obligation to pay (subject to any loss apportionment under s.16 of the Law Amendment and Reform (Consolidation) Ordinance (Cap. 23)). Option A fails because, unlike Herne Bay Steam Boat Co v Hutton (1903) 2 KB 683, there is no residual purpose (the fireworks viewing was the sole stated purpose, not one of several); Option B confuses common mistake — the cancellation occurred after contract formation, so this is frustration, not mistake (contrast Griffith v Brymer (1903) 19 TLR 434, where the procession had already been cancelled before the contract); Option D is hopeless because Harbour Suites made no false statement of fact — silence about a possible future cancellation is not a misrepresentation (caveat emptor).

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係典型 issue-spotting 題。第一步要認到個 legal issue 係 frustration of purpose，而唔係 common mistake 或 misrepresentation。關鍵時間線：巡遊/煙花取消發生喺訂約之後 → frustration；如果取消咗先訂約（雙方唔知）→ common mistake（Griffith v Brymer）。第二步要區分 Krell v Henry 同 Herne Bay：前者個 purpose 係合約嘅 common foundation（寫明晒），後者仲有其他用途（環島遊）所以唔 frustrated。呢度合約白紙黑字寫明 "for the purpose of viewing the fireworks display"，所以穩陣落入 Krell v Henry。Frustration 效果係 automatic discharge — 唔使任何一方 elect。

Rule Check: Krell v Henry (1903) 2 KB 740; contrast Herne Bay Steam Boat Co v Hutton (1903) 2 KB 683; Griffith v Brymer (1903) 19 TLR 434; Law Amendment and Reform (Consolidation) Ordinance (Cap. 23) s.16

Common Trap（繁中）: 最大伏位係 Option B — 將 frustration 同 common mistake 撈亂。記住時間分界：訂約前已存在嘅錯誤事實 → common mistake（void）；訂約後先發生嘅事件 → frustration（automatic discharge）。Option D 亦係陷阱：無人講過大話，純粹沉默唔係 misrepresentation。

Q3 — Answer: D

Model Answer (EN): Kelvin made a negligent misrepresentation: he honestly believed his statement but failed to exercise reasonable care in making it (relying on year-old figures without checking). Doris's strongest route to damages is s.3(1) of the Misrepresentation Ordinance (Cap. 284) because: (1) there is no need to establish a special relationship or duty of care (unlike negligent misstatement at common law under Hedley Byrne & Co Ltd v Heller & Partners Ltd (1964) AC 465); (2) the burden of proof is reversed — Kelvin must prove that he had reasonable grounds to believe, and did believe, that the facts represented were true up to the time the contract was made (which he cannot, given his careless reliance on outdated figures); and (3) by the 'fiction of fraud', damages are assessed as if the misrepresentation had been made fraudulently — all losses directly flowing from the transaction, regardless of foreseeability (Royscot Trust Ltd v Rogerson (1991) 2 QB 297). Option A fails: the tort of deceit requires fraud (statement made knowingly, without belief in its truth, or recklessly — Derry v Peek (1889) 14 App Cas 337), and Kelvin honestly believed his statement. Option B is inferior: at common law Doris bears the burden of proving a special relationship, duty of care and breach, and remoteness is limited to reasonably foreseeable losses. Option C is wrong: s.3(2) of the MO provides only a discretionary award of damages in lieu of rescission for non-fraudulent misrepresentation, not damages as of right.

解題思路（繁中）: 考試真實出過 misrepresentation + remedies，呢類「揀邊條路追討」嘅 comparative analysis 係核心 skill。答題次序：先定性（honest but careless → negligent misrep，唔係 fraud），再比較三條路。記住 s.3(1) MO 三大優勢：唔使 special relationship、burden reversed、fiction of fraud（賠到盡）。Option B 係設計嚟呃人嘅 — 佢將 s.3(1) 嘅好處（burden on representor + fraud measure）錯配落 common law 度。Option C 考你有冇睇清楚 s.3(2) 只係 discretionary 同 in lieu of rescission。另外記住前提：s.3(1) 只適用於 representee 被誘導同 representor 本人訂約（呢度 Doris 直接同 Kelvin 買，啱用）。

Rule Check: Misrepresentation Ordinance (Cap. 284) s.3(1), s.3(2); Derry v Peek (1889) 14 App Cas 337; Hedley Byrne & Co Ltd v Heller & Partners Ltd (1964) AC 465; Royscot Trust Ltd v Rogerson (1991) 2 QB 297

Common Trap（繁中）: (1) 唔好一見虛假陳述就嗌 fraud/deceit — 要睇 representor 嘅 state of mind；(2) 唔好以為 innocent/negligent misrep 喺 common law 有得賠 — innocent misrep 喺 common law 係冇 damages 㗎（Redgrave v Hurd），要靠 MO；(3) s.3(2) 唔係 as of right，係 court discretion。

Q4 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): Patrick may forfeit the HK$600,000. The payment was expressly described as "a deposit and in part payment of the purchase price" — its characterisation depends on the parties' intention objectively ascertained (Mayson v Clouet (1924) AC 980), and these words give it a dual character: if completion occurs it is absorbed into the price, but if the buyer defaults it is forfeited. A deposit is an earnest of performance — a guarantee that the contract will be completed — and is liable to forfeiture upon the payor's default regardless of whether the payee has suffered any loss (Howe v Smith (1884) 27 Ch D 89; Polyset Ltd v Panhandat Ltd (2002) 5 HKCFAR 234). Option A is wrong: the penalty rule applies to liquidated damages clauses stipulating sums payable on breach, not to the forfeiture of a true deposit. Option C is wrong: s.16(2) of the Law Amendment and Reform (Consolidation) Ordinance (Cap. 23) governs loss apportionment after a contract is frustrated, not after breach by the payor. Option D states the rule for advance part payments (recoverable, subject to the innocent party's set-off for damages — Dies v British and International Mining and Finance Corp Ltd (1939) 1 KB 724), but the payment here was expressly characterised as a deposit, not a mere part payment. (Had Patrick's damages exceeded the deposit, he could also sue for damages, giving credit for the deposit forfeited — Shuttleworth v Clews (1910) 1 Ch 176.)

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係 hard tier — 考 deposit vs part payment vs penalty rule 嘅三向分辨，正正係 Panelist 最愛考嘅「看似可以反駁其實唔得」嘅 doctrinal boundary。答題次序：(1) 定性 — 合約寫明 "as a deposit and in part payment"，根據 Mayson v Clouet 嘅 intention 測試，呢個係 deposit；(2) deposit 嘅法律本質係 earnest / 履約保證，買方違約 → 沒收，唔理賣方有無實際損失（Polyset v Panhandat — CFA 權威）；(3) penalty rule（Dunlop Pneumatic / Cavendish v Makdessi）只規管 liquidated damages clause，唔碰 deposit forfeiture — 呢個係 Annie 論據嘅死因；(4) LARCO s.16(2) 係 frustration 嘅 loss apportionment，同 breach 無關。

Rule Check: Polyset Ltd v Panhandat Ltd (2002) 5 HKCFAR 234; Howe v Smith (1884) 27 Ch D 89; Mayson v Clouet (1924) AC 980; Dies v British and International Mining and Finance Corp Ltd (1939) 1 KB 724; Law Amendment and Reform (Consolidation) Ordinance (Cap. 23) s.16(2)

Common Trap（繁中）: 最大陷阱係 Option A — 用 penalty rule 去攻擊 deposit forfeiture 係經典錯誤；penalty doctrine 只打 liquidated damages，唔打 deposit。Option D 都係伏：part payment 先可以 recoverable subject to set-off，但呢度白紙黑字寫明 "deposit"，定性已經唔同。考試遇到預付款題，第一步永遠係 characterisation（deposit or part payment？）。

Q5 — Answer: Bait advertising under s.13G of the Trade Descriptions Ordinance (Cap. 362); maximum penalty: a fine of HK$500,000 and imprisonment for 5 years (s.18(1) of the TDO).

Model Answer (EN): TechStyle Ltd has committed the offence of bait advertising under s.13G of the Trade Descriptions Ordinance (Cap. 362). A trader commits this offence where it advertises products for supply at a specified price and there are no reasonable grounds for believing that it will be able to offer those products for supply at that price for a period that is, and in quantities that are, reasonable — having regard to the nature of the market and the nature of the advertisement (ss.13G(1) and (2)). Here, TechStyle Ltd held only five units city-wide for a heavily promoted one-day flash sale to the general public and did not state the limited quantity in the advertisement; it therefore had no reasonable grounds for believing it could supply in reasonable quantities. (The defence in s.13G(3) — where the advertisement clearly states the period or quantities and the trader honours it — is unavailable.) The maximum penalty on conviction is a fine of HK$500,000 and imprisonment for 5 years (s.18(1) of the TDO); the court may also order compensation to consumers who suffered financial loss (s.18A(1) of the TDO).

解題思路（繁中）: TDO 六宗罪嘅分辨係 consumer law 嘅考核熱點。呢題嘅事實 pattern 係「廣告抵價 + 無講限量 + 實際存貨極少」→ 對正 bait advertising (s.13G) 嘅定義：無 reasonable grounds 相信能以該價供應合理數量。同 neighbouring offences 嘅分別：(1) bait and switch (s.13H) 需要 trader 發出 invitation to purchase 之後，為推銷另一產品而拒絕展示/示範、拒絕接單或展示缺陷樣本 — 呢度 TechStyle 只係賣晒，無拒絕展示或接單嘅行為，所以唔係 13H；(2) misleading omission (s.13E) 係遺漏 material information 導致 average consumer 作出本唔會作嘅 transactional decision — 雖然無披露存貨量都似遺漏，但 TDO 對「廣告價供應量」有專門條文 13G，specific provision 應優先適用；(3) wrongly accepting payment (s.13I) 需要收咗錢兼意圖唔供應或無合理理由相信能供應 — 呢度無收 consumer 錢，唔啱。罰則數字要背：HK$500,000 + 5 年（s.18(1)）。

Rule Check: Trade Descriptions Ordinance (Cap. 362) ss.13G(1)–(3), s.18(1), s.18A(1)

Common Trap（繁中）: (1) 13G vs 13H 係最大伏位 — 問自己：trader 有冇「拒絕展示/接單/俾缺陷樣本」去轉推另一件產品？有 → 13H；無，純粹存貨唔夠兼廣告冇講 → 13G。(2) 唔好一見「無披露」就答 13E — 有專門 offence 就用專門 offence。(3) 13I 一定要收咗錢先成立。
