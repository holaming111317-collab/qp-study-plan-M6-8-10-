M6 Chapter 16: Income Taxes — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives (3–5, English, action verbs)

  1. Distinguish current tax from deferred tax, and accounting profit from taxable income, under HKAS 12 Income Taxes.
  2. Account for current tax liabilities and current tax assets, including converting accounting profit to taxable income via adjustments.
  3. Compute the tax base of assets and liabilities and identify taxable and deductible temporary differences.
  4. Calculate and recognise deferred tax liabilities and deferred tax assets, including the movement (opening → closing balance) and the related journal entries.
  5. Apply the initial recognition exemption and the rule on measuring deferred tax at enacted or substantively enacted tax rates.

必背考點

A. Recognition（確認）

A1. Current tax liabilities and current tax assets - Recognise a current tax liability for any unpaid current tax from the current and prior periods (HKAS 12.12). 考試伏位：唔係淨係計今年，往年未找清嘅稅都要入。 - If the amount already paid in respect of current and prior periods exceeds the amount due, the excess is recognised as a current tax asset (HKAS 12.13). - A tax loss that can be carried back to recover current tax of a previous period is recognised as a current tax asset, because the benefit is probable and reliably measurable (HKAS 12.13–14). - Journal entry (current tax): - Dr Income tax expense ｜ Cr Income tax payable （以 taxable income × 現行稅率計）

A2. Temporary differences — 定義（HKAS 12.5） - Temporary difference = the difference between the carrying amount of an asset or liability in the statement of financial position and its tax base (HKAS 12.5). - Arise from timing differences in recognising transactions/events under HKFRSs vs the Inland Revenue Ordinance (IRO); they reverse over time. - Permanent differences（例如 dividend income not taxable under IRO、fines and penalties non-deductible）never give rise to temporary differences — 考試年年考呢個分別。

A3. Deferred tax liabilities — recognition - A deferred tax liability (DTL) is the income tax payable in respect of a taxable temporary difference (HKAS 12.5). An entity recognises a DTL for all taxable temporary differences（無 probability 條件 — 呢點同 DTA 唔同，常見伏位）。 - Taxable temporary difference exists when（Exhibit 16.1）： - Asset: Carrying amount > Tax base；或 - Liability: Carrying amount < Tax base。

A4. Deferred tax assets — recognition criteria（HKAS 12.24, 34） - A deferred tax asset (DTA) is income tax recoverable in respect of a deductible temporary difference, the carryforward of unused tax losses, and the carryforward of unused tax credits (HKAS 12.5). - DTA recognised only to the extent it is probable that sufficient future taxable income will be available to utilise the deductible temporary difference / unused tax loss / unused tax credit (HKAS 12.24, 34)。有 probable 測試 — 同 DTL 全面確認唔一樣。 - Deductible temporary difference exists when（Exhibit 16.2/16.6）： - Asset: Carrying amount < Tax base；或 - Liability: Carrying amount > Tax base。

A5. Initial recognition exemption（HKAS 12.15(b), 22(c), 24） - No DTL or DTA is recognised on a temporary difference arising on initial recognition of an asset/liability where the transaction: 1. is not a business combination; and 2. affects neither accounting profit nor assessable/taxable profits at the time of the transaction; and 3. does not give rise to equal taxable and deductible temporary differences. - Rationale（LP）：recognising deferred tax would adjust the carrying amount of the asset/liability and make the financial statements less transparent；禁止於 initial recognition 及其後任何時間確認。 - 經典例子：購入 land，無 capital allowance/depreciation deduction，tax base = Nil → TTD 存在但豁免適用（唔係 business combination、唔影響 accounting/taxable profit）。

B. Measurement（計量）

B1. Accounting profit → Taxable income（轉換公式） - Accounting profit - Plus Non-deductible expenses（會計已計、稅務唔准扣） - Minus Deductible charges and allowances not recognised in accounting profit（稅務先扣，例如 capital allowance） - Minus Non-assessable income（會計已計、稅務唔使課稅，例如 dividend income） - Plus Assessable income not recognised in accounting profit - = Taxable income - Current tax expense = Taxable income × tax rate（香港 profits tax 例子用 16.5%/15%，題目話事）。

B2. Tax base（HKAS 12.5 指引） - Asset: Tax base = Carrying amount − future Taxable amounts + future Deductible amounts - Liability: Tax base = Carrying amount − future Deductible amounts + future Taxable amounts - 記法（LP 例子）： - Machine：Tax base = Cost − accumulated capital allowance already deducted（例如 cost 100、已扣 30 → TB 70）。 - Trade receivables（已課稅銷售）+ ECL provision 20 → TB = 100 − Nil + 20 = 120（TB > CA → DTD → DTA）。 - Interest payable（paid 先可扣）→ TB = 100 − 100 = Nil。 - Accrued fines and penalties（永不可扣）→ TB = 100 − Nil = 100（= CA → 無 temporary difference — permanent difference）。

B3. Deferred tax 計算 — 記住 closing balance vs movement - DTL = Taxable temporary difference × tax rate（= CA of asset − TB of asset，或 TB of liability − CA of liability） - DTA = Deductible temporary difference × tax rate（= TB of asset − CA of asset，或 CA of liability − TB of liability） - 計出嚟嘅係 closing balance；journal entry 記 movement = closing − opening： - DTL 增加 → deferred tax expense 增加；DTA 減少 → deferred tax expense 增加（相反方向就係 income）。 - Income tax expense = current tax expense + deferred tax expense（兩者分開計）。

B4. Tax rate 選擇 - Current tax：按 the end of the reporting period 已生效（in effect） 嘅稅率計量（HKAS 12.46）。 - Deferred tax：按 預期資產變現／負債清償期間適用、且於報告期末已 enacted or substantively enacted 嘅稅率計量（HKAS 12.47）。已立法但未來先生效嘅新稅率都要用（例如 15% → 10%，DTL 要重計：64,000 × 10%/15% = 42,667）。

C. Presentation & Journal Entries（本章 LP 以確認/計量為主，無獨立披露段落；以下為必考呈報同分錄）

  • Income tax expense = current tax expense + deferred tax expense，於 statement of profit or loss 呈報；current tax liability/asset 與 deferred tax liability/asset 於 statement of financial position 呈報。
  • Journal entries（全部 balance）：
情況 Dr Cr
記 current tax Income tax expense 140,250 Income tax payable 140,250
DTL 增加（例：movement 151,000） Income tax expense – deferred 151,000 Deferred tax liability 151,000
DTA 增加（例：movement 217,000） Deferred tax asset 217,000 Income tax expense – deferred 217,000
稅率下調，重計 DTL（例：21,333） Deferred tax liability 21,333 Income tax expense – deferred 21,333
稅率下調，重計 DTA Income tax expense – deferred Deferred tax asset

易混淆概念對比

對比一：Accounting profit vs Taxable income

Accounting profit Taxable income
計算依據 HKFRSs（會計準則） Inland Revenue Ordinance（稅例）
報告去邊 Financial statements（畀 investors/creditors） Tax return（交畀 Inland Revenue Department）
Dividend income Recognised when earned Not taxable under IRO → 永久差異，不會產生 temporary difference
Warranty expense Accrual basis（估計即入賬） Deductible only when paid → timing 差異，產生 deductible temporary difference
Depreciation vs capital allowance 按 useful life 折舊 按稅例 capital allowance → 差額屬 temporary difference，會 reverse
考試伏位（點樣分辨） 見到「never assessable / never deductible」= permanent，無 deferred tax 見到「deductible when paid/incurred/written off」= temporary，要計 DTA/DTL

對比二：Taxable temporary difference vs Deductible temporary difference

Taxable temporary difference (TTD) Deductible temporary difference (DTD)
未來效果 未來 taxable income ↑ → 未來多交稅 未來 taxable income ↓ → 未來慳稅
Asset 情況 Carrying amount > Tax base Carrying amount < Tax base
Liability 情況 Carrying amount < Tax base Carrying amount > Tax base
確認 Deferred tax liability（全數確認，無 probable 測試） Deferred tax asset（only to the extent probable 有足夠 future taxable income 可用，HKAS 12.24, 34）
典型例子 Capitalised development costs（稅務已全扣，TB = Nil）；accelerated capital allowance > depreciation ECL allowance、warranty provision、annual leave provision（paid/incurred 先可扣）
考試伏位 同學最易調亂 liability 方向：liability CA > TB 其實係 DTA（唔係 DTL） 記住 JE 方向：DTL↑ 要 Cr DTL / Dr expense；DTA↑ 要 Dr DTA / Cr expense

EN Term 繁中白話 English Model Sentence
Current tax 今期實際要交嘅稅（按 taxable income 計） Current tax is the income tax payable on the taxable income for the current period.
Deferred tax 因 timing 差異而產生嘅未來稅務後果 Deferred tax captures the future tax consequences of recovering assets or settling liabilities.
Temporary difference 賬面值同 tax base 嘅差異，會隨時間 reverse A temporary difference is the difference between the carrying amount of an asset or liability and its tax base.
Tax base 資產/負債喺稅務上嘅「賬面值」 The tax base of the machine is its cost less accumulated capital allowances deducted.
