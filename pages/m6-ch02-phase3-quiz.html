M6 Chapter 2: Fair Value Measurement — Phase 3 Quiz

Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. … — 超時如實申報

Question 1 (MCQ)

Mantis Limited is measuring the fair value of a warehouse in accordance with HKFRS 13 Fair Value Measurement. Which of the following statements correctly describes fair value under HKFRS 13?

A. The price that would be paid to acquire an equivalent asset in an orderly transaction between market participants at the measurement date B. The price that would be received to sell the asset in an orderly transaction between market participants at the measurement date C. The entity-specific value of the asset, based on the cash flows the entity itself expects to generate from continuing use D. The price that would be received to sell the asset in an immediate sale when the seller is under financial pressure

Question 2 (MCQ)

Gecko Logistics Limited is measuring the fair value of a consignment of coffee beans at its year end. The beans must be transported to the selected market, and Gecko will incur transport costs of HK$25 per bag and brokerage fees (transaction costs) of HK$40 per bag on the sale. According to HKFRS 13, which of the following is correct regarding the measurement of the fair value of the coffee beans?

A. Both transport costs and brokerage fees are deducted from the market price B. Only the brokerage fees are deducted from the market price C. Only the transport costs are deducted from the market price D. Neither transport costs nor brokerage fees are deducted from the market price

Question 3 (Calculation MCQ)

Terrapin Trading Limited sells a single product, Model Z, and has access to three markets. Market information at the measurement date is as follows:

East market West market Central market
Total market trade volume (units, per trade publications) 50,000 80,000 65,000
Price per unit (HK$) 820 800 840
Transport costs per unit (HK$) 20 15 35
Transaction costs per unit (HK$) 30 25 40

In accordance with HKFRS 13, what is the fair value of one unit of Model Z?

A. HK$800 B. HK$805 C. HK$785 D. HK$760

Question 4 (MCQ)

Heron Holdings Limited holds 8% of the voting shares of Coral Bay Limited, a private company, and measures the investment at fair value at each reporting date in accordance with HKFRS 9 and HKFRS 13. At 31 December 20X8, Heron values the investment using price-to-earnings multiples of comparable listed companies (an observable input), adjusted by a discount for lack of marketability. The discount is developed from Heron's own internal estimates (an unobservable input) and is assessed as significant to the entire measurement. Within which level of the fair value hierarchy should this fair value measurement be categorised in accordance with HKFRS 13?

A. Level 1, because the comparable companies are listed B. Level 2, because the price-to-earnings multiples are observable inputs C. Level 3 in its entirety, because the significant unobservable adjustment determines the classification D. Partly Level 2 (for the multiples) and partly Level 3 (for the discount)

Question 5 (Calculation — short answer)

Kingfisher Tech Limited is measuring the fair value of a specialised machine, Model Q, using a present value technique under the income approach in accordance with HKFRS 13. Model Q will generate cash flows for one year only. The possible cash flows and associated probabilities are:

Possible cash flows Probability
HK$6,000 30%
HK$10,000 40%
HK$16,000 30%

The risk-free rate for one-year cash flows is 3.5%, and the risk premium for an asset with the same risk profile is 2.5%.

Calculate the fair value of Model Q, showing your workings.
