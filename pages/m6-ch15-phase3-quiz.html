Question 1 (1 mark)

Beacon Lighting Limited (Beacon) is assessing whether it can recognise provisions for several obligations in its financial statements for the year ended 31 December 20X5.

In accordance with HKAS 37, which of the following is NOT a condition that must be met before a provision is recognised?

A. Beacon has a present obligation (legal or constructive) as a result of a past event B. It is probable that an outflow of resources embodying economic benefits will be required to settle the obligation C. A reliable estimate can be made of the amount of the obligation D. The timing and the amount of the future expenditure required in settlement are certain

Question 2 (1 mark)

Lantern Homeware Limited (Lantern), whose reporting date is 31 December 20X5, operates a lighting division that has been loss-making. On 18 December 20X5, Lantern's Board of Directors unanimously approved a detailed formal plan to close the lighting division. The plan identifies the business concerned, the principal locations affected, the number of employees who will receive redundancy payments of HK$900,000, lease termination costs of HK$300,000, and the implementation date (1 March 20X6). As at 31 December 20X5, the decision had not been communicated to any of the employees, customers or suppliers affected. The plan was publicly announced to all affected parties on 8 January 20X6.

In accordance with HKAS 37, how should Lantern account for the closure plan in its financial statements for the year ended 31 December 20X5?

A. Recognise a restructuring provision of HK$1,200,000 because a detailed formal plan had been approved by the Board before the reporting date B. Recognise no restructuring provision; disclose the plan as a non-adjusting event after the reporting period in accordance with HKAS 10 if it is material C. Recognise a restructuring provision of HK$1,200,000 and disclose the announcement on 8 January 20X6 as an adjusting event after the reporting period D. Disclose a contingent liability of HK$1,200,000 because the restructuring is only a possible obligation at the reporting date

Question 3 (2 marks)

Kowloon Kettle Limited (Kowloon Kettle) sells electric kettles with a six-month warranty under which it repairs manufacturing defects free of charge. For kettles sold during the year ended 31 December 20X5, management estimates:

  • 70% probability that the kettles sold will have no defects;
  • 20% probability that the kettles sold will have minor defects, which would cost HK$1,500,000 to repair in total; and
  • 10% probability that the kettles sold will have major defects, which would cost HK$4,000,000 to repair in total.

During the year ended 31 December 20X5, repair costs of HK$280,000 had already been incurred in respect of kettles sold during that year.

In accordance with HKAS 37, what amount should be recognised as a provision for warranties in Kowloon Kettle's statement of financial position at 31 December 20X5?

A. HK$nil B. HK$700,000 C. HK$420,000 D. HK$5,500,000

Question 4 (2 marks)

Harvest Kitchenware Limited (Harvest) entered into a non-cancellable contract to supply 4,000 cookware sets to a customer at HK$300 per set, with delivery due in June 20X6. Due to a sharp rise in steel prices before production commenced, Harvest's incremental costs of direct labour and materials, plus the allocation of depreciation on machinery used to fulfil this and other contracts, now amount to HK$340 per set. If Harvest fails to fulfil the contract, it must pay the customer a penalty of HK$120,000. No inventory had been produced by Harvest's reporting date of 31 December 20X5.

In accordance with HKAS 37, what amount, if any, should Harvest recognise as a provision in respect of this contract at 31 December 20X5?

A. HK$nil — no provision is recognised because the production costs will only be incurred in the future B. HK$120,000 C. HK$160,000 D. HK$1,360,000

Question 5 (2 marks)

On 1 January 20X4, SolarTech Energy Limited (SolarTech) completed the construction of a solar farm at a cost of HK$40,000,000. Under local law, SolarTech is legally required to dismantle the solar panels and restore the site at the end of their useful life of 8 years. SolarTech estimates the decommissioning work will cost HK$5,000,000. The relevant discount rate is 5% and the present value factor for 8 years at 5% is 0.6768.

In accordance with HKAS 37 and HKAS 16, which journal entry should SolarTech record on 1 January 20X4 in respect of the decommissioning obligation?

A. Dr Property, plant & equipment — solar farm HK$5,000,000; Cr Provision for decommissioning HK$5,000,000 B. Dr Decommissioning expense HK$3,384,000; Cr Provision for decommissioning HK$3,384,000 C. Dr Property, plant & equipment — solar farm HK$3,384,000; Cr Provision for decommissioning HK$3,384,000 D. Dr Property, plant & equipment — solar farm HK$3,384,000; Dr Decommissioning expense HK$1,616,000; Cr Provision for decommissioning HK$5,000,000
