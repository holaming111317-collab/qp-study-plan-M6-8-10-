M10 Chapter 11: Company Capital and Financing Companies — Phase 3 Answers

Q1 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): The security is a floating charge. In Re Yorkshire Woolcombers Association, Romer LJ identified three characteristics: (1) the charge is on a class of assets, present and future; (2) that class changes from time to time in the ordinary course of business; and (3) until the chargee takes some step, the company may carry on business and deal with the assets in the ordinary way. All three are present: the charge covers present and future stock-in-trade, the inventory constantly changes, and BLL retains freedom to deal until notice is given. It will become a fixed charge only upon crystallisation.

解題思路（繁中）: 分辨 fixed vs floating 嘅關鍵唔係份文件寫得幾仔細，而係公司仲可唔可以自由處理啲資產。BLL 可以喺日常業務中自由買賣存貨 → 三個 Romer LJ 特徵齊晒 → floating charge。A 錯：用描述方式識別資產唔等於 fixed charge，floating charge 都要話到明係邊類資產。C 錯：charge 唔涉及 title 轉移，唔係 mortgage；「涵蓋將來資產」反而係 floating charge 嘅典型特徵。D 錯：pledge 係 possessory security，貸方要管有資產；題目講明存貨留喺 BLL 手上，所以唔係 pledge（仲有 pledgee 無 foreclosure 權）。考試伏位：記住「公司仲玩得郁啲資產 = floating」。

Rule Check: Re Yorkshire Woolcombers Association (Romer LJ's three characteristics, as cited in the LP); CO (Cap. 622) s.334(6) (floating charge is a specified charge).

Common Trap（繁中）: 以為「寫明係邊啲資產」就係 fixed charge；或者以為「future assets」令佢變成 mortgage。仲有：floating charge 唔需要公司先停止營業先有效——佢一設立就存在，只係結晶前公司可以自由處理資產。

Q2 — Answer: A

Model Answer (EN): A charge on book debts is a "specified charge" under s.334 and must be registered within one month of creation (the period starting on the day after creation) by delivering a statement of particulars in the specified form and a certified copy of the instrument (s.335(1), s.335(5)(a)). The charge was created on 2 May, so the deadline was 2 June; delivery on 20 June is late. Under s.337(4), an unregistered specified charge is void against the liquidator and creditors of the company. However, it remains valid as between the chargee (Bank N) and the company, and also as against unsecured creditors and purchasers of the charged property. The company and its responsible persons also commit a criminal offence (s.337(2)), though the court may extend time under s.346(3) if the failure was accidental or due to inadvertence, which would remove the criminal liability (s.346(4)).

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題考 pinpoint section + 「void 對邊個 void」。Book debts 明明喺 s.334 清單入面（第 4 項），所以 D 錯。期限計法：設立翌日起計一個月，2 May 設立 → 2 June 到期，20 June 先交 = 逾期。B 錯：s.337(4) 唔係令 charge 對全世界無效，只係對 liquidator 同 creditors 無效；chargee 同公司之間仍然有效。C 錯：刑事責任係額外嘅，唔係唯一後果——唔登記嘅實體法後果先係重點（失去對 liquidator/creditors 嘅優先權）。考試伏位：「valid against the company but void against the liquidator and creditors」呢句要背到。

Rule Check: CO (Cap. 622) s.334 (specified charges, incl. charge on book debts), s.335(1) & s.335(5)(a), s.337(4), s.337(2), s.346(3)–(4).

Common Trap（繁中）: 考生最常犯兩個錯：(1) 以為唔登記 = 成個 charge 無效（其實只係對 liquidator/creditors 無效）；(2) 以為刑事責任取代民事後果（其實兩者並存）。記住登記本身唔賦予優先權，但唔登記會令優先權喪失。

Q3 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): The proposal is not a variation of class rights. Shares belong to the same class if the rights attached are in all respects uniform (s.178(1)), and variation of class rights requires written consent of holders of at least 75% of the class's voting rights or a special resolution at a class meeting (s.180(1), (3)). However, in White v Bristol Aeroplane Co Ltd (1953) Ch 65 (CA), the issue of new preference and ordinary shares ranking pari passu with existing shares was held NOT to be a variation: the rights attached to the existing preference shares (e.g. the 8% cumulative dividend) remain untouched; the existing holders are affected only in the enjoyment of their rights as a matter of business. Ms Lau's consent as a class is therefore not required, and her s.182(1) remedy (available only where there IS a variation) does not arise. (Note: as the allotment is non-pro rata, approval of the members in general meeting by ordinary resolution is still required under ss.140–141 — but that is a separate issue.)

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係經典 White v Bristol Aeroplane 應用。分辨方法：問「股票上附著嘅權利改咗未？」Ms Lau 嘅 8% cumulative preference dividend 一蚊都冇改變，改嘅只係佢嘅相對商業地位 → enjoyment only → 唔係 variation。A 錯：dilution 唔等於 variation，呢個正正係 White v Bristol Aeroplane 嘅 holding。C 錯：s.182(1) 嘅入稟權只喺有 variation 嘅情況下先存在，呢度根本冇 variation，所以無從入稟。D 錯：章程無規定時，s.180 默認規則就係要 75% 書面同意或 class meeting special resolution——「從來唔使同意」係大錯。額外位：non-pro rata allotment 仍要 members 以 ordinary resolution 批准（ss.140–141），但嗰個係另一個 issue，唔好溝埋。

Rule Check: CO (Cap. 622) s.178(1), s.180(1) & (3), s.182(1); White v Bristol Aeroplane Co Ltd (1953) Ch 65 (CA).

Common Trap（繁中）: 一見「攤薄/dilution」就衝去答 variation + s.180，係最常見伏位。記住：variation = 權利本身變；enjoyment affected = 商業效果變。仲有，唔好將 s.182 嘅 10% 入稟門檻亂套落冇 variation 嘅情況。

Q4 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): The guarantee is prohibited financial assistance. A company is prohibited from giving financial assistance directly or indirectly for the purpose of acquiring shares in the company, and the prohibition extends to a subsidiary giving assistance for the acquisition of shares in its holding company (s.275(1)). A guarantee falls squarely within the wide definition of financial assistance in s.274(1). The principal purpose exception in s.278 does not apply: following Ricco (Int'l) Co Ltd v Uni-Harvest Int'l Ltd (2017) 6 HKC 487, one must distinguish "a purpose" from "the reason why a purpose was formed"; the anticipated benefit to NFL's business from the group expansion is merely the reason or motive for giving the guarantee, not a separate larger purpose. Breach carries criminal liability (s.275(4)), but the transaction is not invalid per se (s.276) — though it may still be set aside on other grounds such as directors' breach of fiduciary duty (cf. Carney v Herbert (1985) AC 301 on severance).

解題思路（繁中）: 典型 IRAC：Issue = NFL 嘅 guarantee 係咪 prohibited financial assistance？Rule = s.275(1)（連子公司資助收購控股公司股份都包）+ s.274(1)（guarantee 明確喺定義內）+ s.278 例外 + Ricco 嘅 purpose/reason 區分。Application = 「集團擴張對 NFL 有利」只係动机/reason，唔係獨立嘅 larger purpose → 例外失效。Conclusion = 違法，有刑事責任，但交易唔自動無效。A 錯：正正係 Ricco 推翻嘅論點。B 錯：s.275(1) 明確延伸至子公司，呢個係白撞選項。D 錯：s.276 話明 validity 唔受影響 per se；Carney v Herbert 仲話埋非法部分可以 sever。考試伏位：記住「違法 ≠ 交易無效」，同埋 s.278 例外好窄。

Rule Check: CO (Cap. 622) s.275(1), s.274(1), s.275(4), s.276, s.278; Ricco (Int'l) Co Ltd v Uni-Harvest Int'l Ltd (2017) 6 HKC 487; Carney v Herbert (1985) AC 301.

Common Trap（繁中）: 兩大陷阱：(1) 以為講句「為公司利益/更大商業目的」就過到 s.278——Ricco 話 motive/reason 唔係 purpose；(2) 以為違反 s.275 會令成個收購交易無效——s.276 話唔會，頂多因董事 breach of fiduciary duty 等其他理由先會被撤銷。

Q5 — Answer: The court is likely to REFUSE confirmation.

Model Answer (EN): A reduction of capital by special resolution confirmed by the court falls under Sub-division 3 of Division 3 of Part 5 (s.226). The court's main consideration is whether shareholders' and creditors' interests are prejudiced — the shareholders must be treated equitably, the proposal properly explained, creditors safeguarded, and there must be a discernible purpose. In Re Fok Ying Tung Ming Yuan Development Co Ltd (2016) 2 HKLRD 292, the court held that it approaches a reduction on the basis of the share capital shown in the company's balance sheet at the time of reduction: where the company carries accumulated losses, those losses reduce the capital actually available, and a description of the reduction that fails to reflect this is misleading to future creditors. The court also relies heavily on up-to-date audited financial statements that are unqualified or carry only immaterial qualifications. Here, Marble Arch Ltd's balance sheet shows accumulated losses of HK$30 million, yet the resolution asserts that "capital exceeds its needs" — a misleading description — and the auditors have issued a disclaimer of opinion, depriving the court of reliable financial statements. Creditors (current and future) would be prejudiced. Following Re Fok Ying Tung, the petition should be dismissed and confirmation refused.

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係 hard tier，考一個相對冷門但 LP 有詳細講嘅案例。思路：Issue = 法院會唔會確認減資？Rule = s.226 法院確認途徑 + 法院四大考慮（公平對待股東、方案解釋、保障債權人、有可辨識目的）+ Re Fok Ying Tung 兩個原則：(1) 以 balance sheet 上嘅 share capital 為準，accumulated losses 會扣減可用資本；(2) 法院依賴無保留（或只有輕微保留）意見嘅經審計報表。Application = 公司虧蝕 HK$30m 卻聲稱「資本超出需要」= 誤導性描述，會誤導未來債權人；核數師出 disclaimer = 法院冇可靠報表可依；反對嘅 creditor 利益受損。Conclusion = 拒絕確認。攞分位：一定要引 Re Fok Ying Tung 全名同年份，同埋點出「accumulated losses 減少可用資本」呢個核心 ratio。

Rule Check: CO (Cap. 622) s.226 (Sub-division 3 of Division 3 of Part 5); Re Fok Ying Tung Ming Yuan Development Co Ltd (2016) 2 HKLRD 292.

Common Trap（繁中）: 考生成日只背「special resolution + solvency statement 或法院確認」就收工，完全唔提法院確認嘅實質審查標準。呢題嘅伏位係事實入面嘅「accumulated losses + disclaimer of opinion」——兩個都係 Re Fok Ying Tung 嘅翻版事實，見到就要即刻諗起呢個案。唔好答「只要股東一致同意就得」——保障 creditor 先係成個 doctrine of capital maintenance 嘅核心。
