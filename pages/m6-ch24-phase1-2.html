M6 Chapter 24: Investments in Associates — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives

  1. Define an associate and explain the meaning of significant influence in accordance with HKAS 28 (2011).
  2. Apply the equity method of accounting to investments in associates, including adjustments for fair value uplifts, dividends and unrealised gains/losses on upstream and downstream transactions.
  3. Calculate the equity accounted carrying amount of an investment in an associate and the investor's share of the associate's profit or loss.
  4. Determine whether and how an investment in an associate is tested for impairment in accordance with HKAS 28 and HKAS 36.
  5. Prepare the presentation and disclosure of investments in associates in accordance with HKAS 1, HKFRS 12, HKAS 24 and HKAS 27.

必背考點

A. Recognition — What is an associate and when does the equity method apply?

Rule Detail Reference
Definition of associate An entity over which the investor has significant influence. HKAS 28.3
Significant influence The power to participate in the financial and operating policy decisions of the investee, but that power is insufficient to give control or joint control over those policies. HKAS 28.3
Rebuttable presumptions Holding ≥ 20% of voting power → presumed significant influence; holding < 20% → presumed no significant influence. Presumptions are rebuttable by evidence (e.g. associate under administration; contractual rights despite < 20%). HKAS 28.5
Indicators of significant influence Board representation; participation in policy-making processes (incl. dividend decisions); material transactions between the entities; interchange of managerial personnel; provision of essential technical information. HKAS 28.6
Ongoing assessment Significant influence is assessed throughout the duration of the investment, not just at inception; it can be lost without any change in shareholding. HKAS 28.9
Accounting method An associate is not consolidated; with limited exceptions it is accounted for using the equity method. HKAS 28.11

B. Measurement — Equity method (initial + subsequent)

B1. Initial recognition (HKAS 28.10) - The investment is initially recognised at cost, and adjusted thereafter for the post-acquisition change in the investor's share of the associate's net assets. - Investor's profit or loss includes its share of the associate's profit or loss; investor's OCI includes its share of the associate's OCI.

B2. Implicit goodwill / discount on acquisition (HKAS 28.12, 28.32)

Goodwill (or discount) = Cost of investment − (% ownership × net fair value of associate's identifiable assets and liabilities)

  • Goodwill → included implicitly in the carrying amount of the investment; no separate goodwill asset is recognised and no further journal entry is made.
  • Discount on acquisition → recognised in profit or loss as part of the share of the associate's profit or loss in the period of acquisition.

B3. Subsequent measurement — the equity accounted carrying amount (Exhibit 24.2)

Cost                                                              x,xxx
+ Share of associate's net profit for the period                  x,xxx
+ Share of associate's OCI for the period                         x,xxx
− Dividends received/receivable from the associate               (x,xxx)
+/− Other changes in net assets                                   x,xxx
= Equity accounted carrying amount                                x,xxx
  • Share of profit must be based on the associate's profit adjusted for (HKAS 28.32): 1. additional/reduced depreciation or amortisation arising from acquisition-date fair value adjustments (e.g. unrecognised trademark amortised over its useful life); and 2. the investor's share of unrealised gains/losses on upstream/downstream transactions.
  • Associate's financial statements must be adjusted to uniform accounting policies and be of the same reporting date as the investor unless impracticable (a difference of up to three months is acceptable, adjusted for significant transactions in the gap). | HKAS 28.33–36 |
  • Loss floor: the investment (an asset) cannot go below nil — the investor ceases to equity account its share of losses once the net investment is written down to zero (but keeps tracking them off-record, and recognises a liability for any legal/constructive obligations). | HKAS 28.38–39 |

B4. Distributions from the associate (HKAS 28.10) - A dividend from an associate is NOT dividend income — it is a reduction in the carrying amount of the investment (avoids double-counting, since the share of profit is already in P&L).

B5. Upstream and downstream transactions (HKAS 28.28) - Gains/losses on transactions between the investor and its associate are recognised only to the extent of unrelated investors' interests in the associate — i.e. the investor's own share of any unrealised gain/loss is eliminated. - Upstream = associate sells to investor; downstream = investor sells to associate. - HKAS 28 is unclear on the mechanics; the LP uses Approach 2 (adjust the line item of the entity recording the gain and the entity holding the asset): - Upstream: DR Share of associate's profit or loss / CR Inventory (or PPE) - Downstream: DR Cost of Sales (or Gain on sale of PPE) / CR Investment in associate - No elimination of intercompany balances is needed (the associate is not consolidated line-by-line). - For non-current assets, the unrealised gain is effectively realised through excess depreciation over the asset's remaining useful life.

B6. Impairment (HKAS 28.40–42, HKAS 36.4) - Test only when there is objective evidence of impairment (loss events, e.g. significant financial difficulty of the associate, breach of contract, probable bankruptcy, adverse technological/market/economic/legal changes, significant or prolonged decline in fair value below cost). | HKAS 28.40, 36.4 | - The investment is tested as a single asset; implicit goodwill is not separately identified or tested. | HKAS 28.41A–C | - Write down to recoverable amount = higher of fair value less costs to sell and value in use. | HKAS 28.42 | - Full reversal of an impairment loss is possible (subject to HKAS 36 constraints) — the prohibition on reversing goodwill impairment does not apply because the goodwill is not separately recognised. | HKAS 28.42 |

B7. Separate financial statements (HKAS 27.10) - In the investor's separate financial statements (if presented — HKFRSs do not require them), account for associates at: cost, or in accordance with HKFRS 9, or by applying the equity method. Same policy must be applied to all associates.

D. Disclosure requirements

Requirement Reference
On the face of the SFP: carrying amount of all investments accounted for using the equity method (one line); on the face of SPLOCI: share of profit or loss of equity accounted associates/JVs, and share of their OCI (split between items that will / will not be reclassified to P&L). Classified as non-current unless held for sale under HKFRS 5. HKAS 1.54, 82, 82A; HKAS 28.15
Disclosure objective: information enabling users to evaluate the nature of, and risks associated with, interests in associates and their effects on financial position, performance and cash flows. HKFRS 12.1
Significant judgements and assumptions in determining significant influence (and changes); nature, extent and financial effects of interests in associates; nature of and changes in risks. HKFRS 12.5A, 6, 7, 20
Key disclosures: particulars of each associate (name, activity, place of business, ownership interest); summarised financial information for each material associate; reconciliation of summarised financial information to the carrying amount; significant restrictions on the associate's ability to transfer cash; unrecognised share of losses; contingent liabilities. HKFRS 12.21–23
Related party disclosures of transactions and balances with associates. HKAS 24.19
Particulars of associates in separate financial statements, where presented. HKAS 27.17

易混淆概念對比

對比表 1：Subsidiary vs Associate vs Financial asset（control vs significant influence）

Subsidiary (HKFRS 10) Associate (HKAS 28) Financial asset (HKFRS 9)
Level of influence Control — power to direct relevant activities Significant influence — power to participate in financial & operating policy decisions Neither control nor significant influence
Typical voting power > 50% (but HKFRS 10 emphasises control, not a presumption) 20%–49.9% (rebuttable presumption, HKAS 28.5) < 20%
Accounting in group FS Full consolidation — line-by-line; NCI recognised Equity method — one-line investment + share of profit Fair value (FVTPL or FVOCI)
Investee's assets/liabilities Recognised separately in consolidated SFP Not recognised — only net investment Not recognised
Intercompany transactions Fully eliminated Eliminated only to extent of investor's share (HKAS 28.28) No elimination

💬 點樣分辨：關鍵字係 "participate" vs "direct" — 能夠參與決策 ≠ 能夠主導決策。考試伏位：持股 ≥20% 只係 rebuttable presumption，如果有 administrator 接管、或者有相反證據，25% 都可以唔係 associate；反過來 <20% 但有 board representation + material transactions + essential technical information 都可以係 associate。另一伏位：potential voting rights（presently exercisable options）要計埋落 voting power 度，可能令 "associate" 變咗 subsidiary。

對比表 2：Goodwill on acquisition — Business combination vs Associate

Subsidiary (HKFRS 3 business combination) Associate (HKAS 28 equity method)
Goodwill recognition Separately recognised as an asset in consolidated SFP Implicit — included within carrying amount of "Investment in associate"; no separate asset (HKAS 28.32)
NCI in the calculation Yes — full or partial goodwill methods No NCI — only the investor's share of net fair value is compared with cost
Discount (bargain purchase) Gain recognised in P&L (after reassessment) Included in share of associate's profit or loss in the period of acquisition
Impairment testing Annual test (HKAS 36); cannot be reversed Tested only on objective evidence, as part of the single investment asset; full reversal possible (HKAS 28.42)

💬 點樣分辨：見到 associate 題目問 goodwill，記住「唔會出現一條獨立 goodwill 線」— 佢匿埋喺 investment carrying amount 入面。考試伏位：(1) 同學成日將 discount on acquisition 當 goodwill 咁藏喺成本度，其實要即刻落 P&L；(2) 題目問 reversal，business combination goodwill 唔得 reverse，但 associate investment 減值可以全數 reverse。

Phase 2 閃卡

Flashcard 1 / 共 8 張
Question

Define "associate" and "significant influence" in accordance with HKAS 28 (2011).

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：associate 即係「有份出聲但話唔到事」嘅投資。你可以參與（participate）財務同營運政策嘅決策，但冇能力主導（direct）佢哋 — 一旦可以主導就變咗 subsidiary 要 full consolidation。

Flashcard 2 / 共 8 張
Question

What are the rebuttable presumptions in HKAS 28 regarding the level of voting power, and list the indicators of significant influence.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：20% 只係起點，唔係終點。考試會畀 facts（例如被 administrator 接管、或者有董事席位加技術支援）叫你推翻呢個 presumption，所以一定要睇晒所有事實先好下結論。

Flashcard 3 / 共 8 張
Question

Describe the equity method of accounting under HKAS 28.10, including initial recognition and subsequent adjustment.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：equity method 即係每年將投資嘅賬面值「重估」一次 — 賺咗錢你份就上調，派息就扣返落嚟。記住佢唔係真正嘅 consolidation，而係借用 consolidation 原則嘅 measurement technique。

Flashcard 4 / 共 8 張
Question

How are implicit goodwill and a discount on acquisition of an associate accounted for under HKAS 28.32?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：買貴咗（goodwill）就靜靜哋藏喺投資成本度；買平咗（discount）就當係即時賺咗，要計入收購年度嘅 share of profit。呢個對比經常考 — 同 HKFRS 3 嘅 goodwill 分開確認完全唔同。

Flashcard 5 / 共 8 張
Question

Before computing the investor's share of an associate's profit, what adjustments must be made to the associate's reported profit?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
1. Adjust for the effect of different accounting policies and align reporting dates (same date unless impracticable; gap up to 3 months allowed with adjustment for significant transactions) (HKAS 28.33–36). 2. Adjust for additional/reduced depreciation or amortisation arising from acquisition-date fair value adjustments (e.g. unrecognised trademark) (HKAS 28.32). 3. Eliminate the investor's share of unrealised gains/losses on upstream/downstream transactions (HKAS 28.28).

💬 白話解釋：associate 自己報表嘅 profit 唔等於你計 share 用嘅 profit。兩大調整：FV uplift 嘅額外折舊/攤銷 同 未實現利潤。漏咗 FV 攤銷係歷屆考生最常犯嘅錯。

Flashcard 6 / 共 8 張
Question

How does an investor account for a dividend received from an associate under the equity method, and why?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：派息唔係新收入，只係將你已經認咗嘅利潤「套現」。考試伏位：如果公司嘅 own books 已經 DR Cash / CR Dividend income，consolidation worksheet 要做返 DR Dividend income / CR Investment in associate 嚟更正 — 2025 年考官報告特別點名呢個錯誤。

Flashcard 7 / 共 8 張
Question

How are unrealised gains on upstream and downstream transactions with an associate eliminated under HKAS 28.28 (Approach 2 used in the LP)?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
