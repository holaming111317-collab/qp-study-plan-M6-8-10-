M6 Chapter 12: Non-current Assets Held for Sale and Discontinued Operations — Phase 3 Quiz

Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. … — 超時如實申報

Q1. Lantern Lighting Limited ("Lantern") owns a warehouse in Kwai Chung. On 15 November 20X6, the board of directors approved a plan to sell the warehouse. The warehouse is empty and could be sold immediately. Management has set an asking price approximately 40% above the warehouse's current fair value because it hopes to "test the market", and no broker or active programme to locate a buyer has yet been appointed. According to HKFRS 5 Non-current Assets Held for Sale and Discontinued Operations, which of the following statements is correct as at 15 November 20X6?

A. The warehouse must be classified as held for sale because the board has approved a plan to sell it and it is available for immediate sale in its present condition. B. The warehouse cannot be classified as held for sale because the sale is not highly probable: the asset is not being actively marketed at a price that is reasonable in relation to its current fair value, and no active programme to locate a buyer has been initiated. C. The warehouse must be classified as held for sale because it is available for immediate sale in its present condition, and board approval alone makes the sale highly probable. D. The warehouse cannot be classified as held for sale because a non-current asset can only be classified as held for sale once a binding sale agreement has been signed.

Q2. Crane Logistics Limited has classified a disposal group as held for sale in accordance with HKFRS 5. The disposal group contains the following assets: goodwill; property, plant and equipment; an investment property carried under the fair value model of HKAS 40 Investment Property; and intangible assets. Before the carrying amount of the disposal group is compared with its fair value less costs to sell, which of the following assets must FIRST be remeasured in accordance with its own specific standard rather than the measurement requirements of HKFRS 5?

A. Goodwill B. Property, plant and equipment C. The investment property carried under the fair value model of HKAS 40 D. Intangible assets

Q3. Heron Manufacturing Limited ("Heron") has a machine with a carrying amount of HK$2,400,000. On 1 October 20X6, the machine met all the criteria in HKFRS 5 to be classified as held for sale. On that date, the machine's fair value was HK$2,300,000 and the incremental costs directly attributable to its disposal (legal fees and broker commission) were estimated at HK$100,000. Heron depreciates the machine at HK$20,000 per month. In accordance with HKFRS 5:

(a) At what amount should the machine be measured immediately upon classification as held for sale, and what impairment loss (if any) should be recognised? (b) State the journal entry (Dr/Cr) to record the write-down on 1 October 20X6. (c) How much depreciation should Heron charge on the machine for the period from 1 October 20X6 to its year end of 31 December 20X6, assuming it remains classified as held for sale?

Q4. Falcon Retail Group Limited ("Falcon") is considering the presentation of the following four components in its financial statements for the year ended 31 December 20X6. In accordance with HKFRS 5, which component should be presented as a discontinued operation?

A. A single small retail outlet in Mong Kok classified as held for sale; Falcon operates over 200 similar outlets across Hong Kong and this outlet does not represent a separate major line of business or geographical area. B. Falcon's entire European operations, a cash-generating unit representing a separate major geographical area of operations, which has been classified as held for sale during the year. C. A delivery truck that management intends to use until the end of its economic life and then scrap. D. A 20% investment in an associate accounted for using the equity method, which Falcon plans to retain for the long term.

Q5. Otter Marine Limited ("Otter") classified a boat engine as held for sale on 30 June 20X6. At that date the engine's carrying amount was HK$1,800,000 and its fair value less costs to sell was HK$1,500,000; an impairment loss was recognised accordingly. At Otter's year end of 31 December 20X6, the engine is still classified as held for sale and its fair value less costs to sell has increased to HK$1,950,000 as market conditions improved. In accordance with HKFRS 5, what gain (if any) should Otter recognise for the year ended 31 December 20X6 in respect of the subsequent increase in fair value less costs to sell?

A. HK$450,000 B. HK$300,000 C. HK$150,000 D. HK$0
