M10 Chapter 4: The Law of Tort and Negligence — Phase 3 Answers

Q1 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): In Caparo Industries plc v Dickman (1990) 2 AC 605, the House of Lords refined the neighbour principle into a three-fold test for a duty of care: (1) the plaintiff's damage or injury was foreseeable by the defendant; (2) there was a close and direct relationship of proximity between the parties; and (3) it was fair, just and reasonable in the circumstances to impose a duty of care. Option B states exactly these three limbs.

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係 easy-tier rule recall，考你分唔分到 Caparo 三摺測試同其他測試。Option A 係 Anns two-fold test（proximity + policy negation）— 係 Caparo 之前嘅版本，已經被取代；Yuen Kun Yeu v Attorney General of Hong Kong (1988) AC 175 (PC) 批評 Anns 第一步忽略咗 foreseeability。Option C 講「recognised categories of liability」，正正係 Donoghue v Stevenson 之前嘅舊制度， neighbour principle 就係要擺脫佢。Option D 係 Hedley Byrne 嘅 special relationship / assumption of responsibility 框架，只適用於 negligent misstatement，唔係一般 duty of care 嘅測試。考試伏位：題目問邊個 case 嘅測試就要答邊個，唔好將 Anns、Hedley Byrne、Caparo 三個撈埋一碟。

Rule Check: Caparo Industries plc v Dickman (1990) 2 AC 605; contrast Donoghue v Stevenson (1932) AC 562 and Hedley Byrne & Co Ltd v Heller & Partners Ltd (1964) AC 465.

Common Trap（繁中）: 見到「proximity」就揀 A — 記住 Anns 係 two-fold，Caparo 係 three-fold；Caparo 嘅獨有標誌係 foreseeability + proximity + fair, just and reasonable 三項齊全。

Q2 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): Under s.27 of the Limitation Ordinance (Cap. 347), the time limit for commencing an action in tort for personal injuries is three years from the date the cause of action accrued (the accident). Marco must therefore commence his action by 10 June 2026. The six-year period in s.4(1) of the Ordinance applies to tort actions in general, not to personal injury claims.

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題考 pinpoint section knowledge — Exam Lens 明講 open-book 考試期望你準確引用條文。關鍵係分開兩條：s.4(1) = 一般 tort 六年；s.27 = 人身傷害三年。Option A 錯在用咗 s.4(1) 嘅六年落 personal injury；Option C 錯在將「知悉受傷日期」套落一般人身傷害 — 「knowledge」起算只適用於 fatal cases（由死者死亡日期或遺產代理人知悉日期起計，以較後者為準），Marco 冇死，唔適用；Option D 完全虛構，所有訴訟因由都有 limitation period。記住 fatal case 嘅三年先至可以用 knowledge 起算。

Rule Check: Limitation Ordinance (Cap. 347) s.4(1) and s.27.

Common Trap（繁中）: 背咗「六年」就即刻揀 — 一見 personal injuries 就要轉 channel 去 s.27 嘅三年。另一伏位：將 fatal case 嘅 knowledge 起算規則套用落普通受傷個案。

Q3 — Answer: D

Model Answer (EN): Kelvin is not liable. Liability for negligent misstatement cannot be founded on the Donoghue v Stevenson neighbour principle alone; the representor must have expressly or impliedly assumed responsibility for the statement, i.e. a 'special relationship' must exist between representor and recipient (Hedley Byrne & Co Ltd v Heller & Partners Ltd (1964) AC 465, per Lord Reid). Here Kelvin expressly disclaimed responsibility ('I accept no responsibility'), so no assumption of responsibility arose and no duty of care was owed to Dora — just as the 'without responsibility' disclaimer defeated liability in Hedley Byrne itself. Moreover, applying Caparo v Dickman (1990) 2 AC 605, an auditor's duty in respect of published accounts does not extend to non-shareholders contemplating investment.

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係 medium-tier IRAC scenario，考嘅正係 Jun 2025 Panelists' Report 點名嘅弱項：negligent misstatement ≠ ordinary negligence。Issue：Kelvin 欠唔欠 Dora duty of care（misstatement）。Rule：Hedley Byrne special relationship + assumption of responsibility。Application：Kelvin 明確 disclaimer「accept no responsibility」，冇 assumption of responsibility；況且 Dora 係非股東潛在投資者，Caparo 都唔幫佢。Conclusion：唔使賠。Option A 錯在「anyone who foreseeably relies」— misstatement 嘅 duty 遠比 foreseeability 窄，否則就係 indeterminate liability（Caparo 第三個 limb 嘅 policy 考量）。Option B 錯在單靠 neighbour principle — Lord Reid 喺 Hedley Byrne 明講唔得。Option C 太絕對：社交場合都可以有 special relationship，重點唔係場合，係有冇 assumption of responsibility，所以 C 嘅推理唔成立。

Rule Check: Hedley Byrne & Co Ltd v Heller & Partners Ltd (1964) AC 465; Caparo Industries plc v Dickman (1990) 2 AC 605.

Common Trap（繁中）: 一見「會計師講錯嘢 + 有人蝕錢」就寫 negligence 三元素 — 凡涉及陳述同純經濟損失，第一步一定要搵 special relationship / assumption of responsibility，同埋檢查有冇 disclaimer。

Q4 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): GuardForce is vicariously liable. Vicarious liability is a form of strict liability: once the employee's tort is established, the employer's innocent state of mind is irrelevant (Dubai Aluminium Co Ltd v Salaam (2003) 2 AC 366). For an intentional wrong committed for the employee's own purposes, the Salmond test is unhelpful; the applicable test is the Close Connection Test from Lister v Hesley Hall Ltd (2002) 1 AC 215: whether the employee's tort is so closely connected with his employment that it would be fair and just to hold the employer vicariously liable. Rex committed the tort on duty, in uniform, while purporting to deal with a suspected theft on the very premises he was employed to protect. The tort was closely connected with his employment, so GuardForce is vicariously liable.

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係 medium-tier，考 vicarious liability 嘅 modern test。Option A 錯：故意侵權唔係自動豁免 — Lister 正正係故意性侵案都判僱主孭。Option B 錯在死抱 Salmond test 嘅「authorised act」第一枝；就算第二枝「unauthorised mode」都唔啱用，Lister 已經話對故意自肥行為 Salmond test 冇用，要用 Close Connection Test（香港 Ming An Insurance Co (HK) Ltd v Ritz Carlton Ltd (2002) 3 HKLRD 844 有跟用）。Option D 錯在混淆咗僱主個人/非轉承責任（breach of duty to employ competent employees）同 vicarious liability — 後者係 strict liability，唔使證明僱主自己有疏忽。Dec 2024 panel report 話考生對 vicarious liability 理解差，答咗啲 general employment law 嘢，呢題就係針對呢個伏位。

Rule Check: Lister v Hesley Hall Ltd (2002) 1 AC 215; Dubai Aluminium Co Ltd v Salaam (2003) 2 AC 366; cf. Ming An Insurance Co (HK) Ltd v Ritz Carlton Ltd (2002) 3 HKLRD 844.

Common Trap（繁中）: 見到「故意打人有刑事成分」就以為僱主一定甩身 — 記住 vicarious liability 係 strict liability，僱主清白與否唔相干，問題只剩一個：個 tort 同份工夠唔夠 close connection。

Q5 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): SecureCo's argument fails. A novus actus interveniens breaks the chain of causation only if the intervening event was not reasonably foreseeable by the defendant; if the intervening event was something the defendant ought reasonably to have anticipated, the defendant remains liable. In Hui Kay Cheong, Herman v Chi Wo Properties Ltd (1992) HKDCLR 51, a management company that failed to provide adequate security was liable for a robbery because the burglary was a highly foreseeable consequence of its failure to safeguard the building — the criminals' act did not break the chain. By contrast, in Leung Sin Fai v Perfect World Co Ltd (1998) 2 HKLRD 115, an unforeseeable third-party act (inflammable liquid in a toilet bowl) did break the chain. Here, the very purpose of SecureCo's engagement was to guard against theft; the burglary was precisely the foreseeable risk of leaving the warehouse unguarded. Causation is therefore established against SecureCo (but for the absent guard, the theft would not have occurred).

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係 hard-tier，考 Dec 2025 panel report 話考生「poor grasp」嘅 novus actus interveniens。核心規則：介入行為要 unforeseeable 先斷到因果鏈；預見到嘅第三方犯罪行為唔會斷鏈（Hui Kay Cheong），預見唔到先會（Leung Sin Fai）。Option A 錯在「always breaks」— 冇呢個絕對規則，否則保安公司永遠唔使負責。Option C 錯在亂入 pure economic loss — BrightTech 係貨物被盜，屬 property damage，唔係純經濟損失；而且 duty 方面 SecureCo 嘅合約職責就係保安，proximity 充足。Option D 錯在「intention」— 被告有冇意圖促成盜竊同因果鏈完全無關，測試係 foreseeability of the intervention。答呢類題嘅次序：Issue (novus actus?) → Rule (foreseeable intervention唔斷鏈) → Application (保安失守→爆竊係高度可預見) → Conclusion (SecureCo liable)。

Rule Check: Hui Kay Cheong, Herman v Chi Wo Properties Ltd (1992) HKDCLR 51; contrast Leung Sin Fai v Perfect World Co Ltd (1998) 2 HKLRD 115.

Common Trap（繁中）: 一見「第三者犯罪」就直覺答「斷鏈」— 呢個正係 Dec 2025 考生跌分位。口訣：foreseeable intervention = chain intact; unforeseeable intervention = chain broken。答題時兩個 HK 案例一成一敗並列，分數先穩。
