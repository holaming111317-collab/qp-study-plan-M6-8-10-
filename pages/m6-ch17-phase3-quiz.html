M6 Chapter 17: Earnings per Share — Phase 3 Quiz

Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. … — 超時如實申報

Question 1

Alder Limited's profit attributable to shareholders of the parent entity for the year ended 31 December 20X3 was HK$8,000,000. Throughout the year, Alder Limited had non-cumulative preference shares on issue, classified as equity in accordance with HKAS 32. During 20X3, Alder Limited declared and paid a dividend of HK$500,000 on these preference shares out of retained earnings. The applicable tax rate is 16.5%.

In accordance with HKAS 33, what amount of earnings should be used as the numerator in calculating Alder Limited's basic EPS for 20X3?

A. HK$8,000,000 B. HK$7,582,500 C. HK$7,500,000 D. HK$8,417,500

Question 2

Hibiscus Limited had 8,000,000 ordinary shares on issue as at 1 January 20X4. On 1 July 20X4, it issued 2,400,000 ordinary shares for cash at the prevailing market price. On 1 October 20X4, it bought back and cancelled 600,000 ordinary shares at the prevailing market price. Profit attributable to ordinary shareholders of the parent entity for the year ended 31 December 20X4 was HK$21,900,000. (Use monthly time-weighting.)

In accordance with HKAS 33, Hibiscus Limited's basic EPS for the year ended 31 December 20X4 is:

A. HK$2.23 B. HK$2.38 C. HK$2.42 D. HK$2.74

Question 3

Falcon Limited had 4,000,000 ordinary shares on issue throughout 20X4. On 1 September 20X5, it made a rights issue of one new ordinary share for every four shares held at an exercise price of HK$6 per share. The fair value of each share immediately prior to the exercise of the rights was HK$10. All rights were taken up. Falcon Limited reported profit attributable to ordinary shareholders of HK$3,600,000 for the year ended 31 December 20X4 and had reported basic EPS of HK$0.90 in its 20X4 financial statements.

In accordance with HKAS 33, the restated comparative basic EPS for 20X4 presented in Falcon Limited's 20X5 financial statements is (rounded to two decimal places):

A. HK$0.72 B. HK$0.83 C. HK$0.90 D. HK$0.98

Question 4

Heron Limited had 600,000 options outstanding for the whole of the year ended 31 December 20X6. Each option entitles the holder to acquire one ordinary share at an exercise price of HK$15. The average market price of Heron Limited's ordinary shares during 20X6 was HK$12.

In accordance with HKAS 33, how should these options be treated in the calculation of diluted EPS for 20X6?

A. The options are excluded from the diluted EPS calculation because they are anti-dilutive, but are disclosed in the notes as instruments that could potentially dilute basic EPS in the future. B. The options are included in the diluted EPS calculation because all outstanding options must be assumed to be exercised, adding 600,000 shares to the denominator. C. The options are included in the diluted EPS calculation by adding 150,000 shares issued for no consideration to the denominator. D. The options are excluded from the diluted EPS calculation because they have not yet been exercised by the holders.

Question 5

Kestrel Limited's profit from continuing operations attributable to ordinary shareholders of the parent entity for the year ended 31 December 20X7 was HK$10,000,000. The weighted average number of ordinary shares on issue during 20X7 was 5,000,000. Throughout the year, Kestrel Limited had 40,000 convertible bonds on issue with a face value of HK$100 each, paying an annual coupon of 5% (which is tax deductible). Each bond is convertible into 25 ordinary shares. The applicable tax rate is 16.5%.

In accordance with HKAS 33, Kestrel Limited's diluted EPS for the year ended 31 December 20X7 is (rounded to two decimal places):

A. HK$1.67 B. HK$1.69 C. HK$1.70 D. HK$2.00
