M8 Chapter 7: Audit Procedures for Specific Balances and Transactions — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives

  1. Apply tests of control and substantive procedures (tests of details and substantive analytical procedures) to each major business cycle — revenue, purchases, payroll, inventory, receivables, bank and cash, payables, non-current assets, provisions, leases and related parties.
  2. Map each audit procedure to the financial statement assertion(s) it verifies, and identify the direction of testing (existence vs completeness).
  3. Evaluate the design of external confirmation requests (trade receivables, trade payables, bank) in accordance with HKSA 505 External Confirmations, including follow-up procedures for non-responses and doubts over reliability.
  4. Explain the auditor's responsibilities when attending a physical inventory count under HKSA 501 Audit Evidence – Specific Considerations for Selected Items.
  5. Determine the audit implications (including potential modification of the auditor's opinion) when accounting treatments contravene HKFRS 15, HKAS 2, HKFRS 9, HKAS 16, HKAS 38, HKAS 37, HKFRS 16 or HKAS 24 (Revised).

必背考點

A. Assertions — 每個 major balance 嘅 key assertions（背熟！考試年年考 mapping）

Balance / Transaction Key Assertions 主要測試方向
Revenue (HKFRS 15) Cut-off, Accuracy, Completeness, Occurrence 主要防 overstated revenue（fraud risk：bonus、covenants）
Purchases / Expenses Completeness（最主要）、Accuracy、Cut-off 防 understated purchases/expenses → cost of sales understated
Payroll Accuracy, Completeness, Occurrence Fraud risk：'ghost' employees；年結 accrual（paid in arrears）
Inventories (HKAS 2) Valuation, Existence, Completeness, Rights and obligations 主要防 overvalued → overstated current assets + gross profit
Receivables (HKFRS 9 ECL) Valuation and Existence 防 overstatement（連帶 revenue overstatement — Dr Receivables Cr Revenue）
Bank and Cash Existence and Valuation Window dressing 令 bank + payables understated
Tangible non-current assets (HKAS 16) Existence, Valuation, Completeness, Rights and obligations 防 incorrectly capitalised / under-depreciation / revaluation 出錯
Intangible assets (HKAS 38) Existence, Valuation 防 contravene HKAS 38 而 capitalise（如 internally generated goodwill）
Payables and Accruals Completeness（liabilities 嘅焦點永遠係 completeness） 防 understated liabilities → overstated profit
Provisions (HKAS 37) Completeness, Valuation, Rights and obligations, Presentation 防 understated / incomplete；disclosure 唔足
Non-current liabilities Completeness, Classification (current vs non-current split per HKAS 1) Loan split 錯 → current 同 non-current 齊齊 misstated
Leases (HKFRS 16) Valuation, Completeness, Presentation and disclosure ROU asset / lease liability 計錯；interest rate implicit in the lease 用錯
Related parties (HKAS 24 (Revised) / HKSA 550) Presentation and disclosure, Completeness 防 non-disclosure / concealment（fraud risk factor）

點樣記： Liabilities 永遠主攻 completeness（怕少記）；Assets 永遠主攻 existence + valuation（怕多記/值錯）；Revenue 主攻 occurrence + cut-off（怕假數/跳期）。

B. Audit Procedures — assertion ↔ procedure mapping（核心考點）

B1. Revenue（HKFRS 15） - Inspect a sample of GDNs immediately before and after year-end → recorded in correct accounting period（cut-off）。 - Recalculate sales discounts and sales tax on a sample of sales invoices（accuracy）。 - Select a sample of GDNs → agree to sales invoices → follow through to inclusion in the sales day book（completeness）。 - Inspect post-year-end credit notes, trace to GDN and invoice; if relates to pre-year-end sale, ensure reversed（occurrence）。 - Recalculate contract liability（unearned service revenue）at year-end and agree with the statement of financial position（completeness and accuracy）。Service revenue 只可以喺 contractual obligations 履行時先 recognise。 - Analytical：compare revenue with prior year / budget / industry；calculate gross profit margin (gross profit/sales × 100) and investigate significant fluctuations；proof in total。 - Tests of control：credit limit checks、fictitious order rejection、GDN matched to invoice before invoicing、invoice approval、standing data amendment authorisation。

B2. Purchases / Expenses - Inspect GRNs immediately pre- and post-year-end → correct period（cut-off）。 - Sample of purchase orders → agree to GRNs and purchase invoices → through to inclusion in the purchases day book（completeness）。 - Recalculate invoice values including discounts / sales tax（accuracy）。 - Inspect unmatched GRNs not yet invoiced → agree to schedule of accruals（completeness）— goods received but not invoiced 要做 accrual。 - Analytical：category-by-category comparison with prior year and budget；gross profit margin + operating margin cross-check（margin 一升一跌可能代表 classification error：overheads 誤入 cost of sales 或 capital items 誤入 repairs）。 - Gross profit margin 異常上升嘅原因：revenue overstated（cut-off 錯）、purchases incomplete、closing inventory overvalued、opening inventory 錯。

B3. Payroll and Directors' Emoluments - Agree total wages and salaries per payroll system with trial balance / general ledger / financial statements（completeness and presentation）。 - Cast monthly payroll reports；recalculate gross and net pay for a sample（accuracy）。 - Sample of joiners and leavers → agree start/leave dates with first/last payroll run（accuracy）。 - Reperform employment tax calculations（accuracy）；agree year-end tax accrual with payroll records and post-year-end payment（occurrence）。 - Agree total net pay per payroll records with bank transfer listing and cash book（occurrence）。 - Analytical：proof in total（prior year payroll + joiners/leavers + annual pay increase）vs actual。 - Tests of control：authorisation of time sheets/overtime、log of amendments to standing data、Board minutes approval of bonuses、segregation of duties、supervised clocking-in/out。 - Directors' emoluments：schedule split into wages/bonuses/benefits/pension → agree with FS disclosures；inspect Board minutes；obtain written representation that all emoluments disclosed（completeness）。Disclosure 唔足 → opinion 可能要 modified。

B4. Inventories（HKAS 2 — lower of cost and NRV） - Attend the year-end inventory count（HKSA 501 requires attendance where inventory is material, unless impracticable）。 - Sample from count sheets → physical inventory（existence）；sample from physical inventory → count sheets（completeness）。 - Agree sample of inventory items to purchase invoices for cost（valuation）。 - Inspect post-year-end sales invoices to determine NRV; compare NRV with cost → lower of cost and NRV（valuation）。 - Use CAATs to age inventory → identify slow-moving items needing allowance（valuation）。 - Trace goods received immediately pre-year-end to payables and inventory records；trace goods despatched pre-year-end OUT of inventory（completeness and existence — cut-off）。 - Third-party inventory：obtain external confirmation of quantity and condition（rights and obligations）；if material, attend the third party's count（existence and completeness）。 - Analytical：inventory days / inventory turnover vs prior year；gross profit margin vs prior year。 - Standard costs (HKAS 2 allows)：obtain breakdown, agree sample to invoices, discuss update frequency, inspect variance reports。 - Consignment stock：legal ownership stays with the principal；agent's FS 唔可以 include；risk = client includes stock it does not own → overstated inventory。Auditor enquires how consignment stock is segregated。

Inventory count 三階段（HKSA 501）： - Before：obtain count instructions and assess effectiveness；review prior-year working papers；establish locations and third-party holdings；consider circularising third-party warehouses；consider need for experts。 - During — Tests of control：non-warehouse staff count（segregation of duties）；counted items flagged；no movements during count（24/7 client → segregate despatches/deliveries to a different location and count separately）；count sheets in numerical sequence；two people per team；damaged/obsolete items segregated；count sheets must NOT show expected quantities。 - During — Substantive：test counts both directions；inspect condition for damage/obsolescence（valuation）；record last deliveries pre-year-end。 - Auditor's objectives：evaluate management's instructions and procedures; observe the count; inspect the condition of inventory; perform test counts。注意：count 係 client 嘅責任，auditor 唔係去幫手數！ - Perpetual inventory counts：全年輪住數；auditor 要 attend at least one count、inspect exceptions、仍要做 cut-off + NRV 測試。

B5. Receivables and Prepayments（HKFRS 9 ECL） - Obtain aged receivables listing, cast, agree total with FS（valuation and presentation）；agree receivables ledger control account with list of balances（completeness and existence）。 - Inspect post-year-end cash receipts → trace to pre-year-end balances（rights and obligations and existence）— 亦係 valuation 嘅 key evidence。 - Assess reasonableness of expected credit loss allowance per HKFRS 9；discuss aged balances with credit control manager（valuation）。 - Sample of year-end balances → agree back to GDNs and sales orders（existence）。 - Inspect customer correspondence for disputed items（existence, valuation, rights and obligations）。 - Inspect listing for credit balances → reclassify to payables（classification）。 - Analytical：trade receivables days vs prior year（completeness and valuation）。 - Trade receivables confirmation（HKSA 505）： - Client prepares letter on client's letterhead；auditor sends it；customer replies directly to the auditor。 - Positive request：reply whether agree or disagree。Negative request：reply only if disagree — 只係 risk of material misstatement is low 先用得。 - ⚠️ Confirmation does NOT test valuation — 只 verify existence and rights and obligations。就算 customer 確認咗都唔代表佢找得起 → 要做 post-year-end cash receipts testing 補 valuation。 - Follow-up：non-response → second letter / phone call (with client's permission) / alternative procedures（post-year-end cash receipts testing）。 - Doubts about reliability（HKSA 505.A11）：response not received directly / not from intended party → obtain further evidence（e.g. telephone the respondent to verify an email response）。Oral responses are NOT external confirmations — request written response direct to auditor; if none, seek other evidence。 - Prepayments：inspect cash book/bank statements that payment made；inspect invoices that goods/services not yet received；recalculate prepaid amount；compare with prior year。

B6. Bank and Cash - Two key sources of evidence：bank confirmation letter + year-end bank reconciliation。 - Obtain all bank reconciliations, cast, agree cash book balance with TB/FS（accuracy, valuation and allocation）。 - Agree bank confirmation letter balance with year-end reconciliation；agree bank statement balance with original statement（accuracy, valuation and allocation）。 - Trace outstanding payments to post-year-end bank statement and note clearing date（+ completeness）；trace outstanding lodgements to pre-year-end cash book and post-year-end statement（+ existence）。 - Examine old unpresented cheques — write back if no longer valid（completeness）。 - Inspect bank confirmation letter for security pledged / legal right of set-off（presentation and rights and obligations）；亦可以發現 unrecorded loans（completeness of liabilities）。 - Window dressing：payment made immediately AFTER year-end 當作 year-end 前已付 → bank balance 同 payables 齊齊 understated。Auditor 要求 client add back to payables and remove from the bank reconciliation；唔改 + material → modified opinion。 - Bank confirmation request contents：company name; details of current/deposit/loan accounts; account numbers; currency; collateral; contingent liabilities; year-end (audit confirmation) date; authority to disclose date; contact name (engagement partner); audit firm contact details。Bank 要 client permission；auditor 喺 year-end 前提早幾星期直接寄畀 bank。 - Physical cash count（if cash material）：count petty cash, agree with FS（accuracy, valuation, existence）；check insurance cover for large cash balances。

B7. Financial Instruments and Investments - Fair value exercise 高風險；auditor 唔可以幫 client 做 valuation，但要 evaluate 估值者嘅 qualifications, experience, competence, independence and objectivity；assumptions/methodologies 同往年一致；controls over the valuation。 - 有疑慮 → use an auditor's expert per HKSA 620 Using the Work of an Auditor's Expert。 - Derivatives（e.g. forward foreign currency contracts）：remeasure to fair value through profit or loss；agree contracted rates with the contract；agree FV gain/loss with P&L；check no set-off of financial assets/liabilities unless legal right of set-off。 - Financial liabilities：confirm material loans with lender；recalculate interest accruals；examine loan agreements for covenant compliance；inspect security。

B8. Tangible Non-current Assets（HKAS 16） - Cast non-current assets register, agree totals with FS, agree opening balances b/f（completeness, classification, presentation）。 - Sample from register → physically inspect assets（existence）；sample physical assets → register（completeness）。Inspect for damage/impairment indicators（valuation）。 - Additions：agree cost to supplier invoice（valuation）；invoice in company's name（rights and obligations）；confirm capital in nature not repairs（existence）。 - Properties：inspect title deeds in company's name（rights and obligations）。 - Revaluation：inspect valuation report; agree to GL/FS; ensure all assets in the same asset class revalued — no cherry-picking；HKAS 16.31 requires revaluations with sufficient regularity that carrying amount does not differ materially from fair value。Revaluation gain → revaluation reserve via OCI，唔係 P&L。 - Depreciation：recalculate charge for a sample；proof in total per asset category（analytical）；review profits/losses on disposal — large loss suggests under-depreciation（policy too long）；assess useful lives vs asset replacement cycle。 - Tests of control：Board authorisation of capital expenditure；approval of disposals；reconciliation of register reviewed；insurance cover adequate。

B9. Intangible Assets（HKAS 38） - Recognition criteria（HKAS 38.21(a), (b)）：(a) probable that expected future economic benefits will flow to the entity；(b) cost can be measured reliably。 - Internally generated goodwill 永遠唔可以 recognise（no active market → cost cannot be reliably measured）。 - Research costs must always be expensed；只有 development costs 符合 criteria 先可以 capitalise。 - Procedures：inspect purchase documentation / licence agreements（existence, rights and obligations, valuation）；inspect specialist valuer's reports；assess indicators of impairment；for development costs — discuss project with management, inspect project plans and budgets, inspect Board minutes；recalculate amortisation。

B10. Payables and Accruals - Obtain trade payables listing, cast, agree with GL and FS；reconcile purchase ledger control account with listing（completeness）。 - Supplier statement reconciliations — key completeness procedure; supplier statement 係 external evidence，可靠。 - Inspect post-year-end payments relating to current year → agree to purchase ledger / accruals schedule（completeness）。 - Review post-year-end invoices relating to current year → agree to accruals schedule（completeness）。 - Sample of GRNs immediately pre- and post-year-end → pre-year-end included in payables, post-year-end excluded（completeness of payables and cut-off of purchases）。 - Inquire of management re unrecorded liabilities（completeness）。 - Inspect purchase ledger for debit balances → consider reclassification to receivables/prepayments（classification）。 - Trade payables confirmation：verifies completeness and existence；less common than receivables confirmation（因為 supplier statements 已係好證據）；懷疑 unrecorded liabilities 時要 include regular suppliers with small or nil balances；confirmation 唔可以單獨作為 sufficient appropriate evidence。 - Analytical：total payables vs prior year；trade payables days vs prior year。

B11. Provisions（HKAS 37） - Recognition criteria（HKAS 37.10）：(1) present obligation as a result of a past event；(2) payment probable（more likely than not）；(3) amount can be estimated reliably。全部符合先 recognise provision。 - Payment 只係 possible → contingent liability：disclose in notes（唔入帳）；remote → 唔使披露。 - Reimbursement asset：只有 receipt virtually certain 先可以 recognise（higher hurdle than probable）；probable → disclose contingent asset。 - Rare exception（HKAS 37.92）：disclosure would seriously prejudice the entity in a dispute → disclose general nature of dispute + fact and reason for non-disclosure（extremely rare）。 - Procedures：obtain breakdown of provisions, cast, agree to GL/FS；discuss with directors / inspect supporting docs for present obligation；inspect Board minutes for probability；recalculate provision components；review post-year-end cash book for payments；obtain confirmation from lawyers（with client's permission）or inspect legal correspondence；review legal expenses account in GL for unidentified claims；review FS disclosures for HKAS 37 compliance；obtain written representation that provision appropriately valued and complete。 - 應 recognise 而無 recognise / 應 disclose 而無 disclose，material → modified opinion（qualified 'except for' if material but not pervasive）。

B12. Non-current Liabilities - Split loan between current（≤1 year）and non-current（>1 year）per HKAS 1；split 錯 → current liabilities overstated、non-current understated、distorts liquidity ratios（current ratio）。 - Recalculate interest charge and accrual；inspect cash book for repayments；agree balances with original loan statement and bank confirmation letter（completeness — 可以發現 unrecorded loans）；inspect loan agreements for restrictive covenants — breach 可能令 loan immediately repayable → reclassify to current。

B13. Capital and Reserves - 香港公司喺 Companies Ordinance 下 no longer have share premium（無面額制度）— shares issued above former par value → 全部入 share capital。 - Revaluation gain：Dr Property, plant and equipment / Cr Revaluation reserve。 - Dividends：只在 legal obligation 存在時（declared at or before reporting date）先可以 recognise as liability；declared after year-end → 唔可以認；dividends 永遠係 movement on reserves（statement of changes in equity），唔係 P&L expense。 - Procedures：agree share capital with statutory documents；agree share issue proceeds with cash book/bank statements；inspect Board minutes for share issues and dividend declarations；recalculate dividend based on dividend per share；agree dividend to post-year-end cash book。

B14. Leases（HKFRS 16） - Lessee：recognise right-of-use (ROU) asset + lease liability at commencement；exemption（optional）for short-term (<12 months) and low-value leases（tablets, PCs, small office furniture, telephones）。 - ROU asset = lease liability at initial measurement + payments at/before commencement − lease incentives received + initial direct costs + estimated dismantling costs。 - Lease liability = PV of unpaid lease payments using interest rate implicit in the lease；subsequent：b/f + interest − payments ± remeasurement。 - ROU asset depreciated per HKAS 16 cost model over earlier of end of useful life and end of lease term。 - Procedures：agree prior-year lease liabilities b/f；cast lease schedule；obtain lease agreements and recalculate ROU asset and lease liability；reperform closing liability roll-forward（opening − payments + interest at implicit rate）；agree depreciation charge with P&L；review disclosures for HKFRS 16 and HKFRS 7 compliance。

B15. Related Parties（HKAS 24 (Revised) / HKSA 550） - HKSA 550.2：related-party relationships/transactions may give rise to higher risks of material misstatement than unrelated parties — complex structures；systems ineffective at identifying related-party transactions；transactions may not be at market terms（甚至無 consideration）。 - Risk assessment stage：engagement team discussion；inquire of management（identity of related parties, nature of relationships, transactions, controls）；professional scepticism throughout。 - During audit：agree sample of transactions to supporting docs and check market rates；review prior-year working papers；review public register for directors' other interests；review accounting records for large/unusual transactions around year-end；review loan confirmations for related-party guarantors；review tax returns and lawyers' correspondence；inspect bank confirmation letter。 - Previously unidentified related party discovered → inform the engagement team as soon as possible；request management identify all transactions with them；inquire why controls failed；perform substantive procedures；revisit risk assessment；if intentional non-disclosure → fraud risk factor（HKSA 240）。 - Written representation（must obtain）：management have disclosed the identity of all related parties and all related-party relationships/transactions of which they are aware, and have appropriately accounted for and disclosed them per HKAS 24 (Revised)。

C. Evidence — 邊啲 evidence 最強

  • External confirmations（HKSA 505）：trade receivables / trade payables / bank confirmation letters — 由第三方直接回覆 auditor，可靠性高；auditor 必須 maintain control（見 Card 5）。
  • Supplier statements：external evidence，比 client 內部記錄可靠。
  • Written representations（directors' emoluments、provisions、related parties）：internal evidence，只係補充，唔可以取代其他 procedures。
  • 如果 procedures 攞唔到 sufficient appropriate evidence，alternative procedures 又唔得 → limitation of scope → 可能 modify opinion。

D. Ethics 相關要點（本章範圍）

  • Payroll 係 sensitive area — auditor 必須保持 confidentiality。
  • Auditor must not carry out the fair value valuation for the client（只能 evaluate 證據）— 避免 self-review 威脅；需要時用 auditor's expert（HKSA 620）。
  • 使用 client 嘅 investment manager / valuer 嘅工作前，必須評估其 independence and objectivity、qualifications、competence。
  • Related-party 非自願披露 / 管理層刻意隱瞞 → fraud risk factor → HKSA 240。

Question

Per HKSA 505, how does the auditor maintain control over external confirmation requests?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
HKSA 505.7 — four elements: 1. Determining the information to be confirmed or requested; 2. Selecting the appropriate confirming party; 3. Designing the confirmation requests, including determining that requests are properly addressed and contain return information for responses to be sent directly to the auditor; 4. Sending the requests, including follow-up requests when applicable, to the confirming party.
