M6 Chapter 25: Joint Arrangements — Phase 3 Quiz

Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. … — 超時如實申報

Q1 (MCQ)

Entity R, Entity S and Entity T establish an arrangement to operate a logistics hub. Their voting rights in the arrangement are Entity R 40%, Entity S 35% and Entity T 25%. The contractual arrangement requires 60% of the voting rights to make decisions about the relevant activities of the arrangement. The contractual arrangement is silent on which parties must agree to such decisions.

In accordance with HKFRS 11 Joint Arrangements, which of the following statements is correct?

A. Entity R, Entity S and Entity T collectively control the arrangement, so all three parties have joint control. B. Entity R and Entity S have joint control of the arrangement because together they hold 75% of the voting rights. C. The arrangement is not a joint arrangement because the required voting threshold can be achieved by more than one combination of agreeing parties. D. Entity R controls the arrangement because it is the single largest holder of voting rights.

Q2 (MCQ)

Seagull Limited (Seagull) and Otter Limited (Otter) incorporate Seabird Limited (Seabird), a limited company in which each holds 50% of the voting shares. All decisions about Seabird's relevant activities require the unanimous consent of Seagull and Otter. Seabird's legal form gives Seagull and Otter rights to Seabird's net assets, and the contractual arrangement does not change those rights. However, Seagull and Otter agree to purchase all of Seabird's output in a 50:50 ratio at a price equal to Seabird's cost of production, Seabird is prohibited from selling to third parties, and Seabird relies on cash calls from Seagull and Otter to settle its liabilities as they fall due.

In accordance with HKFRS 11, how should Seagull classify its interest in Seabird?

A. Joint venture, because the arrangement is structured through a separate vehicle incorporated as a limited company. B. Joint venture, because the legal form of the vehicle gives the parties rights to the net assets of the arrangement. C. Joint operation, because Seabird's activities primarily aim to provide the parties with output and Seabird depends on the parties on a continuous basis for settling its liabilities. D. Not a joint arrangement, because unanimous consent over relevant activities is not, by itself, sufficient to establish joint control.

Q3 (MCQ — journal entry)

Crane Limited (Crane) is a joint venturer holding 50% of Heron Bay Limited (Heron Bay), a joint venture accounted for using the equity method in Crane's consolidated financial statements. During the year, Heron Bay declares and pays a cash dividend of HK$90,000 in total to its shareholders.

Which journal entry should Crane record in its consolidated financial statements upon receipt of its share of the dividend?

A. Dr Cash HK$45,000 / Cr Dividend income HK$45,000 B. Dr Cash HK$45,000 / Cr Investment in joint venture HK$45,000 C. Dr Cash HK$45,000 / Cr Share of profit of joint venture HK$45,000 D. Dr Investment in joint venture HK$45,000 / Cr Cash HK$45,000

Q4 (Calculation — short answer)

Heron Limited (Heron) and Kite Limited (Kite) are joint operators in a joint operation (not structured through a separate vehicle) in which each party has a 50% share of the assets, liabilities, revenues and expenses of the arrangement. On 1 April 20X4, the joint operation acquired a machine for HK$8,000,000. The machine has a useful life of 8 years and nil residual value; depreciation is charged on a straight-line, monthly basis from the date of acquisition. Heron's reporting date is 31 December 20X4.

In accordance with HKFRS 11 and HKAS 16, calculate the carrying amount of Heron's share of the machine to be recognised in Heron's financial statements at 31 December 20X4. Show your workings.

Q5 (MCQ — calculation)

On 1 January 20X4, Victoria Limited (Victoria) contributed HK$800,000 in cash to establish Peak Limited (Peak), a joint venture in which Victoria holds 50% of the voting rights. Victoria applies the equity method. For the year ended 31 December 20X4, Peak reported a net profit of HK$360,000, which includes a gain of HK$24,000 on the sale of inventory from Peak to Victoria; none of that inventory had been on-sold by Victoria to third parties by the year end. During the year, Peak declared dividends of HK$80,000 in total.

What is the carrying amount of Victoria's investment in Peak at 31 December 20X4?

A. HK$916,000 B. HK$928,000 C. HK$940,000 D. HK$968,000
