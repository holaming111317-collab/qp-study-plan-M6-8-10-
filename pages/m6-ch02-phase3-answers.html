M6 Chapter 2: Fair Value Measurement — Phase 3 Model Answers

Q1 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): Under HKFRS 13, fair value is defined as the price that would be received to sell an asset (or paid to transfer a liability) in an orderly transaction between market participants at the measurement date. Statement B reproduces this definition exactly. 解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係定義題，考兩個核心原則。A 錯 — "paid to acquire" 係 entry price，fair value 係 exit price（賣出唔係買入）。C 錯 — fair value 係 market-based measurement，用 market participants 嘅假設，唔係 entity-specific 嘅自用現金流（嗰個係 value in use 嘅概念）。D 錯 — 賣家財困下嘅即時出售係 distress sale / forced transaction，唔符合 orderly transaction 嘅假設（要有足夠市場曝光期）。考試伏位：定義每個字都有分 — "received to sell"、"orderly"、"market participants"、"measurement date" 缺一不可。 Rule Check: HKFRS 13.9 (definition); HKFRS 13.A (orderly transaction) Common Trap（繁中）: 同學成日將 "received to sell" 同 "paid to acquire" 調轉，又以為用自己公司嘅 forecast 就得 — 記住：exit price + 市場參與者角度，entity-specific 假設永遠唔得。

Q2 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): Under HKFRS 13, transport costs are adjusted in measuring fair value because location is a characteristic of the asset (a commodity). Transaction costs (brokerage fees) are not adjusted — they are not a characteristic of the asset; they are specific to the transaction and differ depending on how the entity transacts. Fair value per bag = market price − HK$25 transport costs only. 解題思路（繁中）: 口訣「運輸扣得，佣金唔扣得」。A 錯 — 連 brokerage fees 都扣，混淆咗「揀 most advantageous market」同「計 fair value」兩個步驟（揀市場時先會扣 transaction costs）。B 錯 — 倒轉咗兩者嘅處理。D 錯 — transport costs 係資產特性（location），必須扣。考試伏位：呢個雙重處理係 LP Knowledge Check 同款伏位 — transaction costs 只用嚟揀 most advantageous market，計最終 FV 時永遠唔扣。 Rule Check: HKFRS 13.25–26 (transaction costs and transport costs, as applied in LP §2.1.2) Common Trap（繁中）: 見到題目兩個成本都列出嚟，就以為兩個都要處理 — 其實一個扣一個唔扣。仲要記住「揀市場」同「計 FV」對 transaction costs 嘅處理係相反嘅。

Q3 — Answer: C (HK$785)

Model Answer (EN): Step 1 — Determine the principal market (market with the greatest volume and level of activity, based on market-wide data): West market (80,000 units). Step 2 — Fair value is the price in the principal market, adjusted for transport costs but not for transaction costs: Fair value = HK$800 − HK$15 = HK$785 per unit. 解題思路（繁中）: 三步走：(1) 有交易量數據 → 一定有 principal market（West，80,000 units 最旺），即使 Central 價錢最高都唔准轉場；(2) FV = 該市場價 − transport costs；(3) transaction costs 唔扣。A (HK$800) 錯 — 直接用報價，唔記得扣 transport costs。B (HK$805) 錯 — 用咗 Central market（840 − 35），係「邊個價高用邊個」嘅直覺錯誤。D (HK$760) 錯 — 連 transaction costs 都扣埋（800 − 15 − 25），混淆咗 most advantageous market 嘅 net proceeds 計法。考試伏位：principal market 存在時，most advantageous market 完全唔使理。 Rule Check: HKFRS 13.17–18 (principal market; most advantageous market only in its absence); HKFRS 13.19 (access to the market); HKFRS 13.25–26 (transport costs adjusted, transaction costs not) Common Trap（繁中）: 兩大錯法 — (1) 用價錢最高嘅市場（應該用交易量最高嘅）；(2) 將 transaction costs 扣埋入 FV（佢哋只用嚟揀 most advantageous market）。

Q4 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): The valuation uses both Level 2 inputs (P/E multiples of comparable listed companies) and Level 3 inputs (the internally developed discount for lack of marketability). Under HKFRS 13, a fair value measurement is categorised in its entirety in the same level as the lowest level input that is significant to the entire measurement. Because the unobservable adjustment is significant, the whole measurement is classified as Level 3. 解題思路（繁中）: 呢題考「in entirety」規則。A 錯 — comparable 公司上市唔等於輸入係 Level 1；Level 1 要 identical item 喺活躍市場嘅未調整報價，unlisted shares 永遠唔會係。B 錯 — 雖然 multiples 係 observable，但重大 unobservable adjustment 會將成個 measurement 拉落 Level 3（呢個係最似啱嘅 distractor — 如果個 discount 唔重大，先會歸 Level 2）。D 錯 — HKFRS 13 唔准拆開分級，成個 measurement 只歸一個 level。考試伏位：關鍵詞係 "significant" — 題目改做 "not significant" 答案就會變 Level 2（對照 LP Knowledge Check Q7(ii)）。 Rule Check: HKFRS 13.72–90 (fair value hierarchy and categorisation in its entirety) Common Trap（繁中）: 同學見到大部分輸入係 observable 就揀 Level 2 — 記住一粒老鼠屎（significant unobservable input）就足以令成個估值降級做 Level 3。

Q5 — Answer: HK$10,000

Model Answer (EN): Step 1 — Probability-weighted expected cash flows: - HK$6,000 × 30% = HK$1,800 - HK$10,000 × 40% = HK$4,000 - HK$16,000 × 30% = HK$4,800 - Expected cash flows = HK$10,600 Step 2 — Discount rate = risk-free rate + risk premium = 3.5% + 2.5% = 6% Step 3 — Fair value = HK$10,600 ÷ 1.06 = HK$10,000 解題思路（繁中）: 呢題完全跟 LP Apply and Analyse 3 嘅現值技術框架（Exhibit 2.5）：先計 probability-weighted expected cash flows（唔係直接用最可能嘅 HK$10,000），再將 time value of money（risk-free rate 3.5%）加上 price for bearing uncertainty（risk premium 2.5%）組成折現率 6%，最後折現。常見錯法：(1) 唔折現直接用 HK$10,600；(2) 只用 risk-free rate 折（10,600 ÷ 1.035 = 10,242），漏咗 risk premium；(3) 用最可能現金流 HK$10,000 折現（= 9,434），唔做 probability weighting。三個都係 LP 強調嘅 present value elements 漏咗其中一環。 Rule Check: HKFRS 13.61–62 (income approach / valuation techniques); HKFRS 13.B10–B30 (present value techniques) Common Trap（繁中）: 折現率一定要兩層加埋 — risk-free rate + risk premium；同埋 expected cash flows 係 probability-weighted，唔係「最可能發生」嗰個數。
