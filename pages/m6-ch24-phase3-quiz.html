Q1 (1 mark)

In accordance with HKAS 28 (2011) Investments in Associates and Joint Ventures, which of the following statements is correct?

A. Heron Limited holds 26% of the voting power of Wader Limited. Because its holding exceeds 20%, Wader Limited must be accounted for as an associate even though an administrator has been appointed to manage Wader Limited's affairs.

B. Significant influence is the power to direct the activities of another entity that significantly affect the investor's returns.

C. Kingfisher Limited holds 18% of the voting power of Tern Limited. Kingfisher Limited has the contractual right to appoint two of Tern Limited's seven directors and supplies the essential software platform on which Tern Limited's operations depend. Kingfisher Limited may have significant influence over Tern Limited.

D. Sandpiper Limited holds 30% of the voting power of Plover Limited and has the right to appoint six of the eight directors on Plover Limited's board. Plover Limited is an associate of Sandpiper Limited.

Q2 (1 mark)

Avocet Limited holds 35% of the ordinary shares of Stilt Limited and accounts for Stilt Limited as an associate using the equity method in its consolidated financial statements. During the year, Stilt Limited declared and paid a total dividend of HK$200,000 to its shareholders out of post-acquisition profits. Avocet Limited received HK$70,000.

In accordance with HKAS 28, how should Avocet Limited account for the dividend received in the financial statements in which the investment is equity accounted?

A. Recognise dividend income of HK$70,000 in profit or loss.

B. Debit Cash HK$70,000 and credit Investment in associate HK$70,000.

C. Recognise dividend income of HK$200,000 in profit or loss.

D. Reduce the share of Stilt Limited's profit for the year by HK$70,000.

Q3 (1 mark)

On 1 January 20X5, Marlin Limited acquired 30% of the ordinary shares of Coral Limited and correctly accounted for Coral Limited as an associate. At the acquisition date, the carrying amount of Coral Limited's identifiable net assets was HK$2,600,000. Marlin Limited identified an unrecognised patent with a fair value of HK$400,000 and a remaining useful life of eight years (straight-line, nil residual value). The carrying amounts of all other identifiable assets and liabilities were equal to their fair values.

For the year ended 31 December 20X5, Coral Limited reported a net profit of HK$500,000.

In accordance with HKAS 28, what is Marlin Limited's share of Coral Limited's profit or loss for the year ended 31 December 20X5?

A. HK$150,000

B. HK$100,000

C. HK$135,000

D. HK$30,000

Q4 (1 mark)

Gull Limited holds 25% of the ordinary shares of Puffin Limited, an associate accounted for using the equity method. During the year ended 31 December 20X4, Puffin Limited sold goods to Gull Limited for HK$200,000. The goods had cost Puffin Limited HK$140,000. All of the goods remained in Gull Limited's inventory at 31 December 20X4.

Using the approach adopted in the Learning Pack under which the accounts to be adjusted are determined by reference to the entity that recorded the gain and the entity that holds the asset, which pro forma journal entry eliminates the unrealised gain in Gull Limited's consolidation worksheet?

A. DR Share of associate's profit or loss HK$15,000 / CR Inventory HK$15,000

B. DR Share of associate's profit or loss HK$60,000 / CR Inventory HK$60,000

C. DR Cost of sales HK$15,000 / CR Investment in associate HK$15,000

D. DR Investment in associate HK$15,000 / CR Share of associate's profit or loss HK$15,000

Q5 (2 marks)

On 1 January 20X4, Kestrel Limited acquired 40% of the ordinary shares of Harrier Limited for HK$2,000,000 cash, giving Kestrel Limited significant influence over Harrier Limited. At the acquisition date, the carrying amount of Harrier Limited's identifiable net assets was HK$3,800,000. Harrier Limited had an unrecognised licence with a fair value of HK$200,000 and a remaining useful life of five years (straight-line, nil residual value). The carrying amounts of all other identifiable assets and liabilities were equal to their fair values.

For the year ended 31 December 20X4, Harrier Limited reported a net profit of HK$700,000 and declared a dividend of HK$100,000 to its shareholders.

In accordance with HKAS 28, what is the equity accounted carrying amount of Kestrel Limited's investment in Harrier Limited at 31 December 20X4?

A. HK$2,240,000

B. HK$2,264,000

C. HK$2,164,000

D. HK$2,224,000
