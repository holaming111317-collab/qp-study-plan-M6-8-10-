M6 Chapter 20: Financial Statements Introduction — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives (3–5, English, action verbs)

  1. Explain the basis of preparation and presentation of general purpose financial statements under HKAS 1 Presentation of Financial Statements, including the eight general features (true and fair view, going concern, accrual basis, materiality, offsetting, frequency of reporting, comparative information, consistency of presentation).
  2. Identify the components of a complete set of financial statements under HKAS 1 and state when a third statement of financial position is required.
  3. Apply the current / non-current classification rules for assets and liabilities, including operating-cycle and refinancing considerations.
  4. Prepare and present a statement of financial position, a statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income (single or two-statement approach; expenses by nature or by function) and a statement of changes in equity in accordance with HKAS 1.
  5. Distinguish items reported in profit or loss, other comprehensive income (with/without subsequent reclassification), the statement of changes in equity and the notes.

必背考點

A. Recognition（元素何時入帳 — 與 Framework 連繫）

  • Accrual basis (HKAS 1 para. 27): FS 以 accrual basis 編製（cash flow information 除外）；income, expenses, assets, liabilities, equity 於滿足 Framework 中各元素的 definition 及 recognition criteria 時確認（詳見 Chapter 1）。
  • Going concern (HKAS 1): FS 一般假設 entity 在 foreseeable future 持續經營（going concern assumption）。
  • All-inclusive approach (HKAS 1 para. 88): 期內所有 income 及 expense items 必須計入 profit or loss，除非另一 HKFRS 要求或准許計入 other comprehensive income (OCI)。
  • Profit or loss (HKAS 1 para. 7): total of income less expenses, excluding OCI items。
  • OCI (HKAS 1 para. 7): items of income and expense (including reclassification adjustments) not recognised in profit or loss as required or permitted by other HKFRSs（例：revaluation surplus 變動 under HKAS 16 para. 39；fair value gains/losses on investments in equity instruments）。
  • Total comprehensive income (HKAS 1 para. 7): profit or loss + OCI = change in equity during a period other than transactions with owners in their capacity as owners。

B. Measurement（計量基礎 — 本章以披露為主）

  • HKAS 1 本身不訂立計量規則；recognition/measurement 由其他 HKFRSs 訂明（e.g. HKAS 16 PPE、HKAS 2 Inventories、HKAS 38 Intangible Assets）。
  • Notes 必須披露 measurement basis (or bases) used in preparing the FS（historical cost、current cost、fair value 等），因為這對用家分析有重大影響（HKAS 1 para. 117 相關；HKAS 16 亦要求披露 measurement bases）。
  • Judgements (HKAS 1 para. 122): management 在應用會計政策時作出、對 FS 確認金額有最重大影響的 judgements 必須披露。
  • Offsetting (HKAS 1 paras. 32–35): assets/liabilities、income/expenses 不得對銷，除非某 HKFRS 要求或准許（例：HKAS 32 在符合條件時准許 financial asset/liability 對銷）。Measuring assets net of valuation allowances（如 trade receivables 減 provision for expected credit loss）不是 offsetting。同一交易或同類交易的 gains/losses 可 net（如出售非流動資產的 gain/loss；外匯收益虧損；HKAS 37 provision 開支與第三方補償），但 material 的 gains/losses 必須分開列報。

C. Disclosure / Presentation requirements（本章核心）

C1. Complete set of financial statements（HKAS 1 para. 10） - statement of financial position as at the end of the period - statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income for the period - statement of changes in equity for the period - statement of cash flows for the period（HKAS 7 — 詳見 Chapter 21） - notes, comprising material accounting policy information and other explanatory information - comparative information in respect of the preceding period - a third statement of financial position as at the beginning of the preceding period when there is retrospective application of an accounting policy / retrospective restatement / reclassification with a material effect on that SOFP (HKAS 1 paras. 40A–40C)；related notes for this third SOFP are not required。例：year-end 31 Dec 20X9 → 三份 SOFP 日期為 31 Dec 20X9、31 Dec 20X8、1 Jan 20X8。

C2. True and fair view / compliance（HKAS 1 paras. 15, 16, 23） - 遵從 HKFRSs（必要時加 additional disclosures）即 presumed to give a true and fair view。 - 遵從 HKFRSs 的 entity 須在 notes 作出 explicit and unreserved statement of compliance (para. 16)。 - 極罕有情況下遵從某 HKFRS 會誤導而監管框架又不准偏離：須披露 (i) 該 HKFRS 名稱、要求性質及管理層認為遵從會誤導的原因；(ii) 每期為達致 true and fair view 所需對各項目的調整 (para. 23)。

C3. Consistency (HKAS 1 paras. 45–46) - Presentation/classification 須逐期一致，除非：(i) significant change in operations；(ii) 改變能提供更 relevant 資訊；或 (iii) 另一 HKFRS 要求。改變時 comparatives 須 reclassify。

C4. Materiality and aggregation (HKAS 1 paras. 29–31; Framework para. 2.11) - 'Material' = information 若 omitted, misstated or obscured 可合理預期影響 primary users 的決定；以 FS as a whole 評估；HKICPA 不能設定 uniform quantitative threshold。 - 每個 material class of similar items 須分開列報；dissimilar items 除非 immaterial 否則分開列報；immaterial 金額可 aggregate。 - HKFRS 指定的 disclosure 若 resulting information not material，可不作披露（但可自願加 additional disclosure）。

C5. Comparative information (HKAS 1 paras. 38, 38A, 38B, 41, 42) - 最少列報 preceding period 的所有金額（即最少兩年）；narrative/descriptive comparatives 若 relevant 亦須提供（例：未解決官司的進展）。 - 改變 presentation/classification 時，comparatives 須 reclassify，並披露 reclassification 的 nature, amount and reason。 - HKAS 8 的 accounting policy 變更及 error 更正亦須調整 comparatives（Chapter 13）。

C6. Identification & reporting period (HKAS 1 paras. 49–51, 36–37) - FS 須 clearly identified 並與同一文件內其他資料區分；prominently display：entity 名稱（及改名）、individual entity 或 group、reporting period 結算日/涵蓋期間、presentation currency（HKAS 21 定義）、level of rounding。 - Complete set of FS at least annually；可採用 52-week period。改變 year-end 致期間長短於一年：須披露原因及 comparatives 不完全可比的事實。

C7. Statement of financial position（HKAS 1 paras. 54–58, 60–70, 77–79） - HKAS 1 不指定 order or format（para. 57；Implementation Guidance 只提供一個可接受格式；可採 Assets = Equity + Liabilities 或 Assets − Liabilities = Equity）。 - Minimum line items (para. 54)：PPE; investment property; intangible assets; financial assets; investments accounted for using the equity method; inventories; trade and other receivables; cash and cash equivalents; assets/disposal groups held for sale (HKFRS 5); trade and other payables; provisions; financial liabilities; current tax liabilities/assets (HKAS 12); deferred tax liabilities/assets; liabilities in disposal groups held for sale; non-controlling interests (within equity); issued capital and reserves attributable to owners of the parent。 - Additional items/headings/subtotals 當 relevant to understanding (para. 55)，按 nature and liquidity of assets、function of assets、amounts/nature/timing of liabilities 評估。 - Current asset (para. 66)：(a) 預期在 normal operating cycle 內變現/出售/消耗；(b) 主要持作 trading；(c) 預期於報告期後 12 個月內變現；(d) cash or cash equivalent（HKAS 7 定義）除非受限於報告期後至少 12 個月不得兌換或用於償債。其他全部 = non-current。 - Current liability (para. 69)：(a) 預期在 normal operating cycle 內結清；(b) 主要持作 trading；(c) 於報告期後 12 個月內到期結清；(d) 於報告期末沒有權利將結清遞延至報告期後至少 12 個月。其他全部 = non-current。 - Operating cycle (para. 68)：由購入資產加工到變現為 cash/cash equivalents 的時間；通常 12 個月內但可更長。Operating cycle > 12 個月時（如物業發展 18 個月），inventories/trade payables 仍可全數列 current，但須就每個 line item 額外披露預期 超過 12 個月後收回/結清的金額 (para. 61)。 - 金融機構等無清晰 operating cycle 的 entity 可按 liquidity order 列報 (para. 63)。 - Share capital 每類別須披露 (para. 79(a))：authorised 股數；issued（fully/partly paid）股數；par value per share 或無面值；期初至期末股數 reconciliation。另須描述每項 reserve 的 nature and purpose (para. 79(b))。Line items 須再作 subclassification（paras. 77–78），如 PPE 按 HKAS 16 para. 37 分類（land、machinery 等，通常於 notes 披露）。

C8. Statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income（HKAS 1 paras. 10A, 81A–82A, 85, 90–93, 97–105） - Presentation choice (para. 10A)：(i) single statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income（profit or loss section 直接接 OCI section）；或 (ii) two statements — separate statement of profit or loss 緊接 statement of comprehensive income（後者以 profit or loss 數字開始）。 - Profit or loss section minimum line items (para. 82)：revenue；gains/losses on derecognition of financial assets at amortised cost；finance costs；impairment losses (including reversals)；share of profit or loss of associates and JVs (equity method)；tax expense；a single amount for the total of discontinued operations。須另列 profit or loss、total OCI 及 comprehensive income for the period (para. 81A)，並分配 profit or loss 及 total comprehensive income 予 owners of the parent 及 non-controlling interests (para. 81B)。 - Material items (paras. 97–98)：性質/金額重大的 income/expense 須單獨披露，例：write-down of inventories to NRV 或 PPE to recoverable amount（及 reversals）；restructuring 及 provision reversals；disposals of PPE / investments；discontinued operations；litigation settlements。 - Expense analysis (paras. 99, 102–103, 105)：按 nature（depreciation、employee benefits、raw materials used 等；簡單，不用分攤）或 function / 'cost of sales'（cost of sales、distribution costs、administrative expenses；較 relevant 但需 arbitrary allocations）分類 — 選能提供 reliable and more relevant information 者。 - OCI section (paras. 82A, 90–91)：分開列示 (a) items that will not be reclassified subsequently to profit or loss；(b) items that may be reclassified subsequently when specific conditions are met；並單獨列示 share of OCI of associates/JVs。每項 OCI 的 income tax 須在 statement 或 notes 披露；OCI items 可 net of tax 或 before tax（連一個合計稅額） 列報。 - Reclassification adjustments (paras. 92–93, 96)：過往在 OCI 確認的 'unrealised' 金額其後 'recycled' 至本期 P&L，須在相關 OCI component 中扣除以避免 double counting。Revaluation surplus 不產生 reclassification adjustments — 永不 recycle 至 P&L；資產 derecognised 時 surplus 可直接轉撥（transfer directly）於 equity 內。

C9. Statement of changes in equity（HKAS 1 paras. 106–110） - 功能：reconcile 每個 equity component 期初至期末的 carrying amount。Total equity 須與 SOFP 的 total equity 相符。 - 必須包括 (para. 106)：(a) total comprehensive income for the period（分開 owners of the parent / NCI）；(b) 每個 component 按 HKAS 8 的 retrospective application/restatement 影響（調整 opening retained earnings，除非準則要求調整其他 component；policy changes 與 error corrections 分開披露）；(d) 每個 component 的 reconciliation，分開列示 profit or loss、OCI、transactions with owners（contributions by / distributions to owners；不導致 loss of control 的 subsidiary ownership 變動）。 - Components of equity (para. 108)：each class of share capital、each class of OCI 的累計結餘（如 revaluation surplus）、retained earnings。 - Dividends (para. 107)：已確認分派予 owners 的股息及每股金額須在 SOCIE 或 notes 披露 — 不得列入 SPLOCI（屬 owner transaction，非 expense）。 - SOCIE 公式：Opening total equity + total comprehensive income −/+ transactions with shareholders（share issues、dividends）= Closing total equity。

D. Key Journal Entries（本章示例中出現、必須識做）

  1. Upward revaluation of PPE（首次、未沖回減值）— gain to OCI（稅前）： - Dr Property, plant and equipment (asset) 120,000 / Cr Revaluation surplus (OCI → equity) 120,000
  2. Deferred tax on revaluation gain（HKAS 12，稅率 16.5% → 120,000 × 16.5% = 19,800）： - Dr Revaluation surplus (equity) 19,800 / Cr Deferred tax liability 19,800 - 淨效應：revaluation surplus 增加 100,200（OCI net of tax）。
  3. Dividend declared（owner transaction，唔係 expense）： - Dr Retained earnings 160,000 / Cr Dividend payable 160,000（ordinary；preference 同理）
  4. Transfer to general reserve（equity 內部轉撥，total equity 不變）： - Dr Retained earnings 20,000 / Cr General reserve 20,000
  5. Issue of shares for cash： - Dr Cash 200,000 / Cr Share capital 200,000
