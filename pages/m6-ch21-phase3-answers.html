M6 Chapter 21: Statement of Cash Flows — Phase 3 Answers

Q1 — Answer: D

Model Answer (EN): Under HKAS 7.6, cash comprises cash on hand and demand deposits, and cash equivalents are short-term, highly liquid investments that are readily convertible to known amounts of cash and which are subject to an insignificant risk of changes in value. Listed ordinary shares, even in a liquid market, are subject to price volatility amounting to a significant risk of changes in value, so they do NOT qualify. 解題思路（繁中）: A（petty cash）同 B（demand deposit）直接落入 "cash" 定義；C 嘅 3 個月 treasury bill 係 textbook cash equivalent（short-term + highly liquid + insignificant risk）。D 嘅上市股票雖然好易賣，但股價波動代表 significant risk of value change — 「highly liquid」單一條件唔足夠。三個條件必須同時成立。 Rule Check: HKAS 7.6 Common Trap（繁中）: 以為「易變現 = cash equivalent」。LP Apply & Analyse 1 明確指出 listed shares 同 high-return money market fund 都因為價格波動而唔算。考試揀 NOT 題時記得逐個選項用三條件測試。

Q2 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): Statement B is NOT correct. HKAS 7.18 permits BOTH the direct and indirect methods for presenting cash flows from operating activities; HKAS 7.19 merely encourages the direct method because it provides more useful information — it is not required. Statements A (HKAS 7.43: non-cash transactions excluded but disclosed in notes), C (lessee's payments to reduce the lease liability are financing cash flows) and D (HKAS 7.31/7.33: interest paid may be operating or financing, separately disclosed and consistent) are all correct. 解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係 NOT-question，要搵出錯嗰句。B 嘅伏位係將 "encouraged" 偷換做 "required" — LP 原文：「both methods are permitted by HKAS 7... the use of the direct method is encouraged」。C 係 LP Exam Practice Q2 嘅考点：lessee 償還 lease liability 本金部分冇得揀，係 financing。D 考 HKAS 7.31 嘅 separately disclosed + consistent 要求。 Rule Check: HKAS 7.18, 7.19, 7.31, 7.33, 7.43 Common Trap（繁中）: 見到 "encouraged" 字眼就當咗 mandatory；同埋以為 lessee 嘅租賃付款可以好似 interest paid 咁自由分類 — lease liability reduction 係指定 financing。

Q3 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): - Net purchases = COGS + Closing inventory − Opening inventory = 640,000 + 120,000 − 96,000 = HK$664,000 - Cash payments to suppliers = Net purchases + Opening accounts payable − Closing accounts payable = 664,000 + 58,000 − 46,000 = HK$676,000 解題思路（繁中）: 兩步計算：先由 COGS 同存貨變動推返 purchases（存貨升咗 24,000，代表買多咗貨，所以要加）；再用應付賬款變動推實付現金（AP 跌咗 12,000，代表找咗舊數，所以加多 12,000）。B (664,000) 係唔記得調整 AP；D (652,000) 係 AP 調相反轉（減 opening 加 closing）；A (616,000) 係存貨調相反轉（640,000 + 96,000 − 120,000）。 Rule Check: HKAS 7.18 (direct method); LP Section 21.3.1 computation rules Common Trap（繁中）: 方向感：存貨/應收等資產增加 = 現金流出（扣）；應付等負債減少 = 現金流出（加返 opening − closing 時已內置）。考試先寫低公式再代數。

Q4 — Answer: D

Model Answer (EN):

HK$
Profit before tax 850,000
Add: Depreciation expense (non-cash) 120,000
Add: Interest expense (accrual, presented separately) 34,000
Less: Gain on disposal of equipment (investing) (35,000)
Less: Increase in trade receivables (48,000)
Add: Decrease in inventory 22,000
Add: Increase in trade payables 15,000
Cash generated from operations 958,000
Less: Interest paid (30,000)
Less: Income tax paid (95,000)
Net cash flows from operating activities 833,000

解題思路（繁中）: 跟 LP Apply & Analyse 2/3 格式：interest expense 屬 accrual，先加返入 profit，然後喺 "Cash generated from operations" 下面用實付利息 (30,000) 同實付稅 (95,000) 做 separate lines。Gain on disposal 係 investing 項目，要扣走。A (863,000) 漏扣 interest paid (958,000 − 95,000)；B (903,000) 將 gain 加返而唔係扣（誤差 70,000）；C (799,000) 冇加返 interest expense（淨計實付利息，變相少加 34,000）。 Rule Check: HKAS 7.18, 7.31, 7.33, 7.35 Common Trap（繁中）: Jun 2025 panel 報告話考生喺呢啲位最弱：gain/loss on disposal 方向調錯、interest 加減錯。記住：expense → add back，cash paid → separate deduction；兩個金額通常唔同（因為 interest payable 有變動）。

Q5 — Answer: A

Model Answer (EN): - Carrying amount of machine = 500,000 − 320,000 = HK$180,000 - Cash proceeds HK$150,000 < carrying amount → Loss on disposal = 180,000 − 150,000 = HK$30,000 - Journal entry: Dr Cash 150,000; Dr Accumulated Depreciation 320,000; Dr Loss on Disposal (P&L) 30,000; Cr Machine (cost) 500,000 (Dr total = Cr total = HK$500,000) - SCF impact: investing cash inflow = HK$150,000 (actual cash proceeds); under the indirect method the HK$30,000 loss is added back to profit before tax. 解題思路（繁中）: 處置固定資產三步：沖銷成本、沖銷累計折舊、差額落 gain/loss。B 嘅錯係當 proceeds = carrying amount (180,000)，完全忽略咗 30,000 虧損；C 係亂谷 accumulated depreciation (350,000) 去迫平條數；D 係新派嘅「net-off」寫法，冇 derecognise 成本同累計折舊，違反 HKAS 16 嘅 derecognition 處理。 Rule Check: HKAS 16 (derecognition of PPE); HKAS 7.16 (investing activities) Common Trap（繁中）: 考試見到 disposal 記住 SCF 入面 investing inflow 係實收現金 150,000，唔係 NBV 180,000；而 P&L 嘅 loss 30,000 喺 indirect method 要 add back — 兩件事唔好撈亂。
