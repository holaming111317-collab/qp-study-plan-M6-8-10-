M6 Chapter 20: Financial Statements Introduction — Phase 3 Answers

Q1 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): A complete set of financial statements under HKAS 1 comprises: (i) a statement of financial position; (ii) a statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income; (iii) a statement of changes in equity; (iv) a statement of cash flows; (v) notes comprising material accounting policy information and other explanatory information; (vi) comparative information for the preceding period; and (vii) a statement of financial position at the beginning of the preceding period when there is retrospective application/restatement or reclassification. A statement of directors' remuneration is NOT a component — directors' remuneration disclosures, where required, appear in the notes, not as a separate primary statement.

解題思路（繁中）: 考「四表一註加比較」嘅完整組合。Option A（SOCIE）、C（notes）、D（comparative information）全部係 HKAS 1 明確列出嘅 components，所以唔係答案。Option B 係虛構嘅「董事薪酬表」— 董事薪酬資料只會喺 notes 披露，唔係一份 primary statement。呢類題目嘅伏位通常係將一個聽落合理嘅披露項目包裝成「statement」。

Rule Check: HKAS 1 para. 10

Common Trap（繁中）: 考生容易漏咗「comparative information」同「notes」都係 complete set 嘅正式組成部分，以為只有四張主表。另外記住第三份 SOFP 只喺追溯調整/重列/重新分類且有重大影響時先需要。

Q2 — Answer: D

Model Answer (EN): Under HKAS 1 para. 66(d), cash or a cash equivalent is classified as current unless it is restricted from being exchanged or used to settle a liability for at least 12 months after the reporting period. The restricted cash deposit (restricted for 24 months) therefore fails all current-asset criteria and is classified as NON-CURRENT. Options A and B are current under para. 66(a) — assets expected to be realised/sold/consumed in the entity's normal operating cycle are current even if realisation is more than 12 months away (the operating cycle here is 15 months). Option C is current under para. 69(a)/70 — liabilities settled within the normal operating cycle are current even if settlement is due more than 12 months after the reporting period.

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題考 operating cycle > 12 個月嘅特殊情況。關鍵概念：current/non-current 分界唔係單純「12 個月」，而係「normal operating cycle 或 12 個月，以適用者為準」。A、B、C 雖然都超過 12 個月先變現/結清，但因為喺 15 個月 operating cycle 之內，所以全部係 current。唯獨 D 嘅受限現金因為 24 個月內唔用得，直接唔符合 HKAS 1 para. 66(d) 嘅例外條件，所以要列 non-current。

Rule Check: HKAS 1 paras. 66, 68, 69, 70

Common Trap（繁中）: 見到「15/14 個月 > 12 個月」就即刻判 non-current 係最常見錯誤 — 一定要諗返 operating cycle。相反方向嘅伏位：受限現金（restricted cash）就算係「cash」，只要被限制使用超過 12 個月都唔可以列 current。

Q3 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): Under HKAS 1 paras. 40A–40B, when an entity makes a retrospective restatement that has a material effect on the information in the statement of financial position at the beginning of the preceding period, it must present three statements of financial position: at the end of the current period (31 December 20X9), at the end of the preceding period (31 December 20X8), and at the beginning of the preceding period (1 January 20X8). Under para. 40C, related notes are NOT required for the third statement of financial position (1 January 20X8). HKAS 8 disclosures about the correction of the error are additionally required.

解題思路（繁中）: 三步判斷：(1) 有冇觸發事件？有 — retrospective restatement of a material prior period error；(2) 對期初 SOFP 有冇 material effect？有（題目講明）；(3) 所以三份 SOFP 嘅日期 = 本期期末、上期期末、上期期初，即 31/12/20X9、31/12/20X8、1/1/20X8。Option A 錯喺「notes required for all three」— HKAS 1 para. 40C 明確話最早嗰份唔使附 notes。Option B 漏咗第三份 SOFP。Option D 日期組合亂嚟（1/1/20X9 唔係規定嘅日期）。

Rule Check: HKAS 1 paras. 40A, 40B, 40C

Common Trap（繁中）: 兩大伏位：(i) 記錯日期 — 「beginning of the preceding period」= 上期期初（1 Jan 20X8），唔係本期期初；(ii) 以為三份 SOFP 全部要附 notes — 其實只有最早嗰份豁免。同埋要記住觸發條件必須有 material effect，冇重大影響就唔使出第三份。

Q4 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): Total comprehensive income = profit or loss + other comprehensive income (net of tax). - Profit for the year: HK$800,000 - OCI — gain on revaluation of building (never reclassified to P&L): HK$200,000 gross; less deferred tax HK$200,000 × 16.5% = HK$33,000; net of tax = HK$167,000 - Total comprehensive income = 800,000 + 167,000 = HK$967,000

Workings for the deferred tax (journal entry form): - Dr Revaluation surplus (equity) 33,000 / Cr Deferred tax liability 33,000

Dividends declared (HK$120,000) and the share issue (HK$300,000) are transactions with owners in their capacity as owners — they are reported in the statement of changes in equity and are EXCLUDED from total comprehensive income.

解題思路（繁中）: Total comprehensive income 嘅定義係「期內 equity 嘅變動，撇除與擁有人（以其擁有人身份）嘅交易」。逐步拆：profit 800,000 照計；重估增值屬 OCI（will not be reclassified 類別），要扣 16.5% deferred tax → 200,000 × (1 − 0.165) = 167,000。Option A (1,000,000) 係唔記得扣稅；Option B (847,000) 係錯誤將股息 120,000 當 expense 扣減 — 股息係 distribution to owners，只入 SOCIE，唔入 SPLOCI；Option D (1,267,000) 係錯誤將發股 300,000 加埋入嚟 — 發股都係 owner transaction，唔影響 TCI。

Rule Check: HKAS 1 paras. 7, 81A, 106; HKAS 16 para. 39; HKAS 12 (deferred tax on revaluation)

Common Trap（繁中）: 最大陷阱係將 dividends 同 share issues 混入 comprehensive income — 呢兩樣永遠屬於 SOCIE 嘅「transactions with owners」部分。其次係 OCI 嘅稅務影響：HKAS 1 para. 90 要求披露每項 OCI 相關所得稅，重估增值要 net of tax 先入到 TCI。

Q5 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): Statement B is NOT correct. Under HKAS 1 para. 96, reclassification adjustments do NOT arise on changes in a revaluation surplus. A revaluation increase on property, plant and equipment is recognised in OCI and accumulated in equity as a revaluation surplus (HKAS 16 para. 39); it is NOT reclassified (recycled) to profit or loss in subsequent periods. When the asset is derecognised, the surplus may be transferred directly within equity (e.g. to retained earnings), not through profit or loss. Statements A, C and D are correct: OCI items are grouped by whether they may be reclassified subsequently to profit or loss (para. 82A); income tax relating to each OCI item must be disclosed in the statement or in the notes (para. 90); and OCI items may be presented net of tax or before tax with a single aggregate tax amount (para. 91).

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係 NOT-question，要搵出邊句唔啱。B 句嘅錯誤核心係「recycled to profit or loss」— revaluation surplus 係教科書級嘅「永不 recycle」項目，資產出售時只可以喺 equity 內部直接轉撥（例如轉去 retained earnings），唔經 P&L。A 對應 HKAS 1 para. 82A 嘅兩大分組（will not be reclassified vs may be reclassified）；C 對應 para. 90 嘅稅項披露要求；D 對應 para. 91 嘅 net-of-tax 或 before-tax 選擇。呢三句都係標準原文嘅正確表述。

Rule Check: HKAS 1 paras. 82A, 90, 91, 92, 96; HKAS 16 para. 39

Common Trap（繁中）: 考生最容易混淆「reclassification adjustments（recycling）」嘅適用範圍 — 記住口訣：「revaluation surplus 永不回收」。另外留意 double counting 概念：recycling 嘅目的係避免同一 gain 計兩次入 comprehensive income，而 revaluation surplus 因為根本唔入 P&L，所以唔存在呢個問題。
