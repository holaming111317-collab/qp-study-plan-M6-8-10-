M6 Chapter 16: Income Taxes — Phase 3 Quiz

Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. … — 超時如實申報

Question 1

Harbour View Limited (Harbour View) recognised a warranty provision of HK$360,000 in its statement of financial position as at 31 March 20X9. Under the Inland Revenue Ordinance, warranty expenditure is deductible for tax purposes only when it is paid. Warranty payments are expected to be made in full in future periods.

In accordance with HKAS 12 Income Taxes, what is the tax base of the warranty provision as at 31 March 20X9?

A. HK$360,000 B. HK$720,000 C. HK$180,000 D. Nil

Question 2

Kowloon Dynamics Limited (Kowloon Dynamics) reported an accounting profit before tax of HK$4,200,000 for the year ended 31 March 20X9. The profit was arrived at after including the following items:

  • Fines and penalties expense of HK$80,000 (not deductible for tax purposes);
  • Depreciation expense of HK$250,000;
  • Dividend income of HK$60,000 (not taxable under the Inland Revenue Ordinance).

A capital allowance deduction of HK$400,000 is available for tax purposes for the year. The profits tax rate is 16.5%.

What is the current tax expense of Kowloon Dynamics for the year ended 31 March 20X9?

A. HK$693,000 B. HK$681,450 C. HK$671,550 D. HK$803,550

Question 3

Beacon Trading Limited (Beacon) recognises interest income when it is earned under HKFRS. At 31 March 20X9, Beacon has an interest receivable of HK$120,000 recognised in its statement of financial position. For tax purposes, interest income is assessable only when the cash is received. The tax rate is 16.5%.

In accordance with HKAS 12, which of the following correctly describes the temporary difference arising and its deferred tax consequence as at 31 March 20X9?

A. A deductible temporary difference of HK$120,000, giving rise to a deferred tax asset of HK$19,800 B. A taxable temporary difference of HK$120,000, giving rise to a deferred tax liability of HK$19,800 C. No temporary difference arises because the interest income has already been accrued in the financial statements D. A taxable temporary difference of HK$120,000, giving rise to a deferred tax asset of HK$19,800

Question 4

On 1 April 20X8, Kestrel Engineering Limited (Kestrel) acquired a machine for HK$2,000,000. The machine has a useful life of four years and no residual value, and is depreciated on a straight-line basis. For tax purposes, a capital allowance deduction of HK$1,000,000 is available for the year ended 31 March 20X9. The tax rate is 16.5%. The deferred tax liability account had a nil balance at 1 April 20X8, and the machine is Kestrel's only depreciable asset.

Which of the following is the correct journal entry to record the deferred tax consequences of the machine for the year ended 31 March 20X9?

Date Description Debit HK$ Credit HK$
A 31 March 20X9 Income tax expense – deferred 82,500
    Deferred tax liability 82,500
B 31 March 20X9 Deferred tax liability 82,500
    Income tax expense – deferred 82,500
C 31 March 20X9 Income tax expense – deferred 500,000
    Deferred tax liability 500,000
D 31 March 20X9 Income tax expense – deferred 75,000
    Deferred tax liability 75,000

Question 5

Meridian Supplies Limited (Meridian) has the following balances in its statement of financial position as at 31 March 20X9:

Carrying amount HK$
Trade receivables (gross) 2,000,000
Allowance for expected credit losses (90,000)
Property, plant and equipment 3,000,000
Provision for warranties 140,000

Additional information:

  1. Credit losses are deductible for tax purposes only when the trade receivable is written off as credit impaired. The related sales revenue has already been taxed.
  2. Warranty costs are deductible for tax purposes only when incurred.
  3. The tax base of property, plant and equipment as at 31 March 20X9 is HK$2,400,000.
  4. The tax rate is 15%. It is probable that sufficient future taxable income will be available to utilise all deductible temporary differences.

In accordance with HKAS 12, what amounts should Meridian recognise as deferred tax asset and deferred tax liability as at 31 March 20X9?

A. Deferred tax asset HK$34,500; Deferred tax liability HK$90,000 B. Deferred tax asset HK$230,000; Deferred tax liability HK$600,000 C. Deferred tax asset HK$90,000; Deferred tax liability HK$34,500 D. Deferred tax asset HK$13,500; Deferred tax liability HK$90,000
