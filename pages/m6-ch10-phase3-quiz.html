M6 Chapter 10: Financial Assets, Financial Liabilities and Equity Instruments — Phase 3 Quiz

Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. … — 超時如實申報

Q1. Crescent Bay Limited issued 200,000 preference shares with a par value of HK$10 each. The shares carry a mandatory dividend of 7% of par value per annum, which the company cannot elect to withhold, and the shares are redeemable at par at a fixed date five years after issue. In accordance with HKAS 32, how should Crescent Bay Limited classify the preference shares in its statement of financial position?

A. As equity instruments, because they are issued in the legal form of shares B. As financial liabilities, because the company has a contractual obligation to deliver cash to the holders C. As compound instruments, with the dividend stream classified as a financial liability and the redemption amount classified as equity D. As financial assets, because the shares will generate future cash outflows

Q2. On 1 October 20X8, Harbour Stone Limited acquired 20,000 listed ordinary shares in Brightfield Limited at HK$12 per share, paying brokerage fees of HK$3,000. Harbour Stone Limited acquired the shares with the intention of actively trading them for short-term profits. In accordance with HKFRS 9, at what amount should the investment be initially measured on 1 October 20X8?

A. HK$237,000 B. HK$240,000 C. HK$243,000 D. HK$260,000

Q3. On 1 January 20X8, Kettlewell Limited purchased bonds with a face value of HK$2,000,000 for HK$1,920,000 (inclusive of directly attributable transaction costs). The bonds pay a contractual coupon of 5% per annum on face value, in arrears on 31 December each year, and are redeemable at face value on 31 December 20Y0. Kettlewell Limited holds the bonds within a business model whose objective is to collect contractual cash flows, and the cash flows are solely payments of principal and interest. The effective interest rate of the bonds is 6.5%. In accordance with HKFRS 9, what is the carrying amount of the bonds in Kettlewell Limited's statement of financial position as at 31 December 20X9?

A. HK$1,944,800 B. HK$1,971,212 C. HK$1,974,800 D. HK$2,044,800

Q4. Maple Grove Limited sells goods on 90-day credit terms. To improve its working capital, it enters into an agreement with a bank under which it transfers the contractual rights to the cash flows of a portfolio of trade receivables with a carrying amount of HK$800,000 to the bank for consideration of HK$760,000. The bank assumes the full risk of the receivables being uncollectible and has no recourse to Maple Grove Limited for any amounts not collected. Which set of journal entries correctly records this transaction in the books of Maple Grove Limited?

A. Dr Cash HK$760,000; Dr Loss on transfer (profit or loss) HK$40,000; Cr Trade receivables HK$800,000 B. Dr Cash HK$760,000; Cr Trade receivables HK$760,000 C. Dr Cash HK$760,000; Cr Loan from bank HK$760,000 D. Dr Cash HK$800,000; Cr Trade receivables HK$800,000

Q5. In accordance with HKFRS 9's general approach to impairment (the expected credit loss model), which of the following statements is correct?

A. In Stage 2, the loss allowance is measured at 12-month expected credit losses and interest revenue is calculated on the net carrying amount. B. In Stage 3, the loss allowance is measured at lifetime expected credit losses and interest revenue is calculated on the gross carrying amount. C. In Stage 1, the loss allowance is measured at 12-month expected credit losses and interest revenue is calculated on the gross carrying amount. D. A financial asset that is more than 30 days past due is always credit-impaired and must be classified in Stage 3.
