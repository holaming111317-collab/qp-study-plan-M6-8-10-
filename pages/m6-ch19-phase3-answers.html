M6 Chapter 19: Related Party Disclosures — Phase 3 Answers

Q1 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): C is correct. Under HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 11(a), two entities are not related parties simply because they have a director or other member of key management personnel in common. The finance director of Falcon Limited serving as a non-executive director of Merlin Limited is, on these facts alone, insufficient to create a related party relationship.

  • A is incorrect: Kestrel Limited is a fellow subsidiary of Falcon Limited — members of the same group are related to each other (para. 9(b)(i)).
  • B is incorrect: the spouse of the CEO is a close member of the family of a member of KMP (paras. 9(a)(iii) and 9 — close family always includes the spouse).
  • D is incorrect: Osprey Limited is a joint venture of Falcon Limited — one entity being a joint venture of the other creates a related party relationship (para. 9(b)(ii)).

解題思路（繁中）: 題目問邊個「唔係」related party。A 係 fellow subsidiary — 同一集團內自動 related。B 係 CEO 配偶 — close member of the family of KMP，必定 related。D 係公司嘅 joint venture — para. 9(b)(ii) 涵蓋。C 只有一位共同董事（common director），para. 11(a) 明確排除。留意：如果題目話該董事同時 control Merlin，答案就會唔同（咁會落入 para. 9(b)(vi)）。

Rule Check: HKAS 24 (Revised) paras. 9(a)(iii), 9(b)(i), 9(b)(ii) and 11(a)

Common Trap（繁中）: 學生常誤以為「共同董事 = related party」。HKAS 24 para. 11(a) 專登寫明淨係有共同 KMP 成員唔構成關係。另一個伏位：Osprey 嘅「另一個 joint venturer」先至唔係 related party — 但 JV 本身（Osprey）一定係。

Q2 — Answer: A

Model Answer (EN): A is correct. The wife of the chief financial officer is a close member of the family of a member of Marble Limited's key management personnel — close members of the family always include the person's spouse (HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 9), and the CFO is KMP (para. 9(a)(iii)).

  • B is incorrect: a supplier with whom the entity transacts a significant volume of business is not a related party merely by virtue of the resulting economic dependence (para. 11).
  • C is incorrect: a provider of finance acting in the ordinary course of business does not become a related party by reason of its lending arrangements alone.
  • D is incorrect: the sales manager has no authority or responsibility for planning, directing and controlling the activities of the entity, so he is not KMP and therefore not a related party.

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題測「person」層面嘅 related party 定義。B 同 C 係 para. 11 嘅經典排除 — 經濟依賴同純融資安排都唔構成關係。D 測 KMP 定義：sales manager 就算職位重要，只要冇 planning/directing/controlling 嘅權責就唔係 KMP（對比 LP 嘅 exam practice：sales staff 唔係 related parties）。A 係唯一符合 para. 9 嘅答案：CFO 係 KMP，配偶係 close family。

Rule Check: HKAS 24 (Revised) paras. 9, 9(a)(iii) and 11

Common Trap（繁中）: 見到「85% 供應量」、「主要客戶」、「主要銀行」呢類字眼，九成係 distractor — economic dependence 永遠唔係 related party 嘅理由。記住 KMP 係功能測試（authority and responsibility），唔係職銜測試。

Q3 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): B is correct. The related parties of Papaya Limited are: - Mango Limited — Mango is the parent of Papaya; members of the same group are related (para. 9(b)(i)); - Lychee Limited — Lychee is an associate of Papaya (significant influence) (para. 9(b)(ii)); - Rambutan Limited — Rambutan is a joint venture of Mango Limited, which is a member of the group of which Papaya is a member (para. 9(b)(ii) — an associate or joint venture of a member of a group of which the other entity is a member).

Durian Limited is NOT a related party of Papaya: Durian merely shares joint control of Rambutan with Mango — two joint venturers simply sharing joint control of a joint venture are not related parties (para. 11(b)), and there is no other relationship linking Durian to Papaya.

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係多步驟 group-structure 分析。第一步：Mango 係 parent（9(b)(i)）。第二步：Lychee 係 Papaya 嘅 associate（9(b)(ii)）。第三步係關鍵：Rambutan 雖然唔係 Papaya 直接持有，但佢係 Mango（Papaya 集團成員）嘅 JV — para. 9(b)(ii) 嘅延伸條款令佢成為 Papaya 嘅 related party。第四步：Durian 只係同 Mango 一齊控制 Rambutan — para. 11(b) 排除。A 漏咗 Rambutan；C 錯誤包括 Durian；D 漏晒 associate 同 JV。

Rule Check: HKAS 24 (Revised) paras. 9(b)(i), 9(b)(ii) and 11(b)

Common Trap（繁中）: 兩個伏位：(1) 漏咗 para. 9(b)(ii) 嘅「JV of a member of the group」延伸 — parent 嘅 JV 都係 subsidiary 嘅 related party；(2) 誤將 fellow joint venturer（Durian）當 related party — 「共同控制一個 JV」本身唔構成關係。

Q4 — Answer: D (HK$3,750,000)

Model Answer (EN): KMP compensation under HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 17 includes, in total and by category:

Category Item HK$
Short-term employee benefits Salaries 2,400,000 + bonuses 600,000 3,000,000
Post-employment benefits Employer's pension contributions 180,000
Termination benefits Termination payment to retiring director 250,000
Share-based payment Fair value of share options granted 320,000
Total KMP compensation 3,750,000
  • Salaries of sales staff (HK$1,900,000) are excluded — the sales staff are not KMP, so their remuneration is not KMP compensation.
  • Dividends on directors' personal shareholdings (HK$90,000) are excluded — dividends are returns on share ownership, not compensation for KMP services.

Distractor analysis: A (HK$3,430,000) omits the share-based payment; B (HK$3,840,000) wrongly includes the dividends; C (HK$5,650,000) wrongly includes the sales staff salaries.

解題思路（繁中）: 計數前先分類：邊啲係 KMP compensation。董事薪酬、花紅（short-term）、退休金供款（post-employment）、遣散費（termination）、購股權（share-based）全部計。Sales staff 唔係 KMP — 佢哋嘅人工與 KMP disclosure 無關。董事個人持股收到嘅股息係股東回報，唔係 compensation — 呢個係最巧妙嘅 distractor。加總：3,000,000 + 180,000 + 250,000 + 320,000 = 3,750,000。

Rule Check: HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 17

Common Trap（繁中）: 兩大伏位：(1) 將非 KMP 員工薪酬計入；(2) 將股息當成董事報酬 — 股息係投資回報，唔屬於 para. 17 五大類。記住口訣「短、退、長、散、股」，逐項歸類再加總。

Q5 — Answer: A

Model Answer (EN): A is correct. HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 18 requires disclosure of the amount of outstanding balances (including commitments), their terms and conditions (including whether secured and the nature of the consideration to be provided in settlement), details of guarantees given or received, provisions for doubtful debts related to the outstanding balances, and the bad/doubtful debt expense recognised in the period. It does not require disclosure of a hypothetical arm's length price for the balance — the standard requires disclosure of the actual terms and effects of related party transactions, not a reconstruction of market-equivalent pricing.

  • B, C and D are all explicitly required by para. 18.

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係 NOT-question，考 para. 18 嘅 minimum disclosure 清單。B（條款、有冇抵押、結算方式）、C（擔保）、D（壞賬撥備）全部係白紙黑字嘅要求。A 聽落好合理，但 HKAS 24 從來唔要求你披露「如果係市價會係幾多」— 準則只要求披露實際交易條款，等用家自己判斷影響。呢個設計係利用學生「related party 披露應該要話畀人知偏離市價幾多」嘅直覺做陷阱。

Rule Check: HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 18

Common Trap（繁中）: 唔好將「披露目的」（等用家知道交易可能唔係市價）同「披露要求」（披露實際條款）混為一談。考試見到 "arm's length equivalent price" / "fair value of the transaction" 呢類選項，通常唔係 HKAS 24 嘅披露要求。
