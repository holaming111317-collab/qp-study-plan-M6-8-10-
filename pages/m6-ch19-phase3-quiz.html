M6 Chapter 19: Related Party Disclosures — Phase 3 Quiz

Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. … — 超時如實申報

Question 1

In accordance with HKAS 24 (Revised) Related Party Disclosures, which of the following parties is NOT a related party of Falcon Limited, a Hong Kong trading company?

A. Kestrel Limited, a fellow subsidiary of Falcon Limited (both are wholly owned by the same parent company) B. Ms. Wong, the spouse of Falcon Limited's chief executive officer C. Merlin Limited, a company whose only connection with Falcon Limited is that the finance director of Falcon Limited also serves as a non-executive director of Merlin Limited D. Osprey Limited, a joint venture of Falcon Limited over which Falcon Limited shares joint control with an unrelated investor

Question 2

According to HKAS 24 (Revised), which of the following persons is a related party of Marble Limited, a Hong Kong property developer, in respect of its financial statements for the year ended 31 December 20X5?

A. The wife of Marble Limited's chief financial officer B. Granite Supplies Co., a supplier that provides 85% of Marble Limited's raw materials, creating significant economic dependence C. Harbour Bank, which provides Marble Limited's mortgage financing in the ordinary course of its banking business D. A sales manager of Marble Limited who is responsible for negotiating property sales contracts but has no authority over the planning, directing or controlling of the entity's activities

Question 3

Mango Limited owns 80% of the issued shares of Papaya Limited. Papaya Limited owns 30% of the issued shares of Lychee Limited and has significant influence over Lychee Limited. Mango Limited and Durian Limited each hold 50% of Rambutan Limited and share joint control of Rambutan Limited; Mango Limited and Durian Limited are otherwise unrelated.

In accordance with HKAS 24 (Revised), which of the following sets correctly identifies the related parties of Papaya Limited?

A. Mango Limited and Lychee Limited only B. Mango Limited, Lychee Limited and Rambutan Limited C. Mango Limited, Lychee Limited, Rambutan Limited and Durian Limited D. Mango Limited only

Question 4

The board of directors of Otter Limited comprises the only key management personnel of the company. For the year ended 31 December 20X6, the following amounts were paid or granted:

Item HK$
Directors' salaries 2,400,000
Directors' performance bonuses 600,000
Employer's pension contributions for directors 180,000
Termination payment to a retiring director 250,000
Fair value of share options granted to directors 320,000
Salaries of sales staff (not key management personnel) 1,900,000
Dividends paid to directors on their personal shareholdings 90,000

In accordance with HKAS 24 (Revised), what is the total key management personnel compensation that Otter Limited must disclose for the year ended 31 December 20X6?

A. HK$3,430,000 B. HK$3,840,000 C. HK$5,650,000 D. HK$3,750,000

Question 5

Beaver Limited has an outstanding loan balance due from its parent company at the year end. In accordance with HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 18, which of the following is NOT required to be disclosed in respect of this outstanding balance with a related party?

A. The price that would have been charged for an equivalent loan had it been negotiated with an unrelated lender on an arm's length basis B. The terms and conditions of the balance, including whether it is secured and the nature of the consideration to be provided in settlement C. Details of any guarantees given or received in respect of the balance D. Provisions for doubtful debts related to the amount of the outstanding balance
