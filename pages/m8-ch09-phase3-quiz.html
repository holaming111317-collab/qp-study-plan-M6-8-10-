M8 Chapter 9: Auditor's Reporting — Phase 3 Quiz

Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. … — 超時如實申報

Question 1

Harbour View Engineering Limited is a non-listed Hong Kong company. During the audit of its financial statements for the year ended 31 December 20X2, the auditor obtained sufficient appropriate audit evidence that the company had capitalised HK$3.8 million of routine repair and maintenance expenditure as property, plant and equipment, instead of charging it to profit or loss. The amount is material to the financial statements. Management refuses to adjust. The auditor is satisfied that the misstatement is confined to the property, plant and equipment balance and the related repairs expense, and does not affect the financial statements as a whole.

Which type of auditor's opinion is most appropriate?

A. Unmodified opinion with an Emphasis of Matter paragraph B. Qualified opinion C. Adverse opinion D. Disclaimer of opinion

Question 2

Starlight Trading Limited is a Hong Kong wholesaler. Three months before the audit commenced, a fire destroyed the company's server room and all accounting records supporting its cash sales, which represent approximately 70% of total revenue and the majority of the company's transactions. No backups exist, and the auditor is unable to perform alternative procedures to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence over these transactions. Management has cooperated fully but simply cannot reconstruct the records. The auditor concludes that the possible effects of undetected misstatements could be both material and pervasive to the financial statements.

Which type of auditor's opinion is most appropriate?

A. Qualified opinion, because management did not impose the limitation B. Adverse opinion, because the financial statements are likely to be materially misstated C. Disclaimer of opinion D. Unmodified opinion with an Other Matter paragraph describing the fire

Question 3

Which of the following matters would most appropriately be communicated in an Other Matter paragraph in the auditor's report?

A. A significant uncertainty surrounding a material accounting estimate that has been adequately disclosed in the notes to the financial statements B. The prior year's financial statements were audited by another firm of auditors C. A material uncertainty related to going concern that has been adequately disclosed in the financial statements D. The early adoption of a new HKFRS, which has been fully disclosed in the financial statements and has a pervasive effect on them

Question 4

Which of the following statements about Key Audit Matters (KAM) under HKSA 701 is correct?

A. KAMs must be communicated in the auditor's reports of all Hong Kong companies, whether listed or not B. The auditor provides a separate opinion on each KAM disclosed in the auditor's report C. A matter that gives rise to a qualified opinion must also be described in full as a separate KAM in the Key Audit Matters section D. KAMs are matters that, in the auditor's professional judgement, were of most significance in the audit of the current period financial statements, and no separate opinion is provided on them

Question 5

Ocean Pearl Restaurant Group Limited has recorded operating losses for two consecutive years and its bank loans of HK$25 million fall due for repayment four months after the date of the auditor's report. Management has prepared the financial statements on the going concern basis, believing that a refinancing plan currently under negotiation will succeed. The auditor has obtained sufficient appropriate audit evidence and concludes that (i) the use of the going concern basis of accounting is appropriate, (ii) a material uncertainty exists about the group's ability to continue as a going concern, and (iii) the financial statements adequately disclose this material uncertainty.

What is the appropriate reporting outcome?

A. Unmodified opinion, with a separate "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern" section in the auditor's report B. Unmodified opinion, with an Emphasis of Matter paragraph referring to the going concern uncertainty C. Qualified opinion, because a material uncertainty exists D. Adverse opinion, because the going concern basis of accounting has been used
