M8 Chapter 5: Sampling — Phase 3 Answers

Q1 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): The test is a test of control — it evaluates the operating effectiveness of the authorisation control (whether the purchasing director authorised invoices as required by policy), not the monetary amount of the transactions. Because each invoice either does or does not possess the attribute of "authorised", it is performed using attribute sampling, and the result is binary (correct / not correct). Since the focus is on the process rather than a figure in the financial statements, the test provides only indirect evidence over the financial statements.

解題思路（繁中）: - 關鍵字眼係「inspect for evidence of authorisation」— 測試目標係個 control（批核程序）有冇運作，而唔係張 invoice 嘅金額啱唔啱，所以係 test of control + attribute sampling。 - A 錯：佢冇驗證 purchases 嘅 valuation，monetary amount 喺 attribute sampling 入面係 irrelevant。 - C 錯：analytical procedures 係分析數據之間嘅 plausible relationships，同逐張 invoice 檢查簽名完全唔同。 - D 錯：呢個唔係 test of details of balances，亦同 completeness of trade payables 無關；test of details 會直接驗證財務報表金額。 - 考試伏位：existence vs completeness 方向感之外，M8 最常見陷阱係將「inspect documents for authorisation」誤當 substantive procedure —— 記住「check 簽名/批核 = test of control」。

Rule Check: HKSA 530 (attribute sampling for tests of control); HKSA 500 (substantive procedures = tests of details + substantive analytical procedures)

Common Trap（繁中）: 見到「inspect invoice」就以為係 substantive test。分辨方法：問自己「呢個測試係咪關心張單嘅銀碼？」— 唔關心銀碼、只關心控制有冇執行，就係 test of control，提供嘅係 indirect evidence。

Q2 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): Non-sampling risk is the risk that the auditor reaches an erroneous conclusion for any reason not related to sampling risk — for example, the application of inappropriate audit procedures, the failure to recognise a misstatement or deviation, or the misinterpretation of audit evidence. Applying an inappropriate audit procedure (option C) would produce an erroneous conclusion even if the entire population had been tested, so it is non-sampling risk.

解題思路（繁中）: - A 錯：sample deviation rate 低過 population 實際率 → 以為 controls 好過實際 —— 呢個係典型 sampling risk（影響 audit effectiveness）。 - B 錯：樣本太細、唔具代表性，係 sampling risk 嘅根本成因（解法：增加 sample size）。 - D 錯：以為 controls 差過實際 → 做多了 substantive work —— 都係 sampling risk（影響 audit efficiency）。 - C 啱：就算抽 100% 嚟驗，用錯程序都會出錯結論，所以同「抽樣」本身無關。 - Panelist 報告（Jun 2024）特別提到考生喺 sampling vs non-sampling risk 上表現差 —— 呢個係必考伏位。

Rule Check: HKSA 530 (definitions of sampling risk and non-sampling risk; two types of erroneous conclusion)

Common Trap（繁中）: 「assessing controls as effective when they are ineffective」聽落好似人為判斷錯誤，但其實 LP 明確歸類為 sampling risk（樣本偏差率唔等於人口偏差率所致）。只有「用錯程序／睇漏咗／解讀錯 evidence」先係 non-sampling risk。

Q3 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): Per HKSA 530, Appendix 3, the more the auditor relies on other substantive procedures (tests of details or substantive analytical procedures) directed at the same assertion to reduce detection risk to an acceptable level, the less assurance is required from sampling, and therefore the smaller the sample size can be. The other options all require a LARGER sample: higher assessed risk of material misstatement (A) and higher expected misstatement (B) both increase the sample size; a decrease in tolerable misstatement (D) also increases the sample size (the lower the tolerable misstatement, the larger the sample).

解題思路（繁中）: - 呢題考 HKSA 530 Appendix 3 嘅因素方向。記憶邏輯：「越驚有錯 → 抽越多」；「有其他證據幫手 / 條線放鬆 → 抽越少」。 - A 錯：RMM 上升 → detection risk 要更低 → sample size 增加。 - B 錯：expected misstatement 越高 → 要更大樣本先估得準實際錯誤 → 增加。 - D 錯：tolerable misstatement 下降（條線收緊）→ sample size 增加；注意係「decrease tolerable misstatement」，方向同你想要嘅相反。 - C 啱：其他 substantive procedures 針對同一 assertion 做多了 → sampling 需要提供嘅 assurance 減少 → sample size 下降。 - 考試伏位：number of sampling units in the population 對大 population 只有 negligible effect —— 考官好鐘意用呢個做 distractor（本題冇用，但記住）。

Rule Check: HKSA 530, Appendix 3 (factors influencing sample size for tests of details)

Common Trap（繁中）: 將「increase in tolerable misstatement」同「decrease in tolerable misstatement」方向調轉。記住：tolerable misstatement 越高（越寬鬆），sample size 越細。

Q4 — Answer: (a) HK$2,880,000; (b) No — the sample does not provide a reasonable basis

Model Answer (EN): (a) Projected misstatement = (misstatement in sample ÷ sample value) × population value = (HK$360,000 ÷ HK$6,000,000) × HK$48,000,000 = 6% × HK$48,000,000 = HK$2,880,000 (overstatement).

(b) Under HKSA 530, the auditor's best estimate of misstatement in the population is the projected misstatement plus the anomalous misstatement (here HK$2,880,000 + nil = HK$2,880,000). Since this total exceeds tolerable misstatement of HK$2,500,000, the sample does NOT provide a reasonable basis for conclusions about the population.

Appropriate course of action (any ONE): - Request management to investigate the identified misstatements and the potential for further misstatements, and make any necessary adjustments; or - Tailor the nature, timing and extent of additional audit procedures to achieve the required assurance — e.g. increase the sample size, or modify related substantive procedures.

解題思路（繁中）: - 投影公式係「sample 錯誤率 × 成個 population 價值」。先計錯誤率：360,000/6,000,000 = 6%，再乘 48,000,000 = 2,880,000。 - (b) 嘅比較基準係 tolerable misstatement（唔係 overall materiality）— 記住 tolerable misstatement 係 performance materiality 應用喺個別抽樣程序上（HKSA 320 para. 9）。 - 因為冇 anomalous error，best estimate 就係 projected misstatement 本身。2.88m > 2.5m → 個 sample 唔能提供 reasonable basis。 - 之後嘅行動有兩大路線：叫管理層查兼改數，或者加做程序（加大 sample / 改 substantive procedures）。答一個已夠，但兩個都識先穩陣。 - 伏位：唔好攞 HK$360,000 直接同 tolerable misstatement 比 —— 未投影嘅 sample error 唔係結論基礎。

Rule Check: HKSA 530.14 (projection of misstatements); HKSA 530.15(a),(b); HKSA 530.A22 (projected + anomalous vs tolerable misstatement); HKSA 530.A23 (responses when no reasonable basis); HKSA 320 para. 9 (performance materiality)

Common Trap（繁中）: 忘記投影，直接話「360,000 < 2,500,000 所以冇問題」— 呢個係 zero-mark 級錯誤。另一个伏位：以為有 anomalous error 就可以唔投影 —— 本題講明冇 anomalous error，所以全部要投影。

Q5 — Answer: (a) Sample deviation rate 4%; upper deviation rate 7%; (b) Yes — reliance can be placed on the control

Model Answer (EN): (a) Sample deviation rate = 8 ÷ 200 × 100% = 4%. Upper deviation rate = sample deviation rate + sampling risk = 4% + 3% = 7%.

(b) The upper deviation rate of 7% is below the tolerable rate of deviation of 8%. Therefore, the auditor can place reliance on the control over the authorisation of purchase invoices over HK$20,000. (Note: the auditor cannot conclude the sample is sufficient merely because the 4% sample deviation rate is below the tolerable rate of 8% — the sampling risk allowance must be added first.)

解題思路（繁中）: - Attribute sampling 嘅結果係 binary：authorized / not authorized，所以 8/200 = 4% 就係個 rate。 - 關鍵一步：一定要將 sampling risk (3%) 加落 sample deviation rate，得出 upper deviation rate 7%，先可以同 tolerable rate of deviation (8%) 比較。 - 7% ≤ 8% → 可以 rely 個 control。 - 伏位 1：直接攞 4% 同 8% 比就落結論 —— LP 明確話「the auditor cannot conclude that the sample is sufficient」除非加埋 sampling risk。 - 伏位 2：confidence level + sampling risk = 100%，所以 3% sampling risk 即 97% confidence level（本題冇問，但要識）。 - 伏位 3：如果 upper deviation rate 超過 tolerable rate，唔可以 ignore deviations —— 要增加 sample size、測試 alternative control 或修改 substantive procedures。

Rule Check: HKSA 530 (attribute sampling; tolerable rate of deviation); upper deviation rate per LP Illustrative Example 9 methodology (population/sample error rate + sampling risk)

Common Trap（繁中）: 最衰格嘅錯係見 4% < 8% 就即刻寫「rely on the control」。考試一定要寫出 upper deviation rate 嘅計算同埋比較，先攞到 full marks。仲有：tolerable rate of deviation 係 test of control 專用，tolerable misstatement 先係 test of details 用 —— 唔好撈亂。
