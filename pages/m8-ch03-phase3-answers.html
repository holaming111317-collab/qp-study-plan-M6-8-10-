M8 Chapter 3: Audit Planning I — Phase 3 Answers

Q1 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): C. Detection risk is the only component of audit risk that is under the auditor's control. Audit risk = risk of material misstatement (inherent risk × control risk) × detection risk. Inherent risk and control risk exist in the entity before the audit starts — the auditor can only assess them, not change them. The auditor controls detection risk by adjusting the nature, timing and extent of audit procedures: the higher the assessed risk of material misstatement, the lower the detection risk that can be accepted, hence more persuasive/more extensive substantive procedures.

解題思路（繁中）: 題目問「邊個 risk 係 auditor 直接控制到」——答案一定係 detection risk。Option A 錯：inherent risk 係客戶業務天生嘅風險（例如複雜 estimate），auditor 無權叫管理層簡化交易。Option B 錯：內控係管理層嘅責任，auditor 只能 assess 同測試，唔可以「direct」客戶點做。Option D 錯：business risk 係公司層面嘅風險（HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para. 12(b)），auditor 唔係管理顧問。記住 inverse relationship：control risk 低 → substantive procedures 可以少；RoMM 高 → detection risk 要壓低（做更多 testing）。

Rule Check: HKSA 200.13(e) (definition of detection risk); Glossary of Terms (audit risk definition); HKSA 300.

Common Trap（繁中）: 考生常答 B（control risk），以為 auditor 測內控即係控制到佢——auditor 測試內控只係「評估」control risk，唔係改變佢。另一伏位：將 audit risk 定義成「FS 有 misstatement 嘅風險」——嗰個係 RoMM，唔係 audit risk。

Q2 — Answer: A

Model Answer (EN): A. The commission scheme tied to a revenue target and the bank's requirement for revenue growth create an incentive/pressure for fraudulent financial reporting — there is a risk that revenue is overstated (e.g. accelerating revenue recognition of next year's sales into the current year). The appropriate planned response is detailed cut-off procedures over revenue recognition to ensure revenue is recorded in the correct accounting period. This is a fraud risk factor (pressure to meet targets), and the team must maintain professional scepticism over revenue.

解題思路（繁中）: 題眼係「bonus 掛鈎 revenue target」＋「銀行要睇業績先批貸款」——兩個都係 HKSA 240 嘅 fraud risk factors（incentive/pressure），直接指向 revenue overstatement，對應 assertion 係 occurrence/cut-off。Option B 錯：佣金制度係增加風險而唔係保證準確，絕對唔可以因此減少 substantive procedures。Option C 錯：rely on controls 必須先做 tests of control 證明 operating effectiveness，而且高 fraud risk 區域通常唔適合全靠內控。Option D 錯：fraud risk factor ≠ 已發生 fraud（LP 明講「the existence of fraud risk factors may not necessarily indicate the existence of fraud」），唔可以未審就斷定 fraud 兼即時 qualify。

Rule Check: HKSA 240 (fraud risk factors — incentive/pressure); HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) (risk assessment and responses); LP §3.5.2, Exhibit 3.3 (bonus structure → cut-off procedures on revenue).

Common Trap（繁中）: 見到「bonus / loan / 業績壓力」要即刻聯想到 revenue overstatement（唔係 understatement）同 cut-off。相反，經濟差嘅時候管理層反而可能 understate 利潤（maximise write-offs）——方向由 pressure 嘅性質決定。

Q3 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): C. The financial controller's ongoing review of whether bank reconciliations are being prepared on time and continue to operate effectively is the entity's process to monitor the system of internal control. Monitoring relates to the entity's process of assessing the effectiveness of controls over time and taking remedial action where necessary. (The preparation of the bank reconciliation itself is a control activity — a reconciliation; but reviewing whether the control continues to operate over time is monitoring.)

解題思路（繁中）: 分辨關鍵：「做 reconciliation」＝ control activity（HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para. 15 — reconciliations）；「check 啲 reconciliation 有冇持續做、有冇效」＝ monitoring。題目問嘅係後者（review ... over time）。Option A 錯：control environment 係 tone at the top（integrity、ethical values、governance oversight），唔係具體 review 動作。Option B 錯：risk assessment process 係管理層識別同管理 business risks 嘅流程，唔係覆核內控運作。Option D 係最強 distractor——因為 bank reconciliation 本身確係 control activity，但題目問嘅係「監察個 control」嘅層次。讀題要睇主語同動詞。

Rule Check: HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para. 12(m) (five components); para. 15 (control activities); LP §3.3.1.3 (monitoring — e.g. timeliness/accuracy of bank reconciliations).

Common Trap（繁中）: 五大 components 嘅 classification 題係 panelist 報告中嘅常見失分位。口訣：integrity/ethics → control environment；「review controls over time / 跟進 deficiency」→ monitoring；authorisation / reconciliation / verification / physical & logical / segregation of duties → control activities。

Q4 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): B. Under HKSA 240, the primary responsibility for the prevention and detection of fraud rests with management and those charged with governance. The auditor is responsible for obtaining reasonable assurance that the financial statements are free from material misstatement, whether caused by fraud or error. Where fraud is identified, the auditor must communicate it on a timely basis to an appropriate level of management; where the fraud involves management (here, a senior finance manager), the matter must be communicated to those charged with governance. If the auditor has doubts about the integrity of those charged with governance, legal advice should be sought. Reporting to third parties (e.g. Police, ICAC) is required only where law or regulation imposes such a duty, and that duty takes precedence over confidentiality.

解題思路（繁中）: 三步判斷：(1) 責任歸屬——prevent/detect fraud 永遠係 management + TCWG，所以 A 錯；(2) 上報路線——fraud 涉及 management 就上 TCWG（B 啱）；涉及 TCWG 誠信有疑問就尋求法律意見；(3) 對外披露——只有法律/法規要求先會報第三方（ICAC、Police、SFC 等），唔係「所有情況都必報警」，所以 C 錯。Option D 錯得離譜：fraud 對 FS 有重大影響而管理層唔處理，auditor 要考慮 modify opinion，絕對唔可以「收埋」出 unmodified opinion；legal duty 亦 override confidentiality。

Rule Check: HKSA 240.10 (auditor's objectives); LP §3.5 (responsibility rests with management and TCWG; communication and third-party reporting).

Common Trap（繁中）: 最常見錯係 A——以為「auditor 提供 reasonable assurance」等於「auditor 負責捉 fraud」。記住：auditor 嘅責任係 identify and assess RoMM due to fraud 同 respond，唔係 prevent/detect。另一陷阱：confidentiality 唔係絕對——法律責任優先，呢種情況 LP 建議 seek legal advice。

Q5 — Answer: (i) Source documents — goods despatched notes (or customer orders); (ii) trace from the source documents FORWARD into the accounting records (sales day book/general ledger)

Model Answer (EN): To test completeness of revenue, the auditor selects a sample from source documents — goods despatched notes (or customer orders) — and traces them forward to sales invoices and their inclusion in the sales day book/general ledger (i.e. from outside the accounting system towards the accounting records). Vouching a sample of invoices from the sales day book back to goods despatched notes starts from what is already recorded; it can confirm occurrence/accuracy of recorded sales, but can never reveal despatches that were never invoiced or recorded — hence it tests for overstatement, not understatement (completeness). Under directional testing, income and liabilities are tested for understatement by tracing from source documents to the records.

解題思路（繁中）: Directional testing 嘅核心邏輯：「起點決定 assertion」。 - 由 records → source documents（vouching，由帳查出去）＝ 證明「記低咗嘅嘢真係存在/發生」→ existence / occurrence（捉 overstatement）。 - 由 source documents → records（tracing，由文件追入帳）＝ 證明「發生咗嘅嘢全部有記低」→ completeness（捉 understatement）。 所以 completeness of revenue 一定要由 goods despatched notes / customer orders 出發追入 sales day book。由 sales day book 抽 invoice 向外 vouch，樣本本身就只包含「已記錄」嘅交易，邏輯上永遠搵唔到「漏咗冇記」嘅交易。Assets/expenses 測 overstatement；liabilities/income 測 understatement——呢句背熟就唔會錯。

Rule Check: HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para. A190(a) (completeness assertion); LP §3.1.4.7 (directional testing) and Exhibit 3.8 (Revenue — Completeness: agree customer orders with despatch notes and sales invoices through to inclusion in the sales day book).

Common Trap（繁中）: 考官最愛嘅 direction-of-testing 陷阱（Jun 2023 報告特別提到考生混淆 existence vs completeness direction）。仲有 observation vs inspection 都係混淆高危：observation 係「睇人做嘢個過程」，inspection 係「睇文件/實物」——唔好亂用。
