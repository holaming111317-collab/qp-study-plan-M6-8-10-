M6 Chapter 8: Borrowing Costs — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives

  1. Explain the criteria for capitalisation of borrowing costs under HKAS 23, including the definition of a qualifying asset. (LO 2.08.01)
  2. Determine the commencement date, suspension and cessation of the capitalisation period.
  3. Calculate the borrowing costs eligible for capitalisation for specific borrowings (actual costs less investment income) and for general borrowings (capitalisation rate × expenditures). (LO 2.08.02)
  4. Prepare journal entries to record capitalised and expensed borrowing costs.

必背考點

A. Recognition criteria（邊啲 borrowing costs 要資本化？）

規則 內容 Citation
Core principle Borrowing costs that are directly attributable to the acquisition, construction or production of a qualifying asset form part of the cost of that asset. All other borrowing costs are recognised as an expense in the period incurred. HKAS 23.1, HKAS 23.8
Directly attributable Borrowing costs that would have been avoided if the expenditure on the qualifying asset had not been made. HKAS 23.10
Qualifying asset An asset that necessarily takes a substantial period of time to get ready for its intended use or sale. May include: inventories, manufacturing plants, power generation facilities, intangible assets, investment properties. HKAS 23.5, HKAS 23.7
NOT qualifying assets Financial assets; inventories manufactured or otherwise produced over a short time period / in large quantities on a repetitive basis; assets ready for their intended use or sale when acquired. HKAS 23.7, HKAS 23.4(b)
Scope exclusion The cost of equity (actual or imputed) is not within the scope of HKAS 23 — only debt-related costs (bank loans, overdrafts, bonds). HKAS 23.1
Borrowing costs defined Interest and other costs an entity incurs in connection with the borrowing of funds. HKAS 23.5

B. Measurement rules（計幾多？）

B1. Capitalisation period（資本化期間）

階段 規則 Citation
Commencement Begin capitalising on the commencement date — the date when the entity first meets ALL THREE conditions: (1) it incurs expenditures for the asset (cash paid, other assets transferred, or interest-bearing liabilities assumed); (2) it incurs borrowing costs; (3) it undertakes activities necessary to prepare the asset for its intended use or sale. Activities include technical/administrative work prior to physical construction (e.g. obtaining permits), but NOT holding the asset with no development (e.g. land-banking). HKAS 23.17
Suspension Suspend capitalisation during extended periods in which active development of the qualifying asset is suspended (e.g. construction put on hold due to an economic downturn). HKAS 23.20
Cessation Cease capitalisation when substantially all the activities necessary to prepare the asset for its intended use or sale are complete — normally when physical construction is complete, even if routine administrative work or minor modifications remain. HKAS 23.22

B2. Specific borrowings（專項借款）— HKAS 23.12 - Eligible amount = actual borrowing costs incurred on that borrowing during the period LESS any investment income on the temporary investment of those borrowings. - Investment income on surplus funds is NOT revenue — it is netted off against borrowing costs capitalised. - 計法：Loan principal × interest rate × (months in capitalisation period / 12) − investment income

B3. General borrowings（一般借款）— HKAS 23.14 - Eligible amount = capitalisation rate × expenditures on the qualifying asset（按支出加權、按時間比例 pro-rate）。 - Capitalisation rate = weighted average of borrowing costs applicable to borrowings outstanding during the period, excluding borrowings made specifically for a qualifying asset. - 上限規則： borrowing costs capitalised in a period cannot exceed the total borrowing costs incurred during that period. (HKAS 23.14)

B4. Key journal entries（Dr/Cr 必須平衡）

Case 1 — Specific borrowing, fully capitalised（如 Chee Entertainment 模式）：

Dr  Qualifying asset (e.g. Games centre)      1,160,000
    Cr  Cash                                                  1,160,000
(Recognition of capitalised borrowing costs)

Case 2 — Specific borrowing, part capitalised / part expensed（如 Beeston Biotech 模式，commencement date 遲過 drawdown date）：

Dr  R&D facility                             18,643,750
Dr  Interest expense (P/L)                    3,107,092
    Cr  Cash                                                 21,751,042
(Capitalise 6 months from commencement date; expense the 1 month before commencement)

Case 3 — General borrowings（如 Marshall Green Energy 模式）：

Dr  Solar power plant                           642,155
Dr  Interest expense (P/L)                    7,952,845
    Cr  Cash                                                  8,595,000
(Capitalise expenditures × capitalisation rate × time apportionment; expense the remainder)

C. Disclosure requirements

  • 本章 LP 聚焦 recognition 與 measurement；HKAS 23 要求披露 the amount of borrowing costs capitalised during the period 及 the capitalisation rate used to determine eligible borrowing costs。
  • ⚠️ 不確定提示：以上 disclosure 要點為 HKAS 23 標準正文內容（para. 26），LP 本章正文未列出具體段落號，考試如問 disclosure 以「amount capitalised + capitalisation rate」兩點作答。

易混淆概念對比

Table 1: Specific borrowings vs General borrowings

Specific borrowings General borrowings
Meaning Funds borrowed specifically to obtain a particular qualifying asset Funds borrowed generally, part of which is used on the qualifying asset
Eligible amount Actual borrowing costs incurred on that borrowing, less investment income on temporary investment of surplus funds Capitalisation rate × expenditures on the asset (time-apportioned)
Rate used Actual rate on that specific loan Weighted average of all general borrowings outstanding (exclude specific borrowings)
Investment income Deducted from capitalisable costs (NOT recognised as revenue) Not relevant — income on general funds is normal investment income
點樣分辨 題目話「borrowed to construct/finance project X」→ specific；話「drew on existing borrowings / general pool of funds」→ general。考試伏位：specific borrowing 嘅 investment income 唔可以入 revenue，要扣減資本化金額（Jun 2024 panelist 話呢度成日錯）。 考試伏位：記住 capitalisation rate 唔好用 simple average，要用 weighted average；仲要按月份 pro-rate。

Table 2: Commencement vs Suspension vs Cessation of capitalisation

Commencement Suspension Cessation
Trigger ALL THREE met: expenditures incurred + borrowing costs incurred + preparation activities underway Extended periods of suspended active development Substantially all activities to prepare the asset for use/sale are complete
Typical example Loan drawn, first payment made, construction/permit work started Construction on hold due to economic downturn Physical construction complete; only routine admin work or minor modifications left
Borrowing costs in period Capitalise from this date onwards Expense during suspension period Expense after this point
考試伏位 三個條件齊先有得開始，用最遲嗰個 date（e.g. 借錢 1 June 但 1 July 先找數兼動工 → 1 July）。Technical/admin work（拎 permits）計 activity，但 land-banking 唔計。 「Extended period」先 suspend；短暫停工（如季節性 delay 係工程必要一部分）唔使 suspend。 唔好等到甩埋所有 paperwork 先停——physical construction complete 就 cease。

Question

State the core principle of HKAS 23 regarding recognition of borrowing costs.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話：借錢起嘢，利息就跟埋個資產成本；借錢做一般生意，利息就即刻 expense。發股集資嘅成本 HKAS 23 完全唔理。

Flashcard 3 / 共 8 張
Question

What are the THREE conditions that must ALL be met before capitalisation of borrowing costs can commence?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
(HKAS 23.17) 1. The entity incurs expenditures for the asset (cash paid / other assets transferred / interest-bearing liabilities assumed); 2. The entity incurs borrowing costs; and 3. The entity undertakes activities necessary to prepare the asset for its intended use or sale (includes pre-construction technical/administrative work like permits; excludes land-banking).

💬 白話：三個條件「晒冷」先可以開始計，所以 commencement date = 三樣嘢入面最遲發生嗰日。攞住塊地乜都唔做（land-banking）唔算 activities。

Flashcard 4 / 共 8 張
Question

When must capitalisation of borrowing costs be (a) suspended and (b) ceased?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話：suspend 係「暫停」——經濟差停工一年嗰啲；cease 係「收工」——樓起好咗就停，唔好等埋啲小執小修。暫停/停止期間嘅利息全部入 P/L。
