M6 Chapter 11: Impairment of Assets — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives

  1. Identify common external and internal indicators of impairment and determine when an impairment test must be performed under HKAS 36.
  2. Calculate the recoverable amount of an asset or CGU as the higher of fair value less costs of disposal (FVLCD) and value in use (VIU).
  3. Account for impairment losses for individual assets (cost model and revaluation model) and allocate impairment losses within a cash-generating unit (CGU), including goodwill.
  4. Account for the reversal of impairment losses, applying the ceiling in HKAS 36.117, and explain why goodwill impairment losses are never reversed.
  5. Prepare the disclosures required by HKAS 36 for impairment losses and reversals.

必背考點

1. Recognition — 何時要做減值測試、減值點樣入賬

  • Scope exclusions (HKAS 36.2): HKAS 36 唔涵蓋 inventories、deferred tax assets、investment property measured at fair value、biological assets within the scope of HKAS 41。考試見到呢啲資產，唔好用 HKAS 36。
  • Assessment each period (HKAS 36.9): At the end of each reporting period, assess whether there is any indication that an asset may be impaired. 一般資產只有喺有 indication 先要做 full impairment test。
  • Mandatory annual testing (HKAS 36.10) — 三類資產每年必測，無論有冇 indication: 1. Intangible assets with an indefinite useful life (HKAS 36.10(a)); 2. Intangible assets not yet available for use (HKAS 36.10(b)); 3. Goodwill acquired in a business combination (tested via the CGU to which it is allocated).
  • Indications of impairment (HKAS 36.12):
  • External: market capitalisation below carrying amount of net assets; adverse technological/market/economic/legal changes; new competitors; unfavourable FX movements; changes in legislation.
  • Internal: obsolescence or physical damage; asset becoming idle or plans to discontinue/restructure/dispose early; reassessing useful life from indefinite to finite; declining sales/net profit; loss of significant customers; actual cash flows significantly worse than budgeted.
  • 伏位：資產損耗比預期快（例如飛機喺嚴寒航線老化快啲）唔係 impairment indicator — 呢個係要 review depreciation（LP Illustrative Example 1）。
  • Impairment exists when carrying amount > recoverable amount (HKAS 36.59): the carrying amount is reduced to the recoverable amount; the reduction is the impairment loss.
  • Recognition — cost model (HKAS 36.60): impairment loss recognised immediately in profit or loss.
  • Recognition — revaluation model (HKAS 36.60–61): treated as a revaluation decrease — recognised in OCI against the revaluation surplus for that same asset, to the extent of the surplus; any excess goes to profit or loss.
  • Cannot reduce below zero (HKAS 36.62): an asset is written down to nil at most, unless a liability is required to be recognised by another standard.
  • Post-impairment depreciation (HKAS 36.63): future depreciation/amortisation is adjusted to allocate the revised carrying amount (less residual value) over the remaining useful life.
  • Reversal recognition (HKAS 36.119): reversal recognised immediately in profit or loss, unless the asset is carried at a revalued amount, in which case it is an increase in the revaluation surplus in OCI (any amount previously taken to P&L is reversed through P&L first).
  • Goodwill reversal prohibited (HKAS 36.124): impairment losses recognised for goodwill cannot be reversed in subsequent periods (any increase is likely internally generated goodwill, which HKAS 38 prohibits recognising).

2. Measurement — Recoverable Amount 點計

  • Recoverable amount (HKAS 36.6) = the HIGHER of:
  • Fair value less costs of disposal (FVLCD) — market-based measure, fair value per HKFRS 13. Costs of disposal = incremental costs directly attributable to disposal, excluding finance costs and income tax expense (HKAS 36.6); e.g. legal costs, stamp duty, dismantling/removal costs.
  • Value in use (VIU) — entity-specific measure: the present value of future cash flows expected from continuing use and ultimate disposal.
  • VIU 兩大步驟: (1) estimate future cash inflows/outflows from continuing use and ultimate disposal; (2) discount them to present value (HKAS 36.30–32). Cash flow projections must be based on reasonable and supportable assumptions (management's best estimate).
  • Discount rate (HKAS 36.55–56): a pre-tax rate reflecting current market assessments of the time value of money and the risks specific to the asset (e.g. WACC as surrogate). 考試伏位：唔好用 post-tax rate 配 pre-tax cash flows 之餘仲要記住 rate 係 pre-tax。
  • Indefinite-life intangible shortcut (HKAS 36.24): may use the most recent detailed recoverable amount calculation if (i) the CGU composition has not changed significantly, (ii) the last calculation exceeded carrying amount by a substantial margin, and (iii) the likelihood that current RA < CA is remote.
  • CGU (HKAS 36.6, 36.66): the smallest identifiable group of assets that generates cash inflows largely independent of the cash inflows from other assets or groups of assets. Test individual asset first; only if its RA cannot be determined do you test the CGU. If an active market exists for the output of an asset, treat the asset's operation as a separate CGU and use market prices (not internal transfer prices) for VIU.
  • CGU carrying amount consistency (HKAS 36.76–79): include only assets directly attributable or allocable on a reasonable and consistent basis; exclude recognised liabilities unless the CGU's RA cannot be determined without considering the liability (integrated, sold together).
  • Allocation of CGU impairment loss (HKAS 36.104): 1. First, reduce the carrying amount of any goodwill allocated to the CGU; 2. Then, reduce the other assets pro rata to their carrying amounts; 3. Floor (HKAS 36.105): no asset is reduced below the highest of its FVLCD (if measurable), its VIU (if determinable), and zero. Any unallocated excess goes pro rata to the other assets.
  • Corporate assets (HKAS 36.100–102(a)): e.g. head office building — do not generate independent cash inflows; if allocable on a reasonable and consistent basis, allocate a portion to each CGU and compare (CGU carrying amount + allocated portion) with RA.
  • Goodwill (HKAS 36.81): on acquisition, allocate to each CGU (or group of CGUs) expected to benefit from the synergies. Testing: annually at the same time each year (different CGUs may test at different times) and whenever an indicator exists (HKAS 36.90, 36.96); CGUs receiving goodwill in the current period must be tested before the end of the current reporting period (HKAS 36.96); if an asset within the CGU is tested at the same time, test the asset first (HKAS 36.97).
  • Reversal ceiling (HKAS 36.117): the increased carrying amount must not exceed the carrying amount that would have been determined (net of depreciation/amortisation) had no impairment loss been recognised in prior years. For a CGU, the reversal is allocated pro rata to assets other than goodwill, subject to the same per-asset ceiling (HKAS 36.122–123).

3. Disclosure (HKAS 36.126, 36.130)

  • By class of assets (HKAS 36.126):
  • Impairment losses recognised in P&L and the line item(s) in which they are included;
  • Impairment losses on revalued assets recognised in OCI;
  • Same two disclosures for reversals.
  • For each material impairment loss or reversal (HKAS 36.130):
  • The amount; the events and circumstances that led to recognition/reversal; the nature of the asset or description of the CGU;
  • The recoverable amount and whether it is FVLCD or VIU;
  • If FVLCD: the level of the HKFRS 13 fair value hierarchy; if Level 2 or 3 — valuation technique(s), any change in technique and reasons, key assumptions, and discount rate(s) if a present value technique is used;
  • If VIU: the discount rate(s) used in the current and previous estimate.

Individual Asset Cash-Generating Unit (CGU)
When used Default level — test the asset alone if its recoverable amount can be determined Only when the asset does not generate largely independent cash inflows and its RA cannot be determined individually (HKAS 36.66)
Unit definition The single asset Smallest identifiable group of assets generating cash inflows largely independent of other assets (HKAS 36.6)

Cost Model Revaluation Model

Question

撳卡翻面 👆

(HKAS 36.10)

Flashcard 3 / 共 8 張
Question

Give two external and two internal indicators of impairment under HKAS 36.12.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 分辨技巧：external = 企業控制唔到嘅大環境（市場、法例、競爭對手、匯率）；internal = 公司內部營運嘅事（資產閒置、業績差過預算、提早出售計劃）。注意伏位：資產折舊快過預期 ≠ impairment indicator，係要 review depreciation。

Flashcard 4 / 共 8 張
Question

What are the two steps in estimating value in use, and what type of discount rate must be used?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 VIU 係 entity-specific（用自己 budget/forecast），FVLCD 係 market-based（用 HKFRS 13）。考試俾你做 PV 計數時，discount rate 題目會俾；概念題就考你「pre-tax」呢個字。
