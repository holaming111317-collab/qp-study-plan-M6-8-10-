M6 Chapter 6: Investment Property — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives

  1. Define investment property and distinguish it from owner-occupied property and inventory under HKAS 40.
  2. Apply the recognition criteria and determine the elements of cost for owned investment property (HKAS 40.16–40.23).
  3. Account for investment property after initial recognition under the cost model and the fair value model (HKAS 40.30–40.35).
  4. Account for transfers between investment property and other asset categories upon a change in use (HKAS 40.57–40.64).
  5. Prepare the disclosures required by HKAS 40 for investment property (HKAS 40.75–40.79).

必背考點

A. Recognition（確認）

考點 規定 出處
Definition of investment property Property (land or a building – or part of a building – or both) held (by the owner or by the lessee as a right-of-use asset) to earn rentals or for capital appreciation or both, rather than for (a) use in production/supply of goods or services or for administrative purposes; or (b) sale in the ordinary course of business. Generates cash flows largely independently of other assets. HKAS 40.5（定義）；例子見 HKAS 40.8
Examples of investment property (a) land held for long-term capital appreciation; (b) land held for a currently undetermined future use (regarded as held for capital appreciation); (c) a building leased out under one or more operating leases; (d) a vacant building held to be leased out under operating leases; (e) property being constructed or developed for future use as investment property HKAS 40.8(a)–(e)
NOT investment property Owner-occupied property (incl. property occupied by employees even if they pay market rent; property held for future use as owner-occupied property; owner-occupied property awaiting disposal) → HKAS 16 / HKFRS 16；Property held for sale in the ordinary course of business or in construction/development for such sale → HKAS 2 Inventories HKAS 40.9(a),(c)
Recognition criteria (owned IP) Recognised as an asset when, and only when: (1) it is probable that future economic benefits will flow to the entity; AND (2) cost can be measured reliably. Same criteria as HKAS 16 PPE; apply to both initial and subsequent costs. HKAS 40.16–40.17
Subsequent costs — component approach Costs to replace a component (e.g. lifts, fittings) are capitalised if the recognition criteria are met; the carrying amount of the replaced part is derecognised. Unit of account = the part, regardless of the life of the whole property. HKAS 40.19
Day-to-day servicing Repairs and maintenance costs are expensed when incurred — never capitalised. HKAS 40（與 HKAS 16 一致）
Leased IP (right-of-use asset) Recognition and initial measurement follow HKFRS 16 Leases (not the HKAS 40.16 criteria); subsequent measurement follows the entity's HKAS 40 model. If leased out under a finance lease → HKFRS 16 (interest = lease receivable). Exhibit 6.1

B. Measurement（計量）

Initial measurement (owned IP) — at cost (HKAS 40.18, 40.20–40.21)

  • Include: purchase price + directly attributable expenditure (transaction costs such as legal fees, property transfer taxes; e.g. architect's fees, construction costs, cost of removing derelict buildings on site, regulatory approval costs).
  • Exclude (HKAS 40.23): start-up costs (unless necessary to bring the property to intended operating condition, e.g. advertising newly built rental accommodation); operating losses incurred before planned occupancy level; abnormal amounts of wasted material, labour and other resources (HKAS 40.23(c) — e.g. typhoon damage repairs during construction → expense to profit or loss).

Subsequent measurement — one model chosen for ALL investment properties (HKAS 40.30, 40.33)

Cost model Fair value model
Carrying amount Cost − accumulated depreciation − accumulated impairment losses (HKAS 16 cost model; impairment per HKAS 36) Fair value at each reporting date per HKFRS 13; no depreciation
Remeasurement gains/losses n/a (impairment only) ALL fair value changes → profit or loss in the period of change (never OCI)
Reference HKAS 40.30；HKAS 16.30 HKAS 40.33, 40.35
Extra rules — Continue measuring at FV even if comparable market transactions become less frequent (HKAS 40.40); FV estimate must reflect rental income from current leases and market-participant assumptions (HKAS 40.55)
Change of policy Cost → fair value: permitted if justified under HKAS 8 Fair value → cost: highly unlikely to be permitted (HKAS 40.31)

Transfers（轉撥）— only on change in use (HKAS 40.57–40.64)

  • Transfer to/from IP when, and only when: (1) there is evidence of a change in use; AND (2) the change causes the property to meet, or cease to meet, the IP definition. A change in management's intentions alone is NOT evidence (HKAS 40.58).
  • Evidence of change in use (HKAS 40.57): end of owner occupation (→ IP); inception of an operating lease to another party (inventory → IP); commencement of owner occupation or development with a view to owner occupation (→ owner-occupied); commencement of development with a view to sale (→ inventories).
  • Deciding to dispose of an IP without development: keep as IP until derecognition — do NOT reclassify as inventory (HKAS 40.58).
Direction Treatment 出處
Owner-occupied (cost model) → IP (fair value model) (1) Update depreciation/impairment to date of change in use under HKAS 16; (2) revalue to fair value at that date — difference treated under HKAS 16 revaluation rules (increase → revaluation surplus in OCI, unless reversing a prior P&L decrease) HKAS 40.61–62
Owner-occupied (cost) → IP (cost) Carrying amount unchanged HKAS 40.59
Inventory → IP (fair value model) Remeasure to fair value at date of change in use; difference → profit or loss (consistent with a sale of inventory) HKAS 40.63–64
Inventory → IP (cost) Carrying amount unchanged HKAS 40.59
IP (fair value) → owner-occupied (cost) or inventory Fair value at date of change in use = deemed cost for subsequent accounting under HKAS 16 / HKFRS 16 / HKAS 2. Remeasure to FV first (gain/loss → P&L while still IP); do NOT reverse cumulative FV gains/losses HKAS 40.60
IP (cost) → owner-occupied / inventory (cost) Carrying amount unchanged HKAS 40.59

Derecognition / disposal：dispose of or permanently withdraw from use with no future benefits expected → derecognise; gain/loss on derecognition → profit or loss.（HKAS 40 mind map）

HKFRS 5 互動：fair value model 下嘅 IP 即使符合 held for sale 分類條件，HKFRS 5 嘅 measurement provisions 唔適用 — 繼續按 HKAS 40 以 fair value 計量（唔好用 lower of carrying amount and fair value less costs to sell）。

C. Disclosure（披露）— HKAS 40.75–40.79

適用範圍 必須披露 出處
Both models Whether the fair value model or the cost model is applied HKAS 40.75(a)
Both models Reconciliation of carrying amount at beginning and end of period: additions; assets classified as held for sale under HKFRS 5; transfers to/from inventories and owner-occupied properties; other changes HKAS 40.76, 40.79(d)
Both models Whether valued by an independent qualified valuer with recent relevant experience (and extent of reliance); rental income from IP; direct operating expenses (including repairs and maintenance) — indirect operating expenses NOT required HKAS 40.75
Fair value model only Net gains/losses from fair value adjustments in the reconciliation; reconciliation between an external valuation and the valuation in the FS where significantly adjusted; plus HKFRS 13 disclosures (valuation techniques, inputs, FV hierarchy level) HKAS 40.76(d)
Cost model only Depreciation methods; useful lives or depreciation rates; gross carrying amount and accumulated depreciation (aggregated with impairment losses) at beginning and end; depreciation and impairment losses/reversals in the reconciliation; the fair value of the investment properties HKAS 40.79(a)–(d), 40.79(d)(iv)–(v)

易混淆概念對比

Table 1: Fair Value Model (HKAS 40) vs Revaluation Model (HKAS 16)

IP — Fair Value Model (HKAS 40) PPE — Revaluation Model (HKAS 16)
Increase in value CR Fair value gain in profit or loss（全數） CR Revaluation surplus in OCI；只有當 reversal of 同一資產之前喺 P&L 認過嘅 decrease，先至可以計入 P&L
Decrease in value DR Fair value loss in profit or loss（全數） 先扣同一資產嘅 revaluation surplus（DR OCI），超出部分先 DR loss in P&L
Depreciation 唔使折舊 照計折舊（按 revalued amount）
考試伏位 見到 IP fair value change 千祈唔好放入 OCI / revaluation surplus — 呢個係最常見失分位；相反 PPE revaluation surplus 唔可以直入 P&L

Table 2: Investment Property vs Owner-Occupied Property vs Inventory（分類判斷）

Investment Property (HKAS 40) Owner-Occupied Property (HKAS 16 / HKFRS 16) Inventory (HKAS 2)
持有目的 Earn rentals / capital appreciation / both Use in production/supply of goods or services, or administrative purposes Sale in the ordinary course of business（或建造中準備出售）
Cash flows Largely independent of other assets Generated jointly with other assets 出售套現
典型例子 Land for undetermined use; vacant building held to lease out; building leased out under operating lease; property under construction for future IP use Head office; property occupied by employees（即使收 market rent！）; held for future owner-occupation Developed/resold property; land acquired for development and resale
考試伏位 「Vacant」≠ owner-occupied — 空置樓持有作出租係 IP；員工宿舍即使收市值租金都係 owner-occupied（HKAS 40.9(c)）；打算出售但未有 development 嘅 IP 繼續係 IP，唔會變 inventory

Phase 2 閃卡

Flashcard 1 / 共 8 張
Question

Define "investment property" under HKAS 40 and list three examples.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話：核心係「收租或者等升值」，而且 cash flows 係獨立於公司其他資產。記住兩個「唔係」：唔係自用、唔係存貨出售。特別陷阱係「未決定用途嘅土地」都當 IP（視為等升值）。

Flashcard 2 / 共 8 張
Question

What are the recognition criteria for an owned investment property under HKAS 40.16, and how are subsequent replacement costs treated?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話：同 HKAS 16 嘅確認條件一模一樣。換升降機呢類「換零件」可以資本化，但要同時 derecognise 舊件嘅賬面值；日常維修就直出 P&L，冇得資本化。

Flashcard 3 / 共 8 張
Question

Which costs are included in, and excluded from, the initial cost of an owned investment property?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話：記住「直接應佔先計得入」。考試最鍾意混入「abnormal waste」同「廣告費」呃你資本化 — 呢兩樣係費用。注意：IP 嘅 transaction costs（如律師費、印花稅）係要計入成本嘅，唔好似金融工具咁即刻出費用。

Flashcard 4 / 共 8 張
Question

Compare the cost model and fair value model for subsequent measurement of investment property (HKAS 40.30–40.35).

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話：揀咗一個 model 就要全部 IP 一齊用。Fair value model 最大特點：唔折舊 + 升跌全部入 P&L。一旦用咗 fair value model 基本上冇得返轉頭用 cost model。

Flashcard 5 / 共 8 張
Question

When can a property be transferred into or out of investment property under HKAS 40.57–40.58?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話：「講」唔算數，要有實際行動（vacate 咗、租約開始、動工起嚟賣）。管理層得個諗字 / 開會決定咗，係唔構成 transfer 嘅 — 考試必考呢個伏位。

Flashcard 6 / 共 8 張
Question

An owner-occupied property under the HKAS 16 cost model is transferred to investment property under the fair value model. What is the accounting treatment? (HKAS 40.61–62)

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話：轉嗰下嘅升值係跟 HKAS 16 遊戲規則（入 OCI revaluation surplus），唔係即刻入 P&L！但轉完之後就係 IP，之後嘅 fair value 升跌先入 P&L。呢個「分界點」係經典考點。

What is "deemed cost" on a transfer from fair-value-model investment property to owner-occupied property or inventory? (HKAS 40.60)
