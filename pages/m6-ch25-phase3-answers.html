M6 Chapter 25: Joint Arrangements — Phase 3 Answers

Q1 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): Under HKFRS 11, joint control exists only when decisions about the relevant activities require the unanimous consent of the parties sharing control. Here the 60% threshold can be reached by more than one combination of agreeing parties (R + S = 75%; R + T = 65%; S + T = 60%). Where the required proportion can be achieved by more than one combination, the contractual arrangement must specify which parties (or combination of parties) must agree unanimously; since the contract is silent, unanimous consent cannot be demonstrated and the arrangement is not subject to joint control. It is therefore not a joint arrangement.

解題思路（繁中）: 關鍵係「多於一個組合可以達到門檻」。三個組合 (R+S、R+T、S+T) 都過到 60%，即係冇任何一個組合係「必須」一致同意，unanimous consent 唔成立。選項 A 錯：三方能「集體」控制唔等於三方都有 joint control，participant 可以存在。選項 B 錯：R+S 只係其中一個可能組合，合約冇指明佢哋必須一致同意，唔能夠斷言佢哋有 joint control。選項 D 錯：40% 單獨過唔到 60% 門檻，R 冇單獨控制權。考試伏位：見到 voting threshold 題，第一步永遠係列晒所有達標組合，多過一個 + 合約冇指明 = 唔係 joint arrangement。

Rule Check: HKFRS 11.7–9; HKFRS 11.B8

Common Trap（繁中）: 考生見到兩方夾埋過門檻就即刻答「joint control」，忽略咗「unanimous consent 必須係唯一組合或合約指明」呢個條件。記住：collective control 只係第一步，仲要過 unanimous consent 嗰關。

Q2 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): Although the arrangement is structured through a separate vehicle whose legal form confers rights to net assets (which normally indicates a joint venture), other facts and circumstances override this: Seabird's activities primarily aim to provide the parties with output (all output is sold to Seagull and Otter at cost; third-party sales are prohibited), and Seabird depends on the parties on a continuous basis for settling its liabilities (cash calls). Both conditions in HKFRS 11 are met, so the parties have rights to substantially all the economic benefits of Seabird's assets and obligations for its liabilities — the arrangement is a joint operation.

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題考 decision tree 最後一關「other facts and circumstances」。選項 A 錯：有 separate vehicle 只係第一步，唔係自動 JV。選項 B 錯：legal form 畀 net assets 權利只係第二關，仲要睇合約條款同其他事實——呢度 output 全部賣俾股東（at cost/break-even）+ 靠股東持續找數，兩個條件齊晒 → joint operation。選項 D 錯：兩位股東一致同意相關活動決策，正正係 unanimous consent + collective control，joint control 成立，所以一定係 joint arrangement（問題只係分類）。考試伏位：break-even 定價 + 禁止賣第三者 + cash call 依賴 = 教科書式 JO 訊號。

Rule Check: HKFRS 11.14–17; HKFRS 11.B31–B32

Common Trap（繁中）: 見「limited company」「net assets」就停喺第二關答 JV。記住 decision tree 有四關：separate vehicle → legal form → contractual terms → output/dependence facts，任何一關指向資產負債權利都係 JO。

Q3 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): Under the equity method, dividends received from a joint venture are a return of the investment, not income. They reduce the carrying amount of the equity-accounted investment:

Dr  Cash                              HK$45,000
        Cr  Investment in joint venture           HK$45,000

(50% × HK$90,000 = HK$45,000)

解題思路（繁中）: 權益法下股息係「收回投資」，唔係收益——JV 嘅利潤已經透過「share of profit」入咗賬，再認 dividend income 會雙重計算。選項 A 錯：Dividend income 係成本法/HKFRS 9 金融資產嘅做法，唔係 equity method。選項 C 錯：Cr Share of profit 會高估當年應佔利潤。選項 D 錯：方向完全調轉（呢個係加注投資嘅分錄）。考試伏位：Jun 2025 panelist report 特別批評考生冇消除 parent 已記嘅 dividend income——CBE journal grid 題好易考呢點。

Rule Check: HKAS 28.3 (equity method: investment adjusted for post-acquisition changes in share of net assets); HKFRS 11.24

Common Trap（繁中）: 混淆「收到股息 = 收入」嘅直覺。Equity method 口訣：利潤加投資、股息減投資。

Q4 — Answer: HK$3,625,000

Model Answer (EN): As a joint operator, Heron recognises its share of the jointly held asset (HKFRS 11.20) and measures it under HKAS 16:

Item Workings HK$
Share of cost of machine 50% × HK$8,000,000 4,000,000
Less: depreciation (1 Apr – 31 Dec 20X4) HK$4,000,000 ÷ 8 years × 9/12 (375,000)
Carrying amount at 31 December 20X4 3,625,000

解題思路（繁中）: 三步：(1) joint operator 認自己份額嘅資產（50% × 8m = 4m）；(2) 折舊按 HKAS 16 計，收購日 1 April 開始，到 31 Dec 係 9 個月，要 pro-rate；(3) 4,000,000/8 = 500,000 全年，× 9/12 = 375,000。常見錯誤：忘記折舊（答 4,000,000）；用足全年折舊（答 3,500,000）；用 100% 成本再減折舊（答 7,250,000）。考試伏位：mid-year acquisition + monthly depreciation 係 M6 指定動作，日期一定要數清楚月份。

Rule Check: HKFRS 11.20, 11.23 (each element measured per the applicable HKFRS); HKAS 16

Common Trap（繁中）: (1) 唔 pro-rate 折舊；(2) 以為 joint operation 嘅機器係「使用權」——LP 明確講 joint operator 有 asset 嘅權利而非 right-of-use，所以用 HKAS 16 唔係其他準則。

Q5 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): Applying the equity method:

HK$
Cost of investment (1 Jan 20X4) 800,000
Add: share of Peak's adjusted net profit — 50% × (HK$360,000 − HK$24,000 unrealised upstream gain) 168,000
Less: share of dividends declared — 50% × HK$80,000 (40,000)
Carrying amount at 31 December 20X4 928,000

The sale from Peak to Victoria is an upstream transaction; the unrealised gain of HK$24,000 is eliminated from Peak's profit before applying the 50% share (per the LP's Approach 2, the adjustment is Dr Share of profit of joint venture / Cr Inventory). (Note: under the LP's alternative Approach 1, the investment would be further reduced by 50% × HK$24,000 = HK$12,000, giving HK$916,000; Approach 2 as presented in the LP's worked solution gives HK$928,000.)

解題思路（繁中）: 考三個調整：(1) upstream 交易嘅 unrealised gain 要喺計 share of profit 前喺 JV 利潤度扣（360,000 − 24,000 = 336,000，×50% = 168,000）；(2) 股息減投資賬面值（40,000）；(3) 起錶用 cost。選項 A (916,000)：將全數 24,000 由「應佔利潤」扣（180,000 − 24,000 = 156,000），即係扣多咗——只需扣應佔部分。選項 C (940,000)：完全冇調整 unrealised gain（800,000 + 180,000 − 40,000）。選項 D (968,000)：冇減股息（800,000 + 168,000）。考試伏位：upstream gain 係對方賣嘢俾我，存貨仲喺我度 → 利潤未實現，必須調整；記住 LP 用 Approach 2（影響 share of profit 同 investment 一致），Approach 1 會得出 916,000，考試時要跟題目指示或 LP 做法。

Rule Check: HKFRS 11.24; HKAS 28.3 (equity method); LP Ch 25 Apply & Analyse 2 (upstream unrealised gain adjustment)

Common Trap（繁中）: (1) 忘記 upstream unrealised gain 調整；(2) 股息當收入而唔係減投資；(3) 將全數 gain 而唔係按比例扣。方向記法：JV 賣俾我 = upstream；調整永遠以 JV 嘅利潤做起點。
