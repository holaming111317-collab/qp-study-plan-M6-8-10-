Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. … — 超時如實申報

Question 1

In accordance with the Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting, which of the following statements best describes the relationship between comparability and consistency?

A. Consistency is the goal of useful financial information, and comparability is one of the means that helps an entity achieve that goal.

B. Comparability requires all entities to adopt uniform accounting methods for similar transactions so that their financial statements look alike in all respects.

C. Comparability is the goal, and consistency — the use of the same methods for the same items from period to period — helps achieve that goal.

D. Comparability and consistency are both fundamental qualitative characteristics, and either one alone is sufficient to make financial information useful.

Question 2

Verdant Limited operates a chain of juice bars in Hong Kong. In accordance with the definition of an asset in the Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting, which of the following items gives rise to an asset of Verdant Limited?

A. The right to use a commercial juicing machine under a signed three-year lease agreement, under which Verdant Limited can decide how and when the machine is operated.

B. The services to be rendered by its team of baristas, who are highly skilled but may resign from the company at any time.

C. A purchase contract signed with a fruit supplier for delivery next month, under which neither Verdant Limited nor the supplier has performed any obligation.

D. The strong loyalty of its regular customers, which is expected to generate repeat purchases in the coming year.

Question 3

In accordance with the Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting, which of the following measurement bases is an exit value that reflects entity-specific assumptions rather than the perspective of market participants?

A. Historical cost

B. Current cost

C. Fair value

D. Value in use

Question 4

Falcon Limited had net assets of HK$4,800,000 at 1 January 20X8 and net assets of HK$5,600,000 at 31 December 20X8. During the year ended 31 December 20X8, Falcon Limited issued new ordinary shares for cash of HK$500,000 and declared and paid dividends of HK$200,000 to its shareholders. In accordance with the Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting, calculate Falcon Limited's profit for the year ended 31 December 20X8 under the financial concept of capital maintenance. Show your workings.

Question 5

Harbour View Limited is a private company incorporated under the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622). It has no subsidiaries and is not a subsidiary of another company. It does not carry on any banking business, insurance business, or any business licensed under Part V of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. Its financial data are as follows:

Year ended 31 Dec 20X7 Year ended 31 Dec 20X8
Total annual revenue HK$96 million HK$98 million
Total assets at year end HK$112 million HK$118 million
Average number of employees 92 95

Harbour View Limited has not obtained any resolution from its members regarding reporting exemptions. In accordance with the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) and the HK SME-FRF, which of the following statements is correct regarding Harbour View Limited's eligibility to adopt the simplified reporting exemption for the year ended 31 December 20X9?

A. Harbour View Limited qualifies as a larger 'eligible' private company because it meets two of the three size tests measured against the HK$200 million thresholds, provided that at least 75% of all its members pass a resolution approving the adoption.

B. Harbour View Limited qualifies as a small private company because it has met two of the three size tests (total annual revenue not exceeding HK$100 million and not more than 100 employees) for two consecutive years, and no shareholders' approval is required.

C. Harbour View Limited does not qualify for the simplified reporting exemption because its total assets exceed HK$100 million at the end of both reporting periods.

D. Harbour View Limited qualifies as a small private company only if at least 75% of all its members pass a resolution approving the adoption and none of the remaining members vote against it.
