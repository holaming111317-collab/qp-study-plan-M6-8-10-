M8 Chapter 4: Audit Planning II — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives

  1. Explain the audit risk model (AR = IR × CR × DR) and analyse the relationship between business risk, inherent risk, control risk and detection risk in determining the audit approach.
  2. Apply materiality and performance materiality (HKSA 320), using quantitative benchmarks and qualitative factors, to plan and evaluate audit work.
  3. Distinguish between tests of controls and substantive procedures (tests of detail and substantive analytical procedures) and select an appropriate combined or substantive audit approach.
  4. Apply analytical procedures (HKSA 520) at the planning, substantive and final review stages, including ratio, trend and common-size analysis.
  5. Evaluate the factors affecting the persuasiveness of audit evidence (HKSA 500/505/580) and the requirements for using the work of experts (HKSA 620) and internal auditors (HKSA 610 (Revised 2013)).

必背考點

A. Audit Risk & Assertions（風險模型 + 斷言）

Audit Risk Model - Audit risk = risk that the auditor expresses an incorrect opinion on the financial statements. HKSAs require a risk-based approach; auditors cannot check every transaction. - AR = IR × CR × DR（量化時用乘數：如 AR 10%, IR 70%, CR 70% → DR = 10% ÷ 49% = 20.4%） - Inherent risk (HKSA 200.13(n)(i)): susceptibility of an assertion to a misstatement that could be material, before consideration of any related controls. 例：complex standards (HKFRS 9 forward contracts)、fast-obsolescing inventory (HKAS 2 lower of cost and NRV)、estimates with high estimation uncertainty（可能是 significant risk，屬 inherent risk spectrum 高端）. - Control risk (HKSA 200.13(n)(ii)): risk that a misstatement that could be material will not be prevented, or detected and corrected, on a timely basis by the entity's controls. 受管理層對內控嘅態度、control environment、員工能力影響。 - Detection risk (HKSA 200.13(e)): risk that the auditor's procedures will not detect a material misstatement that exists. 唯一受 auditor 控制嘅風險；= sampling risk + non-sampling risk。 - Inverse relationship: RoMM（IR × CR）越高 → 可接受 DR 越低 → 越多 substantive testing；反之亦然。 - Business risk (HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para 12(b)): risk resulting from significant conditions/events/circumstances that could adversely affect the entity's ability to achieve its objectives and execute its strategies. 分 operational / financial / compliance 三類。Business risk broader than RoMM — 不是所有 business risk 都影響財務報表（LP 明示：business risk identified by management, reviewed continuously；inherent risk identified by auditor, assessed at time of audit）。 - RoMM 兩個層次：financial statement level（整體，如 fraud、incompetent management、going concern uncertainties）vs assertion level（再分 IR + CR，需 separate assessment）。Presumed fraud risk：fraud in revenue recognition（HKSA 240 要求 professional scepticism，唔可以靠過往對 client integrity 嘅經驗排除 fraud）。 - Cybersecurity risk：auditor 要 evaluate automated controls 同 general IT controls；發現 material breach 要考慮對財務報告及 disclosures 嘅影響。

Assertions（HKSA 315 (Revised 2019)） - 定義：representations by management, explicit or otherwise, inherent in preparing the financial statements per the applicable framework. - Transactions and events (6) — HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para A190(a): Occurrence / Completeness / Accuracy / Cutoff / Classification / Presentation - Account balances at period end (6) — HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para A190(b): Existence / Rights and obligations / Completeness / Accuracy, valuation and allocation / Classification / Presentation - Disclosures：上述 assertions 經適當調整後適用；可用 disclosure checklist（要 up-to-date，符合 HKFRS 及 Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)）。 - Significant risk：必須了解相關 controls；如打算 rely on controls over a significant risk，必須 current period 測試，且仍要做 substantive procedures。 - HKSA 250 (Revised) laws & regulations：management 負責合規；auditor 要 obtain general understanding of legal/regulatory environment、inspect correspondence with regulators、enquire of management/TCWG、obtain written representations on known non-compliance。Direct effect（HKFRS、Cap. 622、tax legislation）vs indirect effect（environmental、employment、health & safety）。

B. Materiality（HKSA 320）

  • 定義 (HKSA 320.2): misstatements are material if they, individually or in aggregate, could reasonably be expected to influence the economic decisions of users.
  • Quantitative benchmarks（起點，非硬性規定，professional judgement）：0.5%–1% of revenue；5%–10% of profit before tax；1%–2% of total assets。
  • Material by nature（qualitative）：misstatement 令 profit 變 loss / net assets 變 net liabilities；directors' transactions / directors' remuneration；going concern material uncertainties；potential legal claims 嘅 disclosures。例：平賣車俾 director — 金額 immaterial 但 material by nature，屬 related party transaction (HKAS 24 (Revised))，唔披露 → modify opinion。
  • Materiality vs audit risk：materiality 越高 → 越少 transactions 被測 → audit risk 越高（inverse）。審計途中可上調/下調 materiality。
  • Performance materiality (HKSA 320.9): amount(s) set at less than materiality for the FS as a whole, to reduce to an appropriately low level the probability that the aggregate of uncorrected and undetected misstatements exceeds FS materiality. 可用百分比（如 75% × FS materiality）計算。
  • 用啱 benchmark：misstatement 要同受影響嘅項目比較（如 irrecoverable receivable → PBT + gross assets，唔係 revenue / net assets）。

C. Procedures（審計程序設計）

Audit strategy vs audit plan - Strategy 先於 plan，定 scope、timing、direction；考慮 characteristics of the engagement、reporting objectives、significant factors（materiality、assessed RoMM 等）、nature/timing/extent of resources。Plan 跟住 strategy，列明每個 audit area 點攞 evidence。 - Audit programme = list of audit procedures per area；audit engagement partner review + authorise 後先可以開始 fieldwork。

Tests of controls - 目的：測 operating effectiveness of controls；唔測金額（indirect evidence only）。有效 → 少啲 substantive；無效/唔存在 → 唔 rely，改做多啲 substantive。 - 理解內控係 mandatory（HKSA 315 (Revised 2019)），無論打唔打算 rely；五個 components：control environment / entity's risk assessment process / process to monitor the system of internal control / information system and communication / control activities。 - Walk-through test：trace a sample of transactions through the accounting system，可作 tests of controls 一部分。 - Service organisation（如外判 payroll）：可 attend/visit provider、arrange another firm、或攞 Type 1 report（description + design）/ Type 2 report（仲包 operating effectiveness）。 - Significant deficiencies in internal control → report to TCWG/management per HKSA 265。 - HKSA 330 要求無論如何都做嘅 substantive procedures：agree FS to underlying accounting records；examine material journals；examine other adjustments。

Substantive procedures - 定義：procedures designed to detect material misstatement at the assertion level；= tests of detail + substantive analytical procedures。內控再好都必須做一啲（唔可以 100% rely on controls）。 - Tests of detail：verify individual transactions/balances（如 trace additions to purchase invoices → rights & obligations + valuation）。

Substantive analytical procedures — 4 steps 1. Develop an independent expectation（先於計算差異！） 2. Define a significant difference / threshold — threshold must not exceed performance materiality 3. Calculate the difference（expectation vs recorded amount） 4. Investigate the significant differences and draw conclusions — 要解釋 full difference，唔係只係超過 threshold 嗰部分 - 適用性 4 factors：assertion being tested / reliability of the data / degree of precision possible / amount of variation acceptable。 - Proof in total（reasonableness test，常用於 payroll）：Total wages last year × (1 + inflation) × No. of employees this year ÷ No. of employees last year。 - Analytical procedures 唔可以單獨用 — 佢哋 identify possible sources of misstatement，唔直接 detect；要配 inquiry / recalculation / reperformance。

Analytical procedures 三個階段（HKSA 520） 1. Planning stage（risk assessment procedures，HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) required）— identify unusual transactions/ratios/trends、fraud risks 2. Substantive analytical procedures（HKSA 520.3(a): obtain relevant and reliable audit evidence） 3. Final review near end of audit（HKSA 520.3(b): assist in forming an overall conclusion whether FS are consistent with auditor's understanding — mandatory） - 定義 (HKSA 520.4): evaluations of financial information through analysis of plausible relationships among both financial and non-financial data + investigation of fluctuations inconsistent or differing significantly from expected values。 - 技術：Comparisons（prior year、budgets/forecasts、auditor's expectations、industry）、Trend analysis、Common-size analysis（line item ÷ base × 100；SoFP base = total assets；P/L base = revenue）、Ratio analysis。 - Ratio categories：Profitability（GP margin = GP/Revenue×100；NP margin = PBT/Revenue×100）｜Efficiency（receivables days = receivables/revenue×365；inventory days = inventory/COS×365；payables days = payables/purchases×365）｜Liquidity（current、quick = (CA − inventory)/CL、gearing）｜Return（gearing、ROCE）。 - 影響 AP precision 嘅 4 factors：disaggregation / data reliability / predictability / type of analytical procedure。 - Data analytics：操作完整 data set；用處包括 cost vs NRV comparison、inventory ageing、revenue trends by product/region、match orders to cash、segregation of duties user-code analysis、GP margin analysis、AR/AP ageing。

D. Evidence（審計證據）

  • HKSA 500 七大 procedures：Inspection / Observation / External confirmation / Recalculation / Reperformance / Analytical procedures / Inquiry（"Review" 唔係 HKSA 500 procedure）。
  • Inspection：文件（rights & obligations、cut-off、控制運作痕跡）+ physical inspection of assets（existence、condition/impairment）— 但 physical inspection 未必 prove rights & obligations。
  • Observation：只證明觀察當刻控制有運作（auditor presence 可能影響行為）— 局限要記。
  • External confirmation（HKSA 505.6(a) 定義：direct written response to the auditor from a third party）：auditor 要 maintain control — auditor prepares letters、selects sample、sends requests（HKSA 505.7）。Receivables confirmation → existence + rights & obligations，唔係 valuation。Bank confirmation letter 包 balances + security pledged。Legal confirmation → HKAS 37 provision/contingent liability 判斷。
  • Recalculation（數學準確性，如 depreciation）vs Reperformance（auditor 獨立重做 client 嘅 control/procedure，如 bank reconciliation）— 唔好撈亂。
  • Inquiry：alone is insufficient（low quality, inherent bias）；要 corroborate；可要求 confirm in written representation。
  • Evidence 質素 hierarchy（更可靠）：independent external source > internally generated under effective controls > auditor direct > documentary form > original documents（scanned/photocopied 可能被 tamper — fraud risk factor）。
  • Sufficiency（quantity）vs Appropriateness（quality = relevance + reliability）：unreliable evidence 攞幾多都冇用。
  • Factors affecting information as audit evidence：relevance / reliability / competence / sufficiency / timeliness（SoFP evidence 越近 reporting date 越可靠；P/L evidence 要 cover 全年）。
  • Written representations（HKSA 580）：internal evidence，只作 complement，唔可以單獨 rely；letter on client letterhead、signed by CEO/CFO、date = date FS authorised for issue、auditor's report 簽署前取得。Management 拒絕 → discuss reasons → re-evaluate integrity of management → consider implications for auditor's report。Representation 與其他 evidence 不一致 → reconsider reliability、risk assessment、further procedures。

E. Using the Work of Others

  • 兩類 expert：Management's expert（HKSA 500 — client 僱用，協助 prepare FS，如 valuer/actuary/lawyer）vs Auditor's expert（HKSA 620 — 審計師僱用，攞 sufficient appropriate audit evidence）。
  • 需要 expert 嘅情況：property valuations、construction WIP、actuarial valuations for defined benefit plans、specialist inventory、legal provisions、complex financial instruments、complex accounting estimates（HKSA 540 (Revised) para A31 情況）。
  • HKSA 620 三步： 1. Agree scope in engagement letter：nature/scope/objectives、roles & responsibilities、communication、confidentiality 2. Assess competence, capability and objectivity（before work starts）：qualifications、experience、professional body membership、published work；business/personal relationships with client → objectivity threat 3. Evaluate adequacy of work：consistency with other audit evidence、relevance & reasonableness of assumptions/methods、relevance/completeness/accuracy of source data。Inadequate → agree further work 或換 expert。
  • HKSA 610 (Revised 2013) Using the Work of Internal Auditors：
  • 評估 IA function（610.15–610.17）：objectivity（organisational status + policies）、competence、systematic and disciplined approach (incl. quality control)
  • 用之前再評估：work properly planned/performed/supervised/reviewed/documented、sufficient appropriate evidence obtained、conclusions appropriate、reports consistent with work；external auditor 要 reperform 部分程序
  • Direct assistance = internal auditors perform audit procedures under direction, supervision and review of external auditor。禁止 (HKSA 610.28(a)(b))：significant threats to objectivity 或 lacks sufficient competence。唔可以 assign：significant judgements / high RoMM areas / IA 曾參與嘅工作 / HKSA 610 下 external auditor 自己嘅決定
  • Direct assistance 條件：management written agreement 唔會干預 + IA written confirmation of confidentiality + external auditor direction/supervision/review；documentation per HKSA 610.36/37（包括 HKSA 230 下 review details）
  • Responsibility for conclusion：auditor's opinion 永遠係 engagement partner 負責；must not refer to the work of others in the auditor's report unless required by law or regulation（即使提咗都要講明唔 diminish responsibility）。

易混淆概念對比
