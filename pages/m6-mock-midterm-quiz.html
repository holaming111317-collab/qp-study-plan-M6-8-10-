M6 Financial Accounting — Midterm Mock (Chapters 1–13)

Mock: M6 Financial Accounting — Midterm Mock Coverage: Chapters 1–13 (Financial Reporting Framework / Fair Value Measurement / Revenue Recognition / Inventories / PPE / Investment Property / Lease Accounting / Borrowing Costs / Intangible Assets / Financial Assets, Liabilities & Equity / Impairment / NCA Held for Sale / Accounting Policies, Estimates & Errors) Time allowed: 60 minutes Total marks: 20 (1 mark per question) CLOSED-BOOK Instructions: - Answer ALL 20 objective test questions (OTQ). - For MCQs, choose the ONE best answer from A–D. - For calculation / short-answer questions, give the exact figure or term required (journal entries must show both Dr and Cr with amounts). - 超時完成者請如實申報（在答案紙上註明實際用時）。 - v1 預載：錯題回測節會按 Active Retest Queue 後補。

Questions

Q1. Which of the following statements about the Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting is correct?

A. It is an accounting standard, and where it conflicts with a HKFRS the Conceptual Framework prevails. B. It is not an accounting standard, and nothing in it overrides any HKFRS. C. It is mandatory for every Hong Kong-incorporated company, including those eligible for the reporting exemption. D. It prescribes the detailed recognition and measurement rules for property, plant and equipment.

Q2. (Calculation) Harbourline Trading Limited sells identical forklifts and has access to three active markets:

Market Annual volume (units) Price per unit (HK$) Transport cost per unit (HK$) Transaction cost per unit (HK$)
Aberdeen 15,000 52,000 1,000 1,500
Kwun Tong 22,000 51,000 800 1,200
Tuen Mun 18,000 53,500 1,200 2,000

All markets have no restrictions on sale. Applying HKFRS 13, what is the fair value per forklift? Give the exact figure in HK$.

Q3. SparkPro Limited won a contract after a competitive tender and incurred the following costs:

  • Sales commissions payable to the sales team on winning the contract: HK$480,000
  • Salaries of permanent staff who evaluated the tender: HK$300,000
  • Travel costs to attend the tender presentations: HK$60,000, of which HK$25,000 is explicitly reimbursable by the customer regardless of whether the contract is won

The costs are expected to be recovered and the amortisation period exceeds one year. Applying HKFRS 15, what amount should be capitalised as an incremental cost of obtaining the contract?

A. HK$480,000 B. HK$505,000 C. HK$565,000 D. HK$840,000

Q4. (Calculation) On 1 January 20X5, Ferndale Limited sold equipment to a customer who will pay HK$266,200 on 31 December 20X6 (two years after delivery). The cash selling price of the equipment is HK$220,000 and the appropriate discount rate is 10%. Control of the equipment passes on 1 January 20X5. Applying HKFRS 15, state (i) the revenue to be recognised in the year ended 31 December 20X5 and (ii) all related journal entries for 20X5 (Dr/Cr with amounts).

Q5. Which of the following costs is excluded from the cost of inventories under HKAS 2?

A. Import duties and non-recoverable purchase taxes on raw materials B. Freight and handling costs incurred in bringing raw materials to the factory C. Storage costs of finished goods that are not necessary in the production process before a further production stage D. Costs of designing products for a specific customer

Q6. (Calculation) Orchid Manufacturing Limited incurred the following in March 20X5, when 960 units of finished goods were produced:

  • Raw materials purchased: HK$300 per unit, less a trade discount of HK$20 per unit; freight inwards HK$10 per unit; import duties HK$6 per unit
  • Direct labour: HK$90 per unit
  • Variable production overheads: HK$44 per unit
  • Fixed production overheads: budgeted HK$96,000 per month; normal capacity is 1,200 units per month
  • Also incurred: storage of finished goods HK$15,000; freight outwards HK$12 per unit sold; sales commissions HK$25 per unit sold; abnormal material waste HK$8,000

Applying HKAS 2, calculate the total cost of the inventories manufactured in March 20X5. Give the exact figure in HK$.

Q7. In 20X3, Maple Logistics Limited revalued a building for the first time and recognised a revaluation surplus of HK$40,000 in other comprehensive income. In 20X6, a further revaluation shows a downward movement of HK$55,000 in the building's fair value. The building is not impaired. Applying HKAS 16, how should the 20X6 revaluation decrease be recognised?

A. The full HK$55,000 as an expense in profit or loss. B. HK$40,000 in other comprehensive income (against the revaluation surplus) and HK$15,000 as an expense in profit or loss. C. The full HK$55,000 in other comprehensive income against the revaluation surplus. D. HK$55,000 against opening retained earnings as a prior period adjustment.

Q8. (Calculation) On 1 January 20X2, Cedar Works Limited acquired a machine for HK$480,000, with an estimated residual value of HK$30,000 and a useful life of six years (straight-line; time-apportioned depreciation charged in years of acquisition and disposal). On 30 September 20X5, the machine was sold for HK$190,000 cash. Applying HKAS 16, calculate the gain or loss on disposal and show all journal entries relating to the machine for the year ended 31 December 20X5 (Dr/Cr with amounts).

Q9. Northside Holdings Limited holds four properties. Which of them fall within the scope of HKAS 40 Investment Property?

I. A condominium provided rent-free to employees as part of their remuneration packages II. Land held for a currently undetermined future use III. A residential block acquired for refurbishment and resale in the ordinary course of business IV. A vacant office building held to be leased out under operating leases

A. I and III only B. II and IV only C. II, III and IV only D. I, II, III and IV

Q10. Which of the following is NOT included in a lessee's initial measurement of the lease liability under HKFRS 16?

A. Fixed lease payments, less any lease incentives receivable B. Variable lease payments that depend on an index or rate, measured using the index or rate as at the commencement date C. Variable lease payments linked to 2% of the lessee's future annual sales D. The exercise price of a purchase option if the lessee is reasonably certain to exercise that option

Q11. (Calculation) On 1 January 20X5, Delta Services Limited entered into a three-year lease of office equipment. Payments of HK$100,000 are made in advance on 1 January 20X5, 1 January 20X6 and 1 January 20X7. The interest rate implicit in the lease is 5%. There are no initial direct costs, lease incentives or end-of-lease obligations. Present value factors at 5%: year 1 = 0.95238; year 2 = 0.90703. Applying HKFRS 16, state (i) the lease liability and the right-of-use asset recognised on 1 January 20X5, and (ii) the interest charge and depreciation charge for the year ended 31 December 20X5 (round to the nearest HK$).

Q12. (Calculation) Granite Build Limited has two general borrowings outstanding for the whole of 20X5: HK$120 million at 5% and HK$80 million at 6%. Construction of its new headquarters (a qualifying asset) began in 20X5; expenditures of HK$30 million were incurred on 1 July 20X5. By that date, borrowing costs were being incurred and all activities necessary to prepare the asset had begun. The year end is 31 December 20X5. Applying HKAS 23, calculate (i) the capitalisation rate, (ii) the borrowing costs capitalised in 20X5, and (iii) show the journal entry for the total interest for 20X5 (Dr/Cr with amounts).

Q13. Helios Bio Limited incurred the following costs in 20X5 on Project Sunrise:

  • Pure research into a new drug compound: HK$1,200,000
  • Development expenditure before all six recognition criteria under HKAS 38 were demonstrably met: HK$600,000
  • Development expenditure after all six criteria were demonstrably met: HK$2,400,000
  • Staff training to operate the new production equipment: HK$300,000

What amount should be recognised as an intangible asset at 31 December 20X5?

A. HK$4,500,000 B. HK$2,400,000 C. HK$3,000,000 D. HK$3,600,000

Q14. Nova Tech Limited issued preference shares carrying a fixed 6% annual dividend which the directors are contractually obliged to pay; the shares are redeemable at the holder's option for cash in five years' time. Under HKAS 32, how should Nova Tech classify the preference shares?

A. As a financial liability, because Nova Tech has a contractual obligation to deliver cash. B. As equity, because they are described as "shares" and rank as ownership interests. C. As a compound financial instrument with separate liability and equity components. D. As a financial asset, because they will be settled in cash.

Q15. (Calculation) On 1 January 20X5, Apex Investments Limited purchased 3-year bonds with a face value of HK$500,000 for HK$475,000 plus brokerage costs of HK$5,000. The bonds pay a coupon of 4% annually on 31 December; the effective interest rate is 5.5%. Apex's business model is to hold the bonds to collect contractual cash flows that are solely payments of principal and interest. Applying HKFRS 9, state (i) the initial carrying amount of the bonds and (ii) the interest revenue for 20X5, and show the related journal entries for 20X5 (Dr/Cr with amounts).

Q16. Which of the following assets must be tested for impairment annually, irrespective of whether there is any indication of impairment, under HKAS 36?

I. Goodwill acquired in a business combination II. An intangible asset with an indefinite useful life III. An intangible asset not yet available for use IV. A factory building measured under the cost model

A. I and II only B. I, II and III only C. II and IV only D. I, II, III and IV

Q17. (Calculation) Willow Limited's cash-generating unit has the following carrying amounts at 31 December 20X5: goodwill HK$40,000; plant HK$120,000; equipment HK$80,000. The recoverable amount of the CGU is HK$190,000. The equipment's fair value less costs of disposal is HK$78,000; the value in use of the individual assets cannot be determined. Applying HKAS 36, calculate the carrying amount of the plant after allocating the impairment loss. Give the exact figure in HK$.

Q18. Under HKFRS 5, a non-current asset may be classified as held for sale only if:

A. The directors have formed an intention to dispose of the asset within the next 18 months. B. The asset is available for immediate sale in its present condition and its sale is highly probable (including an active programme to locate a buyer, a price reasonable in relation to fair value, and an expected completion within one year). C. A binding sale agreement has already been signed at the reporting date. D. The asset will be abandoned rather than sold.

Q19. During the year ended 31 December 20X5, Lantern Trading Limited changed its inventory cost formula from FIFO to the weighted average cost formula, because management believes the new formula provides reliable and more relevant information. Under HKAS 8, how should this change be accounted for?

A. As a change in accounting estimate, recognised prospectively from 20X5. B. As a change in accounting policy, applied retrospectively by adjusting comparatives and opening equity. C. As a correction of a prior period error, by retrospective restatement. D. As a change in accounting policy, recognised prospectively in 20X5 profit or loss only.

Q20. Which of the following are changes in accounting estimates under HKAS 8?

I. Revising the estimated useful life and residual value of a machine II. Revising the estimated warranty provision from 2% to 3% of sales in the light of new claims experience III. Changing the inventory cost formula from FIFO to weighted average IV. Correcting an omission of depreciation charge discovered two years later

A. I and II only B. I, II and IV only C. II and III only D. I, II, III and IV
