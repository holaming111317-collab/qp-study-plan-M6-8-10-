M6 Financial Accounting — Midterm Mock (Chapters 1–13) — Answers & Marking Notes

Q1. Answer: B

The Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting is not an accounting standard. It assists preparers in developing consistent accounting policies and assists the HKICPA in developing and revising standards, but nothing in the Conceptual Framework overrides any HKFRS. Where a HKFRS specifically applies to a transaction, that HKFRS is followed, not the Framework.

解題思路：呢題考 Conceptual Framework 嘅地位。伏位係選項 A——好多同學以為 Framework 凌駕 standards，其實啱啱相反：有 specific HKFRS 時要照 standard 做。選項 C 錯在「mandatory for every company」——合資格小型公司可用 SME-FRF & SME-FRS。選項 D 錯在 CF 只提供概念（資產/負債定義等），唔會 prescribe PPE 嘅 measurement rules（嗰啲係 HKAS 16 嘅嘢）。

Rule Check: Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting — Status and Purpose（LP Ch01 §1.2：The Conceptual Framework is not an accounting standard and nothing in the Conceptual Framework should override any standard）。

Q2. Answer: HK$50,200

Under HKFRS 13, fair value is measured with reference to the principal market (the market with the greatest volume and level of activity), if one can be determined. Kwun Tong has the greatest volume (22,000 units), so it is the principal market.

  • Fair value = price in the principal market − transport costs = HK$51,000 − HK$800 = HK$50,200
  • Transaction costs (HK$1,200) are not adjusted in measuring fair value — they are not a characteristic of the asset.
  • Transport costs are adjusted because location is a characteristic of the asset.

解題思路：兩個伏位。第一，要認住 principal market（成交量最大 = Kwun Tong），唔係 most advantageous market——後者只喺 principal market 確定唔到時先至用；同學成日直接計「邊個市場最有利」（Aberdeen net 49,500 / Kwun Tong net 49,000 / Tuen Mun net 50,300 → 誤揀 Tuen Mun）。第二，transport cost 要扣（location 係資產特徵），transaction cost 唔准扣——調轉咗就全錯。

Rule Check: HKFRS 13.9, 13.17–19（principal / most advantageous market），HKFRS 13.25–26（transaction costs not adjusted; transport costs adjusted）。

Q3. Answer: B (HK$505,000)

Under HKFRS 15, an entity recognises as an asset the incremental costs of obtaining a contract if it expects to recover those costs. Incremental costs are those that would not have been incurred if the contract had not been obtained (e.g. sales commissions of HK$480,000). Costs that would have been incurred regardless (tender staff salaries HK$300,000; the non-reimbursable travel of HK$35,000) are expensed. However, the explicitly reimbursable travel of HK$25,000 is recoverable from the customer and is also capitalised.

Capitalised = HK$480,000 + HK$25,000 = HK$505,000.

解題思路：伏位有兩個：(1) 旅行費要拆開——只有「無論贏唔贏 contract 客戶都一定找返」嘅 HK$25,000 先可以資本化，淨返 HK$35,000 屬於 regardless-of-outcome 嘅費用要即刻 expensed；(2) tender staff salaries 係固定薪金，冇 contract 都照出，唔屬 incremental。選項 A 漏咗 reimbursement，選項 C/D 加多咗唔應該加嘅嘢。

Rule Check: HKFRS 15.91–93（incremental costs of obtaining a contract），HKFRS 15.94（practical expedient — 本題 amortisation period 超過一年，唔適用）。

Q4. Answer: Revenue in 20X5 = HK$220,000

The contract contains a significant financing component because payment is deferred by two years. The transaction price must be adjusted to the cash selling price; the difference between the promised consideration and the cash selling price is recognised as interest revenue over the financing period using the effective interest method.

(i) Revenue recognised on delivery (1 January 20X5): HK$220,000

(ii) Journal entries for the year ended 31 December 20X5:

Dr (HK$) Cr (HK$)
1 Jan 20X5: Contract receivable 220,000
    Revenue 220,000
31 Dec 20X5: Contract receivable (220,000 × 10%) 22,000
    Interest revenue 22,000

(For reference, on 31 Dec 20X6: Dr Contract receivable 24,200 / Cr Interest revenue 24,200; Dr Cash 266,200 / Cr Contract receivable 266,200. Check: 220,000 × 1.10 × 1.10 = 266,200 ✓)

解題思路：伏位係 revenue 唔係記 HK$266,200——deferred payment 兩年代表有 significant financing component，transaction price 要調返 cash selling price HK$220,000，差額 HK$46,200 分兩年做 interest revenue（Year 1：22,000；Year 2：24,200，注意複利）。記住 practical expedient：如果付款期唔超過一年就唔使調。

Rule Check: HKFRS 15.61–15.64（significant financing component — adjust to cash selling price）；interest per HKFRS 9 effective interest method。

Q5. Answer: C

Under HKAS 2, the cost of inventories comprises costs of purchase, costs of conversion and other costs incurred in bringing the inventories to their present location and condition. Costs excluded include: abnormal amounts of wasted materials, storage costs (unless necessary in the production process before a further production stage), administrative overheads not contributing to bringing inventories to present location/condition, and selling costs. Import duties, freight inwards and customer-specific design costs are all inventoriable.

解題思路：伏位係選項 D——「為特定客戶設計產品」嘅 design costs 屬於 bringing inventories to present condition，係要資本化嘅（唔好同一般 admin 混為一談）。C 嘅 finished goods storage 係典型 exclusion，因為貨已經造好，儲倉唔再 contribute to production。

Rule Check: HKAS 2.10–2.15（cost components），HKAS 2.16（excluded costs）。

Q6. Answer: HK$489,600

Cost per unit (HK$):

Component HK$
Raw materials net of trade discount (300 − 20) 280
Freight inwards 10
Import duties 6
Direct labour 90
Variable production overheads 44
Fixed production overheads (96,000 ÷ 1,200 normal capacity) 80
Cost per unit 510

Total cost of inventories manufactured = 960 units × HK$510 = HK$489,600

Because actual production (960) is below normal capacity (1,200), fixed production overheads are allocated using the normal-capacity rate (HK$80/unit). The unallocated fixed overheads of HK$19,200 (96,000 − 960 × 80) are expensed in the period. Storage of finished goods (15,000), freight outwards (12/unit), sales commissions (25/unit) and abnormal waste (8,000) are all excluded from inventory cost and expensed.

解題思路：兩大伏位：(1) 產量低於 normal capacity 時，fixed OH 要按 normal capacity 率（HK$80）攤分，唔可以將 HK$96,000 全數攤落 960 件（咁樣會變 100/件、誇大存貨）——unallocated HK$19,200 要即期 expensed；(2) trade discount 要扣，storage/freight outwards/commission/abnormal waste 全部唔入存貨成本。背熟 HKAS 2.16 exclusion list。

Rule Check: HKAS 2.13（fixed overheads allocated on normal capacity），HKAS 2.16（unallocated overheads and excluded costs expensed）。

Q7. Answer: B

Under HKAS 16, a revaluation decrease is recognised in other comprehensive income to the extent of any existing revaluation surplus for that same asset; any excess is recognised in profit or loss.

  • Existing surplus on this building: HK$40,000 → reversed through OCI
  • Excess decrease: HK$55,000 − HK$40,000 = HK$15,000 recognised as an expense in profit or loss

解題思路：伏位係「逐件資產」嘅 surplus——只能攞呢座樓本身累積嘅 HK$40,000 surplus 嚟對沖，多出嘅 HK$15,000 必須落 P&L，唔可以晒落 OCI（選項 C 錯），更加唔係 prior period adjustment（選項 D 錯，跌價唔係 error）。調轉過嚟：revaluation increase 都係先沖返同一資產以前喺 P&L 認過嘅 decrease（reversal 入 P&L），超出部分先入 OCI。

Rule Check: HKAS 16.39–16.40（revaluation increases/decreases）。

Q8. Answer: Loss on disposal = HK$8,750

Annual depreciation = (HK$480,000 − HK$30,000) ÷ 6 = HK$75,000 Accumulated depreciation at 31 December 20X4 = 3 × 75,000 = HK$225,000 20X5 depreciation (9 months to 30 September) = 75,000 × 9/12 = HK$56,250 Carrying amount at disposal = 480,000 − 225,000 − 56,250 = HK$198,750 Loss on disposal = 190,000 − 198,750 = HK$8,750

Journal entries for the year ended 31 December 20X5:

Dr (HK$) Cr (HK$)
Depreciation expense — machine 56,250
    Accumulated depreciation — machine 56,250
Cash 190,000
Accumulated depreciation — machine 281,250
Loss on disposal of machine (P&L) 8,750
    Machine 480,000

(Self-check: 190,000 + 281,250 + 8,750 = 480,000 ✓)

解題思路：三個伏位：(1) 賣嗰年要補返 9 個月 depreciation——HKAS 16.55 話 depreciation 喺 derecognition 日先停止，好多同學會漏咗 20X5 年嘅 charge，直接用 225,000 計 gain；(2) 處置分錄要一次過「清卡」——Dr cash + Dr accumulated depreciation 對沖 Cr 成個 cost，差額先係 gain/loss；(3) gain/loss on disposal 係 P&L 項目，唔係 revenue。Dr/Cr 逐條核對平衡先好交。

Rule Check: HKAS 16.55（depreciation ceases at the earlier of held-for-sale classification and derecognition），HKAS 16.67–16.68, 16.71–16.72（derecognition; gain/loss to P&L, not revenue）。

Q9. Answer: B

Under HKAS 40:

  • I — Property provided to employees as part of remuneration is owner-occupied → HKAS 16 (excluded from HKAS 40.9).
  • II — Land held for a currently undetermined future use is deemed to be held for capital appreciation → investment property (HKAS 40.8(b)).
  • III — Property held for sale in the ordinary course of business (or in the process of development for such sale) is inventory → HKAS 2 (excluded from HKAS 40.9(a)).
  • IV — A building held for rental under operating leases (even if currently vacant) is investment property.

解題思路：伏位係 I 同 III 睇落似「投資」其實唔係：員工宿舍屬 owner-occupied（HKAS 16），起完放售嘅樓屬 inventory（HKAS 2）。而 II（用途未定嘅地）同 IV（空置但打算出租嘅樓）反而係 investment property——記住「undetermined use」係 HKAS 40.8 明文當作 capital appreciation。

Rule Check: HKAS 40.8（definition incl. land held for undetermined future use），HKAS 40.9（exclusions: owner-occupied property, property held for sale in ordinary course）。

Q10. Answer: C

Under HKFRS 16.27, the lease liability is initially measured at the present value of lease payments not paid at the commencement date, comprising: fixed payments less lease incentives receivable (A); variable payments depending on an index or rate, initially measured using the index/rate at the commencement date (B); amounts expected under residual value guarantees; the exercise price of a purchase option the lessee is reasonably certain to exercise (D); and termination penalties where the lease term reflects exercising a termination option.

Variable payments linked to future sales (or usage) are NOT included in the lease liability; they are recognised in profit or loss in the period in which the event or condition that triggers them occurs (HKFRS 16.38(b)).

解題思路：伏位係選項 B 同 C 都係「variable payments」，但處理完全相反：跟 index/rate（例如 CPI）嘅要按 commencement date 嘅指數入 lease liability；跟未來銷售/用量嘅就唔入，做嘢發生嗰期先至入 P&L。呢個係 QP 最鍾意考嘅對比。

Rule Check: HKFRS 16.27（payments included in lease liability），HKFRS 16.28（variable payments not based on index/rate excluded），HKFRS 16.38(b)。

Q11. Answer: Lease liability HK$185,941; ROU asset HK$285,941; interest HK$9,297; depreciation HK$95,314

(i) On 1 January 20X5:

  • The first payment of HK$100,000 is paid at commencement, so it is not part of the lease liability.
  • Lease liability = 100,000 × 0.95238 + 100,000 × 0.90703 = 95,238 + 90,703 = HK$185,941
  • Right-of-use asset = lease liability + payment made at commencement = 185,941 + 100,000 = HK$285,941

Journal entry on commencement:

Dr (HK$) Cr (HK$)
Right-of-use asset 285,941
    Lease liability 185,941

Q19. Answer: B

Under HKAS 8, an entity may change an accounting policy only if required by a HKFRS or if the change results in reliable and more relevant information. A change in the inventory cost formula (FIFO → weighted average) is a change in measurement basis and therefore a change in accounting policy. A voluntary change in accounting policy is applied retrospectively: the opening balance of each affected component of equity is adjusted for the earliest prior period presented, and comparatives are restated as if the new policy had always been applied.

解題思路：伏位係將「cost formula 改變」誤當 estimate——HKAS 8 明講 change in measurement basis = change in accounting policy，FIFO→WA 係教科書式嘅 policy change（自願改動 → retrospective）。記住三分类：policy change（retrospective）、estimate change（prospective）、error correction（retrospective restatement）。選項 C 錯在之前用 FIFO 唔係錯誤；選項 D 錯在自願 policy change 唔可以 prospective。

Q20. Answer: A

Under HKAS 8:

  • I — Revising useful life/residual value of a machine = change in accounting estimate (recognised prospectively).
  • II — Revising the warranty percentage in the light of new claims experience = change in accounting estimate (recognised prospectively; affects current period only or current and future periods).
  • III — Changing FIFO → weighted average is a change in measurement basis = change in accounting policy (retrospective).
  • IV — An omitted depreciation charge is a failure to use information available at the time = a prior period error (corrected by retrospective restatement).

解題思路：呢題考三分类嘅界線。常用記法：「估數」變咗（useful life、warranty %、ECL、NRV）= estimate（prospective）；「計量基礎/原則」變咗（cost formula、measurement basis）= policy（retrospective）；「當時應該知嘅資料冇用/用錯」（漏計 depreciation、計錯數、fraud）= error（retrospective restatement）。若然 policy 同 estimate 真係分唔開，HKAS 8 規定當 estimate change 處理。
