M6 Chapter 4: Inventories — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives

  1. Describe the scope and definition of inventories under HKAS 2 and classify inventories into raw materials, work in progress and finished goods.
  2. Apply acceptable methods of assigning costs to inventories, including the allocation of fixed and variable production overheads based on normal capacity.
  3. Calculate the cost of inventories and cost of sales under the FIFO and weighted average cost formulas.
  4. Calculate net realisable value (NRV) and prepare the adjustments required when NRV falls below cost, including reversals of write-downs.
  5. Prepare the disclosures in respect of inventories required by HKAS 2.

必背考點

1. Recognition（範圍與定義）

  • HKAS 2.6 — Definition of inventories: assets that are (a) held for sale in the ordinary course of business; (b) in the process of production for such sale; or (c) in the form of materials or supplies to be consumed in the production process or in the rendering of services.
  • 三大類別（按完成程度）:
  • Raw materials — 未開始加工；
  • Work in progress (WIP) — 正在轉化為製成品；
  • Finished goods — 可供出售。
  • Manufacturer 通常三類齊全；merchandiser (retailer/wholesaler) 一般只有 finished goods。
  • Expensing 時點: 存貨售出時，其 carrying amount 於確認相關收入之同期確認為 expense (cost of sales)；若曾 write-down 至 NRV，相關 expense 更早已確認。

2. Measurement — Initial measurement at cost

  • HKAS 2.11 — Costs of purchase: purchase price + import duties and other taxes + transport, handling and other costs directly attributable to acquisition。Trade discounts, rebates and similar items must be DEDUCTED。
  • HKAS 2.12 — Costs of conversion: direct costs (direct materials, direct labour) + systematically allocated production overheads (fixed + variable)。
  • HKAS 2.13 — Overhead allocation:
  • Fixed production overheads: allocated based on normal capacity（正常營運下數個期间的平均預期產量，計及 routine maintenance）。
    • 產量低於 normal capacity → 每單位分配的 fixed overhead 不得增加；unallocated overhead (unused capacity) 於發生當期 expensed。
    • 產量異常高 → 每單位分配額 調低，確保存貨不高於 cost。
  • Variable production overheads: allocated based on actual use。
  • HKAS 2.15 — Other costs: 只計入使存貨達致「現時地點及狀況」(present location and condition) 的成本，例如 specific customer design costs、若干 non-production overheads。
  • HKAS 2.16 — Costs EXCLUDED (expensed as incurred):
  • abnormal amounts of wasted materials, labour or other production costs;
  • storage costs（除非屬生產過程中下一階段前必需）;
  • administrative overheads that do not contribute to bringing inventories to their present location and condition;
  • selling costs (e.g. marketing, sales staff costs, freight outwards / distribution)。

3. Measurement — Cost formulas（成本流轉假設）

  • HKAS 2.23 — Specific identification: 用於 not ordinarily interchangeable 的存貨，或為 specific projects 生產並分開隔離的貨品/服務。
  • HKAS 2.24 — Interchangeable items: 用 FIFO 或 weighted average cost formula。
  • LIFO is NOT permitted（LP 引 IAS 2 para. BC 9 作為禁止依據）。
  • HKAS 2.25 — Consistency: 性質及用途相近的存貨必須使用同一 cost formula；HKAS 2 不指定公式，屬 policy choice，但應配合存貨性質及管理實務（如 perishable goods 宜用 FIFO）。
  • HKAS 2.27:
  • Weighted average: 以期初存貨加本期購入/產出的加權平均成本計算；可定期計算或每次進貨更新（moving average）。適合成本穩定、不易腐壞的存貨。
  • FIFO: 假設先購入/生產的先售出；期末存貨為最近購入/生產的項目。適合 fast-moving 或 perishable 存貨。
  • Inventory accounting systems:
  • Perpetual method: 每宗交易（purchase/sale/return）即時更新存貨帳；cost of sales 於銷售時點記錄；資訊較準確及時但成本較高。
  • Periodic method: 定期（月/年）盤點更新；Cost of sales = Opening inventory + Purchases − Purchase returns − Closing inventory；較簡易但較不準確。

4. Measurement — Subsequent: Lower of cost and NRV

  • HKAS 2.28: inventories are measured at the lower of cost and net realisable value (NRV)。
  • HKAS 2.6 — NRV definition: the estimated selling price in the ordinary course of business LESS the estimated costs of completion AND the estimated costs necessary to make the sale（e.g. marketing and distribution costs）。
  • Cost 可能無法收回的情況 (HKAS 2.28)：存貨 damaged or obsolete；售價下跌；completion 或銷售成本上升。
  • HKAS 2.30: NRV 按評估時最可靠證據估計；須計及報告期後直接相關的價格/成本波動，但僅限於確認報告期末已存在狀況者。
  • HKAS 2.33 — Reversal: 每期期末重新評估 NRV。若導致 write-down 的情況不再存在，或有 clear evidence 顯示 NRV 上升，須撥回 write-down —— 但僅以原 write-down 金額為上限（即存貨絕不可高於 original cost）。Reversal 確認為當期 inventories expense 的減少。

5. Disclosure（HKAS 2.36–2.39）

HKAS 2.36 要求披露： - (a) 計量存貨的 accounting policies，包括所用 cost formula； - (b) 存貨 total carrying amount 及按適當分類（RM / WIP / FG）的 carrying amount（HKAS 2.36(b)）； - (d)/(e) 當期確認為 expense 的存貨金額（cost of sales）及當期 write-down 金額（HKAS 2.36(e)）； - (f) 當期 write-down 的 reversal 金額（作為 expense 減項）； - (g) 導致 reversal 的 circumstances or events； - (h) pledged as security for liabilities 的存貨 carrying amount（HKAS 2.36(h)）。 - HKAS 2.38: cost of sales 包括已售存貨成本、unallocated production overheads、abnormal production costs。 - HKAS 2.39: 若損益表按性質分類費用 (by nature)，須披露 raw materials、labour 等及存貨淨變動。 - 考試伏位: HKAS 2 並不要求 RM/WIP/FG 之間變動的 reconciliation（只需分類 carrying amounts）。

6. Key Journal Entries（Dr/Cr 必須平衡）

(1) Write-down of inventories to NRV (HKAS 2.28):

Account Dr (HK$) Cr (HK$)
Loss on write-down of inventories x,xxx
    Inventories x,xxx

(2) Reversal of a previous write-down — capped at original write-down (HKAS 2.33):

Account Dr (HK$) Cr (HK$)
Inventories x,xxx
    Loss on write-down of inventories x,xxx

(3) Sale of goods under the perpetual method（兩 entry 同時做）:

Account Dr (HK$) Cr (HK$)
Cash / Accounts receivable x,xxx
    Sales revenue x,xxx
Cost of sales x,xxx
    Inventories x,xxx

(4) Purchase of raw materials on credit (perpetual):

Account Dr (HK$) Cr (HK$)
Inventories (raw materials) x,xxx
    Accounts payable x,xxx

易混淆概念對比

Table 1: FIFO vs Weighted Average Cost（HKAS 2.27）

比較點 FIFO Weighted Average Cost
Cost flow assumption 先買入的先賣出；closing inventory = 最近購入成本 每次進貨（或期末）重算平均單位成本
價格上升時 COGS 較低（舊平貨先出）→ profit 較高 較高（平均價被新貴貨拉高）→ profit 較低
Closing inventory 反映 較接近現時成本 平滑後的混合成本
適用存貨 Fast-moving / perishable（如時令植物、短保質期食品） 成本穩定、不易腐壞（如長期固定價供應的瓷磚）
點樣分辨 / 考試伏位 題目講「shelf life」「spoilage」「first purchased sold first」→ FIFO 題目講「stable input costs」「fixed-price supply contract」→ WAC；moving average 記得每次 purchase 後先重算平均價才扣減銷售

Table 2: Net Realisable Value (NRV) vs Fair Value (FV)

比較點 NRV (HKAS 2.6) Fair Value (HKFRS 13)
定義 Estimated selling price in the ordinary course of business − costs of completion − costs necessary to make the sale Price received to sell an asset in an orderly transaction between market participants at the measurement date
性質 Entity-specific 價值（實體日常生意能賣幾多） Market-based measurement
用途 HKAS 2 存貨後續計量（lower of cost and NRV） 適用於多個準則的公允價值計量
點樣分辨 / 考試伏位 MCQ 常見 "definition swap" 伏：將 HKFRS 13 fair value 定義當 NRV 選項；記住 NRV 必須扣兩樣嘢（completion + selling costs），漏咗 costs of completion 嘅選項都係錯 見到「market participants」「orderly transaction」字眼 = fair value，唔係 NRV

Phase 2 閃卡

Flashcard 1 / 共 8 張
Question

Under HKAS 2.6, what three categories of assets qualify as "inventories"?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 繁中白話：記住「賣緊、做緊、用緊」三部曲 —— 未做完嘅材料、做緊嘅半製品、做完等賣嘅貨，三樣都係 inventory。

Flashcard 2 / 共 8 張
Question

According to HKAS 2.11 and 2.16, which costs are included in / excluded from the cost of inventories?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 繁中白話：問自己「呢舊錢係咪令貨品達到而家嘅地點同狀態？」係 → 入 cost；唔係（例如儲起製成品、運俾客、賣嘢佣金、不正常浪費）→ 即刻 expense。

Flashcard 3 / 共 8 張
Question

How are fixed and variable production overheads allocated to inventories under HKAS 2.13, and what happens when production is abnormally low or abnormally high?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 繁中白話：fixed overhead 用「正常產能」做分母，唔好用「實際產量」。產量少咗，每件貨唔會因而背多啲 overhead；多餘嗰份直接出 P&L。產量異常多就要反過來調低每單位分配額。

Flashcard 4 / 共 8 張
Question

Which cost formulas are permitted by HKAS 2 for interchangeable inventories, and when must specific identification be used (HKAS 2.23–2.25)?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 繁中白話： interchangeable（件件一樣）→ FIFO 或 WAC 二揀一；每件都獨一無二（如訂造遊艇）→ specific identification；LIFO 喺 HKAS 2 係禁品，見到即刻剔走。

Flashcard 5 / 共 8 張
Question

Define net realisable value (NRV) under HKAS 2.6 and state the measurement rule in HKAS 2.28.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 繁中白話： NRV = 賣價 − 未完成成本 − 賣嘢使費，兩樣扣項缺一不可。存貨永遠取 cost 同 NRV 嘅較低者 —— 資產唔可以報大數。

Flashcard 6 / 共 8 張
Question

Give the journal entries for (a) a write-down of inventories to NRV and (b) a reversal of a previous write-down (HKAS 2.28, 2.33).

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 繁中白話：減值 → Dr Loss、Cr Inventory；翻生 → 調轉。但翻生有「天花板」：最多返到原本 cost，唔准升穿。記住 credit 邊唔係 "revaluation surplus"，係沖返個 loss account。

Question

How does the perpetual inventory system differ from the periodic system, and what is the cost of sales formula under the periodic system?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 繁中白話：perpetual = 每單交易即時入帳，隨時知庫存；periodic = 期末先數貨，COGS 靠「期初 + 買 − 退 − 期末」倒挤出嚟。考試見到「physical count」「updated periodically」就係 periodic。

Question

List the main disclosure requirements for inventories under HKAS 2.36.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 繁中白話：口訣「Policy、分類、Expense、減值、撥回、原因、抵押」七件事。留意：分類之間的 movement reconciliation 唔使披露 —— 呢個係常見 MCQ 伏。

Net realisable value (NRV) 估計賣價減埋完工同銷售成本後，真正落到袋嘅錢 Inventories are measured at the lower of cost and net realisable value in accordance with HKAS 2.28.
Normal capacity 正常營運下、計及例行維修後，幾個期間平均預期做到嘅產量 Fixed production overheads are allocated to inventories based on the normal capacity of the production facilities (HKAS 2.13).
Cost formula 將可售貨品成本分配去 COGS 同期末存貨嘅方法（FIFO / WAC） The same cost formula shall be used for all inventories having a similar nature and use to the entity (HKAS 2.25).
Specific identification 逐件貨認住佢嘅實際成本，適用於唔可以互換嘅貨 Specific identification of cost is required for items that are not ordinarily interchangeable (HKAS 2.23).
Write-down 存貨成本高過 NRV 時，將帳面值調低嘅動作 The entity recognised a write-down of inventories to net realisable value as an expense in the period.
Reversal of write-down 之前減值嘅原因消失，將減值撥返（上限係原來減值額） The reversal of the write-down is limited to the amount of the original write-down, so inventories are not carried above cost (HKAS 2.33).
Perpetual inventory system 每單交易即時更新存貨帳嘅制度 Under the perpetual method, cost of sales is recorded at the point the sale is made.
Work in progress (WIP) 做咗一半、未變成製成品嘅存貨 The manufacturer's statement of financial position includes raw materials, work in progress and finished goods.
