M8 Chapter 4: Audit Planning II — Phase 3 Quiz

Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. … — 超時如實申報

Question 1 (Calculation / short answer)

The audit engagement partner of Harbour Textiles Ltd has set a firm policy that overall audit risk must not exceed 5%. Following the risk assessment at the planning stage, the engagement team has assessed inherent risk at 80% and control risk at 50% for the revenue cycle.

Calculate the maximum acceptable detection risk, and state what this level of detection risk implies for the extent of substantive procedures the auditor should perform over the revenue cycle.

Question 2 (Scenario MCQ)

You are the audit senior of Bright Logistics Ltd, a warehousing company. At the year-end inventory count, you select a sample of inventory items physically located on the warehouse shelves and trace each item into the client's inventory listing records.

Which financial statement assertion is this procedure primarily designed to test?

A. Existence of recorded inventory B. Completeness of recorded inventory C. Accuracy, valuation and allocation of inventory D. Rights and obligations over inventory

Question 3 (Scenario MCQ)

During the audit of Pearl Catering Group Ltd, the audit junior discovers that the company lent HK$45,000 interest-free to one of its directors during the year. The amount is quantitatively immaterial against all of the firm's materiality benchmarks (0.5% of revenue, 5% of profit before tax, 2% of total assets), and the loan has been correctly recorded in the accounting records. No disclosure of the loan has been made in the draft financial statements.

Which of the following best describes how the auditor should treat this matter?

A. Ignore the matter, because the amount is quantitatively immaterial and correctly recorded B. Treat the loan as material by nature, because transactions with directors require disclosure as related party transactions C. Increase financial statement materiality so that the loan becomes clearly immaterial D. Request the director to repay the loan before the auditor's report is signed

Question 4 (Scenario MCQ)

Dragon Manufacturing Ltd has an internal audit function, and the engagement partner of the external auditor is considering using internal auditors to provide direct assistance on the audit. The proposed assignments are:

(1) Reperforming the year-end bank reconciliations, a routine procedure involving limited judgement (2) Evaluating the reasonableness of a highly subjective provision for a legal claim, which involves significant judgement and a high risk of material misstatement

Under HKSA 610 (Revised 2013) Using the Work of Internal Auditors, which of the following is correct?

A. Both (1) and (2) may be assigned, provided the internal auditors are supervised B. (1) may be assigned, but (2) must not be assigned to internal auditors C. (2) may be assigned, but (1) must not be assigned because bank reconciliations involve the control system D. Neither (1) nor (2) may be assigned, because direct assistance is prohibited for all external audits in Hong Kong

Question 5 (MCQ)

The audit manager is designing substantive analytical procedures over the payroll expense of a client. Which of the following statements about the design of substantive analytical procedures is correct?

A. The acceptable threshold should be set by reference to materiality for the financial statements as a whole B. The auditor should first calculate the difference between the recorded amount and prior-year balances, then develop the expectation C. The threshold must not exceed performance materiality and must be small enough to detect misstatements that could be material in aggregate D. Substantive analytical procedures alone provide sufficient appropriate audit evidence over payroll and require no corroborating procedures
