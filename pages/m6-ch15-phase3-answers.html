M6 Chapter 15: Provisions, Contingent Liabilities and Contingent Assets — Phase 3 Answers

Q1 — Answer: D

Model Answer (EN): Under HKAS 37 para. 14, a provision is recognised only when ALL three of the following conditions are met: (a) the entity has a present obligation (legal or constructive) as a result of a past event; (b) it is probable (more likely than not) that an outflow of resources embodying economic benefits will be required to settle the obligation; and (c) a reliable estimate can be made of the amount of the obligation. Certainty of timing and amount is NOT required — indeed, a provision is by definition a liability of uncertain timing or amount (HKAS 37.10/37.11). If timing and amount were certain, the item would be a trade payable or accrual, not a provision.

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係 NOT-question，考你 provision 嘅定義本質。Provision 嘅核心特徵就係 timing 或 amount 有 uncertainty — 所以 D 所講「time 同 amount 都確定」唔單止唔係確認條件，仲同 provision 定義相反。A、B、C 就係 HKAS 37.14 嘅三大條件，全部係真。考試伏位：睇漏 "NOT" 字就會揀 A/B/C。遇到 NOT/EXCEPT 題目，先圈起個關鍵字再答。

Rule Check: HKAS 37 para. 14; HKAS 37 para. 11

Common Trap（繁中）: 考生經常將「reliable estimate」同「certainty」混淆。HKAS 37 要求嘅係可以可靠估計（reliable estimate），而唔係金額確定 — 如果金額完全確定，根本就唔係 provision，而係 payable/accrual。

Q2 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): Under HKAS 37 para. 72, a constructive obligation for restructuring arises only when the entity (a) has a detailed formal plan for the restructuring AND (b) has raised a valid expectation in those affected that it will carry out the restructuring by starting to implement the plan or announcing its main features to those affected. At 31 December 20X5, Lantern had a detailed formal plan but had NOT communicated it to employees, customers or suppliers — a board decision alone is not an obligating event because it can still be altered or reversed (HKAS 37.20). Therefore no restructuring provision is recognised in the 20X5 financial statements. The public announcement on 8 January 20X6 is a non-adjusting event after the reporting period which requires disclosure under HKAS 10 if material.

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係 restructuring 嘅經典考試伏位 — 正正係 Jun 2024 panelist report 點名批評嘅錯誤：考生以為董事會批准咗就要入賬。板斧：board approval ≠ constructive obligation；必須已經公佈俾受影響人士（或已開始實施），令佢哋產生 valid expectation 先算。Option A 錯在齋睇 formal plan 忽略 communication；Option C 錯在將 1 月 8 日嘅公佈當 adjusting event — 佢唔係為報告期末已存在嘅狀況提供證據，而係報告期後先產生 obligation；Option D 錯在分類：呢度唔係 possible obligation，而係根本未有 obligating event，所以連 contingent liability 都唔係，只係 HKAS 10 嘅 non-adjusting event 披露。Redundancy HK$900,000 + lease termination HK$300,000 = HK$1,200,000 係將來（20X6 年公佈後）先確認嘅數字。

Rule Check: HKAS 37 paras. 20, 72; HKAS 10 Events after the Reporting Period (non-adjusting event disclosure)

Common Trap（繁中）: 「Board 已批准 + 有詳細計劃」只滿足 HKAS 37.72(a) 一半條件。記住兩個條件係 AND 關係：detailed formal plan AND valid expectation raised。缺第二個就乜都唔入賬。

Q3 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN):

Workings — expected value of warranty obligation at 31 December 20X5 (HKAS 37.39, large population of similar obligations):

Likelihood Cost (HK$) Expected value (HK$)
70% no defects 0 0
20% minor defects 1,500,000 300,000
10% major defects 4,000,000 400,000
Total expected value 700,000

Less: repair costs already incurred during 20X5: (280,000)

Provision for warranties at 31 December 20X5 = HK$700,000 − HK$280,000 = HK$420,000

解題思路（繁中）: 兩步計算：第一步用 expected value method（HKAS 37.39）— 因為係一大 population 嘅同類 warranty obligations，要 probability-weighted，唔可以攞最可能結果（70% 無事 → nil）。第二步記住扣返年內已經使咗嘅維修費 HK$280,000，剩低嘅先係年結日 provision 餘額。Distractor 分析：A（HK$nil）= 錯用 most likely outcome 喺大 population 度；B（HK$700,000）= 計啱 expected value 但唔記得扣已付嘅 claims；D（HK$5,500,000）= 完全唔用概率，直接將 minor + major 成本相加。

Rule Check: HKAS 37 paras. 24, 36, 39

Common Trap（繁中）: 兩個常見錯：(1) 見到 70% 機會無 defect 就以為唔使做 provision — 對成個 population 嚟講 probable outflow 係成立嘅；(2) 忘記 provision 係「年結日尚欠嘅責任」，已經賠咗嘅錢要扣走。

Q4 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN):

Step 1 — Is the contract onerous (HKAS 37.66)? Economic benefits expected (contract revenue) = 4,000 × HK$300 = HK$1,200,000. Cost of fulfilling the contract = 4,000 × HK$340 = HK$1,360,000. Since the unavoidable costs of meeting the obligations exceed the economic benefits expected, the contract is onerous and a provision must be recognised.

Step 2 — Measure at the least net cost (HKAS 37.68): the lower of (a) the net cost of fulfilling the contract = HK$1,360,000 − HK$1,200,000 = HK$160,000; and (b) the penalty from failure to fulfil = HK$120,000.

Provision for onerous contract = lower of HK$160,000 and HK$120,000 = HK$120,000. Journal entry: Dr Loss on onerous contract (P&L) HK$120,000 / Cr Provision for onerous contract HK$120,000.

解題思路（繁中）: Onerous contract 兩步曲：第一步測試係咪 onerous — 成本 340/set 高過售價 300/set，履行就蝕，所以係 onerous。第二步計 provision 金額 — 記住係「least net cost」：履行嘅淨虧損 HK$160,000 同退出罰款 HK$120,000 取其低。Distractor 分析：A（nil）= 混淆咗「future operating losses 唔入賬」嘅規則 — onerous contract 係現存合約義務，必須入賬；C（HK$160,000）= 只睇履行成本，忘記同 penalty 比較取低；D（HK$1,360,000）= 錯用履行總成本，冇扣除合約收益 HK$1,200,000。注意 HKAS 37.68A：cost of fulfilling 包括 incremental costs（direct labour、materials）加分攤直接相關成本（如 machinery depreciation），題目已經包埋喺 HK$340 入面。

Rule Check: HKAS 37 paras. 66, 68, 68A

Common Trap（繁中）: 最大陷阱係忘記「lower of」比較 — 履行定退出，邊個平計邊個。其次係唔記得將合約收益對銷履行成本（要用 net cost，唔係 gross cost）。

Q5 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN):

The decommissioning obligation meets the HKAS 37 recognition criteria: a present (legal) obligation exists as a result of a past event (the construction of the solar farm), an outflow is probable, and a reliable estimate (HK$5,000,000) exists. Because settlement occurs 8 years after the reporting date, the time value of money is material and the provision must be measured at present value (HKAS 37.45–46):

PV of decommissioning cost = HK$5,000,000 × 0.6768 = HK$3,384,000

Under HKAS 16, the initial estimate of dismantling/removal/restoration costs is included in the cost of the item of property, plant and equipment — it is NOT expensed.

Journal entry on 1 January 20X4:

Dr HK$ Cr HK$
Property, plant & equipment — solar farm 3,384,000
    Provision for decommissioning 3,384,000

解題思路（繁中）: 考兩個規則疊埋一齊：(1) discounting — 8 年後先使嘅錢要折現（LP 指引：settlement 超過 12 個月就要折），用題目俾嘅 PV factor 0.6768；(2) HKAS 16 嘅資本化規則 — decommissioning 成本嘅現值要加落 PPE 成本，唔係當 expense。Distractor 分析：A 錯在唔折現，直接用面值 HK$5,000,000；B 折現啱但錯誤費用化（debit Decommissioning expense）— 初期估計永遠資本化入 PPE；D 係古惑混合體，將部分當 expense 部分入 PPE，仲要 Cr 面值，完全違規。留意：之後每年 unwinding of discount 先會出現 Dr Interest expense / Cr Provision（HKAS 37.60），嗰部分先入 P&L。

Rule Check: HKAS 37 paras. 45–46; HKAS 16 (initial estimate of dismantling/removal/restoration costs included in cost of PPE); unwinding — HKAS 37 para. 60

Common Trap（繁中）: 兩大陷阱：(1) 見到 settlement 好耐之後先發生都唔折現，直接入面值；(2) 將 PV 當 expense 而唔係資本化。口訣：「初期入 PPE，滾息入 P&L」— initial PV capitalise under HKAS 16；unwinding 先係 interest expense。
