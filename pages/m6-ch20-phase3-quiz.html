M6 Chapter 20: Financial Statements Introduction — Phase 3 Quiz

Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. … — 超時如實申報

Question 1

Mantis Limited is preparing its first annual general purpose financial statements in accordance with HKFRSs for the year ended 31 December 20X9. According to HKAS 1 Presentation of Financial Statements, which of the following is NOT a component of a complete set of financial statements?

A. A statement of changes in equity for the period B. A statement of directors' remuneration for the period C. Notes, comprising material accounting policy information and other explanatory information D. Comparative information in respect of the preceding period

Question 2

Harbour View Limited is a property developer with a normal operating cycle of 15 months. Its reporting period ended on 31 December 20X9. In accordance with HKAS 1, which of the following items should be classified as NON-CURRENT in its statement of financial position as at 31 December 20X9?

A. Inventories of properties under development that are expected to be sold within the company's 15-month operating cycle B. Trade receivables expected to be realised within the company's 15-month operating cycle C. Trade payables due to be settled in 14 months as part of the company's normal operating cycle D. A cash deposit that is restricted from being exchanged or used to settle liabilities for 24 months after the reporting period

Question 3

During 20X9, Falcon Limited discovered a material error: plant purchased in 20X7 had been incorrectly recorded as repairs expense. The error is corrected by retrospective restatement in its financial statements for the year ended 31 December 20X9, and the restatement has a material effect on the information in the statement of financial position at the beginning of the preceding period. In accordance with HKAS 1, which statements of financial position must Falcon Limited present in its 20X9 financial statements?

A. 31 December 20X9, 31 December 20X8 and 1 January 20X8, with related notes required for all three statements B. 31 December 20X9 and 31 December 20X8 only, because the error relates to an earlier period C. 31 December 20X9, 31 December 20X8 and 1 January 20X8, with related notes not required for the statement as at 1 January 20X8 D. 31 December 20X9, 1 January 20X9 and 1 January 20X8, with related notes required for all three statements

Question 4

Egret Limited reported a profit for the year ended 31 December 20X9 of HK$800,000. During the year, the company revalued a building upwards by HK$200,000 (the building had never been revalued before). The income tax rate is 16.5% and a deferred tax liability arises on the revaluation gain. The company also declared ordinary dividends of HK$120,000 and issued new shares for cash of HK$300,000 during the year. In accordance with HKAS 1, what is Egret Limited's TOTAL COMPREHENSIVE INCOME for the year ended 31 December 20X9?

A. HK$1,000,000 B. HK$847,000 C. HK$967,000 D. HK$1,267,000

Question 5

In accordance with HKAS 1, which of the following statements about the presentation of other comprehensive income (OCI) is NOT correct?

A. Items of OCI must be grouped into those that will not be reclassified subsequently to profit or loss and those that may be reclassified subsequently to profit or loss when specific conditions are met B. Reclassification adjustments arise on changes in the revaluation surplus of property, plant and equipment, so the surplus is recycled to profit or loss when the related asset is derecognised C. The amount of income tax relating to each item of OCI must be disclosed either in the statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income or in the notes D. Items of OCI may be presented either net of related tax effects or before related tax effects with one aggregate amount shown for the related income tax
