M8 Chapter 6: Auditing IT Systems — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives

  • Explain how the complexities of computerised systems (including IT security risks) affect the audit of financial statements (HKSA 315 (Revised 2019), para. 25).
  • Distinguish between general IT controls and application controls (input, processing, output, master file controls) and evaluate their impact on evidence accumulation.
  • Distinguish manual controls from automated controls and explain when the auditor may audit only manual controls to assess control risk.
  • Illustrate computer-assisted auditing techniques (CAATs): the test data approach, audit software and parallel simulation.
  • Describe the auditor's concerns when processing is outsourced to a service bureau (Type 1 / Type 2 reports) and the auditor's role in cybersecurity.

必背考點

A. Procedures（審計程序）

1. Understanding the IT system — HKSA 315 (Revised 2019), para. 25 - The auditor MUST obtain an understanding of the entity's information system and communication relevant to the preparation of the financial statements, through risk assessment procedures: - How transactions are initiated, recorded, processed, corrected, incorporated into the general ledger and reported in the financial statements; - The accounting records, specific accounts and supporting records relating to information flows; - The financial reporting process used to prepare the FS (including disclosures); - The entity's resources, including the IT environment; - How the entity communicates significant financial reporting matters (within the entity; management ↔ TCWG; external parties); - Evaluate whether the information system and communication appropriately support preparation of the FS per the applicable financial reporting framework.

2. Risk assessment procedures over general controls（防止未授權改動系統） LP Apply & Analyse 3 示範程序： - Observe the client's procedures to confirm adequate segregation of duties; - Review the log of amendments for evidence changes were made by an appropriate individual; - Attempt to log in using credentials of a non-finance staff member (system should reject access); - Check anti-virus systems are in place and up to date; - Review the backup policy for the accounting system; - Inspect logs of access into the accounting system.

3. Procedures over backup and recovery（business continuity） - Inquire of management / TCWG and inspect backup procedures — key check: backups taken off-site; - Inspect disaster recovery procedures; - Inspect insurance contracts and maintenance agreements; - Inspect equipment protection against hazards (fire, flood).

4. Testing manual controls - Manual controls = operated by employees (e.g. approvals, reconciliations, follow-up of reconciling items, manual data entry controls such as pre-numbering source documents). - Manual controls are inherently riskier than automated controls (human error / override) → auditor selects a sample of transactions to confirm the control operated as expected; inconsistent operation → deviation/exception. - Auditor may choose to audit only manual controls to assess control risk where appropriate (HKSA 315 (Revised 2019), Appendix 5, para. 1 acknowledges the mix of manual and automated elements varies with the entity's use of IT).

B. Evidence（審計證據 — CAATs）

1. CAATs 總論 - CAATs use computers to perform audit procedures but do NOT change the overall scope or objective of the audit. - Two common types: audit software and test data. - Uses: tests of details on transactions/balances; analytical review (e.g. gross profit margins vs prior year); designing sample sizes & sampling; reperforming calculations; tests of controls over general and application controls. - Advantages: time efficiency; test larger sample sizes; trace key controls/processes otherwise impossible manually; cost-effective long-term (if client doesn't change IT system). - Disadvantages: initial setup cost & staff training time; possible incompatibility with client's system; risk of data corruption / loss of client's live data.

2. Test data（測試數據） - Used for tests of controls (compliance tests): auditor enters a sample of transactions into the client's system and compares results with pre-determined results. - Examples: process a sale taking a customer over its credit limit → system should reject; input a non-existent customer account number → system should reject; enter an inappropriately large bonus for junior staff → payroll should flag exception; create a dummy employee to test mandatory fields. - Inherent problems (6.5.1.1): - Risk of corrupting the client's system / difficulty removing test data → use copy files instead of live data (must be satisfied copy files are genuine and virus-free); - Tests the system only at a specific point in time — processing at other times of the period is not covered.

3. Audit software（審計軟件） - Used to carry out substantive procedures to detect material misstatement (general or customised packages). - Uses: analytical procedures (AR/AP days, year-on-year comparisons); designing & extracting samples; reperforming calculations (e.g. recasting inventory valuation report); checking file data (e.g. invoice prices agree to sales master file); preparing confirmation letters; scanning files for large/unusual items and completeness of document ranges. - Reduces detection risk (gives more resources to analyse data and investigate unusual items). ⚠️ It does NOT reduce inherent risk or control risk — those are not under the auditor's control. - Consider requesting client permission before extracting data; may need to reference data extraction in the engagement letter.

4. Parallel simulation（平行模擬） - Auditor creates programmed procedures to duplicate part of the client's IT system; takes live data processed by the client's system and reprocesses it through the auditor's version; compares outputs. - Advantages: facilitates substantive testing of balances; evidence on controls in application programs; tests accuracy of large volumes; focuses attention on differences. - Disadvantage: expensive to implement, especially for complex calculations.

5. PCs as an audit tool - Speed and accuracy; embedded checks identify inordinate transactions/balances; detect calculation errors. - ⚠️ Using PCs does NOT remove the need for normal audit procedures (e.g. verifying receivables valuation, payables completeness still required).

C. Ethics（專業道德 — Internal Audit 角色）

  • Internal audit (IA) is part of the entity's system of internal control and may evaluate IT controls.
  • IA CANNOT implement internal controls — doing so creates a self-review threat (reviewing their own work) and destroys independence/objectivity. IA can only recommend improvements and test operating effectiveness of controls implemented by management.
  • External auditors may choose to rely on IA's work (see Chapter 4 for the detailed criteria).

D. Report modifications（報告修改 — IT 失效嘅後果）

  • Ineffective general controls → application controls unlikely to be effective → auditor places little/no reliance on the IT system → adopt a more substantive approach (risk of material misstatement likely high). 呢個係 extent of testing 嘅問題，唔係自動 modify opinion。
  • Inadequate backup / loss of accounting data → auditor may be unable to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence → if so serious that the auditor cannot form an opinion → disclaimer of opinion (see Chapter 9).
  • Disaster without continuity controls may threaten going concern → disclosure required; if FS prepared on going concern basis but basis inappropriate → modified opinion (see Chapter 9).
  • Cybersecurity breach / unauthorised access may cause material misstatement and is a fraud risk factor — but does NOT automatically lead to a disclaimer; unmodified opinion still possible if reasonable assurance obtained.

易混淆概念對比

Table 1: General IT Controls vs Application Controls

General IT Controls Application Controls
Definition Controls over the environment within which the computer system is developed, maintained and operated Controls operating within the business application to ensure data is processed completely, accurately, validly and timely
Scope Pervasive — affect ALL application systems Specific to individual transaction processing (e.g. sales, payroll, purchases)
Examples Program change control; logical access controls (passwords, user ID); backup & recovery; systems development controls; IT operations controls; physical access to data centre Batch total checks; sequence checks; arithmetic checks; range checks; existence checks; authorisation checks; exception reporting; check digit / hash total verification
LP reference HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) App. 5 para. 18 (risks from IT); App. 6 para. 2 (examples: access management, change management, IT operations) LP §6.4 (input / processing / output / master file controls)
點樣分辨 問自己：呢個控制係咪「成個 IT 環境」都受影響？係 → general。只係針對某個 application 嘅 data input/processing/output → application 考試伏位：考官審計順序係 先 general 後 application —— general controls ineffective ⇒ application controls generally ineffective ⇒ more substantive approach。Password/user ID 屬 general (logical access)，唔好當 application control

Table 2: Test Data vs Audit Software vs Parallel Simulation

Test Data Audit Software Parallel Simulation
Purpose Tests of controls (compliance tests) Substantive procedures (detect material misstatement) Both: substantive testing + evidence on application controls
Mechanism Auditor inputs fictitious transactions into client's system; compares results with pre-determined results Auditor's own software interrogates client's real data files (analyse, sample, recalculate) Auditor reprocesses client's live data through auditor's replica of part of the system; compares outputs
Data used Auditor-created data (valid + invalid) Client's actual data Client's live data, run through both systems
Risk reduced / key issue Does not directly reduce detection risk; tests whether controls operate Reduces detection risk (larger coverage, faster analysis) Expensive to implement; best for complex calculations
點樣分辨 口訣：Test data 測 control，audit software 做 substantive。見到「input dummy/fictitious data to see if system rejects」= test data；見到「extract / recalculate / scan client's file」= audit software；見到「reprocess live data through auditor's version & compare」= parallel simulation 考試伏位：(1) audit software reduces detection risk only，唔可以話佢 reduce inherent/control risk；(2) test data 兩大 inherent problems = corrupt client data + point-in-time only；(3) CAATs 唔會改變 audit scope/objective

Phase 2 閃卡

Flashcard 1 / 共 8 張
Question

According to HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para. 25, what must the auditor understand about the entity's information system relevant to financial reporting?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：呢段係 risk assessment 嘅基本功——核數師要由「交易點樣入系統、點樣流到落 FS」成條鏈都要明白，仲要評估個系統撐唔撐到份報表。考試常見伏位係以為淨係要識 IT，其實連 communication 同 evaluation 都係 para. 25 要求。

Flashcard 2 / 共 8 張
Question

Define "general IT controls" and give FOUR examples.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：General controls 係「大環境」嘅控制，唔係針對個別交易。記住審計次序：general controls 差 → application controls 都靠唔住 → 唯有做多啲 substantive procedures。

Flashcard 3 / 共 8 張
Question

What are the FOUR categories of application controls, and what is each designed to ensure?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：跟住數據流向記：Input → Processing → Output，外加 master file（standing data 如薪金率、客戶 credit limit）。Master file 俾人亂改 = 長期性錯誤，係 auditor 嘅 major concern。

Flashcard 4 / 共 8 張
Question

Distinguish between manual controls and automated controls. Which is inherently riskier and why?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：記住「人會攰、會呃、會撳錯掣；機器唔會」——但機器可以俾人 override，所以兩者都要 test。Manual control 要靠 sample testing 去 confirm 有持續運作。

Flashcard 5 / 共 8 張
Question

What is the "test data" approach, and what are its TWO inherent problems?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：Test data = 擺啲假交易落客個系統度，睇佢擋唔擋到。兩個弱點：污糟咗人哋啲 live data 點算（所以用 copy files）；同埋你只係測嗰一刻，唔代表成年都 work。

Flashcard 6 / 共 8 張
Question

List FIVE procedures an auditor can perform using audit software, and state which component of audit risk it reduces.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：Audit software 係幫你「查數」——搵 sample、重計、做 ratio。考試經典伏位：佢 reduce detection risk，唔係 inherent/control risk（RoMM = IR × CR，呢啲係 client 嘅嘢，auditor 控制唔到）。

Flashcard 7 / 共 8 張
Question

What are a Type 1 report and a Type 2 report in the context of a service bureau (third-party computer processing company)?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：口訣「Type 1 = 設計，Type 2 = 設計 + 運作成效」。Client 外咗包 IT 俾人，auditor 唔可以求其信——要攞 service auditor report 或者直接同 third party 傾。

Flashcard 8 / 共 8 張
Question

Explain the audit consequences if a client has inadequate backup and recovery procedures and accounting data is irrecoverably lost.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：數據冇咗 = 冇證據 = 出唔到意見 → disclaimer。呢個係 evidence insufficiency 嘅極端例子。但記住：backup 不足本身唔會即刻觸發 disclaimer，要睇實際係咪失去咗證據。

本章急救詞彙

EN Term 繁中白話 English Model Sentence
General IT controls 籠統嘅 IT 環境控制，影響晒所有應用系統 "General IT controls include program change control, logical access controls, and backup and recovery controls."
Application controls 針對個別應用系統嘅輸入/處理/輸出控制 "Batch total checks are application controls that ensure supplier invoices are input completely and accurately."
Logical access control 用 user ID + password 限制邊個可以入邊個範圍 "A logical access control requires user identification, checks authenticity, and restricts access to authorised areas only."
CAATs (computer-assisted auditing techniques) 用電腦幫手做審計程序嘅技術 "CAATs do not change the overall scope or objective of the audit."
