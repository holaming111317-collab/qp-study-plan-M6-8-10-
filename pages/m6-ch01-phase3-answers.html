M6 Chapter 1: Financial Reporting Framework — Phase 3 Answers

Q1 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): Comparability is the goal: information is more useful if it can be compared with similar information about other entities and about the same entity for another period or date. Consistency — the use of the same methods for the same items from period to period — is related to, but different from, comparability; it helps achieve the goal of comparability. Both comparability and consistency relate to the enhancing qualitative characteristics, not the fundamental ones.

解題思路（繁中）: - 呢題考 goal vs means 嘅層次關係：Conceptual Framework 明確講 comparability 係 goal，consistency 係幫手達成 goal 嘅工具（例如同一折舊方法年年用，數字先可比較）。 - Option A 掉轉咗主客關係——將 consistency 當 goal，係典型 reversal trap。 - Option B 係 uniformity trap：comparability ≠ uniformity，「like things look alike, unlike things look different」，強迫所有公司用一模一樣方法反而降低可比性。 - Option D 錯喺分類：comparability 同 consistency 都唔係 fundamental qualitative characteristics（fundamental 只得 relevance 同 faithful representation），而且 enhancing characteristics 唔可以單獨令資訊 useful。 - 考試伏位：見到「uniformity」「一致方法令報表完全一樣」呢類字眼，即刻諗起 comparability ≠ uniformity。

Rule Check: HKFRS CF 2.24–2.29

Common Trap（繁中）: 考生常將 consistency 誤當成 comparability 嘅同義詞或者上位概念。記住：consistency 係同一間公司跨期用同一方法；comparability 仲包括跨公司比較。MCQ 好鍾意將兩者角色對調做 distractor。

Q2 — Answer: A

Model Answer (EN): An asset is a present economic resource controlled by the entity as a result of past events, where an economic resource is a right that has the potential to produce economic benefits. Option A: the signed lease gives Verdant Limited a right to use the machine; the machine has the potential to produce economic benefits (used in juice production); and Verdant Limited has control (the present ability to direct how and when the machine is used and to obtain the benefits) — all three elements are met, so it is an asset (a right-of-use type resource), even without legal ownership.

解題思路（繁中）: - Asset 定義三大件：right + potential to produce economic benefits + control，仲要係 past event 產生嘅 present resource。A 全中：簽咗租約（past event）→ 有使用權（right）→ 機器可以榨汁生財（potential benefits）→ 公司決定點用（control）。冇 legal ownership 唔緊要，使用權本身就係 asset。 - Option B：員工服務 fail control test——baristas 可以隨時辭職，公司控制唔到，所以唔係 asset（LP 原文例子）。 - Option C：呢個係 executory contract（雙方都未履行），只係交換經濟資源嘅權利義務組合，未產生 asset/liability。 - Option D：customer loyalty 唔係公司可以 control 嘅 right——顧客嚟唔嚟幫襯由佢哋決定，所以唔符合定義。 - 考試伏位：唔好以為「有所有權先係 asset」；亦唔好以為「預期帶來利益」就夠——必須係 controlled right。

Rule Check: HKFRS CF 4.3–4.25（asset definition; executory contracts: HKFRS CF 4.56–4.58）

Common Trap（繁中）: 最大陷阱係 B 同 D——「好有價值嘅嘢」唔等於 asset，關鍵永遠係 control。記住 LP 金句：right to receive employee services is not an asset because the entity lacks control（員工可以隨時走）。

Q3 — Answer: D

Model Answer (EN): Value in use is the discounted cash flows an entity expects to receive from using and disposing of an asset. It is an exit value (like fair value) but is based on entity-specific assumptions, whereas fair value reflects the perspective of market participants. Historical cost and current cost are entry values (prices in the market in which the entity acquires the asset or incurs the liability).

解題思路（繁中）: - 題目有兩個篩選條件：(1) exit value；(2) entity-specific 而非 market participants 角度。兩個條件同時符合嘅只有 value in use。 - Option A historical cost：entry value（取得時嘅交易價），出局。 - Option B current cost：都係 entry value（measurement date 買同等資產嘅價），出局。 - Option C fair value：係 exit value 冇錯（"price received to sell…in an orderly transaction"），但佢反映 market participants 角度，唔係 entity-specific，所以唔係最佳答案——呢個係本题核心陷阱。 - Option D value in use：exit value + entity-specific discounted cash flows，全中。 - 順帶背埋：entry values 計 transaction costs；fair value 同 value in use 唔計 transaction costs。

Rule Check: HKFRS CF 6.17–6.20（value in use）；HKFRS CF 6.12–6.16（fair value）；HKFRS CF 6.4–6.9、6.21–6.22（entry values）

Common Trap（繁中）: 考生成日記住「fair value＝exit value」就秒選 C，忽略咗題目第二個條件「entity-specific」。雙條件題要兩個都 check 先好揀。另一常見混淆：以為 value in use 係 entry value 或者要加 transaction costs——兩樣都錯。

Q4 — Answer: HK$500,000

Model Answer (EN): Under the financial concept of capital, capital is regarded as the net assets (equity) of the entity, and profit is earned if the financial (money) amount of net assets has increased during the period, excluding any distributions to and contributions from the owners.

Workings:

HK$
Net assets at 31 December 20X8 5,600,000
Less: Net assets at 1 January 20X8 (4,800,000)
Increase in net assets 800,000
Less: Contributions from owners (share issue) (500,000)
Add: Distributions to owners (dividends paid) 200,000
Profit for the year 500,000

解題思路（繁中）: - Financial capital maintenance 下，profit = 期末 net assets − 期初 net assets，再 剔除同業主之間嘅往來（注資要扣返、派息要加返）。 - 計算：5,600,000 − 4,800,000 = 800,000（淨資產升幅）；發新股係 contribution，唔係 income → 減 500,000；派息係 distribution，唔係 expense → 加返 200,000。Profit = 800,000 − 500,000 + 200,000 = HK$500,000。 - 常見錯法對應：(i) 直接答 800,000（完全唔剔除股東往來）；(ii) 答 300,000（加咗股息當 expense 扣，方向掉轉）；(iii) 答 1,100,000（將發股當收入加埋）。 - 呢題同時暗考 income/expense 定義：equity holders 嘅 contributions 同 distributions 唔係 income/expense，係直接喺 equity 出入。

Rule Check: HKFRS CF 8.3（concepts of capital and capital maintenance）；HKFRS CF 4.68–4.69（income/expenses exclude contributions from and distributions to holders of equity claims）

Common Trap（繁中）: 最大伏位係 dividend 嘅方向——股息令資產（cash）減少，但佢 唔係 expense（定義明確剔除 distributions to equity holders），所以計 profit 時要 加返。記住口訣：「注資扣、派息加」。

Q5 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): Under the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) s.359 and the HK SME-FRF size tests, a small private company must not exceed any two of: (i) total annual revenue of HK$100 million; (ii) total assets of HK$100 million; (iii) 100 employees. Harbour View Limited met the revenue test (HK$96m / HK$98m ≤ HK$100m) and the employee test (92 / 95 ≤ 100) in both 20X7 and 20X8 — i.e. two of the three tests for two consecutive years. No shareholders' approval is required for a small private company, so it may adopt the simplified reporting exemption for the year ended 31 December 20X9.

解題思路（繁中）: - 逐步分析：先查公司類型（private company ✓）→ 查 ineligible business（冇 banking/insurance/SFO Part V ✓）→ 查 size test。 - 三年數據：revenue ≤ HK$100m（✓ ✓）；total assets ≤ HK$100m（✗ ✗，兩年都超）；employees ≤ 100（✓ ✓）→ 三項中符合兩項，達到 small private company 嘅「唔超過任何兩項」要求，且連續兩年符合（two-year rule），20X9 可用 SME-FRF。 - Small private company 唔需要 shareholders' approval——題目話冇攞任何 resolution，唔影響資格。 - Option A 錯：佢用 HK$200m 嘅 larger 'eligible' 門檻嚟套，但公司已經符合更細嘅 small private company 門檻；而且 larger eligible 先要 75% approval，呢度唔適用。 - Option C 錯：呢個係「三項全過」嘅常見誤解——規則係 2 of 3，assets 超標唔會一票否決。 - Option D 錯：將 larger 'eligible' private company 嘅 approval 要求（≥75% + 無人反對）錯套落 small private company 度。

Rule Check: Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) s.359；HK SME-FRF para. 28–43（size tests and shareholders' approval）

Common Trap（繁中）: 三大伏位：(1) 以為三項 size tests 要全過（其實 2 of 3）；(2) 將 small private company 同 larger 'eligible' private company 嘅股東批准要求調亂（細嘅唔使批、大嘅要 75%＋其餘無人反對）；(3) 唔記得 two consecutive years 先可以首次採用。呢啲 boundary/threshold 題係 QP MCQ 常客，數字要逐項對照門檻表。
