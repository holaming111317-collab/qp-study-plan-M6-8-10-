M6 Chapter 8: Borrowing Costs — Phase 3 Answers

Q1 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): B is correct. Under HKAS 23.5, a qualifying asset is an asset that necessarily takes a substantial period of time to get ready for its intended use or sale. A cruise terminal taking 30 months to construct satisfies this definition (manufacturing plants and similar long-construction assets are listed as possible qualifying assets in HKAS 23.7). A is not qualifying: inventories manufactured in large quantities on a repetitive basis over a short period are excluded (HKAS 23.4(b)/23.7). C is not qualifying: assets ready for their intended use when acquired are excluded. D is not qualifying: financial assets are explicitly excluded.

解題思路（繁中）: 考「qualifying asset」定義。A 係大量重複生產、兩日搞掂嘅 inventory —— HKAS 23 明確排除，唔使因為佢係 inventory 就以為可以資本化。C 買返嚟即刻用得 → 「ready for intended use when acquired」唔係 qualifying asset。D 係 financial asset，直接排除。B 起 30 個月，明顯係 substantial period。考試伏位：唔好以為「inventory 一定係/一定唔係」——關鍵係生產期長短同埋係咪大量重複製造。

Rule Check: HKAS 23.5, HKAS 23.7, HKAS 23.4(b)

Common Trap（繁中）: 見到「inventory」就諗起 HKAS 23.7 話 inventories 可以係 qualifying assets，但忘記咗「manufactured in large quantities on a repetitive basis / short time period」嘅排除條款。

Q2 — Answer: D

Model Answer (EN): D is correct. Under HKAS 23.17, capitalisation commences on the date when the entity first meets ALL three conditions: (1) expenditures for the asset are incurred; (2) borrowing costs are incurred; and (3) activities necessary to prepare the asset for its intended use or sale are undertaken. Borrowing costs began on 1 February 20X8, expenditures on 1 April 20X8, and preparation activities on 1 May 20X8. All three are first simultaneously met on 1 May 20X8. Note that obtaining development permits counts as a preparatory activity, but merely holding the asset (e.g. land-banking) does not.

解題思路（繁中）: 三個條件「齊腳」先可以開始，所以 commencement date = 三個日子入面最遲嗰個。A（1 Jan）純粹干擾，同年初無關；B（1 Feb）只有 borrowing costs，未找數未動工；C（1 Apr）有 borrowing costs + expenditures，但 activities 未開始；D（1 May）三樣齊晒。考試伏位：題目講明拎 permits 都計 activities（technical/administrative work），但如果公司只係揸住塊地乜都唔做（land-banking），就唔計。

Rule Check: HKAS 23.17

Common Trap（繁中）: 以為借錢嗰日（1 Feb）就可以開始資本化 —— 呢個係 past paper 反覆測試嘅弱點（commencement of capitalisation 被 panelist 點名為常錯位）。

Q3 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): Capitalised = HK$40,000,000 × 6.00% × 7/12 = HK$1,400,000 (1 June – 31 December 20X8).

Workings: - Commencement date = 1 June 20X8 (expenditures incurred, borrowing costs incurred, and activities underway all first met on this date). - Capitalisation period in the year = 7 months (June to December). - Interest from 1 April to 31 May (2 months) = HK$40m × 6.00% × 2/12 = HK$400,000 → expensed in profit or loss. - Since the funds were borrowed specifically for the hub and no surplus funds were invested, the actual borrowing costs incurred during the capitalisation period are capitalised in full (HKAS 23.12).

解題思路（繁中）: A（HK$1,800,000）= 成個借款期 9 個月（Apr–Dec）全數資本化 —— 錯在 4、5 月未動工未找數，未過 commencement date，嗰兩個月利息要 expense。C（HK$1,200,000）= 只計 6 個月，數錯月份。D（HK$2,400,000）= 成 12 個月全年度利息，完全無視 drawdown 同 commencement 都係年中。正確係 B：由 1 June commencement date 起計 7 個月。考試伏位：specific borrowing 如果有 temporarily invested 嘅 surplus funds，投資收入要扣減資本化金額（本题話明冇投資，所以唔使扣）。

Rule Check: HKAS 23.12, HKAS 23.17

Common Trap（繁中）: 「借錢日 = 資本化開始日」嘅謬誤；仲有月份數錯（Jun–Dec 係 7 個月唔係 6 個月 —— 用 12 − 5 嚟數）。

Q4 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): Capitalised = Expenditures × capitalisation rate × time apportionment = HK$24,000,000 × 5.60% × 5/12 = HK$560,000.

Workings (HKAS 23.14 — capitalisation rate = weighted average of general borrowings outstanding, excluding specific borrowings):

Borrowing Rate Weight Contribution
HK$60m bank loan 5.00% 60/150 2.00%
HK$90m bonds 6.00% 90/150 3.60%
Total HK$150m 5.60%
  • HK$24m × 5.60% × 5/12 = HK$560,000.
  • Cap check: capitalised HK$560,000 < total borrowing costs incurred (HK$60m × 5% + HK$90m × 6% = HK$8,400,000) — cap not breached.

解題思路（繁中）: A（HK$500,000）= 只用 5.00% 最低利率 —— 錯，一般借款要用 weighted average。B（HK$550,000）= 用 simple average (5%+6%)/2 = 5.50% —— 錯，兩筆借款本金唔同，一定要按金額加權。D（HK$1,344,000）= 5.60% 乘足 12 個月，冇按 5 個月 pro-rate —— 錯。正確 C：HK$24m × 5.60% × 5/12。考試伏位：(1) weighted average 唔係 simple average；(2) 記住乘月份比例；(3) 記住上限規則 —— 資本化金額唔可以超過期內實際總借貸成本。

Rule Check: HKAS 23.14

Common Trap（繁中）: 用 simple average 利率（唔加權）或者唔做 time apportionment —— 兩者都係 distractor 嘅來源，亦係真實考生常犯錯誤。

Q5 — Answer: D

Model Answer (EN):

Dr  Refrigerated warehouse           450,000
Dr  Interest expense (P/L)         6,750,000
    Cr  Cash                                  7,200,000
(Recognition of borrowing costs: capitalised portion added to the cost of the
qualifying asset; the remainder expensed in profit or loss)

Total Dr (7,200,000) = Total Cr (7,200,000). The entry mirrors the LP treatment for general borrowings: only the eligible amount (expenditures × capitalisation rate, time-apportioned) is debited to the asset; all remaining borrowing costs are expensed (HKAS 23.8, 23.14).

解題思路（繁中）: A 將全部 HK$7.2m 入 expense —— 錯，直接歸屬 qualifying asset 嘅部分必須資本化（唔係 optional）。B 將全部利息資本化 —— 錯，只有合資格嘅 HK$450k 先可以入資產成本，其餘係一般融資成本。C 只入咗資本化部分，完全冇記錄其餘 HK$6.75m 利息 —— 錯，entry 唔平衡之餘仲漏咗 expense。D 正確：Dr 資產 450k + Dr Interest expense 6,750k = Cr Cash 7,200k，借貸平衡。考試伏位：CBE 格式可能用 journal-entry grid 考呢種 entry —— 記住「Dr asset（合資格部分）+ Dr Interest expense（剩餘部分）/ Cr Cash（總額）」呢個三段式結構。

Rule Check: HKAS 23.8, HKAS 23.14

Common Trap（繁中）: 以為資本化係 accounting policy choice（其實 HKAS 23 強制資本化直接歸屬嘅 borrowing costs）；或者整個 entry 得一半，漏記 expensed 部分。
