M10 Chapter 9: Internal Administration of a Company — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives (3–5, English, action verbs)

  1. Apply the accounting records, annual financial statements and simplified reporting requirements under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) ('CO') to a given company (ss.361–380, 383, 387–391).
  2. Analyse the appointment, duties, rights, liabilities and cessation of office of auditors under the CO (ss.393–430) and at common law.
  3. Distinguish the decision-making powers of members in general meetings from those of the board of directors, and apply the procedural rules on notices, quorum, proxies, resolutions and written resolutions (ss.548–622).
  4. Classify the different types of directors (de jure, de facto, shadow, alternate, nominee, reserve, ED/NED/INED) and apply the rules on appointment, board meeting procedures, vacation of office and disqualification (CO Part 10; CWUMPO (Cap. 32) Part IVA).
  5. Evaluate the validity of company decisions using the irregularity principle and the inspection rights of directors and members (ss.374–378, 620, 740).

必背考點

除另有註明外，本章所有 section 編號均指 Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) ('CO')（LP 原文：「Unless otherwise stated, all references to statutory provisions herein are made to the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)」）。

A. Relevant Ordinances — Audit & Accounts（會計紀錄與財務報表）

Topic Rule Citation
Accounting records 標準 Records must be sufficient (a) to show and explain the company's transactions; (b) to disclose with reasonable accuracy, at any time, the company's financial position and financial performance; (c) to enable the directors to ensure the financial statements comply with the CO. Must contain daily entries of all sums received/expended and a record of assets and liabilities. s.373(2), s.373(3)
存放地點與披露間隔 Kept at registered office or any place approved by directors; must disclose financial position at intervals of not more than 6 months. s.374(1), s.374(3)
形式與保存期 Hard copy or electronic form; retained for 7 years. s.376(2), s.377
刑事責任 Director failing to take all reasonable steps to ensure proper accounting records commits a criminal offence. ss.373(5)–(7)
每年編製文件 Directors must prepare audited financial statements + directors' report + auditor's report each financial year. s.379
Accounting reference period First period: incorporation date → last day of the month of the anniversary of incorporation ('primary accounting reference date'); directors may specify a date within 18 months of incorporation; financial year cannot be lengthened beyond 18 months nor changed more than once every 5 years. ss.369(5)(a)–(b); ss.371(5)–(6)
True and fair view Annual financial statements must give a true and fair view; detailed requirements per HKFRS issued by HKICPA (specified body under Companies (Accounting Standards) (Prescribed Body) Regulation (Cap. 622C) s.2). s.380
財務報表內容 (1) Statement of profit or loss and comprehensive income; (2) Statement of financial position — signed by two directors; (3) Directors' emolument notes; (4) Consolidated FS for holding companies. s.387(1); s.383(1); s.379(2)
Directors' report Narrative supplement; explains principal activities and matters material for members' appreciation of the company's affairs; must be approved by directors and signed by a director or the company secretary. s.388(1); s.390; s.390(2)(a); Sch. 5; s.391(1); Companies (Directors' Report) Regulation (Cap. 622D)
Simplified reporting — small private company Any 2 of 3: total revenue ≤ HK$100m; total assets ≤ HK$100m; average employees ≤ 100. Non-qualifying for 2 consecutive years → disqualified in the following financial year. s.361; ss.359(3)–(4); s.363 & Sch. 3 (guarantee companies)
Simplified reporting — eligible private company Election by ≥ 75% of members' votes with no veto (or all members' written approval), plus any 2 of 3: revenue ≤ HK$200m; assets ≤ HK$200m; employees ≤ 100. s.360; s.359(1)(b); Sch. 3
永不合資格公司 Banking (Banking Ordinance (Cap. 155)); licensed corporations under SFO (Cap. 571) Part V; insurance business (other than solely as agent); business of accepting loans of money. ss.359(4)(a)–(c)
Annual return Private company delivers annual return within 42 days after the company's return date (= anniversary of incorporation); specified form NAR1; failure = offence and fine. ss.662(1), 662(2), 662(6); s.664 & Sch. 6
Publication Private companies: no general duty to publish FS; public/guarantee companies lodge FS with annual returns → open to public inspection. Sch. 6; s.7; s.45; s.664
送交 members 時間 Reporting documents (FS + directors' report + auditor's report) sent to members at least 21 days before the AGM; electronic means allowed with members' agreement; summary financial report possible with agreement. s.429; s.831; s.442

B. Relevant Ordinances — Inspection Rights（查閱權）

Who Rule Citation
Directors (statute) Accounting records open to inspection by directors at all times without charge; directors entitled to make copies; may apply to court for assistance. Right is individual, exercisable by executive and non-executive directors alike. s.374(1)(b); s.375; s.378
Directors (common law) Wider than statute: extends to corporate material, records, accounts, information and any documents reasonably assisting the director in carrying out duties. Tsai Shao Chung v Asia Television Ltd (2012) 4 HKLRD 52 (CA)
Members (statute) Right to inspect (free) and obtain copies (prescribed fee) of: minutes of general meetings; register of members; register of directors and company secretaries; register of debenture holders; register of charges. Also right to receive reporting documents (electronically per s.833). s.620; s.631; ss.642, 649; s.310; s.355; s.831
Members (common law) No general right to inspect books of account or other company documents (reflection of separate legal entity). Model Articles: wider member inspection subject to directors'/GM approval. LP §9.1.4
Members — court order Application requires ≥ 2.5% of voting rights OR ≥ 5 members; court orders only if satisfied the application is (1) in good faith and (2) for a proper purpose (must relate to applicant's status as shareholder; dominant purpose test — secondary improper purpose irrelevant). Covers any 'record or document' of the company but not subsidiaries' records (save those in the company's possession). s.740; s.740(6); Wong Kar Gee Mimi (2011) 5 HKLRD 241; Re Lehman Brown Ltd (2011) 5 HKLRD 668

C. Relevant Ordinances — Auditors（核數師）

Topic Rule Citation
Qualification Must be a CPA holding a practising certificate under the Professional Accountants Ordinance (Cap. 50) ('PAO'); a CPA firm can be appointed under its firm name (all partners deemed appointed); a corporate practice may be appointed; employees of the company cannot be appointed (conflict of interest). s.393; PAO s.29(2); PAO s.28A(2); s.399; s.393(2)
Appointment Auditor must be appointed for each financial year; first auditor appointed by directors before first AGM; members reappoint/appoint at first AGM — refreshed annually; if AGM dispensed with, existing auditor deemed re-appointed. s.394; s.396; s.403
Casual vacancy Directors may fill; if directors fail within 1 month, members may appoint by resolution at a GM. s.397; s.395(2); s.396(1); s.396(3)
Auditor's report 內容 Opinion on: (1) true and fair view; (2) consistency of directors' report with FS; (3) adequacy of accounting records; (4) agreement of FS with records. Duty to investigate items (3)–(4). Report signed by auditor; sent to members and laid before AGM. s.405; s.406(1)(b); s.406(2); s.407(2); s.407(1); s.409; s.430; s.429
Auditor's rights Access to accounting records and information/explanations from officers (non-compliance = criminal offence unless not reasonably practicable); attend and speak at any general meeting; information from subsidiary undertakings; outgoing auditor may pass information to incoming auditor in good faith. s.412(1); ss.413(1)–(2); s.411(1)(a)–(b); s.412(2); s.414
Criminal liability Knowingly or recklessly causing a required statement to be omitted from the auditor's report = offence. Liability depends on whether the auditor actually held the opinion but omitted it — an honestly held (even negligent/erroneous) belief that all information was obtained is not an offence. s.408; s.408(2); s.407(2)(b); s.407(3)
Civil liability (common law) Duty of care in tort and contract (implied term); reasonable care and skill; auditor provides only reasonable assurance — 'not an insurer'; 'a watch-dog, but not a bloodhound'; entitled to rely on management assurances if reasonable; once put on enquiry, must make exhaustive enquiry. Duty owed to the company, not the public at large; no 'trading loss' claims; special proximity (e.g. special audit before acquisition) may create duty to third parties. Re Kingston Cotton Mill Co (No. 2) (1896) 2 Ch 279; Re Thomas Gerrard & Son Ltd (1968) Ch 455; Re London and General Bank (No. 2) (1895) 2 Ch 673; Guang Xin Enterprises Ltd v Kwan Wong Tan & Fong (2002) 2 HKLRD 319; Yue Xiu Finance Co Ltd v Agnew (1995) 2 HKLR 186; The Registrar of HKICPA v Wong Tak Man Stephen (2017) 20 HKCFAR 556
Duty to report Report impropriety/fraud to the company immediately; statutory duty to report to regulators may override confidentiality (e.g. good-faith communication to the SFC immune from civil liability). s.381, Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571)
Indemnities void Articles provisions exempting an auditor from duty of care, or indemnifying an auditor for breach of that duty, are void. s.415(2); s.415(3)
Resignation Written notice deposited at registered office; must give a statement of circumstances (or a statement of no circumstances); may require directors to convene a GM and circulate a cessation statement; right to attend that meeting; court may block circulation if used to secure needless publicity for defamatory matter. s.417; s.424; s.421; s.422(1)(c); s.426(3)
Retirement Term expires without re-appointment; retiring auditor also owes statement of circumstances; right to call meeting / cessation statement. s.416(1)(a); s.425; s.422(2)
Removal Ordinary resolution of members in GM; special notice required and copy sent to the auditor; removal does not deprive auditor of compensation/damages; auditor has cessation statement and attendance rights. s.419; s.419(2); s.419(3); s.420; s.422(3); s.411
Disqualification Auditor who ceases to be eligible/becomes disqualified must immediately cease to act and notify the company in writing within 14 days; failure = criminal offence. s.418; s.418(1)(b); s.418(2)

D. Relevant Ordinances — Members' Meetings & Resolutions

Topic Rule Citation
AGM Required annually after each financial year; for private/guarantee companies within 9 months after the end of the accounting reference period; reporting documents laid before the AGM; members may apply to court to order an AGM on default; AGM not required if all AGM business done by written resolution and documents provided to each member. s.610(1); s.610(1)(a); s.430; ss.610(7)–(8); s.612
GM Any general meeting other than an AGM; convened by directors; members holding ≥ 1/20 (5%) of paid-up voting capital may requisition a GM (company's expense); shareholders may convene where there are insufficient directors. s.565; ss.566–568; s.569
保留予股東嘅事項 Change of name (special resolution); alteration of articles/objects; alteration of max number of shares; allotment of shares; removal of director before expiry of term; payment out of capital for redemption/buy-back; financial assistance for share acquisition. s.107; ss.88, 89; s.88(3); s.141; s.462; ss.257–266; ss.283–289
Residual power (common law) GM acts where (1) the board cannot function (deadlock/failure to act) or (2) ratification of directors' acts in excess of powers is necessary. Re Commonwealth Printing Press Ltd, SCt(HK) (unrep.), HCCW 15/1974, 31 May 1974; Re Mandarin Capital Advisory Ltd (2011) 2 HKLRD 1003
Notice — AGM 21 days' notice; shorter only with all shareholders' consent; articles may require longer. s.571(1)(a)
Notice — GM 14 days' notice; shorter if agreed by a majority representing ≥ 95% of voting rights. s.571(1)(b); s.571(3)
Special notice Notice of intention to move a resolution given ≥ 28 days before the meeting; given to members with the notice of meeting; if impracticable, ≥ 14 days before by newspaper advertisement or other manner allowed by the articles. Required for: ordinary resolution to remove a director (copy to the director) and to remove an auditor (copy to the auditor). s.578(1); s.578(2); s.578(3); s.462; s.419
Chairperson Model Articles: board chairman chairs GMs; failing that, directors present elect one of themselves; without articles provision, any member may be elected by resolution; chairperson acts neutrally; limited adjournment power; declaration of result is conclusive evidence. Art. 40(1), (3); s.586; Art. 42; s.590(1)
Quorum Two members present in person (Model Articles: in person or by proxy); one-member company: sole member suffices; no quorum → meeting invalid. s.585(3); Art. 39(1); s.585(1); Re Alma Spinning Co (Bottomley's case) (1880) 16 Ch D 681
Voting / poll Default = show of hands; poll demandable (under the Model Articles: chairperson, ≥ 2 members present in person or by proxy, or member(s) holding ≥ 5% of total voting rights); articles provisions void if they block a poll demanded by 5 voting members / 5% holders / the chairperson; chairperson must demand a poll if proxies show a different result. Model Articles (LP §9.2.4.3); s.591(2); s.592
Proxy Proxy need not be a member; ordinary vs two-way proxy forms (directors must ensure forms allow instructions); proxy votes on show of hands and poll (but multiple proxies cannot vote on show of hands); revocation effective only if received before commencement of the meeting; member attending in person overrides the proxy. s.596(1); s.601; s.588; s.588(2); s.604(3); s.605; s.606 (corporate representative)
Ordinary resolution Simple majority; default type where the CO/documents do not specify. s.563; s.562(3)
Special resolution ≥ 75% majority; notice must state the text and that it is proposed as a special resolution; required for alteration of constitution, change of name, reduction of capital, winding-up; copy filed with the Registry within 15 days (not for change of name). s.564; s.564(4)(a)–(b); s.88(2); s.107; s.215; s.622
Minutes Records of all GM proceedings kept ≥ 10 years; members inspect free / copy for a fee. s.618(1)(b)–(c); s.618(2); s.620; s.655(7)
Irregularity principle (GM) A decision cannot be questioned if the only defect is a mere informality/irregularity and the intention of the meeting is clear — especially where the result would have been the same. Only the majority can complain unless fraud on the minority or defect of substance. Insufficient notice that is not 'full and frank' → resolutions invalid. Yip Peter v Asian Electronics Ltd (1998) 2 HKC 96; Baillie v Oriental Telephone and Electric Co Ltd (1915) 1 Ch 503
Electronic meetings GM may be held at two or more places using technology enabling members to listen, speak and vote. s.584; Part 18

F. IRAC Application Points（考試答題骨架）

  1. Member seeking company documents: Issue — does a member have inspection rights? Rule — no general common law right; statutory registers only (s.620, s.631, ss.642/649, s.310, s.355); court order under s.740 requires ≥ 2.5% voting rights or ≥ 5 members, plus good faith + proper purpose (dominant purpose test). Application — identify the member's purpose (investigating suspected director misconduct = proper; personal vendetta alone = improper). Conclusion — likely granted/denied.
  2. Removing a director: Issue — valid removal? Rule — s.462 ordinary resolution + special notice (s.462(4), s.578(1): ≥ 28 days) + s.463 right to be heard/representations; cannot be done by written resolution (s.548). Application — check each procedural step against the facts. Conclusion — prima facie invalid if any step missed; consider whether the irregularity principle rescues it (mere informality vs substantive loss of representation rights).
  3. Auditor negligence: Issue — breach of duty of care? Rule — reasonable skill and care; watch-dog not bloodhound (Re Kingston Cotton Mill); entitled to rely on management only if reasonable; once on notice of irregularity, must enquire exhaustively (Re Thomas Gerrard). Application — was the auditor put on enquiry? Was reliance reasonable? Conclusion — liable / not liable.
  4. De facto vs shadow director: Issue — status of a non-appointed influencer. Rule — de facto = assumes to act as director/held out (Re Hydrodan; DCT v Austin); shadow = board accustomed to act on instructions, excluding professional advice (s.2). Application — map the person's actual conduct. Conclusion — de facto → full directors' duties; shadow → not necessarily fiduciary.
  5. Board vs GM irregularity: Board meeting irregularity (e.g. deliberately withheld notice) → void, even if outcome unchanged (Billion Express). GM irregularity → saved by the irregularity principle if mere informality and intention clear (Yip Peter), unless fraud on minority or defect of substance (Baillie).
