M6 Chapter 6: Investment Property — Phase 3 Quiz

Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. … — 超時如實申報

Question 1

Kestrel Holdings Limited (Kestrel), a Hong Kong property company, holds the following four properties. In accordance with HKAS 40, which one of the following properties qualifies as an investment property?

A. A residential block leased by Kestrel as a lessor to its own employees under operating leases at market rates of rent.

B. A vacant retail podium currently held by Kestrel to be leased out to tenants under one or more operating leases.

C. An office building held by Kestrel with a view to disposal in the near future in the ordinary course of its business.

D. A building occupied by Kestrel as its administrative head office.

Question 2

Marina Vista Properties Limited (Marina Vista) purchased a completed office building on 1 March 20X1 to earn rental income under operating leases. In accordance with HKAS 40, Marina Vista classifies the building as an owned investment property. The following amounts were incurred:

Item HK$’000
Purchase price 80,000
Legal fees on the purchase 1,200
Property transfer taxes 3,400
Advertising campaign to attract tenants 500
Abnormal wasted materials caused by a typhoon interrupting renovation 2,000

At what amount should Marina Vista initially measure the investment property?

A. HK$85,100,000 B. HK$86,600,000 C. HK$84,600,000 D. HK$81,200,000

Question 3

Trident Land Limited (Trident) adopts the fair value model under HKAS 40 for all its investment properties. An investment property was purchased for HK$45 million on 1 January 20X0. Its fair value was HK$52 million at 31 December 20X0 and HK$48 million at 31 December 20X1. Depreciation is not charged under Trident's chosen model.

Which accounting entry should Trident record for the year ended 31 December 20X1?

A.

Debit HK$m Credit HK$m
Fair value loss (profit or loss) 4
Investment property 4

B.

Debit HK$m Credit HK$m
Investment property 3
Fair value gain (profit or loss) 3

C.

Debit HK$m Credit HK$m
Fair value loss (other comprehensive income) 4
Investment property 4

D.

Debit HK$m Credit HK$m
Depreciation expense 4
Accumulated depreciation – investment property 4

Question 4

Beacon Hill Properties Limited (Beacon Hill) occupied an office building as its head office until 30 April 20X5, when the company vacated the building and began holding it to earn rentals under operating leases. The building had a cost of HK$90 million, a residual value of HK$6 million and a useful life of 40 years, depreciated on a straight-line basis under the HKAS 16 cost model. At 1 January 20X5, the building had been used for exactly 12 years. Beacon Hill measures its investment properties under the fair value model. The building's fair value on 30 April 20X5 was HK$70 million. The building had never previously been revalued.

In accordance with HKAS 40 and HKAS 16, what amount should be recognised in other comprehensive income on 30 April 20X5 in respect of the transfer from owner-occupied property to investment property?

A. HK$5,200,000 B. HK$5,900,000 C. HK$0 D. HK$7,750,000

Question 5

Coral Bay Development Limited (Coral Bay) adopts the fair value model for its investment properties. It holds a parcel of land carried at HK$40 million at 31 December 20X0. On 1 September 20X1, Coral Bay commenced development of the land with a view to sale in the ordinary course of business. The land's fair value was HK$47 million on 1 September 20X1. At 31 December 20X1, the fair value of the property under development was HK$70 million, and its net realisable value exceeded its cost. Coral Bay's management had already decided in June 20X1 to sell the land, but no development or other action occurred before 1 September 20X1.

In accordance with HKAS 40 and HKAS 2, which of the following correctly states (i) the gain recognised in profit or loss and (ii) the carrying amount of the property in the statement of financial position at 31 December 20X1?

A. (i) Gain of HK$7 million; (ii) HK$70 million B. (i) Gain of HK$30 million; (ii) HK$70 million C. (i) No gain; (ii) HK$47 million D. (i) Gain of HK$7 million; (ii) HK$47 million
