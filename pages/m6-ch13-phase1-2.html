M6 Chapter 13: Accounting Policies, Changes in Accounting Estimates and Errors — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives

  1. Distinguish between accounting policies, accounting estimates and prior period errors in accordance with HKAS 8.
  2. Apply the criteria in HKAS 8 for selecting and changing accounting policies, including mandatory and voluntary changes.
  3. Account for a change in accounting policy retrospectively, a change in accounting estimate prospectively, and a material prior period error by retrospective restatement.
  4. Compute the effect of accounting changes and error corrections on profit or loss, carrying amounts, tax and retained earnings.
  5. Prepare the required note disclosures for changes in accounting policies, changes in accounting estimates and corrections of prior period errors.

必背考點

A. Recognition / Classification — 點樣分三類更改

Definitions (HKAS 8 para. 5) - Accounting policies: the specific principles, bases, conventions, rules and practices applied by an entity in preparing and presenting financial statements. Example: choosing FIFO or weighted average cost formula under HKAS 2; cost model vs revaluation model under HKAS 16. - Accounting estimates: monetary amounts in financial statements that are subject to measurement uncertainty (HKAS 8 para. 32A). Examples: loss allowance for expected credit losses; net realisable value of inventory; warranty provision; fair value of an asset or liability; depreciation expense. - Prior period errors: omissions from, and misstatements in, the entity's financial statements for one or more prior periods arising from a failure to use, or misuse of, reliable information that (a) was available when those financial statements were authorised for issue, and (b) could reasonably be expected to have been obtained and taken into account. Examples: mathematical mistakes, mistakes in applying accounting policies, oversights or misinterpretations of facts, fraud.

When may an accounting policy be changed? (HKAS 8 para. 14) — Only if the change: - is required by a HKFRS (mandatory); or - results in the financial statements providing reliable and more relevant information (voluntary).

NOT changes in accounting policy (HKAS 8 para. 16): - applying a policy for transactions/events/conditions that differ in substance from those previously occurring; and - applying a new policy for transactions/events/conditions that did not occur previously or were immaterial (e.g. previously immaterial leases becoming material → apply HKFRS 16 going forward).

Special case (HKAS 8 para. 17): first-time adoption of the revaluation model under HKAS 16 / HKAS 38 is a change in accounting policy, BUT it is dealt with as a revaluation under HKAS 16 / HKAS 38 — HKAS 8 paras. 19–31 do not apply.

Estimate vs policy boundary rules: - A change in the measurement basis applied is a change in accounting policy, NOT a change in estimate (HKAS 8 para. 35; e.g. FIFO → weighted average). - If it is difficult to distinguish a policy change from an estimate change → treat as a change in accounting estimate (HKAS 8 para. 35). - Revising an estimate because of new information / more experience is NOT a correction of an error (HKAS 8 paras. 32–34): an estimate uses information available at the time; an error is failure to use / misuse of information that was available.

Materiality (HKAS 8 paras. 5, 41; HKAS 1 definition): - HKFRS accounting policies need not be applied when the effect is immaterial. - Financial statements do not comply with HKFRSs if they contain material errors. - Material prior period errors must be corrected retrospectively in the first set of financial statements authorised for issue after discovery.

B. Measurement / Accounting Treatment — Retrospective vs Prospective

Change in accounting policy (HKAS 8 paras. 19–24): - Mandatory change with transitional provisions in the HKFRS → follow those transitional provisions (retrospective or prospective as specified) (HKAS 8 para. 19(a)). - Mandatory change without transitional provisions → apply retrospectively (HKAS 8 para. 19(b)). - Voluntary change → apply retrospectively (HKAS 8 para. 19(b)). - Retrospective application (HKAS 8 paras. 5, 22): apply the new policy as if it had always been applied; adjust (a) the opening balance of each affected component of equity (e.g. retained earnings) for the earliest prior period presented, and (b) the other comparative amounts disclosed for each prior period presented. - Limitation: retrospective application only to the extent practicable (HKAS 8 paras. 23–24).

Change in accounting estimate (HKAS 8 paras. 36–38): - Recognise prospectively — apply from the date of the change; never adjust prior periods. - Effect goes to profit or loss in: (a) the period of change only, if it affects only that period (e.g. annual warranty / ECL allowance); or (b) the period of change and future periods, if it affects both (e.g. revised useful life → depreciation for current + remaining years). - To the extent the change affects assets/liabilities/equity, adjust the carrying amount of the related item in the period of change (HKAS 8 para. 37).

Correction of prior period error (HKAS 8 paras. 42–46): - Correct by retrospective restatement in the first set of financial statements issued after discovery: - restate the comparative amounts for the prior period(s) presented in which the error occurred; or - if the error occurred before the earliest prior period presented → restate the opening balances of assets, liabilities and equity for the earliest prior period presented. - Purpose: current period financial statements are presented as if the error never occurred. - Remember the tax effect: restate income tax expense/payable at the applicable rate (LP examples use HK profits tax rate 16.5%).

C. Disclosure Requirements

Change in accounting policy — initial application of a HKFRS (HKAS 8 para. 28): - the title of the HKFRS; - that the change is made in accordance with its transitional provisions (if any); - the nature of the change; - a description of the transitional provisions and those that might affect future periods (if applicable); - for the current period and each prior period presented, to the extent practicable, the amount of the adjustment for each financial statement line item affected and (if HKAS 33 applies) for basic and diluted EPS; - the amount of the adjustment relating to periods before those presented, to the extent practicable.

New HKFRS issued but not yet effective (HKAS 8 para. 30): disclose that fact + known or reasonably estimable information relevant to assessing the possible impact on initial application.

Change in accounting policy — voluntary (HKAS 8 para. 29): - the nature of the change; - the reasons why the new policy provides reliable and more relevant information; - the same line-item / EPS adjustment amounts as above (current + each prior period presented; periods before those presented), to the extent practicable.

Change in accounting estimate (HKAS 8 para. 39): - disclose the nature and amount of the change that has an effect in the current period or is expected to have an effect in future periods (if practicable to quantify the future effect).

Correction of prior period error (HKAS 8 para. 49): - the nature of the prior period error; - for each prior period presented, to the extent practicable, the amount of the correction for each financial statement line item affected and (if HKAS 33 applies) for basic and diluted EPS; - the amount of the correction at the beginning of the earliest prior period presented.

Timing rule for all three: disclosures are given in the period of change / period the error is corrected; subsequent periods need not repeat the disclosures.

How does HKAS 8 define "accounting policies", and give two examples of policy choices.

Answer

💬 白話解：新嘢第一次出現、或者以前唔重要依家先重要，咁樣用新政策唔算「改政策」，所以唔使 retrospective restate。考試鍾意用呢個做 distractor。

Flashcard 4 / 共 8 張
Question

Explain the mechanics of retrospective application of a change in accounting policy (HKAS 8 paras. 22–24).

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解：即係「扮成一向都係咁做」——最早呈報嗰年嘅期初 retained earnings 要調整，之後每年 comparative 都要重報。計唔到（impracticable）先可以豁免。

Flashcard 5 / 共 8 張
Question

How is a change in accounting estimate recognised, and how does the treatment differ between a one-period effect and a multi-period effect (HKAS 8 paras. 36–38)?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解：估計改咗就「由今日起計」，唔准返轉頭改舊數。Warranty 呢類一年一估嘅只影響今年；useful life 呢類長命估計就影響今年加埋以後每年嘅折舊。
