M6 Chapter 4: Inventories — Phase 3 Answers

Q1 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): Under HKAS 2.11, the costs of purchase include the purchase price, import duties and other taxes, transport, handling and other costs directly attributable to the acquisition of the inventories. Import duties and freight inwards on timber are therefore included in cost. Storage costs of finished goods, abnormal waste and selling costs (sales commissions) are excluded under HKAS 2.16 and expensed as incurred.

解題思路（繁中）: 判斷標準只有一條：「呢項成本係咪令存貨達致現時嘅地點同狀態？」Import duties + freight inwards 係買料必要使費 → 入 cost（B 啱）。A 係儲起製成品嘅倉租 —— 貨已做完，儲存唔係生產下一階段前必需，HKAS 2.16 明確剔除；C 係 abnormal waste，正常浪費先入 cost，異常浪費即刻 expense；D 係 selling costs，同貨品嘅 location and condition 無關，必定 expense。

Rule Check: HKAS 2.11 (costs of purchase); HKAS 2.16 (costs excluded from inventories)

Common Trap（繁中）: 考生最易中伏係將「storage costs」一刀切全部當 expense 或全部當 cost —— 關鍵係睇佢係咪生產下一階段必需（如熟成過程）先入 cost。另外 trade discount/rebate 記得要扣減 purchase cost，唔係當其他收入。

Q2 — Answer: B (HK$24,000)

Model Answer (EN): Fixed production overheads are allocated based on normal capacity, not actual production (HKAS 2.13). - Normal-capacity allocation rate = HK$1,152,000 ÷ 9,600 units = HK$120 per unit - Fixed overhead allocated to April production = 600 units × HK$120 = HK$72,000 - Budgeted fixed overhead for April = HK$1,152,000 ÷ 12 = HK$96,000 - Unallocated overhead expensed = HK$96,000 − HK$72,000 = HK$24,000

解題思路（繁中）: 產量低於 normal capacity 時，每單位分配率唔准加碼 —— 照用 HK$120/unit，所以只有 600 × 120 = HK$72,000 入咗存貨成本，剩低 HK$24,000 即時出 P&L。Distractor A（HK$0）係用實際產量做分母（96,000 ÷ 600 = HK$160/unit），將所有 overhead 吸晒落存貨 —— HKAS 2.13 明令禁止；D（HK$96,000）係將成個月 overhead 全數 expense，完全冇分配落存貨；C（HK$72,000）係調轉咗 allocated 同 unallocated 嘅概念。

Rule Check: HKAS 2.13 (allocation of fixed production overheads based on normal capacity; unallocated overhead recognised as an expense in the period incurred)

Common Trap（繁中）: 伏位在「分母」：見到「production significantly below plan / equipment breakdown」就要即刻諗 normal capacity，唔好用實際產量。相反，若產量異常高，分配率要調低避免存貨高於成本 —— 兩個方向都要識。

Q3 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): Under HKAS 2.23, the cost of inventories of items that are not ordinarily interchangeable, and goods produced and segregated for specific projects, must be assigned using specific identification. Each Regatta yacht is built to an individual customer's design and is not interchangeable, so specific identification is required. FIFO and weighted average cost (HKAS 2.24) apply only to interchangeable items; LIFO is not permitted under HKAS 2 (LP cites IAS 2 para. BC 9).

解題思路（繁中）: 題眼係「no two yachts are interchangeable」+「built to an individual customer's design」—— 每件貨獨一無二、為特定項目而生產，就一定要 specific identification（C 啱）。A、B 只適用於可以互換嘅存貨；D 係雙重錯：既唔啱性質，而且 LIFO 喺 HKAS 2 根本被禁止，任何情況都唔揀得。

Rule Check: HKAS 2.23 (specific identification); HKAS 2.24 (FIFO/weighted average for interchangeable items); IAS 2 para. BC 9 (LIFO not permitted, as cited in the LP)

Common Trap（繁中）: MCQ 好鍾意將 LIFO 放落選項度引你 —— 記住 HKAS 2 得三個公式：specific identification、FIFO、weighted average。另外「same cost formula for inventories of similar nature and use」(HKAS 2.25) 都係常考點。

Q4 — Answer: C (HK$5,000)

Model Answer (EN): NRV = estimated selling price − costs of completion − costs to make the sale; compare with cost item by item (HKAS 2.6, 2.28).

Product line Cost (HK$) NRV (HK$) Write-down per unit (HK$) Units Write-down (HK$)
Down jackets 850 880 − 0 − 40 = 840 10 200 2,000
Trail shoes 420 450 − 50 − 10 = 390 30 100 3,000
Total 5,000

Both lines have NRV below cost, so total write-down = HK$2,000 + HK$3,000 = HK$5,000, recognised as an expense in the period.

解題思路（繁中）: NRV 要扣兩樣嘢：costs of completion 加 costs to make the sale。Distractor A（HK$0）係齋用賣價同 cost 比（880 > 850、450 > 420 所以「唔使減值」）—— 完全冇扣 completion 同銷售成本；B（HK$2,000）係只減咗 down jackets，trail shoes 嗰邊漏扣 HK$50 completion costs，以為 450 − 10 = 440 高過 cost 420；D（HK$14,000）係將全部 completion + selling costs 當成 write-down（40×200 + 60×100 = 14,000），冇做 cost vs NRV 嘅比較。

Rule Check: HKAS 2.6 (definition of NRV); HKAS 2.28 (inventories measured at the lower of cost and NRV)

Common Trap（繁中）: 三大伏位：(1) 漏扣 costs of completion；(2) 齋睇 selling price 唔計任何銷售成本；(3) 用「總額對總額」比較而唔係逐項/逐類比較。記住 NRV 測試係 item-by-item，唔可以話 A 產品賺嘅補返 B 產品蝕嘅。

Q5 — Answer: A

Model Answer (EN): At 31 December 20X5, NRV (HK$295,000) exceeds the written-down carrying amount (HK$260,000), so the previous write-down is reversed. The reversal restores the carrying amount to the lower of cost (HK$300,000) and NRV (HK$295,000), i.e. HK$295,000 — a reversal of HK$35,000 (within the HK$40,000 original write-down cap). The entry is:

Account Dr (HK$) Cr (HK$)
Inventories 35,000
    Loss on write-down of inventories 35,000

The reversal is recognised as a reduction in the amount of inventories recognised as an expense in the period.

解題思路（繁中）: 兩個上限要同時 check：(1) 撥回額唔可以超過原本 write-down（HK$40,000）；(2) 撥回後帳面值唔可以高過 lower of cost and NRV —— 呢度 NRV 得 HK$295,000，低過 cost HK$300,000，所以只可以撥 HK$35,000（A 啱）。B（HK$40,000）全數撥返會令帳面值 = HK$300,000 > NRV HK$295,000，違反 lower of cost and NRV；C 用錯 account —— 存貨冇 revaluation surplus 呢樣嘢（嗰個係 HKAS 16 PPE 嘅概念），reversal 係沖返 Loss on write-down；D 方向完全調轉，變成再減值多一次。

Rule Check: HKAS 2.33 (reversal of write-down limited to the original write-down); HKAS 2.28 (lower of cost and NRV)

Common Trap（繁中）: 考生成日記住「reversal capped at original write-down」就機械式撥足 HK$40,000，忘記咗 reversal 之後個帳面值仲要過多次 lower of cost and NRV 測試 —— 兩個 cap 取其嚴。另外 reversal 嘅 credit 係 expense 嘅減少（冲 loss account），唔係 revenue，亦唔係 OCI/surplus。
