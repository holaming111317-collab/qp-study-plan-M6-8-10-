M6 Chapter 18: Effects of Changes in Foreign Exchange Rates — Phase 3 Quiz

Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. … — 超時如實申報

Question 1

According to HKAS 21 The Effects of Changes in Foreign Exchange Rates, which of the following combinations contains only monetary items?

A. Inventories and prepaid insurance B. Trade receivables and dividends payable C. Goodwill and trade payables D. Property, plant and equipment and borrowings

Question 2

Lantau Electronics Limited is incorporated in Hong Kong. 85% of its sales are made to customers in Mainland China; these sales prices are denominated and settled in Renminbi (RMB), and are driven by competitive forces and regulations in the Mainland China market. Most of its labour and material costs are also denominated and settled in RMB, although the company's bank borrowings are denominated in HKD and it is incorporated in Hong Kong. In accordance with HKAS 21, what is the most likely functional currency of Lantau Electronics Limited?

A. HKD, because the company is incorporated in Hong Kong and its borrowings are denominated in HKD B. USD, because it is a common currency for international trade C. RMB, because the primary factors in HKAS 21.9 (sales prices and costs) point to RMB D. Any currency of management's choice, because an entity can freely choose its functional currency

Question 3

Kowloon Trading Limited (functional currency: HKD) purchased goods on credit from a US supplier for US$40,000 on 1 November 20X8, when the exchange rate was HK$7.82 = US$1.00. The amount was still unpaid at the financial year-end of 31 December 20X8, when the exchange rate was HK$7.78 = US$1.00. In accordance with HKAS 21, what should Kowloon Trading Limited recognise in profit or loss for the year ended 31 December 20X8 in respect of this trade payable?

A. An exchange gain of HK$1,600 B. An exchange loss of HK$1,600 C. No exchange difference, because the payable has not yet been settled D. An exchange gain of HK$800

Question 4

Pearl River Limited (functional currency: HKD) sold goods on credit to a customer in France for EUR50,000 on 15 December 20X7, when the exchange rate was HK$8.50 = EUR1.00. The trade receivable was correctly retranslated at the closing rate of HK$8.60 = EUR1.00 at the year-end of 31 December 20X7. The customer settled the invoice in full on 20 January 20X8, when the exchange rate was HK$8.55 = EUR1.00. Which journal entry correctly records the settlement on 20 January 20X8?

A.

Dr HK$ Cr HK$
Cash 430,000
    Trade receivable (EUR) 430,000

B.

Dr HK$ Cr HK$
Cash 427,500
    Trade receivable (EUR) 425,000
    Gain on foreign exchange difference (P/L) 2,500

C.

Dr HK$ Cr HK$
Cash 427,500
Loss on foreign exchange difference (P/L) 2,500
    Trade receivable (EUR) 430,000

D.

Dr HK$ Cr HK$
Cash 425,000

    Trade receivable (EUR) 427,500

Question 5

Harbourview Limited (functional currency: HKD) holds inventories in an offshore warehouse in Malaysia. The inventories cost MYR200,000 and were purchased when the exchange rate was HK$1.90 = MYR1.00. At the financial year-end, the net realisable value of the inventories is MYR210,000, and the closing exchange rate is HK$1.75 = MYR1.00. In accordance with HKAS 2 Inventories and HKAS 21, what amount, if any, should Harbourview Limited recognise as a write-down of inventories at the year-end?

A. No write-down, because the net realisable value exceeds cost when both are measured in MYR B. A write-down of HK$12,500 C. A write-down of HK$30,000 D. A write-down of HK$31,500
