Q1. Answer: B — HK$9,000,000

Consideration transferred is measured at acquisition-date fair values:

Component HK$
Cash consideration 3,000,000
Shares issued (200,000 × HK$28, the fair value at the acquisition date, 1 September 20X5) 5,600,000
Contingent consideration (fair value at acquisition date) 400,000
Consideration transferred 9,600,000

Shares issued as consideration are measured at their fair value on the acquisition date (the date control passes), not the agreement date nor the date of actual issue. Contingent consideration is included at its acquisition-date fair value. The due diligence fees (HK$150,000) are acquisition-related costs and are expensed as incurred; share issue costs (HK$20,000) are accounted for in accordance with HKAS 32/HKFRS 9 (deducted from equity) — neither forms part of the consideration.

解題思路：伏位有三：(1) 股份代價用邊日嘅股價——要用 acquisition date（1 Sep，HK$28），唔係協議日（HK$25，即選項 A 嘅 8,400,000），亦唔係實際發行日（HK$30，選項 D 仲將或有代價用面值 HK$1,000,000，雙重錯）；(2) due diligence 等 acquisition-related costs 唔入代價，選項 C（9,170,000）就係加埋 HK$170,000 嘅陷阱；(3) 或有代價用 acquisition-date fair value（HK$400,000），唔係面值。記住「control passes 嗰日」先係所有計量嘅基準日。

Rule Check: HKFRS 3(R).37 (consideration measured at acquisition-date fair values); HKFRS 3(R).39 (contingent consideration at acquisition-date fair value); HKFRS 3(R).59 (acquisition-related costs expensed; debt/equity issue costs per HKFRS 9/HKAS 32).

Q2. Answer: Goodwill = HK$320,000

Fair value of identifiable net assets at acquisition = HK$200,000 + HK$550,000 + HK$100,000 + HK$50,000 (fair value uplift to land) = HK$900,000.

HK$
Consideration transferred 980,000
Add: NCI at fair value 240,000
Less: Fair value of identifiable net assets (900,000)
Goodwill (full goodwill method) 320,000

Consolidation adjustment (pro forma) journal entry as at 1 July 20X4:

Debit HK$ Credit HK$
Share capital (Shell) 200,000
Retained earnings (Shell) 550,000
Reserves (Shell) 100,000
Business combinations reserve 50,000
Goodwill 320,000
Investment in Shell Limited 980,000
Non-controlling interest — equity 240,000
Totals 1,220,000 1,220,000

(If Pearl had measured NCI at the proportionate share of net assets instead, NCI = 20% × HK$900,000 = HK$180,000 and goodwill would have been HK$980,000 + HK$180,000 − HK$900,000 = HK$260,000 — the 'partial goodwill' method.)

解題思路：三個常見錯誤：(1) 唔記得將 fair value uplift（HK$50,000）計入可辨認淨資產， goodwill 會計錯做 HK$370,000；(2) NCI 用 fair value（HK$240,000）定 proportionate share（HK$180,000）——題目講明 fair value，goodwill 會較高，因為包含埋 NCI 應佔嘅 goodwill；(3) elimination entry 要 Dr 晒 subsidiary 100% 嘅 pre-acquisition equity（唔係只係 80%），差額一邊係 goodwill，另一邊 Cr NCI。條 entry Dr/Cr 必須平衡（1,220,000）。

Rule Check: HKFRS 3(R).32 (goodwill = consideration + NCI + previously held interest − identifiable net assets); HKFRS 3(R).19 (NCI at fair value or proportionate share, acquisition-by-acquisition); HKFRS 10.B86 (consolidation procedures — eliminate investment against pre-acquisition equity).

Q3. Answer: B

Under the five-step model, an entity determines at contract inception whether each performance obligation is satisfied over time or at a point in time. A performance obligation is satisfied over time where the entity's performance creates or enhances an asset that the customer controls as the asset is created or enhanced (e.g. work in progress on the customer's site). Because SkyBuild is constructing the facility on the customer's own site, the customer controls the asset as it is created, and revenue is recognised over time (e.g. by an input or output method measuring progress).

解題思路：伏位係選項 C——「simultaneously receives and consumes benefits」係 over-time 三個條件之一，但只適用於服務類（如清潔、保安）；呢度係建造資產，正確依據係「customer controls the asset as it is created」。選項 A 係常見直覺錯誤：以為起好先交貨就係 point in time，關鍵唔係幾時「handover」，而係 asset 邊個控制緊佢被創造嘅過程。三個 over-time 條件滿足其一即可。

Rule Check: HKFRS 15 (LP Ch3, Exhibit 3.7 — over-time criteria); HKFRS 15.38 (if not satisfied over time, the performance obligation is satisfied at a point in time).

Q4. Answer: A

Event (1) is an adjusting event: the court settlement provides evidence of conditions that existed at the end of the reporting period (a present obligation at 31 December 20X7), so the amounts recognised must be adjusted (a provision recognised/measured).

Event (2) is a non-adjusting event: the fire is indicative of a condition that arose after the reporting period. If material, it is disclosed in the notes (nature of the event and an estimate of its financial effect), but no adjustment is made to the 20X7 amounts.

Event (3) is also non-adjusting: dividends declared after the reporting period are not recognised as a liability at the end of the reporting period because no obligation existed at that date; disclosure is made in the notes instead.

解題思路：核心判斷係「conditions existed at year end 定 arose after year end」。訴訟和解係典型 adjusting（證實年結日已存在嘅狀況）；火災係年結後先發生嘅新狀況，只披露。股息 declaration 係最高頻伏位——學生成日以為「屬於 20X7 嘅 final dividend」要計返 20X7，其實年結日冇 obligation，唔可以認負債。仲要留意 window：事件必須發生喺 reporting period end 至 authorised for issue 之間先落入 HKAS 10 範圍。

Rule Check: HKAS 10.3 (definitions of adjusting/non-adjusting events); HKAS 10.11–12 (dividends declared after the reporting period not recognised as a liability); HKAS 1 (note disclosure of dividends proposed/declared before authorisation).

Q5. Answer: Investing activities — proceeds from sale of equipment HK$690,000; purchase of equipment (HK$1,000,000)

Cash receipts from disposal of equipment = Net book value of equipment disposed + Gain on disposal + Opening related receivable − Closing related receivable = HK$650,000 + HK$120,000 + HK$0 − HK$80,000 = HK$690,000 (inflow)

Purchase of equipment (paid fully in cash) = (HK$1,000,000) (outflow)

Net cash used in investing activities = HK$690,000 − HK$1,000,000 = (HK$310,000).

解題思路：伏位有兩個：(1) gain on disposal HK$120,000 唔係現金流——佢只係用嚟推返實際售價（NBV 650,000 + gain 120,000 = proceeds 770,000），然後再扣未收到嘅 HK$80,000 應收，先係今年實收 690,000；(2) 記住 investing 係 long-term assets 嘅買賣，唔好將 gain 本身當 operating inflow。若順便問 indirect method，個 gain 要喺 operating 度 subtract（non-operating item）。

Rule Check: HKAS 7.16 (investing cash flows — payments to acquire, and receipts from disposal of, property, plant and equipment).

Q6. Answer: B — At amortised cost

Under HKFRS 9, a financial asset (debt instrument) is subsequently measured at amortised cost if both conditions are met: (1) the asset is held within a business model whose objective is to hold financial assets in order to collect contractual cash flows; and (2) the contractual terms give rise, on specified dates, to cash flows that are solely payments of principal and interest on the principal amount outstanding. Both conditions are satisfied here.

解題思路：分類係兩重測試：business model test + contractual cash flow characteristics (SPPI) test。選項 C（FVTOCI）適用於「hold to collect and sell」嘅 business model——題目冇講會賣，所以唔啱；選項 A（FVTPL）係兩個條件都唔符合時嘅 default/residual 類別；選項 D 係混淆咗舊式 incurred-cost 概念。記住 default 係 FVTPL，amortised cost 同 FVTOCI 都係要符合特定條件先得。

Rule Check: HKFRS 9.4.1.1 (amortised cost conditions); HKFRS 9.4.1.2 (FVTOCI); HKFRS 9.4.1.4 (FVTPL).

Q7. Answer: Provision for warranties = HK$620,000

Where a provision involves a large population of similar obligations (product warranties), the best estimate is the expected value, weighting all possible outcomes by their probabilities:

Likelihood Repair cost if defect in all units HK$ Expected value HK$
70% — no defects 0 0
20% — minor defects 2,000,000 400,000
10% — major defects 4,000,000 400,000
Total expected value of warranty obligation 800,000

Less: repairs already made in 20X6 (HK$180,000) → Provision required at 31 December 20X6 = HK$620,000.

Journal entry:

Debit HK$ Credit HK$
Warranty expense (profit or loss) 620,000
Provision for warranties (current liability) 620,000

解題思路：三個檢查點：(1) expected value 唔係「most likely outcome」——雖然 70% 冇 defects，但 population 整體計概率加權先係 HKAS 37 要求；(2) 記得扣已做咗嘅維修 HK$180,000——provision 係未來仲要使嘅錢，唔係成個 obligation 嘅 gross amount；(3) recognition criteria 已滿足：sale + warranty = past event 產生 present (legal) obligation、probable outflow（population 整體睇）、reliable estimate。

Rule Check: HKAS 37.14 (recognition criteria); HKAS 37.23–24 (probability assessed for the population as a whole); HKAS 37.36–39 (best estimate/expected value).

Q8. Answer: A

A warranty provision is a liability with a carrying amount recognised for accounting purposes, but its tax base is nil (carrying amount less future deductible amounts: the deduction is available only when the claims are paid). Carrying amount of the liability > tax base → a deductible temporary difference, giving rise to a deferred tax asset (recognised if probable that sufficient future taxable income will be available).

Option B gives a taxable temporary difference (carrying amount of the asset > its nil tax base) → deferred tax liability. Option C: accrued interest income is an asset whose recovery will be taxable in future → taxable temporary difference. Option D: tax base = carrying amount (no future deduction, no future tax) → no temporary difference; this is a permanent difference.

解題思路：秒殺口訣——liability 嘅 CA > tax base（未來有 deduction 未攞）= deductible temporary difference → DTA；asset 嘅 CA > tax base = taxable temporary difference → DTL。選項 D 係經典 permanent difference 陷阱：fines 永遠唔 deductible，tax base = CA，乜 difference 都冇。選項 B 同 C 方向調轉咗，係 taxable（未來要交多啲稅）。

Rule Check: HKAS 12.5 (definitions of temporary differences, tax base, DTL/DTA); HKAS 12.24, 34 (DTA recognised only if probable future taxable income available).

Q9. Answer: B

A revision of the estimated remaining useful life of an asset is a change in accounting estimate — it results from new information/new developments and is not a correction of an error nor a change in the measurement basis applied. Changes in accounting estimates are recognised prospectively: the carrying amount of the asset is depreciated over the revised remaining useful life in the period of change and future periods affected. No restatement of prior periods is made.

解題思路：分類三部曲——policy（計量基準改變，retrospective）、estimate（新資訊導致估計改變，prospective）、error（錯咗，retrospective restatement）。Useful life/residual value/depreciation method 嘅改變全部屬 estimate，因為佢哋本身就係估計。伏位係選項 C：學生以為「舊嘅 useful life 錯咗」係 error——唔係，舊估計係基於當時可得資料作出，唔算錯。

Rule Check: HKAS 8.5 (definition of change in accounting estimate); HKAS 8.36 (recognised prospectively in the period of change and future periods).

Q10. Answer: Basic EPS = HK$4.00

Weighted average number of ordinary shares:

Shares Time weighting Weighted
Shares on issue at 1 January 20X6 2,000,000 12/12 2,000,000
Shares issued 1 July 20X6 (at full market price) 600,000 6/12 300,000
