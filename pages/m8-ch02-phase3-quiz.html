M8 Chapter 2: Legal Liability and Corporate Governance — Phase 3 Quiz

Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. … — 超時如實申報

Question 1 (MCQ)

The articles of association of Bright Harbour Limited, a Hong Kong incorporated company, contain a clause stating that the company's auditor shall not be liable to the company for any negligence, default, breach of duty or breach of trust in relation to the performance of the auditor's duties. The board is also considering whether the company may purchase insurance for the auditor against liabilities arising from the audit.

Which of the following correctly states the position under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)?

A. The clause in the articles is valid, and the company may also purchase insurance for the auditor. B. The clause in the articles is void, but the company may purchase insurance for the auditor against liability arising from defending civil or criminal proceedings. C. The clause in the articles is void, and the company is prohibited from purchasing insurance for the auditor. D. The clause in the articles is valid, but only if approved by the shareholders by special resolution at a general meeting.

Question 2 (Scenario MCQ)

Cedar Textiles Limited is negotiating a HK$20 million loan from Pearl Delta Bank. Pearl Delta Bank informs Cedar's auditor, Lo & Co, in writing before the audit report is signed that it requires the audited financial statements specifically to assess this loan application. Lo & Co issues an unmodified auditor's report addressed to the shareholders, knowing the report will be given to Pearl Delta Bank for this purpose. The financial statements are subsequently found to contain material misstatements that a reasonably careful audit would have detected. Pearl Delta Bank relied on the audited financial statements, advanced the loan, and suffered a loss when Cedar Textiles Limited collapsed.

Under common law, what is the minimum basis on which Lo & Co may be held liable to Pearl Delta Bank?

A. Fraud, because Pearl Delta Bank is a foreseeable third party. B. Ordinary negligence, because Pearl Delta Bank is a third-party primary beneficiary. C. Gross negligence, because Pearl Delta Bank is a foreseen beneficiary. D. No liability can arise, because Pearl Delta Bank has no privity of contract with Lo & Co.

Question 3 (Scenario MCQ)

During the audit of Marina Logistics Limited for the year ended 31 December 20X3, the audit manager of Kwok & Co decided, purely to save time, not to perform any substantive procedures on the revenue cycle and not to follow several mandatory requirements of the HKSAs. There is no evidence that any member of the audit team intended to deceive anyone. A material misstatement in revenue was not detected, and the client suffered losses as a result.

Which of the following best describes Kwok & Co's potential liability to the client?

A. Fraud, because a material misstatement was not detected. B. Ordinary negligence, because the audit manager failed to gather sufficient appropriate audit evidence. C. Gross negligence, because the failure to follow the HKSAs when carrying out the audit indicates reckless behaviour. D. No liability, because there was no intention to deceive the client.

Question 4 (Scenario MCQ)

Victoria Peak Holdings Limited, a company listed on the Main Board of the HKEX, proposes the following audit committee:

  • Chair: the company's Chief Financial Officer (an executive director), who is a HKICPA member;
  • Member: an independent non-executive director with accounting expertise;
  • Member: an independent non-executive director with legal expertise.

Which of the following statements is correct regarding this proposed audit committee?

A. The composition complies with the Listing Rules because the committee has three members and includes an accounting expert. B. The composition does not comply with the Listing Rules because an audit committee must comprise non-executive directors only. C. The composition does not comply with the Listing Rules because an audit committee must have at least five members. D. The composition complies with the Listing Rules because the 'comply or explain' approach allows the company to appoint an executive director as chair if reasons are disclosed.

Question 5 (MCQ)

Dragon Peak Properties Limited, a Hong Kong listed company, proposes that Mr. Chan should hold both the role of chairman of the board and the role of chief executive. Which of the following best describes the company's obligation under the Hong Kong Corporate Governance Code (Appendix 14)?

A. Combining the two roles is strictly prohibited by the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622). B. The company may combine the two roles but must give its reasons for doing so, because the roles of chairman and chief executive should be separated under the Code. C. The company may freely combine the two roles with no disclosure, because this is only a Recommended Best Practice. D. The company must obtain prior approval from the Securities and Futures Commission before combining the two roles.
