M8 Chapter 6: Auditing IT Systems — Phase 3 Quiz

Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. … — 超時如實申報

Q1 (MCQ, scenario)

You are the audit senior of Beacon Toys Limited, a toy wholesaler whose sales and receivables are fully computerised. You want to obtain evidence that the invoicing system calculates the early-settlement discount correctly on every sales invoice issued during the year. Using your firm's own software, you extract the client's complete sales invoice file and recalculate the discount on each of the 48,000 invoices, comparing your results to the amounts actually charged.

Which computer-assisted auditing technique are you using, and what type of procedure is it?

A. Test data approach — a test of controls B. Audit software — a substantive procedure C. Parallel simulation — a test of controls D. Audit software — a risk assessment procedure

Q2 (MCQ)

Which of the following is an application control?

A. Requiring all program changes to be logged and authorised by a responsible official before migration to the production environment B. Storing a copy of the daily backup of the accounting system at an off-site location C. The payroll system rejecting an overtime hours entry of 320 hours for a single month because it falls outside a pre-set acceptable range D. Restricting physical access to the data centre to authorised IT personnel only

Q3 (MCQ, scenario)

During the audit of Harbour Freight Limited, you conclude that the company's general IT controls are ineffective: ex-employees still have active login credentials, there is no log of program changes, and backups have not been tested for over a year. The application controls over the sales cycle (batch totals and sequence checks) appear to be well designed.

What is the MOST likely consequence for your audit approach?

A. Place full reliance on the application controls because they are well designed, and reduce substantive testing accordingly B. Place little (if any) reliance on the IT system and adopt a more substantive approach to auditing the financial statements C. Immediately issue a disclaimer of opinion because general controls are ineffective D. Ask internal audit to implement new general controls during the audit so that reliance can be placed on them

Q4 (MCQ, scenario)

Norton Logistics Limited outsources the processing of its entire payroll to CompServe Limited, a third-party computer processing company. With Norton's permission, you contact CompServe to assess whether you can rely on the payroll data. You ask CompServe's service auditor for a report that addresses not only how the payroll controls are designed, but also whether those controls operated effectively throughout the year.

Which report should you request?

A. A Type 1 report B. A Type 2 report C. An engagement letter referencing data extraction D. A management representation letter from CompServe

Q5 (Short answer)

(a) State TWO disadvantages of using computer-assisted auditing techniques (CAATs).

(b) State TWO inherent problems with using the test data approach on a client's computerised system.

(Answer each part in no more than two short bullet points.)
