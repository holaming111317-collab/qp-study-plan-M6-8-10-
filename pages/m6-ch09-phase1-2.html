M6 Chapter 9: Intangible Assets — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives (3–5, English, action verbs)

  1. Define an intangible asset and apply the identifiability, control and future economic benefits criteria in accordance with HKAS 38.
  2. Apply the recognition criteria and initial measurement rules for separately acquired intangible assets, intangible assets acquired in a business combination, and internally generated intangible assets.
  3. Distinguish the research phase from the development phase and determine which expenditures may be capitalised under the six recognition criteria of HKAS 38.57.
  4. Account for subsequent measurement of intangible assets under the cost model and the revaluation model, including amortisation, residual value, impairment (HKAS 36) and revaluation journals.
  5. Prepare the disclosure requirements for intangible assets under HKAS 38, distinguishing internally generated from other intangible assets.

必背考點

A. Recognition（確認）

考點 Rule Reference
Definition An intangible asset is an identifiable non-monetary asset without physical substance. HKAS 38.8
Identifiability Identifiable if it is separable (can be sold, transferred, licenced, rented or exchanged) or arises from contractual or other legal rights (regardless of transferability). HKAS 38.12
Control Entity can obtain the future economic benefits and restrict others' access (usually via enforceable legal rights). Skilled workforce, market share, uncontracted customers → fail control test. HKAS 38.13
Future economic benefits Revenue, cost savings or other benefits; assessed using reasonable and supportable assumptions. HKAS 38.13, 38.17
Recognition criteria Recognise if, and only if: (1) probable future economic benefits will flow to the entity; and (2) cost can be measured reliably. HKAS 38.21
Subsequent expenditure Generally expensed as incurred (rarely adds to, or is distinguishable from, the existing asset). Applies to both acquired and internally generated intangibles. HKAS 38.20

Research vs Development（核心考點）

考點 Rule Reference
Research definition Original and planned investigation undertaken with the prospect of gaining new scientific or technical knowledge and understanding. HKAS 38.8
Research costs Always expensed as incurred — probable future economic benefits cannot be demonstrated. HKAS 38.54
Development definition Application of research findings or other knowledge to a plan or design for new or substantially improved materials, devices, products, processes, systems or services before commercial production or use. HKAS 38.8
Development capitalisation — ALL SIX criteria ("PIRATE") ① technical feasibility of completion; ② intention to complete and use/sell; ③ ability to use or sell; ④ how the asset will generate probable future economic benefits; ⑤ availability of adequate technical, financial and other resources; ⑥ ability to measure expenditure reliably. HKAS 38.57
Cannot distinguish phases Treat all expenditure as research-phase (expense) until costs can be reliably allocated. HKAS 38.53
Prohibited internally generated intangibles Internally generated brands, mastheads, publishing titles, customer lists and similar items shall not be recognised. HKAS 38.63
Internally generated goodwill Never recognised — not an identifiable resource controlled by the entity measurable reliably at cost. HKAS 38.48–49

B. Measurement（計量）

Initial measurement（HKAS 38.24: all intangible assets initially measured at COST）

Source Initial measurement Reference
Separately acquired Cost = purchase price (incl. import duties and non-refundable purchase taxes, after deducting trade discounts/rebates) + directly attributable costs of preparing the asset for intended use (e.g. legal fees to draw up acquisition contract). General/administration-type costs (e.g. executives' time) are expensed. HKAS 38.27–28
Acquired in a business combination Fair value at acquisition date (HKFRS 13), even if not recognised in the acquiree's own books (e.g. internally generated brand of acquiree). HKFRS 3; HKAS 38.68
Goodwill in a business combination Residual = consideration − fair value of identifiable net assets acquired; recognised only in a business combination. HKFRS 3
Internally generated (development) Cost = expenditure from the date ALL six HKAS 38.57 criteria are first met; earlier expensed amounts are not reinstated. HKAS 38.57

Subsequent measurement — two models (policy choice applied per class of assets, HKAS 38.72)

Model Carrying amount Reference
Cost model Cost − accumulated amortisation − accumulated impairment losses HKAS 38.74
Revaluation model Fair value at revaluation date − subsequent accumulated amortisation − subsequent accumulated impairment losses. Only permitted if an active market exists (HKFRS 13 definition: transactions with sufficient frequency and volume). Rarely exists for brands, mastheads, patents, trademarks; may exist for licences, quotas, franchises, taxi licences. Cannot revalue assets never previously recognised; cannot initially recognise at other than cost. HKAS 38.75–76, 38.78
Revaluation increase Recognise in OCI and accumulate in equity under revaluation surplus — except to the extent it reverses a previous revaluation decrease of the same asset recognised in P&L (then credit P&L first). HKAS 38.85
Revaluation decrease Recognise in profit or loss — except to the extent a credit balance exists in revaluation surplus for that asset (debit OCI/surplus first, remainder to P&L). HKAS 38.86
Active market disappears Use last revalued amount − subsequent amortisation/impairment; disappearance is an impairment indicator → test under HKAS 36. HKAS 38.81

Amortisation & useful life

考點 Rule Reference
Finite vs indefinite Assess whether useful life is finite or indefinite. Indefinite ≠ infinite: only when there is no foreseeable limit to the period of net cash inflows. HKAS 38.88
Contractual/legal rights Useful life = shorter of expected useful life and the contractual/legal period (renewal only if at holder's option without significant cost). HKAS 38.94, 38.96
Finite life — amortisation Depreciable amount allocated on a systematic basis over useful life reflecting the pattern of benefits; if pattern cannot be determined reliably → straight-line. Begins when the asset is available for use; ceases at earlier of HKFRS 5 held-for-sale classification or derecognition. Charge to P&L unless included in carrying amount of another asset (e.g. inventories). HKAS 38.97, 38.99
Residual value Assumed zero unless: third party contractually committed to buy, or an active market exists that will likely still exist at end of useful life. Reviewed each year-end; change = change in estimate (HKAS 8). HKAS 38.100, 38.102
Indefinite life Not amortised; impairment test annually AND whenever there is an indication (HKAS 36). Review indefinite assessment each period; change to finite = change in estimate (HKAS 8) and an impairment indicator. HKAS 38.107–110
Impairment Both cost-model and revaluation-model intangibles are subject to impairment under HKAS 36. HKAS 36

C. Disclosure（披露）

Requirement Reference
By class of intangible assets, distinguishing internally generated from other intangibles: useful lives (indefinite/finite) or amortisation rates; amortisation methods; gross carrying amount and accumulated amortisation/impairment at beginning and end; line item(s) of the income statement containing amortisation; a reconciliation of opening to closing carrying amount (additions split: internal development / separately acquired / business combinations; disposals & held for sale; revaluation and impairment movements in OCI and P&L amortisation; other changes). HKAS 38.118
Indefinite-life assets: carrying amount and reasons/factors supporting the indefinite assessment; description, carrying amount and remaining amortisation period of any individually material intangible asset. HKAS 38.122
Changes in useful life assessment, amortisation method or residual values with material effect → disclose nature and impact (change in estimate per HKAS 8). HKAS 38.121
Revalued intangibles: effective date of revaluation; carrying amount of revalued assets; carrying amount had the cost model been used; revaluation surplus opening/closing, changes and distribution restrictions. HKAS 38.124

易混淆概念對比

對比表 1：Research Phase vs Development Phase

Research Phase Development Phase
Definition (HKAS 38.8) Original and planned investigation to gain new scientific or technical knowledge Application of research findings to a plan/design for new or substantially improved products/processes before commercial use
Typical activities Obtaining new knowledge; searching for/evaluating/selecting alternatives for materials, products, processes Design/construction/testing of pre-production prototypes; design of tools, jigs, moulds with new technology; pilot plant not of commercially feasible scale
Accounting treatment Always expense (HKAS 38.54) Capitalise only if ALL six criteria in HKAS 38.57 met; otherwise expense
If phases cannot be distinguished Treat everything as research → expense all (HKAS 38.53) —

💬 點樣分辨／考試伏位： - 記口訣：Research = 搵知識（investigative, high risk）→ 必費用化；Development = 用知識做嘢（application）→ 六條件齊先可資本化。 - 伏位一：「search for / evaluation and final selection of alternatives」聽落似做緊產品，其實係 research。 - 伏位二：「pilot plant」要睇規模——not of economically feasible scale for commercial production 先係 development。 - 伏位三：六個條件要 ALL 滿足，差一個都係 expense；而且係由 全部條件首次達成當日 起先開始資本化，之前已費用化嘅支出 不得重撥。 - 伏位四（Jun 2025 panel report）：考生常混淆 definition 同 recognition criteria——定義啱唔代表可以資本化。

對比表 2：Cost Model vs Revaluation Model（HKAS 38.72–86）

Cost Model Revaluation Model
Carrying amount Cost − accumulated amortisation − accumulated impairment (HKAS 38.74) FV at revaluation date − subsequent accumulated amortisation − impairment (HKAS 38.75)
Availability Always available Only if an active market exists (HKFRS 13: sufficient frequency & volume of transactions)
Typical assets Patents, brands, trademarks, mastheads (unique, no active market) Licences, production quotas, franchise agreements, taxi licences, broadcast rights
Value increases Not reflected in financial statements Increase → OCI / revaluation surplus (HKAS 38.85)
Value decreases Only via HKAS 36 impairment → P&L Decrease → P&L, unless surplus balance exists for that asset → debit OCI first (HKAS 38.86)
Policy scope Applied per class of intangible assets Same class rule; asset in class without active market → must use cost model for that asset

💬 點樣分辨／考試伏位： - 伏位一：goodwill 永遠唔可以 revalue（唔 separable，無 active market）；acquired brand name 都係 unique，無 active market。 - 伏位二：revaluation model 唔可以 用嚟首次確認資產（initial recognition 永遠係 cost）——business combination 嗰個 fair value 係 HKFRS 3 嘅要求，唔係 revaluation。 - 伏位三：revaluation decrease 落 P&L 前，要睇 同一資產 嘅 revaluation surplus 有冇 credit balance；increase 入 OCI 前，要睇同一資產有冇之前入過 P&L 嘅 decrease（先 reversal 入 P&L）。 - 伏位四：active market 消失 = impairment indicator（要按 HKAS 36 做減值測試），唔係「唔使理」。

Phase 2 閃卡 (exactly 8 cards)

Flashcard 1 / 共 8 張
Question

What is the definition of an intangible asset under HKAS 38, and what are its three key attributes?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：無形資產要過三關——「認得出（可分割或有法律權利）」、「控制到（啲利益入你袋、人哋攞唔到）」、「有錢景（未來經濟利益）」。 skilled workforce、market share 呢啲過唔到 control 關，所以唔係資產。

Flashcard 2 / 共 8 張
Question

State the recognition criteria for an intangible asset and its initial measurement basis (HKAS 38.21, 38.24).

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：兩個條件係 "AND" 關係，缺一不可。記住所有無形資產出世嗰刻一律用 cost——revaluation model 唔可以用嚟做首次確認。

Flashcard 3 / 共 8 張
Question

What is included in the cost of a separately acquired intangible asset (HKAS 38.27–28)?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 白話解釋：同 PPE 邏輯一樣——「為咗令件資產用得返而直接使嘅錢」先入 cost。高層飞去美國傾 deal 嘅人工係「唔買都會使」嘅錢，要費用化。

Flashcard 4 / 共 8 張
Question

List the SIX criteria for capitalising development costs (HKAS 38.57).

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
1. Technical feasibility of completing the asset for use/sale; 2. Intention to complete and use or sell it; 3. Ability to use or sell it; 4. How it will generate probable future economic benefits; 5. Availability of adequate technical, financial and other resources to complete it; 6. Ability to measure the expenditure reliably.
