M10 Chapter 13: Receivership, Liquidation and Winding-up — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives

  1. Distinguish compulsory winding-up, members' voluntary winding-up and creditors' voluntary winding-up, and apply the correct procedure for each.
  2. Analyse the appointment, powers, duties, liabilities and removal of liquidators and receivers.
  3. Apply the avoidance powers of a liquidator: void dispositions (s.182), unfair preferences (ss.266/266A/266B), invalid floating charges (s.267), fraudulent trading (s.275) and misfeasance (s.276).
  4. Determine the provability of debts and the statutory order of priority of payments in a winding-up.
  5. Advise on receivership as a corporate rescue / debt enforcement device and the grounds for compulsory winding-up under s.177 CWUMPO.

除非另註明，本章 s.177 起的條文均屬 Companies (Winding Up and Miscellaneous Provisions) Ordinance (Cap.32) ('CWUMPO')；重組／收購條文（如 ss.670–721）屬 Companies Ordinance (Cap.622) ('CO')。

必背考點

A. Relevant Ordinances & Sections (以 LP 所引為準)

Receivership (LP s.13.3) - CWUMPO ss.297A & 168D(1)(c): receiver/manager 必須係 natural person；undischarged bankrupt 或受 disqualification order 者唔做得。 - CWUMPO s.300A: floating charge 下委任嘅 receiver 須 forthwith 通知公司；公司 14 日內交 statement of affairs；receiver 收咗後 2 個月內將副本送 Registrar、court、trustees/debenture holders。 - CWUMPO s.298A(1): receiver 可向 court 尋求指示；s.298A(2): receiver 對履行職能中訂立嘅合約負 personal liability，但有 statutory right of indemnity 從公司資產彌償。 - Conveyancing and Property Ordinance (Cap.219), Fourth Schedule: receiver 對 mortgaged land 嘅 implied powers（insure、possess、lease、sell 等 9 項）。 - High Court Ordinance (Cap.4) s.21L / CWUMPO s.725 / inherent jurisdiction：CFI 委任 receiver 嘅三個來源。

Compulsory winding-up (LP s.13.4.2) - s.177: 六個 grounds — (1) special resolution to wind up by court; (2) 無開業一年／停業一年; (3) no members; (4) unable to pay its debts; (5) articles 指定事件發生; (6) just and equitable。 - s.178(1): deemed inability to pay debts — (a) statutory demand ≥ HK$10,000 未喺 21 日內清償（但 debt 有 genuine dispute on substantial grounds 可拒絕）; (b) execution unsatisfied; (c) court 信納無力償債（cash flow test + balance sheet test）。 - s.179: petition 呈請方式；standing 包括 company、creditors、contributory、trustee in bankruptcy、official receiver、Financial Secretary、Registrar、SFC (s.212 SFO Cap.571)。 - s.184(2): winding-up deemed commenced at presentation of petition（relation-back）。 - s.182: commencement 後 disposition of property void，除非 court sanction → 公司通常要申請 validating order（如出糧、日常營運開支）。 - s.186: winding-up order 後未經 court leave 不得開始或繼續針對公司嘅訴訟。 - s.190: directors 交 statement of affairs 及接受 interview；ss.221–222: private / public examination。 - s.197: liquidator 接管全部資產；s.199(1)/(2)(a): carry on business（beneficial winding-up）、出售資產；s.268: compromises + disclaimer of onerous property。 - ss.266, 266A, 266B: unfair preference — 破產前 6 個月內（associate = 2 年）；要素：put creditor in better position + desire to prefer + insolvent at the time（借 Bankruptcy Ordinance (Cap.6) ss.50(3)/(4), 51(1)/(2)）。 - s.267: winding-up 前 12 個月內批出嘅某些 floating charges 可被推翻。 - s.275(1): fraudulent trading — intent to defraud creditors / fraudulent purpose + knowingly a party；civil（個人負全部債務）+ criminal（最高 5 年監禁）。 - s.264B: extortionate credit transaction — winding-up order / special resolution 前 3 年內，grossly exorbitant payments 或 grossly contravenes fair dealing，court 可 set aside / vary。 - s.276: misfeasance summary procedure — 針對 officer、liquidator、receiver、promoter；official receiver / liquidator / creditor / contributory 可申請；不創建新訴因。 - s.265(1): preferential creditors — 僱員工資/遣散費/長服金 (EO Cap.57)、僱員補償 (Cap.282)、ORSO (Cap.426)、MPFA (Cap.485 s.17(7))、government statutory debts (12 個月內到期, s.265(1)(d))、銀行存戶 (≤HK$500,000, s.265(1)(db))、保險索償 (s.265(1)(e))。 - s.265(3B)/(4): preferential creditors rank above floating chargee；liquidator's costs 先於一般分派。 - s.263 + s.34 BO: proof of debts；s.264A: statutory interest；ss.265(5)/(5A): 三個月內 distrained goods 嘅 landlord。 - s.227F: 資產 < HK$200,000 → summary procedure，official receiver 做 liquidator。 - ss.271–275: 刑事罪行（concealment/falsification s.271–272、fraudulent conduct s.273、無 proper books s.274、fraudulent trading s.275）。

Voluntary winding-up (LP s.13.4.3) - s.228(1)(a)/(b), s.228A, s.228(1)(d): 開始方式 — articles 期限屆滿（ordinary resolution）、special resolution、directors' resolution (liabilities)、s.228A winding-up statement。 - s.233: certificate of solvency — directors 認為公司可喺 commencement 後 12 個月內全數還債；證明須喺 resolution 前 5 星期內發出；s.117(1): certificate + resolution 15 日內交 Registrar → 有 = members' voluntary winding-up；無 = creditors' voluntary winding-up (s.233(4))。 - s.241(1)/(2): creditors' meeting 須喺公司會議後 14 日內舉行；notice 同時發出 + Gazette + 中英文報章各一次；違反可致 liquidation invalid。 - s.242: creditors 提名 liquidator 優先於 members' nominee（7 日內可向法院申請裁定）。 - s.235(1): members' voluntary — liquidator 由 members ordinary resolution 委任；s.237A(1): liquidator 認為還唔切債 → summon creditors' meeting。 - s.229(1): resolution 14 日內 Gazette 公告；s.230: voluntary winding-up commenced at passing of resolution；s.231: 停業（beneficial winding-up 除外）；s.253: liquidator 21 日內公告委任；s.255A: 帳目須審計。 - s.227: court order dissolution；s.226A: 註冊證明書後 2 年自動解散；s.752: 剩餘資產 bona vacantia 歸政府。 - Liquidator 移除: ss.196(1) & 252（court, on cause shown）；voluntary 另可 s.235A(1) special resolution / s.235A(2) court；resignation s.194(1) + CWUR (Cap.32H) Rules 154–155。

B. Leading Cases (full citations 以 LP 為準)

Case Principle
Ebrahimi v Westbourne Galleries Ltd (1973) AC 360 Just & equitable winding-up：quasi-partnership 小公司，股東被踢出管理層、合理期望受挫 → 清盤令；legal rights 受制於 equitable considerations
Re ePlaza Ltd (unreported) HCCW 1122/2002 Statutory demand 下 debt 有 genuine dispute → petition dismissed；deemed insolvency 可被推翻
Re Wah Nam Group Ltd (unreported, 26 July 2000) HCCW 166/2000 Cash flow test + balance sheet test 雙軌判斷 unable to pay debts
Yeung Siu Ming v Man Lung Textiles Ltd (2018) HKCA 521 Registered shareholder 即有 standing 呈請；實益擁有與否無關
Golden Gate International Kindergarten and Nursery Ltd (2018) HKCFI 641 股東貸款以「公司盈利」為還款條件 → 永遠唔會 provable → petitioner 非 contingent creditor，無 standing
Forsyth v Blundell (1973) 129 CLR 477 Mortgagee/receiver duty of good faith：已知有人肯出高價仍私下低價賣 = breach
Tse Kwong Lam v Wong Chit Sen (1983) 3 All ER 54 (PC) 賣俾自己有利益嘅公司非絕對禁止，但須證明 good faith + reasonable precautions 取得 best price（應取 expert advice on method of sale & reserve price）
Downsview Nominees Ltd v First City Corp Ltd (1993) AC 295 (PC) Receiver 接受委任目的係阻撓另一 chargee 執行 → breach of good faith & proper purpose
Super Master Holdings Ltd v Chung Nam Securities Ltd (unreported, 26 March 2004) HCA 4573/2003 Mortgagee（同理 receiver）可為自身利益行使 power of sale，唔係 trustee；但須 good faith + reasonable care 取得 true market value
Re Tyler (1907) 1 KB 865 Court-appointed receiver 係 officer of the court，須 act lawfully + fairly + honourably（high-mindedness）
Macau First Universal International Ltd v Ding Xiaohong (No 2) (2012) 2 HKLRD 494 Court 唔會輕易撤換自己嘅 officer；honestly done his best 而任務 onerous → slow to remove
Achieve Goal Holdings Ltd v Zhong Xin Ore-Material Holding Co Ltd (2018) HKCFI 2718 Court receiver 對各方 impartial；unfitness/forfeit of confidence 先撤換；申請人負舉證責任
Liquidator of Almond Ltd v Cheong Chan Kao (2019) HKCFI 634 Unfair preference：提前還有董事擔保嘅銀行貸款 → inferred desire to prefer（objective test + subjective desire）
Aktieselskabet Dansk Skibsfinansiering v Brothers (2000) 3 HKCFAR 70, (2000) 1 HKC 511 Fraudulent trading 須 actual dishonesty；honest belief 母公司會打救 → 唔算 fraud，即使客觀上存活機會低
Advance Housing Pty Ltd (in liq) v Newcastle Classic Development Pty Ltd (1994) 14 ACSR 230 Liquidator 係 fiduciary：自己 firm 收過公司錢又要查 unfair preference = inherent conflict
Re Rainbow Gate Ltd (unreported, 20 December 2007) HCCW 593/1998 / Re Action Industrial (Intl) Ltd (unreported, 2 December 2009) HCCW 35/2007 Liquidator 違 statutory duties（唔交帳、唔將款項存入 companies liquidation account、拖唔做）→ removed
Re Carrian Holdings Ltd (in liq) (No 2) (1984) 1 HKC 598 Disclaimer of onerous property 可被拒絕（如有 indemnity 令第三方嚴重受損）

C. IRAC Application Points（答題骨架）

  • Issue spotting: 分清 scenario 係 receivership（secured creditor 自救，公司未死）、provisional liquidation（petition 後 interim 保護）、定 winding-up（公司終局）。考官伏位：deregistration ≠ liquidation；receivership ≠ liquidation。
  • Rule selection: 先問 (1) 邊條 route — compulsory (s.177) / members' voluntary (s.233 COS) / creditors' voluntary (s.233(4))；(2) 涉事條文 pinpoint 到 subsection。
  • Application: 時間線最緊要 — 計返 commencement date（compulsory = petition presentation, s.184(2)；voluntary = resolution, s.230），再數 6 個月 / 2 年（unfair preference）、12 個月（floating charge s.267）、3 年（extortionate credit s.264B）。
  • Conclusion: 要果斷 — 「the floating charge is liable to be set aside」「the liquidator should be removed」；引用 case 做 analogy（考評報告多次批評漏引 case law）。

Liquidator 委任 Court；OR 先做 provisional liquidator (s.194(1)(a)) Members ordinary resolution (s.235(1)) Creditors 提名優先 (s.242)
Commencement Petition presentation (s.184(2)) — relation-back Passing of resolution (s.230) Passing of resolution (s.230)
自動 stay / void dispositions 有 (ss.182, 186) 無 無

1. Costs & expenses of liquidation incl. liquidator's remuneration (s.265(4)) 2.
