M10 Chapter 7: Introduction to Employment Law in Hong Kong — Phase 3 Answers

Q1 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): B is correct. Under s.4(2) of the Employment Ordinance (Cap. 57), the EO applies to all employees in Hong Kong regardless of the nature of their work, except: (1) a family member employed by and living in the same dwelling as the employer; (2) an overseas contract worker; (3) a person serving under a crew agreement within the meaning of the Merchant Shipping (Seafarers) Ordinance (Cap. 478) or on board a ship not registered in Hong Kong; (4) an apprentice under the Apprenticeship Ordinance (Cap. 47) (per s.2A of the EO); and (5) a civil servant. A registered apprentice therefore falls outside the EO. Options A, C and D describe ordinary employees covered by the EO (a foreign domestic helper is covered by the EO — her exemption is from the MPF scheme, not the EO).

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題考 s.4(2) 嘅五類豁免。選項 B 嘅學徒係經 EO s.2A 豁免，直接命中條文。A 係兼職但符合 4–18 連續合約要求，一樣受 EO 保障；C 係經典伏位——外傭豁免嘅係 MPF（MPFSO 下 domestic employees 豁免），唔係 EO；D 口頭合約唔影響 EO 適用（僱傭合約可以係 oral）。考試伏位：唔好將 MPF 豁免同 EO 適用範圍撈亂。

Rule Check: Employment Ordinance (Cap. 57) s.4(2) and s.2A; Apprenticeship Ordinance (Cap. 47)

Common Trap（繁中）: 以為「外傭唔受 EO 管」——錯，外傭受 EO 全面保障，只係唔使供 MPF。仲有以為合約冇書面就唔受保障，其實 EO 適用於 oral contracts。

Q2 — Answer: HK$112,000

Model Answer (EN): - Eligibility: Marco has been employed under a continuous contract for not less than 24 months (8 years) and was dismissed by reason of redundancy (closure of the employer's business in Hong Kong falls within s.31B(2) EO: the employer has ceased, or intends to cease, the business in the place where the employee was employed). He did not refuse suitable alternative employment, so he qualifies under s.31B(1) EO. - Formula for a monthly-paid employee: 2/3 of the last month's wages × reckonable years of service. - Working: 2/3 × HK$21,000 = HK$14,000 per year of service; HK$14,000 × 8 years = HK$112,000. - The statutory maximum of HK$390,000 does not apply, as HK$112,000 is below the cap.

解題思路（繁中）: 兩步驗證：第一步 eligibility——連續合約 ≥24 個月 + redundancy（僱主結束香港廠房業務，正正係 s.31B(2) 嘅「ceased business in the place」情況）。第二步代入公式：月薪僱員用「2/3 × 最後一個月工資 × 服務年資」。2/3 × 21,000 = 14,000；14,000 × 8 = 112,000。最後一定要檢查 HK$390,000 上限——呢個案例未觸頂，所以要明確講句「cap 唔適用」先算完整。考試伏位：唔好用錯 daily-rated 公式（18 日工資），同埋遣散費係 last month's wages 唔係平均工資。

Rule Check: Employment Ordinance (Cap. 57) s.31B(1) and s.31B(2); statutory maximum HK$390,000

Common Trap（繁中）: (1) 忘記檢查 24 個月連續服務門檻；(2) 用「平均月薪」代替「last month's wages」；(3) 忘記陳述 HK$390,000 上限是否適用——計算題要主動交代。

Q3 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): C is correct. Under the multifactor test endorsed by the CFA in Poon Chau Nam v Yim Siu Cheung (2007) 1 HKLRD 951 (following Market Investigations Ltd v Minister of Social Security (1969) 2 QB 173 and Lee Ting Sang v Chung Chi-Keung (1990) 1 HKLR 764), the court examines all features of the relationship and forms an overall impression — 'a nuanced and not a mechanical approach'. Here, Bright exercises substantial control (fixed days and hours, internal manual, Financial Controller's instructions), supplies the equipment, pays fixed monthly remuneration with no financial risk to Fiona, and Fiona cannot delegate the work — all pointing to a contract of service. Labelling a person a 'freelance consultant' does not conclude the question of status (A is wrong), and self-registration with an MPF scheme is only one factor and not determinative (B is wrong). The control test is no longer the sole determinative factor (D is wrong).

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題係 multifactor test 嘅應用。逐個 factor 落 facts：控制（固定返工時間、要跟 manual、聽上司指示）→ 指向僱員；工具（用公司電腦軟件）→ 指向僱員；唔可以請替工 → 指向僱員；固定月薪、無財務風險 → 指向僱員。整體印象 = contract of service。A 錯因為 label 唔係 conclusive（LP 明講 "Labeling someone as an employee or independent contractor does not conclude the status"）；B 錯因為自己供 MPF 只係其中一個因素（Poon Chau Nam 嘅 P 都係自己供 MPF，CFA 都判佢係僱員）；D 錯因為 control test 早已唔係唯一測試。考試伏位：status 題一定要行晒幾個 factor 先落 conclusion，唔好淨係靠合約點寫。

Rule Check: Poon Chau Nam v Yim Siu Cheung (2007) 1 HKLRD 951 (CFA); Market Investigations Ltd v Minister of Social Security (1969) 2 QB 173; Lee Ting Sang v Chung Chi-Keung (1990) 1 HKLR 764 (PC)

Common Trap（繁中）: 最大伏位係俾合約標籤誤導——法庭睇實質關係。第二個伏位係以為高技術/部分自主 = 一定是 contractor；要問工作是否「part and parcel」of the employer's organisation（organisation test）。

Q4 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): B is correct. Summary dismissal under s.9(1)(a) of the EO is available only where the employee has committed very serious misconduct or fails to improve after repeated warnings (wilful disobedience of a lawful and reasonable order; misconduct inconsistent with the due and faithful discharge of duties; fraud or dishonesty; habitual neglect). It is a drastic measure and a last resort. In Thomas Vincent v South China Morning Post Publishers (2005) 4 HKLRD 258, the CFA held that the misconduct relied upon must be factual, real and relevant, and that ordinarily a first offender should not be instantly dismissed without any warning or a further chance. A single isolated error by an employee with a clean record, with no evidence of dishonest intent and no prior warning, does not meet the s.9 threshold; the dismissal is likely wrongful/unreasonable. A is wrong: the 'fraud or dishonesty' ground requires actual dishonest conduct, not an innocent mistake. C is wrong: while an employer need not give a reason at the time of termination, that does not dispense with the need for s.9 grounds to justify dismissal without notice. D is wrong: no prior Labour Tribunal approval is required for summary dismissal.

解題思路（繁中）: 題眼係「第一次犯 + 無警告 + 無證據有 dishonest intent」。S.9 嘅 dishonesty ground 要真有欺詐/不誠實，找錯錢嘅 innocent mistake 唔算；而且 CFA 喺 Thomas Vincent 話即時解僱初犯者（無警告、無第二次機會）通常 unreasonable。A 係 distractor 專攻「見 fraud or dishonesty 四個字就衝」嘅同學；C 係 hybrid 陷阱——「解僱時唔使講理由」係真嘅（LP 有講），但唔代表即炒唔使有 s.9 理由；D 無中生有，勞審處唔使事先批准。考試伏位：summary dismissal = last resort；有警告 + 持續重犯（如 Ying Kee 嘅持續遲到）先企得穩。

Rule Check: Employment Ordinance (Cap. 57) s.9(1)(a); Thomas Vincent v South China Morning Post Publishers (2005) 4 HKLRD 258 (CFA); cf. Ying Kee Safes and Furniture Limited v Wong Yam Tak, HCLA 105/1995

Common Trap（繁中）: (1) 將「找錯錢」自動當 dishonesty——intent 係關鍵；(2) 將「僱主解僱時無責任講理由」同「即炒無需理由」撈亂；(3) 以為要勞審處事先批准先炒得人——無呢回事。

Q5 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): C is correct. Indirect discrimination occurs where an apparently neutral requirement or condition is applied equally to everyone, but the proportion of one sex who can comply with it is considerably smaller, the requirement causes detriment to the complainant, and it is not justifiable. A minimum height requirement is the LP's classic example of a condition favourable to male applicants that discriminates against female applicants. Wing suffered detriment (rejection solely for height). SecureGuard can escape liability only by justifying the requirement under the three-step test affirmed in Equal Opportunities Commission v Director of Education (2001) 2 HKLRD 690: (1) the requirement pursues a legitimate aim; (2) it is rationally connected to that aim; and (3) it is necessary to accomplish that aim — objectively balancing the discriminatory effect against the employer's reasonable needs. A is wrong: equal application of a neutral condition is precisely the hallmark of indirect discrimination, not a defence. B is wrong: Wing was treated the same as male applicants on its face, so this is not direct discrimination under the 'but for' test (Secretary for Justice v Chan Wah (2000) 3 HKLRD 641). D is wrong: the genuine occupational qualification exception applies only in very limited circumstances and the onus is on the employer to prove it — it does not apply automatically.

解題思路（繁中）: 呢題分層好清楚：表面中性條件（身高 170cm）+ 對兩性一視同仁 → 排除 direct discrimination，lock 入 indirect。之後三步 justification test 係僱主唯一出路：legitimate aim → rational connection → necessity，客觀平衡。A 係最常見錯誤——「人人都一樣要求所以冇歧視」正正係 indirect discrimination 嘅定義場景；B 錯因為 direct 係「因為你係女性所以唔請你」，呢度條件係中性；D 錯因為 GOQ 係極窄豁免、舉證責任喺僱主，而且身高要求一般唔係 GOQ。考試伏位：記住 LP 本身就用身高要求做 indirect discrimination 嘅經典例子；仲有 comparator pool 要揀啱（University of Manchester v Jones）。

Rule Check: Sex Discrimination Ordinance (Cap. 480); Equal Opportunities Commission v Director of Education (2001) 2 HKLRD 690; Secretary for Justice v Chan Wah and Others (2000) 3 HKLRD 641 (CFA); University of Manchester v Jones (1993) ICR 474

Common Trap（繁中）: (1) 見到「一視同仁」就以為安全——neutral condition 正係 indirect discrimination 嘅核心；(2) 漏咗三步 justification test，只答「有歧視」唔答僱主可以點 justify；(3) GOQ 唔係自動適用，僱主要舉證而且適用範圍極窄。
