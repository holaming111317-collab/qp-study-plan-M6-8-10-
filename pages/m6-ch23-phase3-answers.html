M6 Chapter 23: Preparation of Consolidated Financial Statements — Phase 3 Quiz (Model Answers)

Q1 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): The NCI's share of profit is measured as NCI% × the subsidiary's reported profit, adjusted only for consolidation adjustments that change consolidated net assets. An unrealised profit adjustment is attributed to NCI only when the subsidiary is the selling entity (an upstream transaction), because that profit was earned and recorded by the subsidiary. In option B, Swift Limited (the subsidiary) sold inventory to the parent and 40% remains unsold, so the unrealised profit embedded in that inventory reduces Swift Limited's reported profit before computing the NCI share.

解題思路（繁中）: 核心口訣係「跟錢走」——NCI 只分擔子公司自己賺嘅利潤。Option B 係 upstream（子公司做賣方），子公司簿入面有 profit，年尾有 40% 未賣出，unrealised profit 要由子公司 reported profit 扣減，所以啱。Option A 係 downstream，profit 記喺母公司 Heron 簿度，同子公司 profit 無關，唔使調整 NCI 計算（呢個係 Jun 2025 panel report 點名嘅考生錯誤）。Option C 管理費同 Option D 利息，雙方記帳金額相等，income 同 expense 完全對銷，唔影響集團淨資產，所以唔使郁子公司嘅 reported profit。

Rule Check: HKFRS 10 paras 22, B94 (attribution of profit or loss between owners of the parent and NCI); HKFRS 10 para. B86(c) (intra-group transactions eliminated in full); LP §23.3.1.3 (upstream/downstream attribution approach).

Common Trap（繁中）: 以為所有 intra-group 交易都要調整子公司 profit 先計 NCI——其實只有「影響集團淨資產」兼且「子公司係賣方」嘅調整先計。反向都係陷阱：見到 upstream 又唔記得扣。

Q2 — Answer: HK$191,250

Model Answer (EN):

Working HK$
Cub Limited's reported profit for the year 800,000
Less: additional depreciation on FV uplift (HK$120,000 ÷ 8 years) (15,000)
Less: unrealised profit on upstream sale ((HK$250,000 − HK$200,000) × 40% on hand) (20,000)
Cub Limited's contribution to consolidated profit 765,000
NCI share (25% × HK$765,000) 191,250

The unrealised profit is based only on the 40% of inventory still on hand; the 60% on-sold externally is realised by the group.

解題思路（繁中）: 三步：先計 FV uplift 每年額外折舊 15,000（收購日 uplift 120,000 ÷ 8 年）；再計 upstream 存貨嘅 unrealised profit——mark-up 係 50,000，但只有 40% 存貨未賣出，所以只扣 20,000；最後 adjusted profit 765,000 × NCI 25% = 191,250。注意呢度 NCI 係 25%（母公司持 75%），唔好撈錯用 75%。

Rule Check: HKFRS 10 paras 22, B94; HKFRS 3 (Revised) (fair value measurement of identifiable net assets and consequential depreciation); LP §23.3.1.3 (NCI share = NCI% × subsidiary's reported profit adjusted for net-asset-affecting consolidation adjustments).

Common Trap（繁中）: 三大失分位：(1) 成條 mark-up 50,000 全扣（得 183,750），忘記只有未售出部分先係 unrealised；(2) 唔記得扣 FV uplift 嘅額外折舊（得 195,000）；(3) 完全唔調整，直接 800,000 × 25% = 200,000。Panel reports 年年話 NCI profit allocation 係考生最弱一環。

Q3 — Answer: A

Model Answer (EN):

Goodwill (HKFRS 3 (Revised) acquisition method) HK$
Consideration transferred 2,000,000
Add: NCI at proportionate share (20% × HK$2,250,000) 450,000
Less: fair value of identifiable net assets acquired (HK$1,000,000 + HK$1,100,000 + HK$150,000) (2,250,000)
Goodwill 200,000

The due diligence costs of HK$80,000 are acquisition-related costs and are expensed as incurred — they are not added to the consideration transferred.

解題思路（繁中）: Option B (280,000) 錯在將 80,000 due diligence costs 加埋入 consideration——HKFRS 3 規定 acquisition-related costs 要費用化。Option C (320,000) 錯在完全唔理 150,000 FV uplift，直接用賬面淨資產 2,100,000 計 NCI 同 goodwill。Option D (350,000) 錯在 NCI 有跟比例計，但扣減嘅 net assets 冇計 FV uplift（2,000,000 + 450,000 − 2,100,000）。記住 goodwill 條式入面「net assets」同「NCI（proportionate basis）」要用同一把 fair value 尺。

Rule Check: HKFRS 3 (Revised) (acquisition method: consideration transferred + NCI − recognised amounts of identifiable net assets; acquisition-related costs expensed); LP Ch 23 §23.3.1.1 and Exam Practice Q4 (due diligence costs excluded from consideration).

Common Trap（繁中）: 混淆 goodwill 計算中兩個 NCI 計法：呢題用 proportionate share（partial goodwill），所以 NCI = 20% × FV net assets，唔好用 fair value（full goodwill）計。另外記得 goodwill 出現在 consolidated SFP，子公司自己嘅簿係唔會見到嘅。

Q4 — Answer: D

Model Answer (EN): The intra-group sale must be eliminated in full, and the unrealised profit relating to inventory still on hand must be eliminated:

  • Unrealised profit = (HK$120,000 − HK$90,000) × 30% on hand = HK$9,000
  • Eliminate the sale: DR Sales revenue HK$120,000 / CR Cost of sales HK$120,000
  • Eliminate the unrealised profit: DR Cost of sales HK$9,000 / CR Inventory HK$9,000
  • Combined (net) entry: DR Sales revenue HK$120,000 / CR Cost of sales HK$111,000 / CR Inventory HK$9,000 (Dr total = Cr total = HK$120,000 — balanced)

解題思路（繁中）: Option A 只對銷咗個 sale，冇處理年尾存貨入面嘅 9,000 unrealised profit，inventory 同 profit 都 overstated。Option B 將成個 mark-up 30,000 當晒 unrealised——但其實 70% 存貨已賣畀外人，嗰部分 profit 集團已經 realised，錯在冇按 30% 比例計。Option C 本末倒置：只調整 unrealised profit 但冇對銷 intra-group sales revenue 同 COGS，revenue 同 expenses 各自 overstated，誤導使用者。Option D 先係完整：sale 全額對銷 + 只扣 30% on-hand 部分嘅 unrealised profit，COGS 淨額 111,000 即係「120,000 對銷後加返 9,000」。

Rule Check: HKFRS 10 para. B86(c) (intra-group transactions eliminated in full); LP §23.2.4.4 (eliminate sale against purchases; eliminate unrealised profit only for inventory still on hand).

Common Trap（繁中）: 考生最易犯「全數扣 mark-up」（Option B）。記住：realised 與否係由集團角度睇——貨離開咗集團先算數。另外 downstream 交易（呢題母公司賣畀子公司）嘅 unrealised profit 唔影響 NCI，不過因為呢間係 wholly owned subsidiary，呢點唔影響答案。

Q5 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN):

NCI in consolidated equity at 31 December 20X6 HK$
NCI at acquisition (20% × (HK$2,000,000 + HK$1,500,000 + HK$400,000)) 780,000
Mink's post-acquisition movement in retained earnings (HK$2,100,000 − HK$1,500,000) 600,000
Less: accumulated additional depreciation on FV uplift (3 years × HK$400,000 ÷ 10) (120,000)
Adjusted post-acquisition movement 480,000
NCI share of post-acquisition movement (20% × HK$480,000) 96,000
NCI in equity at 31 December 20X6 876,000

解題思路（繁中）: 兩層加埋：收購日 NCI（proportionate share，記得計埋 400,000 FV uplift = 780,000）+ NCI 分佔收購後權益變動。收購後 RE 升咗 600,000，但要扣返三年（20X4–20X6）累計額外折舊 120,000，adjusted 係 480,000，NCI 分 20% = 96,000，總數 876,000。Option D (900,000) 忘記扣 FV uplift 折舊。Option C (892,000) 只扣咗一年折舊——由 1 Jan 20X4 到 31 Dec 20X6 係三年。Option A (836,000) 將 NCI 股息份額（20% × 200,000 = 40,000）再扣多次——但 2,100,000 嘅 closing retained earnings 本身已經係除息後數字，再扣就係 double-count。

Rule Check: HKFRS 10 paras 22, B94; HKAS 1 para. 54(q); LP §23.3.2 (NCI share of consolidated equity = NCI at acquisition + NCI share of post-acquisition movements in equity, adjusted).

Common Trap（繁中）: 用「closing RE − 收購日 RE」計 post-acquisition movement 時，股息已經自動反映咗，千祈唔好再扣一次。同埋額外折舊要計累計（由收購日到報告日），唔係只計本年度。另一伏位：唔小心用 80% 去計 NCI——NCI 係 20%。
