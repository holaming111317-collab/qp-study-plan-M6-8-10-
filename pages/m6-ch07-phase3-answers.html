M6 Chapter 7: Lease Accounting — Phase 3 Answers

Q1 — Answer: C

Model Answer (EN): The contract does not contain a lease because there is no identified asset. Under HKFRS 16.B13–B14, an asset explicitly specified in a contract is still not an identified asset if the supplier has a substantive substitution right. Victoria's substitution right is substantive because both conditions are met: (1) Victoria has the practical ability to substitute alternative units throughout the period of use (other comparable units in the same complex, goods on movable pallets, minimal relocation cost); and (2) Victoria would benefit economically from substitution (it can lease the premium unit to others at significantly higher rentals). Without an identified asset, the contract cannot be, or contain, a lease (HKFRS 16.9); it is a service arrangement.

解題思路（繁中）: 本題考 identified asset 嘅 substantive substitution right，兩個條件必須同時成立：practical ability + economic benefit。題目刻意畀齊兩個事實（可以隨時搬、搬咗賺多啲租金），所以 substitution right 係 substantive → 冇 identified asset → 唔係 lease。Distractor A 係最常見錯誤：「合約白紙黑字寫明 Unit 8」就當有 identified asset——但 LP 明確講 "Despite an asset being specified, a customer does not have the right to use the asset if the supplier has the substantive right to substitute"。Distractor B 用 control 嘅第二元素嚟混淆，但三元素要有 identified asset 先講到 control。Distractor D 錯在原因：customer 唔需要自己 operate 資產都可以有 lease（supplier 負責操作唔影響），而且真正原因係 substitution right。

Rule Check: HKFRS 16.9; HKFRS 16.B13; HKFRS 16.B14

Common Trap（繁中）: 見「資產喺合約入面寫明咗」即刻答「有 lease」。記住：寫明 ≠ identified asset，仲要 supplier 冇 substantive substitution right。反方向伏位：維修／故障時先可以替換（如壞船換船）唔算 substantive，嗰種情況反而係 lease。

Q2 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): Under HKFRS 16.18, the lease term comprises the non-cancellable period (four years) plus periods covered by an option to extend if the lessee is reasonably certain to exercise that option. At the commencement date, Harbour Retail has strong economic incentives to extend: (1) the rental during the optional period is set at 30% below market rates; and (2) it has undertaken significant leasehold improvements expected to generate benefits over seven years which can only be enjoyed through continued tenancy (HKFRS 16.B37–B40). It is therefore reasonably certain the extension option will be exercised, and the lease term is 4 + 2 = 6 years.

解題思路（繁中）: 考 lease term 嘅「reasonably certain + economic incentive」判斷。題目畀咗兩個典型經濟誘因：option period 租金平過市價 30%、自己做咗大額裝修要繼續住先享受到。所以 extension option 要計入 lease term → 6 年。Distractor A 係死背「non-cancellable period」嘅半桶水答案——non-cancellable period 只係起點，extension option 要按 reasonable certainty 加入。Distractor C 錯在等行使先計——assessment 係喺 commencement date 做（LP 原話：The assessment ... is made at the commencement date of the lease）。Distractor D 亂用裝修受益期 7 年——裝修期只係證據，唔係 lease term 本身。

Rule Check: HKFRS 16.18; HKFRS 16.B37–B40

Common Trap（繁中）: 兩個方向都會考：有 economic incentive 唔續租 → termination option 期間唔計入（lease term 縮短）；有 incentive 續租 → extension period 計入（lease term 拉長）。睇清楚題目係 extension 定 termination option，同埋 incentive 指向邊一邊。

Q3 — Answer: B (HK$281,169)

Model Answer (EN): The lease liability is initially measured at the present value of the lease payments not paid at the commencement date (HKFRS 16.26–27): - Fixed payments: HK$80,000 × 4 years, in arrears, discounted at 6%: - 80,000/1.06 + 80,000/1.06² + 80,000/1.06³ + 80,000/1.06⁴ = 75,472 + 71,200 + 67,170 + 63,367 = HK$277,209 - Expected payment under the residual value guarantee: 5,000/1.06⁴ = HK$3,960 - Variable payments linked to Bamboo's sales (2% of annual sales): excluded — they depend on the lessee's performance, not on an index or rate. - Lease liability = 277,209 + 3,960 = HK$281,169

解題思路（繁中）: 三個考點一次過：(1) RVG 只計 expected to be payable（HK$5,000），唔係擔保額全數 HK$20,000——錯用全數就會揀 A（HK$293,051 = 277,209 + 20,000/1.06⁴ = 277,209 + 15,842）；(2) 銷售掛鈎 variable payment 唔入 liability——錯誤加入 HK$10,000/年會揀 D（HK$315,820）；(3) 要折現——唔折現直接加總（80,000×4 + 5,000）會揀 C（HK$325,000）。留意 payments in arrears 用普通年金 t=1 至 4；如果係 in advance，第一期喺 commencement date 已付，根本唔入 liability（呢個係另一常見伏位）。

Rule Check: HKFRS 16.26; HKFRS 16.27; HKFRS 16.28

Common Trap（繁中）: 記住 RVG 口訣「計預期、唔計足額」；variable payment 只有掛鈎 index or rate（CPI、benchmark interest rate、market rental、匯率）先入 liability，掛鈎銷售/用量一律 expense as incurred。

Q4 — Answer: B

Model Answer (EN): Under HKFRS 16.36, the lease liability is increased by interest (effective interest method) and reduced by lease payments made. Interest for the year = HK$500,000 × 5% = HK$25,000. The HK$120,000 payment in arrears is therefore split between interest and principal repayment:

Account Dr HK$ Cr HK$
Interest expense 25,000
Lease liability (120,000 − 25,000) 95,000
— Cash 120,000

After the payment, the lease liability = 500,000 + 25,000 − 120,000 = HK$405,000.

解題思路（繁中）: 題目係 payment in arrears，所以年尾嗰 HK$120,000 入面已經包含咗全年利息，要拆開 interest 同 principal 兩份——呢個係 LP Knowledge Check Q4 嘅標準做法。Distractor A 全數減 liability，完全冇計 interest，違反 effective interest method。Distractor C 只入 interest accretion 嗰條（呢條分錄本身係啱嘅，但係未記錄付款——題目問嘅係「in respect of the lease payment」嗰條 entry）。Distractor D 將 principal 部分 debit 落 ROU asset，大錯——付款只影響 lease liability 同 cash，ROU asset 只會因 depreciation／liability remeasurement 而變。

Rule Check: HKFRS 16.36; HKFRS 16.BC182 (effective interest method)

Common Trap（繁中）: 分清楚兩種付款時點：in arrears → 一條 entry（Dr Lease liability + Dr Interest expense / Cr Cash）；in advance（年頭付款）→ 付款時已冇未到期利息，entry 係 Dr Lease liability / Cr Cash，利息係之前一年年尾已經 accrete 咗（Dr Interest expense / Cr Lease liability）。考試見到 1 Jan 付款就唔好再計當年利息入嗰條 payment entry。

Q5 — Answer: A

Model Answer (EN): Dragon Motors is a manufacturer or dealer lessor, so the finance lease is accounted for as an outright sale of the underlying asset at commencement (HKFRS 16.71–74): - Revenue = lower of the fair value of the underlying asset (HK$400,000) and the present value of the lease payments discounted at a market rate of interest (HK$380,000) → HK$380,000 - Cost of goods sold = carrying amount of the van = HK$320,000 - Selling profit = 380,000 − 320,000 = HK$60,000 - Costs incurred in obtaining the finance lease (HK$8,000) are expensed at the commencement date — they relate mainly to earning the selling profit and are not included in the net investment in the lease. - (Finance income on the net investment is subsequently recognised over the lease term at a constant periodic rate of return.)

解題思路（繁中）: Manufacturer/dealer lessor 兩個 anti-abuse rule 要記死：(1) revenue 取 FV 同 PV（market rate 折現）嘅較低者——呢度 PV 380,000 低過 FV 400,000，所以 revenue 係 380,000，唔係 400,000；用 FV 就會揀 B 或 D。(2) 攞租約嘅成本 commencement date 即刻 expense——錯誤咗加入 net investment 就會揀 B。Distractor C 係 financier lessor 嘅處理（只有 finance income，冇 selling profit）——題目講明 Dragon Motors 係 dealer，賣車賺差價係佢嘅 business model，所以一定要認 revenue + COGS。

Rule Check: HKFRS 16.71; HKFRS 16.72; HKFRS 16.73; HKFRS 16.74

Common Trap（繁中）: 「lower of」規則嘅立法原意係防止 lessor 谷大 selling price（虛高 FV）或用超低利率谷大 PV，從而喺 commencement date 認過多 revenue。另一伏位：financier lessor vs manufacturer/dealer lessor 嘅 initial direct costs 處理相反——前者入 net investment，後者即時 expense。考試見題先問自己：呢個 lessor 係賣貨嘅定係做融資嘅？
