M8 Chapter 9: Auditor's Reporting — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

Phase 1 速查表

Learning Objectives

  1. Analyse the basis of an auditor's report, including the importance of the report and how materiality drives reporting decisions.
  2. Describe the elements of an auditor's report under HKSA 700 (Revised) and the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622).
  3. Determine the appropriate form of auditor's opinion (unmodified / qualified / adverse / disclaimer) by applying the materiality-and-pervasiveness decision matrix in HKSA 705 (Revised).
  4. Explain the format, content and placement of Emphasis of Matter, Other Matter, Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern paragraphs and the Key Audit Matters section (HKSA 706 (Revised), HKSA 701, HKSA 570 (Revised)).
  5. Evaluate the ethical pressures (e.g. intimidation threat) arising when a modified opinion or KAM is proposed, per the HKICPA Code of Ethics for Professional Accountants.

必背考點

A. Assertions & Materiality（影響 opinion 嘅根本概念）

  • Auditor's two objectives in forming an opinion: (1) form an opinion on the financial statements based on an evaluation of conclusions drawn from audit evidence obtained; (2) clearly express that opinion through a written report.
  • Materiality & the opinion: to express an unmodified opinion, the auditor must conclude the financial statements as a whole are prepared "in all material respects, in accordance with the applicable financial reporting framework" (HKSA 700 (Revised); LP cites HKSA 700.11, 700.16). If not free from material misstatement → modify per HKSA 705 (Revised).
  • Misstatements arising from fraud are always qualitatively material, even if quantitatively immaterial.
  • Never offset misstatements: a material revenue overstatement misstates the FS as a whole even if a cost of sales misstatement offsets the P&L effect.
  • Individually immaterial misstatements within one balance/class may, in aggregate, be material → re-evaluate risk of material misstatement for that balance/class.
  • Materiality is re-evaluated throughout the audit, with a final "stand-back" reflection at the opinion stage (cf. HKSA 540 (Revised) stand-back for accounting estimates; HKSA 320 for materiality).

B. Audit Procedures / Evidence relevant to reporting

  • Before finalising the opinion: stand back from working papers, confirm detection risk is sufficiently low across all assertions for material balances/disclosures, and evaluate the impact of accumulated unadjusted/uncorrected misstatements.
  • Inability to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence arises from three sources (HKSA 705 (Revised) context): 1. Circumstances beyond the entity's control — e.g. accounting records destroyed; records seized by a government authority. 2. Nature or timing of the auditor's work — e.g. auditor appointed too late to attend the inventory count; substantive procedures alone cannot provide sufficient evidence; entity cannot obtain information from an equity-accounted investee. 3. Limitation imposed by management — e.g. management prevents attendance at the inventory count, blocks third-party confirmations, or refuses to provide supporting details for material balances.
  • Going concern evidence chain (HKSA 570 (Revised), Exhibit 9.7 logic): risk assessment per HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) → can the auditor obtain sufficient appropriate evidence on whether a material uncertainty exists? → is the going concern basis appropriate? → is disclosure adequate? Each branch drives a different reporting outcome (see decision matrix below).

C. Report modifications — 本章核心 ★

  • When to modify (HKSA 705 (Revised) para. 4): (a) the auditor concludes, based on evidence obtained, the FS as a whole are not free from material misstatement; or (b) the auditor is unable to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence to conclude on possible effects of uncertainties.
  • Decision matrix (HKSA 705 (Revised) para. A1 — Exhibit 9.4, 必背！):
Nature of matter Material but NOT pervasive Material AND pervasive
FS are materially misstated Qualified Opinion Adverse Opinion
Inability to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence Qualified Opinion Disclaimer of Opinion
  • Qualified opinion (HKSA 705 (Revised) para. 7): two circumstances — (1) quantified material misstatement confined to a particular balance/class/disclosure; (2) scope limitation where possible undetected misstatements could be material but not pervasive. Wording: "except for the effects of the matter…" (misstatement) or "except for the possible effects of the matter…" (inability to obtain evidence). Basis for Qualified Opinion must, to the extent practicable, quantify the financial effects.
  • Adverse opinion (HKSA 705 (Revised) para. 8): auditor HAS obtained sufficient appropriate evidence and concludes misstatements are both material and pervasive — e.g. failure to consolidate a subsidiary; going concern basis inappropriate but FS prepared on that basis. Wording: "because of the significance of the matter… the financial statements do not give a true and fair view…". Basis for Adverse Opinion sits immediately after the Opinion paragraph.
  • Disclaimer of opinion (HKSA 705 (Revised) paras. 9–10): unable to obtain sufficient appropriate evidence AND possible effects judged material and pervasive — essentially no opinion is expressed ("We were engaged to audit… We do not express an opinion…"). No Basis for Opinion in the normal form and the auditor's responsibilities section is abbreviated (HKSA 705 (Revised) Illustrations 4 and 5).
  • Going concern outcomes map (HKSA 570 (Revised) + Exhibit 9.7):
  • GC basis inappropriate but FS prepared on GC basis → Adverse Opinion (pervasive misstatement).
  • GC basis appropriate + material uncertainty exists + adequate disclosure → Unmodified opinion with a "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern" paragraph (NOT an EoM, NOT a modified opinion).
  • GC basis appropriate + material uncertainty + inadequate disclosure → Qualified or Adverse (misstatement of disclosure).
  • Cannot obtain evidence on whether a material uncertainty exists → Qualified or Disclaimer (inability to obtain evidence).

D. Additional communications in the auditor's report

  • Key Audit Matters — HKSA 701 (effective 15 December 2016):
  • Definition: matters that, in the auditor's professional judgement, were of most significance in the audit of the current period FS (HKSA 701 paras. 9–10 — selection funnel: matters communicated to TCWG → matters requiring significant auditor attention / significant risks per HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) / high estimation uncertainty → KAMs).
  • Mandatory for listed entities only; voluntary for others.
  • Each KAM description must include (HKSA 701 para. 11): (a) why the matter was considered most significant; (b) reference to related note disclosures; (c) how the matter was addressed in the audit (approach, procedures, key observations).
  • Introductory language must state KAMs were addressed in the context of the audit as a whole and no separate opinion is provided on them.
  • Matters giving rise to a modified opinion or MURGC are by nature KAMs but are NOT repeated in the KAM section — the KAM intro cross-refers to the Basis for Qualified/Adverse Opinion or MURGC section instead.
  • If there are no KAMs (rare), state so under the KAM heading.
  • Emphasis of Matter paragraph — HKSA 706 (Revised): refers to a matter appropriately presented or disclosed in the FS that is fundamental to users' understanding. Must state "Our opinion is not modified in respect of this matter." Common triggers: significant uncertainty around accounting estimates; special-purpose framework; early adoption of a HKFRS with pervasive effect; prior-period material error restated. A GC material uncertainty goes in the separate MURGC section, NOT an EoM.
  • Other Matter paragraph — HKSA 706 (Revised): refers to a matter OTHER than those presented or disclosed in the FS, relevant to users' understanding of the audit, the auditor's responsibilities or the auditor's report. Placed after the Opinion paragraph, and after EoM and KAM sections. Common triggers: prior-period FS audited by another auditor; new/amended auditor's report after discovery of material subsequent events; certain law/regulation reporting.
  • Other Information — HKSA 720 (Revised) (effective for periods ending on/after 15 December 2016): auditor reads other information (chairman's statement, MD&A, corporate governance report etc.) for material inconsistency with the FS or audit knowledge; no opinion or any form of assurance is given on it (HKSA 720 paras. 16, 21–22). Uncorrected material misstatement of other information → discuss with management; if unresolved, consider impact on the report. Directors are responsible for other information.
  • Report components (HKSA 700 (Revised)): Title (must say "Independent Auditor's Report"); Addressee (normally members/shareholders; state place of incorporation); Opinion presented FIRST (700.22, 700.23–27); Basis for Opinion — conducted per HKSAs, independence per the HKICPA Code of Ethics, evidence sufficient and appropriate (700.28); Other Information (700.32); Responsibilities of Directors/TCWG incl. going concern statement (700.33–36, 700.34(b), A49); Auditor's Responsibilities; Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements (700.37–42); plus engagement partner's name (listed audits), auditor's name, CPA (Practising)/CPA designation, address, date.
  • Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) overlay (PN 600.1 (Revised)): s.407(2) — auditor must investigate whether adequate accounting records kept and FS agree with records; s.407(3) — must state opinion in the report if adequate records NOT kept or FS NOT in agreement in any material respect. Applies to ALL reports including modified ones. Consolidation obligation: s.379(2).

E. Ethics（reporting 階段嘅道德壓力）

  • Client pressure NOT to issue a modified opinion / NOT to include a KAM = intimidation threat under the Code of Ethics.
  • Auditor must stand ground to comply with HKSAs, HKFRSs and the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), upholding the fundamental principles — integrity, objectivity, professional competence and due care, confidentiality, professional behaviour (COE Section 110, subsections 111–115, Chapter A revised 2021).
  • Threats of "second opinion" shopping or changing auditor do not justify softening the opinion.

易混淆概念對比

Table 1 — Modified opinion 決策：點樣分辨 Qualified / Adverse / Disclaimer

Qualified Opinion Adverse Opinion Disclaimer of Opinion
Root cause Material misstatement OR inability to obtain evidence — not pervasive Material misstatement (evidence obtained) — material AND pervasive Inability to obtain sufficient appropriate evidence — possible effects material AND pervasive
Evidence position Misstatement quantifiable (or possible effects bounded) Auditor HAS the evidence and knows FS are wrong Auditor CANNOT conclude — "essentially not an opinion"
Opinion wording "except for the effects / possible effects of the matter…" "…do not give a true and fair view…" "We do not express an opinion…"
Basis section Basis for Qualified Opinion (quantify effects where practicable) Basis for Adverse Opinion Basis for Disclaimer of Opinion; no normal Basis for Opinion; abbreviated auditor's responsibilities (HKSA 705 Illustrations 4–5)
考試伏位 「except for」= qualified；分清 misstatement 定 scope limitation 有證據 + pervasive 先係 adverse；唔好同 disclaimer 調亂 Disclaimer 只係「無法取證」先用得；已知錯誤（HKFRS departure）唔可以出 disclaimer

Table 2 — EoM vs OM vs MURGC vs KAM（考生最常調亂，Jun 2025 panelist 點名批評）

Emphasis of Matter (HKSA 706) Other Matter (HKSA 706) Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern (HKSA 570) Key Audit Matters (HKSA 701)
Matter located where? Already disclosed in the FS NOT presented/disclosed in the FS Going concern material uncertainty, adequately disclosed in FS Matters of most significance in the current audit
Relevant to… Users' understanding of the financial statements Users' understanding of the audit / auditor's responsibilities / report Users' understanding of the going concern uncertainty Insight into the auditor's focus areas

Flashcard 2 / 共 8 張
Question

State the HKSA 705 (Revised) opinion decision matrix (nature of matter × pervasiveness).

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 呢個 2×2 表係全章靈魂。口訣：「有證據 → Qualified/Adverse；無證據 → Qualified/Disclaimer；Pervasive 先會升級去 Adverse/Disclaimer。」

Flashcard 4 / 共 8 張
Question

How does a Disclaimer of Opinion differ from the other modified opinions?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 Disclaimer = 「我做唔到判斷」，唔係「份數有錯」。如果已經知道錯喺邊，就唔可以出 disclaimer — 呢個係 LP Knowledge Check 嘅經典伏位。

Flashcard 6 / 共 8 張
Question

Define a Key Audit Matter and state the THREE required elements of each KAM description (HKSA 701).

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 三元素口訣「Why–Where–How」。KAM 係近年新增、panelist 話考生温唔夠 — 定義同 purpose 一定要背到。(HKSA 701 paras. 9–11)

Flashcard 7 / 共 8 張
Question

Distinguish an Emphasis of Matter paragraph from an Other Matter paragraph (HKSA 706 (Revised)).

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 分辨關鍵：件事喺唔喺 FS 入面？喺 → EoM；唔喺 → OM。Jun 2025 考生將 OM 同 other information/KAM 調亂被批評，記實呢條分界線。

Flashcard 8 / 共 8 張
Question

Which statements must appear in the Basis for Opinion section of an (unmodified) auditor's report under HKSA 700 (Revised)?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer

💬 Basis for Opinion 三件寶：HKSAs、independence/Code、sufficient appropriate evidence。Disclaimer 就冇正常嘅 Basis for Opinion — 呢個對比考試成日考。

EN Term 繁中白話 English Model Sentence
Unmodified opinion 「無保留意見」— 最佳結果，FS 大致上跟足框架 An unmodified opinion states that the financial statements give a true and fair view in accordance with HKFRSs.
Qualified opinion 「保留意見」— 得某一處有問題，其餘冇事 Except for the effects of the matter described in the Basis for Qualified Opinion section, the financial statements give a true and fair view.
Adverse opinion 「否定意見」— 錯到成份 FS 唔可信 Because of the significance of the matter, the financial statements do not give a true and fair view.
Disclaimer of opinion 「無法表示意見」— 攞唔到證據，索性唔出意見 We do not express an opinion on the consolidated financial statements of the Group.
Pervasive 影響「遍及成份 FS」— 決定升級 adverse/disclaimer 嘅關鍵 The effects of the failure to consolidate the subsidiary are both material and pervasive.
Emphasis of Matter paragraph 提醒段 — 指向 FS 已披露嘅重要事項，不改變意見 We draw attention to Note X of the financial statements; our opinion is not modified in respect of this matter.
Key Audit Matters (KAM) 關鍵審計事項 — 上市先強制，講述核數師最關注嘅範疇 Key audit matters are those matters that, in our professional judgement, were of most significance in our audit of the current period.
