# M10 Chapter 12: Listed Companies and the Regulation of Financial Markets — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. Identify the key regulators of Hong Kong's financial markets (SFC, HKEX, HKMA, AFRC, IA, Financial Secretary) and distinguish the legal status of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571) from the Listing Rules.
2. Apply the prospectus regime under the Companies (Winding Up and Miscellaneous Provisions) Ordinance (Cap. 32), including exemptions, contents, registration, and civil/criminal liability for misstatements.
3. Explain the listing requirements for the Main Board and GEM Board, the role of sponsors, and the continuing disclosure obligations of listed issuers (notifiable transactions, connected transactions, public float, disclosure of interests).
4. Apply the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Buy-backs, including the 30% mandatory general offer trigger and directors' duties during an offer.
5. Analyse the six types of market misconduct under Parts XIII–XIV of the SFO, in particular insider dealing under s.270, and apply the statutory defences.

### 必背考點

#### A. Relevant Ordinances & Rules (按 LP 引用為準)

**Regulators & framework**
- **SFC** — statutory body established under the **Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571) ('SFO')**, Part II; six regulatory objectives in **s.4 SFO** (fairness/efficiency/transparency; public understanding; investor protection; minimise crime and misconduct; reduce systemic risk; assist Financial Secretary).
- **HKEX** — operates markets via wholly owned subsidiaries **SEHK** (equities/debt) and **HKFE** (derivatives); makes and administers the **Listing Rules** pursuant to **s.23 SFO**. Listing Rules are **NOT statute** — breach gives regulatory sanctions only: private reprimand, public criticism, public censure, requisition for rectification, and (most severe) delisting/rejection of application. No criminal or civil liability per se.
- **HKMA** — banking stability, Linked Exchange Rate system, Exchange Fund, FMIs. **AFRC** — established under the **Accounting and Financial Reporting Council Ordinance (Cap. 588)**; investigates auditors of listed entities (relevant irregularities, **s.4 AFRCO**; enquiry powers s.9). **IA** — established under the **Insurance Ordinance (Cap. 41)**.
- **Market Misconduct Tribunal (MMT)** — established under **Part XIII SFO**; independent; chaired by a High Court judge or former judge; hears civil market misconduct proceedings.

**Public issue of shares — prospectus regime (CWUMPO Cap. 32 + SFO)**
- Public company defined negatively: neither a private company nor a company limited by guarantee — **s.12 Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) ('CO')**; private company cannot invite the public to subscribe — **s.11(1) CO**.
- "Prospectus" widely defined — **s.2 CWUMPO** ("any prospectus, notice, circular, brochure, advertisement or other document offering shares or debentures…").
- Prohibition on public offer without a compliant prospectus — **s.38(3) CWUMPO**; "offer to the public" includes a section of the public; not an offer to the public if not calculated to result in shares becoming available to others — **s.48A(2) CWUMPO** (e.g. employee share schemes).
- Exemptions (**Sch.17 CWUMPO**, underwriters **s.38(3)(a)**, SFC exemption power **s.38A**): professional investors (incl. high net worth individuals with portfolio ≥ HK$8m); minimum consideration ≥ HK$500,000; offers to ≤ 50 persons or total consideration ≤ HK$5m; qualifying persons (directors/employees); charity/educational establishments; SFC-granted exemptions.
- Contents — **Sch.3 CWUMPO** (business, share capital, directors, 3-year turnover, material contracts, details of offer, substantial holders, auditor's report, land valuation report > HK$3m or >10% of assets); expert consent — **s.38C**; checklist at ss.38(1), 38(1A), 38D(2), Sch.3, Sch.18 Pt.1. LR Chapter 11A also applies — compliance with LR does **not** guarantee compliance with the CO/CWUMPO.
- Authorisation & registration — **s.38D(1) CWUMPO**: authorised by the SFC (for listing applicants, via the HKEX) and registered with the Registrar of Companies (**s.38D(7)**).
- Liability for misstatement:
  - Civil: **s.40 CWUMPO** (false/misleading statements incl. material omissions; directors, promoters, company and persons authorising issue liable — s.40(1); compensation to subscribers — s.40(7); defences: no consent / reasonable grounds to believe truth).
  - Civil: **s.108 SFO** (fraudulent, reckless or negligent misrepresentation inducing subscription; damages; does NOT apply where s.40 CWUMPO applies); **s.277 SFO** (false/misleading disclosure inducing transactions — also market misconduct).
  - Criminal: **s.40A CWUMPO** (untrue statement in prospectus; defences: immaterial / reasonable grounds of belief); **s.107 SFO** (fraudulent or reckless misrepresentation to induce acquisition/subscription); general criminal law (e.g. conspiracy to defraud).
- Advertising restrictions — **s.38B CWUMPO** (no extracts/abridged versions/advertisements except Sch.19 exceptions or SFC-approved ads under **s.105 SFO**); **s.103(1) SFO** blanket prohibition on unauthorised invitations to the public (exception: CWUMPO-compliant prospectus, **s.103(3)(a)**).

**Listing on the SEHK (Main Board Listing Rules 'LR'; GEM Rules)**
- Methods of listing (LR Ch.7): offers for subscription, offers for sale, placing, introduction, rights issue, open offer, capitalisation issue, consideration issue, exchange.
- Main Board qualifications (LR Ch.8, as from Jan 2022): profit test (≥ HK$35m latest year + ≥ HK$45m aggregate prior two years; 3-year track record); OR market cap/revenue/cash flow test (mkt cap ≥ HK$2bn + revenue ≥ HK$500m + 3-yr aggregate operating cash flow ≥ HK$100m); OR market cap/revenue test (mkt cap ≥ HK$4bn + revenue ≥ HK$500m); public float ≥ 25% + ≥ 300 shareholders; public mkt cap ≥ HK$125m; total mkt cap ≥ HK$500m; ≥ 2 executive directors ordinarily resident in HK; ≥ 3 INEDs = ≥ 1/3 of board.
- GEM (as at July 2019): 2-year trading record; positive 2-year aggregate operating cash flow ≥ HK$30m; mkt cap ≥ HK$150m; 2-year management / 1-year ownership continuity; ≥ 25% float + ≥ 100 shareholders; public mkt cap ≥ HK$45m; ≥ 3 INEDs = 1/3 of board.
- New regimes: LR Ch.8A weighted voting rights (innovative companies; mkt cap ≥ HK$40bn, or ≥ HK$10bn + revenue ≥ HK$1bn); LR Ch.18A pre-revenue biotech (mkt cap ≥ HK$1,500m; ≥ 2 years' operation; working capital ≥ 125% of costs for 12 months); LR Ch.18B SPACs (from 1 Jan 2022; De-SPAC deadline 36 months; target must satisfy all new listing requirements).
- Sponsor: corporation/authorised financial institution licensed for **Type 6 regulated activity (advising on corporate finance)**; due diligence duties (understand business, identify risks/irregularities, ensure listing criteria met, ensure adequate prospectus disclosure, ensure internal controls and competent directors); see HKEX Practice Note 21.
- Price stabilisation: **Securities and Futures (Price Stabilizing) Rules (Cap. 571W)** — permitted stabilising action within 30 days after IPO.

**Continuing obligations of listed issuers**
- LR Ch.13 continuing obligations (avoid false market; advances > 8% asset ratio; pledging of shares by controlling shareholder; winding-up; monthly returns; public float; annual/interim reports, etc.).
- Inside information disclosure — now statutory: **Part XIVA SFO** general duty of disclosure; directors must use best endeavours to ensure compliance and may incur personal liability.
- Notifiable transactions (LR Ch.14.06) — five ratios: assets, profits, revenue, consideration, equity capital. Six categories (ascending): share transaction (<5%, share consideration); disclosable (one ratio ≥5%, all <25%); major (one ratio ≥25%, all <75% disposal/100% acquisition); very substantial disposal (≥75%); very substantial acquisition (≥100%); reverse takeover. Requirements scale up: announcement → circular → shareholders' approval.
- Connected transactions (**LR Ch.14A**): connected persons include directors/chief executives, substantial shareholders (≥10%), past directors (12 months), close relatives, associates (spouse, children, trustees, ≥30%-controlled companies), supervisors (PRC issuers). Required actions: written agreement (14A.34); annual report disclosure (14A.71/14A.49); announcement (14A.35/14A.36); independent shareholders' approval with interested parties abstaining (14A.36–39); independent board committee of INEDs (14A.40–43); independent financial adviser (14A.44–45); circular (14A.46–48).
- Minimum public float: ≥ 25% at all times (LR Ch.13); inform HKEX and restore if breached; trading may be suspended.
- Disclosure of interests — **Part XV SFO**: substantial shareholders ≥ 5% of any class of voting shares (**s.310**); directors & chief executives (**s.341**); deemed interests of spouse/children/controlled corporations/trusts (**ss.316, 344**); registers kept by the listed company (**ss.336, 352**).

**Takeovers & share buy-backs (Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Buy-backs 'TC' + CO)**
- Mandatory general offer trigger: person (with concert parties) crossing **30% of voting rights**; acting-in-concert holdings aggregated.
- Scheme of arrangement — **s.166 CO**; cancellation or transfer scheme; driven by target (only if target supportive; common for privatisations); approval: ≥75% of votes cast by disinterested shareholders + votes against ≤ 10% of disinterested shares + court sanction.
- Key TC Rules: offer first to offeree board (R1.1); independent board committee + independent financial adviser (R2.1); delisting resolution 75% independent shareholders (R2.2); announcement of offer (R3); no frustrating action without shareholders' approval (R4); board not to resign mid-offer (R7); equal information to all shareholders (R8.1); highest standard of accuracy; directors jointly and severally responsible (R9.1–9.3); profit forecasts with due care (R10.1); valuations by named independent valuer (R11.1). Sanction: "Cold Shoulder Order" (denial of market access).
- Share buy-backs (listed): (1) on-market — ordinary resolution, valid until next AGM (**s.239(3) CO**); (2) off-market general offer — ordinary resolution (**s.238(1) CO**); (3) off-market other than general offer — **special resolution** (differential treatment → onerous approval). TC supplements the statutory regime.

**Corporate Governance Code (Appendix 14 LR, renamed 'Corporate Governance Code' from 1 Jan 2022)**
- Part 1: mandatory disclosure in CG Report (12 headings: CG practices, board, chairman/CEO, NEDs, committees, company secretary, directors' securities transactions/Model Code, risk management & internal control, auditor's remuneration, diversity, shareholders' rights, investor relations).
- Part 2: Principles + Code Provisions (CPs) + Recommended Best Practices (RBPs); **"comply or explain"** — deviation from a CP without explanation breaches the Listing Rules; RBPs are guidance only.
- Key CPs: C.2.1 chairman and chief executive roles separated; C.5.1 ≥ 4 board meetings p.a. at quarterly intervals; C.5.3 ≥ 14 days' notice of regular board meetings; C.6.1 company secretary an employee (or disclose senior contact of external provider); C.6.3 company secretary reports to chairman/CEO; B.3.1 nomination committee duties; D.2.1 annual review of risk management/internal control; D.2.6 whistleblowing policy; D.2.7 anti-corruption policy; E.1.5 disclose remuneration policy and senior management remuneration by band; F.1.1 dividend policy; F.2.1 separate resolutions (no bundling).
- ESG Reporting Guide (Appendix 27): ESG reports published with annual reports.

**Market misconduct (SFO Parts XIII & XIV)**
- Inside information — **s.245 SFO**: (1) about the listed corporation / its shareholder or officer / its listed securities or derivatives; (2) specific; (3) not generally known to those accustomed or likely to deal; (4) likely to materially affect price if generally known.
- Six types of market misconduct (civil, Part XIII): insider dealing (**s.270**); false trading (**s.274**); price rigging (**s.275**); disclosure of information about prohibited transactions (**s.276**); disclosure of false or misleading information inducing transactions (**s.277**); stock market manipulation (**s.278**).
- Insider dealing (s.270): connected person (director, employee, substantial shareholder of the corporation or a related corporation — **s.247**) with information he knows is inside information who (a) deals, (b) counsels or procures another to deal, or (c) discloses knowing/having reasonable cause to believe the other will deal. Covers tippees too. Defences **ss.271–273** (e.g. qualification shares; good-faith underwriting; liquidator/receiver/trustee; no profit or loss-avoidance motive s.271(3); Chinese walls s.271(2); innocent agents; equal information off-market; trustees on advice; pre-acquired subscription rights) — onus on defendant, balance of probabilities.
- False trading (s.274): intention to create false/misleading appearance of active trading or of the market (wash sales; matched orders); actual effect unnecessary — "real chance" suffices; defence s.274(6) (purposes did not include creating the false appearance; defendant proves the negative on balance of probabilities).
- Price rigging (s.275): wash sale or fictitious/artificial transaction with effect (or intention) of maintaining/increasing/reducing/stabilising/causing fluctuations in price; defence s.275(4).
- s.276: disclosing that a prohibited transaction will affect prices (secondary misconduct); defences s.276(2).
- s.277: disclosure of false/misleading information (as to material fact or omission) likely to induce dealings, with knowledge/recklessness/negligence; defences s.277(2)–(4) (mere reproducers/publishers, IT retransmitters, live broadcasters).
- s.278: two or more transactions intended to induce dealings by affecting/maintaining price; legitimate price stabilisation under Cap. 571W (made pursuant to s.282 SFO) is a defence.
- Dual regime: civil market misconduct (Part XIII, MMT, balance of probabilities) vs criminal offences (Part XIV, ss.291–299, proof beyond reasonable doubt; e.g. s.291 mirrors s.270). Other offences: s.300 (fraudulent/deceptive devices), s.302 (falsely representing futures dealings), s.384 (false or misleading information to the SFC).

#### B. Leading Cases (full citations — 以 LP 所引為準)

- **Securities and Futures Commission v Chan Pak Hoe Pablo (2011) 5 HKC 484** — "specific" information needs sufficient particularity to be identified, defined and unequivocally expressed; contrast mere rumour/vague hopes; lack of precision does not defeat specificity; ongoing negotiations needing further approval can still be inside information.
- **Securities and Futures Commission v Young Bik Fung (2016) 1 HKLRD 1249** — tipping by a solicitor who learnt of a privatisation at his firm; tippees who knew the information was confidential price-sensitive information liable; inference from circumstantial evidence.
- **Leung Chi Keung v Market Misconduct Tribunal (2012) 2 HKLRD 786** — two elements: (1) information connection (specific, non-public, price-sensitive); (2) person connection to a defined insider; "connected person" test is objective (what others might reasonably expect of the position); a Chinese wall is only a relevant consideration; information need not be "certain".
- **HKSAR v Fu Kor Kuen Patrick (2012) 15 HKCFAR 524** — circular warrant trading for commission rebates; s.295(5) deeming provision; statutory defence in s.295(7) succeeded — persuasive burden on defendants on balance of probabilities; genuine economic purpose (rebate farming) negatived the purpose of creating a false appearance.
- **HKSAR v Li Jialin (2014) 2 HKLRD 538** — price rigging: chairman's cross-account trades with no change in beneficial ownership; "price" = nominal price (last traded price informed by bid/ask queues) per s.296(1) SFO.
- **SEHK Circular dated 18 December 2018 re Golden Meditech Holdings Ltd (Stock Code: 801)** — privatisation structured as disposal was a very substantial disposal (Rules 14.48, 14.49, 14.51); failure to announce (Rule 14.34) and obtain shareholders' approval = breach.
- **SEHK Circular dated 20 July 2018 re SRE Group Ltd (Stock Code: 1207)** — undisclosed guarantees for chairman-connected parties were connected transactions; breach of LR 14A.35, 14A.36, 14A.46, 14A.49.
- **Disciplinary Proceedings against Daqing Dairy and Its Directors (SFC Takeovers Bulletin Issue 27, Dec 2013)** — failure to dispatch offeree board circular under TC Rule 8.4; Cold Shoulder Order (2 years) + public censure.
- **SFC Takeovers and Mergers Panel decision re ENM Holdings Ltd (announcement 2 July 2015)** — nominee acquisition hiding concert-party stake to avoid mandatory offer; Cold Shoulder Orders (10 years / 2 years).

#### C. IRAC Application Points

- **Issue**: classify first — is the question about (a) prospectus liability, (b) listing/continuing obligations, (c) disclosure of interests, (d) takeovers, or (e) market misconduct? 每類有唔同嘅 statute/rule source，分清 SFO（刑事/民事法律責任）vs Listing Rules/TC（監管後果）係第一步。
- **Rule**: state the pinpoint provision — e.g. insider dealing = s.270 SFO + definition of inside information s.245 + connected person s.247; disclosure of interests = ss.310/341/316/344 SFO; mandatory offer = 30% under TC; prospectus misstatement = s.40 (civil) / s.40A (criminal) CWUMPO.
- **Application**: map facts to elements one by one — for insider dealing check (i) information connection (specific? not generally known? price-sensitive? — apply *Chan Pak Hoe* and *Leung Chi Keung*); (ii) person connection (director/employee/substantial shareholder or tippee); (iii) dealing limb vs counselling/procuring limb vs disclosure limb; then consider defences (ss.271–273).
- **Conclusion**: decisive — e.g. "Therefore X engaged in insider dealing under s.270 SFO and Y, as tippee, is equally liable." 如果涉及 Listing Rules 違反，結論要講明後果係監管制裁（public censure / Cold Shoulder Order / delisting），唔係刑事責任。
- **考試伏位**: (1) spouse/children/controlled-company shares are **deemed interests** — hiding by transferring to spouse does NOT escape Part XV; (2) negotiation-stage information can still be "specific"; (3) 30% trigger counts **concert parties' aggregate** holdings; (4) compliance with Listing Rules ≠ compliance with CWUMPO prospectus law; (5) GEM vs Main Board thresholds 數字要熟（HK$80m 3-yr profit / HK$30m 2-yr cash flow; HK$500m vs HK$150m mkt cap）。

### 易混淆概念對比

**Comparison 1: Market misconduct — civil regime vs criminal regime under the SFO**

| 項目 | Civil regime (Part XIII SFO) | Criminal regime (Part XIV SFO) |
|---|---|---|
| Forum | Market Misconduct Tribunal (chaired by High Court judge/former judge) | Criminal courts |
| Provisions | s.270 (insider dealing), ss.274–278 | ss.291–299 (mirror provisions, e.g. s.291 mirrors s.270) |
| Standard of proof | Balance of probabilities | Beyond reasonable doubt |
| Enforcer | SFC (proceedings before MMT) | Prosecution (DoJ) |
| Outcome | Regulatory/civil orders (e.g. Cold Shoulder Order, disqualification, disgorgement) | Criminal sanctions (fines, imprisonment) |

點樣分辨：睇題目講 "market misconduct" 定 "offence/charged"。MMT 處理 civil limb；同一行為可以同時觸發兩個 regime。考試伏位：唔好以為 insider dealing 只係刑事 — LP 強調 dual regime，寫答案要兩邊都提。

**Comparison 2: Insider dealing (s.270) vs False trading (s.274) vs Price rigging (s.275)**

| 項目 | Insider dealing (s.270) | False trading (s.274) | Price rigging (s.275) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core vice | Trading on / tipping inside information (unfair informational advantage) | Creating false or misleading appearance of active trading or of the market | Wash sale or fictitious/artificial transaction affecting the **price** |
| Key element | Inside information + connected person + deal/counsel/disclose | Intention to create false appearance (wash sales, matched orders); real chance suffices | No change in beneficial ownership + effect/intention to move or stabilise price |
| Defence | ss.271–273 (e.g. no profit motive, Chinese walls) | s.274(6): purposes did not include creating false appearance | s.275(4): same structure as s.274(6) |
| Leading case | *SFC v Chan Pak Hoe*; *SFC v Young Bik Fung*; *Leung Chi Keung v MMT* | *HKSAR v Fu Kor Kuen Patrick* (defence succeeded — rebate farming) | *HKSAR v Li Jialin* ("price" = nominal price) |

點樣分辨：問「佢係咪知咗啲未公開嘅價格敏感消息先買賣？」→ insider dealing。問「佢係咪左手交右手製造交投暢旺假象？」→ false trading（重點係 appearance of activity）。問「左手交右手之餘，效果係推高/穩定個價？」→ price rigging。考試伏位：false trading 同 price rigging 都涉及 wash sales，分別在於 aim/effect 係 "appearance of trading/market" 定 "price"；一個事實可以同時構成兩者，記住分開討論。

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** Under the SFO, which body hears civil market misconduct proceedings, and how is it constituted?

**Back:**
- The Market Misconduct Tribunal (MMT), established under Part XIII of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571).
- It is independent of the SFC and the government; chaired by a judge or former judge of the High Court of Hong Kong.
- Civil regime: balance of probabilities; the criminal regime sits separately in Part XIV (ss.291–299, beyond reasonable doubt).

💬 白話解釋：SFC 係「捉人」嗰個，MMT 係「審案」嗰個（民事線），記住 Part XIII = civil、Part XIV = criminal，考試成日問 dual regime。

### Card 2
**Front:** What does s.38(3) of the CWUMPO prohibit, and what are the main prospectus exemptions under Sch.17?

**Back:**
- S.38(3) CWUMPO (Cap. 32): a company must not offer shares to the public without a prospectus complying with the CWUMPO.
- Sch.17 exemptions: offers to professional investors (incl. individuals with portfolio ≥ HK$8m); minimum consideration ≥ HK$500,000; offers to ≤ 50 persons or total consideration ≤ HK$5m; offers to qualifying persons (directors/employees/officers/consultants); charity/educational establishments; takeovers/share repurchases compliant with the Takeover Codes.
- Also: underwriting invitations in good faith (s.38(3)(a)); SFC exemption power (s.38A).

💬 白話解釋：招股就一定要招股書，但係「專業投資者、大額、少人數、自己友」呢幾類就豁免。記三個數字：HK$8m（portfolio）、HK$500,000（minimum consideration）、50 人 / HK$5m（small-scale）。

### Card 3
**Front:** State the three alternative financial tests for a Main Board listing (LR Ch.8, as from Jan 2022).

**Back:**
- Profit test: profit ≥ HK$35m in the most recent year AND ≥ HK$45m aggregate for the two preceding years (3-year track record; total mkt cap ≥ HK$500m).
- Market cap/Revenue/Cash flow test: mkt cap ≥ HK$2bn; revenue ≥ HK$500m (latest audited year); positive aggregate operating cash flow ≥ HK$100m over 3 preceding years.
- Market cap/Revenue test: mkt cap ≥ HK$4bn; revenue ≥ HK$500m (latest audited year).
- All tests: 3-year management continuity, 1-year ownership continuity, ≥ 25% public float, ≥ 300 shareholders, ≥ 3 INEDs (≥ 1/3 of board).

💬 白話解釋：主板三條路 —「有盈利」、「有市值有收入有現金流」、「超大市值有收入」。GEM 就簡單啲：兩年正經營現金流 ≥ HK$30m + 市值 ≥ HK$150m。數字係必考位。

### Card 4
**Front:** Define "inside information" under s.245 of the SFO.

**Back:**
Information:
1. About (i) a listed corporation, (ii) a shareholder or officer of it, or (iii) its listed securities or their derivatives;
2. Specific — sufficient particularity to be identified, defined and unequivocally expressed (*SFC v Chan Pak Hoe Pablo (2011) 5 HKC 484*);
3. Not generally known to persons accustomed or likely to deal in those securities; and
4. Likely to materially affect the price of the listed securities if generally known.

💬 白話解釋：四個元素 = 「關於間公司/股東/證券」+「具體」+「未公開」+「會郁個價」。記住 Chan Pak Hoe：講緊嘅交易傾緊、仲未簽，都可以係 specific information — 唔係謠言咁虛就得。

### Card 5
**Front:** What are the three limbs of insider dealing under s.270 of the SFO, and who is a "connected person"?

**Back:**
- A person connected with a listed corporation, having information he knows is inside information, who: (1) deals in the listed securities of the corporation or a related corporation; (2) counsels or procures another to deal; or (3) discloses the information knowing/having reasonable cause to believe the other will deal or procure another to deal.
- Connected person (s.247): director, employee or substantial shareholder of the listed corporation or a related corporation (parent/subsidiary/sister).
- Tippees who receive inside information from a connected person and deal are also caught.
- Defences: ss.271–273 (onus on defendant, balance of probabilities).

💬 白話解釋：三招都中 — 自己買賣、叫人買賣、爆畀人知佢會買賣。收料嗰個（tippee）都一齊中。考試鍾意考「淨係講咗畀朋友聽」夠唔夠成 — 答案係夠（disclosure limb）。

### Card 6
**Front:** When is a mandatory general offer triggered under the Takeover Codes, and what is the relevance of "acting in concert"?

**Back:**
- Trigger: a person initially holding less than 30% of voting rights acquires voting rights taking his holding to 30% or more of the voting rights of the listed company.
- Concert parties: the Takeover Codes aggregate the collective holdings of persons acting in concert when testing the 30% threshold.
- Effect: the offeror must make a general offer to all shareholders, protecting minorities' right to exit on a change of control (*ENM Holdings* — hiding a concert-party stake via nominees breached the Codes; Cold Shoulder Orders imposed).

💬 白話解釋：一過 30% 就要向全部股東提出全面收購，冇得揀。搵人代持（nominee）夾埋過 30% 都照計 — ENM 案就係咁，結果 Cold Shoulder Order 十年唔准掂市場。

### Card 7
**Front:** Distinguish a "notifiable transaction" from a "connected transaction" under the Listing Rules, and outline the key requirements for a connected transaction.

**Back:**
- Notifiable transactions (LR Ch.14): classified by SIZE using five ratios (assets, profits, revenue, consideration, equity capital) — share / disclosable / major / very substantial disposal / very substantial acquisition / reverse takeover.
- Connected transactions (LR Ch.14A): classified by PARTIES — transaction with a connected person (director, chief executive, ≥10% substantial shareholder, past director within 12 months, close relatives, associates).
- Connected transaction requirements: written agreement (14A.34); annual report disclosure (14A.71/49); announcement (14A.35/36); independent shareholders' approval with interested shareholders abstaining (14A.36–39); independent board committee of INEDs (14A.40–43); independent financial adviser (14A.44–45); circular (14A.46–48).

💬 白話解釋：Notifiable 睇「幾大單」，Connected 睇「同邊個做」。Connected transaction 因為唔係 arm's length，所以要獨立股東批准 + INED 委員會 + 獨立財務顧問三重保障，利益相關股東要避席唔准投票。

### Card 8
**Front:** What are the civil and criminal liabilities for misstatements in a prospectus, and the respective defences?

**Back:**
- Civil — s.40 CWUMPO: false or misleading statement (incl. material omission); directors, promoters, the company and persons who authorised issue liable to compensate subscribers (s.40(1), (7)). Defences: issued without consent; reasonable grounds to believe the statement true.
- Civil — s.108 SFO: fraudulent/reckless/negligent misrepresentation inducing subscription; damages (inapplicable where s.40 CWUMPO applies).
- Criminal — s.40A CWUMPO: authorising issue of a prospectus with an untrue statement. Defences: statement immaterial; reasonable grounds to believe and did believe it true up to issue.
- Criminal — s.107 SFO: fraudulent or reckless misrepresentation to induce acquisition/subscription.
- Also: s.277 SFO (false/misleading disclosure inducing transactions — market misconduct); general law of misrepresentation; conspiracy to defraud.

💬 白話解釋：招股書講大話，民事刑事都走唔甩。民事主戰場係 s.40 CWUMPO（董事、promoters、公司都要賠）；刑事係 s.40A CWUMPO 同 s.107 SFO。防線係「有合理理由相信係真」或「唔關我事我冇同意出」。

| Initial Public Offering (IPO) | 公司第一次向公眾發售股份 | The first time a company offers its shares to the public is known as an Initial Public Offering. |
| Prospectus | 招股書 — 向公眾投資者介紹公司嘅文件，受 CWUMPO 嚴格規管 | Under s.38(3) of the CWUMPO, a company must not offer shares to the public without a compliant prospectus. |
| Sponsor | 保薦人 — 持 Type 6 牌照、幫公司做上市申請同盡職審查嘅機構 | A sponsor must be licensed for Type 6 regulated activities (advising on corporate finance) and must conduct due diligence on the listing applicant. |
| Public float | 公眾持股量 — 上市公司至少要 25% 股份喺公眾手上 | An issuer must maintain a public float of at least 25% of its total issued share capital at all times under Chapter 13 of the Listing Rules. |
| Connected person | 關連人士 — 董事、主要股東（≥10%）、過去 12 個月嘅董事及佢哋嘅 associates | A substantial shareholder holding 10% or more of a listed issuer is a connected person under Chapter 14A of the Listing Rules. |
| Mandatory general offer | 強制性全面收購 — 持股達 30% 就要向全體股東提出收購 | When a person's holding reaches 30% of the voting rights, the Takeover Codes require a mandatory general offer to all shareholders. |
| Inside information | 內幕消息 — 具體、未公開、會重大影響股價嘅消息（s.245 SFO） | Inside information is specific information not generally known which would likely materially affect the price of the listed securities if generally known. |
| Cold Shoulder Order | 冷淡對待令 — 禁止違規者直接或間接參與證券市場嘅制裁 | The Takeovers Panel imposed a Cold Shoulder Order denying the director access to the securities markets for ten years. |
