# M10 Chapter 12: Listed Companies and the Regulation of Financial Markets — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** The Listing Rules are made pursuant to s.23 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571) but are not statute. They are a set of rules applicable to issuers who choose to list on the HKEX — effectively the "playbook" of the HKEX. Breach of the Listing Rules does not give rise to criminal or civil liability per se; the consequences are regulatory: the HKEX may impose a private reprimand, a public statement with criticism, a public censure, a requisition for rectification, and at the extreme, delisting or rejection of a listing application.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題考 Listing Rules 嘅法律地位，係 LP 12.1.3.3 嘅核心比較。A 錯：Listing Rules 唔係立法會制定嘅 primary legislation，違反亦唔會有刑事責任。C 錯：Listing Rules 由 HKEX 頒布（依據 SFO s.23），唔係 SFC 嘅 subsidiary legislation；MMT 處理嘅係 SFO 下嘅 market misconduct，唔關 Listing Rules 事。D 錯：LP 冇講 Listing Rules 只適用於香港註冊公司；規則綁定嘅係所有選擇喺 HKEX 上市嘅 issuers。B 係唯一準確描述「非法例、無刑事民事責任、但 HKEX 有監管制裁（最嚴重係除牌）」嘅選項。

**Rule Check:** Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571) s.23; LP §12.1.3.3 (Listing Rules: Legal Status and Enforcement).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 學生最易將 Listing Rules 當成法例嚟答「違反會坐監/罰款」。記住分層：SFO = statute（刑事+民事責任）；Listing Rules / Takeover Codes = 監管規則（public censure、Cold Shoulder Order、delisting）。考試凡問 "consequences of breach" 一定要分清呢兩層。

### Q2 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** Under s.310 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571), a substantial shareholder of a listed corporation is a person (individual or corporation) who is interested in 5% or more of any class of voting share capital of the listed corporation. Such a person must disclose the interest under Part XV of the SFO, and the listed corporation must keep a register of the disclosed interests (s.336 of the SFO). "Interest" is widely defined and includes deemed interests through a spouse, children, controlled corporations and trusts (ss.316 and 344 of the SFO).

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題考精準嘅百分比門檻，四個選項全部都係本章出現過嘅數字，所以係典型「鄰近概念」distractors。A（10%）係 Chapter 14A Listing Rules 下 "connected person" 嘅 substantial shareholder 門檻 — 唔同 regime 唔同數字。B（25%）係 public float 嘅最低要求。D（30%）係 Takeover Codes 下 mandatory general offer 嘅觸發線。C（5%）先係 SFO Part XV s.310 嘅 substantial shareholder 披露門檻。

**Rule Check:** Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571) ss.310, 316, 336, 344; LP §12.3.2.4 (Disclosure of Interests).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 四個百分比（5% / 10% / 25% / 30%）分別屬於四個唔同制度：Part XV 披露 5%、connected person 10%、public float 25%、mandatory offer 30%。考試最鍾意將佢哋溝埋，溫習時要整齊排成一條線記。

### Q3 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** Wendy has engaged in insider dealing under s.270 of the SFO. Issue: whether a sale made to avoid a loss, before disclosure of an adverse confidential judgment, constitutes insider dealing. Rule: under s.245 of the SFO, inside information is specific information about a listed corporation, not generally known to persons likely to deal in its securities, which would likely materially affect the price if generally known (*SFC v Chan Pak Hoe Pablo (2011) 5 HKC 484*). Under s.270, a person connected with the corporation (a director, employee or substantial shareholder — s.247) who deals in its listed securities whilst possessing information she knows to be inside information engages in insider dealing. Application: the judgment is specific (a decided court order for a quantified sum), non-public and plainly price-sensitive (40% of net assets; the price fell 30% on announcement). Wendy is an officer/employee of the company and dealt in its shares. The s.271(3) defence (absence of a profit or loss-avoidance motive) cannot assist her: her very purpose was to avoid a loss. Conclusion: Wendy engaged in insider dealing under s.270 of the SFO (and is equally exposed to the mirror criminal offence in s.291 under Part XIV).

**解題思路（繁中）:** IRAC 四步：(I) 賣股避損係咪 insider dealing；(R) s.245 定義 + s.270 dealing limb + s.247 connected person；(A) 逐個元素對應事實 — 判決具體（有判決金額）、未公開、price-sensitive（公佈後跌 30% 證明），Wendy 係 company secretary = officer/employee，佢落盤賣股 = deals；(C) 成立。A 錯：s.271(3) 嘅 defence 係「冇謀利**或避損**意圖」— Wendy 正正係為咗避損，所以反而係失敗關鍵，唔係免責理由。C 錯：false trading (s.274) 重點係製造交投/市場假象（wash sales、matched orders），單純一個人真實賣自己嘅股完全唔啱。D 錯：s.278 要求兩宗或以上交易且意圖誘使他人買賣，Wendy 得一次真實沽貨，冇呢個意圖。

**Rule Check:** Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571) ss.245, 247, 270, 271(3), 291; *Securities and Futures Commission v Chan Pak Hoe Pablo (2011) 5 HKC 484*.

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 學生最常犯兩個錯：(1) 以為「冇賺錢」就冇罪 — 法例包埋 loss avoidance，s.271(3) defence 反而要求你證明連避損意圖都冇；(2) 見到「影響股價」就亂揀 s.274/s.278 — 記住 insider dealing 嘅 fingerprint 係「未公開價格敏感消息 + 自己人 + 買賣/教唆/披露」。

### Q4 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** Issue: whether the joint acquisition triggers a mandatory general offer. Rule: under the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Buy-backs, a person who initially holds less than 30% of the voting rights of a listed company and acquires voting rights taking his holding to 30% or more must make a mandatory general offer to all shareholders; the collective holdings of persons acting in concert are aggregated for this purpose. Application: Mr. Fu (18%) and Ms. Kam (14%) consult and vote together under a long-standing arrangement — they are acting in concert, and their holdings are aggregated (32% even before the latest acquisition, 34% after). The 30% threshold is therefore crossed, triggering the mandatory general offer obligation; concealing the arrangement (as in the *ENM Holdings* decision, where Cold Shoulder Orders were imposed) does not avoid it. Conclusion: a mandatory general offer is required.

**解題思路（繁中）:** A 錯：呢個係題目刻意設計嘅陷阱 — 單獨睇每人都低過 30%，但 Takeover Codes 明文會將 acting in concert 人士嘅持股加埋計，18% + 14% + 2% = 34%，一早過線。C 錯：Part XV 披露責任（個人 ≥5%）固然存在，但唔係「唯一」責任 — 題目問嘅係收購義務，而 acting in concert 過 30% 觸發 mandatory general offer 先係核心 issue；揀 C 即係 spot 錯 issue。D 錯：呢個係將 connected transaction / notifiable transaction 嘅股東批准機制亂搬過嚟，Takeover Codes 下冇呢個程序。B 準確點出 concert party aggregation 同 mandatory offer 後果。

**Rule Check:** Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Buy-backs (mandatory general offer at 30% of voting rights; aggregation of concert parties' holdings); *SFC Takeovers and Mergers Panel Decision in Relation to Shares of ENM Holdings Limited (announcement dated 2 July 2015)*; LP §12.3.3.1.

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 最大伏位係「逐個人單獨計」而忽略 acting in concert aggregation — ENM 案就係用 nominee 隱藏一致行動持股，結果中 Cold Shoulder Order。答題時見到「朋友/親人一齊投票、代持、聯名公司買股」就要即刻諗 concert parties。

### Q5 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** Issue: whether Kelvin's self-dealing circular trades are conclusively false trading, or whether a statutory defence is available. Rule: wash-sale style dealings fall within the deeming provision in s.295(5) of the SFO (offers to sell and buy at substantially the same price involving the same or an associate's offers), but s.295(7) provides a statutory defence — as does s.274(6) for the civil misconduct — where the defendant proves on the balance of probabilities that the purposes of the transactions did not include creating a false or misleading appearance of active trading (*HKSAR v Fu Kor Kuen Patrick (2012) 15 HKCFAR 524*). Application: Kelvin's daily self-dealing at substantially the same prices falls within s.295(5). However, the evidence of a genuine economic purpose — earning commission rebates ("commission farming") — may, as in *Fu Kor Kuen Patrick*, support an inference that creating a false appearance was not among his purposes; the persuasive burden lies on him. Conclusion: Kelvin is prima facie caught but may succeed on the statutory defence on the facts given.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題係 hard tier，考「deeming provision + 法定免責辯護」兩層分析，原型係 CFA 嘅 Fu Kor Kuen Patrick 案。A 錯：自我交易只係觸發 s.295(5) 嘅 deemed false trading，唔係「conclusively guilty」— s.295(7) 明確畀辯護。C 錯：price rigging (s.275) 同 false trading 係兩個獨立罪名，檢控官揀咗 false trading 條 charge 就唔會「只能」變 price rigging；而且本案重點係交投假象唔係價格操縱。D 錯：自己同名戶口之間嘅買賣仍然係 "transactions"，正正係 wash sale 嘅典型。B 準確覆述判決：burden 喺被告、balance of probabilities、真正經濟目的（賺回佣）可以成立 defence。

**Rule Check:** Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571) ss.274(6), 295(1)(a), 295(5), 295(7); *HKSAR v Fu Kor Kuen Patrick (2012) 15 HKCFAR 524*.

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 學生通常背咗「wash sale = false trading」就以為一定入罪，忽略埋 s.274(6)/s.295(7) 嘅 purpose defence 同埋被告要「證明一個 negative」（證明自己冇製造假象嘅意圖）。考試遇到 circular trading 題，記住三段式：deeming → burden on defendant → genuine economic purpose 可脫身。
