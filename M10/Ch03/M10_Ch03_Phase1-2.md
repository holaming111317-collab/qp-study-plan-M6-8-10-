# M10 Chapter 3: Principles of Contract and Consumer Law in Hong Kong (Part 2) — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives

1. **Analyse** the different vitiating factors (common mistake, misrepresentation, duress, undue influence, unconscionability, illegality) and their legal effects on a contract (void vs voidable).
2. **Analyse** how a contract can be discharged by performance, agreement, breach and frustration, and **apply** the rules on loss apportionment under the Law Amendment and Reform (Consolidation) Ordinance (Cap. 23).
3. **Apply** the rules relating to the award of damages (remoteness, mitigation, liquidated damages) and **evaluate** the range of remedies (action for an agreed sum, deposits, specific performance, injunctions).
4. **Apply** the key provisions of the Sale of Goods Ordinance (Cap. 26), the Supply of Services (Implied Terms) Ordinance (Cap. 457) and the Trade Descriptions Ordinance (Cap. 362) to business-to-consumer scenarios.

### 必背考點

#### A. Vitiating Factors（令合約 void 或 voidable 嘅因素）

**1. Common Mistake（雙方共同錯誤）— 效果：void ab initio**
- Rule: 雙方犯同一錯誤，訂約後先發現 → contract void from the beginning。
- Necessary elements（*Great Peace Shipping Ltd v Tsavliris Salvage (International) Ltd* (2002) EWCA Civ 1407）：(1) common assumption as to a state of affairs; (2) no promise by either party that it exists; (3) non-existence not attributable to either party's fault; (4) non-existence renders performance impossible; (5) the state of affairs is the existence/vital attribute of the consideration。
- Limitations：(a) contractual allocation of risk（*Jan Albert (HK) Ltd v Shu Kong Garment Factory Ltd* (1990) 1 HKLR 317 — 被告 expressly undertook to procure export quota → 承擔風險，no common mistake）; (b) fault of one party。
- Three categories: mistake as to subject matter（*Couturier v Hastie* (1856) 5 HL Cas 673 — 粟米已被賣咗，買方唔使畀錢）; mistake as to ability to perform（physical/legal impossibility，*Cooper v Phibbs* (1867) LR 2 HL 149）; mistake as to quality — **test of essential difference**（*Bell v Lever Bros Ltd* (1932) AC 161 — 極窄；買錯畫唔夠，*Leaf v International Galleries* (1950) 2 KB 86）。事件喺訂約**前**已發生 → common mistake（*Griffith v Brymer* (1903) 19 TLR 434）；訂約**後**先發生 → frustration。

**2. Misrepresentation — 效果：voidable（可以 rescind）**
- Actionable misrepresentation = **false statement of fact that induces** the representee to enter into the contract。
- Silence 一般唔算（caveat emptor, *Keates v The Earl of Cadogan* (1851) 10 CB 591），但 exceptions：(1) active steps to conceal（*Horsfall v Thomas* (1862) 1 H & C 90 — 但必須 known to representee 先算 induced）; (2) half-truths（*Dimmock v Hallett* (1866) LR 2 Ch App 21）; (3) change of circumstances（*With v O'Flanagan* (1936) Ch 575）; (4) fiduciary relationships; (5) contracts uberrimae fidei（e.g. insurance）。
- Fact vs opinion：opinion 一般唔算（*Bisset v Wilkinson* (1927) AC 177），但 maker in a better position to know → implied assertion of fact（*Smith v Land & House Property Corp* (1884) 28 Ch D 7）；future intention 一般唔算，除非無該真實意圖（*Edgington v Fitzmaurice* (1885) 24 Ch D 459）。
- Inducement 四要件：material / known to representee / intended to be acted upon / actually acted upon（*Attwood v Small* (1838) 6 Cl & F 232 — 自行查證 → 無 reliance；但 *Redgrave v Hurd* (1881) 20 Ch D 1 — 有機會查但無查，仍可有 actionable misrep）。
- Types: **fraudulent**（knowingly / without belief / recklessly — *Derry v Peek* (1889) 14 App Cas 337）; **negligent**（honest but no reasonable care）; **innocent**（honest + reasonable grounds）。
- **Rescission**：equitable, all-or-nothing，voidable contract 被 set aside。**Bars to rescission**: (1) affirmation; (2) lapse of time（fraud → time runs from discovery; negligent/innocent → from contract formation — *Leaf v International Galleries*）; (3) restitution impossible（*Clarke v Dickson* (1858) EB & E 148）; (4) third party rights（*Car and Universal Finance Co Ltd v Caldwell* (1961) 1 QB 525；s.25 SOGO (Cap. 26) sale under voidable title）。
- **Damages**：
  - Fraudulent (tort of deceit): 所有直接 flowing from the transaction 嘅損失，唔使 foreseeable（*Doyle v Olby Ironmongers Ltd* (1969) 2 QB 158；*Smith New Court Securities Ltd v Scrimgeour Vickers (Asset Management) Ltd* (1997) AC 254 — 可計及成交後跌價）；contributory negligence 不相干。
  - Negligent at common law (negligent misstatement): 要 special relationship + duty of care（*Hedley Byrne & Co Ltd v Heller & Partners Ltd* (1964) AC 465；*Caparo Industries Plc v Dickman* (1990) 2 AC 605）；burden on representee；remoteness = reasonably foreseeable。
  - Innocent at common law: **no damages**（*Redgrave v Hurd*）。
  - **Misrepresentation Ordinance (Cap. 284)（MO）s.3(1)**：statutory negligent misrep — (i) 唔使 special relationship; (ii) burden reversed（representor 要證明有 reasonable grounds believe 屬真）; (iii) remoteness = fiction of fraud（all direct losses — *Royscot Trust Ltd v Rogerson* (1991) 2 QB 297）。**前提**：representee 被誘導同 **representor 本人**訂約（誘導去同第三者訂約 → 只可以 common law 追討）。
  - **MO s.3(2)**：non-fraudulent misrep → court discretion 判 damages in lieu of rescission（若 rescission 已 barred 就用唔到）；s.3(3)：s.3(1) award 要扣減 s.3(2) award。
  - Contributory negligence 可減 damages（s.21(1) Law Amendment and Reform (Consolidation) Ordinance (Cap. 23)），但 fraud 除外。

**3. Duress（尤指 economic duress）— 效果：voidable**
- 三類：duress to the person（*Barton v Armstrong* (1976) AC 104）/ to goods（*Astley v Reynolds* (1731) 2 Str 915）/ **economic duress**（最常考）。
- Economic duress 三要件：(1) **illegitimate pressure**（多為威脅 breach of contract；區分正常商業討價還價 — *Pao On v Lau Yiu Long* (1980) AC 614；*Tung Wing Steel Co Ltd v George Wimpey International Ltd* HCA No. 3285/1984 — 買方可以轉去第二個供應商 → 無 illegitimate pressure）; (2) **causal link**（but-for / significant cause — *Dimskal Shipping Co SA v ITF (The Evia Luck) (No. 2)* (1992) 2 AC 152）; (3) **no practicable alternative**（*Estinah v Golden Hand Indonesian Employment Agency* (2001) 4 HKC 607）。
- Remedy: rescission（若只是 variation 受脅迫，只撤銷 variation 部分）；若構成 tort of intimidation 可索償。**Trap**：delay/affirmation 會失權 — *North Ocean Shipping Co Ltd v Hyundai Construction Co Ltd (The Atlantic Baron)* (1979) QB 705（17 個月後先 protest → 已 affirm，收唔返 extra 10%）。

**4. Undue Influence — 效果：voidable（equitable doctrine，闊過 duress）**
- Class 1 actual undue influence（overt improper pressure，唔使有 threat）。
- Class 2 presumed undue influence = (1) relationship of trust and confidence + (2) transaction calling for explanation（*Royal Bank of Scotland Plc v Etridge (No. 2)* (2002) 2 AC 773；*Li Sau Ying v Bank of China (Hong Kong) Ltd* (2005) 1 HKLRD 106）。
  - Class 2A（irrebuttable presumption of influence）：parent–child、religious leader–disciple、doctor–patient、solicitor–client、fiancé–fiancée。**Husband–wife 唔屬 2A**（考試伏位！）。
  - Class 2B：要受害人提出 trust and confidence 嘅證據（包括 husband–wife、employer–employee）。
  - Rebuttable presumption 成立後，burden shifts to the influencer。
- Spouse surety：wife 要 set aside guarantee，須證明 bank (1) **put on inquiry**（非商業 surety 關係）+ (2) **failed to take reasonable steps**（最穩陣做法：取得獨立律師 confirmation letter — Etridge）。
- Remedy: rescission（同樣受 bars 限制）；barred 時或可獲 damages in lieu。

**5. Unconscionability — 效果：voidable**
- Two-stage test（*Commercial Bank of Australia Ltd v Amadio* (1982–1983) 151 CLR 447；HK 承認 — *Ming Shiu Chung v Ming Shiu Sum* (2006) 2 HKLRD 831）：(1) victim has a **special disadvantage**（seriously affecting ability to judge own best interests）; (2) 另一方 **unconscientiously took advantage**（knowledge 包括 constructive knowledge / wilful ignorance — *Kakavas v Crown Melbourne Ltd* (2013) HCA 25）。對方除非證明交易 fair, just and reasonable，否則 set aside。
- HK cases：*Evelyn Semana Bachicha v Poon Shiu Man Henry* (2000) 3 HKC 452（settlement HK$50,000 → HK$975 set aside）；*Lo Wo & Ors v Cheung Chan Ka Joseph & Anor* (2001) 3 HKC 70（老婦人三分之一市價賣樓 set aside）。
- 同 undue influence 嘅分別：unconscionability 睇 **強者嘅行為**（弱者意志仍可 independent）；undue influence 睇 **弱者意志被壓倒**（consent 唔 independent）。
- Statutory consumer protection: Unconscionable Contracts Ordinance (Cap. 458)（詳見 Ch.2）。

**6. Illegality**
- Sources: common law rule / statutory rule / public interest（*Pearce v Brooks* (1866) LR 1 Ex 213）。
- Illegal at formation → void ab initio（*Re Mahmoud and Ispahani* (1921) 2 KB 716 — 無 licence）；illegal mode of performance → 唔一定 unenforceable，視乎立法目的（*St John Shipping Corp v Joseph Rank Ltd* (1957) 1 QB 267 — 超載唔影響運費申索）；illegal purpose remote from contract → not tainted。
- General rule: money/benefits under illegal contract 唔可以 recover（*Holman v Johnson* (1775) 1 Cowp 341）。
- Gambling: Gambling Ordinance (Cap. 148) ss.3, 4（unauthorised gambling unlawful）; s.14（明知借錢俾人非法賭博 → offence；loan irrecoverable）。
- Restraint of trade: void unless reasonable（*Nordenfelt v Maxim Nordenfelt Guns & Ammunition Co Ltd* (1894) AC 535）— reasonable as between parties + public interest, protecting a legitimate interest, scope/area/time balanced。

#### B. Discharge of the Contract

**1. Performance** — must be **complete and precise**（*Arcos Ltd v EA Ronaasen & Son* (1933) AC 470 — 1/16 吋偏差都係 breach；*Re Moore & Co and Landauer & Co* (1921) 2 KB 519 — 24 罐裝 vs 30 罐裝，buyer 可以 reject 全部）；de minimis rule 只係 possible exception。
- **Entire obligations**：incomplete performance → 一分錢都收唔到（*Cutter v Powell* (1795) 6 Term Rep 320 — 船員中途死亡，widow 收唔到人工）。**分開 entire contracts**：substantial performance doctrine 適用於 entire contracts 但**唔適用於 entire obligations**（*Hoenig v Isaacs* (1952) 2 All ER 176）。
- SOGO 語境：sale of goods 數量屬 entire obligation（*Oxendale v Wetherell* (1829) 9 B & C 386），qualifications：相反約定 / trivial discrepancies / instalments（s.33(2) SOGO）；接受咗錯誤數量 → pro rata 付款（s.32(1) SOGO）。
- Severable obligations：分階段付款（月薪、進度款）。
- Unjust enrichment：自願接受 partial performance → 要畀合理價錢；wrongful prevention of performance → damages 或 **quantum meruit**（*Planché v Colburn* (1831) 8 Bing 14）。

**2. Agreement** — 需要 consideration：雙方都有未完成義務 → mutual release 即 consideration；一方已全做晒 → 對方要畀新 consideration。例外：deed / force majeure clause / termination clause。

**3. Breach**
- Actual breach（fail to perform / defective performance；liability 多屬 **strict**，如 s.16 SOGO merchantable quality；但 s.5 SSITO (Cap. 457) 只係 duty of reasonable care and skill — qualified obligation）vs **anticipatory breach**（refuses / makes known unable to perform — 非真正 breach 但 law 容許 treat as breach）。
- Types of promissory terms（後果關鍵）：
  - **Condition**：goes to the root → repudiatory → terminate + damages。Express label 唔係 conclusive；custom/precedent/statute 自動屬 condition（e.g. 'time is of the essence' — *Union Eagle Ltd v Golden Achievement Ltd* (1997) UKPC 5，遲 10 分鐘都可以 terminate；s.16(2) SOGO）。
  - **Warranty**：lesser term → damages only，**唔可以 terminate**。
  - **Innominate term**：睇 breach 後果嚴重性 — deprive substantially the whole benefit → 當 condition 處理（*Hong Kong Fir Shipping Co Ltd v Kawasaki Kisen Kaisha Ltd* (1962) 2 QB 26 — seaworthiness clause 唔係 condition，charterers wrongful repudiation 反而自己 breach）。
- Terminate or affirm：affirm after anticipatory breach → 要等到 performance due date 先有 damages；affirm 後若自己 breach 或 contract frustrated，可能乜都冇（*Avery v Bowden* (1855) E & B 714）。

**4. Frustration** — supervening event（outside parties' control）令 performance (1) impossible / (2) illegal / (3) radically different → **automatic discharge**，唔理雙方意願。
- Grounds: destruction of subject matter（*Taylor v Caldwell* (1863) 3 B & S 826 — music hall 燒毀）；death in personal service contract；method stipulated 變成 impossible；supervening illegality（*Fibrosa Spolka Akcynja v Fairbairn Lawson Combe Barbour Ltd* (1943) AC 32 — trading with enemy）；**frustration of purpose**（*Krell v Henry* (1903) 2 KB 740 — 租樓睇加冕巡遊，巡遊取消 → frustrated；但 *Herne Bay Steam Boat Co v Hutton* (1903) 2 KB 683 — 海軍檢閱取消但仲可以環島遊 → **not** frustrated；common foundation 係關鍵）。
- Limitations：(1) express provisions（**force majeure clause** 三大特徵：trigger events / reporting obligations / consequences — 可 suspend/vary/extend，闊過 frustration）; (2) foreseen or foreseeable event（*Davis Contractors Ltd v Fareham UDC* (1956) AC 696；*Walton Harvey Ltd v Walker & Homfrays Ltd* (1931) 1 Ch 274 — 明知有 compulsory purchase 風險 → 唔係 frustration，要賠）; (3) **self-induced**（*J Lauritzen AS v Wijsmuller BV (The Super Servant Two)* (1990) 1 Lloyd's Rep 1 — 自己分配 SS1 去其他合約 → 唔算 frustration；*Maritime National Fish Ltd v Ocean Trawlers Ltd* (1935) AC 524）。
- Consequences — LARCO (Cap. 23)：
  - **s.16(2)**：frustration 前已付嘅錢 prima facie recoverable（突破 common law 嘅 total failure of consideration 要求 — *Fibrosa*）；payee 可 retain/recover **just sum**，上限 = min（advance paid/payable, actual expenses incurred）。
  - **s.16(3)**：一方 performance 令對方獲得 **valuable benefit** → 可追 just sum（唔超過 benefit 價值）；two-stage：先 identify + value the benefit（end product），再 assess just sum（*BP Exploration Co (Libya) Ltd v Hunt (No. 2)* (1979) 1 WLR 783）。

#### C. Remedies

**1. Damages** — primary remedy, available as of right；compensate 唔係 punish（**no punitive damages** — *Addis v Gramophone Co Ltd* (1909) AC 488）。
- Types of loss: expectation loss / reliance loss / restitution（total failure of consideration）/ incidental loss；可以組合但 **no double recovery**（*Millar's Machinery Co Ltd v David Way & Son* (1935) 40 Com Cas 204）。
- Date of assessment：一般 as at date of breach（*Johnson v Agnew* (1980) AC 367）；SOGO s.53(3) non-delivery → contract price vs market price at delivery time；非絕對 — *Golden Straight Corp v Nippon Yusen Kubishika Kaisha (The Golden Victory)* (2007) 2 WLR 691（計及 breach 後已知事件）。
- Limitations：
  - **Causation**（*C&P Haulage Co Ltd v Middleton* (1983) EWCA Civ 5）。
  - **Remoteness** — *Hadley v Baxendale* (1854) 9 Ex 341 two-limb test：(1) normal loss（usual course of things）; (2) special loss（breaching party 訂約時 actually knew of special circumstances — *Victoria Laundry (Windsor) Ltd v Newman Industries Ltd* (1949) 2 KB 528 — 一般 laundry profit 賠得，特別政府合約唔賠）。Foreseeability = 'not unlikely'（*Koufos v C Czarnikow Ltd (The Heron II)* (1969) 1 AC 350）；知識評估時點 = **time of contracting**（*Jackson v Royal Bank of Scotland* (2005) 1 WLR 377）。**Assumption of responsibility**：即使 foreseeable，若雙方無意令違約方承擔該類 loss → 唔賠（*Transfield Shipping Inc v Mercator Shipping Inc (The Achilleas)* (2008) 2 Lloyd's Rep 275）。
  - **Mitigation**：無法律義務但可避免嘅 loss 唔賠；只需 reasonable steps；burden on breaching party；reasonable 嘅 mitigation 措施造成嘅進一步 loss 都可以追（*Banco de Portugal v Waterlow* (1932) AC 452）；mitigation 成功晒 → 只得 nominal damages。Anticipatory breach：accept breach 後即要 mitigate；affirm 的話到 actual breach 先要 mitigate。
  - **Non-money losses**：一般唔賠 distress/disappointment；exceptions：(1) loss of amenity（*Ruxley Electronics and Construction Ltd v Forsyth* (1996) 1 AC 344 — 泳池深度，cost of cure 唔成比例 → 判 loss of amenity）; (2) physical inconvenience causing distress（*Farley v Skinner (No. 2)* (2002) 2 AC 732）; (3) contract purpose 係 pleasure/relaxation（*Jarvis v Swan Tours Ltd* (1973) QB 233 — holiday）。
- **Liquidated damages**：genuine pre-estimate of loss → enforceable；disproportionately high / unconscionable → **penalty**, unenforceable（*Dunlop Pneumatic Tyre Ltd v New Garage and Motor Co Ltd* (1915) AC 79；*Philips Hong Kong Ltd v A-G of Hong Kong* (1993) 61 BLR 41；*Cavendish Square Holding BV v Talal El Makdessi; ParkingEye Ltd v Beavis* (2015) UKSC 67）。
- **Equitable damages**：s.17 High Court Ordinance (Cap. 4) — in lieu of / in addition to specific performance or injunction；可涵蓋 anticipated cause of action。

**2. Action for an Agreed Sum (the Price)** — claim in **debt**（唔係 damages）：唔使 quantify loss；remoteness/mitigation 唔適用；可 summary judgment（Order 14, Rules of the High Court (Cap. 4A) / Rules of the District Court (Cap. 336H)）。前提：plaintiff 已 fully perform。Anticipatory breach 後 affirm + 繼續履行 → 可追 price（*White & Carter (Councils) Ltd v McGregor* (1962) AC 413），但若 plaintiff **no legitimate interest** in continuing → 唔得（*Ocean Marine Navigation Ltd v Koch Carbon Inc (The Dynamic)* (2003) 2 Lloyd's Rep 693）。

**3. Deposits and Part Payments**（*Polyset Ltd v Panhandat Ltd* (2002) 5 HKCFAR 234 — CFA）
- 定性取決於 parties' intention（*Mayson v Clouet* (1924) AC 980）。
- **Part payment**：impliedly conditional on completion → payor breach 都可收回，但 innocent party 有 right of set-off for damages（*Dies v British and International Mining and Finance Corp Ltd* (1939) 1 KB 724）；若 intended unconditional（e.g. shipbuilding instalments）→ 唔可以收返（*Hyundai Heavy Industries Co Ltd v Papadopoulos* (1980) 1 WLR 1129）。
- **Deposit**：an **earnest** / guarantee of performance → buyer default → **forfeited regardless of whether seller suffered any loss**（*Howe v Smith* (1884) 27 Ch D 89）；seller 仲可以 sue for damages（超過 deposit 嘅部分，credit for deposit — *Shuttleworth v Clews* (1910) 1 Ch 176）。**注意**：deposit forfeiture 唔受 penalty rule 規管。

**4. Specific Performance** — equitable, **discretionary**，唔係 as of right；damages inadequate 先考慮（unique goods / land）。
- Limitations：claimant 要 ready, willing and able to perform（*Lau Suk Ching Peggy v Ma Hing Lam* (2010) 3 HKLRD 247）；damages adequate → 唔批（貨物有市場替代品）；equitable maxims — **clean hands**（*Quadrant Visual Communications Ltd v Hutchison Telephone (UK) Ltd* (1993) 1 BCLC 442）+ **delay defeats equity / laches**（Limitation Ordinance (Cap. 347) s.4 六年時效唔適用於 SP，但要 act promptly）；hardship to defendant（*Patel v Ali* (1984) Ch 283）；lack of mutuality；contract too vague；需要 mutual co-operation。
- 永唔批：contracts for **personal service**；contracts requiring **constant supervision**（*Co-operative Insurance Society Ltd v Argyll Stores (Holdings) Ltd* (1998) AC 1 — 唔會強迫超市繼續營業）。正面例子：*Beswick v Beswick* (1968) AC 58（批咗）。

**5. Injunctions** — equitable, discretionary；final/perpetual vs interim/interlocutory。
- **Prohibitory injunction**：enforce negative promise；可能構成 **indirect specific performance** — 若 SP 會被拒，同效果嘅 injunction 亦應被拒；但 *Lumley v Wagner* (1852) 21 LJ Ch 898 — 可以禁佢去對手戲院唱歌（唔等於逼佢唱）；*Warner Bros Pictures Inc v Nelson* (1937) 1 KB 209；HK: *Beacon College Ltd v Yiu Man Hau Alfred & Ors* (2001) 4 HKC 433 — 有 realistic alternative employment 就可以批（補習老師可轉去官立學校）；越長期越難批（*Warren v Mendy* (1989) 1 WLR 853）。
- **Mandatory injunction**：更難攞；triviality / disproportion / public interest 可拒（*Wrotham Park Estate Co Ltd v Parkside Homes Ltd* (1974) 1 WLR 798 — 拒拆樓，改判 damages in lieu = 5% anticipated profit）。

#### D. Consumer Law Legislation

**1. Sale of Goods Ordinance (Cap. 26)（SOGO）**
- Contract of sale of goods：seller transfers/agrees to transfer **property in goods** to buyer **for a money consideration (the price)**（s.3(1)）；無特定形式（s.5）；goods = personal chattels（s.2(1)），唔包 land / fixtures / money；existing vs future goods（s.7(1)）；無定價 → reasonable price（ss.10(1), (2)）；retention of title / **Romalpa clause**（*Aluminium Industrie Vaassen BV v Romalpa Aluminium Ltd* (1976) 1 WLR 676）。
- Implied terms：
  - s.12(1)：time of payment **not** of the essence（除非另有意思）。
  - s.13(1)–(3)：buyer 可 waive condition 或當 warranty 處理；accept 咗貨之後，breach of condition 只當 warranty。
  - **s.14(1)**：implied **condition** seller has right to sell（title）；implied **warranty** free from encumbrance + quiet possession。CECO s.11(1)：**任何情況下都唔可以豁免**。
  - **s.15(1)**：sale by description → goods must correspond with description（condition）。
  - **s.16(2)**：in the course of a business → **merchantable (satisfactory) quality**（condition）；exceptions：defects drawn to attention / buyer examined / sale by sample defects apparent。s.2(5) 定義 merchantable quality（fit for common purposes; appearance and finish; free from minor defects — 以 reasonableness 為準）。
  - **s.16(3)**：buyer made known **particular purpose** → reasonably fit for that purpose（condition），除非無合理 reliance on seller's skill/judgement。
  - **s.17(2)**：sale by sample — bulk corresponds with sample 等（condition）。
  - CECO (Cap. 71)：buyer 係 **consumer** → s.15/16/17 責任**完全唔可以豁免**（s.11(2)）；non-consumer → 受 **reasonableness test** 規管（CECO s.3 + s.11(3)）；CECO s.5(1)(a) 防止用 onerous conditions 變相豁免。
- **Dealing as a consumer**（s.2A(1)）：(1) 唔係以 business capacity 訂約; (2) 對方係 business; (3) 貨品屬 ordinarily supplied for personal use or consumption 嘅類型。拍賣/competitive tender 買家永遠唔算 consumer（s.2A(2)）；主張對方**唔係** consumer 嘅一方負舉證責任（s.2A(3)）。（*Artech Printing Ltd v Yee Fat Printing Equipment Ltd* CACV92/2000 — printing press 唔係 consumer goods。）
- **Passing of property**：unascertained goods 未 ascertain 前唔會過戶（s.18）；specific goods → 按 parties' intention（s.19(1), (2)）；無意圖 → s.20 五條 default Rules（Rule 1: specific goods deliverable state → 訂約時過戶，唔理付款/交貨時間；Rule 4: sale-or-return；Rule 5: unascertained goods by description — unconditional appropriation / delivery without reserving right of disposal, s.21）。
- **Passing of risk**（s.22）：risk follows property（除非另有協議），**唔理交付咗未**；一方 fault 延誤交付 → 該方承擔風險。
- **Nemo dat quod non habet**（s.23(1)）：非物主出售 → buyer 拎唔到 title（即使 good faith）。**Statutory exceptions**：s.23(1) estoppel；s.23(2)(a) mercantile agent；s.23(2)(b) power of sale；**s.24(1) market overt**（公開商店/市場正常營業中出售，buyer good faith + without notice → 攞到好 title；*R v Tai Shing Jewellery Co* (1983) 2 HKC 441 — 只保護向商店買嘢嘅公眾，唔保護商店收購）；**s.25 sale under voidable title**；s.27(1) seller in possession after sale；s.27(2) buyer in possession after sale。
- Performance：seller 交貨 + buyer 收貨付款（s.29）；交付與付款 concurrent（s.30）；交貨地點/時間（s.31(1), (2)）；wrong quantity（s.32(1)–(3)：少咗可全拒；多咗可收啱嗰部分或全拒；接受咗就 contract rate 找數）；instalment delivery 可拒（s.33(1)）；交 carrier 當交付（s.34(1)）。
- **Acceptance**（s.37）：intimates acceptance / acts inconsistent with seller's ownership / reasonable time 後 retain 唔 reject；未檢查過嘅貨 → 有 reasonable opportunity to examine 先當 accept（s.37(2)）；consumer buyer — 絕對（s.37(3)）。（*Master Weaving Ltd v Hanbo Enterprises Ltd* HCA 676/2012 — 出 cargo receipt + 繼續用貨 = accepted。）Buyer 合法拒收後無義務退貨，seller 自己攞返（s.38）。
- **Unpaid seller's remedies against goods**：withhold delivery（s.42，title 未過戶）；**lien**（s.43(1)，即使 title 過咗 — 但 s.45(1) 交付 carrier 等情況會失去）；**stoppage in transit**（s.46，buyer insolvent）；**re-sale**（s.50(2) 新買家攞好 title；s.50(3) perishable 或通知後 buyer 唔找數可轉售 + 追 damages；s.50(4) 保留轉售權）。
- Actions for breach：seller — action for the price（s.51）/ damages for non-acceptance（s.52）；buyer — damages / specific performance for non-delivery（ss.53, 54）/ damages for breach of warranty（s.55）。

**2. Supply of Services (Implied Terms) Ordinance (Cap. 457)（SSITO）**
- 適用於 contract **for** the supply of a service（one-off work），唔包 contract **of** service（continuous, e.g. employment）（ss.3(1), (2)）。
- Implied terms：**s.5** reasonable care and skill；**s.6(1)** reasonable time（無指定時）；**s.7(1)** reasonable price（無指定時）。
- Exemption：consumer → **s.8(1) 完全唔可以豁免**；non-consumer → s.8(2) 可以，但要過 CECO reasonableness test。
- *Thomsen & Anor v Johnson Burglar Alarms Co Ltd* (2001) 3 HKLRD 571 — common law 同 s.5 SSITO 嘅 duty 相同。

**3. Trade Descriptions Ordinance (Cap. 362)（TDO）** — 執法：Customs and Excise Department
- 六大 offences：
  - **s.7(1)** false trade description of goods（apply / supply / possess for sale；s.7(3) 管有製造工具）（*KCS14225/2017* — 冬蟲夏草冇蟲草成分，罪成）。
  - **s.7A(1)** false trade description of **services** to consumer（*KTCC717/2017* — 假稱港隊羽毛球代表）；s.7A(2) + Sch.4 — 受其他條例規管嘅服務（Insurance Ordinance (Cap. 41)、Banking Ordinance (Cap. 155)、MPFSO (Cap. 485)、SFO (Cap. 571)）除外。
  - **s.13E** misleading omissions — omit/hide **material information** / unclear, unintelligible, ambiguous or untimely / fail to identify commercial intent → 導致 **average consumer**（reasonably well informed, observant and circumspect — s.13D）作出本唔會作嘅 transactional decision。（*ESCC3747/2014* — 「580」大字、per tael 細字；*KCS22095/2017* — 10% 服務費無事先告知。）
  - **s.13F** aggressive commercial practices — harassment, coercion or undue influence（coercion 包括 physical force — s.13F(4)）significantly impair average consumer's freedom of choice → 改變 transactional decision；考慮 timing/location/persistence/threatening language/exploiting misfortune（s.13F(3)）。（*KCCC3903/2014* — 美容院嚇客患癌迫買 $140,000 療程。）
  - **s.13G** bait advertising — 廣告價無 reasonable grounds 相信可供應（合理期間+數量）；若廣告清楚註明供應期/數量並照做 → 唔構成（s.13G(3)）。
  - **s.13H** bait and switch — invitation to purchase 後，意圖推銷另一產品而：拒絕展示/示範、拒絕接單或合理時間內交貨、展示有缺陷樣本。
  - **s.13I** wrongly accepting payment — 收款時意圖唔供應 / 意圖供應物料上不同嘅產品 / 無 reasonable grounds 相信能於指定或合理時間內供應。（*KTCC5094/2016* — 公司已清盤仲繼續收客戶錢。）
- Penalty：最高 **HK$500,000 罰款 + 5 年監禁**（s.18(1)）；法庭可下令向受害 consumer 賠償（s.18A(1)）。
- 範圍：**exempt persons**（Sch.3 — CPA、醫生、律師、地產代理等受專業團體規管者，唔算 trader）；**excluded products**（Sch.4 — 受其他條例規管嘅貨品/服務）。

### 易混淆概念對比

**Table 1: Condition / Warranty / Innominate Term**

| | Condition | Warranty | Innominate term |
|---|---|---|---|
| Definition | Term going to the **root** of the contract | Term of **lesser** importance | **No pre-breach classification** |
| Consequence of breach | Repudiatory → terminate **and/or** damages | Non-repudiatory → **damages only** | 睇 seriousness of effects：deprived of substantially the whole benefit → 當 condition；否則當 warranty |
| Key authority | *Union Eagle Ltd v Golden Achievement Ltd* (1997) UKPC 5（time is of the essence）；s.16(2) SOGO（merchantable quality 自動係 condition） | e.g. 車身顏色（唔影響車嘅主要功能） | *Hong Kong Fir Shipping Co Ltd v Kawasaki Kisen Kaisha Ltd* (1962) 2 QB 26 |
| 點樣分辨 | Custom/precedent/statute 自動歸類；express label 唔係 conclusive | 違約只造成次要損失 | 問：breach 有冇剝奪 innocent party substantially the whole benefit？ |
| 考試伏位 | 條款寫明 "condition" 都未必係 condition，court 睇實質 | 考生常錯誤以為任何 breach 都可以 terminate — warranty 永遠唔得 | 要分析 breach 嘅 **consequences**，唔係睇條款本身重唔重要 |

**Table 2: Deposit / Part Payment / Liquidated Damages（違約後預付款項嘅命運）**

| | Deposit | Part payment | Liquidated damages clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nature | An **earnest** — guarantee that contract will be performed | Advance payment **on account** of the price | Pre-agreed sum payable on breach |

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** What are the four elements of an actionable misrepresentation, and what are the five exceptions to the rule that silence is not a misrepresentation?

**Back:**
- A **false statement of fact** which **induces** the representee to enter into the contract.
- Inducement requires: material / known to the representee / intended to be acted upon / actually acted upon.
- Silence is not a misrepresentation (caveat emptor), EXCEPT: (1) active steps to conceal; (2) half-truths (*Dimmock v Hallett*); (3) change of circumstances (*With v O'Flanagan*); (4) fiduciary relationships; (5) contracts uberrimae fidei.

💬 記住口訣：「假嘅事實陳述 + 誘導訂約」。純粹沉默唔算（buyer beware），但五個例外情況要背熟——尤其 half-truth（講一半呃人）同 change of circumstances（之前講嘅嘢變咗唔啱要更正），呢兩個最常用嚟出 scenario 題。

### Card 2
**Front:** Why is it usually better for a representee to claim damages for negligent misrepresentation under s.3(1) of the Misrepresentation Ordinance (Cap. 284) than at common law?

**Back:**
- (1) No need to establish a **special relationship** / duty of care (unlike *Hedley Byrne* at common law).
- (2) **Burden of proof reversed**: representor must prove he had reasonable grounds to believe, and did believe, the statement was true.
- (3) **Fiction of fraud**: remoteness test = same as the tort of deceit — all losses directly flowing from the transaction, regardless of foreseeability (*Royscot Trust Ltd v Rogerson* (1991) 2 QB 297).
- BUT: only available where the representee was induced to contract **with the representor himself** (not a third party).

💬 s.3(1) MO 三大優勢：唔使證 special relationship、舉證責任掉轉、賠償範圍當佢欺诈咁計（fiction of fraud）。伏位：如果 misrep 誘導你同**第三者**訂約（如 Hedley Byrne 嘅 facts），s.3(1) 就用唔到，只可以行 common law。

### Card 3
**Front:** List the four bars to rescission for misrepresentation and state when time begins to run for lapse of time.

**Back:**
- (1) **Affirmation** of the contract (with knowledge of the misrep and right to rescind).
- (2) **Lapse of time** — fraud: time runs from discovery (or when reasonably discoverable); negligent/innocent: from **contract formation** (*Leaf v International Galleries* (1950) 2 KB 86 — 5 年後先發現，barred).
- (3) **Restitution impossible** (e.g. goods consumed/irreversibly changed — *Clarke v Dickson*).
- (4) **Third party rights** (bona fide purchaser without notice — *Car and Universal Finance Co Ltd v Caldwell* (1961) 1 QB 525; s.25 SOGO).
- Rescission is **all or nothing** — no partial rescission.

💬 四個 bars：affirm、拖得太耐、還唔返原狀、第三者權利。時間起算係大伏位：**fraud 由發現日起計**，negligent/innocent 由**訂約日**起計 — 所以 fraud 受害人隔好多年都可能仲 rescind 得，innocent misrep 就很快就 bar 咗。

### Card 4
**Front:** What must a party show to establish economic duress, and what is the remedy?

**Back:**
- (1) **Illegitimate pressure** (usually a threat to breach the contract; distinguish from normal commercial bargaining — *Pao On v Lau Yiu Long* (1980) AC 614); relevant factors: good/bad faith, protest at the time, subsequent affirmation.
- (2) **Causal link**: would not have agreed but for the pressure / pressure was a significant cause.
- (3) **No practicable alternative** but to submit (*Estinah v Golden Hand Indonesian Employment Agency* (2001) 4 HKC 607).
- Remedy: contract/variation is **voidable** → rescission (variation only rescinded as to the varied part); damages if pressure amounts to the tort of intimidation.
- Right to rescind can be lost by **affirmation/delay** (*The Atlantic Baron* (1979) QB 705).

💬 三件套：illegitimate pressure → caused the agreement → 無實際選擇。考試最常見係威脅 breach 去逼對方同意加價/改條款。記住 Atlantic Baron 嘅伏位：就算真係有 duress，拖十幾個月先出聲 = affirmed， rescind 唔到。

### Card 5
**Front:** When is a contract discharged by frustration, and what are the three limitations on the doctrine?

**Back:**
- A supervening event (outside the parties' control, no fault) makes performance (1) **impossible** (*Taylor v Caldwell*), (2) **illegal** (*Fibrosa*), or (3) **radically different** / common purpose destroyed (*Krell v Henry* (1903) 2 KB 740; contrast *Herne Bay Steam Boat Co v Hutton*).
- Effect: **automatic discharge**, regardless of the parties' intentions.
- Limitations: (1) **express provision** (e.g. force majeure clause — trigger events / reporting / consequences); (2) event **foreseen or foreseeable** (*Davis Contractors v Fareham UDC*); (3) **self-induced** frustration is not frustration (*The Super Servant Two* (1990) 1 Lloyd's Rep 1).
- Loss apportionment: LARCO (Cap. 23) s.16(2) (money paid/payable) and s.16(3) (valuable benefit → just sum).

💬 Frustration = 訂約後發生嘅意外令合約做唔落/犯法/完全變質，**自動**終止。三大限制：合約自己有寫（force majeure）、預見到、自己造成。同 common mistake 分清楚：**訂約前已存在**嘅事實錯誤 → mistake；**訂約後先發生**嘅事件 → frustration（睇 Griffith v Brymer vs Krell v Henry 嘅加冕巡遊對比）。

### Card 6
**Front:** State the two-limb test for remoteness of damage in *Hadley v Baxendale* and the effect of the 'assumption of responsibility' analysis.

**Back:**
- Limb 1 (normal loss): loss arising **naturally**, i.e. according to the usual course of things — recoverable if within the breaching party's reasonable contemplation as a likely result of breach.
- Limb 2 (special loss): loss from **special circumstances** — recoverable only if the breaching party **actually knew** of those circumstances **at the time of contracting** (*Victoria Laundry* — general laundry profits recoverable; lucrative government contract not, because unknown).
- Foreseeability = loss 'not unlikely' to result (*The Heron II* (1969) 1 AC 350).
- Even if foreseeable, no liability if the parties did not (objectively) intend the breaching party to **assume responsibility** for that type of loss (*The Achilleas* (2008) 2 Lloyd's Rep 275).

💬 兩肢測試：正常損失（自然發生 → 賠）vs 特殊損失（訂約時對方**實際知道**特殊情況先賠）。The Achilleas 加多一層：就算 foreseen 到，如果客觀上雙方無諗過違約方會孭呢類 loss，都唔使賠。時間點係 **訂約時**，唔係違約時！

### Card 7
**Front:** What are the implied conditions under ss.14–17 of the Sale of Goods Ordinance (Cap. 26), and to what extent can they be excluded under the Control of Exemption Clauses Ordinance (Cap. 71)?

**Back:**
- **s.14(1)**: seller has the **right to sell** (condition); goods free from undisclosed encumbrances + quiet possession (warranties).
- **s.15(1)**: sale by description — goods **correspond with description** (condition).
- **s.16(2)**: goods of **merchantable (satisfactory) quality** (condition); **s.16(3)**: reasonably **fit for a particular purpose** made known (condition).
- **s.17(2)**: sale by sample — bulk corresponds with sample, reasonable opportunity to compare, free from hidden unmerchantable defects (condition).
- CECO: s.14 liability — **never excludable** (s.11(1)); ss.15–17 — **not excludable against a consumer** (s.11(2)); against a non-consumer, excludable only if **reasonable** (s.11(3) + s.3).
- Dealing as a consumer: s.2A(1) SOGO; auction/tender buyers never consumers (s.2A(2)); burden on the party alleging non-consumer (s.2A(3)).

💬 一條龍記法：14 = 有權賣、15 = 貨對辦、16 = 品質好+啱用途、17 = 大貨同板。豁免規則：s.14 永遠唔豁免得；15–17 對 consumer 永遠唔豁免得，對 business 就要過 reasonableness test。邊個係 consumer 睇 s.2A(1) 三要件 — 貨品要係「通常私人使用」嘅類型（printing press 唔係 — Artech 案）。

### Card 8
**Front:** List the six offences against consumers under the Trade Descriptions Ordinance (Cap. 362), with section numbers, and state the maximum penalty.

**Back:**
- (1) **s.7(1)**: false trade description of goods; **s.7A(1)**: false trade description of services.
- (2) **s.13E**: misleading omissions.
- (3) **s.13F**: aggressive commercial practices (harassment / coercion / undue influence).
- (4) **s.13G**: bait advertising.
- (5) **s.13H**: bait and switch.
- (6) **s.13I**: wrongly accepting payment.
- Maximum penalty: **HK$500,000 fine + 5 years' imprisonment** (s.18(1)); court may order compensation to the consumer (s.18A(1)).
- Enforcement: Customs and Excise Department; exempt persons (Sch. 3) and excluded products (Sch. 4).

💬 六宗罪口訣：「假描述（貨7/服務7A）、漏料13E、硬銷13F、誘餌廣告13G、誘餌轉移13H、收錢唔出貨13I」。Bait advertising vs bait and switch 嘅分別：前者係**根本無合理理由相信有貨賣**；後者係**有 invitation 之後拒絕展示/接單/俾缺陷樣本**，意圖轉推另一產品。最高罰 50 萬 + 坐 5 年，呢個數字成日考。
