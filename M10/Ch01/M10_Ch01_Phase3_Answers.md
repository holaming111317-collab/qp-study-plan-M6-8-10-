# M10 Chapter 1: Legal System and Regulatory Bodies in Hong Kong — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: A
**Model Answer (EN):**
Mandy should commence her claim in the **Small Claims Tribunal**. The Small Claims Tribunal, set up under the Small Claims Tribunal Ordinance (Cap. 338), hears monetary claims involving amounts **not exceeding HK$75,000**, and its main types of claims include debts and service charges. Mandy's claim of HK$68,000 is within this limit. The hearing is informal and **no legal representation is allowed**, which matches Mandy's wish to avoid instructing a lawyer.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- **Issue**: HK$68,000 債務申索應去邊個 forum？
- **Rule + Application**: Small Claims Tribunal 上限係 HK$75,000，HK$68,000 喺範圍內，而且唔准律師代表，完全配合 Mandy 嘅需要。
- **Distractor 分析**：B (District Court) — 區院合約申索上限 HK$3 million，HK$68,000 技術上可以入稟，但題目明確講 Mandy 想平、快、唔請律師，所以 District Court 唔係 **best** answer（MCQ 揀最貼題嗰個）；C (CFI) — 處理超過 HK$3 million 嘅民事申索，完全唔啱；D (Labour Tribunal) — 只處理僱傭合約申索（工資、遣散費等），呢單係商業債務。
- **考試伏位**：唔好見到「contract/service agreement」就直覺揀 District Court — 題目畀咗金額同「唔想請律師」兩個線索，係指住 Small Claims Tribunal。

**Rule Check:** Small Claims Tribunal Ordinance (Cap. 338); LP §1.3.2.3

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 混淆各法院金額門檻：SCT ≤ HK$75,000；District Court 合約申索 ≤ HK$3 million（收地 ≤ HK$7 million）；CFI > HK$3 million 且無上限。另外記住：勝訴後被告唔找數，申索人仍要自行採取行動強制執行判決。

### Q2 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):**
The court has most likely applied the **golden rule**. Under the literal rule, Oscar was "in" the station and not "in the vicinity of" it, so no offence would be committed. However, the court applies the golden rule where the literal rule would produce an **absurd result** the legislature cannot have intended: it would be absurd if obstructing staff just outside a station were an offence but the same conduct inside the station itself were not. The court therefore reads "in the vicinity of" as "in or in the vicinity of" and adopts the interpretation producing the least absurd result. This mirrors **Adler v George (1964) 2 QB 7**, where "in the vicinity of" a prohibited place was read to include "in" the prohibited place.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- **Issue**: 被告喺車站「入面」犯事，條文寫 "in the vicinity of"，點樣解釋？
- **Rule + Application**: 字面解釋會令「站外阻礙有罪、站內阻礙無罪」，荒謬 → golden rule 修正字面意思。同 Adler v George 嘅邏輯一模一樣。
- **Distractor 分析**：A — literal rule 會導致 Oscar **無罪**（佢唔係 "in the vicinity"），同題目「法院定罪」矛盾；C — ejusdem generis 係處理「具體詞 + 概括詞」嘅同類限制，同 "in the vicinity" 嘅荒謬性無關；D — mischief rule 雖然都係探究立法意圖，但佢唔係「must always be applied before any other rule」，而且呢類「字面造成荒謬」嘅經典情況係 golden rule 嘅地盤。
- **考試伏位**：golden rule vs mischief rule 最易混：「字面意思荒謬」→ golden；「條文含糊/有漏洞，要問立法想杜絕咩 mischief」→ mischief（香港法院依 Cap. 1 s.19 常用 mischief rule，但本題 scenario 係典型 golden rule 事實）。

**Rule Check:** Adler v George (1964) 2 QB 7; cf. s.19, Interpretation and General Clauses Ordinance (Cap. 1)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 見到「legislative intent / purpose」就自動揀 mischief rule。本題嘅關鍵字係 **absurd result**（站內無罪、站外有罪），呢個係 golden rule 嘅 trigger。

### Q3 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):**
The Court of Appeal has **overruled** the principle in *Chan v Lee*. A precedent is overruled when a **higher court**, in a **different (later) case**, declares that the precedent established by a **lower court** in a previous case is no longer binding authority and puts forward a different rule. Here the Court of Appeal (higher court), in the appeal arising from *Wong v Cheung*, declared the District Court's principle in *Chan v Lee* (a previous, different case) wrong and no longer binding. This matches the LP's Illustrative Example: the CA/CFA has the power to overrule the decision of a lower court made in a different case.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- **Issue**: CA 喺另一單案嘅上訴中推翻 DC 舊先例嘅法律原則，術語係咩？
- **Rule + Application**: Overrule 三要素：(1) 上級法院 (CA vs DC)；(2) **另一案件**（唔係 Chan v Lee 本身嘅上訴）；(3) 宣佈舊原則不再是 binding authority。三項齊全。
- **Distractor 分析**：A (reversed) — reversing 係**同一案件**上訴改判下級判決；CA 推翻嘅係 *Chan v Lee* 嘅原則，而 *Chan v Lee* 本身無上訴，所以唔係 reverse（CA 可以 reverse *Wong v Cheung* 嘅判決，但題目問嘅係佢對 *Chan v Lee* 原則做咗咩）；B (distinguished) — distinguishing 係法官認為兩案 material facts 唔同而拒絕跟隨，呢度係 CA 直接宣佈舊原則錯，唔係話事實唔同；D (per incuriam) — 指法官漏考慮相關法例/案例而犯錯，係偏離先例嘅**理由**之一，但題目描述嘅**動作**係上級法院推翻下級先例，即 overrule。
- **考試伏位**：overrule vs reverse 係經典伏位 — 記住「overrule 係另一案、reverse 係同一案」。

**Rule Check:** LP §1.4.3 (grounds for departing from precedent); cf. Grant v Australian Knitting Mills Ltd (1936) AC 85 and Griffiths v Peter Conway (1939) 1 All ER 685 (distinguishing)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 見到「CA 話 DC 錯」就揀 reverse。問自己：CA 處理緊嗰單案（Wong v Cheung）同被推翻嘅先例（Chan v Lee）係咪同一單案？唔係 → overrule。

### Q4 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):**
The Regulation was subject to the **negative vetting procedure**. Where subsidiary legislation is intended to be subject to negative vetting, the enabling provision **does not need to provide for it expressly** — the absence of any approval requirement in the parent ordinance points to negative vetting. The Regulation is published in the Government Gazette and laid on the table at the next sitting of the Legislative Council. **Not later than 28 days** after that sitting, the Legislative Council may amend the subsidiary legislation by resolution at a Council meeting, or extend the scrutiny period. Unless another date is provided, the Regulation took effect on its gazettal date (1 June); the amendment on 20 June was within the 28-day window and was valid.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- **Issue**: Parent ordinance 無講要立法會批准，立法會 28 日內修訂附屬法例 — 屬邊種 vetting？有無效？
- **Rule + Application**: 無明文規定 = negative vetting（預設機制）；刊憲後下次會議席上提交，28 日內可以決議修訂。20 June 喺期限內，修訂有效。
- **Distractor 分析**：A — 錯，negative vetting **唔需要** enabling provision 明文規定；B — positive vetting 需要 parent ordinance **明文**規定須立法會批准，呢度無，所以唔係 positive vetting（而且 positive vetting 下立法會都可以 amend，所以「只能 approve or reject」本身都錯）；D — 附屬法例喺**刊憲日（1 June）已生效**，唔係等立法會決議先生效（除非另有訂明日期）。
- **考試伏位**：兩大混淆位：(1) 「無明文 = 無審議」係錯，無明文 = negative vetting；(2) 生效日期 = 刊憲日，唔係審議完結日。

**Rule Check:** LP §1.5.2.2 (negative and positive vetting procedures; 28-day scrutiny period)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 以為立法會修訂附屬法例一定要經 positive vetting。記住：negative vetting 下立法會都可以 amend（或延長審議期），分別在於 positive vetting 要主動批准（approve/reject/amend by motion），negative vetting 係「唔出聲就自動過關」。

### Q5 — Answer: Kelvin may seek an order of specific performance, but he is NOT entitled to it as of right; the remedy is discretionary and likely to be granted because the building is unique.

**Model Answer (EN):**
- **Issue**: Whether Kelvin is automatically entitled to a court order compelling Doris to complete the sale.
- **Rule**: At common law, the only remedy for breach of contract is **monetary damages**. Equity, developed to relieve the harshness and rigidity of the common law, provides remedies in the form of **decrees (court orders)**, such as **specific performance** — an order requiring a party to a contract to perform his/her part. Equitable remedies are **discretionary**: the court awards them where common law damages would be inadequate (e.g. where the subject matter is unique); if damages are sufficient, the remedy will not be granted. Equity recognises new rights and, where there is conflict between common law and equity, **equity prevails**. Rules of equity are a source of Hong Kong law under Articles 8 and 18 of the Basic Law.
- **Application**: The *tong lau* is one of only a handful left in Hong Kong with unique architectural features, so damages would not adequately compensate Kelvin — no equivalent substitute can be bought with money. This is precisely the situation (unique subject matter) where equity intervenes, as in the LP's example of a unique property.
- **Conclusion**: Kelvin is **not entitled to the remedy as of right**. He may apply for specific performance, and the court is likely to exercise its discretion in his favour because damages are inadequate; but the award remains at the court's discretion.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- 呢題考你兩層：(1) 認到 remedy 係 equity 嘅 specific performance（而唔係 common law damages）；(2) 識講「**not as of right，法院酌情**」— 呢個係 LP 明確強調嘅位，都係考生最常漏嘅位。
- IRAC 寫法：先點出 issue（可否自動獲得強制履行令）→ rule（common law 只有 damages；equity 有 specific performance，酌情授予，damages 不足先用）→ application（唐樓獨一無二，damages 唔夠補償）→ conclusion（可申請、好大機會批，但唔係自動有）。
- **Distractor 式錯誤答案**：答「Kelvin 一定贏，Doris 必須交樓」= 忽略 discretion；答「Kelvin 只能收 HK$5 million 差價」= 忽略咗 equity 喺 damages 不足時嘅角色；答「equity 一定跟 common law」= 調轉咗，衝突時 equity prevails。

**Rule Check:** LP §1.2.1.3–1.2.1.4 and Illustrative Example 1 (specific performance is discretionary; equity prevails over common law); Basic Law Arts 8 & 18 (rules of equity as a source of law). *(Note: the LP cites no specific case for specific performance in this chapter — no case citation is invented here.)*

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 最大陷阱係「unique property = 一定有 specific performance」。正確講法係「damages inadequate → 法院**酌情**授予；法院認為 damages 足夠就唔會批」。答題時一定要寫 "not as of right / at the discretion of the court"。
