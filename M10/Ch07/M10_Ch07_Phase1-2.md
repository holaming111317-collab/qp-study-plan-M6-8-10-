# M10 Chapter 7: Introduction to Employment Law in Hong Kong — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. Identify the sources of employment law in Hong Kong and determine the scope of application of the Employment Ordinance (Cap. 57).
2. Apply the control test, organisation test and multifactor test to distinguish an employee (contract of service) from an independent contractor (contract for service).
3. Analyse the express and implied terms of an employment contract, including the implied duties of employers and employees at common law.
4. Calculate and apply statutory entitlements on wages, MPF, rest days, holidays, leave, severance payment and long service payment under the Employment Ordinance (Cap. 57) and related legislation.
5. Apply the rules on termination (notice, summary dismissal, constructive dismissal, unlawful dismissal and remedies) and the four anti-discrimination ordinances to scenario facts using the IRAC approach.

### 必背考點

#### A. Relevant Ordinances（Cap. + section numbers，以 LP 所引為準）

**1. Sources of law & scope of the Employment Ordinance (Cap. 57) ('EO')**
- Three sources: (1) international law — ICCPR & ICESCR, implemented via **Articles 39 and 153 of the Basic Law**; (2) common law (formation, status, implied duties, vicarious liability); (3) domestic legislation.
- Key domestic statutes: **EO (Cap. 57)**; Occupational Safety and Health Ordinance (Cap. 509); Factories and Industrial Undertakings Ordinance (Cap. 59); Employees' Compensation Ordinance (Cap. 282); Minimum Wage Ordinance (Cap. 608) ('MWO'); Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes Ordinance (Cap. 485) ('MPFSO'); Labour Tribunal Ordinance (Cap. 25).
- **EO s.4(2)**: EO applies to ALL employees regardless of nature of work, EXCEPT: (i) a family member employed by and living in the same dwelling as the employer; (ii) an overseas contract worker; (iii) a person serving under a crew agreement within the Merchant Shipping (Seafarers) Ordinance (Cap. 478) or on a ship not registered in HK; (iv) an apprentice under the Apprenticeship Ordinance (Cap. 47) (per **EO s.2A**); (v) a civil servant.
- **EO s.70**: any express contractual clause that extinguishes or reduces EO rights/benefits/protection is **void**.

**2. Who is employable**
- Age of Majority (Related Provisions) Ordinance (Cap. 410) **s.2(1)**: age of majority = 18.
- Employment of Children Regulations (Cap. 57B) ('ECR') **s.4(1)**: employment of a child under 13 generally prohibited; **s.5**: child aged 13–15 may be employed only if completed Form III AND parent consents in writing.
- Employment of Young Persons (Industry) Regulation (Cap. 57C): governs employment of young persons aged 15–18.
- Immigration Ordinance (Cap. 115) **s.17I**: offence to employ anyone not lawfully employable (no valid HK Permanent Resident Identity Card or valid working visa).

**3. Contract of employment — key EO provisions**
- **EO s.5(1)**: absent express agreement, every continuous contract is deemed a contract for **one month renewable from month to month**; onus on employer to prove otherwise. A fixed-term contract ends automatically at term expiry without notice.
- **EO s.6(3)** (probation): within the **first month** of probation, either party may terminate **without notice or payment in lieu**; after the first month, notice of **not less than 7 days** (or as agreed).
- **EO s.6**: termination by notice or payment in lieu; if contract silent, **not less than one month's notice** or one month's wages in lieu. Parties may not contract out but may waive at the time.
- **EO s.17**: employee under continuous contract entitled to **not less than one rest day in every seven days**; substitute rest day within 30 days if worked in emergency; if rest day coincides with statutory holiday, an additional rest day must be given.

**4. Remuneration and benefits**
- Continuous employment ('**4–18 requirement**', First Schedule EO): at least **18 hours per week for 4 consecutive weeks**; **EO s.3(2)**: onus on the **employer** to disprove continuity.
- **EO s.2(1)**: 'wages' = all remuneration, earnings, allowances (incl. travelling/attendance), attendance bonus, commission, overtime pay, tips and service charges capable of being expressed in money — subject to 11 exclusions (e.g. value of accommodation/food/medical care; employer's retirement scheme contributions; gratuitous or purely discretionary commission/bonus; non-recurrent travelling allowance; end of year payment under Part IIA; gratuity on completion/termination; gratuitous annual bonus).
- **EO s.23**: wages due at expiry of wage period; payable as soon as practicable and **no later than 7 days** after the end of the wage period. **EO s.22**: wage period presumed to be one month. **EO s.25(2)**: on termination, all termination payments (wages up to termination, payment in lieu of notice, unused annual leave pay, severance/long service payment, etc.) due within 7 days.
- **EO s.32** lawful wage deductions (10 categories) — key caps: deduction for damage/loss directly attributable to employee's neglect or default capped at the **value of damage/loss or HK$300, whichever is less**, per case; total deductions in one wage period ≤ **one quarter of wages**. Absence deductions must be proportionate to time absent. **EO s.25(3)**: if employee quits without proper notice, employer may deduct wages in lieu of outstanding notice.
- **MWO (Cap. 608) s.8(2)**: minimum wage = total hours worked × minimum hourly rate; rate since 1 May 2019 = **HK$37.5/hour** (per LP).
- **EO s.43C**: principal contractor/superior subcontractor liable for subcontractor employees' unpaid wages (site-related work); employee must serve written notice within **60 days** after wages due; principal contractor pays within **30 days** of notice.
- **EO s.49A**: keep wage/employment records covering preceding **12 months**, retained **6 months** after employment ceases.
- **MPFSO (Cap. 485) s.7(1A)**: employer must ensure 'relevant employees' (aged **18–65**, continuous employment ≥ **60 days**) join a registered MPF scheme. Exempt: domestic employees; self-employed hawkers; persons under statutory pension schemes (e.g. civil servants, subsidised school teachers); overseas persons working <13 months or covered by overseas schemes; EU Office employees.
- MPF contributions: **5% employer + 5% employee** of relevant income; monthly income < HK$7,100 → employee pays nothing (employer still 5%); > HK$30,000 → capped at **HK$1,500 each**. 'Relevant income' (MPFSO s.2) excludes **severance payments and long service payments**.

**5. Rest days, holidays and leave**
- **EO s.39**: **12 statutory holidays** for every employee regardless of length of service; if required to work, a substituted holiday within **60 days** before/after; **payment in lieu of holiday is forbidden**. General Holidays Ordinance (Cap. 149): 17 general holidays (12 overlap); binds banks, educational establishments, public offices and government departments only.
- **EO s.41AA** paid annual leave (continuous employment ≥ 12 months): 7 days (years 1–2), 8 (yr 3), 9 (yr 4), 10 (yr 5), 11 (yr 6), 12 (yr 7), 13 (yr 8), 14 (yr 9+). Unbroken period unless employee consents: ≤10 days → up to 3 days separately; >10 days → at least 7 consecutive; payment in lieu allowed only for the part exceeding 10 days. Daily rate = average daily wages over preceding 12 months.
- Sick leave (sickness allowance): continuous contract ≥ 1 month; sick leave ≥ **4 consecutive days** (pregnancy check-up/post-confinement/miscarriage excepted); medical certificate; sufficient accumulated paid sick days. Rate = **four-fifths** of average daily wages (12-month average). Accrual: **2 days/month in first 12 months**, **4 days/month thereafter**, capped at **120 days**.
- Maternity leave: **14 weeks** (Employment (Amendment) Ordinance 2020, effective 11 Dec 2020); paid if continuous contract ≥ **40 weeks** before scheduled leave + notice + medical certificate; pay = four-fifths of average daily wages; additional 4-week pay capped at **HK$80,000** (government reimburses employers). <40 weeks → 14 weeks unpaid leave still available with notice + certificate. Paternity leave: **5 days** per confinement (notice ≥ 3 months before expected confinement, else ≥ 5 days before leave).

**6. Termination of employment**
- Valid reasons for dismissal (**EO s.32K**): (a) misconduct; (b) incapability or disqualification; (c) redundancy or genuine operational requirements; (d) continued employment would contravene the law; (e) any other sufficient cause. Employee with ≥ **24 months** continuous employment dismissed without valid reason → presumed dismissal to extinguish/reduce EO rights → **unreasonable dismissal** (consider all circumstances, **EO s.32L(2)**). Note: employer is NOT obliged to give a reason at the time of termination.
- Summary dismissal (**EO s.9(1)(a)**): very serious misconduct or failure to improve after repeated warnings — (1) wilful disobedience of a lawful and reasonable order; (2) misconduct inconsistent with due and faithful discharge of duties; (3) fraud or dishonesty; (4) habitual neglect of duties. **S.9(1)(b)**: any other common law ground justifying termination without notice. Summary dismissal = **last resort / drastic measure**. Dismissal for taking part in a strike is prohibited.
- Statutory restrictions: unlawful to dismiss (i) a pregnant employee; (ii) an employee on paid sick leave; (iii) an employee who is a witness in court against the employer; (iv) a trade union member actively seeking to improve working conditions; (v) an injured employee before compensation settlement — UNLESS summary dismissal criteria are independently satisfied.
- Remedies for unlawful/unreasonable dismissal (**EO ss.32N & 33M**): (1) **reinstatement** (same position, rights, seniority, pension — considered first); (2) **re-engagement** (comparable terms, by employer/successor/associated company); (3) **terminal payments** as just and appropriate (**EO s.32O**). Since the Employment (Amendment) (No. 2) Ordinance 2018, the Labour Tribunal may order reinstatement/re-engagement **without employer's consent**; non-compliance → additional award of **3 × average monthly salary, capped at HK$72,000**, and possible criminal liability.
- Constructive dismissal (**EO s.10**): employee may terminate without notice/payment if — (a) reasonable fear of physical danger by violence or disease not contemplated by the contract; (aa) ≥5 years' employment + certified permanently unfit for the particular type of work by a registered medical/Chinese medicine practitioner while engaged in that work; (b) ill-treatment by the employer; (c) any common law ground (employer's **repudiatory/fundamental breach**). Recognised grounds include: failure to provide sufficient work; failure to pay statutory remuneration/benefits; transfer without consent; failure to provide a safe and decent work environment; unlawful discriminatory conduct.
- Severance payment (**EO s.31B(1)**): continuous contract ≥ **24 months** + dismissed by reason of **redundancy** or **laid off**; voluntary resignation excluded. Redundancy (**s.31B(2)**): (1) employer closes/intends to close the business; (2) ceases business at the place where employee worked; (3) requirement for work of a particular kind ceases/diminishes. Lay-off: no work/no wages > half of normal working days in any 4 consecutive weeks, or > one-third in any 26 consecutive weeks.
- Long service payment: continuous contract ≥ **5 years**, dismissed (not summarily for serious misconduct, not for redundancy) and — (1) employee dies; (2) employee terminates due to illness; (3) employee retires at age 65 or above.
- Formula (both payments): monthly-paid → **2/3 × last month's wages × reckonable years of service**; daily/piece-rated → any 18 days' wages chosen out of last 30 normal working days × reckonable years. **Cap: HK$390,000**.
- Labour Tribunal (Labour Tribunal Ordinance, Cap. 25): **exclusive jurisdiction** over claims arising from non-compliance with the EO, MWO or Apprenticeship Ordinance (Cap. 47); NO jurisdiction over tort claims, bankruptcy/winding-up matters, or claims under the Minor Employment Claims Adjudication Board Ordinance (Cap. 453).

**7. Anti-discrimination ordinances**
- Four ordinances: **SDO (Cap. 480)**; **DDO (Cap. 487)**; **FSDO (Cap. 527)**; **RDO (Cap. 602)**. Equal Opportunities Commission (EOC) issues codes of practice.
- Direct discrimination: less favourable treatment **on the ground of** sex/marital status/pregnancy, disability, family status or race. Test: objective '**but for**' test — intention/motive irrelevant to liability (relevant only to remedies); claimant bears burden on balance of probabilities; where reason unclear, apply the **two-part test** (comparator question + causation question) per *M v Secretary for Justice*.
- Indirect discrimination: a seemingly neutral requirement/condition which a considerably smaller proportion of the protected group can comply with, causing detriment, and not justifiable. Group-based comparison (correct comparator pool: *University of Manchester v Jones*). Justification = 3-step test: (1) legitimate aim; (2) rational connection; (3) necessity (*EOC v Director of Education*).
- Harassment: unwelcome conduct a reasonable person would anticipate would offend, humiliate or intimidate. Sexual harassment: 'quid pro quo' + 'hostile environment' types; subjective unwelcomeness + objective reasonable-person assessment (*Chen v Tamara Rus*). Victimisation = a form of direct discrimination ('but for' test applies: *Chang Ying Kwan v Wyeth (HK) Ltd*).
- SDO (Cap. 480): sex, marital status, pregnancy; sexual harassment; victimisation. Religion exception: **s.22(1) SDO**.
- DDO (Cap. 487): covers disability of the person **or their associates**; harassment: **s.3(6) DDO**. Exception — **s.12(2) DDO**: not discrimination if the person, because of disability, cannot carry out the **inherent requirements** of the job, or would impose **unjustifiable hardship** (factors in **s.4 DDO**).
- FSDO (Cap. 527): 'family status' = responsibility for care of an **immediate family member** (blood, marriage, adoption, affinity). Direct discrimination: **s.5(a)**; indirect: **s.5(b)**; unlawful to dismiss by reason of family status: **s.8(2)(c)**.
- RDO (Cap. 602): race discrimination, harassment (**s.7(1)**, hostile environment **s.7(2)**), and **vilification** (public activity inciting hatred/serious contempt/severe ridicule). Religion exception: **s.23 RDO**.
- Genuine occupational qualification (GOQ) exception: SDO/DDO/RDO, narrow; onus on employer.
- Remedies (**s.76(3A) SDO; s.72(4) DDO; s.54(3) FSDO; s.70(4) RDO**): declaration; order to redress; employ/re-employ; promote; damages; punitive/exemplary damages; declare contract void. Court has wide discretion, global approach, but will **not force an apology** (*Ma Bik Yung v Ko Chuen*).
- Discrimination Legislation (Miscellaneous Amendments) Ordinance 2020: workplace-participant harassment (**s.23A SDO; s.22A DDO; s.24A RDO** — covers interns, volunteers, contract workers); RDO 'near relative' replaced by '**associate**' (**s.5 RDO**); damages available for **indirect** discrimination on sex/family status/race **without proof of intention**.
- Sex Discrimination (Amendment) Ordinance 2021 (effective 19 June 2021): **breastfeeding** protection — direct discrimination (**s.8A(1)(a) SDO**), indirect discrimination (**s.8A(1)(b)**), harassment (**s.2A(1)–(2)**), victimisation; child need not be biological; no time limit.

#### B. Leading Cases（full citation）
- **Ready Mixed Concrete (South East) Ltd v Minister of Pensions and National Insurance (1968) 2 QB 497** — Mackenna J's three conditions for a contract of service: (1) servant provides work/skill for remuneration; (2) sufficient degree of control; (3) other provisions consistent with a contract of service. Owner-drivers held to be independent contractors.
- **Stevenson, Jordan and Harrison Ltd v Macdonald and Evans (1952) 1 TLR 101** — Lord Denning: under a contract of service the work is an **integral part** of the business; under a contract for services it is only **accessory** to it (organisation/integration test).
- **Market Investigations Ltd v Minister of Social Security (1969) 2 QB 173** — Cooke J's flexible multifactor approach.
- **Lee Ting Sang v Chung Chi-Keung (1990) 1 HKLR 764 (PC)** — Privy Council endorsed the multifactor approach; control no longer sole determinant; no exhaustive list of factors.
- **Poon Chau Nam v Yim Siu Cheung (2007) 1 HKLRD 951 (CFA)** — modern approach: 'a nuanced and not a mechanical approach: painting a picture from the accumulation of detail'; factors include control, own equipment, own helpers, financial risk, investment/management responsibility, opportunity to profit. Casual workers: umbrella contract vs specific engagement contract.
- **Kao Lee & Yip v Koo Hoi Yan (2003) 3 HKLRD 296** — employee's fiduciary duty; merely informing a client of departure is not solicitation; going along with a client's proposal to move business without employer's consent is breach.
- **Kuoni Travel (China) Ltd v Kelly Frances Richards, HCA 1265/2006 (unreported)** — four factors for confidentiality: nature of employment; nature of information; whether employer impressed confidentiality; whether information easily isolated.
- **Deacons v White & Case Ltd Liability Partnership (2003) 3 HKLRD 670** — after termination, confidentiality protection is restricted to **trade secrets only**.
- **Thomas Vincent v South China Morning Post Publishers (2005) 4 HKLRD 258 (CFA)** — misconduct under s.32K EO must be factual, real and relevant; ordinarily a first offender should not be instantly dismissed without warning or a further chance; dismissal without any formal disciplinary process was unreasonable.
- **Ying Kee Safes and Furniture Limited v Wong Yam Tak, HCLA 105/1995 (unreported)** — lateness is unauthorised absence and misconduct; isolated lateness insufficient, but **persistent and repeated lateness after warnings** justifies summary dismissal.
- **Secretary for Justice v Chan Wah and Others (2000) 3 HKLRD 641 (CFA)** — adopted the objective '**but for**' test (from *R v Birmingham City Council, ex parte Equal Opportunities Commission (1989) 1 AC 1155*) for direct discrimination; motive irrelevant to liability.
- **M v Secretary for Justice (2009) 2 HKLRD 298 (CA)** — two-part test: (1) comparator question (same or not materially different circumstances); (2) causation question; employer's actual knowledge of disability irrelevant — knowledge of the **manifestation** of disability = knowledge of disability.
- **Shamoon v Chief Constable of Royal Ulster Constabulary (2003) ICR 337** — where comparator identification is problematic, focus on the factual enquiry of the **reason** for the treatment (applied in *Sit Ka Yin Priscilla v Equal Opportunities Commission (2010) HKEC 208*).
- **Equal Opportunities Commission v Director of Education (2001) 2 HKLRD 690** — three-step justification test for indirect discrimination: legitimate aim; rational connection; necessity.
- **University of Manchester v Jones (1993) ICR 474 (CA)** — correct comparator pool = all men and women with the necessary qualifications/experience, not a distorted subgroup.
- **Chang Ying Kwan v Wyeth (HK) Ltd (2001) 2 HKC 129** — pregnancy discrimination + victimisation after EOC complaint; 'but for' test applies to victimisation.
- **Chen v Tamara Rus (2001) 3 HKLRD 541 (CA)** — 'unwelcome' is subjective to the victim; then objective assessment of anticipated offence/humiliation/intimidation.
- **B v King of the King Group Limited, DCEO 9/2010** — employer vicariously liable for co-worker's sexual harassment for failing to take reasonable preventive steps; HK$80,000 damages for emotional distress.
- **Ma Bik Yung v Ko Chuen (2002) 2 HKLRD 1 (CFA)** — wide discretion on remedies, global approach; court will not force an unwilling respondent to apologise.

#### C. IRAC Application Points（常見 scenario 落點）
- **Issue**: status dispute → **Rule**: three tests (control → organisation → multifactor, *Poon Chau Nam*) → **Application**: run through control/ownership of tools/delegation/financial risk on facts; **label is not conclusive** → **Conclusion**: employee vs independent contractor; only employees get EO/ECO protection.
- **Issue**: dismissal without notice → distinguish **summary dismissal (s.9 EO — employer-initiated, last resort)** from **constructive dismissal (s.10 EO — employee-initiated, employer's repudiatory breach)**; check warnings given (*Thomas Vincent*; *Ying Kee*).
- **Issue**: dismissal reason → if ≥24 months continuous service, employer must justify under **s.32K EO**, else unreasonable dismissal (s.32L(2)); remedies ss.32N/33M/32O (reinstatement first).
- **Issue**: redundancy → s.31B(1)–(2) EO; watch for refusal of suitable alternative employment and whether requirement for the work truly 'ceased or diminished' (mere cost-saving regrade ≠ redundancy).
- **Issue**: discrimination → identify protected ground and ordinance; choose **direct ('but for')** vs **indirect (neutral condition + disparate impact + 3-step justification)**; check exceptions (GOQ; DDO s.12(2) inherent requirements); remedies under the relevant ordinance; victimisation if complaint-triggered retaliation.

### 易混淆概念對比

**Table 1 — Employee vs Independent Contractor**

| 比較點 | Employee (Contract of Service) | Independent Contractor (Contract for Service) |
|---|---|---|
| Tools / Equipment | Provided by the employer | Self-provided |
| Pay / Remuneration | Not affected by how work is done; bears no financial risk | Prospect of profit and bears financial risk |
| Mode / Method of Work | Determined by the employer | Self-determined |
| Subcontract work to others | No | Yes |
| Discipline by the company | Yes | No |
| Statutory protection | EO, ECO, MPF (as employee) etc. | Not under EO; only MPF as self-employed person |

點樣分辨：睇「實質關係」唔睇合約標籤——就算合約寫明 "self-employed"，法庭仍會用 multifactor test（*Poon Chau Nam*）從整體印象判斷。考試伏位：(1) 自備工具**唔係**決定性因素（高技術工人常客自備工具）；(2) 有權請幫手 ≠ 一定是僱主（supervisor/foreman 都可以請下屬）；(3) 答 status 題要逐個 factor 落 facts，最後俾 overall impression，唔好得個「control test」就收工。

**Table 2 — Severance Payment vs Long Service Payment**

| 比較點 | Severance Payment (EO s.31B) | Long Service Payment |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum continuous service | ≥ 24 months | ≥ 5 years |
| Trigger | Dismissal by reason of **redundancy** or **lay-off** | Dismissal (not summary dismissal for serious misconduct, not redundancy) + death / resignation due to illness / retirement at ≥ 65 |
| Voluntary resignation | Not entitled | Only if due to illness (or retirement ≥65) |
| Formula | 2/3 × last month's wages × reckonable years (monthly-paid) | Same formula |
| Cap | HK$390,000 | HK$390,000 |

點樣分辨：兩者**互斥**——同一次離職唔可以兩样都攞。考試伏位：(1) redundancy 定義係「職位需求消失/減少」，純粹慳錢降職降薪但工作量不減 ≠ redundancy（見 LP Flora 例），但可以構成 constructive dismissal（EO s.10(c) repudiatory breach）；(2) 自動辭職一般乜都冇，除非符合 illness/retirement 條件；(3) 記得檢查 24 months / 5 years 門檻先至計數。

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** What are the three sources of employment law in Hong Kong, and which Basic Law articles implement the international covenants?
**Back:**
- (1) International law — ICCPR and ICESCR
- (2) Common law — contract formation, employee status, implied duties, vicarious liability
- (3) Domestic legislation — EO (Cap. 57), ECO (Cap. 282), OSHO (Cap. 509), etc.
- Implemented via Articles 39 and 153 of the Basic Law

💬 白話解釋：記「國際、普通法、本地法例」三層。國際公約（ICCPR/ICESCR）靠 Basic Law 第 39 同 153 條落地香港。考試問 sources 就咁答三點已經攞分。

### Card 2
**Front:** Under s.4(2) of the Employment Ordinance (Cap. 57), which five categories of persons are excluded from the EO's application?
**Back:**
- (1) A family member employed by and living in the same dwelling as the employer
- (2) An overseas contract worker
- (3) A person serving under a crew agreement (Merchant Shipping (Seafarers) Ordinance, Cap. 478) or on a non-HK-registered ship
- (4) An apprentice under the Apprenticeship Ordinance (Cap. 47) (EO s.2A)
- (5) A civil servant
- Also note EO s.70: clauses reducing EO rights are void

💬 白話解釋：口訣「同住家人、海外合約工、船員、學徒、公務員」。留意學徒係經 s.2A 排除。合約條款想「contract out」EO 權益？s.70 話直接無效。

### Card 3
**Front:** State Mackenna J's three conditions for a contract of service in Ready Mixed Concrete (South East) Ltd v Minister of Pensions and National Insurance (1968) 2 QB 497.
**Back:**
- (1) The servant agrees to provide his own work and skill in consideration of a wage or other remuneration
- (2) He agrees, expressly or impliedly, to be subject to a sufficient degree of control by the other party (a 'master')
- (3) The other provisions of the contract are consistent with its being a contract of service
- Held on facts: owner-drivers were independent contractors

💬 白話解釋：三個條件記「俾工資、受控制、條款相符」。呢單案嘅司機要自己買車、可以請替工，所以係 independent contractor——即係話 control 重要但唔係全部。

### Card 4
**Front:** According to Poon Chau Nam v Yim Siu Cheung (2007) 1 HKLRD 951 (CFA), how does a court determine employment status under the multifactor test?
**Back:**
- 'A nuanced and not a mechanical approach: painting a picture from the accumulation of detail' — examine ALL features and form an overall impression
- Factors: degree of control; ownership of equipment; whether he hires his own helpers; degree of financial risk; responsibility for investment and management; opportunity to profit from sound management
- For casual workers: distinguish (1) umbrella/global contract (mutual obligation to offer and accept work relevant) from (2) contract for each specific engagement (mutual obligation irrelevant)
- Not a checklist exercise

💬 白話解釋：CFA 話唔好逐格剔 checklist，要「睇成幅畫」。散工都可以每次 engagement 都係僱員——mutual obligation 只係睇 umbrella contract 先關鍵。考試答 status 題必引此案。

### Card 5
**Front:** What are the six implied duties of an employee at common law?
**Back:**
- (1) To carry out lawful and reasonable orders of the employer
- (2) To perform work with reasonable care and skill
- (3) To serve the employer with fidelity and good faith (fiduciary duty — no conflict of interest, no solicitation of clients: Kao Lee & Yip v Koo Hoi Yan (2003) 3 HKLRD 296)
- (4) Not to disclose trade secrets or confidential information (post-termination: trade secrets only — Deacons v White & Case (2003) 3 HKLRD 670)
- (5) To account for property and secret profits obtained in the course of employment
- (6) To indemnify the employer for tortious liability caused by the employee's negligence

💬 白話解釋：記「聽命令、有手藝、忠誠、保密、交數、賠償」。伏位：話俾客知「我會離職」唔算 solicitation，但順住客人提議把生意帶走就係 breach——matter of degree。

### Card 6
**Front:** What are the MPF contribution rules under the MPFSO (Cap. 485) for employees?
**Back:**
- 'Relevant employee': aged 18–65, continuous employment ≥ 60 days (s.7(1A) MPFSO)
- Employer + employee each contribute 5% of relevant income
- Monthly relevant income < HK$7,100: employee contributes nil; employer still 5%
- HK$7,100–30,000: each pays 5%
- > HK$30,000: each pays HK$1,500 (capped)
- 'Relevant income' excludes severance payments and long service payments (s.2 MPFSO)
- Exempt: domestic employees, self-employed hawkers, statutory pension scheme members, short-term (<13 months) overseas workers, EU Office employees

💬 白話解釋：記兩條界線 7,100 同 30,000，同封頂 1,500。Domestic helper 係豁免 MPF 但**唔係**豁免 EO——呢個係經典伏位。

### Card 7
**Front:** When may an employer lawfully summarily dismiss an employee under s.9 of the EO, and what limits did the CFA set in Thomas Vincent v SCMPP (2005) 4 HKLRD 258?
**Back:**
- S.9(1)(a) EO grounds: (1) wilful disobedience of a lawful and reasonable order; (2) misconduct inconsistent with due and faithful discharge of duties; (3) fraud or dishonesty; (4) habitual neglect of duties — i.e. very serious misconduct or failure to improve after repeated warnings
- S.9(1)(b): any other common law ground justifying termination without notice
- Summary dismissal is a drastic measure / last resort
- Thomas Vincent: misconduct must be factual, real and relevant; a first offender should ordinarily not be instantly dismissed without warning or a further chance
- Dismissal for taking part in a strike is prohibited

💬 白話解釋：即炒係「最後手段」。單次 minor misconduct（例如第一次遲到）唔夠，要有 warning 同重複犯（Ying Kee 案：持續遲到 + 警告後再犯先算）。考試見「第一次犯錯即炒」通常係 unreasonable/wrongful。

### Card 8
**Front:** Distinguish direct discrimination from indirect discrimination, naming the key tests and authorities.
**Back:**
- Direct: less favourable treatment on a prohibited ground; objective 'but for' test (Secretary for Justice v Chan Wah (2000) 3 HKLRD 641, adopting R v Birmingham City Council, ex parte EOC (1989) 1 AC 1155); motive irrelevant to liability; two-part test (comparator + causation) per M v Secretary for Justice (2009) 2 HKLRD 298
- Indirect: facially neutral requirement/condition; considerably smaller proportion of the protected group can comply; detriment; not justifiable — 3-step justification: legitimate aim, rational connection, necessity (EOC v Director of Education (2001) 2 HKLRD 690); correct comparator pool (University of Manchester v Jones (1993) ICR 474)
- Victimisation is a form of direct discrimination (Chang Ying Kwan v Wyeth (HK) Ltd (2001) 2 HKC 129)

💬 白話解釋：Direct = 因為你個身份直接差別對待；indirect = 條件表面中性但實際排斥某群體（例如身高要求排斥女性）。答 indirect 一定要行埋三步 justification test，同埋講岩 comparator pool。
