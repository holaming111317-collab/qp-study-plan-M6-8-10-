# M10 Chapter 13: Receivership, Liquidation and Winding-up — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. Distinguish compulsory winding-up, members' voluntary winding-up and creditors' voluntary winding-up, and apply the correct procedure for each.
2. Analyse the appointment, powers, duties, liabilities and removal of liquidators and receivers.
3. Apply the avoidance powers of a liquidator: void dispositions (s.182), unfair preferences (ss.266/266A/266B), invalid floating charges (s.267), fraudulent trading (s.275) and misfeasance (s.276).
4. Determine the provability of debts and the statutory order of priority of payments in a winding-up.
5. Advise on receivership as a corporate rescue / debt enforcement device and the grounds for compulsory winding-up under s.177 CWUMPO.

> 除非另註明，本章 s.177 起的條文均屬 **Companies (Winding Up and Miscellaneous Provisions) Ordinance (Cap.32) ('CWUMPO')**；重組／收購條文（如 ss.670–721）屬 **Companies Ordinance (Cap.622) ('CO')**。

### 必背考點

#### A. Relevant Ordinances & Sections (以 LP 所引為準)

**Receivership (LP s.13.3)**
- **CWUMPO ss.297A & 168D(1)(c)**: receiver/manager 必須係 natural person；undischarged bankrupt 或受 disqualification order 者唔做得。
- **CWUMPO s.300A**: floating charge 下委任嘅 receiver 須 forthwith 通知公司；公司 14 日內交 statement of affairs；receiver 收咗後 2 個月內將副本送 Registrar、court、trustees/debenture holders。
- **CWUMPO s.298A(1)**: receiver 可向 court 尋求指示；**s.298A(2)**: receiver 對履行職能中訂立嘅合約負 personal liability，但有 statutory right of indemnity 從公司資產彌償。
- **Conveyancing and Property Ordinance (Cap.219), Fourth Schedule**: receiver 對 mortgaged land 嘅 implied powers（insure、possess、lease、sell 等 9 項）。
- **High Court Ordinance (Cap.4) s.21L** / **CWUMPO s.725** / inherent jurisdiction：CFI 委任 receiver 嘅三個來源。

**Compulsory winding-up (LP s.13.4.2)**
- **s.177**: 六個 grounds — (1) special resolution to wind up by court; (2) 無開業一年／停業一年; (3) no members; (4) unable to pay its debts; (5) articles 指定事件發生; (6) just and equitable。
- **s.178(1)**: deemed inability to pay debts — (a) statutory demand ≥ **HK$10,000** 未喺 **21 日**內清償（但 debt 有 genuine dispute on substantial grounds 可拒絕）; (b) execution unsatisfied; (c) court 信納無力償債（cash flow test + balance sheet test）。
- **s.179**: petition 呈請方式；standing 包括 company、creditors、contributory、trustee in bankruptcy、official receiver、Financial Secretary、Registrar、SFC (s.212 SFO Cap.571)。
- **s.184(2)**: winding-up deemed commenced at presentation of petition（relation-back）。
- **s.182**: commencement 後 disposition of property void，除非 court sanction → 公司通常要申請 **validating order**（如出糧、日常營運開支）。
- **s.186**: winding-up order 後未經 court leave 不得開始或繼續針對公司嘅訴訟。
- **s.190**: directors 交 statement of affairs 及接受 interview；**ss.221–222**: private / public examination。
- **s.197**: liquidator 接管全部資產；**s.199(1)/(2)(a)**: carry on business（beneficial winding-up）、出售資產；**s.268**: compromises + **disclaimer of onerous property**。
- **ss.266, 266A, 266B**: unfair preference — 破產前 **6 個月**內（associate = **2 年**）；要素：put creditor in better position + desire to prefer + insolvent at the time（借 Bankruptcy Ordinance (Cap.6) ss.50(3)/(4), 51(1)/(2)）。
- **s.267**: winding-up 前 **12 個月**內批出嘅某些 floating charges 可被推翻。
- **s.275(1)**: fraudulent trading — intent to defraud creditors / fraudulent purpose + knowingly a party；civil（個人負全部債務）+ criminal（最高 5 年監禁）。
- **s.264B**: extortionate credit transaction — winding-up order / special resolution 前 **3 年**內，grossly exorbitant payments 或 grossly contravenes fair dealing，court 可 set aside / vary。
- **s.276**: misfeasance summary procedure — 針對 officer、liquidator、receiver、promoter；official receiver / liquidator / creditor / contributory 可申請；**不創建新訴因**。
- **s.265(1)**: preferential creditors — 僱員工資/遣散費/長服金 (EO Cap.57)、僱員補償 (Cap.282)、ORSO (Cap.426)、MPFA (Cap.485 s.17(7))、government statutory debts (12 個月內到期, s.265(1)(d))、銀行存戶 (≤HK$500,000, s.265(1)(db))、保險索償 (s.265(1)(e))。
- **s.265(3B)/(4)**: preferential creditors rank above floating chargee；liquidator's costs 先於一般分派。
- **s.263 + s.34 BO**: proof of debts；**s.264A**: statutory interest；**ss.265(5)/(5A)**: 三個月內 distrained goods 嘅 landlord。
- **s.227F**: 資產 < HK$200,000 → summary procedure，official receiver 做 liquidator。
- **ss.271–275**: 刑事罪行（concealment/falsification s.271–272、fraudulent conduct s.273、無 proper books s.274、fraudulent trading s.275）。

**Voluntary winding-up (LP s.13.4.3)**
- **s.228(1)(a)/(b), s.228A, s.228(1)(d)**: 開始方式 — articles 期限屆滿（ordinary resolution）、special resolution、directors' resolution (liabilities)、s.228A winding-up statement。
- **s.233**: certificate of solvency — directors 認為公司可喺 commencement 後 **12 個月**內全數還債；證明須喺 resolution 前 **5 星期**內發出；**s.117(1)**: certificate + resolution 15 日內交 Registrar → 有 = **members' voluntary winding-up**；無 = **creditors' voluntary winding-up** (s.233(4))。
- **s.241(1)/(2)**: creditors' meeting 須喺公司會議後 **14 日**內舉行；notice 同時發出 + Gazette + 中英文報章各一次；違反可致 liquidation invalid。
- **s.242**: creditors 提名 liquidator 優先於 members' nominee（7 日內可向法院申請裁定）。
- **s.235(1)**: members' voluntary — liquidator 由 members ordinary resolution 委任；**s.237A(1)**: liquidator 認為還唔切債 → summon creditors' meeting。
- **s.229(1)**: resolution 14 日內 Gazette 公告；**s.230**: voluntary winding-up commenced at passing of resolution；**s.231**: 停業（beneficial winding-up 除外）；**s.253**: liquidator 21 日內公告委任；**s.255A**: 帳目須審計。
- **s.227**: court order dissolution；**s.226A**: 註冊證明書後 2 年自動解散；**s.752**: 剩餘資產 bona vacantia 歸政府。
- **Liquidator 移除**: ss.196(1) & 252（court, on cause shown）；voluntary 另可 s.235A(1) special resolution / s.235A(2) court；resignation s.194(1) + CWUR (Cap.32H) Rules 154–155。

#### B. Leading Cases (full citations 以 LP 為準)

| Case | Principle |
|---|---|
| **Ebrahimi v Westbourne Galleries Ltd (1973) AC 360** | Just & equitable winding-up：quasi-partnership 小公司，股東被踢出管理層、合理期望受挫 → 清盤令；legal rights 受制於 equitable considerations |
| **Re ePlaza Ltd (unreported) HCCW 1122/2002** | Statutory demand 下 debt 有 genuine dispute → petition dismissed；deemed insolvency 可被推翻 |
| **Re Wah Nam Group Ltd (unreported, 26 July 2000) HCCW 166/2000** | Cash flow test + balance sheet test 雙軌判斷 unable to pay debts |
| **Yeung Siu Ming v Man Lung Textiles Ltd (2018) HKCA 521** | Registered shareholder 即有 standing 呈請；實益擁有與否無關 |
| **Golden Gate International Kindergarten and Nursery Ltd (2018) HKCFI 641** | 股東貸款以「公司盈利」為還款條件 → 永遠唔會 provable → petitioner 非 contingent creditor，無 standing |
| **Forsyth v Blundell (1973) 129 CLR 477** | Mortgagee/receiver duty of good faith：已知有人肯出高價仍私下低價賣 = breach |
| **Tse Kwong Lam v Wong Chit Sen (1983) 3 All ER 54 (PC)** | 賣俾自己有利益嘅公司非絕對禁止，但須證明 good faith + reasonable precautions 取得 best price（應取 expert advice on method of sale & reserve price） |
| **Downsview Nominees Ltd v First City Corp Ltd (1993) AC 295 (PC)** | Receiver 接受委任目的係阻撓另一 chargee 執行 → breach of good faith & proper purpose |
| **Super Master Holdings Ltd v Chung Nam Securities Ltd (unreported, 26 March 2004) HCA 4573/2003** | Mortgagee（同理 receiver）可為自身利益行使 power of sale，唔係 trustee；但須 good faith + reasonable care 取得 true market value |
| **Re Tyler (1907) 1 KB 865** | Court-appointed receiver 係 officer of the court，須 act lawfully + fairly + honourably（high-mindedness） |
| **Macau First Universal International Ltd v Ding Xiaohong (No 2) (2012) 2 HKLRD 494** | Court 唔會輕易撤換自己嘅 officer；honestly done his best 而任務 onerous → slow to remove |
| **Achieve Goal Holdings Ltd v Zhong Xin Ore-Material Holding Co Ltd (2018) HKCFI 2718** | Court receiver 對各方 impartial；unfitness/forfeit of confidence 先撤換；申請人負舉證責任 |
| **Liquidator of Almond Ltd v Cheong Chan Kao (2019) HKCFI 634** | Unfair preference：提前還有董事擔保嘅銀行貸款 → inferred desire to prefer（objective test + subjective desire） |
| **Aktieselskabet Dansk Skibsfinansiering v Brothers (2000) 3 HKCFAR 70, (2000) 1 HKC 511** | Fraudulent trading 須 actual dishonesty；honest belief 母公司會打救 → 唔算 fraud，即使客觀上存活機會低 |
| **Advance Housing Pty Ltd (in liq) v Newcastle Classic Development Pty Ltd (1994) 14 ACSR 230** | Liquidator 係 fiduciary：自己 firm 收過公司錢又要查 unfair preference = inherent conflict |
| **Re Rainbow Gate Ltd (unreported, 20 December 2007) HCCW 593/1998** / **Re Action Industrial (Intl) Ltd (unreported, 2 December 2009) HCCW 35/2007** | Liquidator 違 statutory duties（唔交帳、唔將款項存入 companies liquidation account、拖唔做）→ removed |
| **Re Carrian Holdings Ltd (in liq) (No 2) (1984) 1 HKC 598** | Disclaimer of onerous property 可被拒絕（如有 indemnity 令第三方嚴重受損） |

#### C. IRAC Application Points（答題骨架）

- **Issue spotting**: 分清 scenario 係 receivership（secured creditor 自救，公司未死）、provisional liquidation（petition 後 interim 保護）、定 winding-up（公司終局）。考官伏位：deregistration ≠ liquidation；receivership ≠ liquidation。
- **Rule selection**: 先問 (1) 邊條 route — compulsory (s.177) / members' voluntary (s.233 COS) / creditors' voluntary (s.233(4))；(2) 涉事條文 pinpoint 到 subsection。
- **Application**: 時間線最緊要 — 計返 commencement date（compulsory = petition presentation, s.184(2)；voluntary = resolution, s.230），再數 6 個月 / 2 年（unfair preference）、12 個月（floating charge s.267）、3 年（extortionate credit s.264B）。
- **Conclusion**: 要果斷 — 「the floating charge is liable to be set aside」「the liquidator should be removed」；引用 case 做 analogy（考評報告多次批評漏引 case law）。

### 易混淆概念對比

**Table 1: Compulsory Winding-up vs Members' Voluntary vs Creditors' Voluntary**

| 項目 | Compulsory (s.177) | Members' Voluntary | Creditors' Voluntary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 發動者 | Court order on petition（creditor/contributory/company/SFC 等） | Members（special resolution） | Members 發動，creditors 主導 |
| 公司狀態 | 通常 insolvent | **Solvent**（directors 簽 s.233 certificate of solvency，12 個月內還清） | Insolvent / 無 COS (s.233(4)) |
| Liquidator 委任 | Court；OR 先做 provisional liquidator (s.194(1)(a)) | Members ordinary resolution (s.235(1)) | Creditors 提名優先 (s.242) |
| Commencement | Petition presentation (s.184(2)) — relation-back | Passing of resolution (s.230) | Passing of resolution (s.230) |
| 自動 stay / void dispositions | 有 (ss.182, 186) | 無 | 無 |
| 點樣分辨 | 睇 scenario：有冇 petition/court order？有冇 certificate of solvency？無 COS 嘅 voluntary 一定係 creditors' | | |
| 考試伏位 | 「Solvent 公司股東想結束」→ members' voluntary，唔係 deregistration/just & equitable；CVWU 漏發 creditors' meeting notice → liquidation invalid (s.241) | | |

**Table 2: Privately Appointed Receiver vs Court-appointed Receiver vs Liquidator**

| 項目 | Private Receiver | Court-appointed Receiver | Liquidator |
|---|---|---|---|
| 委任來源 | Debenture/appointment instrument | Court order（HCO s.21L / CWUMPO s.725 / inherent） | Court / creditors / members |
| 身份 | Agent of debtor company（「only non-fiduciary agent」），唔係 court officer | **Officer of the court** | Fiduciary of the company |
| 首要職責對象 | Appointor debenture holder 嘅利益（good faith + proper purpose） | **All interested parties**，lawfully + fairly + honourably (Re Tyler) | 公司 + 全體 creditors/contributories，impartial |
| 目的 | 執行抵押、收返 secured assets（公司仍可生存） | 保護/保存資產 pending proceedings | 變賣全部資產、分派、解散公司 |
| 終止 | 目的達成 / appointor 隨時撤換 / resignation | 只可由 court discharge（除非 order 有期限）；court 唔輕易撤換 (Macau First) | ss.196(1)/252 court remove on cause shown；s.235A |
| 點樣分辨 | 睇邊個委任 + 目的係咪「保債權人收返抵押品」定「結束公司」 | | |
| 考試伏位 | Receiver 唔使為公司整體利益行事（Downsview 唯一紅線：good faith/proper purpose）；但 court receiver 標準高好多，唔好混淆兩套 duty | | |

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** What are the six grounds for compulsory winding-up under s.177 CWUMPO?
**Back:**
- Special resolution resolving to be wound up by the court
- No commencement of business within 1 year of incorporation, or suspension for a whole year (plus no intention to recommence)
- Company has no members
- Company is unable to pay its debts (s.178(1): statutory demand ≥HK$10,000 unpaid for 21 days; unsatisfied execution; proof to court)
- Event specified in articles for dissolution occurs
- Just and equitable (Ebrahimi v Westbourne Galleries Ltd (1973) AC 360)

💬 記「六大死因」：自己話要死、冇開過工、冇股東、還唔到錢、章程話到期、法庭覺得唔公道。考試最常考 (4) 同 (6)。

### Card 2
**Front:** How do members' voluntary winding-up and creditors' voluntary winding-up differ?
**Back:**
- Dividing line = solvency: directors sign a **certificate of solvency** (s.233) → members'; no COS (s.233(4)) → creditors'
- COS: full enquiry; opinion debts payable in full within **12 months**; issued within **5 weeks** before the members' resolution; filed with Registrar within 15 days (s.117(1))
- Creditors' VWU: creditors' meeting within **14 days** of the company meeting; notice simultaneous + Gazette + one English & one Chinese newspaper (s.241); creditors' nominee for liquidator prevails (s.242)
- Members' VWU: liquidator appointed by ordinary resolution (s.235(1))

💬 分界線就係嗰張「償債證明書」：有 = 股東話事；冇 = 債權人話事。記住 12 個月、5 星期、14 日三個數字。

### Card 3
**Front:** What are the elements of an unfair preference liable to be invalidated under ss.266/266B CWUMPO?
**Back:**
- Given to a creditor / surety / guarantor, putting them in a **better position** on winding-up (s.50(3) BO)
- Within **6 months** before commencement of winding-up — **2 years** if an associate (ss.266(1), 266B(1)(b); s.51(1) BO)
- Company **influenced by a desire** to prefer (s.266(4); s.50(4) BO)
- Company insolvent at the time or made insolvent by it (s.266B(2); s.51(2) BO)
- Examples: repaying unsecured creditor; granting security for pre-existing debt; undervalued transaction with existing creditor (Liquidator of Almond Ltd v Cheong Chan Kao (2019) HKCFI 634)
- NOT preferences: security for a **new** loan/creditor; paying secured creditor up to security value

💬 口訣「更好位置 + 有心偏幫 + 已經資不抵債 + 六個月內（自己人兩年）」。新貸款嘅抵押唔算，因為公司攞到對價。

### Card 4
**Front:** What must be proved for fraudulent trading liability under s.275(1) CWUMPO, and what are the consequences?
**Back:**
- Elements: (1) business carried on; (2) with intent to defraud creditors or for a fraudulent purpose; (3) defendant knowingly a party
- **Actual dishonesty** required — continuing to trade with no reasonable prospect of paying creditors is NOT enough by itself (Aktieselskabet Dansk Skibsfinansiering v Brothers (2000) 3 HKCFAR 70)
- Dishonesty = defendant knows the conduct is dishonest by standards of reasonable/honest people
- Civil: personally liable for all/any debts as court directs (no limit); Criminal: up to **5 years' imprisonment** + fine

💬 考試伏位：「明知冇希望仲繼續做生意」本身唔等於 fraudulent trading，一定要證到主觀不誠實。Dansk 案董事真心信母公司會救 → 甩身。

### Card 5
**Front:** State the order of distribution of free assets in a winding-up.
**Back:**
1. Costs & expenses of liquidation incl. liquidator's remuneration (s.265(4))
2. Preferential creditors (s.265(1)) — employees' wages/severance/long-service, MPF/ORSO, government debts, bank depositors (≤HK$500,000), insurance claims
3. Landlords who distrained goods within 3 months before the order (ss.265(5)/(5A))
4. Ordinary unsecured creditors **pari passu** (provable debts, s.263)
5. Statutory interest (s.264A)
6. Non-provable debts
7. Contributories: return of share capital, then surplus

💬 記住 fixed charge 資產完全唔入 pool；floating charge 資產要**先畀 preferential creditors** 先輪到 floating chargee (s.265(3B))。呢個係最常考嘅 priority 陷阱。

### Card 6
**Front:** What are a privately appointed receiver's duties and to whom are they owed?
**Back:**
- Primary duty: realise charged assets for the benefit of the **appointor debenture holder** (contractual, per appointment instrument)
- Equitable duty of **good faith & proper purpose** — no wilful/reckless sacrifice of the security holder's interests (Forsyth v Blundell (1973) 129 CLR 477; Downsview Nominees v First City Corp (1993) AC 295 (PC))
- Duty of **reasonable care** — obtain proper price; take professional advice on method of sale & reserve (Tse Kwong Lam v Wong Chit Sen (1983) 3 All ER 54); higher price obtainable ≠ automatic breach
- Duty to provide information: common law 'need to know' + s.300A CWUMPO
- Not a trustee of the power of sale; may prefer own/appointor's interest if acting in good faith (Super Master Holdings v Chung Nam Securities, HCA 4573/2003)

💬 Private receiver 係「唯一唔使對公司盡 fiduciary duty 嘅 agent」，佢嘅老闆係抵押權人；但唔可以為整垮公司而做嘢。

### Card 7
**Front:** On what bases may a liquidator be removed, and under which sections?
**Back:**
- Court may remove in compulsory AND voluntary winding-up: **ss.196(1) & 252** — "on cause shown"
- Causes: misconduct; personal unfitness; breach of statutory/fiduciary/other duties; lack of vigour; failure to proceed with reasonable expedition (Re Kaypak Homecare Ltd (1987) BCLC 409; Re Rainbow Gate Ltd HCCW 593/1998; Re Action Industrial (Intl) Ltd HCCW 35/2007)
- Voluntary only: special resolution of members (s.235A(1)) or court on creditor/contributory application (s.235A(2))
- Resignation: s.194(1) + CWUR Rules 154–155 (creditors'/contributories' meetings, then court if refused)
- Serious cases: damages under s.276

💬 撤換 liquidator 門檻係 "cause shown"；違法定職責（唔入帳、唔交報告）+ 利益衝突 + 拖足幾年唔做 = 必炒，Apply and Analyse 3 就係咁。

### Card 8
**Front:** What is the effect of a winding-up petition/order on dispositions and proceedings, and how can a company continue trading?
**Back:**
- Winding-up deemed to commence at **presentation of petition** (s.184(2))
- Any disposition of property after commencement is **void** unless the court orders otherwise (s.182) — covers sale, lease, charge, gift, loan, liens
- On the winding-up order, no action commenced/continued against the company without **leave of court** (s.186); provisional liquidator appointment also triggers a stay
- Directors' powers cease upon appointment of provisional liquidator/liquidator
- Company should apply for a **validating order** for payments in the ordinary course (e.g. salaries); refused if insolvent and trading would deplete assets further
- Title to assets stays with the company (no real trust; vesting order possible under s.198)

💬 關鍵係 relation-back：order 3 月先做，但 commencement 計返 12 月入紙嗰日。s.182 先係點解 petition 一出公司戶口即凍結、要攞 validating order 出糧。
