# M10 Chapter 5: Aspects of Agency Law — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: D
**Model Answer (EN):** An agency relationship can be created in four ways: (1) by written agreement (express appointment, in writing or verbally); (2) by estoppel (agency by holding out); (3) by ratification (the principal's subsequent approval of an act done without authority); and (4) by necessity (urgent circumstances requiring protection of the principal's property). Voluntary performance of services without an emergency creates no agency; the person is a mere volunteer.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題係純框架 recall。四個形成方式：written agreement、estoppel、ratification、necessity。Option D「by performance」係捏造出嚟嘅——冇緊急情況、冇協議，自願幫人做嘢唔會創造 agency（如果係緊急兼聯絡唔到 principal，先有可能係 agency by necessity，但 D 明確寫 "without any emergency"）。A、B、C 都係正宗形成方式。考試伏位：唔好將「agency by necessity」同一般好心幫忙混淆——necessity 有三大嚴格條件。

**Rule Check:** LP Ch.5 s.5.1 (Formation of Agency); *Great Northern Railway v Swaffield* (1874) for the necessity conditions.

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 以為「默示同意/事後多謝」都係一種形成方式。記住：無協議、無緊急、無追認，就無 agency。

### Q2 — Answer: Estate Agents Ordinance (Cap. 511), s.45
**Model Answer (EN):** Section 45 of the Estate Agents Ordinance (Cap. 511) requires an estate agent to execute an estate agency agreement with the client in order to pursue a claim against the client for remuneration. Because Mr Cheung never signed an estate agency agreement with Mrs Lau, he cannot claim commission from her, even though he successfully procured a purchaser who completed the sale.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 地產代理追佣金的 statutory precondition：必須先同 client 簽 estate agency agreement。口頭協議唔算數。Exam Lens 強調考試要求 pinpoint section——答「Estate Agents Ordinance」而冇 Cap. 511 同 s.45 會被扣分。順帶記住：就算簽咗協議，agent 一般要「完成交易」先有佣金（merely introducing a purchaser is not enough），但呢題嘅致命位係冇書面協議。

**Rule Check:** Estate Agents Ordinance (Cap. 511) s.45.

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 誤以為「成功搵到買家完成交易」就一定有佣金；或者引用錯 Ordinance（例如 Powers of Attorney Ordinance (Cap. 31)——嗰個係管 donor/donee，唔關事）。

### Q3 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** Issue: whether THT is bound by Kelvin's unauthorised purchase of teapots. Rule: where an agent acts without or in excess of authority, the principal may ratify the act, provided (1) the principal existed at the time of the transaction, (2) the principal was made aware of all material facts, (3) the principal ratifies the whole transaction, and (4) ratification occurs within a reasonable time. Application: THT existed when Kelvin signed; the managing director was told the full details of the contract; THT adopted the whole transaction by selling half the teapots at a profit, promptly upon delivery. All four conditions are satisfied. Conclusion: THT has ratified the contract and is bound to pay PorcelainCo; Kelvin's liability is absolved.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題係 Exam Lens 點名嘅經典伏位（Jun 2024 考生將 ratification 誤答 estoppel）。分辨關鍵：時間次序同 representation 來源。
- **A 錯**：estoppel/holding out 需要 principal 喺簽約前向「呢個 third party」作 representation。THT 同 PorcelainCo 從未交易過，THT 冇向 PorcelainCo hold out 過 Kelvin 有買茶壺嘅權；Kelvin「purchasing supervisor」嘅頭銜唔係 THT 向 PorcelainCo 做嘅 representation。
- **C 錯**：如果 THT 冇認可，C 先啱（breach of warranty of authority, *Collen v Wright* (1857)）。但事實係 MD 知情後賣咗一半貨——用行為 adopt 咗成單交易，呢個就係 ratification。
- **D 錯**：agency by necessity 需要 (1) 受託管有 principal 財產、(2) 緊急兼聯絡唔到 principal、(3) 真誠行事（*Great Northern Railway v Swaffield* (1874)）。買茶壺根本無緊急，Kelvin 可以請示。
- **B 啱**：四個 ratification 條件全部滿足——principal 存在、知悉全部重要事實、全盤（唔係部分）接納、合理時間內追認。

**Rule Check:** LP Ch.5 s.5.1.4 (ratification conditions); *Gimex Development Ltd v Cua Wai Tai & Another* (2000) (adoption/acquiescence); *Collen v Wright* (1857); *Great Northern Railway v Swaffield* (1874).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 見到「principal 有啲行為（賣貨）」就衝去答 estoppel。Estoppel 嘅 representation 一定係「簽約之前、由 principal 向 third party 作出」；事後認可係 ratification，唔係 estoppel。

### Q4 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** Issue: whether BathLux is bound by the tile purchase made by its former manager. Rule: an agent acting within apparent authority binds the principal. Apparent authority requires (1) a representation by the principal, by words, conduct or previous dealings, that the agent has authority; (2) reliance by the third party; (3) reliance to the third party's detriment. Termination of actual authority does not end apparent authority unless third parties are notified. Application: BathLux's conduct over five years of settled dealings represented to TileMart that Gloria had authority to order on BathLux's account; TileMart relied on that representation and parted with HK$80,000 of tiles to its detriment; BathLux never notified TileMart of Gloria's resignation. Conclusion: BathLux is bound to pay; its remedy is against Gloria personally.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- **A 錯**：actual authority 完咗唔等於 apparent authority 完咗。Principal 有責任通知有來往嘅 third parties；唔通知，表面權限繼續存在。
- **B 錯**：ratification 係用嚟處理「無權代理人事後被追認」；呢度 Gloria 喺 TileMart 眼中本來就有 apparent authority，合約一簽已綁住 BathLux，唔需要 ratify，BathLux 亦冇得「選擇唔認」。
- **C 啱**：三大要求齊晒——representation by past dealings（五年嚟 BathLux 找晒數）、reliance、detriment（交咗貨）。呢個正正係 LP 內 Stan/紅酒例子同 *Freeman & Lockyer* 嘅應用。
- **D 錯**：breach of warranty of authority 係 third party 告唔到 principal 時嘅後備（*Collen v Wright* (1857)）；呢度 TileMart 可以直接告 BathLux。話「former agent 永遠綁唔到 principal」係絕對錯。

**Rule Check:** LP Ch.5 s.5.2.3 (three requirements for apparent authority) and s.5.3.1 Apply and Analyse 1 (ex-MD ordering wine); *Freeman & Lockyer v Buckhurst Park Property Ltd* (1964); *Panorama Developments (Guildford) Ltd v Fidelis Furnishing Fabrics* (1971).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 以為「離職 = 權限即時歸零」。考試鍾意考「principal 冇通知 third party」呢個位——沉默嘅 principal 要孭返個 apparent authority。

### Q5 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** Issue: whether PBC is bound by a pre-incorporation contract signed by Alice. Rule: one condition of effective ratification is that the principal must exist at the time of the transaction and the agent must have been expressly contracted as an agent. A company that did not exist when the contract was made cannot ratify it; the person who signed is personally liable (*Kelner v Baxter* (1866)). Application: PBC did not exist when Alice signed the supply contract with Andes. PBC is therefore incapable of ratifying the contract, and it never adopted it (the board refused delivery and payment). Conclusion: Andes' claim against PBC fails; Andes should pursue Alice personally on the contract.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- **A 錯**：「註冊成立 + 做返預期生意」唔等於 ratification。Ratification 第一個條件（principal 喺交易時已存在）永遠滿足唔到，之後做咩都補救唔到（除非另立新合約/novation，但事實冇）。
- **B 啱**：*Kelner v Baxter* (1866) 直接對辦——公司未註冊，唔存在嘅 principal 冇能力 ratify，promoter/agent 自己孭個人責任。
- **C 錯**：agency by necessity 要緊急情況、聯絡唔到 principal、真誠保護 principal 財產。簽兩年供豆合約係普通商業決定，無緊急可言；而且當時根本無 principal 嘅財產可保護。
- **D 錯**：apparent authority 嘅 representation 必須來自 principal（或獲授權人士）。PBC 當時唔存在，冇可能 hold out Alice；representation 只係 Alice 自己作出，estoppel/apparent authority 唔成立。

**Rule Check:** *Kelner v Baxter* (1866); LP Ch.5 s.5.1.4 (ratification condition 1) and s.5.5.1 (personal liability — acting for a non-existing principal).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 以為「公司成立後追認就得」或者「promoter 天然有 apparent authority」。記住 Kelner v Baxter 係 ratification 四條件嘅考試切入點：principal 唔存在 = 永遠 ratify 唔到 = promoter 個人孭鑊。
