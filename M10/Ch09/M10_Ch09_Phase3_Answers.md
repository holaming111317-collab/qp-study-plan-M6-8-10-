# M10 Chapter 9: Internal Administration of a Company — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: C

**Model Answer (EN):**
For a private company (or a company limited by guarantee), the AGM must be held within **9 months** after the end of the company's accounting reference period (s.610(1)(a), CO). The accounting reference period ended on 31 March 2025, so the deadline is **31 December 2025**. If the company defaults, a member may apply to the court for an order to call or direct the calling of an AGM (ss.610(7)–(8)); the AGM could only be avoided if all its business were done by written resolution with the reporting documents provided to each member (s.612), which the question excludes.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- C 啱：private company 嘅 AGM deadline = accounting reference period 結束後 9 個月內。31 March + 9 months = 31 December。
- A 錯：3 個月冇任何 statutory basis，係干擾項。
- B 錯：6 個月係 s.374(3) 會計紀錄披露財政狀況嘅最長間隔，唔係 AGM 期限 —— 呢個係典型「數字搬家」伏位。
- D 錯：12 個月聽落合理（「annual」嘛），但 CO 對 private/guarantee companies 寫明係 9 個月，唔可以憑常識作答。
- 考試伏位：AGM 舉行期限、AGM notice（21 日，s.571(1)(a)）、annual return（incorporation anniversary 後 42 日，s.662(1)）三個時限成日撈亂，要分開背。

**Rule Check:** Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) s.610(1)(a); ss.610(7)–(8); s.612.

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 見到「annual」就以為係一年內（12 months）——錯。仲有：9 個月規則只適用於 private companies 同 companies limited by guarantee；如果題目係 public company，唔好套用呢個答案（LP 本章未講 public company 嘅期限）。

### Q2 — Answer: B

**Model Answer (EN):**
A company may change its name only by **special resolution** (s.107, CO) — a resolution passed by a majority of at least 75% of the votes cast (s.564). Re-appointing an auditor at the AGM is done by an ordinary resolution of the members (ss.394, 396). Removing a director before the expiry of his term requires only an **ordinary resolution**, albeit with **special notice** (s.462, s.462(4), s.578(1)). Filling a casual vacancy in the office of auditor is a matter for the **directors** (or, failing them, the members by ordinary resolution) — no special resolution is involved (s.397).

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- B 啱：s.107 明確規定改名要 special resolution（≥75%，s.564）。其他要 special resolution 嘅事項：alteration of constitution（s.88(2)）、reduction of capital（s.215）、winding-up。
- A 錯：委任/續任核數師係 AGM 上以普通決議做（s.396(1)），每年 refresh。
- C 錯（最大伏位）：罷免董事係 **ordinary resolution + special notice** —— 好多考生因為「好嚴重」就以為要 special resolution。記住：special notice 係通知程序（28 日前通知動議），同 75% 門檻完全冇關。
- D 錯：casual vacancy 由董事填補，董事一個月內唔做先輪到股東喺 GM 以普通決議委任（s.397）。
- Default rule：CO 或文件冇指明決議類型時，ordinary resolution 已足夠（s.562(3)）。

**Rule Check:** Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) s.107; s.564; s.462; s.462(4); s.578(1); s.396(1); s.397; s.562(3).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 「Special notice ≠ special resolution」係本章最高頻失分位。罷免董事/核數師都係 ordinary resolution，只係通知程序升級咗。答 MCQ 時見到「removal of director = special resolution」呢類 option 要立即剔除。

### Q3 — Answer: C

**Model Answer (EN):**
**Issue:** Whether the ordinary resolution removing Leo as a director is valid despite the procedural defects.
**Rule:** Members may remove a director before the expiry of his term by ordinary resolution notwithstanding anything in the articles (s.462(1), CO), but **special notice** is required — notice of the intention to move the resolution given not less than **28 days** before the meeting (s.462(4); s.578(1)). The company must also send a copy of the notice to the director concerned, who is entitled to **be heard** at the meeting and to make written representations (s.463).
**Application:** Only 14 days' notice of the meeting was given and no special notice was given at all; Leo never received a copy of the notice and was denied the opportunity to be heard or to make representations. These are not mere informalities: Leo lost a substantive statutory right to make representations as to his proposed removal. The irregularity principle (Yip Peter v Asian Electronics Ltd (1998) 2 HKC 96) saves only mere informalities where the intention of the meeting is clear; it does not cure the denial of a director's s.463 rights.
**Conclusion:** The removal is prima facie invalid. Ivy and Ken must start afresh with proper special notice and a fresh general meeting.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- C 啱：三個程序缺陷齊晒 —— (1) 冇 28 日 special notice（s.462(4)、s.578(1)）；(2) 冇 send copy 俾 Leo（s.463）；(3) Leo 冇機會陳詞。呢啲唔係 technicality，係實質權利。
- A 錯：s.462 嘅「notwithstanding anything in the articles」只係話 articles 唔可以奪走股東罷免權，**唔係**豁免法定程序。70% 票數再高都補救唔到程序缺陷。
- B 錯：irregularity principle 只覆蓋「mere informality and irregularity」而且結果唔會變嘅情況。Leo 失去咗 s.463 嘅陳詞機會，complaint 超越程序層面 —— LP Exam Practice Q1(c) 嘅分析正正係咁講。
- D 錯：罷免董事係 ordinary resolution（simple majority，s.563），唔係 75% special resolution —— 混淆咗 special notice 同 special resolution。
- 考試伏位：見到「majority 大比數通過」就答 valid 係常見錯誤；程序權利（notice + 被聽取）係獨立要求。

**Rule Check:** Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) s.462(1); s.462(4); s.463; s.578(1); s.578(2); Yip Peter v Asian Electronics Ltd (1998) 2 HKC 96.

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 同學成日背咗「罷免董事 = ordinary resolution」就以為冇其他要求，漏咗 special notice 同 s.463 嘅被罷免者權利。記住完整公式：**s.462 ordinary resolution + s.462(4)/s.578(1) 28-day special notice + s.463 copy & right to be heard + 唔可以用 written resolution（s.548）**。

### Q4 — Answer: B

**Model Answer (EN):**
**Issue:** Whether M & Co breached its common law duty of care in accepting the managing director's explanation of the altered invoices.
**Rule:** An auditor owes the company a duty of care in tort and contract to exercise reasonable skill and care. The auditor is 'a watch-dog, but not a bloodhound' and may rely on management's assurances **only where such reliance is reasonable** (Re Kingston Cotton Mill Co (No. 2) (1896) 2 Ch 279). Once the auditor discovers irregularities — such as altered invoices — he is put on enquiry and must make an **exhaustive enquiry** and inform the board; he is not entitled to rest content with assurances, however much he trusts the officer (Re Thomas Gerrard & Son Ltd (1968) Ch 455).
**Application:** M & Co actually discovered the altered invoices — suspicion was aroused. From that point, blind reliance on Owen's bare assurance was not reasonable; a sample verification, exhaustive enquiry into the alterations and a report to the full board were required.
**Conclusion:** M & Co is likely liable in negligence to the company.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- B 啱：關鍵事實係核數師**已經發現**改過嘅單據 —— 一旦被 put on enquiry，信賴管理層就唔再 reasonable，必須 exhaustive enquiry（Thomas Gerrard）。
- A 錯：Kingston Cotton Mill 嘅 reliance 之所以合理，係因為當時**冇任何嘢引起懷疑**，而且證明文件嚟自「acknowledged competence and high reputation」嘅經理。呢度已經見到 altered invoices，事實唔同，rule 嘅例外情況唔啱用。
- C 錯：「auditor is not an insurer」只係界定 duty 嘅範圍（reasonable assurance），唔係 negligence 嘅免死金牌 —— 佢唔可以豁免「被發現異常後唔追查」呢種 breach。
- D 錯：s.408 係刑事罪行，針對**明知或罔顧**咁漏咗 report 入面應有嘅 statement；而且 LP 講明，如果核數師真誠相信資料齊全（即使判斷錯或疏忽），唔構成該罪行。呢度係 civil negligence 問題，criminal liability 亦唔會「automatically」establish civil liability。
- 考試伏位：兩個 case 嘅分水嶺係「有冇被 put on notice」；答題時第一句就要指出呢個 distinction。

**Rule Check:** Re Thomas Gerrard & Son Ltd (1968) Ch 455; Re Kingston Cotton Mill Co (No. 2) (1896) 2 Ch 279; Re London and General Bank (No. 2) (1895) 2 Ch 673; Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) s.408.

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 背咗「watch-dog not bloodhound」就逢 auditor 題都答 not liable —— 呢句格言只保護**冇可疑跡象**下嘅合理信賴。凡係 fact pattern 寫明核數師「發現咗/留意到」異常，答案方向多數係 liable（Thomas Gerrard 路線）。

### Q5 — Answer: (a) Fiona = de facto director; Gordon = shadow director. (b) No — s.548.

**Model Answer (EN):**
(a) **Fiona — de facto director.** Although never validly appointed, she has 'assumed to act as a director': she is held out as a director (signing contracts as 'Director of PRL') and exercises top-level management functions (negotiating with creditors, countersigning cheques) over a sustained three-year period. Under the statutory definition, a director is 'any person occupying the position of director, by whatever name called' (s.2(1), CO); see also Re Hydrodan (Corby) Ltd [1994] BCC 161 (Millett J) and Deputy Commissioner of Taxation v Austin (1998) 28 ACSR 565 (occupation of the position of director is a question of degree). **Consequence:** Fiona is subject to the same duties (including fiduciary duties) as a validly appointed de jure director.
**Gordon — shadow director.** He is a person in accordance with whose directions or instructions the directors (or a majority of them) are accustomed to act (s.2, CO). His instructions are given in a private, not professional, capacity, so the carve-out for professional advice does not help him; see Re Unisoft Group Ltd (No. 2) [1994] BCC 766. Unlike a de facto director, a shadow director does **not necessarily owe fiduciary duties** to the company, though the CO imposes similar statutory duties on him.
(b) **No.** Anything that may be done by ordinary or special resolution at a general meeting may be done by written resolution, **except** the removal of an auditor or a **director** before the expiry of the term of office (s.548, CO). Fiona's removal must therefore be effected by an ordinary resolution at a **general meeting** with special notice (s.462; s.462(4); s.578(1)), with a copy of the notice sent to her and her right to be heard respected (s.463).

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- (a) Fiona：未經任命但「做晒出嚟」——對外自稱 Director、行使最高管理職能、為期三年（唔係一次性幫手）。按 DCT v Austin，question of degree：length of time + breadth of activities 都指向 de facto director。呢個身份受 s.2(1) 嘅廣義定義覆蓋，所以 full directors' duties 上身。
- Gordon：佢唔露面，但 Sam 同 Tina「accustomed to act」on his instructions —— textbook shadow director（s.2）。注意 carve-out：如果佢係以**專業身份**（retired accountant 提供專業意見）俾 advice 就唔算；題目特登寫明「private capacity」就係封咗呢條出路。
- 伏位：Fiona 同 Gordon 容易對調 —— 記住「拋頭露面做嘢 = de facto；幕後發號施令 = shadow」。另外 shadow director 唔一定孭 fiduciary duties，de facto 就一定孭。
- (b) 考 s.548 嘅例外：written resolution 萬能，**唯獨**罷免董事/核數師（任期屆滿前）唔用得。所以一定要返 GM + special notice 路線。
- 考試伏位：見到 unanimous shareholder agreement 就以為 written resolution 搞得掂 —— 罷免董事係法定例外，全體同意都冇用。

**Rule Check:** Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) s.2(1); s.2 (definition of shadow director); s.548; s.462; s.462(4); s.463; s.578(1); Re Hydrodan (Corby) Ltd [1994] BCC 161; Deputy Commissioner of Taxation v Austin (1998) 28 ACSR 565; Re Unisoft Group Ltd (No. 2) [1994] BCC 766.

**Common Trap（繁中）:** (1) 將 de facto 同 shadow 撈亂 —— 前者靠 conduct/held out，後者靠 accustomed to act on instructions；(2) 以為專業人士必然豁免 shadow director 身份 —— 只有「advice given in a professional capacity」先豁免，意見一旦超越專業範疇就可能中招（LP Exam Practice Q1(a) 嘅 Andrew 正係咁）；(3) 忘記 s.548 例外，以為 written resolution 可以罷免董事。
