# M10 Chapter 9: Internal Administration of a Company — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives (3–5, English, action verbs)

1. **Apply** the accounting records, annual financial statements and simplified reporting requirements under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) ('CO') to a given company (ss.361–380, 383, 387–391).
2. **Analyse** the appointment, duties, rights, liabilities and cessation of office of auditors under the CO (ss.393–430) and at common law.
3. **Distinguish** the decision-making powers of members in general meetings from those of the board of directors, and **apply** the procedural rules on notices, quorum, proxies, resolutions and written resolutions (ss.548–622).
4. **Classify** the different types of directors (de jure, de facto, shadow, alternate, nominee, reserve, ED/NED/INED) and **apply** the rules on appointment, board meeting procedures, vacation of office and disqualification (CO Part 10; CWUMPO (Cap. 32) Part IVA).
5. **Evaluate** the validity of company decisions using the irregularity principle and the inspection rights of directors and members (ss.374–378, 620, 740).

| Who | Rule | Citation |

| Members (common law) | **No general right** to inspect books of account or other company documents (reflection of separate legal entity). Model Articles: wider member inspection subject to directors'/GM approval. | LP §9.1.4 |

| Topic | Rule | Citation |
|---|---|---|

| Criminal liability | Knowingly or recklessly causing a required statement to be **omitted** from the auditor's report = offence. | Civil liability (common law) | Duty of care in **tort and contract** (implied term); reasonable care and skill; auditor provides only **reasonable assurance** — 'not an insurer'; 'a watch-dog, but not a bloodhound'; entitled to rely on management assurances **if reasonable**; once put on enquiry, must make exhaustive enquiry. #### E. Relevant Ordinances — The Board of Directors

| Topic | Rule | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | 'Director' = any person occupying the position of director, by whatever name called. | **s.2(1)** |

#### F. IRAC Application Points（考試答題骨架）

1. **Member seeking company documents**: Issue — does a member have inspection rights? Rule — no general common law right; statutory registers only (s.620, s.631, ss.642/649, s.310, s.355); court order under s.740 requires ≥ 2.5% voting rights or ≥ 5 members, plus good faith + proper purpose (dominant purpose test). Application — identify the member's purpose (investigating suspected director misconduct = proper; personal vendetta alone = improper). Conclusion — likely granted/denied.
2. **Removing a director**: Issue — valid removal? Rule — s.462 ordinary resolution + special notice (s.462(4), s.578(1): ≥ 28 days) + s.463 right to be heard/representations; **cannot** be done by written resolution (s.548). Application — check each procedural step against the facts. Conclusion — prima facie invalid if any step missed; consider whether the irregularity principle rescues it (mere informality vs substantive loss of representation rights).
3. **Auditor negligence**: Issue — breach of duty of care? Rule — reasonable skill and care; watch-dog not bloodhound (Re Kingston Cotton Mill); entitled to rely on management **only if reasonable**; once on notice of irregularity, must enquire exhaustively (Re Thomas Gerrard). Application — was the auditor put on enquiry? Was reliance reasonable? Conclusion — liable / not liable.
4. **De facto vs shadow director**: Issue — status of a non-appointed influencer. Rule — de facto = assumes to act as director/held out (Re Hydrodan; DCT v Austin); shadow = board accustomed to act on instructions, excluding professional advice (s.2). Application — map the person's actual conduct. Conclusion — de facto → full directors' duties; shadow → not necessarily fiduciary.
5. **Board vs GM irregularity**: Board meeting irregularity (e.g. deliberately withheld notice) → void, even if outcome unchanged (Billion Express). GM irregularity → saved by the irregularity principle if mere informality and intention clear (Yip Peter), unless fraud on minority or defect of substance (Baillie).

### 易混淆概念對比

#### Table 1: De facto Director vs Shadow Director

| 比較點 | De facto director | Shadow director |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | A person who **assumes to act as a director** and is held out as one, though never validly appointed | A person in accordance with whose **directions or instructions** the directors (or a majority) are **accustomed to act** |
| Statutory basis | Covered by the general definition in **s.2(1) CO** | Defined separately in **s.2 CO**; **not** within the general 'director' definition |
| Key authority | Re Hydrodan (Corby) Ltd [1994] BCC 161; Deputy Commissioner of Taxation v Austin (1998) 28 ACSR 565 | Re Unisoft Group Ltd (No. 2) [1994] BCC 766 |
| Fiduciary duties | **Yes** — same duties as an appointed (de jure) director | **Not necessarily** — CO imposes similar statutory duties but fiduciary duties not automatic |
| Professional adviser carve-out | N/A — conduct-based test (question of degree) | Advice given in a **professional capacity** is excluded |
| 點樣分辨 | 行為導向：睇佢有冇「做出嚟」— 簽文件、對外自稱董事、行使最高管理職能（top-level management functions） | 控制導向：睇佢係咪「幕後操盤」— 董事會習慣性跟佢指示行事，但佢自己唔拋頭露面 |
| 考試伏位 | 做咗一兩次幫手唔算 —— 要睇 time span 同 breadth of activities（DCT v Austin）；emergency/friend 嘅藉口未必成立 | 專業人士（律師/會計師）喺專業範疇內俾意見 ≠ shadow director；但如果意見超越專業範疇（如商業決策），就可能中招 |

#### Table 2: AGM vs GM vs Special Notice（開會通知期最易混）

| 比較點 | Annual General Meeting (AGM) | General Meeting (GM) | Special notice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nature | Mandatory annual meeting after each financial year | Any general meeting that is not an AGM | A notice of **intention to move a particular resolution** |
| Timing | Private/guarantee companies: within **9 months** after end of accounting reference period (s.610(1)(a)) | Any time in the year | Given **≥ 28 days before the meeting** (s.578(1)) |
| Notice period | **21 days** (s.571(1)(a)) | **14 days** (s.571(1)(b)) | Given to members at the same time as the notice of meeting (s.578(2)); fallback: ≥ 14 days by newspaper advertisement (s.578(3)) |
| Short notice | Only if **all shareholders** consent | Majority holding ≥ **95%** of voting rights (s.571(3)) | Cannot be shortened by consent — statutory minimum |
| When required | Every year unless dispensed with (s.612 — written resolution route) | Convened by directors (s.565); requisition by ≥ 5% paid-up voting capital (ss.566–568) | Removal of a **director** before term (s.462) and removal of an **auditor** (s.419) — both are **ordinary resolutions** |
| 點樣分辨 | 記口訣：「AGM 廿一、GM 十四、special notice 廿八」；問你邊種 notice 就對返邊個 section | 股東 requisition 嘅 5% 係 **paid-up capital carrying voting rights**，唔係人數 | Special notice ≠ special resolution！前者係通知程序（28 日），後者係 75% 大多數通過 |
| 考試伏位 | 9 個月期限只係 private/guarantee companies；public companies 規則唔同（LP 本章未展開，唔好亂寫） | 漏咗 send copy 俾被罷免嘅董事/核數師（s.463 / s.419(3)）→ 決議 prima facie invalid | 考生成日將 removal of director 答做 special resolution —— 其實係 **ordinary resolution + special notice**；亦唔可以用 written resolution（s.548） |

### Card 1
**Front:** What standard must a company's accounting records meet under s.373(2) of the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), and for how long must they be retained?

**Back:**
- Records must be sufficient to: (a) show and explain the company's transactions; (b) disclose with reasonable accuracy, at any time, the company's financial position and financial performance; (c) enable directors to ensure financial statements comply with the CO (s.373(2)).
- Must contain daily entries of all sums received/expended and a record of assets and liabilities (s.373(3)).
- Retention: **7 years** (s.377); hard copy or electronic form (s.376(2)).
- Directors failing to take all reasonable steps commit a criminal offence (ss.373(5)–(7)).

💬 白話解釋：記住「三大足夠」——解釋交易、隨時反映財政狀況、幫董事確保報表合規。保存期係 7 年（s.377），唔好同 minutes 嘅 10 年（s.618(2)）搞亂。董事疏忽冇保存好係刑事罪行。

### Card 2
**Front:** When does a private company qualify as a 'small private company' for simplified reporting, and how can it lose the exemption?

**Back:**
- Qualifies by satisfying **any 2 of 3** (s.361): (1) total revenue ≤ HK$100m; (2) total assets ≤ HK$100m; (3) average employees ≤ 100.
- A company not initially qualifying may qualify by meeting 2 conditions in **2 consecutive financial years** (ss.359(3)–(4)).
- Disqualification: fails any 2 conditions for **2 consecutive years** → loses exemption in the **immediately following** financial year.
- Eligible private company route: ≥ 75% member approval (no veto) + higher thresholds (revenue/assets ≤ HK$200m) (s.360, Sch. 3).
- Banks, SFO-licensed corporations, insurers and money-lending businesses can never qualify (s.359(4)).

💬 白話解釋：口訣「三揀二」：收入、資產、員工，三個條件滿足兩個就得。跌出資格要連續兩年唔達標先會喺第三年失去豁免。睇題目時一定要逐年 check，唔好睇一年就下結論。

### Card 3
**Front:** A shareholder holding 3% of voting rights suspects the directors of misappropriating company funds. What is her route to inspect the company's accounting records, and what must she prove?

**Back:**
- Members have **no general common law right** to inspect books of account; statutory inspection covers only specified registers/minutes (s.620, s.631, ss.642/649, s.310, s.355).
- Route: apply to court under **s.740**.
- Threshold (s.740(6)): ≥ **2.5% of voting rights** OR ≥ **5 members** — a 3% holder qualifies.
- Must prove: (1) application made in **good faith**; (2) inspection for a **proper purpose** (must relate to shareholder status, e.g. investigating suspected director misconduct — Re Lehman Brown Ltd (2011) 5 HKLRD 668).
- Dominant purpose proper = sufficient even with an improper secondary purpose (Wong Kar Gee Mimi (2011) 5 HKLRD 241).

💬 白話解釋：股東同董事查閱權差好遠 —— 董事可以隨時免費睇（s.374(1)(b)），股東就要過 s.740 兩關：先過門檻（2.5% 或 5 個人），再證明 good faith + proper purpose。答題記住 dominant purpose test：主要目的正當就得，就算有心報仇都唔緊要。

### Card 4
**Front:** What is the common law standard of care owed by an auditor, and when is the auditor entitled to rely on management's assurances?

**Back:**
- Duty of care in both **tort and contract** (implied term); reasonable care and skill judged by prevailing professional standards.
- Auditor provides **reasonable assurance** only — 'not an insurer', does not guarantee the accounts (Re London and General Bank (No. 2) (1895) 2 Ch 673).
- 'A watch-dog, but **not a bloodhound**' — not bound to be suspicious (Re Kingston Cotton Mill Co (No. 2) (1896) 2 Ch 279).
- Reliance on assurances is allowed **only if reasonable**: once put on notice of irregularity, must make **exhaustive enquiry** and inform the board (Re Thomas Gerrard & Son Ltd (1968) Ch 455).
- Duty owed to the **company** — no duty to shareholders/public at large; trading loss not recoverable (Guang Xin Enterprises Ltd v Kwan Wong Tan & Fong (2002) 2 HKLRD 319).

💬 白話解釋：核數師係看門狗唔係獵犬 —— 平時可以信管理層，但一見到可疑嘢（e.g. 改過嘅單據）就一定要查到底。兩個 case 一對記：Kingston Cotton Mill（冇可疑 → 可以信）vs Thomas Gerrard（有可疑 → 唔可以齋信）。信唔信得過，關鍵係 reasonable。

### Card 5
**Front:** How may an auditor be removed or resign, and what procedural protections exist?

**Back:**
- **Removal**: ordinary resolution at GM (s.419) + **special notice** (s.419(2)) + copy to the auditor (s.419(3)); removal does not deprive compensation/damages (s.420).
- **Resignation**: written notice deposited at registered office (s.417); must deposit a **statement of circumstances** (or nil statement) (s.424); company must send it to members.
- Auditor may require directors to convene a GM to explain the circumstances (s.421) and has a right to attend (s.422(1)(c)).
- Court may block circulation of the statement if used to secure needless publicity for defamatory matter (s.426(3)).
- Removal of an auditor before expiry of term **cannot** be done by written resolution (s.548).

💬 白話解釋：核數師走人唔可以靜靜哋走 —— 一定要交 statement of circumstances，等股東知道有冇被管理層阻撓查數。罷免核數師同罷免董事一樣：ordinary resolution + special notice，同埋都係 s.548 嘅 written resolution 例外。

### Card 6
**Front:** State the notice periods for an AGM, a GM and a special notice, and the ways each may be shortened.

**Back:**
- **AGM: 21 days** (s.571(1)(a)); shorter only with consent of **all** shareholders; articles may lengthen.
- **GM: 14 days** (s.571(1)(b)); shorter with consent of a majority holding ≥ **95%** of voting rights (s.571(3)).
- **Special notice: ≥ 28 days** before the meeting (s.578(1)), given to members with the meeting notice (s.578(2)); fallback ≥ 14 days by newspaper advertisement if impracticable (s.578(3)).
- Special notice required for removing a director (s.462) or an auditor (s.419) before expiry of term.

💬 白話解釋：「21-14-28」三個數一定要背。縮短規則唔同：AGM 要全體同意，GM 要 95%，special notice 冇得縮（法定最低）。小心唔好將 special notice（通知程序）同 special resolution（75% 投票門檻）撈埋一齊。

### Card 7
**Front:** What are the quorum and voting rules for a members' general meeting, including proxies?

**Back:**
- Quorum: **2 members** present in person (s.585(3)); Model Articles: in person or by proxy (Art. 39(1)); one-member company: sole member suffices (s.585(1)); no quorum → meeting invalid (Re Alma Spinning Co (1880) 16 Ch D 681).
- Default voting = **show of hands**; poll demandable (chairperson / ≥ 2 members / ≥ 5% voting rights under Model Articles); articles void if blocking poll by 5 members, 5% holders or chairperson (s.591(2)); chairperson must demand poll if proxies indicate a different result (s.592).
- Proxy: need not be a member (s.596(1)); two-way forms must allow instructions (s.601); multiple proxies cannot vote on show of hands (s.588(2)); revocation effective only if received **before the meeting starts** (s.604(3)); member attending in person overrides proxy (s.605).
- Chairperson's declaration of result = conclusive evidence (s.590(1)).

💬 白話解釋：法定 quorum 係兩個人，一人公司例外。Proxy 最考起考生嘅兩點：(1) 撤銷 proxy 要喺會議開始前送達公司；(2) 本人親自出席投票就自動蓋過 proxy（s.605），就算撤銷通知遲咗都冇所謂。

### Card 8
**Front:** On what grounds may the court disqualify a person from being a director under Part IVA of the Companies (Winding Up and Miscellaneous Provisions) Ordinance (Cap. 32), and what are the consequences of contravention?

**Back:**
- Grounds (s.168): (1) conviction of indictable offences (s.168E — max 15/10/5 yrs by court level); (2) persistent breaches of filing provisions (s.168F — ≥ 3 defaults in 5 yrs = conclusive proof; max 5 yrs); (3) fraudulent trading/fraud in winding-up (ss.168G, 168L — max 15 yrs); (4) unfit conduct as director of an **insolvent** company (s.168H — **mandatory** order; 1–15 yrs; Sch. 15 factors); (5) public interest on Financial Secretary's application (ss.168I, 168J(1); s.879(6) CO — max 15 yrs).
- Brackets: > 10 yrs very serious; 6–10 yrs serious; 2–5 yrs minimum (Re Well Bond Group Ltd (2008) 5 HKLRD 147; Official Receiver v Chan Kin Hang Danvil (2012) 4 HKLRD J3).
- Contravention: offence — max **2 years' imprisonment + HK$150,000 fine** (s.168M); personal liability for company debts incurred while acting (s.168O(1)).

💬 白話解釋：五大 grounds 記「定罪、累犯、欺詐、不稱職、公眾利益」。唯獨 s.168H（insolvent 公司 + unfit）係 mandatory —— court 必須頒令，其他都係 discretionary。違令最辣：坐監兩年、罰十五萬、仲要個人孭返公司債。
