**Q1 (MCQ)**

Oscar is the sole shareholder and sole director of Oasis Trading Limited, a private company limited by shares that he incorporated in Hong Kong. All of his shares are fully paid. The company has just been wound up with substantial unpaid debts owed to unsecured creditors. One creditor demands that Oscar personally repay the company's debts, arguing that "the company is just Oscar in another name". Which statement is correct?

A. Oscar is personally liable for the company's unpaid debts because he is the sole shareholder and controller of the company.
B. Oscar is personally liable for the company's unpaid debts because, as sole director, he managed the business that incurred them.
C. Oscar is not personally liable for the company's debts, because the company is a separate legal person distinct from him.
D. Oscar is jointly and severally liable together with the company for the unpaid debts.

**Q2 (Short answer)**

Golden Years Limited, a private company limited by shares, wishes to apply to the Registrar of Companies for deregistration. State ALL of the conditions that must be satisfied before such an application can be made, and cite the relevant section of the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622).

**Q3 (MCQ — scenario)**

The articles of association of Marine Supplies Limited state its object as "to carry on the business of retail sale of marine equipment". Derek, a director, signs a contract on behalf of the company with Harbour Tours Limited under which Marine Supplies Limited will operate a yacht chartering service. The other members of Marine Supplies Limited discover the contract only after it has been signed. They want (i) to stop the company from performing the yacht chartering contract, and (ii) to have the contract declared void as beyond the company's objects. Which statement best states the legal position under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)?

A. The contract is void as ultra vires, following the doctrine in Ashbury Railway Carriage and Iron Co Ltd v Riche.
B. The members may obtain a court order restraining the company from performing the contract, because performance would breach s.116(1).
C. The contract is not invalid merely because it breaches the objects clause, and proceedings to restrain the company cannot be brought because performance would fulfil a legal obligation arising from the breach; the members should instead pursue Derek for breach of his duties as a director.
D. The contract is valid, and the members may additionally obtain a court order restraining the company from performing it at any time before full performance.

**Q4 (MCQ — scenario)**

Fiona is the sole shareholder and sole director of Fable Design Limited, a private company limited by shares incorporated in Hong Kong. She is considering the following arrangements for the office of company secretary:

(1) Appoint herself as company secretary in addition to being sole director;
(2) Appoint Fiona Corporate Services Limited, a Hong Kong body corporate wholly owned by her and of which she is the sole director;
(3) Appoint her friend Gary, a natural person who ordinarily resides in Hong Kong;
(4) Appoint Global Admin Inc., a body corporate whose registered office is in New York and which has no place of business in Hong Kong.

Which arrangement(s) is/are permissible under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)?

A. (1) only
B. (2) only
C. (3) only
D. (1) and (4) only

**Q5 (MCQ — scenario)**

Ms Ho holds 70% of the shares in Alpha Limited. Alpha Limited holds 60% of the shares in Beta Limited. Beta Limited holds 30% of the shares in Coral Limited. None of Alpha Limited, Beta Limited or Coral Limited is listed, and no other person holds more than 25% of the shares or voting rights in Coral Limited. Coral Limited is an applicable company required to keep a significant controllers register under Part 12, Division 2A of the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622). Who must be entered in Coral Limited's significant controllers register?

A. Beta Limited only.
B. Ms Ho only.
C. Alpha Limited and Beta Limited.
D. Ms Ho and Beta Limited.
