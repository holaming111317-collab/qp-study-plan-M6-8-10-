# M10 Chapter 6: Money Laundering, Bribery and Corruption — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** B is correct. Section 25(1) of the Organized and Serious Crimes Ordinance (Cap. 455) ('OSCO') makes it an offence to deal with property, knowing or having reasonable grounds to believe that the property, in whole or in part, directly or indirectly, represents any person's proceeds of an **indictable offence**. On conviction upon indictment, the maximum penalty under s.25(3) OSCO is 14 years' imprisonment and a fine of HK$5 million.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 題眼係「indictable offence」——呢個係 OSCO 嘅關鍵字。
- A 錯：s.25(1) DTROPO 條文措辭幾乎一樣，但只涵蓋 **proceeds of drug trafficking**，唔係一般 indictable offences。
- C 錯：s.5 AMLO 處理嘅係**金融機構明知或意圖欺詐監管機構而違反指定條文**嘅刑事責任，唔係處理黑錢罪。
- D 錯：s.7 UNATMO 係**提供或收集財產用於恐怖活動**，屬反恐範疇。
考試伏位：DTROPO 同 OSCO 嘅 s.25/s.25A 係「孿生條文」，見到毒品 → Cap. 405；見到 fraud/theft/tax evasion 等其他可公訴罪行 → Cap. 455。

**Rule Check:** Organized and Serious Crimes Ordinance (Cap. 455) s.25(1), s.25(3); cf. Drug Trafficking (Recovery of Proceeds) Ordinance (Cap. 405) s.25(1)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 見「s.25(1)」就亂揀——兩條條例嘅 section number 相同，分別只在於上游罪行類型（drug trafficking vs indictable offence）。答題時一定要連埋 Cap. number 寫。

### Q2 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** B is correct. Records must be retained for **at least five years** after the transaction or the cessation of the business relationship. The records must be sufficient to identify and verify the beneficial owner, keep a clear and complete audit trail, and be available to relevant authorities (e.g. the HKMA, the SFC or the Insurance Authority) on a timely basis.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題係純記憶題，但係 AML 合規嘅核心數字。
- A（3 年）、C（7 年）、D（10 年）都係 plausible distractors——3 年係 tipping-off/failure-to-disclose 嘅最高監禁年期（s.25A(8)），容易記混；7 年係 POBO 一般罪行最高監禁（s.12）；10 年係 POBO ss.5/6 最高監禁。呢啲數字成日喺同一章出現，考官最鍾意用嚟做干擾項。

**Rule Check:** Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (Cap. 615) Schedule 2, Part 3; HKICPA Guidelines on AML/CTF for Professional Accountants (2018), Section 660

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 唔好將「5 年保存期」同其他章節嘅時限（例如 TCSP 牌照有效期 3 年——s.53O AMLO）撈亂。

### Q3 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** B is correct. Under **Oei Hengky Wiryo v HKSAR (No. 2) (2007) 10 HKCFAR 98**, the prosecution does not have to prove that the property dealt with was in fact the proceeds of a predicate offence; it is sufficient to establish that the defendant had **reasonable grounds to believe** the property represented the proceeds of an indictable offence. Applying the **Seng Yuet Fong v HKSAR (1999) KCQ3** formulation (endorsed in **HKSAR v Yeung Ka Sing Carson (FACC Nos. 5 and 6 of 2015)**), a reasonable person looking objectively at Derek's grounds — an unexplained HK$8 million round-sum in-and-out transfer via a friend's account with a HK$50,000 "handling fee" — **would believe** (the test in **HKSAR v Pang Hung Fai (FACC 8 of 2013)**) the money represented criminal proceeds, and Derek knew those grounds. Derek can therefore be convicted under s.25(1) OSCO.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題考案例原則應用，唔係背書。
- A 錯：正好係 Oei Hengky Wiryo 推翻咗嘅講法——控方**唔使**證明啲錢真係犯罪得益，都唔使指出邊單上游罪行（HKSAR v Yang Sigai (2016) 進一步確認連海外行為都唔使識別具體罪行）。
- C 錯：「只係幫朋友過一筆數」唔係抗辯；mens rea 可以靠「reasonable grounds to believe」成立，唔使實際知悉。Pang Hung Fai 雖然顯示被告個人信任可作考慮，但 Derek 自己都「 suspected illegal gambling 」，主客觀元素齊。
- D 錯：DTROPO 只限毒品得益；唔涉及毒品唔等於無罪——OSCO 正正涵蓋非法賭博呢類 indictable offence 嘅得益。
考試伏位：呢類「轉帳過戶」情境（friend's account + round sum + handling fee）係 LP Apply and Analyse 1（Caleb 案例）嘅標準 pattern——見到即諗 s.25(1) OSCO + Oei Hengky Wiryo。

**Rule Check:** Organized and Serious Crimes Ordinance (Cap. 455) s.25(1); Oei Hengky Wiryo v HKSAR (No. 2) (2007) 10 HKCFAR 98; Seng Yuet Fong v HKSAR (1999) KCQ3, FAMC No. 26 of 1998; HKSAR v Pang Hung Fai (FACC 8 of 2013); HKSAR v Yeung Ka Sing Carson (FACC Nos. 5 and 6 of 2015)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 考生最常犯嘅錯係以為「證唔到上游罪行 = 甩身」。記住：s.25(1) OSCO 嘅 mens rea 係 belief-based，唔係 fact-based。

### Q4 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** C is correct. Marco is an **agent** of Homer Living Ltd (s.2 POBO — any person employed by or acting for another). The HK$200,000 interest-free loan is an **advantage** (s.2 expressly includes "a gift, **loan**, fee, reward or commission"; the fact that it was paid into his wife's account does not change this — Marco is the ultimate beneficiary). It was accepted as an inducement connected with awarding the supply contract, i.e. an act in relation to his **principal's affairs**, and was **without lawful authority or reasonable excuse** — Board approval was required and never obtained (s.9(5) would require permission applied for and given as soon as reasonably possible). All four elements of **s.9(1) POBO** are satisfied. Forest Way Ltd's responsible officers would correspondingly be liable under **s.9(2)** as offerors.

**解題思路（繁中）:** IRAC：Issue（私營機構僱員收受利益）→ Rule（s.2 advantage/agent；s.9(1) 四元素）→ Application（loan = advantage；存入老婆戶口唔改變受益人性質；無 Board 批准；判 contract 係 principal's affairs）→ Conclusion（s.9(1) 罪成）。
- A 錯：**loan 明確列明喺 s.2 advantage 定義入面**——呢個係最常見嘅誤解，以為「借錢唔係送錢」就冇事。
- B 錯：s.9(3) 係用**虛假或錯誤嘅 receipt/account/document 呃僱主**（如假單據、假申請）——呢度冇假文件。
- D 錯：s.4 只適用於 **public servants**（公職人員）；Marco 係私營公司僱員，屬 s.9 嘅私營條文。
考試伏位：Jun 2026 panel report 有考生將 anti-corruption 誤判為 money laundering——呢度 Marco 嘅行為係 bribery（POBO），唔係 laundering（OSCO），因為焦點係收受利益而非處理犯罪得益。

**Rule Check:** Prevention of Bribery Ordinance (Cap. 201) ss.2, 9(1), 9(2), 9(5); cf. HKSAR v Leung Yat Ming & Another (1999) 2 HKLRD 402 (s.9(3) contrast)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** (1) 以為 loan 唔算 advantage；(2) 以為錢入親屬戶口就切斷咗同 agent 嘅關係；(3) 見「private company」仲揀 s.4——記住 ss.3–5 係公營、s.9 係私營。

### Q5 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** B is correct. Under **s.12(1) of the UNATMO (Cap. 575)**, a person who knows or suspects that any property is terrorist property must disclose that knowledge or suspicion to an authorised officer as soon as practicable; failure to do so is an offence punishable under **s.14(5)** with a maximum of **3 months' imprisonment and a fine**. Peggy suspected the remitted funds were terrorist property and made no report — the offence is complete. **Tipping-off under s.12(5) UNATMO does not arise**: that offence requires the person to know or suspect **that a disclosure has been made**, and then to disclose to another person a matter likely to prejudice an investigation. Since no STR or disclosure had been made by anyone, this precondition is absent, and her WhatsApp warning — however improper — cannot constitute tipping-off on these facts.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題係 hard item，關鍵係認清 tipping-off 嘅**前提條件**。
- A 錯：佢嘅確警告咗客戶，行為似 tipping-off，但 s.12(5) 嘅其中一個元素係「knowing or suspecting **that a disclosure has been made**」——題目明確話**冇任何人報過案**，所以 tipping-off 唔成立。呢個係成條題嘅伏位所在。
- C 錯：UNATMO 嘅舉報責任由 **suspicion** 觸發——「suspicion is more than speculation but less than proof or knowledge」，唔使確定先要報。以「可能係人道用途」為由唔報，正正係 s.12(1) 要懲罰嘅不作為。
- D 錯：s.7 懲罰嘅係**提供或收集財產**意圖/明知/有合理理由相信會用於恐怖活動嘅人——Peggy **拒絕咗**處理最新匯款，佢自己冇提供或收集任何財產；用嘅係客戶嘅錢，唔係佢提供。
Conclusion：Peggy 唯一成立嘅罪名係 s.12(1) 知情不報，最高罰則 s.14(5)——3 個月監禁 + 罰款（比 OSCO/DTROPO s.25A(8) 嘅 3 年 + HK$500,000 輕，記住呢個差異）。

**Rule Check:** United Nations (Anti-Terrorism Measures) Ordinance (Cap. 575) ss.12(1), 12(5), 14(5), 14(6); cf. s.7 UNATMO; Organized and Serious Crimes Ordinance (Cap. 455) s.25A(8)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 見到「警告客戶」三個字就即揀 tipping-off——永遠先問：**已經有人作出 disclosure 未？**冇 disclosure 就冇 tipping-off，只剩 failure to disclose。另外記住 UNATMO 知情不報嘅罰則（3 個月）遠輕於 DTROPO/OSCO（3 年 + HK$500,000），數字題常考。
