**Question 1 (MCQ)**

During the planning of an audit, the engagement partner reminds the team that the auditor must reduce audit risk to an acceptably low level. Which of the following risks is the ONLY one directly controllable by the auditor through the design and performance of audit procedures?

A. Inherent risk, because the auditor can require management to simplify complex transactions.
B. Control risk, because the auditor can direct management to strengthen internal controls.
C. Detection risk, because it is the risk that the auditor's procedures will not detect a misstatement that exists and could be material.
D. Business risk, because the auditor can advise management on strategy to protect the entity's objectives.

**Question 2 (Scenario MCQ)**

Harbour Link Logistics Limited operates a freight forwarding business in Hong Kong. During the year, the company introduced a new commission scheme under which the sales team's annual bonus depends on achieving a revenue target. The company has also applied for a bank loan, and the bank requires audited financial statements showing revenue growth before approving the facility. As the audit senior planning this engagement, which of the following is the MOST appropriate response to the principal risk arising from these facts?

A. Perform detailed cut-off procedures over revenue to ensure revenue is recognised in the correct accounting period, given the risk that revenue is deliberately overstated.
B. Reduce substantive procedures over revenue because the commission scheme motivates staff to record sales accurately.
C. Place reliance on the internal controls over the sales cycle without performing tests of control, to save audit time.
D. Issue a qualified opinion immediately because a fraud has been committed by the sales team.

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

The financial controller of an audit client personally reviews the monthly bank reconciliations each month, investigates any unusual reconciling items and checks that reconciliations are being prepared on time by the accounts clerk. Into which of the five components of the entity's system of internal control (HKSA 315 (Revised 2019)) does the financial controller's review of whether the reconciliations continue to be prepared and operate over time fall?

A. Control environment.
B. The entity's risk assessment process.
C. The entity's process to monitor the system of internal control.
D. Control activities.

**Question 4 (Scenario MCQ)**

During the audit of Bright Pearl Jewellery Limited, the audit team discovers that a senior finance manager has been diverting company funds into a personal bank account disguised as payments to a fictitious supplier. Which of the following statements correctly describes the responsibilities in this situation?

A. The auditor is primarily responsible for the prevention and detection of the fraud, because the auditor provides reasonable assurance on the financial statements.
B. The primary responsibility for the prevention and detection of fraud rests with management and those charged with governance; as the fraud involves management, the auditor should communicate the matter to those charged with governance.
C. The auditor must report the fraud to the Hong Kong Police Force in all circumstances before informing the client, because confidentiality never applies to fraud.
D. The auditor should conceal the matter in the auditor's report by issuing an unmodified opinion, as disclosing the fraud would breach client confidentiality.

**Question 5 (Short answer)**

An auditor wishes to test the **completeness** assertion for the client's revenue (i.e. whether all goods despatched have been invoiced and recorded). Using directional testing principles, state (i) the population from which the auditor should select the sample, and (ii) the direction of the test. Explain in one or two sentences why selecting invoices from the sales day book and vouching them to goods despatched notes would NOT satisfy the completeness assertion.
