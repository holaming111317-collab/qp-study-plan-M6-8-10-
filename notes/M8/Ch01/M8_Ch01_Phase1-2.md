# M8 Chapter 1: Introduction to Auditing — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. Explain the elements of an assurance engagement under the Hong Kong Framework for Assurance Engagements (Amended) and apply them to a financial statement audit.
2. Distinguish between reasonable and limited assurance engagements, and between attest and direct reporting engagements.
3. Describe the legal and regulatory framework governing statutory audits in Hong Kong under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) and the co-regulation model.
4. Apply the HKICPA Code of Ethics for Professional Accountants (COE) conceptual framework to identify, evaluate and address threats to the fundamental principles and independence.
5. Differentiate the roles, responsibilities and reporting lines of external auditors and internal auditors.

### 必背考點

#### A. Assurance Concepts（Assurance Framework & Engagement Types）
- **Definition of an assurance engagement**（Hong Kong Framework for Assurance Engagements (Amended), paras 10–11）：practitioner obtains sufficient appropriate evidence to express a conclusion designed to enhance the degree of confidence of intended users (other than the responsible party) about the outcome of measurement/evaluation of the underlying subject matter against criteria。注意：Framework 本身 **不 mandate requirements**，佢只係發展準則嘅框架。
- **Five elements of an assurance engagement**（Framework paras 22, 26）：
  1. Three-party relationship — practitioner / responsible party / intended users；
  2. Appropriate underlying subject matter；
  3. Suitable criteria（e.g. HKFRS under the CO）；
  4. Sufficient appropriate evidence（需 professional scepticism + professional judgement + materiality）；
  5. Written assurance report。
- **Attest vs Direct engagement**（Framework paras 12, 13）：attest = responsible party 做出 written assertion，auditor 評論該 assertion（FS audit 多數係 attest）；direct = auditor 自己 measure/evaluate subject matter 並直接報告，responsible party **不作書面 assertion**（e.g. reporting on compliance with regulations）。
- **Levels of assurance**（Framework paras 15, 16）：
  - **Reasonable assurance** = high but **not absolute**（HKSA 200.5; HKICPA Glossary）；audit → **positive expression of opinion**（e.g. "true and fair in accordance with the financial reporting framework" under the CO）。
  - **Limited assurance** = review engagement；fewer procedures（mainly **enquiry and analytical procedures**），less evidence → **negative expression**（"nothing has come to the auditor's attention…"）。
- **Non-assurance engagements**：agreed-upon procedures engagements 受 **HKSRS 4400 (Revised) Agreed-Upon Procedures Engagements** 規管 — 只報告 **factual findings**，**no assurance** 提供。Compilation engagements 受 HKSRS 4410 (Revised) 規管。
- **Purpose of an audit**（HKSA 200 para. 3）：enhance the degree of confidence of intended users by expressing an opinion on whether the FS are prepared, in all material respects, in accordance with the applicable financial reporting framework (HKFRS)。
- **Inherent limitations of an audit**：audit ≠ absolute assurance — sampling、professional judgement、internal control override、human error、fraud concealment 均令部分 misstatement 可能唔被發現（就算 audit 做得啱）。

#### B. Evidence & Basic Principles of a Financial Statement Audit（HKSA 200）
- **ASOBAT (AAA, 1972) definition of auditing**："A systematic process of objectively obtaining and evaluating evidence regarding assertions about economic actions and events to ascertain the degree of correspondence between those assertions and established criteria and communicating the results to interested users." 六大元素：systematic process / objectivity / evidence / assertions / established criteria / communication。
- **HKSA 200 basic principles**（HKSA 200.14–17）：(1) compliance with ethical requirements (incl. independence — para. 14)；(2) professional scepticism；(3) professional judgement；(4) sufficient appropriate audit evidence & audit risk reduced to acceptably low level；(5) conduct audit in accordance with all relevant HKSAs。
- **Reasonable assurance objective**（HKSA 200.4, 5）：obtain reasonable assurance whether the FS as a whole are free from material misstatement, whether due to **fraud or error**。
- **Materiality**（HKSA 200.6, 7）：misstatements are material if, individually or in aggregate, they could reasonably be expected to influence the economic decisions of users — auditor 用 materiality 去 plan、perform audit 及評估 misstatements 影響。
- **Sufficient appropriate evidence**：sufficiency = quantity；appropriateness = quality（relevance + reliability）。

#### C. Ethics（HKICPA Code of Ethics for Professional Accountants, COE）
- **法源**：COE 由 HKICPA Council 根據 **Professional Accountants Ordinance s.18A** 發出；Chapter A 採納 IESBA International Code。COE 結構：Part 1（conceptual framework）、Part 2（accountants in business）、Part 3（public practice）、Part 4A（independence — audit & review）、Part 4B（other assurance）。
- **Five fundamental principles**（COE 110.1 A1, R110.2）：
  1. **Integrity** — straightforward；不得明知而與 misleading 嘅信息有關聯；
  2. **Objectivity** — 不受 bias、conflict of interest、undue influence 影響；
  3. **Professional competence and due care** — 維持知識技能、act diligently；
  4. **Confidentiality** — 未經授權不得披露，除非有 legal/professional right or duty；不得用作個人利益；
  5. **Professional behaviour** — 遵守法律、public interest、避免 discredit the profession。
- **Conceptual framework approach**（COE 120.1, 2; R120.5）：**Identify → Evaluate → Address** threats；用 safeguards 將 threat 消除或降至 acceptable level；無法處理就要 decline / resign。判斷標準 = **reasonable and informed third party test**（COE 120.5 A6）；需 inquiring mind + professional judgement（COE 120.5 A1）。
- **Five threats**：**Self-interest**（financial interest in client、fee dependence）、**Self-review**（audit 自己 firm 做嘅工作，e.g. 代客設計會計系統後再做 audit）、**Advocacy**（promoting client's position，e.g. 推銷客戶股份）、**Familiarity**（long/close relationship）、**Intimidation**（actual or perceived pressure，e.g. 威脅不續聘）。
- **Independence 兩層面**：**independence of mind**（事實上：integrity、objectivity、professional scepticism 不受損）+ **independence in appearance**（睇落去：reasonable and informed third party 唔會認為受損）。Independence required during **the engagement period AND the period covered by the financial statements**。
- **Key independence rules（Part 4A）**：
  - No direct or material indirect financial interest in an audit client（firm / team member / immediate family）；
  - Gifts & hospitality：只接受 **trivial or inconsequential**；
  - **Contingent fee for an audit engagement 被禁止**；
  - Overdue fees 長期未付 = 形同 loan to client → 要考慮應否繼續；
  - No partner/firm employee may serve as **director or officer** of an audit client（threat cannot be reduced to acceptable level）；
  - Designing/implementing IT systems **integral to** accounting/financial reporting systems 被禁止（safeguards 都救唔返）；off-the-shelf systems 則可接受；
  - Key audit partner of a **public interest entity**：max **7 years** on engagement，然後 **5 years** cooling-off（COE Section 540）；
  - Engagement quality reviewer 冷卻期：engagement partner 轉任 EQR 前建議 **2 years cooling-off**（COE 325.8 A3），同 Section 540 rotation 係兩回事；
  - PIE fee dependence（COE 15 Dec 2022 前版本）：同一客戶連續兩年 total fees > **15%** of firm's total fees → disclose to TCWG + pre- or post-issuance review。
- **HKSQM 1**：firm 須 design, implement and operate a **system of quality management**（risk-based approach），replaced HKSQC 1；**HKSQM 2** 處理 Engagement Quality Review（EQR 資格、執行、文件）。

#### D. Regulatory Framework（Statutory Audit — Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)）
- **Co-regulation model**：政府立法（CO）+ 自我監管（HKICPA）。主要法定監管機構：**SFC** 及 **HKEX**；**HKICPA 係香港唯一法定會計師發牌機構**（professional training, development and regulation）。
- **PAO (Professional Accountants Ordinance) s.18A**：HKICPA Council 發出 standards of practice 及 statements of ethics 嘅權力來源。**AASC**（Auditing and Assurance Standards Committee）負責制訂 HKSAs 等，並採 **convergence policy** 與 IAASB/IFAC 國際準則趨同（HK standards 可加本地要求或喺特殊情況下偏離，但 professional standards 唔會 override 本地法律）。
- **CO 關鍵條文**：
  - **ss.373–378**：公司須保存 accounting records（hard copy or electronic），**保存 7 年**，可供董事隨時查閱；
  - **ss.379–387**：directors 須編製 give a **true and fair view** 嘅財務報表；**s.380(4)(b)** 規定採用 HKICPA 發出嘅 accounting standards (HKFRS)；**s.383** 規定 notes 披露董事 emoluments、retirement benefits、termination payments、loans；directors 須 approve and sign；
  - **ss.388–391**：directors' report；
  - **ss.429–436**：AGM 前向股東發送 FS 及 reports；
  - **s.393**：只有 **practice unit**（CPA firm / practising individual CPA / corporate practice）有資格獲委任；公司 officer/employee 及其 partner 無資格（法定 independence 要求）；
  - **ss.395–400**：auditor 每個財政年度由 **shareholders 於 AGM 以 resolution 委任**；casual vacancy 可由 directors 委任；委任 firm = 委任其 partners；**s.404**：remuneration 由股東 resolution 釐定（若由 directors 委任則可由 directors 釐定）；
  - **HKSA 210 Agreeing the Terms of Audit Engagements**：engagement letter 建立 contractual relationship，配合 statutory appointment；
  - **ss.405–406**：auditor 於 AGM 向股東報告，意見包括 FS 是否 properly prepared in accordance with the CO + true and fair view；directors' report 若與 FS 不一致須在報告中表明；
  - **s.407**：auditor 須另就 (i) adequate accounting records 及 (ii) FS agree with accounting records 形成意見；未能取得所有 necessary information and explanations 須在報告中聲明；knowingly or recklessly 遺漏上述報告事項屬 **offence**；
  - **s.410**：qualified privilege（無惡意下免於 defamation）；**s.411**：有權出席 general meeting 並就 audit matters 發言。
- **Standards suite（HKICPA pronouncements）**：HKSQM 1–99（quality management）、Hong Kong Framework for Assurance Engagements (Amended)、HKSAs 100–999（mandatory for FS audits）、HKSREs 2000–2699（reviews）、HKSAEs 3000–3699（e.g. HKSAE 3000 (Revised)、HKSAE 3410 greenhouse gas）、HKSRSs 4000–4699（related services）、HKSIRs 100–999（investment circulars）；PNs/AGs 非強制但唔跟要解釋；HKAPGs 屬 non-authoritative（e.g. HKAPG 1000）。HKSA 結構：Introduction / Objectives / Definitions / Requirements / Conformity & Compliance with ISAs / Application material。
- **Group audits**（HKSA 600 (Revised) para. 11）：group engagement partner 對 group audit opinion 負全責，auditor's report 不得提及 component auditor（除非法律要求）。

#### E. Types of Audits（External vs Internal）
- **External auditor**：independent of the entity，由 shareholders 委任（CO 下），向 shareholders 報告對 FS 嘅意見。
- **Internal audit**（HKSA 610 (Revised 2013) para. 14）："A function of an entity that performs assurance and consulting activities designed to evaluate and improve the effectiveness of the entity's governance, risk management and internal control processes." 屬於 entity 內部控制系統一部分，向 management 報告；公司 **無法定義務** 設立 internal audit function。
- **External auditor using internal audit work**（HKSA 610 (Revised 2013) paras 8–9, 15, A1）：可以 (1) use the work of internal auditors 以 modify nature, timing, extent of procedures；或 (2) direct assistance（在 external auditor direction, supervision, review 下執行程序）。但 **external auditor 必須自行評估 internal auditor 工作是否 adequate**，且 **不能 delegate audit responsibility**；external audit scope 不會因設立 internal audit 而改變。
- **IIA definition of internal auditing**：independent, objective assurance and consulting activity designed to **add value** and improve operations — systematic, disciplined approach to evaluate and improve risk management, control, and governance processes。Internal auditor 嘅 independence 係 **organisational independence**（charter 賦予 status、Audit Committee oversight、不審核自己負責嘅活動）。
- **其他 audit types**：**Compliance audits**（遵守 policies / laws / contracts）；**Performance audits**（value for money — **Economy**（以最低成本取得適當質量數量資源）/ **Efficiency**（最小投入最大產出）/ **Effectiveness**（達成目標），3 Es 要分清）；**Comprehensive audits**（FS + compliance + performance 混合，公共部門常見）；**Corporate social responsibility (CSR) audits**（ESG 風險，suitable criteria 可能難定）。
- **Audit Committee**：Board 轄下 sub-committee（多為 independent directors），oversee financial reporting & auditing functions，擔任 Board 與 auditor 之間嘅 intermediary；可就 auditor appointment 及 audit fee 提出 **recommendation**（唔係委任！），review broad audit strategy and results（唔係 detailed procedures）。HKICPA 2002 年發出 *A Guide for Effective Audit Committees*。

### 易混淆概念對比

#### 對比 1：Reasonable Assurance (Audit) vs Limited Assurance (Review)

| 項目 | Reasonable Assurance — Audit | Limited Assurance — Review |
|---|---|---|
| Risk of inappropriate conclusion | Reduced to an **acceptably low** level | Higher than reasonable assurance（可以接受） |
| Procedures | Full range of audit procedures（extensive evidence） | Mainly **enquiry and analytical procedures**（less evidence） |
| Form of conclusion | **Positive** expression（"the FS give a true and fair view…"） | **Negative** expression（"nothing has come to our attention…"） |
| Assurance level | High, but **not absolute** | Moderate / lower than high |
| 滿足 CO 法定要求？ | Yes — statutory audit 必須係 reasonable assurance | **No** — review 唔能代替 CO 下嘅 audit |

💬 點樣分辨：考試最鍾意問「換個平啲嘅 review 得唔得」— 唔得，CO 要求 audit。伏位：review 唔係「無 assurance」，係 limited assurance；兩者都會出 report/opinion，分別在於 **extent of procedures** 同 **form of expression**。

#### 對比 2：External Auditor vs Internal Auditor

| 項目 | External Auditor | Internal Auditor |
|---|---|---|
| 委任／存在依據 | Statutory — shareholders 於 AGM 委任（CO ss.395–400） | Voluntary — 由 management 決定是否設立（charter 規管） |
| 向誰報告 | **Shareholders**（及其他 intended users） | **Management** / Audit Committee |
| 地位 | Independent of the entity（第三方） | Integral part of the entity（內部控制系統一部分） |
| 目標 | 對 FS 是否 true and fair 發表 opinion | Evaluate and improve governance, risk management and internal control（HKSA 610 (Revised 2013) para. 14） |
| Independence 來源 | 法定 + COE Part 4A | **Organisational** independence（charter、AC oversight） |
| 責任可否轉移 | 不可 delegate；用 internal audit work 都要自行評估（HKSA 610.15） | 唔承擔 FS preparation 責任（嗰個係 management） |

💬 點樣分辨：記住「誰請我、我向誰報告」— external 向 shareholders，internal 向 management。考試伏位：設立 internal audit function **唔會改變 external audit 嘅 scope**，external auditor **唔可以 delegate 責任**畀 internal audit（Jun 2023 Q2 考過呢個區域，但呢度係概念區分）。

## Phase 2 閃卡（8 cards）

### Card 1
**Front:** What are the FIVE elements of an assurance engagement under the Hong Kong Framework for Assurance Engagements (Amended)?

**Back:**
- Three-party relationship (practitioner / responsible party / intended users)
- An appropriate underlying subject matter
- Suitable criteria
- Sufficient appropriate evidence
- A written assurance report

💬 白話：記口訣「三方、主題、準則、證據、報告」。Framework paras 22, 26。考試常見伏位：將 engagement letter 或 management representation 誤當第六個元素。

### Card 2
**Front:** Distinguish an attest engagement from a direct reporting engagement (Framework paras 12, 13).

**Back:**
- Attest: responsible party measures/evaluates the subject matter and makes a **written assertion**; auditor reports on that assertion (e.g. FS audit)
- Direct: auditor measures/evaluates the subject matter directly; **no written assertion** by the responsible party (e.g. report on compliance with regulations)
- Most FS audits are attest engagements

💬 白話：關鍵係「有冇 written assertion」— 有就 attest，冇就 direct。伏位：direct engagement 唔代表 management 無責任，佢哋仍然 retain responsibility for the subject matter。

### Card 3
**Front:** State the level of assurance and form of conclusion for (a) an audit and (b) a review engagement.

**Back:**
- Audit: **reasonable assurance** (high but not absolute); **positive** expression of opinion
- Review: **limited assurance**; **negative** expression ("nothing has come to the auditor's attention that causes the auditor to believe the subject matter is materially misstated")

💬 白話：audit = 正面肯定式 + 高保證；review = 反面「冇發現問題」式 + 有限保證。記住 reasonable ≠ absolute — audit 有 inherent limitations（sampling、fraud concealment 等）。

### Card 4
**Front:** What is the ASOBAT (AAA, 1972) definition of auditing and its key elements?

**Back:**
"A systematic process of objectively obtaining and evaluating evidence regarding assertions about economic actions and events to ascertain the degree of correspondence between those assertions and established criteria and communicating the results to interested users."
- Systematic process / Objectivity / Evidence / Assertions / Established criteria / Communication

💬 白話：呢個係 auditing 嘅經典定義，HKSA 200 嘅概念（evidence、assertions、criteria、report）都係由此延伸。背熟原句，exam 定義題直接用。

### Card 5
**Front:** Under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), who appoints the auditor, and what must the auditor's report opine on (ss.395–407)?

**Back:**
- Shareholders appoint by resolution at each AGM (ss.395–400); directors may fill a casual vacancy; only a **practice unit** is eligible (s.393)
- Report opinion: FS properly prepared per the CO + **true and fair view** (ss.405–406); adequate accounting records kept + FS agree with records (s.407); inconsistency of directors' report must be stated

💬 白話：委任權喺股東手（唔係董事、唔係 Audit Committee — AC 只可以 recommend）。s.407 嘅兩項額外意見係常見遺漏位。

### Card 6
**Front:** List the FIVE fundamental principles of the HKICPA Code of Ethics and the FIVE categories of threats.

**Back:**
- Principles: **Integrity; Objectivity; Professional competence and due care; Confidentiality; Professional behaviour**
- Threats: **Self-interest; Self-review; Advocacy; Familiarity; Intimidation**
- Conceptual framework: Identify → Evaluate → Address (safeguards / eliminate / decline)

💬 白話：兩組「五虎將」必背。Scenario 題搵 threat 時問自己：係錢（self-interest）？審自己嘢（self-review）？幫客撐腰（advocacy）？太熟（familiarity）？被施壓（intimidation）？

### Card 7
**Front:** Explain the two components of independence and when independence is required (COE Part 4A).

**Back:**
- **Independence of mind**: conclusion expressed without factors compromising professional judgement — acting with integrity, objectivity, professional scepticism
- **Independence in appearance**: avoiding circumstances where a reasonable and informed third party would conclude integrity/objectivity/professional scepticism is compromised
- Required during **both the engagement period and the period covered by the financial statements**

💬 白話：「事實上獨立」+「睇落獨立」缺一不可。時間上兩段都要 cover — 唔係淨係做 audit 嗰陣。

### Card 8
**Front:** May an external auditor use the work of internal auditors? State the conditions under HKSA 610 (Revised 2013).

**Back:**
- Yes — two ways: (1) **using the work** of the internal audit function to modify the nature, timing and extent of external audit procedures; (2) **direct assistance** by internal auditors under the external auditor's direction, supervision and review (paras 8–9)
- External auditor must evaluate whether the internal auditors' work is adequate for audit purposes (para. 15)
- External auditor **cannot delegate responsibility**; the scope of the external audit is not altered by the existence of internal audit

💬 白話：可以用，但要自己 review 過先可以用；責任永遠喺 external auditor 度。考試伏位：「設咗 IA 可以縮細 audit scope」— 錯！
