# M8 Chapter 1: Introduction to Auditing — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** A review engagement provides **limited assurance**. The practitioner performs fewer procedures — primarily **enquiry and analytical procedures** — and therefore obtains less evidence than in an audit. The conclusion is expressed in a **negative form**: whether anything has come to the practitioner's attention that causes the practitioner to believe the subject matter information is materially misstated.

**解題思路（繁中）:** C 正確：review = limited assurance + negative form，程序以 enquiry 同 analytical procedures 為主。A 錯：positive form + full procedures 係 audit（reasonable assurance）嘅特徵。B 錯：「no assurance + factual findings」係 **agreed-upon procedures engagement**（HKSRS 4400 (Revised)），唔係 review — review 仍然係 assurance engagement，只係 level 低啲。D 錯：review 嘅 conclusion 唔會用 positive form — 呢個係「有限保證 vs 正面表達」嘅混搭陷阱，考試最愛將兩個維度交叉錯配。

**Rule Check:** Hong Kong Framework for Assurance Engagements (Amended) paras 15–16; HKSA 200 para. 5 (reasonable assurance = high but not absolute)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 學生經常將「review 都會出 opinion」誤解為「review = positive opinion」。記住：audit 同 review **都係** assurance engagement、**都會**出報告；分別在於 assurance level 同 form of expression。另外唔好將 review 同 AUP 撈亂 — AUP 先係零保證。

### Q2 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** This is an **agreed-upon procedures engagement** under **HKSRS 4400 (Revised) Agreed-Upon Procedures Engagements**. The procedures are agreed in advance between the engaging parties; the practitioner has no discretion to extend them to the extent needed to support a conclusion, and reports only **factual findings**. No assurance is expressed.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 題眼係三個事實：(1) 程序由客戶同銀行**預先議定**；(2) 只報告 **factual results**；(3) **唔會發表 opinion or conclusion**。呢三點齊齊指向 AUP — 無 assurance。A 錯：audit 要 practitioner 自行決定 scope 並發表意見，呢度程序係人哋定晒。B 錯：review 至少提供 limited assurance（negative form），題目講明唔發表任何結論。D 錯：HKSAE 3000 (Revised) 係 other assurance engagements，仍然要出 conclusion；而且 assurance level 唔係「at the practitioner's discretion」咁隨意。

**Rule Check:** HKSRS 4400 (Revised) Agreed-Upon Procedures Engagements; Hong Kong Framework for Assurance Engagements (Amended) paras 10–11（assurance engagement 定義 — AUP 唔符合，因為冇 conclusion 去 enhance confidence）

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 「由 CPA 做 + 驗證數字」唔自動等於 audit。判斷流程：有冇 conclusion/opinion？冇 → AUP（no assurance）。有 → 睇 positive 定 negative form → reasonable 定 limited assurance。

### Q3 — Answer: D
**Model Answer (EN):** This is a **direct reporting engagement**. Under paragraph 13 of the Framework, in a direct engagement the responsible party retains responsibility for the subject matter but does **not** make a written assertion; the practitioner measures or evaluates the underlying subject matter against the criteria and reports the outcome directly in the assurance report.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 關鍵事實：**management 冇出 written assertion**，auditor 自己直接 measure/evaluate compliance 然後報告 — 正正係 Framework para. 13 嘅 direct engagement 定義。A 錯：attest engagement（para. 12）要求 responsible party 先作 written assertion，auditor 再評論嗰個 assertion；題目講明冇 assertion。B 錯：assurance engagement 嘅 subject matter 可以係 non-financial（LP 列出 compliance with legislation、greenhouse gas emissions 等七類）— 「非財務」唔等於「非保證」。C 錯：題目冇資料顯示 assurance level；「compliance 主題 = limited assurance」係無根據嘅聯想。

**Rule Check:** Hong Kong Framework for Assurance Engagements (Amended) paras 12–13

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 唔好俾「attest」個字面意思（作證）誤導 — 佢嘅技術定義係「對 responsible party 嘅 written assertion 發表意見」。一個實用判斷法：搵「written assertion 有冇出現」— 有 = attest，冇 = direct。FS audit 多數係 attest（management 簽 FS = assertion）。

### Q4 — Answer: A
**Model Answer (EN):** Accepting the appointment creates a **self-review threat**. Vertex & Co would be auditing accounting records and financial statements generated by a system that the firm itself designed and implemented — the auditor would be evaluating the output of its own firm's previous work. The LP notes that providing services to design or implement IT systems that are **integral to** the client's accounting, internal control and financial reporting systems is prohibited because the threat cannot be reduced to an acceptable level by safeguards.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 事實鏈：firm 設計+實施會計系統 → 系統產生賬目同 FS → 同一 firm 審核呢啲 FS = **審自己做嘅嘢** = self-review。B 錯：self-interest 係「財務或其他利益不當影響判斷」（e.g. 持股、fee dependence）— 呢度核心問題唔係錢。C 錯：advocacy 係「幫客戶站台推銷佢嘅立場」（e.g. 推銷客戶股份、訴訟中代表客戶）。D 錯：intimidation 係「被實際或感知壓力嚇到唔敢客觀」（e.g. 威脅撤換 auditor）— 題目冇施壓情節。

**Rule Check:** HKICPA Code of Ethics for Professional Accountants, Section 606 (information technology systems services); COE Section 120 conceptual framework (threat categories)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 揀 threat 類別時用「一句話測試」：錢 → self-interest；審自己嘢 → self-review；幫客撐腰 → advocacy；太熟 → familiarity；被施壓 → intimidation。仲要記住呢類 integral IT system 服務係 **prohibited** — safeguards 救唔返，唔係「做個 review 就得」。

### Q5 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** Statement B is correct. **Section 18A of the Professional Accountants Ordinance** gives the HKICPA Council the power to issue standards of practice and statements of ethics to be applied by its members; the Council established the Auditing and Assurance Standards Committee (AASC) to develop Hong Kong pronouncements, and mandated the Ethics Committee to develop the COE.

**解題思路（繁中）:** A 錯：香港唯一法定會計師發牌機構係 **HKICPA**；HKEX 同 SFC 係證券市場監管機構，唔發 CPA 牌。C 錯：LP 明確講 professional standards **do not override local laws and regulations** — 方向啱啱相反。D 錯：AASC 係採用 **convergence policy**，以 IAASB（IFAC 轄下）嘅國際準則為基礎發展 HKSAs，唔係「獨立發展」；不過香港準則可以加入本地額外要求或喺特殊情況下偏離國際準則。

**Rule Check:** Professional Accountants Ordinance s.18A; LP Ch.1 §1.2.1–1.2.2 (co-regulation model; HKICPA as the only statutory licensing body; convergence policy)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** Legal/regulatory framework 係 Jun 2025 考官報告點名嘅弱項。三個機構分工要背：**HKICPA** = 發牌 + 準則 + 紀律；**SFC / HKEX** = 證券及上市監管；**AASC** = HKICPA 轄下制訂準則嘅委員會。仲有「convergence」同「harmonisation」— 前者係香港層面嘅政策名，後者係國際層面嘅講法。
