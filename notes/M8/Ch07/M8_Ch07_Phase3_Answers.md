# M8 Chapter 7: Audit Procedures for Specific Balances and Transactions — Phase 3 Quiz Answers

### Q1 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** A trade receivables confirmation — even a positive confirmation returned directly to the auditor — verifies only the **existence** and **rights and obligations** assertions. It does **not** verify **valuation**: a customer agreeing that a balance is owed gives no assurance that the customer is able to pay. The auditor must not place total reliance on the confirmation and must perform further procedures to test valuation, such as **post-year-end cash receipts testing** (inspecting post-year-end cash receipts and tracing them to the pre-year-end balance) and assessing the reasonableness of the **expected credit loss allowance per HKFRS 9**, including discussing aged balances with the credit control manager.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題考 LP 嘅 Key Learning Point：confirmation 唔 test valuation。Distractor 分析：
- **A 錯**：高估咗 confirmation 嘅威力——就算正面確認直接寄返 auditor，都只覆蓋 existence 同 rights and obligations，valuation（收唔收得返）完全無掂到。呢個係考官最鍾意嘅「確認咗 = 搞掂晒」陷阱。
- **C 錯**：letterhead 同 related party 同「使唔使做埋 valuation 測試」無關；letterhead 只係 confirmation 程序嘅行政安排（client 出信紙、auditor 寄、直接回覆 auditor）。
- **D 錯**：兩重錯——confirmation 唔係 test completeness（receivables 主攻 overstatement，唔係 understatement），而且 negative confirmation 只喺 risk of material misstatement 低嘅情況先合用，material balance 唔會用 negative。
- **B 啱**：準確指出 valuation gap 同補救程序（post-year-end cash receipts + ECL allowance）。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 505 *External Confirmations*（HKSA 505.7 維持控制；HKSA 505.A11 可靠性疑慮）；HKFRS 9 *Financial Instruments*（expected credit loss model）；LP Ch 7.6.3–7.6.4 Key Learning Point。

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 記住口訣「認數 ≠ 找數」。任何答「confirmation 提供 sufficient appropriate evidence 覆蓋晒所有 assertions」嘅選項都係伏。Valuation 永遠要靠期後收款同 ECL 評估補位。

### Q2 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** The partner's concern is **understatement of trade payables**, i.e. the **completeness** assertion (the primary assertion for liabilities). Procedure B starts from an independent source document population (goods received notes dated immediately pre-year-end) and traces **forward** into the accounting records (payables balance and purchases). This direction of testing — from source to records — detects omitted liabilities and also verifies **cut-off of purchases**. The other procedures test different assertions or are not substantive: A starts from the recorded population and traces back to source documents, which verifies **existence** (overstatement), not completeness; C verifies mathematical accuracy/completeness of the listing only within the recorded population — it cannot detect liabilities never recorded; D is a **test of control**, not a substantive procedure addressing completeness.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題係典型嘅 direction trap（panelist 年年鬧嘅 mismatch 弱項）：
- 關鍵第一步：「payables understated」→ 目標 assertion 係 **completeness**。
- 第二步：completeness 要「由源頭追到入帳」（GRN → payables）；existence 先係「由帳面追到源頭」（listing → invoice/GRN）。
- **A 錯**：方向倒轉，test existence/overstatement。
- **C 錯**：cast listing 只證明「已記錄嘅嘢加數無錯」，搵唔到「根本無記錄」嘅負債。
- **D 錯**：審批授權係 test of control，唔係 substantive procedure，題目明確要求 substantive。
- **B 啱**：GRN pre-year-end → 追入 year-end payables = completeness + cut-off，正中紅心。

**Rule Check:** LP Ch 7.11.2（payables procedures：GRNs pre- and post-year-end → completeness of payables and cut-off of purchases）；HKSA 500 *Audit Evidence*（substantive procedures vs tests of control）；liabilities completeness focus per LP Ch 7.11.1。

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 見到「supplier / payable / understated」即諗 completeness + 由源頭追起。仲有，記住 supplier statement reconciliations 都係 completeness 嘅強力程序（external evidence）——如果選項出現佢同 B 二選一，要睇題目場景（本題話 invoices recorded late，GRN tracing 更直接回應 cut-off/omission 風險）。

### Q3 — Answer: (a) HK$150,000; (b) understated by HK$2,000; (c) investigate the difference with management / compare against materiality
**Model Answer (EN):**
(a) Expected depreciation charge (proof in total) = HK$1,200,000 ÷ 8 years = **HK$150,000** per annum (straight-line, no residual value, no additions or disposals during the year).

(b) Recorded charge = HK$148,000. Difference = HK$150,000 − HK$148,000 = **HK$2,000 understated** (depreciation expense understated; carrying amount of plant and machinery overstated by HK$2,000). The difference is 1.3% (HK$2,000 / HK$150,000) below expectation, which is clearly trivial and would not be material.

(c) Audit response (any ONE):
- Discuss the difference with management and obtain an explanation (e.g. minor timing of assets entering service).
- Compare the difference with performance materiality/overall materiality and, if clearly trivial, record it on the summary of uncorrected misstatements without further work.
- Recalculate the depreciation charge for a sample of individual assets and agree with the non-current assets register to identify the source of the difference.

**解題思路（繁中）:** Proof in total 係 LP 7.9.2 明確列出嘅 depreciation analytical procedure。步驟：(1) 用 policy 參數自己計一個 expectation（$1,200,000 / 8 = $150,000）；(2) 同 recorded figure 比較，$148,000 係低咗 → expense understated（記住 direction：depreciation 少計 = 資產帳面值多咗）；(3) 差異 $2,000 只佔 1.3%，屬 clearly trivial——照計入 uncorrected misstatements summary，唔使大費周章。考試伏位：有同學會答「 overstated」因為見到 recorded 數細過預期就淆亂——記住 expense 低咗，資產先至係 overstated，但題目問 depreciation charge 本身，所以係 understated。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 16 *Property, Plant and Equipment*（depreciation per cost model）；LP Ch 7.9.2（proof in total calculation for the depreciation charge — analytical procedure, verifies completeness and valuation）；HKSA 520 *Analytical Procedures*（investigating differences from expectations）。

**Common Trap（繁中）:** (1) 唔好加 residual value——題目話無。(2) 唔好 prorate——題目話無 additions/disposals。(3) Direction 要分清：charge 低咗 → depreciation understated → PPE carrying amount overstated。(4) 呢種 proof in total 係 substantive **analytical** procedure，唔好當佢係 test of details。

### Q4 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** Under HKAS 37 *Provisions, Contingent Liabilities and Contingent Assets* (HKAS 37.10), a provision is recognised only when ALL three criteria are met: (1) a present obligation as a result of a past event; (2) payment is **probable** (more likely than not); and (3) the amount can be **estimated reliably**. Here, the lawyers advise the likelihood of an outflow is only approximately 30% — possible but not probable — so the recognition criteria are NOT met and no provision can be recognised. However, because the claim is **material**, the company must **disclose a contingent liability** in the notes to the financial statements. If the finance director refuses to disclose a material contingent liability, the financial statements would not comply with HKAS 37 and the auditor would express a **modified opinion — qualified 'except for'** (material but not pervasive).

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題考 HKAS 37 三級門檻：
- **A 錯**：30% likelihood 唔係「probable」（more likely than not，即 >50%），recognition criteria 唔齊，唔可以認 provision。
- **B 錯**：trade payable 係畀「已知、確定」嘅負債；官司輸贏未知、金額未確定，唔可以用 payable。
- **D 錯**：呢個係最危險嘅 distractor——「below 50% 所以乜都唔使做」。錯！Possible（唔係 remote）+ material = 必須 **disclose contingent liability**。只有 remote 先可以唔披露。Finance director 嗰句「probably fail」係日常口語，唔係 HKAS 37 嘅 technical meaning，考試要分得好清楚。
- **C 啱**：possible + material → disclose only，唔入帳。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 37.10（provision recognition criteria）；HKAS 37（contingent liabilities — disclose unless possibility of settlement is remote）；LP Ch 7.12.1–7.12.2（non-disclosure of a material contingent liability → modified opinion, qualified 'except for'）。

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 三級門檻背到滾瓜爛熟：probable → recognise provision；possible → disclose contingent liability；remote → nothing。另外記住「probable」嘅口語同準則定義唔同——準則入面 probable = more likely than not。如果題目轉咗做 reimbursement/contingent asset，門檻係 **virtually certain** 先可以認資產。

### Q5 — Model Answer (any FOUR of the following)
**Model Answer (EN):**
1. **Inspect post-year-end cash receipts** in the post-year-end cash book/bank statements and trace them to pre-year-end trade receivable balances — verifies **existence and rights and obligations** (and provides strong evidence on **valuation**, since receipt of cash demonstrates recoverability).
2. **Select a sample of year-end trade receivable balances and agree them back to supporting documentation** (goods despatched notes and sales orders) — verifies **existence**.
3. **Obtain the aged receivables listing, cast it, and agree the total with the financial statements**; agree the receivables ledger control account with the list of balances — verifies **valuation and presentation** (and **completeness and existence** for the control account reconciliation).
4. **Review the aged receivables listing for slow-moving/overdue balances and discuss them with the credit control manager** to assess whether the **expected credit loss allowance under HKFRS 9** is adequate — verifies **valuation**.
5. **Inspect customer correspondence** for material disputed items that may require write-off or an increased allowance — verifies **existence, valuation and rights and obligations**.
6. **Calculate trade receivables days, compare with the prior year and investigate significant differences** (substantive analytical procedure) — verifies **completeness and valuation**.
7. **Inspect the receivables listing for credit balances** and consider reclassification to payables — verifies **existence, completeness (of payables) and classification**.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題係 Exam Practice 風格嘅「partner 話唔准做 confirmation，用 alternative procedures 頂上」情境，但場景係全新（Vertex Electronics）。得分關鍵：
- 每個 procedure 要 **寫明 assertion**——題目有要求，唔寫就跌分（考官最鬧「唔跟 requirement」）。
- 必勝兩式：**post-year-end cash receipts**（existence + valuation 雙殺）同 **agree balances to GDNs/sales orders**（existence）一定要識。
- 唔好寫 confirmation 相關嘢（題目話明 other than confirmation）；唔好寫 tests of control（題目要 substantive procedures）。
- Analytical（receivables days）都算分，但要註明佢主要 verify completeness 同 valuation。

**Rule Check:** LP Ch 7.6.2（receivables audit procedures and assertion mapping）；HKFRS 9 *Financial Instruments*（expected credit loss allowance）；HKSA 500 *Audit Evidence*（alternative procedures where external confirmation is not used）；HKSA 505 *External Confirmations*。

**Common Trap（繁中）:** (1) 寫 procedure 唔寫 assertion = 唔跟題目要求。(2) 將「agree listing total to FS」當 existence——佢其實係 valuation/presentation（加 control account reconciliation 先有 completeness/existence）。(3) Post-year-end cash receipts 係呢類題嘅王牌：existence、rights and obligations、valuation 三個都掂。(4) 管理層拒絕畀做 confirmation 本身仲要 assess 係咪 scope limitation——呢度 partner 已判斷有足夠 alternative procedures，所以跟住题意答就得。
