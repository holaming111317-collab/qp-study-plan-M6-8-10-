# M8 Chapter 2: Legal Liability and Corporate Governance — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. **Describe** the auditor's legal liability to clients under contract law, common law and the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), and the available defences.
2. **Explain** the auditor's liability to third parties, distinguishing the minimum basis of liability and burden of proof for each class of plaintiff.
3. **Distinguish** ordinary negligence, gross negligence and fraud, and apply the elements of each to audit-failure scenarios.
4. **Describe** the Hong Kong corporate governance framework (CG Code, 'comply or explain', board committees) and the auditor's responsibility to communicate with those charged with governance under HKSA 260 (Revised).
5. **Apply** the client acceptance and continuance requirements of HKSQM 1 and the preconditions for an audit under HKSA 210.

> 備註：本章屬法律責任與公司治理，未直接覆蓋 Assertions / audit procedures for specific balances 類目，故必背考點按 Liability / Fraud & Ethics / Governance & Communication / Acceptance & Continuance 組織。

### 必背考點

#### A. Auditor's Objectives & Statutory Liability（CO Cap. 622）
- **HKSA 200 para. 11(a)–(b)**: 核數師目標係 (a) obtain **reasonable assurance**（非 absolute assurance）whether the FS as a whole are free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error; (b) report on the FS and communicate as required by the HKSAs.
- **CO (Cap. 622) ss.405–406**: auditor must report to **shareholders** at the AGM on whether the FS show a true and fair view and conform with the CO.
- **CO (Cap. 622) s.407**: auditor also opines on whether **adequate accounting records** have been kept and whether the FS agree with those records. Failure to comply with ss.405–407 → legal liability to clients.
- **CO Subdivision 5 'Auditor's Liability' (subsection 3)**: any provision in the company's articles or contracts that **exempts or indemnifies an auditor** from liability for negligence, default, breach of duty or breach of trust is **void**。但唔禁止公司幫核數師買保險（insurance against liability, including costs of defending civil/criminal proceedings），亦唔阻止喺核數師勝訴／獲 Court 按 **ss.903 或 904** 寬免時作出補償。
- Criminal liability: auditors can be criminally liable (fine and imprisonment) for **knowingly or recklessly omitting** required statements from the auditor's report (e.g. adequate accounting records not kept; FS not in agreement with records; information/explanations not obtained) — 4th ed. LP 引用 Companies Bill Clause 398（即新 CO 相關條文）；**COE Section R115.1** 要求 professional accountants comply with all applicable laws, rules, regulations and standards.

#### B. Liability Under Contract Law & Common Law
- **Engagement letter（HKSA 210 para. 10）**內容必背：
  1. Objective and scope of the audit;
  2. Responsibilities of the auditor;
  3. Responsibilities of management（CO 下 directors 負責編製 true and fair FS）;
  4. Identification of the applicable financial reporting framework;
  5. Reference to the expected form and content of reports;
  6. A statement that the report may differ from its expected form and content.
- **Privity of contract** 只存在於 auditor 與 client 之間；breach of contract 例子：遲交 auditor's report、未按 HKSAs 執行審計。Contract law 下嘅抗辯：證明已履行 engagement letter 全部條款。
- **Ordinary negligence**: failure to exercise the standard of care a reasonable person would exercise in like circumstances。Elements: (1) duty of care; (2) breach of that duty; (3) failure to comply with required standard of professional care (HKFRS/HKSA); (4) causation; (5) damages/actual losses.
- **Gross negligence**: lack of duty of care indicative of **reckless behaviour**（例：carrying out the audit without following the HKSAs）；elements 相同但 breach is **more egregious**。
- **Fraud（HKSA 240.11(a)）**: intentional act by management / TCWG / employees / third parties, involving **deception** to obtain an unjust or illegal advantage。Elements to prove fraud: misrepresentation of material facts + knowledge/belief it was false + intent to induce reliance + third party suffered damages.
- **Defences to negligence claims**: (1) no duty of care owed（難證明）; (2) performance was not negligent (due diligence); (3) **contributory negligence** by client/third parties; (4) **absence of cause**（loss 唔係因為 reliance on auditor's report）.
- **Exhibit 2.1 — Plaintiff 類別（必背表）：**

| Plaintiff | Minimum Basis for Liability | Burden of Proof |
|---|---|---|
| Client | Ordinary negligence | Damage or loss |
| Third party – Primary beneficiary | Ordinary negligence | Misstated FS or erroneous advice |
| Foreseen beneficiary | Gross negligence | Reliance upon FS or advice |
| Foreseeable third parties | Gross negligence and fraud | Deficient auditor conduct |

- 香港法下 auditor 對 third parties 嘅法律責任範圍 **limited**，只限於 auditors have an **assumed responsibility to identified and particular third parties**。

#### C. Fraud Responsibility（Ethics 相關）
- **HKSA 240 para. 4**: primary responsibility for the **prevention and detection of fraud** rests with **those charged with governance AND management**（TCWG oversight 包括考慮 management override of controls 嘅可能性）。
- **HKSA 240 para. 5**: inherent limitations of an audit → unavoidable risk that some material misstatements may not be detected even if the audit is properly planned and performed.
- **HKSA 240 para. 33**: auditor must review accounting estimates for **biases**（包括 retrospective review of prior-year significant estimates）and evaluate whether bias represents a risk of material misstatement due to fraud.
- **HKSA 540 (Revised)**: 'spectrum of inherent risk' — 複雜 estimates 可構成 fraud risk factor。
- 考試金句：auditor 唔負責防範／偵查 fraud，但必須 obtain reasonable assurance 並保持 **professional scepticism**；audit failure = failing to discover material misstatements or failing to report discovered misstatements to management and TCWG.

#### D. Corporate Governance（HK framework）
- 定義：process of managing the company for the benefit of its **stakeholders**, aligning management's interests with shareholders'（agency theory vs regulatory perspective）。
- 三大 primary functions: **Oversight**（board of directors）、**Managerial**（management）、**Monitoring**（shareholders）。
- 來源：Model Articles、**CO (Cap. 622, effective March 2014)**、**SFO (Cap. 571)**、Common Law、HK Listing Rules（**Appendix 14** CG Code）、Takeover Code。
- **Appendix 14 兩層建議**：
  - **Code Provisions** — listed companies 必須 **comply or explain** 任何 non-compliance;
  - **Recommended Best Practices** — 自願性 guidance。
- CG Code sections: A Directors / B Board evaluation & remuneration / C Accountability and audit / D Delegation by the board / E Communication with shareholders / F Company secretary。
- **Board of directors**: meet regularly（**at least 4 times a year**, approx. quarterly）; 組成包括 executive + non-executive + INEDs；**Listing Rules 要求 INEDs 佔 board 至少 one-third**；Chairman 與 Chief Executive 角色 **should be separated**（如合併須按 Appendix 14 解釋理由）。
- 必設 committees: **nomination / remuneration / audit**。
- **Audit committee**: 只可由 **non-executive directors** 組成；**at least 3 members**；其中 **至少一名 INED** 具備 appropriate professional qualifications or accounting/related financial management expertise。職責：oversee financial reporting、internal control、risk management、code of ethics/whistle-blowing、appoint/remove/compensate external auditors and monitor their independence（每年 review auditor appointment）。
- **Nomination committee**: majority INEDs，chairman 由 INED 或 board chairman 出任；duties 見 **Appendix 14 para. A5.2**：(a) review board structure/size/composition at least annually; (b) identify suitably qualified individuals; (c) assess independence of INEDs; (d) recommend appointment/re-appointment and succession planning（尤其 chairman 與 chief executive）。
- **Remuneration committee**: formal and transparent procedure for directors' remuneration；no director should decide his/her own remuneration；link pay to performance。

#### E. Auditor–TCWG Communication & Management Responsibilities
- **HKSA 260 (Revised) Communication with Those Charged with Governance** — 溝通目的/事項：
  - auditor's responsibilities 與 engagement terms 的理解；
  - 從 TCWG obtain audit-relevant information；
  - overview of **planned scope, timing and strategy**；
  - timely observations significant to financial reporting；
  - effective **two-way communication**（preserving independence & objectivity）；
  - significant qualitative aspects of accounting policies, **accounting estimates** and FS disclosures。
- **HKSA 540 (Revised) para. 38**: communicate with management/TCWG about estimates giving rise to risk of material misstatement due to estimation uncertainty, complexity, subjectivity or other inherent risk factors.
- **HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para. 19**（examinable，periods beginning on/after 15 Dec 2021）：risk assessment procedures to understand (a) entity & environment（organizational structure, ownership & governance, business model incl. IT; industry/regulatory factors; performance measures）; (b) applicable financial reporting framework & accounting policies; (c) how inherent risk factors affect susceptibility of assertions to misstatement.
- Management responsibilities：prepare FS per GAAP（首要責任）、design/implement/monitor internal control & risk management、確保 auditor 有 **unrestricted access** to records、explanations、attend general meetings。

#### F. Client Acceptance & Continuance
- **Auditor appointment 次序（CO）**: board of directors / audit committee → shareholders at AGM → the court（董事唔委任先輪到股東，股東都唔委任先由 court 委任）。
- **HKSQM 1 para. 30**（risk-based quality objectives，取代 HKSQC 1）:
  - (a) accept/continue judgments based on (i) information about the engagement and **integrity and ethical values of the client**, and (ii) the firm's **ability to perform** the engagement per professional standards and legal/regulatory requirements;
  - (b) the firm's **financial and operational priorities** must not lead to inappropriate acceptance/continuance judgments.
- **HKSA 210 para. 6 — Preconditions for an audit**: (a) acceptable financial reporting framework; (b) management acknowledges responsibility for (i) FS preparation, (ii) necessary internal control, (iii) providing access to all information, additional information requested, and unrestricted access to persons within the entity.
- **Client due diligence / AML**：Code Chapter F para. 600.1.4 — 'know your client' procedures，查業務及 ownership structure，防 money laundering。
- **Predecessor auditor communication**：必須先獲 client permission（否則 outgoing auditor 會違反 confidentiality）；商討事項包括 accounting/audit disagreements、fraud/illegal acts/internal control communications、**reasons for the change of auditors**。Client 拒絕 permission 且無 valid reasons → incoming auditor 應 decline。
- **Continuing engagements**：每年 revisit acceptance considerations + continued client due diligence（HKSQM 1）；如 management integrity 有疑問（例如發現重大 fraud）→ resign，除非法律強制繼續。

### 易混淆概念對比

#### 表 1：Ordinary Negligence vs Gross Negligence vs Fraud

| | Ordinary Negligence | Gross Negligence | Fraud |
|---|---|---|---|
| Definition | Failure to exercise the standard of care a reasonable person would exercise in like circumstances | Lack of duty of care indicative of **reckless behaviour** (e.g. not following HKSAs at all) | **Intentional** act involving deception to obtain unjust/illegal advantage (HKSA 240.11(a)) |
| Mindset | 疏忽、唔小心 | 魯莽、罔顧後果（程度更嚴重） | 故意欺騙 |
| Typical example | 冇攞到 sufficient appropriate audit evidence | 做 audit 完全唔跟 HKSAs | 篡改 working papers、作假陳述 |
| Liable to | Client + primary beneficiary | Foreseen/foreseeable third parties | Foreseeable third parties |
| 點樣分辨 | 問「有冇合理謹慎？」冇 = ordinary | 問「係咪完全忽視專業準則？」= gross | 問「係咪 intentional？」有 intent 先係 fraud |
| 考試伏位 | 見「unintentional overstatement」係 **error** 唔係 fraud；gross negligence 唔需要 intent，但 fraud 必須有 intent to deceive | | |

#### 表 2：Code Provisions vs Recommended Best Practices（Appendix 14）

| | Code Provisions | Recommended Best Practices |
|---|---|---|
| Status | 'Comply or explain' — 上市公司必須遵守，否則解釋 non-compliance | 自願性 guidance，幫 issuer 設計自己嘅 CG measures |
| Mandatory? | 唔係 mandatory rules，但唔遵守就要 **explain** | 完全自願，唔使解釋 |
| 適用對象 | HKEX listed companies only（principles-based, non-statutory） | 同上 |
| 例子 | Chairman/CEO roles should be separated（如合併須給予理由） | 每季度 45 日內公佈業績、audit committee 設 whistleblowing policy |
| 點樣分辨 | 題目問「must comply or explain」→ Code Provision | 問「encouraged / voluntary」→ RBP |
| 考試伏位 | CG Code **唔係法例**（non-statutory），違反唔等於犯法；但 CO (Cap. 622) 先係 statutory framework — 兩者唔好撈亂 | |

## Phase 2 閃卡（8 cards）

### Card 1
**Front:** What are the auditor's two objectives under HKSA 200 para. 11(a)–(b)?
**Back:**
- (a) Obtain **reasonable assurance** about whether the FS as a whole are free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, enabling expression of an opinion on whether the FS are prepared, in all material respects, in accordance with the applicable financial reporting framework;
- (b) Report on the FS, and communicate as required by the HKSAs, in accordance with the auditor's findings.
> 💬 記住係 **reasonable**（高但非絕對）assurance，因為 audit 有 inherent limitations（抽樣、依賴管理層陳述等）；如果題目講 absolute assurance 一定錯。

### Card 2
**Front:** Who bears the primary responsibility for the prevention and detection of fraud, and what is the auditor's responsibility? (HKSA 240)
**Back:**
- Primary responsibility: **those charged with governance and management** (HKSA 240 para. 4), including oversight of the potential for management override of controls.
- Auditor: obtain **reasonable assurance** that the FS are free from material misstatement whether caused by fraud or error.
- HKSA 240 para. 5: due to **inherent limitations**, some material misstatements may not be detected even in a properly planned and performed audit.
> 💬 考試必考陷阱：「auditor 負責防止同偵查 fraud」= 錯。Auditor 嘅責任係合理保證＋professional scepticism，唔係捉賊。

### Card 3
**Front:** List the six items typically included in an engagement letter per HKSA 210 para. 10.
**Back:**
1. Objective and scope of the audit;
2. Responsibilities of the auditor;
3. Responsibilities of management;
4. Identification of the applicable financial reporting framework;
5. Reference to the expected form and content of any reports;
6. A statement that a report may differ from its expected form and content.
> 💬 Engagement letter 係 auditor 同 client 嘅 **書面合約**，確立 privity of contract；contract law 下嘅抗辯就係證明已履行晒入面所有條款。

### Card 4
**Front:** What are the five elements required to prove ordinary negligence against an auditor?
**Back:**
1. **Duty of care** — the care others in the profession would exercise in similar circumstances;
2. **Breach** of that duty of care;
3. Failure to comply with the required standard of reasonable professional care (HKFRS/HKSA and other rules);
4. **Causation** — causal link between negligence and the plaintiff's losses;
5. **Damages** — actual losses incurred.
> 💬 記口訣 D-B-C-C-D（Duty, Breach, Compliance failure, Causation, Damages）。Gross negligence elements 一樣，分別在於 breach 程度更 egregious（reckless）。

### Card 5
**Front:** State the four defences available to an auditor facing a negligence claim.
**Back:**
1. **No duty of care** was owed (difficult to prove);
2. Performance was **not negligent** — due diligence was exercised;
3. **Contributory negligence** by the client and/or third parties;
4. **Absence of cause** — reliance on the auditor's report was not the reason for the financial loss.
> 💬 Contributory negligence 例子：client 收埋關鍵資料（如匿名告密信）唔話畀 auditor 知；absence of cause 例子：債權人無論 audit opinion 點都會照借錢。

### Card 6
**Front:** Under CO (Cap. 622) Subdivision 5, what happens to provisions that exempt or indemnify an auditor from liability, and what is still permitted?
**Back:**
- Any provision in articles/contracts exempting or indemnifying an auditor (of the company or an associated company) from liability for negligence, default, breach of duty or trust is **void**.
- Still permitted: (1) company taking out and maintaining **insurance** for the auditor against such liabilities (including defence costs in civil/criminal proceedings); (2) indemnifying the auditor where judgement is given in the auditor's favour, the auditor is acquitted, or relief is granted by the Court under **ss.903 or 904**.
> 💬 考試伏位：「公司可以喺 articles 加條款豁免核數師疏忽責任」= 錯，條款 void；但「公司可以幫核數師買保險」= 啱。

### Card 7
**Front:** What are the Listing Rules / CG Code composition requirements for the audit committee of a Hong Kong listed company?
**Back:**
- Composed of **non-executive directors only**;
- **At least three members**;
- At least **one independent non-executive director** with appropriate professional qualifications or accounting/related financial management expertise;
- Oversees financial reporting, internal control, risk management, audit activities (appoint/remove/compensate external auditors, monitor independence), code of ethics and whistle-blowing.
> 💬 記住「NEDs only + ≥3 + ≥1 INED 會計專才」。Executive director（如 CFO）坐入 audit committee = 即時違規，呢個係常見 scenario 陷阱。

### Card 8
**Front:** What quality objectives does HKSQM 1 para. 30 require a firm to establish for client acceptance and continuance?
**Back:**
- (a) Acceptance/continuance judgments are appropriate based on:
  - (i) information about the nature and circumstances of the engagement and the **integrity and ethical values of the client** (management and, where appropriate, TCWG); and
  - (ii) the firm's **ability to perform** the engagement in accordance with professional standards and applicable legal/regulatory requirements;
- (b) The firm's **financial and operational priorities** do not lead to inappropriate acceptance/continuance judgments.
> 💬 HKSQM 1 係 risk-based，取代咗 HKSQC 1。常見 red flags：頻密換 auditor、director 被調查、異常豐厚 audit fee + 豪華招待（self-interest threat）、client 拒絕畀 predecessor auditor 溝通權限。
