# M8 Chapter 4: Audit Planning II — Phase 3 Quiz — Model Answers

### Q1 — Answer: Detection risk = 12.5%

**Model Answer (EN):**
Audit risk = Inherent risk × Control risk × Detection risk
- 5% = 80% × 50% × DR
- DR = 0.05 ÷ (0.80 × 0.50) = 0.05 ÷ 0.40 = **0.125 = 12.5%**

Because the acceptable detection risk is low (12.5%), the auditor must perform **more extensive substantive procedures** over the revenue cycle — i.e. modify the nature, timing and extent of planned substantive procedures (larger samples, more tests of detail, procedures closer to/after the year-end) to reduce the risk that the auditor's procedures fail to detect a material misstatement. Placing little or no reliance on the entity's internal controls over revenue would be consistent with a control risk assessed at 50% or higher.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
審計風險模型係乘法：AR = IR × CR × DR。移項就係 DR = AR ÷ (IR × CR) = 5% ÷ 40% = 12.5%。背後邏輯：IR 同 CR 都唔受 auditor 控制，唯一可以郁嘅係 DR；而 DR 同 substantive testing 嘅 extent 係反比 — 可接受 DR 越低，就要做越多、越細緻嘅 substantive procedures 嚟壓低實際 DR。呢題同 LP 嘅 Apply and Analyse 4（AR 10%、IR/CR 各 70% → DR 20.4%）係同一條數式，換咗數字啫。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 200.13(n)(i), 200.13(n)(ii), 200.13(e) (definitions of inherent risk, control risk, detection risk); audit risk model per LP Section 4.1.2 (AR = IR × CR × DR)

**Common Trap（繁中）:**
- 唔好將三個風險**相加**（模型係乘法，唔係加法）。
- 唔好答「12.5% 好低所以要減少 substantive testing」— 方向啱啱倒轉：DR 低 = 要**多**做 substantive work。呢個 inverse relationship 係 Jun 2023 panelist report 明確指出嘅考生弱點。
- 記住 detection risk = sampling risk + non-sampling risk，係唯一 auditor 控制到嘅風險。

### Q2 — Answer: B

**Model Answer (EN):**
Selecting items from the **physical inventory** and tracing them **to the inventory records** tests the **completeness** assertion — it verifies that all inventory that physically exists has been recorded in the accounting records. The direction of testing is decisive: floor-to-records = completeness; records-to-floor = existence.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
測試方向係關鍵：由「實物」追到「紀錄」，係搵「有冇漏記」= **completeness**。
- A (Existence) 錯：existence 係由 inventory records 抽樣、追到 physical inventory（records → floor），方向相反。
- C (Accuracy, valuation and allocation) 錯：valution 要測 cost vs NRV、recalculation，唔係 trace 方向。
- D (Rights and obligations) 錯：要睇 supplier invoices / title documents，physical trace 唔能夠證明擁有權。
LP 原文（Section 4.6.3.1 Relevance）就用咗同一對例子：physical → records = completeness；records → physical = existence。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para A190(b) (assertions about account balances); LP Section 4.6.3.1 (relevance — direction of testing at the inventory count)

**Common Trap（繁中）:**
「Existence vs completeness 測試方向倒轉」係 Exam Lens 列明嘅 panelist 反覆投訴弱點（Jun 2023/Jun 2024）。考試見到 inventory count trace 題，第一件事問自己：起點係 floor 定 records？Floor → records = completeness；records → floor = existence。

### Q3 — Answer: B

**Model Answer (EN):**
The loan to a director is **material by nature**, even though it is quantitatively immaterial. Materiality has both quantitative and qualitative dimensions: transactions with directors (such as loans and directors' remuneration) are material by nature because of their impact on the financial statements and their sensitivity. The loan must be disclosed as a related party transaction in accordance with HKAS 24 (Revised) Related Party Disclosures. If management refuses to make the disclosure, the auditor would be required to modify the opinion due to a material disclosure being omitted from the financial statements.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
呢題考「material by nature」。LP 嘅 Apply and Analyse 5 幾乎同款：賤賣車俾 director，金額 immaterial 但係 related party transaction，必須披露。
- A 錯：「金額細 + 記錄正確」唔代表可以唔理 — qualitative factors 可以令 immaterial amount 變 material。
- C 錯：auditor 唔可以為咗令 misstatement 變 immaterial 而**調高** materiality — 調整 materiality 必須基於新發現嘅事實，唔係為遷就結果。
- D 錯：auditor 冇權要求董事還錢；auditor 嘅角色係要求**披露**／修正財務報表，否則考慮 modify opinion。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 320 Materiality in Planning and Performing an Audit (qualitative and quantitative factors); HKAS 24 (Revised) Related Party Disclosures; LP Section 4.2.2 (material by nature)

**Common Trap（繁中）:**
考生成日以為 materiality 淨係數字遊戲。記住 LP 嘅 material-by-nature 清單：profit 變 loss、net assets 變 net liabilities、**directors' transactions**、going concern uncertainties、legal claims disclosures。見到「director」兩個字就要即刻諗 related party + material by nature。

### Q4 — Answer: B

**Model Answer (EN):**
Under HKSA 610 (Revised 2013), the external auditor must not assign to internal auditors work that involves **making significant judgements in the audit**, work relating to a **high risk of material misstatement**, or work with which the internal auditor has been involved. Assignment (2) — evaluating a highly subjective legal provision involving significant judgement and high RoMM — therefore must not be assigned. Routine reperformance such as bank reconciliations (assignment 1) may be assigned, subject to evaluating the internal auditors' objectivity and competence, obtaining management's written agreement and confidentiality confirmation, and the external auditor directing, supervising and reviewing their work.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- B 啱：HKSA 610 (Revised 2013) 嘅禁區 — 唔可以 assign significant judgements、high RoMM、IA 曾參與嘅工作、或 HKSA 610 下嘅決定 — (2) 踩中兩個禁區，唔可以外派。
- A 錯：supervision 唔可以洗白禁區工作 — 禁區係絕對嘅，唔係「supervise 就得」。
- C 錯：bank reconciliation 係 routine reperformance，唔涉及 significant judgement，可以 assign（前提係評估咗 objectivity + competence）。
- D 錯：direct assistance 唔係全面禁止 — 只係當法律/規例禁止、或 610.28(a)(b) 嘅情況（significant objectivity threats / lacks competence）先唔可以用。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 610 (Revised 2013) Using the Work of Internal Auditors — paras 610.28(a)–(b) (prohibitions on direct assistance: significant objectivity threats / lack of competence) and the "must not assign" requirements (significant judgements, high RoMM) as set out in LP Section 4.7.5

**Common Trap（繁中）:**
唔好以為「direct assistance = 乜都可以俾內審做」。考試伏位通常係：(i) 禁區工作類型（significant judgement / high RoMM）；(ii) direct assistance 嘅先決條件（management written agreement + IA written confidentiality confirmation + direction/supervision/review by external auditor）；(iii) 內審個名**永遠唔會**出現喺 auditor's report。

### Q5 — Answer: C

**Model Answer (EN):**
The four steps of a substantive analytical procedure are: (1) develop an independent expectation; (2) define a significant difference or threshold; (3) calculate the difference; (4) investigate significant differences and draw conclusions. The threshold (maximum acceptable difference) **must not exceed performance materiality** and must be sufficiently small to enable the auditor to identify misstatements that could be material individually or when aggregated.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- C 啱：LP 明確寫「the threshold does not exceed performance materiality」。
- A 錯：threshold 嘅上限係 **performance materiality**（較低嗰個），唔係 FS materiality — 呢個係本章最經典嘅 MCQ 伏位。
- B 錯：次序錯 — LP 明講「it would be inappropriate to first calculate the difference from prior-year/period balances and then allow the results to influence the expected difference and acceptable threshold」。一定要先建立 **independent expectation**。
- D 錯：substantive analytical procedures **唔可以單獨用** — 佢哋 identify possible sources of misstatement，唔直接 detect misstatements，必須配合 inquiry / recalculation / reperformance 等 corroborative testing。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 520 Analytical Procedures; LP Section 4.6.1.2 (four elements/steps; threshold must not exceed performance materiality)

**Common Trap（繁中）:**
兩個陷阱位：(1) threshold 上限揀 FS materiality（錯，係 performance materiality）；(2) 步驟次序倒轉 — 睇咗數先度 expectation 會產生 anchoring bias，LP 明文禁止。記住口訣：Expectation → Threshold → Difference → Investigate（ETDI），順序唔可以亂。
