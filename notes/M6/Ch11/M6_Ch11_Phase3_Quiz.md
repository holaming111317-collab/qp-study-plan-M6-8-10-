**Q1.** In accordance with HKAS 36, which of the following assets of Heron Retail Limited MUST be tested for impairment at least annually, irrespective of whether any indication of impairment exists?

**Q2.** Falcon Logistics Limited owns a cargo vehicle with a carrying amount of HK$350,000 at 31 December 20X8. Following a significant fall in freight rates, an impairment test was performed. The vehicle's fair value is estimated at HK$300,000 and estimated costs of disposal are HK$15,000. If the vehicle continues to be used, it is expected to generate net cash inflows of HK$120,000, HK$110,000 and HK$100,000 at the end of each of the next three years respectively, after which it will be scrapped for nil. The appropriate discount rate is 8%. (Present value factors at 8%: Year 1 = 0.926; Year 2 = 0.857; Year 3 = 0.794.)

In accordance with HKAS 36, what is the impairment loss, if any, to be recognised?

A. HK$20,000
B. HK$50,000
C. HK$65,000
D. HK$65,210

**Q3.** Marlin Manufacturing Limited carries a machine under the revaluation model in HKAS 16. At 30 June 20X9, the machine has a carrying amount of HK$800,000 and a revaluation surplus of HK$30,000 relating to this same machine is included in equity. An impairment test determined the machine's recoverable amount to be HK$720,000.

In accordance with HKAS 36, which set of journal entries correctly records the impairment loss of HK$80,000 (ignore the elimination of accumulated depreciation)?

A. Dr Impairment loss (P&L) HK$80,000 / Cr Machine HK$80,000
B. Dr Revaluation surplus (OCI) HK$80,000 / Cr Machine HK$80,000
C. Dr Revaluation surplus (OCI) HK$30,000; Dr Impairment loss (P&L) HK$50,000 / Cr Machine HK$80,000
D. Dr Impairment loss (P&L) HK$50,000; Dr Machine HK$30,000 / Cr Revaluation surplus (OCI) HK$80,000

**Q4.** Otter Beverages Limited has identified its juice division as a cash-generating unit (CGU). At 31 March 20X9, the carrying amounts of the assets of the CGU are: goodwill HK$200,000; bottling machine HK$500,000; factory building HK$300,000. The recoverable amount of the CGU is HK$700,000. Neither the bottling machine's nor the factory building's individual fair value less costs of disposal or value in use is measurable.

In accordance with HKAS 36, what amount of the impairment loss is allocated to the bottling machine?

A. HK$62,500
B. HK$50,000
C. HK$150,000
D. HK$100,000

**Q5.** On 1 January 20X1, Pelican Freight Limited acquired a crane for HK$1,800,000 with a useful life of nine years and no residual value, depreciated on a straight-line basis (cost model). On 31 December 20X4, an impairment test showed a recoverable amount of HK$700,000, and an impairment loss was recognised; the useful life was unchanged. On 31 December 20X5, indicators showed the impairment loss had decreased, and the recoverable amount was estimated at HK$950,000.

In accordance with HKAS 36, what is the amount of the impairment loss reversal that Pelican Freight Limited should recognise on 31 December 20X5?

A. HK$100,000
B. HK$240,000
C. HK$300,000
D. HK$390,000
