# M6 Chapter 6: Investment Property — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** A vacant building held to be leased out by the entity as a lessor under one or more operating leases is specifically identified as an example of investment property (HKAS 40.8(d)). Property occupied by employees is owner-occupied property even if employees pay rent at market rates (HKAS 40.9(c)) — option A. Property held for sale in the ordinary course of business is inventory within HKAS 2 (HKAS 40.9(a)) — option C. A building used as the entity's administrative head office is owner-occupied property within HKAS 16 — option D.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 關鍵係睇「持有目的」同「cash flows 係咪獨立」。A 係經典伏位：員工宿舍即使收市值租金都係 owner-occupied property，因為佢嘅 cash flows 係同公司營運一齊產生。C 係「短期內出售」→ inventory（HKAS 2）。D 係自用行政樓 → HKAS 16。只有 B 係「空置但持有作出租」— vacant 唔代表自用，HKAS 40.8(d) 明確列為 IP。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 40.8(d); HKAS 40.9(a), (c)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 以為「收租 = IP」就會揀 A — 但對象係員工，仍然係 owner-occupied。另外唔好以為空置樓冇用途就唔係 IP；相反，「用途未定嘅土地」反而係 IP（HKAS 40.8(b)）。

### Q2 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):**
- Purchase price 80,000 + legal fees 1,200 + property transfer taxes 3,400 = **HK$84,600,000**
- Legal fees and property transfer taxes are transaction costs — directly attributable expenditure included in cost (HKAS 40.20–21).
- Advertising to attract tenants is a start-up cost — expensed (HKAS 40.23).
- Abnormal wasted materials from the typhoon interruption — expensed, not capitalised (HKAS 40.23(c)).

**解題思路（繁中）:** A (HK$85.1m) 錯在將廣告費資本化 — advertising 係 start-up cost，HKAS 40.23 明確排除。B (HK$86.6m) 錯在將 abnormal waste（颱風造成嘅異常浪費）計入成本 — 呢類支出要即刻出 P&L。D (HK$81.2m) 錯在漏咗 property transfer taxes — 印花稅／轉讓稅係 transaction costs，必須計入 IP 成本（唔同 HKFRS 9 下 FVTPL 金融資產嘅處理）。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 40.20–21 (elements of cost); HKAS 40.23(a),(c) (exclusions)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 記住 IP 嘅 transaction costs 係「加」入成本；考試成日混埋「abnormal waste」同埋「達到預計入住率前嘅經營虧損」嚟呃你 — 三樣排除項要背熟。

### Q3 — Answer: A
**Model Answer (EN):** Under the fair value model, the investment property is remeasured to fair value at each reporting date with the change recognised in profit or loss (HKAS 40.33, 40.35). The fair value movement during 20X1 is measured from the previous reporting date's fair value: HK$52m − HK$48m = HK$4m decrease → Dr Fair value loss (profit or loss) HK$4m / Cr Investment property HK$4m. No depreciation is charged under the fair value model.

**解題思路（繁中）:** B (Dr IP 3 / Cr gain 3) 係用咗「由買入價 HK$45m 到而家 HK$48m」嘅累計升值 — 錯，因為上年嘅 HK$7m gain 已經喺 20X0 年認咗，本年只認「由上年年結 fair value 起計」嘅變動。C 錯在將 fair value loss 放入 OCI — IP 嘅 fair value 升跌永遠入 P&L，唔入 OCI（呢點同 HKAS 16 revaluation model 相反）。D 錯在 fair value model 下根本唔折舊，而且題目已講明唔計折舊。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 40.33; HKAS 40.35

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 兩大伏位：(1) 用累計變動（成本對比最新 FV）而唔係年度變動；(2) 將 IP fair value 變動當成 PPE revaluation 咁擺入 OCI / revaluation surplus — 記住 fair value model = 全數入 profit or loss。

### Q4 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):**
Workings:
- Annual depreciation = (HK$90m − HK$6m) ÷ 40 = HK$2.1m
- Carrying amount at 1 January 20X5 = 90 − (2.1 × 12) = HK$64.8m
- Depreciation for 4 months to 30 April 20X5 (date of change in use) = 2.1 × 4/12 = HK$0.7m
- Carrying amount at 30 April 20X5 = 64.8 − 0.7 = HK$64.1m
- Revaluation increase to fair value = 70 − 64.1 = **HK$5.9m**, recognised in revaluation surplus through OCI under the HKAS 16 revaluation rules (no prior P&L revaluation decrease exists).

Per HKAS 40.61–62: depreciation is updated to the date of change in use; the uplift on transfer to fair-value-model investment property is treated as an HKAS 16 revaluation (→ OCI), not as an HKAS 40 fair value gain.

**解題思路（繁中）:** A (HK$5.2m) 係漏咗計 20X5 年頭 4 個月折舊（70 − 64.8）— 轉撥前要將折舊計到 change in use 嗰日。C (HK$0) 係錯用「cost model → cost model 賬面值不變」嘅規則 — 但呢度轉入嘅係 fair value model IP，必須重估。D (HK$7.75m) 係計折舊時漏咗扣 residual value（90 ÷ 40 × 12⅓ 年 = 27.75；CA = 62.25；升值 7.75）— depreciable amount = cost − residual value。仲有個隱藏伏位：轉撥當日嘅升值係入 OCI（HKAS 16 規則），唔係入 P&L；轉咗做 IP 之後嘅 fair value 變動先至入 P&L。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 40.61–62; HKAS 16 (revaluation rules); HKAS 40.57 (end of owner occupation as evidence of change in use)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 三個常見錯：(1) 唔計轉撥前嗰段期間嘅折舊；(2) 唔記得扣 residual value；(3) 將轉撥升值直接入 P&L — 分界點係「轉嗰刻跟 HKAS 16，轉完之後跟 HKAS 40」。

### Q5 — Answer: D
**Model Answer (EN):** Commencement of development with a view to sale is evidence of a change in use, triggering a transfer from investment property to inventories on 1 September 20X1 (HKAS 40.57(d)). The June 20X1 management decision alone is not evidence of a change in use (HKAS 40.58). On transfer: (i) remeasure the IP to fair value at the date of change in use — gain of HK$47m − HK$40m = HK$7m recognised in profit or loss (HKAS 40.35, 40.60); (ii) the fair value of HK$47m becomes the deemed cost of the inventory under HKAS 2 (HKAS 40.60). At 31 December 20X1 the property is measured at the lower of deemed cost (HK$47m) and NRV; since NRV exceeds cost, it is carried at HK$47m. The subsequent fair value of HK$70m is irrelevant.

**解題思路（繁中）:** A 錯在年結時用返 fair value HK$70m — 轉咗做 inventory 之後就係 HKAS 2 嘅天下（lower of cost and NRV），之後嘅 fair value 唔關事。B 錯在當咗成個年度升值（70 − 40 = 30）都係 gain — 得轉撥前嗰 HK$7m 先入 P&L，而且存貨唔會以高於成本列賬。C 錯在冇喺轉撥日重估 — IP（fair value model）轉出前必須先 remeasure 到當日 fair value，唔可以按舊賬面值 HK$40m 直接過戶。仲要留意：6 月管理層「決定」出售唔算 change in use，9 月 1 日「動工起嚟賣」先算 — intention alone 唔係證據。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 40.57; HKAS 40.58; HKAS 40.60; HKAS 40.35; HKAS 2 (lower of cost and NRV)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 記住「deemed cost = 轉撥日 fair value」，之後唔准 reverse 累計 fair value gains/losses 去還原成本；亦唔好被年結嘅高 fair value 引誘去重估存貨 — inventory 上限係 NRV，唔係 fair value。
