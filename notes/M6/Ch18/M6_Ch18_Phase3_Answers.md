# M6 Chapter 18: Effects of Changes in Foreign Exchange Rates — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** Monetary items are units of currency held and assets and liabilities to be received or paid in a **fixed or determinable number of units of currency** (HKAS 21.8). Trade receivables and dividends payable both meet this definition. Inventories, prepaid insurance, goodwill and property, plant and equipment are non-monetary items — their essential feature is the absence of a right to receive (or obligation to deliver) a fixed or determinable number of currency units (HKAS 21.16).
**解題思路（繁中）:** 判斷方法係問「到期收/付嘅係咪固定金額嘅錢」。A 嘅 inventories 同 prepaid insurance 將來交嘅係貨同服務保障，唔係錢 → 錯；C 嘅 goodwill 係 non-monetary → 錯；D 嘅 PPE 係 non-monetary → 錯；只有 B 兩項都係收/付固定金額貨幣。Borrowings 同 trade payables 本身係 monetary，可惜佢哋同 non-monetary item 綁埋一齊，成個 option 就錯。
**Rule Check:** HKAS 21.8 (definition of monetary items); HKAS 21.16 (essential feature of non-monetary items)
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 見到 "payable" 就以為一定 monetary — prepaid expenses 同 unearned revenue 雖然有現金流過，但將來交嘅係貨/服務，係 non-monetary。反向伏位：share investments 都唔係 monetary（冇收固定現金嘅權利）。

### Q2 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** The functional currency is the currency of the primary economic environment in which the entity operates (HKAS 21.8). HKAS 21.9 primary factors: (1) the currency that mainly influences sales prices — RMB (85% of sales denominated and settled in RMB); (2) the currency of the country whose competitive forces and regulations determine sales prices — Mainland China, i.e. RMB; (3) the currency that mainly influences labour, material and other costs — RMB. All three primary factors indicate RMB. The HKD bank borrowings are only an additional (secondary) factor under HKAS 21.10, and the place of incorporation is not determinative.
**解題思路（繁中）:** 三個 primary factors（售價、競爭/規管所在地、成本）全部指向 RMB，所以答案係 C。A 用咗次要因素（融資貨幣，HKAS 21.10）同註冊地嚟壓過主要因素 — 次序調轉咗；B 完全無事實支持；D 混淆咗 presentation currency（先可以自由揀）同 functional currency（必須按 HKAS 21.9 因素判斷）。
**Rule Check:** HKAS 21.8; HKAS 21.9 (primary factors); HKAS 21.10 (additional factors); HKAS 21.12 (judgement when indicators are mixed, primary factors given priority)
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 「公司註冊喺香港 → functional currency 係 HKD」係典型錯覺；考試鍾意畀 mixed indicators 考你知唔知 primary factors 優先。

### Q3 — Answer: A
**Model Answer (EN):** The trade payable is a foreign currency **monetary item** and must be translated at the **closing rate** at the reporting date (HKAS 21.23(a)).
- Initial recognition (1 Nov 20X8): US$40,000 × 7.82 = HK$312,800
- Closing rate (31 Dec 20X8): US$40,000 × 7.78 = HK$311,200
- Decrease in liability = HK$312,800 − HK$311,200 = **HK$1,600** → exchange **gain** in profit or loss (HKAS 21.28).
Journal entry: Dr Trade payable (US$) HK$1,600 / Cr Gain on foreign exchange difference (P/L) HK$1,600.
**解題思路（繁中）:** 負債嘅 HKD 價值由 312,800 跌到 311,200，即係將來使少咗錢找數 → gain 1,600。B 錯在方向調轉（畀 HKD 貶值先會係 loss）；C 係常見誤解，以為未找數就唔使計 — HKAS 21.28 明確規定 monetary item 嘅 exchange differences（包括 unrealised）喺產生期間入 P/L；D 用平均匯率 7.80 重譯（40,000 × 7.80 = 312,000，gain 800）— 年結必須用 closing rate，唔係平均率。
**Rule Check:** HKAS 21.23(a) (monetary items at closing rate); HKAS 21.28 (exchange differences to profit or loss)
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 記住「負債升 = loss、負債跌 = gain」（應收款就反轉）。同埋 unrealised gain/loss 都要入賬，唔使等 settlement。

### Q4 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** At 31 December 20X7 the trade receivable was retranslated at the closing rate: EUR50,000 × 8.60 = HK$430,000. On settlement (20 January 20X8), cash received = EUR50,000 × 8.55 = HK$427,500. The receivable settled exceeds cash received by HK$2,500 → exchange **loss** in profit or loss (HKAS 21.28). Correct entry: **Dr Cash 427,500; Dr Loss on foreign exchange difference (P/L) 2,500; Cr Trade receivable (EUR) 430,000.**
**解題思路（繁中）:** 做呢類題目三步走：(1) 年結已重譯 receivable = 430,000（用 closing rate 8.60）；(2) 找數日用當日 spot rate 計實收現金 = 427,500；(3) 差額 2,500 係 loss（收少咗）。A 完全忽略匯率由 8.60 跌到 8.55，當足額收 430,000 → 錯；B 用返最初交易日匯率 8.50 做基準（425,000）兼方向調轉 → 錯；D 現金金額用錯 8.50（425,000），仲要將 loss 寫成 gain → 錯。只有 C 金額、方向、科目全對。
**Rule Check:** HKAS 21.23(a) (closing rate at reporting date); HKAS 21.28 (exchange differences arising on settlement recognised in profit or loss)
**Common Trap（繁中）:** Settlement 日嘅差額係同「年結重譯後嘅賬面值」比較，唔係同最初交易日金額比較 — 跨年交易一定要分段計。

### Q5 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** Under HKAS 2, inventories are carried at the lower of cost and net realisable value. Under HKAS 21.25, each amount is translated at the exchange rate in effect at the date that amount was determined:
- Cost: MYR200,000 × 1.90 (rate when cost was determined) = HK$380,000
- NRV: MYR210,000 × 1.75 (closing rate when NRV was determined) = HK$367,500
- NRV < cost in HKD → write-down = HK$380,000 − HK$367,500 = **HK$12,500**.
Journal entry: Dr Loss on inventory write-down (P/L) HK$12,500 / Cr Inventories HK$12,500.
**解題思路（繁中）:** 雖然用 MYR 睇 NRV（210,000）高過成本（200,000），但 HKAS 21.25 要求兩個數各自用「該金額確定當日」嘅匯率折做 functional currency 先比較。折做 HKD 之後 NRV 反而低過成本，所以要減值 12,500。A 係最大伏位 — 只睇外幣層面就話唔使減值；C（30,000）係錯用 closing rate 重譯成本（200,000 × 1.75 = 350,000；380,000 − 350,000）— 歷史成本唔可以重譯；D（31,500）係亂用匯率差（210,000 × (1.90 − 1.75)），無視兩數分開翻譯嘅規則。
**Rule Check:** HKAS 21.25 (carrying amount determined by comparing two amounts — translate each at the rate at the date the amount was determined); HKAS 2 (lower of cost and NRV)
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 「外幣冇減值 → functional currency 都冇減值」係錯嘅；匯率變動本身可以製造（或消除）減值。記住：cost 用 acquisition-date rate，NRV 用 closing rate，兩個率通常唔同。
