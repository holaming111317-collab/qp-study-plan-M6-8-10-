**Question 1**

KitchKraft Limited (KitchKraft) enters into a contract with a hotel to design, supply and install a fully customised commercial kitchen. KitchKraft will source ovens, refrigeration units and steel worktops from third-party manufacturers, and then perform a significant service of integrating and substantially modifying these components on-site so that they function as a single combined kitchen system. The customer contracted for one integrated commercial kitchen.

In accordance with HKFRS 15, how many performance obligations does the contract contain?

A. One — the components are not distinct within the context of the contract because KitchKraft provides a significant service of integrating them into a combined output
B. Three — the ovens, refrigeration units and steel worktops are each capable of being distinct goods
C. Four — the three components plus the installation service, because installation is a separate promise in the contract
D. Two — the supply of the components and the installation service, because goods and services are always separately identifiable

**Question 2**

TixHub Limited (TixHub) operates an online platform selling concert tickets on behalf of event organisers. For a concert with a ticket price of HK$500, TixHub collects HK$500 from each concertgoer, remits HK$460 to the organiser and retains HK$40 as its fee. The ticket prices are set by the event organisers; the organisers are responsible for staging the concert; and TixHub bears no inventory risk on unsold tickets.

In accordance with HKFRS 15, what amount of revenue should TixHub recognise for each ticket sold?

A. HK$500
B. HK$460
C. HK$40
D. Nil — no revenue is recognised because the organiser fulfils the performance obligation

**Question 3**

On 1 January 20X5, FitPeak Limited (FitPeak) sells a bundle to a customer for HK$8,100, comprising an exercise bike and a one-year maintenance plan that commences on the delivery date. FitPeak regularly sells the exercise bike separately for HK$7,200 and the maintenance plan separately for HK$1,800. Both the bike and the maintenance plan are distinct performance obligations. The bike is delivered (and control transfers) on 1 January 20X5; the maintenance service is provided evenly over the year.

In accordance with HKFRS 15, how much revenue should FitPeak recognise in January 20X5?

A. HK$675
B. HK$6,480
C. HK$6,615
D. HK$7,350

**Question 4**

On 1 April 20X5, BuildSpan Limited (BuildSpan) enters into a fixed-price contract to construct a highly customised production line directly on the customer's premises for HK$6,000,000. The customised asset has no alternative use to BuildSpan, and the contract gives BuildSpan an enforceable right to payment for performance completed to date (costs incurred plus a reasonable margin) if the customer terminates the contract. BuildSpan measures progress using an input method based on costs incurred. Total expected contract costs are HK$4,800,000. For the year ended 31 December 20X5, costs incurred to date amount to HK$1,200,000.

In accordance with HKFRS 15, how much revenue should BuildSpan recognise for the year ended 31 December 20X5?

A. HK$1,200,000
B. HK$1,500,000
C. HK$1,800,000
D. HK$2,000,000

**Question 5**

On 1 March 20X5, ServTech Limited (ServTech) receives HK$36,000 in cash from a customer for a 12-month equipment maintenance contract that commences on the same date. ServTech's reporting date is 30 June 20X5. The maintenance service is provided evenly over the contract period, and the customer simultaneously receives and consumes the benefits as ServTech performs.

In accordance with HKFRS 15, which set of journal entries correctly records the above transactions?

A. 1 March 20X5: Dr Cash 36,000 / Cr Service revenue 36,000; no entry required on 30 June 20X5
B. 1 March 20X5: Dr Cash 36,000 / Cr Contract liability 36,000; 30 June 20X5: Dr Contract liability 12,000 / Cr Service revenue 12,000
C. 1 March 20X5: Dr Contract asset 36,000 / Cr Service revenue 36,000; 30 June 20X5: Dr Cash 36,000 / Cr Contract asset 36,000
D. 1 March 20X5: Dr Cash 36,000 / Cr Contract liability 36,000; 30 June 20X5: Dr Contract liability 36,000 / Cr Service revenue 36,000
