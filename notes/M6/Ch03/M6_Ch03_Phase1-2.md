# M6 Chapter 3: Revenue Recognition — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. **Define** revenue and distinguish it from other income within the scope of HKFRS 15 *Revenue from Contracts with Customers*.
2. **Apply** the five-step revenue recognition model to identify contracts, identify performance obligations, determine the transaction price, allocate it, and recognise revenue when (or as) performance obligations are satisfied (HKFRS 15 para. 2).
3. **Determine** whether a performance obligation is satisfied over time or at a point in time, and measure progress using output or input methods (HKFRS 15 paras. 35–38, B14–B15).
4. **Account for** contract costs (costs to obtain and costs to fulfil a contract), right of return, warranties, consignments and principal–agent arrangements.
5. **Prepare** the presentation and disclosure requirements for revenue, contract balances and capitalised contract costs (HKFRS 15 paras. 113–120).

### 必背考點

#### A. Recognition（認唔認、幾時認）

**Core principle (HKFRS 15 para. 2):** recognise revenue to depict the transfer of promised goods or services to customers in an amount that reflects the consideration to which the entity expects to be entitled.

**The Five-Step Model（必背順序）:**
1. Identify the contract(s) with a customer
2. Identify the performance obligations in the contract
3. Determine the transaction price
4. Allocate the transaction price to the performance obligations
5. Recognise revenue when (or as) the performance obligation is satisfied

**Step 1 — Contract criteria (HKFRS 15 para. 9, Exhibit 3.3):** account for a contract only when ALL of:
- approved by all parties (written, oral or customary business practice — para. 10 level);
- rights to goods/services are identifiable;
- payment terms are identifiable;
- contract has commercial substance;
- it is **probable** the consideration will be collected (customer is able and intends to pay).
- A wholly unperformed contract where each party can terminate **without penalty** is NOT yet a contract (HKFRS 15 para. 12).

**Step 2 — Performance obligations (HKFRS 15 para. 22):** a promise to transfer (a) a **distinct** good or service (or bundle), or (b) a **series** of distinct goods/services that are substantially the same with the same pattern of transfer (para. 23). Administrative set-up tasks that transfer nothing are NOT performance obligations (para. 25). "Free"/marketing items ARE performance obligations if distinct (no exception for incidental goods).
- **Distinct** (HKFRS 15 paras. 27–28) — BOTH required:
  1. *Capable of being distinct*: customer can benefit from it on its own or with readily available resources (regularly sold separately = indicator);
  2. *Distinct within the context of the contract*: promise is separately identifiable from other promises.
- Indicators of NOT separately identifiable (para. 29): significant integration service; significant modification/customisation; highly interdependent/interrelated.

**Step 5 — Over time vs point in time (HKFRS 15 paras. 35–37, Exhibit 3.7):** satisfied **over time** if ANY ONE of:
1. customer simultaneously receives and consumes the benefits as the entity performs (e.g. cleaning services);
2. the entity's performance creates or enhances an asset the **customer controls** as it is created/enhanced;
3. the asset has **no alternative use** to the entity AND the entity has an **enforceable right to payment** for performance completed to date (assessed at contract inception and not changed).
- Otherwise satisfied **at a point in time** — when the customer gains **control**. Indicators (para. 38): present right to payment; legal title; physical possession; significant risks and rewards of ownership; customer acceptance.
- **Measuring progress (over time):** output methods (value transferred to customer, e.g. time elapsed — 1/12 per month) or input methods (costs incurred to date ÷ total expected costs) — HKFRS 15 paras. B14–B15.

**Specific issues:**
- **Right of return (HKFRS 15 para. B21):** recognise revenue only for consideration expected to be entitled to; recognise a **refund liability** for expected returns AND an **asset** (right to recover products) with a corresponding adjustment to cost of sales. Revenue IS still recognised on transfer for goods not expected to be returned.
- **Consignments (HKFRS 15 paras. B77–B78):** no revenue on delivery to the consignee/dealer — control has not passed. Indicators: entity controls goods until a specified event; entity can require return or redirect goods; dealer has no unconditional obligation to pay.
- **Principal vs Agent (HKFRS 15 paras. B34, B37):** an entity is a **principal** if it controls the good/service before transfer → recognise revenue **gross**; an **agent** arranges for another party to provide it → recognise revenue **net (commission)**. Indicators of control: primarily responsible for fulfilment; inventory risk; discretion in setting prices. Misidentifying an agent as a principal **overstates revenue**.

#### B. Measurement（計幾多）

**Step 3 — Transaction price (HKFRS 15 para. 47–48):** the consideration the entity expects to be entitled to, **excluding amounts collected on behalf of third parties**. Consider four effects:
1. **Variable consideration** (discounts, rebates, refunds, performance bonuses, penalties — paras. 51–54): estimate using **expected value method** (probability-weighted; many similar contracts) or **most likely amount method** (only two possible outcomes); apply the chosen method consistently throughout the contract.
2. **Significant financing component (paras. 60–65):** split into a **revenue component** (= notional cash selling price, using a discount rate from a separate financing transaction at inception) and a **loan component** (interest revenue/expense under HKFRS 9). *Practical expedient:* no adjustment if the financing period is **one year or less**. Applies even when the offer is advertised as "interest-free".
3. **Non-cash consideration:** measure at **fair value**.
4. **Consideration payable to a customer (paras. 70–73):** reduce the transaction price, UNLESS the payment is in exchange for a distinct good/service from the customer (then account as a normal purchase from a supplier).

**Step 4 — Allocation (HKFRS 15 para. 73 & 76):** allocate the transaction price to each performance obligation based on **relative stand-alone selling prices** at contract inception. Best evidence = observable price when sold separately (para. 77); list price is NOT presumed to be the SSP; estimate if not observable.
- **Bundle discount (para. 81):** allocate proportionately to ALL performance obligations, UNLESS observable evidence shows the discount relates to only one or more (but not all) of them → then allocate only to those.

**Contract costs (HKFRS 15 paras. 91–100):**
- **Costs to obtain** (paras. 91–93): capitalise if **incremental** (would not have been incurred if the contract had not been obtained, e.g. sales commission) and expected to be recovered; OR not incremental but explicitly chargeable to the customer. *Practical expedient (para. 94):* expense immediately if the amortisation period would be **one year or less**.
- **Costs to fulfil** (paras. 95–96): if within scope of another standard (HKAS 2 / HKAS 16 / HKAS 38) apply that standard; otherwise capitalise only if the costs (1) relate directly to an identifiable contract, (2) generate/enhance resources to satisfy future performance obligations, and (3) are expected to be recovered. Expense as incurred: general & administrative costs (unless explicitly chargeable), wasted materials/labour, costs relating to past performance.
- **Amortisation (paras. 99–100):** systematic basis consistent with the transfer of the related goods/services; a change in the expected timing of transfer is a **change in accounting estimate**.

**Warranties (Exhibit 3.8):**
- **Assurance-type** (warranty required by law / only assures compliance with agreed specifications, not sold separately) → account under **HKAS 37** (provision/expense); revenue recognition unaffected.
- **Service-type** (customer can buy it separately / provides a service beyond assurance) → **separate performance obligation under HKFRS 15**; allocate transaction price; recognise as **contract liability** then revenue over the warranty period.

#### C. Key Journal Entries（Dr/Cr 必須平衡）

**1. Sale with significant financing component**（cash price HK$5,800; nominal HK$6,500 payable in 24 months; 5.86%）:
```
Year 0 — on transfer of goods
Dr Receivable                     5,800
    Cr Sales revenue                        5,800
Year 1 — interest (5,800 × 5.86%)
Dr Receivable                       340
    Cr Interest revenue                       340
Year 2 — interest (6,140 × 5.86%)
Dr Receivable                       360
    Cr Interest revenue                       360
On settlement
Dr Cash                           6,500
    Cr Receivable                           6,500
```

**2. Conditional right to consideration → Contract asset**（laptops delivered before server; payment conditional）:
```
31 July — laptops transferred (control passed, right conditional)
Dr Contract asset                  50,000
    Cr Sales revenue                        50,000
31 August — server transferred; right becomes unconditional
Dr Receivable                      75,000
    Cr Contract asset                       50,000
    Cr Sales revenue                        25,000
```

**3. Capitalised incremental costs of obtaining a contract**（sales commission HK$660,000）:
```
Dr Contract acquisition costs (asset)  660,000
    Cr Cash                                        660,000
```
（Non-recoverable costs, e.g. base salaries and non-reimbursable travel → expense as incurred: Dr Salaries expense / Dr Travel expense, Cr Cash.）

**4. Sale with service-type warranty**（desktop HK$4,400 + extended warranty HK$800, cash HK$5,200 on 1 May 20X2）:
```
1 May 20X2
Dr Cash                           5,200
    Cr Sales revenue (desktop computer)             4,400
    Cr Contract liability (warranty)                  800
Over warranty period 1 May 20X3 – 30 Apr 20X5 (as service rendered)
Dr Contract liability (warranty)    800
    Cr Service revenue (warranty)                     800
```

#### D. Disclosure（披露要求）

HKFRS 15 requires entity-specific disclosures so users understand the **nature, amount, timing and uncertainty** of revenue and cash flows:
- Revenue from contracts with customers disclosed **separately** from other revenue; impairment losses (HKFRS 9) on receivables and contract assets (para. 113).
- **Disaggregation of revenue** into categories (e.g. geographical region, customer type, contract type, contract duration, timing of transfer, sales channels).
- **Contract balances (para. 116):** opening/closing balances of receivables, contract assets, contract liabilities; revenue recognised that was in the opening contract liability; revenue from performance obligations satisfied in previous periods.
- **Performance obligations (para. 119):** when typically satisfied; significant payment terms; nature of goods/services (highlight agency arrangements); obligations for returns/refunds; types of warranties.
- **Transaction price allocated to remaining performance obligations (para. 120):** aggregate amount + explanation of when it will be recognised (qualitative or quantitative time bands).
- **Significant judgements** affecting amount/timing of revenue (with HKAS 1 material accounting policy information).
- **Capitalised contract costs:** judgements, amortisation method, closing balances, amortisation and impairment losses.

### 易混淆概念對比

**Comparison 1: Assurance-type Warranty vs Service-type Warranty**

| | **Assurance-type warranty** | **Service-type warranty** |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Only assures the good complies with agreed specifications (e.g. statutory manufacturer's warranty) | Provides a service beyond assurance (e.g. purchased extended warranty) |
| Separate performance obligation? | No — bundled with the good | Yes |
| Accounting standard | HKAS 37 — warranty provision & expense | HKFRS 15 — contract liability, then revenue over warranty period |
| Effect on revenue | No effect on revenue recognition | Transaction price allocated to warranty; defers revenue |
| Total obligation | May exceed transaction price (separate HKAS 37 liability) | Limited to the transaction price of the contract |

📌 **點樣分辨：** 問自己兩條問題 — (1) 客戶可唔可以**另外買**呢個 warranty？(2) 佢有冇提供 assurance 以外嘅**額外服務**？兩條都「No」→ assurance-type → HKAS 37。**考試伏位：** 法律規定嘅 manufacturer warranty 一定係 assurance-type；題目話「extended warranty sold separately」→ service-type，記住要 allocate transaction price 同認 contract liability，唔好直接全部認做收入。

**Comparison 2: Principal vs Agent**

| | **Principal** | **Agent** |
|---|---|---|
| Nature of promise | Provides the specified goods/services itself | Arranges for another party to provide them |
| Control test | Controls the good/service **before** transfer to customer | Does NOT control before transfer |
| Indicators (HKFRS 15 para. B37) | Primarily responsible for fulfilment; bears inventory risk; discretion in setting prices | Another party fulfils; no inventory risk; principal sets the price |
| Revenue recognised | **Gross** amount of consideration | **Net** commission/fee only |
| P&L impact | Revenue and cost of sales both grossed up | Revenue = commission only; no related cost of sales |

📌 **點樣分辨：** 睇邊個**負責履行承諾**、邊個**承擔存貨風險**、邊個**有定價權**。就算 agent 負責收錢（collects cash on behalf of principal），都唔影響 agent 嘅判斷。**考試伏位：** 將 agent 錯誤當 principal 會**誇大 revenue**（net profit 一樣，但 revenue 同 expense 同時 gross up）—— 呢個正正係 examiner 最鍾意考嘅 implication。

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** State the core principle of HKFRS 15 and list, in order, the five steps of the revenue recognition model.

**Back:**
- Core principle: recognise revenue to depict the transfer of promised goods or services to customers in an amount that reflects the consideration to which the entity expects to be entitled (HKFRS 15 para. 2).
- Step 1: Identify the contract(s) with a customer
- Step 2: Identify the performance obligations in the contract
- Step 3: Determine the transaction price
- Step 4: Allocate the transaction price to the performance obligations
- Step 5: Recognise revenue when (or as) each performance obligation is satisfied

💬 白話解釋：五步曲要順序背到出嚟 — 「合約 → 履約義務 → 交易價 → 分攤 → 認收入」。考官報告話好多考生連呢個 basic outline 都寫唔出，呢 5 步係送分位。

### Card 2
**Front:** Under HKFRS 15 para. 9, what five criteria must ALL be met before a contract with a customer is accounted for under the revenue recognition principles?

**Back:**
- The contract is approved by all parties (written, oral or customary practice);
- Each party's rights regarding the goods/services are identifiable;
- Payment terms are identifiable;
- The contract has commercial substance;
- It is probable the entity will collect the consideration.
- Note: a wholly unperformed contract that either party can terminate without penalty is not yet a contract (para. 12).

💬 白話解釋：記住「批咗、權利清、付款清、有實質、收得返」五件事，缺一不可。「收得返」係 probable 層級；另外，大家都可以零代價取消嘅框架協議根本未算 contract，唔好急住認收入。

### Card 3
**Front:** When is a promised good or service "distinct" under HKFRS 15 paras. 27–28? What indicators show promises are NOT separately identifiable?

**Back:**
- BOTH criteria required:
  1. Capable of being distinct — customer can benefit from it on its own or with readily available resources;
  2. Distinct within the context of the contract — separately identifiable from other promises.
- NOT separately identifiable when: significant integration service into a combined output; significant modification/customisation; highly interdependent or interrelated (para. 29).
- Non-distinct items are combined until a distinct bundle is identified.

💬 白話解釋：兩關都要過 —「自己有用」+「喺份合約入面獨立」。賣車連引擎：引擎單獨有用，但合約承諾係「一架車」，integration service 令佢哋綁死 → 一個 performance obligation。「免費贈品」只要 distinct 都要 allocate 價錢。

### Card 4
**Front:** How is variable consideration estimated under HKFRS 15, and when is each estimation method appropriate?

**Back:**
- Two methods (paras. 51–54):
  1. **Expected value** — probability-weighted sum; appropriate for a large number of similar contracts;
  2. **Most likely amount** — single most likely outcome; appropriate when only two possible outcomes (e.g. performance bonus achieved or not).
- Use the method that better predicts the entitlement; apply it consistently throughout the contract; use all reasonably available information.

💬 白話解釋：「大量類似合約 → expected value（加權平均）；得兩個結果 → most likely amount（二選一）」。揀咗方法就要一份合約內貫徹用，唔可以中途換。

### Card 5
**Front:** How does an entity account for a significant financing component in a contract with a customer? What practical expedient is available?

**Back:**
- Split consideration into a revenue component (notional **cash selling price**, discounted at a rate reflecting a separate financing transaction at inception) and a loan component (interest revenue or interest expense under HKFRS 9).
- Entity financing the customer (payment after delivery) → interest **revenue**; customer financing the entity (payment in advance) → interest **expense**.
- Practical expedient: no adjustment if the financing period is **one year or less**.
- Applies even to arrangements advertised as "interest-free" if the contract price differs from the cash selling price.

💬 白話解釋：「零息分期」≠ 冇融資成分！只要合約價高過現金價、還款期超過一年，就要拆開 revenue（用現金價）同 interest revenue 兩部份。一年或以內可以偷懶唔拆。

### Card 6
**Front:** State the three criteria for a performance obligation satisfied OVER TIME, and the indicators that control of a good has passed for point-in-time recognition.

**Back:**
- Over time if ANY ONE (HKFRS 15 paras. 35–37):
  1. Customer simultaneously receives and consumes the benefits as the entity performs;
  2. Entity creates/enhances an asset the customer controls as it is created/enhanced;
  3. Asset has no alternative use to the entity AND the entity has an enforceable right to payment for performance completed to date.
- Point in time — control indicators (para. 38): present right to payment; legal title; physical possession; significant risks and rewards; customer acceptance.
- Progress: output methods (e.g. time elapsed) or input methods (e.g. costs incurred ÷ total expected costs) (paras. B14–B15).

💬 白話解釋：三個 over-time 條件係「ANY 一個中就得」，常見伏位：第三個條件要「no alternative use **AND** enforceable right to payment」**兩者齊全**。得返「起咗一半嘅資產冇替代用途」但冇收錢權 → 仍然係 point in time。

### Card 7
**Front:** Which costs of obtaining/fulfilling a contract are capitalised under HKFRS 15, and what practical expedient applies?

**Back:**
- Costs to obtain (paras. 91–93): capitalise **incremental** costs expected to be recovered (e.g. sales commissions), plus non-incremental costs explicitly chargeable to the customer.
- Practical expedient (para. 94): expense immediately if the amortisation period would be one year or less — this is an accounting **policy choice**.
- Costs to fulfil (paras. 95–96): apply other standards first (HKAS 2/16/38); otherwise capitalise only if costs relate directly to the contract, generate/enhance resources for future performance obligations, and are recoverable.
- Expense as incurred: general & admin costs (unless chargeable), wasted materials/labour, costs relating to past performance.
- Amortisation: systematic, consistent with transfer of related goods/services; changes = change in accounting estimate (paras. 99–100).

💬 白話解釋：commission = incremental cost，要 capitalize（唔係即時 expense，更加唔係扣 transaction price）。但攤銷期 ≤1 年可以揀即時 expense — 呢個係 policy choice，MCQ 好鍾意考「must capitalise / must expense」呢類絕對字眼，其實答案係「有得揀」。

### Card 8
**Front:** How is a sale with a right of return accounted for under HKFRS 15 para. B21?

**Back:**
- Recognise revenue only for the consideration the entity expects to be entitled to (no revenue for products expected to be returned);
- Recognise a **refund liability** for amounts received/receivable for expected returns;
- Recognise an **asset** for the right to recover products on settling the refund liability, with a corresponding adjustment to cost of sales.
- Portfolio approach may be applied if results would not differ materially.

💬 白話解釋：賣 100 部機預計退 8 部 → 92 部認 revenue，8 部認 refund liability + recoverable asset。伏位：唔係「等退貨期完先全部認收入」— 貨嘅 control 一過戶，預計唔會退嗰部份就要即刻認收入。

## 本章急救詞彙

| EN Term | 繁中白話 | English Model Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Performance obligation | 合約入面承諾俾客戶嘅貨或服務 | Each distinct promised good or service is identified as a separate performance obligation. |
| Transaction price | 預期有權收取嘅代價（唔計代第三方收嘅錢） | The transaction price excludes amounts collected on behalf of third parties, such as sales taxes. |
| Contract asset | 已轉移貨品但收款權附帶其他條件 | A contract asset arises when the right to consideration is conditional on something other than the passage of time. |
| Contract liability | 收咗錢但仲欠客戶貨或服務 | Cash received before the service is rendered is recognised as a contract liability. |
| Refund liability | 預計要退返俾客戶嘅款項 | For sales with a right of return, a refund liability is recognised for the expected returns. |
| Stand-alone selling price (SSP) | 單獨出售嘅價錢，用嚟分攤交易價 | The transaction price is allocated in proportion to the relative stand-alone selling prices. |
| Significant financing component | 付款時間造成嘅重大融資成分 | Where payment is deferred for two years, the contract contains a significant financing component. |
| Incremental costs of obtaining a contract | 唔攞到份約就唔會使嘅成本（如佣金） | Sales commissions are incremental costs of obtaining a contract and are capitalised if recoverable. |
