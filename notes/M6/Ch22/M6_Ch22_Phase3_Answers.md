# M6 Chapter 22: Business Combinations — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** A business combination requires that (i) a **business** is acquired and (ii) the acquirer obtains **control** of it. A business is an integrated set of activities and assets comprising at least an **input** and a **substantive process** that together significantly contribute to the ability to create outputs. In C, Heron acquires the bakery's net assets (inputs) together with a trained workforce performing production processes critical to creating outputs (a substantive process), and by acquiring all net assets it controls the business — this is a business combination effected by acquiring underlying net assets.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- A 錯：一架貨車只係單一資產，唔係「integrated set of activities and assets」，屬資產購置（HKAS 16 範圍）。
- B 錯：新註冊空殼公司冇 input、冇 process、冇 output，唔具備 substantive process，所以唔係 business（LP Knowledge Check Q2(d) 同款邏輯）。
- D 錯：25% 持股只得 significant influence（HKAS 28 associate），**冇 control**，所以唔係 business combination。
- C 啱：inputs（設備、舖位）+ substantive process（熟練烘焙團隊嘅生產流程）+ 控制權，三樣齊。
- 考試伏位：「有 workforce」係 substantive process 嘅 indicator，但要睇佢係咪 **critical to creating outputs**；back-office 行政系統一般唔算。

**Rule Check:** HKFRS 3 (Revised) App A (definitions of "business combination" and "business"); HKFRS 3(R).B7, B8, B12A, B12D; HKFRS 3(R).7 (control requirement).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 以為「買公司 = 一定係 business combination」。其實要過兩關：買嘅係咪 business（空殼唔係），同埋有冇攞到 control（25% 冇）。

### Q2 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):**
```
Consideration transferred:                                   HK$
Cash consideration                                       3,000,000
Share consideration (200,000 shares × HK$16 — FV at
  acquisition date, 31 March 20X5)                         3,200,000
Contingent consideration (acquisition-date fair value)       350,000
                                                         6,550,000
```
Legal fees (HK$90,000) are acquisition-related costs expensed in profit or loss as incurred; share issue costs (HK$30,000) are a deduction from equity under HKAS 32. Neither forms part of the consideration transferred.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- 關鍵一：**股代價用 acquisition date（31 March，即 control passes 嗰日）股價 HK$16**，唔係簽約日（1 March）嘅 HK$14。
- 關鍵二：contingent consideration 用 **acquisition-date fair value HK$350,000**，唔係最高可能付嘅 HK$500,000。
- 關鍵三：legal fees 同 share issue costs 都係 **acquisition-related / issue costs**，一個落 P&L、一個扣 equity，**兩個都唔入 consideration**。
- A 錯：用咗簽約日股價 HK$14（3,000,000 + 2,800,000 + 350,000）。
- C 錯：將 legal fees 同 share issue costs 加埋入 consideration（6,550,000 + 90,000 + 30,000）。
- D 錯：contingent consideration 用 full HK$500,000（3,000,000 + 3,200,000 + 500,000）。

**Rule Check:** HKFRS 3(R).37 (consideration at acquisition-date fair values); HKFRS 3(R).39 (contingent consideration at acquisition-date FV); HKFRS 3(R).59 (acquisition-related costs expensed); HKAS 32 (share issue costs deducted from equity).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 三個日期（簽約、控制轉移、結算）擺埋一齊考你——鎖死「**control passes 嗰日**」嘅股價。另外見到「付咗啲 fees」就想加落代價，係最高頻失分位。

### Q3 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** Although HKAS 37 prohibits the acquiree from recognising contingent liabilities, HKFRS 3 (Revised) contains a **recognition exception**: in a business combination, the acquirer must recognise a contingent liability assumed if it is a **present obligation arising from past events** and its **fair value can be measured reliably**, measured at its **acquisition-date fair value** (HK$180,000). The fact that an outflow is not probable does not prevent recognition by the acquirer.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- 核心考點：business combination 入面，acquirer 確認標準**闊過** HKAS 37 ——唔需要「probable outflow」，只要是 present obligation + FV reliably measurable。
- A 錯：HKAS 37 嘅 prohibition 只約束 acquiree 自己嘅財報；HKFRS 3(R).22–23 明確係 exception。
- B 錯：acquirer 要獨立評估確認條件，唔可以照抄 acquiree 嘅處理（LP Key Learning Point §22.3.4.1）。
- D 錯：「等到 probable 先確認」係把 HKAS 37 邏輯錯誤搬入收購會計；確認時點係 acquisition date。
- 考試伏位：題目強調「not probable」係煙幕——呢個正正係 exception 存在嘅原因；但如果係 **possible obligation**（連存在都唔確定），就真係唔確認。

**Rule Check:** HKFRS 3(R).22–23 (contingent liabilities recognised at acquisition-date FV if present obligation and FV reliably measurable); HKAS 37 (contingent liabilities not recognised by the acquiree).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 背咗 HKAS 37「contingent liability 唔確認」就直情套用，忘記 HKFRS 3 有 recognition exception。兩條準則嘅分工要分清：acquiree 自己財報 vs acquirer 收購會計。

### Q4 — Answer: HK$3,600,000
**Model Answer (EN):**

Step 1 — Fair value of identifiable net assets at acquisition date:
```
                                                        HK$
Carrying amount of recognised net assets             7,200,000
Add: FV of unrecognised trademark                      600,000
Add: FV uplift on property, plant and equipment        400,000
Less: FV of contingent liability assumed              (200,000)
                                                     8,000,000
```

Step 2 — Goodwill (full goodwill method, NCI at fair value):
```
                                                        HK$
Consideration transferred                              9,000,000
Add: NCI at fair value                                 2,600,000
                                                      11,600,000
Less: FV of identifiable net assets                   (8,000,000)
Goodwill                                               3,600,000
```
(For reference: under the partial goodwill method, NCI = 25% × HK$8,000,000 = HK$2,000,000 and goodwill = 9,000,000 + 2,000,000 − 8,000,000 = HK$3,000,000. The HK$600,000 difference is the goodwill attributable to the NCI.)

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- 先執 identifiable net assets：**unrecognised trademark 要加**（separability/contractual-legal criterion 通過就得，acquiree 冇確認唔緊要）；**contingent liability 要減**（HKFRS 3(R).22–23 exception）。
- NCI 題目講明 **at fair value → full goodwill method**，直接用 HK$2,600,000，唔好用 25% × net assets。
- 常見錯：(1) 用 partial goodwill 計出 HK$3,000,000；(2) 忘記確認 contingent liability，net assets 計錯做 8,200,000 → goodwill 3,400,000；(3) 漏咗 trademark。
- 計 goodwill 公式要記實：consideration + NCI − FV of identifiable net assets。

**Rule Check:** HKFRS 3(R).32 (goodwill measured as a residual); HKFRS 3(R).19 (NCI at fair value option — full goodwill); HKFRS 3(R).10, 18 (recognition and FV measurement of identifiable net assets); HKFRS 3(R).22–23 (contingent liability); HKFRS 3(R).B31–B34 (identifiable intangible asset).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 題目同時畀「FV of NCI」同「NCI%」，係引你用錯方法。題目講明 NCI at fair value 就一定用 FV 嗰個數；只有講「proportionate share」先至用百分比乘 identifiable net assets。

### Q5 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):**
```
Dr  Investment in subsidiary              6,800,000
    Cr  Cash                                              4,000,000
    Cr  Share capital (100,000 × HK$1)                      100,000
    Cr  Share premium (100,000 × HK$24)                   2,400,000
    Cr  Contingent consideration liability                  300,000

Dr  Administrative expenses (P&L)            80,000
    Cr  Cash                                                   80,000
```
Investment in subsidiary = consideration transferred = 4,000,000 + (100,000 × HK$25) + 300,000 = HK$6,800,000. Shares issued are credited at nominal value to share capital with the excess to share premium. Legal fees are acquisition-related costs expensed as incurred (HKFRS 3(R).59), not capitalised. Each entry balances: Dr 6,800,000 = Cr 6,800,000; Dr 80,000 = Cr 80,000.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- 三步檢查：(1) investment 入賬金額 = total consideration（唔包 legal fees）；(2) 股份要拆 **nominal → share capital、溢價 → share premium**；(3) contingent consideration 以 FV 確認為 liability。
- A 錯：legal fees 資本化埋入 investment（6,880,000），而且 share capital 用咗 FV HK$2,500,000 全額入賬、冇拆 premium。
- C 錯：漏咗 contingent consideration（investment 只有 6,500,000）。
- D 錯：雖然 investment 金額同 expense 啱，但 share capital 成筆用 FV 入賬（2,500,000），冇分出 share premium——股本只可以按 nominal value 入賬。
- 考試伏位：CBE journal-entry grid 最興考「Dr/Cr 戶口名 + 金額」配對——share capital 永遠係 nominal value！

**Rule Check:** HKFRS 3(R).37 (consideration at acquisition-date FV, including equity interests issued); HKFRS 3(R).39 (contingent consideration); HKFRS 3(R).59 (acquisition-related costs expensed); HKAS 32 (equity issued at nominal value; issue costs as equity deduction).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 兩大伏：(1) 將 acquisition-related costs 加落 investment cost（HKFRS 3 明令禁止，連 separate FS 都唔准）；(2) share capital 按 fair value 入賬。記住：**Share capital = nominal，Share premium = FV − nominal**。
