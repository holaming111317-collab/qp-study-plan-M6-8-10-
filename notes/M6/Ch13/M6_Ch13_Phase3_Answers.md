# M6 Chapter 13: Accounting Policies, Changes in Accounting Estimates and Errors — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** B is correct. A change in the inventory cost formula (FIFO → weighted average) is a change in the measurement basis applied, which is a change in accounting policy under HKAS 8 (HKAS 8 para. 35; the LP's Illustrative Example 2 treats exactly this change as a voluntary change in accounting policy).
- A is a change in accounting estimate — the warranty percentage is a monetary amount subject to measurement uncertainty, revised due to new information (HKAS 8 paras. 32–34).
- C is NOT a change in accounting policy — applying a new policy to transactions that did not occur previously or were immaterial is excluded by HKAS 8 para. 16.
- D is NOT a change in accounting policy — applying a policy to transactions that differ in substance from those previously occurring is excluded by HKAS 8 para. 16.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 關鍵係分清楚「把尺」（measurement basis / 原則做法）同「讀數」（估計金額）。B 改成本公式＝換把尺＝policy change。A 改保養百分比只係估計修訂，屬 estimate change。C 同 D 係 HKAS 8 para. 16 明文講明「唔算改 policy」嘅兩種情況：以前冇發生/唔重要嘅交易第一次用新政策，同埋實質唔同嘅交易用唔同政策。考試伏位：見到「HKFRS 16 first-time application to newly material leases」唔好即刻當係 policy change。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 8 paras. 5, 16, 35

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 考生成日將「新交易類型用現行政策」或者「以前 immaterial 依家 material 嘅交易改用新準則」誤當 change in accounting policy，因而錯誤咁要求 retrospective restatement。

### Q2 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** B is correct. A voluntary change in accounting policy must be applied retrospectively: the new policy is applied as if it had always been applied, adjusting the opening balance of each affected component of equity (e.g. retained earnings) for the earliest prior period presented and the other comparative amounts disclosed for each prior period presented, subject to practicability (HKAS 8 paras. 19(b), 22–24).
- A is wrong — prospective application applies to changes in accounting estimates, not voluntary policy changes.
- C is wrong — a change in inventory cost formula is a policy change (change in measurement basis), not an estimate change, and the cumulative effect is adjusted against opening equity, not current profit or loss.
- D is wrong — transitional provisions are relevant only for mandatory changes required by the initial application of a HKFRS (HKAS 8 para. 19(a)); this change is voluntary and HKAS 2 contains no transitional provisions for a voluntary switch of cost formula.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 第一步判斷 mandatory 定 voluntary——呢度係自願改，所以冇 transitional provisions 可以跟，直接 retrospective。Retrospective 嘅標準操作：最早呈報比較期間嘅期初 retained earnings 調整累計影響，每個呈報嘅前期 comparative 全部重報，以 practicable 為限。A 同 C 係「estimate change」嘅處理方法，係最強 distractor；D 混淆咗 mandatory change 先會用到嘅 transitional provisions 概念。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 8 paras. 19, 22, 23–24

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 以為所有改變都係 prospective。記住口訣：Policy change 同 error correction → retrospective（向後改）；Estimate change → prospective（向前行）。

### Q3 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** B is correct. The revision from 2% to 3% of sales is a change in accounting estimate recognised prospectively in profit or loss of the current period (HKAS 8 paras. 36–38). Warranty expense for 20X4 = 3% × HK$5,000,000 = HK$150,000:

| Account | Dr (HK$) | Cr (HK$) |
|---|---|---|
| Warranty expense | 150,000 | |
| Warranty provision | | 150,000 |

- A is the entry for warranty claims **paid** in cash (Dr Warranty provision / Cr Cash), not the expense accrual.
- C wrongly charges the estimate change to retained earnings — a change in estimate never adjusts prior periods or opening equity; only retrospective items (policy changes, error corrections) touch opening retained earnings.
- D uses the superseded 2% estimate (HK$100,000) instead of the revised 3%.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題考兩層：(1) 保養百分比修訂 = estimate change，prospective，所以用新比率 3% 落今年 P&L；(2) 分錄方向——計提保養費用係 Dr Warranty expense / Cr Warranty provision。A 係「賠咗錢」嘅分錄（用撥備找數），唔係計提；C 係 retrospective item 先會用嘅 retained earnings 路徑；D 用舊比率。Balanced check：Dr 150,000 = Cr 150,000 ✓。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 8 paras. 34, 36–38

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 誤將 estimate change 當 error 處理而借記 retained earnings。記住：只有 policy change 同 prior period error correction 先會掂 opening retained earnings；estimate change 永遠行 P&L。

### Q4 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** C is correct — HK$390,000. The revision of useful life and residual value is a change in accounting estimate applied prospectively (HKAS 8 paras. 36–38).

Workings:
- Original annual depreciation = (HK$1,800,000 − HK$120,000) ÷ 8 = HK$210,000
- Accumulated depreciation at 31 December 20X3 = 3 years × HK$210,000 = HK$630,000
- Carrying amount at 1 January 20X4 = HK$1,800,000 − HK$630,000 = HK$1,170,000
- Revised depreciation = (HK$1,170,000 − HK$0) ÷ 3 = **HK$390,000** per year for 20X4–20X6

Distractor analysis: A (HK$210,000) ignores the revision entirely; B (HK$350,000) wrongly deducts the superseded residual value of HK$120,000 from the carrying amount; D (HK$600,000) wrongly spreads the original cost over 3 years, ignoring accumulated depreciation already charged.

**解題思路（繁中）:** Prospective 嘅核心做法：用「修訂日嘅 carrying amount」減「新 residual value」，再除以「新剩餘年期」。最常見錯誤：用返 cost（1,800,000）而唔係 carrying amount（1,170,000）做起點——即係 D；或者繼續扣舊 residual value——即係 B；或者根本冇更新——即係 A。Jun 2025 考官報告特別提到考生識計數但唔識解釋呢個係 prospective treatment，所以答題時要講明「change in accounting estimate, applied prospectively」。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 8 paras. 34, 36–38

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 忘記 estimate change 唔 restate 以前年度——20X1–20X3 嘅折舊維持每年 HK$210,000，唔准返轉頭改；修訂只影響 20X4 起嘅折舊。

### Q5 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** B is correct — a decrease of HK$668,000. The counting error is a material prior period error corrected by retrospective restatement (HKAS 8 para. 42): the 20X4 comparatives are restated in the 20X5 financial statements as if the error had never occurred.

Workings:
- Closing inventory overstated by HK$800,000 → cost of goods sold understated and profit overstated by HK$800,000 in 20X4.
- Tax effect = HK$800,000 × 16.5% = HK$132,000 (income tax expense and income tax payable restated downward).
- Net-of-tax reduction in retained earnings as at 31 December 20X4 = HK$800,000 − HK$132,000 = **HK$668,000**.

Correcting journal entry (balanced: Dr total 800,000 = Cr 800,000):

| Account | Dr (HK$) | Cr (HK$) |
|---|---|---|
| Retained earnings | 668,000 | |
| Income tax payable | 132,000 | |
| Inventories | | 800,000 |

- A ignores the tax effect.
- C deducts only the tax amount.
- D is wrong — material prior period errors are never corrected prospectively; retrospective restatement is mandatory (HKAS 8 paras. 41–42).

**解題思路（繁中）:** Prior period error 修正三步：(1) 判斷方向——closing inventory 高估 → 20X4 COGS 低估 → profit 高估 → retained earnings 要**下調**；(2) 計稅務影響——LP 所有例子都用 16.5%，稅隨利潤跌而跌，所以淨影響 = 800,000 × 83.5% = 668,000；(3) 記住 retained earnings 永遠用 **net of tax** 金額調整。A 忘記稅；C 本末倒置；D 用錯 prospective——error correction 係 retrospective restatement，冇得揀。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 8 paras. 41–42, 46

**Common Trap（繁中）:** (1) 調整 retained earnings 時忘記扣稅效應；(2) 方向搞錯——inventory 高估代表 profit 高估，要**減**唔係加；(3) 以為 error 可以 prospective 處理——material prior period error 必須 retrospective restatement，呢個係 HKAS 8 嘅硬性規定。
