**Question 1 (MCQ)**

In accordance with HKFRS 10, Heron Limited owns 75% of Swift Limited. When determining the non-controlling interest's share of Swift Limited's profit for the year, which ONE of the following intra-group transactions requires an adjustment to Swift Limited's **reported** profit?

A. Heron Limited sold inventory to Swift Limited during the year, and 40% of that inventory remains unsold at the reporting date.
B. Swift Limited sold inventory to Heron Limited during the year, and 40% of that inventory remains unsold at the reporting date.
C. Heron Limited provided management services to Swift Limited for a fee of HK$100,000, fully settled in cash before the reporting date.
D. Swift Limited lent HK$500,000 to Heron Limited, on which both entities recorded interest of HK$25,000 for the year.

**Question 2 (Calculation)**

Puma Limited acquired 75% of Cub Limited on 1 July 20X3. Cub Limited's reported profit for the year ended 30 June 20X5 is HK$800,000. The following matters are relevant:

- At acquisition, the fair value of a plant held by Cub Limited exceeded its carrying amount by HK$120,000. The plant had a remaining useful life of eight years from acquisition, nil residual value, and is depreciated straight-line. No adjustment for this has yet been made to Cub Limited's reported profit.
- During the year ended 30 June 20X5, Cub Limited sold inventory costing HK$200,000 to Puma Limited for HK$250,000. At 30 June 20X5, 40% of that inventory remained on hand in Puma Limited's warehouse.

Calculate the non-controlling interest's share of Cub Limited's profit for the year ended 30 June 20X5.

**Question 3 (Calculation / MCQ)**

Wren Limited's equity at the acquisition date comprised share capital of HK$1,000,000 and retained earnings of HK$1,100,000. On that date, the fair value of Wren Limited's equipment exceeded its carrying amount by HK$150,000; the fair values of all other identifiable assets and liabilities equalled their carrying amounts and met the measurement requirements of HKFRS 3 (Revised). Falcon Limited measures the non-controlling interest at its proportionate share of the recognised amounts of Wren Limited's identifiable net assets.

In accordance with HKFRS 3 (Revised), what is the goodwill arising on the acquisition of Wren Limited?

A. HK$200,000
B. HK$280,000
C. HK$320,000
D. HK$350,000

**Question 5 (Calculation / MCQ)**

At the acquisition date, Mink Limited's share capital was HK$2,000,000 and its retained earnings were HK$1,500,000; the fair value of a building held by Mink Limited exceeded its carrying amount by HK$400,000 (remaining useful life 10 years, straight-line, nil residual value). The fair values of all other identifiable net assets equalled their carrying amounts. Lutra Limited measures the non-controlling interest at its proportionate share of the recognised amounts of Mink Limited's identifiable net assets.

At 31 December 20X6, Mink Limited's retained earnings in its own financial statements are HK$2,100,000, after declaring a dividend of HK$200,000 during the year ended 31 December 20X6. There have been no movements in Mink Limited's other equity balances since acquisition and no intra-group trading.

In accordance with HKFRS 10, what is the balance of the non-controlling interest in equity in Lutra Limited's consolidated statement of financial position at 31 December 20X6?

A. HK$836,000
B. HK$876,000
C. HK$892,000
D. HK$900,000
