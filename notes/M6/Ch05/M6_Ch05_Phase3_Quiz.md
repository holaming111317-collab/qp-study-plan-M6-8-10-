### Q1
Heron Manufacturing Limited purchased a machine for its Kwun Tong factory and incurred the following costs:

| Item | HK$ |
|---|---|
| List price of machine (before trade discount) | 500,000 |
| Trade discount (5% of list price) | (25,000) |
| Import duty (non-refundable) | 8,000 |
| Value Added Tax on purchase (fully refundable) | 12,000 |
| Delivery and installation | 20,000 |
| Testing whether the machine functions properly | 6,000 |
| Staff training on operating the machine | 9,000 |
| Costs of the ceremony to celebrate the machine's commissioning | 4,000 |

In accordance with HKAS 16, what is the cost of the machine on initial recognition?

A. HK$521,000
B. HK$534,000
C. HK$509,000
D. HK$530,000

### Q2
Otter Logistics Limited has just finished assembling a new sorting machine at its depot. The machine was in the location and condition necessary for it to be capable of operating in the manner intended by management on 1 October 20X4, but management decided not to start using it until 1 January 20X5 because demand was seasonally low. Identify which one of the following statements is correct under HKAS 16.

A. Depreciation of the machine commences on 1 January 20X5, the date the machine is first used in operations.
B. Depreciation of the machine commences on 1 October 20X4, even though the machine has not yet been used.
C. Depreciation of the machine is not required because the machine is temporarily idle, and resumes only when it is used at full capacity.
D. Depreciation of the machine ceases automatically whenever the machine is idle for more than one month.

### Q3
On 1 April 20X4, Falcon Freight Limited acquired a delivery truck for HK$480,000. The truck has an estimated residual value of HK$60,000 and an estimated useful life of six years. The entity applies the straight-line method of depreciation, charges depreciation on a monthly basis, and has a 31 December year-end.

Calculate the depreciation expense for the truck for the year ended 31 December 20X4.

A. HK$52,500
B. HK$70,000
C. HK$60,000
D. HK$80,000

### Q4
Pelican Engineering Limited carries its equipment under the revaluation model and eliminates accumulated depreciation against the gross carrying amount when an asset is revalued (the net method). At 31 December 20X4, an item of equipment has a gross carrying amount of HK$400,000, accumulated depreciation of HK$100,000 (i.e. carrying amount of HK$300,000), and a fair value of HK$360,000. The equipment has never been revalued before.

In accordance with HKAS 16, which set of journal entries correctly records the revaluation at 31 December 20X4?

A. Dr Equipment HK$60,000; Cr Revaluation surplus (OCI) HK$60,000.
B. Dr Accumulated depreciation HK$100,000; Cr Equipment HK$100,000; then Dr Equipment HK$60,000; Cr Gain on revaluation (profit or loss) HK$60,000.
C. Dr Revaluation loss (profit or loss) HK$40,000; Cr Equipment HK$40,000.
D. Dr Accumulated depreciation HK$100,000; Cr Equipment HK$100,000; then Dr Equipment HK$60,000; Cr Revaluation surplus (OCI) HK$60,000.

### Q5
Seahorse Properties Limited carries its office buildings under the revaluation model. A particular office building has a carrying amount of HK$4,200,000 at 31 December 20X4, immediately before a revaluation. The revaluation surplus in equity includes a credit balance of HK$300,000 arising from a previous revaluation increase of this same building. An independent valuer has determined the building's fair value at 31 December 20X4 to be HK$3,700,000.

In accordance with HKAS 16, how should the revaluation of HK$500,000 be accounted for?

A. Recognise the entire HK$500,000 as a loss in profit or loss.
B. Debit HK$500,000 to the revaluation surplus through other comprehensive income.
C. Debit HK$300,000 to the revaluation surplus through other comprehensive income and recognise HK$200,000 as a loss in profit or loss.
D. Recognise HK$300,000 as a loss in profit or loss and debit HK$200,000 to the revaluation surplus through other comprehensive income.
