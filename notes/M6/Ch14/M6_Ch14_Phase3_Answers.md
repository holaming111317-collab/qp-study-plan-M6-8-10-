# M6 Chapter 14: Events after the Reporting Period — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** Under HKAS 10, events after the reporting period are those that occur between the end of the reporting period and the date when the financial statements are **authorised for issue**. For Harbour Lighting, the window runs from 31 December 20X7 to **12 March 20X8**, the date the board of directors authorises the financial statements for issue. The upload date (20 March), the filing with the Companies Registry (2 April) and shareholder approval at the AGM (28 May) are all irrelevant to fixing the authorisation date.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題考 HKAS 10 時間窗嘅終點。時間窗 = 年結日 → authorised for issue 嗰日。Board of directors 授權刊發嗰日先係 authorisation date。A 錯：18 Feb 只係完成草稿，報表仍未 ready to be authorised。C 錯：交 Companies Registry 係 administrative filing，同 authorisation 無關。D 錯：shareholders 喺 AGM approve 唔等於 authorisation for issue；除非股東有權喺刊發後修改報表（咁要先披露），否則 AGM 日期完全唔用。考試伏位：題目特登俾晒五個日期，咪俾「最後嗰個日期」或者「最正式嗰個程序」呃到。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 10.3

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 以為 shareholder approval 先係終點。HKAS 10 嘅 window 喺 board authorises for issue 嗰刻已經關閉；股東批准日之後發生嘅事完全喺 scope 之外。

### Q2 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** The discovery of fraud (deliberate overstatement of revenue) is an **adjusting event** under HKAS 10.9 — the discovery of fraud or errors that show the financial statements are incorrect provides evidence of conditions that existed at the end of the reporting period (30 September 20X6). Velvet Curtain must adjust the amounts recognised in its 20X6 financial statements (HKAS 10.8). Options A (major business combination), C (announcing a plan to discontinue an operation) and D (a major share issue) are all examples of **non-adjusting events** listed in HKAS 10.22 — they reflect conditions that arose after the reporting period.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 分辨關鍵：個 condition 喺年結日存唔存在？B 嘅收入造假係喺 20X6 年度內發生嘅，audit 只係「發現」佢——discovery of fraud or errors 係 HKAS 10.9 明文嘅 adjusting example。A、C、D 全部係 HKAS 10.22 嘅 non-adjusting examples：acquisition、discontinuation plan、share issue 都係年結後先出現嘅新情況，再大單都唔可以調整，最多做 disclosure。考試伏位：唔好見到「大件事/影響大」就覺得要 adjust——materiality 只決定 non-adjusting event 使唔使披露，唔會將佢變成 adjusting。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 10.3(a), 10.8, 10.9; HKAS 10.22

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 將「事件被發現/發生嘅日期」當成分類標準。正確問題係：件事反映嘅 underlying condition 喺年結日存在嗎？Fraud 發生喺年內 → adjusting；announcement 同 issue 先係年後嘅新嘢 → non-adjusting。

### Q3 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** A dividend declared after the reporting period is a **non-adjusting event** (HKAS 10.12–13): the entity had no obligation to pay the dividend at 31 December 20X7, so **no liability is recognised** at the reporting date. Under HKAS 1.137, Marble Fountain must disclose in the notes the amount of dividends proposed or declared before the financial statements were authorised for issue but not recognised as a distribution to owners during the period — here **HK$2,400,000** (3,000,000 shares × HK$0.80) — and the **related amount per share (HK$0.80)**.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題係 dividends declared 嘅標準考法。A 錯：雖然宣派發生喺 authorisation date 之前，但年結日（31 Dec 20X7）公司根本無 obligation，所以唔可以 confirm 做負債。B 錯：股息唔屬於 20X7 年度嘅 distribution，唔可以喺 20X7 嘅 SOCIE 扣 retained earnings——佢會喺 20X8 先確認。D 錯：雖然唔 adjust，但因為宣派發生喺 authorisation 之前，HKAS 1.137 要求 notes 披露總額同每股金額，唔可以隻字不提。計數：3,000,000 × $0.80 = $2,400,000。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 10.12–13; HKAS 1.137

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 見到「declared before authorisation for issue」就以為可以入賬做 liability。「宣派」先係製造 obligation 嘅動作——宣派之前無 obligation，所以永遠唔會係年結負債。

### Q4 — Answer: Write-down of HK$264,000; Dr Loss on inventory write down 264,000 / Cr Finished goods inventory 264,000
**Model Answer (EN):**

(a) **Classification:** Adjusting event. The competitor's product launch in May 20X8 (before the reporting date) had already reduced demand for the model at 30 June 20X8; the post-year-end clearance sale merely provides evidence of the **net realisable value** of the inventories at the end of the reporting period (HKAS 10.3(a), 10.8, 10.9(b)(ii)).

**Workings:**
- NRV = (4,000 units × HK$180) − HK$24,000 = HK$720,000 − HK$24,000 = **HK$696,000**
- Write-down = Cost − NRV = HK$960,000 − HK$696,000 = **HK$264,000**

(b) **Journal entry as at 30 June 20X8:**

| Account | Dr HK$ | Cr HK$ |
|---|---|---|
| Loss on inventory write down | 264,000 | |
| Finished goods inventory | | 264,000 |

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題嘅伏位喺個日期：clearance sale 係 20 July（年結後），所以好多同學會當佢 non-adjusting。但題目特登講明 demand decline 係由 **May 20X8**（年結前）嘅 competitor launch 引起——即係話 NRV 低於成本呢個 condition 喺 30 June 已經存在，7 月嘅平賣只係提供證據。NRV 記得要扣 selling costs（$180 × 4,000 − $24,000 = $696,000），唔係淨係用售價。分錄要平衡：Dr 損失 / Cr 存貨 $264,000。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 10.3(a), 10.8, 10.9(b)(ii)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** (1) 見到「年結後先賣」就判 non-adjusting——要睇減值嘅原因幾時出現；(2) 計 NRV 唔記得扣 selling costs，變咗 write-down $240,000；(3) 分錯方向，將存貨放 Debit。

### Q5 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** After the reporting period, management determined that it must liquidate the entity — the going concern assumption is no longer appropriate. Under HKAS 10.14, Coral Island **shall not prepare its financial statements on a going concern basis**. Because the inability to continue as a going concern has a **pervasive effect** on the entity's financial position, HKAS 10.15 requires a **fundamental change in the basis of accounting** (a new basis of preparation), rather than adjustments to amounts recognised within the original going concern basis. The disclosures required by HKAS 1 (HKAS 1.25) must also be given. Note that this outcome applies even though the typhoon is a non-adjusting event (a condition arising after the reporting period).

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題測 going concern override。A 錯：雖然 typhoon 本身係 non-adjusting event，但管理層已決定清盤——going concern 一失效就唔可以只係披露。B 錯：HKAS 10.15 講明，影響係 pervasive 嘅話，唔係「調整某幾個數」而係 fundamental change in the basis of accounting，成個 basis 都要換。D 錯：雙重錯誤——（1）繼續用 going concern basis 違反 HKAS 10.14；（2）material non-adjusting event 一定要喺 20X8 報表 notes 披露，唔可以無披露。考試伏位：non-adjusting event 唔一定「只係披露」——如果佢令 going concern 唔成立，後果係改變編製基礎。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 10.14, 10.15; HKAS 1.25

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 以為「事件年結後發生 = non-adjusting = 只係披露」呢條公式適用於所有情況。Going concern 係例外：無論觸發原因係 adjusting 定 non-adjusting，只要管理層 intend to liquidate / cease trading，就唔可以用 going concern basis。
