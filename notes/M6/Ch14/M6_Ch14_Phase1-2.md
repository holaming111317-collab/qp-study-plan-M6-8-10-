# M6 Chapter 14: Events after the Reporting Period — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives

1. **Define** "events after the reporting period" and identify the time window to which HKAS 10 applies.
2. **Distinguish** between adjusting and non-adjusting events after the reporting period.
3. **Apply** the correct accounting treatment (adjust vs do not adjust; going concern basis) to events after the reporting period.
4. **Prepare** the required disclosures in respect of events after the reporting period, including the date of authorisation for issue and material non-adjusting events.

### 必背考點

#### A. Recognition（認唔認入賬）

- **Scope of HKAS 10 (HKAS 10.3):** Events after the reporting period are those **favourable or unfavourable events** that occur **between the end of the reporting period and the date when the financial statements are authorised for issue**. Events outside this window are beyond the scope of HKAS 10.
- **"Authorised for issue" is not defined in HKAS 10** — it depends on the entity's authorisation process. If shareholders have the power to amend the financial statements after issue, that fact must be disclosed. The authorisation date is when the board/management authorises issue — **not** the date the statements are posted on a website, filed with the Companies Registry, or approved by shareholders at the AGM.
- **Adjusting events (HKAS 10.3(a)):** Events that provide **evidence of conditions that existed at the end of the reporting period**. The entity **shall adjust** the amounts recognised in its financial statements (HKAS 10.8) — either adjusting an existing balance or recognising an amount not previously recognised.
- **Non-adjusting events (HKAS 10.3(b)):** Events that are **indicative of conditions that arose after the end of the reporting period**. The entity **shall not adjust** the amounts recognised (HKAS 10.10); the accounting consequences are recognised in the reporting period in which they occur. If **material**, disclosure in the notes is required (HKAS 10.21).
- **Going concern override (HKAS 10.14–15):** If management determines after the reporting period that it intends to liquidate the entity or cease trading (or has no realistic alternative), the entity **shall not prepare its financial statements on a going concern basis**. This applies whether the deterioration stems from an adjusting or a non-adjusting event.

**Examples of adjusting events (HKAS 10.9):**
- Settlement after the reporting period of a court case that confirms the entity had a **present obligation** at the end of the reporting period.
- **Bankruptcy of a customer** after the reporting period — usually confirms the customer was **credit-impaired** at the end of the reporting period.
- **Sale of inventories below cost** after the reporting period — may give evidence about their **net realisable value** at the end of the reporting period (HKAS 10.9(b)(ii)).
- Determination after the reporting period of the **cost of assets purchased / proceeds of assets sold** before the end of the reporting period.
- Discovery of **fraud or errors** showing the financial statements are incorrect.

**Examples of non-adjusting events (HKAS 10.11, 10.22):**
- A **decline in the fair value of investments** held at the end of the reporting period (HKAS 10.11).
- **Declaration of dividends** payable to shareholders — not recognised as a liability at the end of the reporting period (HKAS 10.12–13), because there is no obligation to pay at period end.
- Major business combination or disposal of a major subsidiary; announcing a plan to discontinue an operation; major purchases/disposals of assets or classification as held for sale under HKFRS 5; destruction of a major production plant by natural disaster; announcement/commencement of a major restructuring; major new share issue; abnormally large changes in asset prices or foreign exchange rates; changes in tax rates/laws enacted or announced after the reporting period (HKAS 12 effect); significant commitments or contingent liabilities (e.g. issuing a significant guarantee); major litigation arising solely out of events that occurred after the reporting period (HKAS 10.22).

#### B. Measurement（點樣計數 / 分錄）

- **Adjusting events:** revise the measurement of items as at the end of the reporting period in light of the new evidence (e.g. write receivables down when a customer goes bankrupt; write inventories down to NRV; recognise a liability confirmed by a court settlement).
- **Non-adjusting events:** measurement consequences fall into the **next** reporting period — no retrospective measurement change for the period just ended.
- **Going concern (HKAS 10.15):** if the inability to continue as a going concern has a **pervasive effect**, HKAS 10 requires a **fundamental change in the basis of accounting** (i.e. prepare a new set of financial statements on a non-going-concern basis) — **not** merely adjusting amounts within the original basis. Disclosure per HKAS 1 (HKAS 1.25) is required.

#### C. Disclosure（要披露乜）

- **Date of authorisation for issue (HKAS 10.17–18):** disclose **the date** the financial statements were authorised for issue and **who gave that authorisation**; if the owners have power to amend the financial statements after issue, disclose that fact. Rationale: the financial statements do not reflect events after this date.
- **Updating disclosure about conditions at the end of the reporting period (HKAS 10.19–20):** where information received after the reporting period relates to conditions existing at period end (e.g. a contingent liability that was not recognised), the entity must **update the related disclosures** in light of the new information — even where no recognised amount changes.
- **Material non-adjusting events (HKAS 10.21):** for **each material category** of non-adjusting event, disclose:
  - the **nature of the event**; and
  - an **estimate of its financial effect**, or a **statement that such an estimate cannot be made**.
  Immaterial non-adjusting events require **no** disclosure.
- **Dividends (HKAS 1.137):** disclose in the notes the **amount of dividends proposed or declared** before the financial statements were authorised for issue but not recognised as a distribution to owners during the period, and the **related amount per share**.

### 易混淆概念對比

**Table 1: Adjusting events vs Non-adjusting events**

| | Adjusting events | Non-adjusting events |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | Provide evidence of conditions **existing at** the end of the reporting period (HKAS 10.3(a)) | Indicative of conditions that **arose after** the end of the reporting period (HKAS 10.3(b)) |
| Accounting treatment | **Adjust** amounts recognised / recognise items not previously recognised (HKAS 10.8) | **Do not adjust**; recognise in the period the event occurs (HKAS 10.10) |
| Disclosure | Update disclosures about conditions existing at period end (HKAS 10.19–20) | Disclose nature + estimate of financial effect only **if material** (HKAS 10.21) |
| Typical examples | Court case settlement; customer bankruptcy; inventory sold below cost; fraud/errors discovered | Dividends declared; major share issue; plant destroyed by storm; FV decline of investments; major acquisition |

點樣分辨：問自己「呢件事反映嘅情況，喺年結日當日存唔存在？」存在 → adjusting；年結後先出現 → non-adjusting。考試伏位：件事「幾時被發現」唔重要，重要嘅係「個 condition 幾時存在」—— customer bankruptcy、訴訟和解雖然年結後先知道，但都係 adjusting。Dividends declared 係最常考嘅 non-adjusting trap：宣派股息唔可以 confirm 做年結負債。

**Table 2: Material non-adjusting event vs Going concern failure**

| | Material non-adjusting event | Event indicating entity is no longer a going concern |
|---|---|---|
| Root cause | New condition arising after period end | Deterioration from conditions existing at period end **or** arising after period end (either can trigger it) |
| Effect on the 20X1 financial statements | No adjustment; note disclosure of nature + financial effect (HKAS 10.21) | **Do not prepare on a going concern basis** (HKAS 10.14) |
| Scale of change | Amounts in the original financial statements untouched | **Fundamental change in the basis of accounting** — a new basis, not adjustments within the old basis (HKAS 10.15) |
| Other disclosure | Estimate of financial effect (or statement it cannot be made) | HKAS 1 disclosures that the going concern basis is not used (HKAS 1.25) |

點樣分辨：non-adjusting event 再大件事，只要公司仲係 going concern，都只係「披露」；但一旦管理層決定清盤/停業（或無其他現實選擇），就唔係調幾個數咁簡單，而係成個編製基礎都要改。考試伏位：唔好以為 non-adjusting event 永遠只係披露——如果佢令 going concern assumption 失效，HKAS 10 要求 fundamental change in basis。

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** According to HKAS 10.3, what are "events after the reporting period"?

**Back:**
- Favourable or unfavourable events occurring **between the end of the reporting period and the date the financial statements are authorised for issue**.
- Two types: adjusting events (evidence of conditions existing at period end) and non-adjusting events (conditions arising after period end).

💬 繁中白話解釋：HKAS 10 管嘅時間窗係「年結日之後、財務報表獲授權刊發之前」。記住終點係 authorisation date，唔係 AGM 批准日、唔係交 Companies Registry 嗰日。

### Card 2
**Front:** State the accounting treatment for adjusting events under HKAS 10.8.

**Back:**
- The entity **shall adjust the amounts recognised** in its financial statements to reflect adjusting events.
- This may mean: (1) adjusting an existing balance, or (2) recognising an amount not previously recognised.

💬 繁中白話解釋：Adjusting event 即係話「年結日其實已經係咁，只係我哋遲知」——所以要返轉頭改返份報表嘅數。

### Card 3
**Front:** Give FOUR examples of adjusting events listed in HKAS 10.9.

**Back:**
- Settlement of a court case confirming a present obligation at period end.
- Bankruptcy of a customer confirming credit-impairment at period end.
- Sale of inventories below cost giving evidence of NRV at period end.
- Determination of cost/proceeds of assets bought/sold before period end.
- Discovery of fraud or errors showing the financial statements are incorrect. *(any four)*

💬 繁中白話解釋：共通點：件事本身或者背後嘅 condition 喺年結日已經存在，年結後嘅新消息只係「證實」咗佢。

### Card 4
**Front:** State the accounting treatment for non-adjusting events under HKAS 10.10 and HKAS 10.21.

**Back:**
- The entity **shall not adjust** amounts recognised in the financial statements (HKAS 10.10).
- Accounting consequences are recognised in the reporting period in which the event occurs.
- If **material**, disclose the **nature of the event** and an **estimate of its financial effect** (or a statement that an estimate cannot be made) (HKAS 10.21).

💬 繁中白話解釋：Non-adjusting = 新年新事。數留返落個新年度先計，但如果夠大單（material），舊年度報表嘅 notes 要講清楚件事同埋財務影響。

### Card 5
**Front:** How should dividends declared after the reporting period be treated? (HKAS 10.12–13, HKAS 1.137)

**Back:**
- **Not recognised as a liability** at the end of the reporting period — no obligation existed at period end (non-adjusting event).
- Disclose in the notes: the **total amount** of dividends proposed/declared before authorisation for issue and the **amount per share** (HKAS 1.137).

💬 繁中白話解釋：呢個係經典考試陷阱！年結後宣派股息 ≠ 年結負債，因為宣派之前公司無任何義務要派。只可以喺 notes 披露總額同每股金額。

### Card 6
**Front:** What does HKAS 10 require if events after the reporting period indicate the entity is no longer a going concern? (HKAS 10.14–15)

**Back:**
- The entity **shall not prepare its financial statements on a going concern basis** (HKAS 10.14).
- Where the effect is **pervasive**: a **fundamental change in the basis of accounting** is required — not mere adjustments within the original basis (HKAS 10.15).
- HKAS 1 disclosures apply (HKAS 1.25).

💬 繁中白話解釋：Going concern 一冇咗，就唔係「調整」層次，而係成盤賬用另一個 basis 重編。記住關鍵字：fundamental change in the basis of accounting。

### Card 7
**Front:** What must an entity disclose about the date of authorisation for issue? (HKAS 10.17–18)

**Back:**
- The **date** when the financial statements were authorised for issue.
- **Who** gave that authorisation.
- If owners have the power to **amend** the financial statements after issue, disclose that fact.

💬 繁中白話解釋：因為報表唔會反映 authorisation date 之後發生嘅事，所以用家一定要知道呢個日期同埋邊個授權，先至識判斷資料嘅時效。

### Card 8
**Front:** An entity receives post-year-end information about a contingent liability that existed at year-end but was never recognised. What is required? (HKAS 10.19–20)

**Back:**
- The entity must **update the related disclosures** about conditions existing at the end of the reporting period in light of the new information.
- This applies even where the new information does **not** change any recognised amounts (unless the information now causes a liability to be recognised).

💬 繁中白話解釋：就算個數唔郁（例如 contingent liability 仍然唔 recognise），notes 嘅披露都要跟新資料 update。Adjusting event 唔單止調數，仲要 update 披露。
