**Question 1**

According to HKAS 23 *Borrowing Costs*, which of the following assets of Kestrel Holdings Limited is a qualifying asset?

A. Smartphones assembled in large quantities on a repetitive production line, each taking two days to complete
B. A cruise terminal under construction that is expected to take 30 months to complete and make ready for use
C. A completed office floor purchased from a developer, ready for immediate occupation
D. A portfolio of listed equity securities held for trading

**Question 2**

Harbour Vista Properties Limited is constructing a ferry pier that meets the definition of a qualifying asset under HKAS 23. The relevant dates are:

- **1 February 20X8** — the company drew down a bank loan and began incurring borrowing costs
- **1 April 20X8** — the company paid the first construction costs to the contractor
- **1 May 20X8** — the company began activities necessary to prepare the pier for its intended use (obtaining development permits and commencing site works)

In accordance with HKAS 23, on which date should Harbour Vista Properties Limited commence capitalisation of borrowing costs?

A. 1 January 20X8
B. 1 February 20X8
C. 1 April 20X8
D. 1 May 20X8

**Question 3**

Falcon Logistics Limited borrowed HK$40 million from Metro Bank on 1 April 20X8 at a fixed interest rate of 6.00% per annum, specifically to construct an automated distribution hub. The hub is a qualifying asset; construction is expected to take 20 months. Construction activities began on 1 June 20X8, when the first payment of HK$15 million was made to the contractor. Falcon Logistics did not invest any surplus funds. Interest is paid annually. The company's year end is 31 December 20X8.

In accordance with HKAS 23, what amount of borrowing costs should Falcon Logistics capitalise as part of the cost of the distribution hub for the year ended 31 December 20X8?

A. HK$1,800,000
B. HK$1,400,000
C. HK$1,200,000
D. HK$2,400,000

**Question 4**

Lantau Renewables Limited is constructing a wind turbine assembly plant, a qualifying asset, funded entirely from its general borrowings. Its borrowings, outstanding unchanged throughout the year, are:

- HK$60 million bank loan at 5.00% per annum
- HK$90 million bonds issued at par at 6.00% per annum

Expenditure on the plant of HK$24 million was incurred evenly from the commencement date, and five months have elapsed between the commencement date and the financial year end. No borrowings were made specifically for the plant.

In accordance with HKAS 23, what amount of borrowing costs should be capitalised for the year?

A. HK$500,000
B. HK$550,000
C. HK$560,000
D. HK$1,344,000

**Question 5**

Which journal entry correctly records the borrowing costs for the year in accordance with HKAS 23?

C. Dr Refrigerated warehouse 450,000; Cr Cash 450,000
