# M6 Chapter 1: Financial Reporting Framework — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives

1. Explain the objective of general purpose financial reporting and the qualitative characteristics of useful financial information under the Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting (2018).
2. Define the elements of financial statements (assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses) and apply the recognition and derecognition criteria of the Conceptual Framework.
3. Distinguish between the measurement bases (historical cost, current cost, fair value, value in use / fulfilment value) and the two concepts of capital maintenance.
4. Describe the financial reporting framework in Hong Kong, including the regulatory bodies, the HKFRS standard-setting due process, and the hierarchy of mandatory vs non-mandatory pronouncements.
5. Identify the eligibility conditions for adopting the HK SME-FRF / HK SME-FRS and the HKFRS for Private Entities.

### 必背考點

#### A. Objective & Qualitative Characteristics（概念框架嘅根基）

**Objective of general purpose financial reporting (HKFRS CF 1.2–1.11)**
- Provide useful financial information about the entity to **primary users** (existing and potential investors, lenders and other creditors) for decisions relating to **providing resources to the entity** (buying/selling/holding instruments, providing or settling loans, voting on management's actions).
- 用戶需要評估：(1) prospects for future net cash inflows；(2) management's **stewardship** of the entity's economic resources。

**Qualitative characteristics**

| Characteristic | 內容 | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| **Relevance** (fundamental) | Capable of making a difference in decisions; has **predictive value**, **confirmatory value**, or both; **materiality** is an entity-specific aspect of relevance | HKFRS CF 2.6–2.11 |
| **Faithful representation** (fundamental) | **Complete + Neutral + Free from error**; substance over form; measurement uncertainty does not undermine usefulness if estimates are clearly described and explained | HKFRS CF 2.12–2.22 |
| **Comparability** (enhancing) | The **goal**; consistency (same methods period to period) helps achieve it; comparability ≠ uniformity | HKFRS CF 2.24–2.29 |
| **Verifiability** (enhancing) | Different knowledgeable and independent observers reach the same/similar conclusion; direct (count cash) or indirect (recalculate inputs) | HKFRS CF 2.30–2.32 |
| **Timeliness** (enhancing) | Available in time to influence decisions | HKFRS CF 2.33 |
| **Understandability** (enhancing) | Clear and concise classification/presentation; users assumed to have reasonable business knowledge | HKFRS CF 2.34–2.36 |
| **Cost constraint** | Benefits of reporting information must justify the costs of providing and using it | HKFRS CF 2.39–2.43 |

> 💬 記住：enhancing characteristics **唔可以**將 irrelevant 或唔 faithful 嘅資訊變成 useful —— 兩個 fundamental characteristics 係必要條件。Prudence = exercise of caution under uncertainty，**唔准** overstate **亦唔准** understate assets/income/liabilities/expenses（考試伏位！）。

#### B. Recognition（要素定義 + 確認/終止確認準則）

**Elements — definitions (HKFRS CF 4.3–4.25; 4.68–4.69)**

| Element | Definition | 三大測試 / 關鍵點 |
|---|---|---|
| **Asset** | A **present economic resource controlled** by the entity **as a result of past events** | (1) Right；(2) Potential to produce economic benefits（唔需要 certain 甚至唔需要 likely）；(3) Control = present ability to direct the use and obtain the benefits。員工服務 **唔係** asset，因為冇 control（員工可以隨時走） |
| **Liability** | A **present obligation** of the entity **to transfer an economic resource** as a result of past events | (1) Obligation = no practical ability to avoid；(2) Obligation to transfer an economic resource；(3) Present obligation exists as a result of a past event |
| **Equity** | Residual interest in assets after deducting all liabilities | Claims that do not meet the definition of a liability（例如 ordinary shares） |
| **Income** | Increases in assets or decreases in liabilities resulting in increases in equity, **other than contributions from holders of equity claims** | HKFRS CF 4.68 |
| **Expenses** | Decreases in assets or increases in liabilities resulting in decreases in equity, **other than distributions to holders of equity** | HKFRS CF 4.69。Dividend 係 distribution → 直接扣 equity，**唔係** expense（常見伏位） |

**Executory contract (HKFRS CF 4.56–4.58)**
- A contract that is **equally unperformed**: neither party has performed, or both have partially performed **to an equal extent**。
- 一旦只有一方履行咗，合約就唔再係 executory —— 先履行嗰方產生 asset（e.g. trade receivable）；另一方產生 liability（e.g. contract liability）。

**Recognition criteria (HKFRS CF 5.6–5.23)**
- Recognition = capturing an item that meets the definition of an element, in words and by a monetary amount, in the statement of financial position or statement(s) of financial performance。
- An asset or liability is recognised **only if** recognition provides: (i) **relevant information**；AND (ii) a **faithful representation**。
- Relevance 受 **existence uncertainty**（e.g. 權利存在與否有爭議）同 **low probability of inflow/outflow** 影響；faithful representation 受 **measurement uncertainty**（e.g. cash flow-based techniques with exceptionally wide range of outcomes）影響。
- **Cost constraint** 同樣適用：cost of recognising 唔可以大過 benefits。
- **Derecognition** = mirror image of recognition：當 item 唔再符合 asset/liability 定義時，從 statement of financial position 移除。

> 💬 呢一章冇 journal entries；考試重心係 **definition → recognition** 嘅兩層分析：先問「符唔符合 element 定義？」，再問「recognition 會唔會提供 relevant + faithfully represented 嘅資訊？」兩關都過先 confirm 落賬。

**Going concern assumption (HKFRS CF 3.2–3.3)**
- 唯一 underlying assumption：entity 會喺 foreseeable future（實務上 12 個月）繼續經營，冇意圖亦冇需要 liquidate 或 cease trading。唔成立 → 可能要用另一個 basis 編製（e.g. 唔係 accrual basis）。

**Reporting entity (HKFRS CF 3.9–3.14)**
- Boundary 可以是：a single entity / a portion of an entity (e.g. a division) / more than one entity。
- 多於一個 entity → **consolidated**（parent + subsidiaries）/ **unconsolidated**（parent alone）/ **combined**（非 parent-subsidiary 關係嘅組合）。

#### C. Measurement（計量基礎）

**Two categories of measurement basis (HKFRS CF Chapter 6)**

| Basis | 定義要點 | Entry / Exit value | Transaction costs | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Historical cost** | Asset：取得時付出嘅代價（consideration paid + transaction costs）；Liability：收到嘅代價 − transaction costs | Entry value | Included | HKFRS CF 6.4–6.9 |
| **Current cost** | 於 measurement date 購入同等 item 要付嘅金額 | Entry value | Included | HKFRS CF 6.21–6.22 |
| **Fair value** | Price received to sell an asset (paid to transfer a liability) in an **orderly transaction between market participants** at the measurement date | Exit value — market participants' perspective | **Not** adjusted | HKFRS CF 6.12–6.16 |
| **Value in use (asset) / Fulfilment value (liability)** | Entity-specific discounted cash flows expected from using/disposing the asset（或履行 liability 要 transfer 嘅貼現現金流） | Exit value — **entity-specific** assumptions | **Not** adjusted | HKFRS CF 6.17–6.20 |

- 選擇 measurement basis 由 relevance + faithful representation 驅動；historical cost 常用因為 objective and verifiable；fair value 可能更 relevant 但無 active market quote 時較 subjective。

**Concepts of capital (HKFRS CF 8.3)**

| | Financial concept of capital | Physical concept of capital |
|---|---|---|
| Capital = | Net assets (equity) of the entity | Productive capacity (e.g. units of output per day) |
| Profit earned if | Money amount of net assets increased during the period | Physical productive capacity increased during the period |
| 共同點 | 都要 exclude distributions to and contributions from owners | 同上 |

#### D. Presentation and Disclosure（表達與披露原則）

**Three communication principles (HKFRS CF 7.4–7.6, 7.7–7.19, 7.20–7.22)**
1. Focus on presentation and disclosure **objectives and principles rather than rules** — 避免 boilerplate（標準化罐頭描述）同 duplication。
2. **Classify** information：group similar items, separate dissimilar items，按 nature / role or function / measurement basis 分類。
3. **Aggregate** appropriately：唔可以因 unnecessary detail 或 excessive aggregation 而 obscure 資訊。

#### E. Hong Kong Framework & Regulatory Bodies

- **HKFRS** 由 HKICPA 發佈，modelled after IFRS，自 2005 年起 fully converged（有少量例外）；HKFRS = HKFRS + HKAS + Interpretations（HK(IFRIC)-Int, HK-Int, HK(SIC)-Int），全部 **mandatory** on HKICPA members。
- **IFRS 係 principles-based**（要 professional judgement）；U.S. GAAP 係 rules-based。
- **Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)**：Part 9 'Accounts and Audits' 及 Schedule 4 規定財務報表編製；香港註冊公司必須按 HKICPA 頒佈嘅 accounting standards（即 HKFRS）編製財務報表 (Preface para 25A)；s.359 規定 SME reporting exemption 條件；s.383 規定 directors' remuneration 披露（AB 3 提供指引）。
- **上市公司**（HKEX）可用 HKFRS、IFRS 或 CASBE（限內地企業）編製。Main Board：interim + annual reports；GEM：quarterly + interim + annual。
- **監管機構**：Companies Registry（執行 Cap. 622，true and fair view + directors' report business review）；HKEX（listing rules）；HKICPA（唯一法定 CPA 註冊及執業證書簽發機構，依 Professional Accountants Ordinance 運作）；SFC（證券期貨市場、takeovers and mergers，Securities and Futures Ordinance）；AFRC（依 Financial Reporting Council Ordinance 成立嘅獨立法定機構，監察上市實體財務匯報質素、調查核數師違規）。
- **Standard-setting due process (Preface para 27–28)**：FRSC 負責制訂、ROB 提供策略建議；新準則前要發 exposure draft（附 basis for conclusions）公開諮詢，有 comment period —— IFRS **唔會**自動成為 HKFRS。
- **AGs（Preface para 14）**：guidance statements、best practice，非強制但 normally should be followed，須 Council 批准（e.g. AG 1 Incomplete Records）。**ABs（Preface para 34–36）**：FRSC 發佈嘅 topical 刊物，非強制，**唔需要** Council 批准（e.g. AB 3 Directors' Remuneration, AB 5 Business Review under Cap. 622）。

#### F. HK SME-FRF / HK SME-FRS（Cap. 622 s.359）

**公司類型（HK SME-FRF para. 22–26）**：只限 **private company**（成員 ≤ 50、股份轉讓受限、禁止公開招股）或 **company limited by guarantee**（無股本）。

**Ineligible businesses（HK SME-FRF para. 27）**：持牌進行 SFO Part V 受規管業務；接受附息或溢價償還嘅貸款（放債）；banking business；insurance business。

**Size tests（HK SME-FRF para. 28–43）**

| Type | Size test | Shareholders' approval |
|---|---|---|
| Small guarantee company | Total annual revenue ≤ **HK$25 million** | Not required |
| Small private company | 三項中**唔超過任何兩項**：revenue ≤ HK$100m；total assets ≤ HK$100m；employees ≤ 100 | Not required |
| Larger 'eligible' private company | 三項中唔超過任何兩項：revenue ≤ HK$200m；total assets ≤ HK$200m；employees ≤ 100 | **≥75%** of all members 通過決議 **AND** 其餘 voting rights **無人反對** |
| Private company（無 subsidiaries，亦唔係別人嘅 subsidiary） | No limit | **100%** shareholders agree（每年） |

- **Two-year rule**：連續兩年符合 size tests 先合資格；連續兩年唔符合先失去資格。
- **Benefits（HK SME-FRF）**：simpler and briefer；less disclosure（**唔使** cash flow statement）；**豁免 true and fair view**（只需 comply with SME-FRF/FRS）；directors' report **唔使** business review。
- **HK SME-FRS 要點**：accrual basis，但**唔准用 fair value**（靠 historical cost，para. 20）；PPE 只有 cost model（Section 3）；investment property 當 PPE 處理（Section 4）；intangibles 可反駁推定 useful life ≤ 10 年（Section 9）；**deferred tax 唔確認**（Section 14）。

#### G. HKFRS for Private Entities（HKFRS for PEs）

- 適用於 **do not have public accountability** AND **publish general purpose financial statements for external users** 嘅 entity（Section 1.1–1.4）。
- **Public accountability**（Section 1.6–1.7）：(i) debt/equity instruments 喺公開市場交易；(ii) 正在發行工具準備公開交易（e.g. IPO 申請中）；(iii) 以受託身份為廣大 outsiders 持有資產作為主要業務（banks, insurance companies, securities brokers）。
- **冇 size tests，冇 shareholders' approval 要求** —— 即使 HK$10 billion assets 都可以合資格。
- Group context：parent 用 full HKFRS，subsidiary 自己冇 public accountability 仍可喺 own FS 用 HKFRS for PEs；parent 評估自己 separate FS 資格時唔使理會集團整體。
- 約 250 頁；measurement 承認 historical cost **和** fair value（Section 2.34）；有 **'undue cost or effort' exemption**（Section 21.4A/B）。
- 與 SME-FRF 差異（Exhibit 1.15）：cash flow statement **required**、statement of changes in equity required；investment property 可用 fair value（如可靠計量）；R&D 全部 expensed；goodwill 無法可靠估計 useful life 時上限 10 年；deferred tax 處理類似 HKAS 12。

### 易混淆概念對比

**對比一：Relevance vs Faithful Representation（兩大 fundamental characteristics）**

| | Relevance | Faithful Representation |
|---|---|---|
| 核心問題 | 資訊會唔會 **make a difference** 喺用戶決策？ | 資訊係咪 **如實反映** 佢聲稱代表嘅現象？ |
| 組成 | Predictive value + Confirmatory value + Materiality（entity-specific） | Complete + Neutral + Free from error |
| 關鍵子概念 | Materiality：omitting/misstating/obscuring 會影響 primary users 決策 | Substance over form；prudence 支持 neutrality（唔准兩個方向嘅 bias）；free from error ≠ perfectly accurate |
| Recognition 關卡 | Existence uncertainty、low probability of economic benefits → 可能唔 relevant | High measurement uncertainty → 可能唔 faithful |
| 點樣分辨／考試伏位 | 見到「預測將來 / 確認過去估計」= relevance。考生常誤將 completeness、neutral 當 relevance —— 佢哋屬於 faithful representation | 「Free from error」唔等於 perfectly accurate（合理估計照樣可以 faithful）；prudence **雙向**——understate assets 一樣唔准 |

**對比二：HK SME-FRF/FRS vs HKFRS for Private Entities**

| | HK SME-FRF / SME-FRS | HKFRS for PEs |
|---|---|---|
| 淵源 | HKICPA **home-grown** 本地研製 | Modelled after **IFRS for SMEs**（"SMEs" 改名做 "Private Entities"） |
| Qualifying entities | Private / guarantee company + **size tests**（Cap. 622 s.359）+ **股東批准**（大 eligible 要 ≥75% 且無人反對；非集團私人公司免 size test 要 100%） | **No public accountability** + publish general purpose FS for external users；**冇 size test、冇股東批准** |
| True and fair view | **豁免** | 要公平呈列（依 PEs 準則） |
| Cash flow statement | Optional（唔使） | **Required** |
| Measurement | Historical cost only，**禁用 fair value** | Historical cost + fair value 都承認；有 undue cost or effort exemption |
| Deferred tax | **不確認** | 類似 HKAS 12，要確認 |
| Goodwill | Useful life ≤ 5 年（更長須每年檢討） | 無法可靠估計時上限 10 年 |
| 點樣分辨／考試伏位 | 見到 HK$100m/HK$200m 門檻、75%/100% approval ＝ SME-FRF。記住 small private company 係「三選二」而**唔係**三項全過 | 見到 public accountability 三測試（公開交易 / 發行中 / fiduciary 持有資產）＝ PEs。公司**規模完全無關**——巨型私人公司都可以合資格 |

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** What is the objective of general purpose financial reporting under the Conceptual Framework, and who are the primary users?

**Back:**
- To provide useful financial information about the entity to users for making decisions relating to **providing resources to the entity** (HKFRS CF 1.2–1.11).
- Primary users: **existing and potential investors, lenders and other creditors**.
- Users assess: (i) prospects for future net cash inflows; (ii) management's **stewardship** of the entity's economic resources.

💬 白話：財務報告唔係寫俾所有人嘅——首要服務對象係「出錢嗰班人」（股東、潛在投資者、銀行債權人），幫佢哋決定買賣持有、借唔借錢、點投票。記住 stewardship（管理層睇住啲資產睇得幾好）呢個字，written question 成日要提。

### Card 2
**Front:** List the two fundamental and four enhancing qualitative characteristics of useful financial information, with one-line meanings.

**Back:**
- Fundamental: **Relevance** (predictive/confirmatory value; materiality); **Faithful representation** (complete, neutral, free from error).
- Enhancing: **Comparability** (the goal; consistency helps), **Verifiability** (independent observers reach same conclusion), **Timeliness** (available in time), **Understandability** (clear and concise).
- Enhancing characteristics cannot make irrelevant or unfaithful information useful.

💬 白話：口訣「相關＋如實」係入場券，另外四個（比較、驗證、及時、易懂）係加分項。試場伏位：completeness / neutrality 屬於 faithful representation，唔係 relevance；comparability ≠ uniformity。

### Card 3
**Front:** Define 'asset' under the Conceptual Framework and state the three elements of the definition. Why is the right to receive employee services NOT an asset?

**Back:**
- Asset = a **present economic resource controlled by the entity as a result of past events**; an economic resource is a right that has the potential to produce economic benefits (HKFRS CF 4.3–4.25).
- Three elements: (1) **Right**; (2) **Potential to produce economic benefits** (need not be certain or even likely); (3) **Control** (present ability to direct the use and obtain the benefits).
- Employee services fail the **control** test — employees can leave at any time.

💬 白話：三關——「有權、有潛力生財、控制得到」。員工會辭職，公司控制唔到佢，所以請人本身唔產生 asset。注意「唔使肯定甚至唔使很可能有利益流入」——probability 唔係定義嘅一部分，係 recognition 層面先考慮。

### Card 4
**Front:** Define 'liability' under the Conceptual Framework and state the three criteria. Is an executory contract a liability?

**Back:**
- Liability = a **present obligation of the entity to transfer an economic resource as a result of past events** (HKFRS CF 4.3–4.25).
- Three criteria: (1) the entity has an **obligation** (no practical ability to avoid); (2) the obligation is **to transfer an economic resource**; (3) it is a **present obligation existing as a result of a past event**.
- An executory contract is **equally unperformed** — rights and obligations to exchange economic resources only; once one party performs first, the other party's obligation becomes a liability (e.g. contract liability).

💬 白話：liability 三大件＝「有責任、要俾嘢出去、過去事件造成嘅現時責任」。Executory contract 即「大家都未做嘢」嘅合約——雙方未履行或履行程度相等，一般未產生 element；一方先做咗（例如先收錢），另一方先至有 liability。

### Card 5
**Front:** State the recognition criteria for an asset or liability under the Conceptual Framework, and the factors that may cause recognition to fail each criterion.

**Back:**
- Recognise only if recognition provides: (i) **relevant information**; and (ii) a **faithful representation** of the asset or liability (HKFRS CF 5.6–5.23).
- Relevance may fail due to: **existence uncertainty**; **low probability** of an inflow/outflow of economic benefits.
- Faithful representation may fail due to high **measurement uncertainty** (e.g. exceptionally wide range of possible outcomes).
- **Cost constraint** also applies.
- **Derecognition** = removal from the statement of financial position when the item no longer meets the definition of an asset or liability.

💬 白話：定義過咗關唔代表可以入賬——仲要過「relevant＋faithful」雙關。記住配對：existence uncertainty 同 low probability 影響 **relevance**；measurement uncertainty 影響 **faithful representation**。呢個 matching 係 MCQ 常客。

### Card 6
**Front:** Compare the four measurement bases in the Conceptual Framework: which are entry values and which are exit values? Which reflect entity-specific assumptions?

**Back:**
- **Entry values**: Historical cost (consideration paid + transaction costs); Current cost (cost of acquiring an equivalent item at the measurement date) — transaction costs included.
- **Exit values**: **Fair value** (orderly transaction between **market participants** at the measurement date); **Value in use / fulfilment value** (discounted entity-specific cash flows) — transaction costs NOT adjusted.
- Value in use reflects **entity-specific** assumptions; fair value reflects the **market participants'** perspective.

💬 白話：Entry＝「買入價邏輯」（歷史成本、現行成本，計 transaction costs）；Exit＝「賣出價邏輯」（fair value、value in use，唔計 transaction costs）。Fair value 跟市場參與者角度，value in use 跟自己公司角度——呢個對照必考。

### Card 7
**Front:** A private company wants to adopt the HK SME-FRF. What types of companies qualify, which businesses are ineligible, and what are the size tests and approval requirements?

**Back:**
- Qualifying types: **private companies** (≤50 members, transfer restrictions, no public subscription) and **companies limited by guarantee** (Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) s.359).
- Ineligible: SFO Part V licensed business; accepting loans at interest/premium; **banking**; **insurance**.
- Size tests: small guarantee co. — revenue ≤ HK$25m (no approval); small private co. — **2 of 3**: revenue ≤ HK$100m / assets ≤ HK$100m / ≤100 employees (no approval); larger 'eligible' private co. — 2 of 3 at HK$200m thresholds + **≥75% members approve and none of the rest vote against**; standalone private co. (no subsidiary, not a subsidiary) — no size limit but **100% approval**.
- Two consecutive years meeting/failing the tests to gain/lose status.

💬 白話：背門檻口訣「25 / 100 / 200」，「三選二」唔係全過。批准規則：小型唔使批；大型要 75%＋剩返嗰啲冇人反對；非集團私人公司免 size test 就要全體 100%。考試最鍾意將 75% 同 100%、small 同 large eligible 調亂。

### Card 8
**Front:** What is 'public accountability' under the HKFRS for Private Entities, and what are the eligibility conditions for adopting it?

**Back:**
- An entity has **public accountability** if: (i) its debt or equity instruments are **traded in a public market**; (ii) it is **in the process of issuing** such instruments for public trading; or (iii) it **holds assets in a fiduciary capacity** for a broad group of outsiders as one of its primary businesses (e.g. banks, insurers, securities brokers) (HKFRS for PEs Section 1.1–1.4, 1.6–1.7).
- Eligibility: **no public accountability** AND publishes **general purpose financial statements for external users**.
- **No size tests and no shareholders' approval required.**

💬 白話：三條問題——「上市未？準備上市未？係咪幫人託管資產做本業？」三條都答「唔係」，加上有出 general purpose FS 俾外部用戶（例如稅局），就合資格。規模幾大都冇所謂——呢點同 SME-FRF 嘅 size tests 完全相反，係經典對比考點。

| Executory contract | 「大家都未做嘢」嘅合約——雙方未履行或履行程度相等 | "An executory contract is one that is equally unperformed by both parties." |
| Going concern | 持續經營假設——預期公司喺可見將來（實務上 12 個月）唔會倒閉或停業 | "The going concern assumption presumes the entity will remain in operation for the foreseeable future." |
| Public accountability | 公眾責任——股份公開交易、準備上市、或以受託身份為外人持有資產 | "An entity whose shares are traded in a public market has public accountability and cannot adopt the HKFRS for PEs." |
| Entry value / Exit value | 買入價邏輯 vs 賣出價邏輯嘅計量基礎 | "Historical cost and current cost are entry values, while fair value and value in use are exit values." |
| Reporting entity | 報告實體——可以係單一公司、公司一部分（如分部）、或多於一間公司（集團） | "The boundary of a reporting entity may be a single entity, a portion of an entity, or more than one entity." |
| Cost constraint | 成本制約——披露資訊嘅好處要大過收集同提供嘅成本 | "The cost constraint requires that the benefits of reporting information justify the costs of providing it." |
| Derecognition | 終止確認——當項目唔再符合 asset/liability 定義時從財務狀況表移除 | "Derecognition occurs when an item no longer meets the definition of an asset or a liability." |
