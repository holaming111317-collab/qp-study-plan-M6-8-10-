**Question 1**

Harbour View Limited (Harbour View) recognised a warranty provision of HK$360,000 in its statement of financial position as at 31 March 20X9. Under the Inland Revenue Ordinance, warranty expenditure is deductible for tax purposes only when it is paid. Warranty payments are expected to be made in full in future periods.

In accordance with HKAS 12 *Income Taxes*, what is the tax base of the warranty provision as at 31 March 20X9?

A. HK$360,000
B. HK$720,000
C. HK$180,000
D. Nil

**Question 2**

Kowloon Dynamics Limited (Kowloon Dynamics) reported an accounting profit before tax of HK$4,200,000 for the year ended 31 March 20X9. The profit was arrived at after including the following items:

- Fines and penalties expense of HK$80,000 (not deductible for tax purposes);
- Depreciation expense of HK$250,000;
- Dividend income of HK$60,000 (not taxable under the Inland Revenue Ordinance).

A capital allowance deduction of HK$400,000 is available for tax purposes for the year. The profits tax rate is 16.5%.

What is the current tax expense of Kowloon Dynamics for the year ended 31 March 20X9?

A. HK$693,000
B. HK$681,450
C. HK$671,550
D. HK$803,550

**Question 3**

The tax rate is 16.5%.

**Question 5**

Meridian Supplies Limited (Meridian) has the following balances in its statement of financial position as at 31 March 20X9:

| | Carrying amount HK$ |
|---|---|
| Trade receivables (gross) | 2,000,000 |
| Allowance for expected credit losses | (90,000) |
| Property, plant and equipment | 3,000,000 |
| Provision for warranties | 140,000 |

Additional information:

1. Credit losses are deductible for tax purposes only when the trade receivable is written off as credit impaired. The related sales revenue has already been taxed.
2. Warranty costs are deductible for tax purposes only when incurred.
3. The tax base of property, plant and equipment as at 31 March 20X9 is HK$2,400,000.
4. The tax rate is 15%. It is probable that sufficient future taxable income will be available to utilise all deductible temporary differences.

In accordance with HKAS 12, what amounts should Meridian recognise as deferred tax asset and deferred tax liability as at 31 March 20X9?

A. Deferred tax asset HK$34,500; Deferred tax liability HK$90,000
B. Deferred tax asset HK$230,000; Deferred tax liability HK$600,000
C. Deferred tax asset HK$90,000; Deferred tax liability HK$34,500
D. Deferred tax asset HK$13,500; Deferred tax liability HK$90,000
