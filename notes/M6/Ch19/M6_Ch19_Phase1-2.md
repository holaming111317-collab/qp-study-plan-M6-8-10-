# M6 Chapter 19: Related Party Disclosures — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. **Define and identify** related parties of a reporting entity in accordance with HKAS 24 (Revised) *Related Party Disclosures*.
2. **Distinguish** relationships that are related parties from those explicitly excluded by HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 11.
3. **Explain** the significance of sufficient disclosure of related party relationships, transactions and outstanding balances.
4. **Describe and apply** the disclosure requirements for related party relationships, related party transactions and key management personnel (KMP) compensation.

> ⚠️ 本章定位：HKAS 24 (Revised) 係一個 **disclosure standard** — 佢唔改變任何 transaction 嘅 recognition 或 measurement（嗰啲由其他準則處理，例如 HKFRS 16 處理 lease 本身嘅入賬）。所以本章冇專屬 journal entries；考試重點係**邊個係 related party**同**要披露啲乜**。相關披露會補充其他法規要求，例如 **Section 383 of the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance**（directors' emoluments）同 **Chapter 14A of the Hong Kong Listing Rules**（connected transactions）。

### 必背考點

#### A. Recognition / Identification — 邊個先係 Related Party？（核心考點）

**A1. Person (or a close member of that person's family) — HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 9(a)**
A person (or close family member) is related to the reporting entity if that person:
1. has **control or joint control** of the reporting entity — para. 9(a)(i);
2. has **significant influence** over the reporting entity — para. 9(a)(ii);
3. is a member of the **key management personnel (KMP)** of the reporting entity **or of a parent** of the reporting entity — para. 9(a)(iii).

**Key definitions (HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 9):**
- **Key management personnel (KMP)**: those persons having authority and responsibility for planning, directing and controlling the activities of the entity, directly or indirectly, **including any director (whether executive or otherwise)** of that entity.
- **Close members of the family of a person**: family members who may be expected to influence, or be influenced by, that person in their dealings with the entity. **Always includes**: (i) the person's children and spouse or domestic partner; (ii) children of the person's spouse or domestic partner; (iii) dependants of that person or that person's spouse or domestic partner. Principles-based — parents/grandparents *could* qualify depending on facts and circumstances.
- **Related party transaction**: a transfer of resources, services or obligations between a reporting entity and a related party, **regardless of whether a price is charged**（免費 transfer 都照計！）.

**A2. Entity — HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 9(b)**
An entity is related to the reporting entity if **any** of the following applies:
1. The entity and the reporting entity are **members of the same group** (each parent, subsidiary and fellow subsidiary is related to the others) — para. 9(b)(i);
2. One entity is an **associate or joint venture** of the other entity (or an associate or JV **of a member of a group** of which the other entity is a member) — para. 9(b)(ii);
3. Both entities are **joint ventures of the same third party** — para. 9(b)(iii);
4. One entity is a **joint venture** of a third entity and the other entity is an **associate** of the third entity — para. 9(b)(iv);
5. The entity is **controlled or jointly controlled** by a person identified in para. 9(a) — para. 9(b)(vi);
6. A person identified in para. 9(a)(i) (i.e. a person with control or joint control of the reporting entity) has **significant influence** over the entity or is a member of the **KMP** of the entity (or of a parent of the entity) — para. 9(b)(vii);
7. The entity, or any member of a group of which it is a part, **provides KMP services** to the reporting entity or to the parent of the reporting entity — para. 9(b)(viii).

> 💬 記憶法：集团内（same group）、有影响力（associate/JV 及延伸）、同一第三者嘅 JV、被关键人控制、提供 KMP 服务 — 一有任何一项就係 related party。LP 提醒：list 唔係 exhaustive，要睇 **substance over legal form**。

**A3. NOT related parties — HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 11（考試必考陷阱）**
The following are **NOT** related parties:
- Two entities **simply because they have a director or other member of KMP in common**, or because a member of KMP of one entity has significant influence over the other entity — para. 11(a);
- **Two joint venturers simply because they share joint control** of a joint venture — para. 11(b);
- A **customer, supplier, franchisor, distributor or general agent** with whom the entity transacts a significant volume of business, **merely by virtue of the resulting economic dependence**（經濟依賴 ≠ related party；但其他準則可能要求另行披露重大依賴）;
- Providers of finance / lenders in the ordinary course — financing arrangements alone do not constitute a related party relationship.

**A4. Timing point（Key Learning Point）**: An entity's related parties **may change from period to period** — a person who was KMP for only **part of the year** is still a related party for that period（年中離任嘅 director 都照計）。

#### B. Measurement — 本章無獨立計量規則
- HKAS 24 (Revised) 係 disclosure standard，**唔規定** related party transactions 點樣計量；交易本身按相關準則（HKFRS 15、HKFRS 16、HKAS 2 等）確認及計量。
- 因此本章**無指定 journal entries**。但記住：related party transactions 包括 **arm's length 及 non-arm's length** 價格嘅交易 — 就算係市價交易，**一樣要披露**；nil consideration 嘅 transfer（例如 parent 免費送 inventory 畀 subsidiary）都係 related party transaction。
- 相關披露責任疊加於：Section 383 of the HK Companies Ordinance（directors' emoluments）、HK Listing Rules Chapter 14A（connected transactions）、HKAS 1、HKFRS 12。

#### C. Disclosure — HKAS 24 (Revised) 具體要求

**C1. Relationships — para. 13 & para. 16（無論有冇交易都要披露！）**
- Disclose relationships between a **parent and its subsidiaries irrespective of whether there have been transactions** between them — para. 13.
- A subsidiary must disclose the **name of its parent** and, if different, the **name of the ultimate controlling party** — para. 13.
- If neither the parent nor the ultimate parent produces consolidated financial statements available for public use, disclose the name of the **next most senior parent** that does (the first parent above the immediate parent that produces publicly available consolidated FS) — para. 16.

**C2. Related party transactions — para. 18（有交易時）**
Disclose the **nature of the related party relationship** plus information about the transactions and outstanding balances (including commitments) necessary for users to understand the effect on the FS. **At a minimum**:
- the **amount of the transactions**;
- the **amount of outstanding balances, including commitments**, and:
  - their **terms and conditions**, including whether they are **secured**, and the **nature of the consideration** to be provided in settlement; and
  - details of any **guarantees given or received**;
- **provisions for doubtful debts** related to the amount of outstanding balances; and
- the **expense recognised during the period in respect of bad or doubtful debts** due from related parties.

**C3. Examples of related party transactions — para. 21**
Purchases/sales of goods (finished or unfinished); purchases/sales of property and other assets; rendering or receiving of services; **leases**; transfers of research and development; transfers under licence agreements; transfers under finance arrangements (including loans and equity contributions in cash or in kind); provision of **guarantees or collateral**; **commitments** to do something if a particular event occurs or does not occur; **settlement of liabilities** on behalf of the entity (or by the entity on behalf of the related party).

**C4. KMP compensation — para. 17（必背五大類）**
Disclose KMP compensation **in total** and for **each** of the following categories:
1. **Short-term employee benefits** (e.g. wages and salaries);
2. **Post-employment benefits** (e.g. retirement benefits);
3. **Other long-term benefits** (e.g. long-term leave or sabbatical leave);
4. **Termination benefits** (e.g. severance payments);
5. **Share-based payment** (e.g. share options and share appreciation rights).

> ⚠️ HKAS 24 (Revised) does **NOT** require disclosure of the compensation of **each individual** key management person — total + 五大類就夠（個別董事薪酬係 Companies Ordinance s.383 嘅要求，唔好混淆）。

### 易混淆概念對比

**表 1：Related party vs NOT related party（HKAS 24 para. 9 vs para. 11）**

| Situation | Related party? | 點樣分辨／考試伏位 |
|---|---|---|
| Fellow subsidiary (same group) | ✅ Yes — para. 9(b)(i) | 同一集團內 automatically related，唔使有交易 |
| Associate / joint venture of the reporting entity | ✅ Yes — para. 9(b)(ii) | Significant influence / joint control 就夠 |
| Both entities are JVs of the **same third party** | ✅ Yes — para. 9(b)(iii) | 伏位：同下一条好易搞乱 |
| **Two venturers sharing joint control** of the same JV | ❌ No — para. 11(b) | 淨係「一齊控制一個 JV」唔構成關係 — 經典陷阱 |
| Entity controlled by a KMP member of the reporting entity | ✅ Yes — para. 9(b)(vi) | 人係 related → 佢控制嘅公司都係 related |
| Two entities with merely a **common director** | ❌ No — para. 11(a) | 只有共同董事唔夠；除非該董事同時 control 其中一方 |
| Major customer / supplier (economic dependence) | ❌ No — para. 11 | 經濟依賴 ≠ related party，就算 100% 生意都係 |
| Lender / provider of finance | ❌ No — para. 11 層次 | 純融資安排唔構成 related party relationship |

**表 2：Control vs Joint control vs Significant influence vs KMP**

| Concept | 定義重點 | Related party 後果 | 考試伏位 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Control** | Power over the entity + exposure/rights to variable returns + ability to affect those returns through power | Person/entity with control 係 related party（para. 9(a)(i), 9(b)(i)） | 持股百分比本身唔等於 control（HKFRS 10 概念） |
| **Joint control** | Contractually agreed sharing of control; decisions require unanimous consent | Joint control of reporting entity → related；同第三者 joint control 一個 JV，嗰個 JV 係 related，但 **另一個 venturer 唔係** | 「JV 本身 related」vs「fellow venturer not related」係最常見失分位 |
| **Significant influence** | Power to participate in financial and operating policy decisions, but not control/joint control | 產生 associate 關係 → related party（para. 9(a)(ii), 9(b)(ii)） | 一般 20–50% 持股推定，但要睇實質 |
| **KMP** | Authority & responsibility for planning, directing, controlling activities; **includes ALL directors (executive or otherwise)** | KMP + 其 close family + 其控制嘅 entity 全部 related（para. 9(a)(iii), 9(b)(vi)） | Non-executive / independent directors 都係 KMP；年中上任或離任都計（part-period） |

## Phase 2 閃卡（8 cards）

### Card 1
**Front:** According to HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 9, define a "related party" and a "related party transaction".

**Back:**
- A related party is a person or entity that is related to the entity preparing its financial statements (the reporting entity).
- A related party transaction is a transfer of resources, services or obligations between a reporting entity and a related party, **regardless of whether a price is charged**.

💬 白話：related party 唔一定係公司，可以係人（director、佢屋企人）；交易唔使有價 — parent 免費送貨畀 subsidiary 都照樣要披露。

### Card 2
**Front:** List the family members who are ALWAYS "close members of the family of a person" under HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 9.

**Back:**
1. The person's children and spouse or domestic partner;
2. Children of the person's spouse or domestic partner;
3. Dependants of that person or that person's spouse or domestic partner.
- Parents / grandparents: not automatic — may qualify depending on facts and circumstances (principles-based).

💬 白話：三類必中 — 仔女＋配偶、配偶嘅仔女、受養人。父母、祖父母唔係自動包，要睇情況判斷。

### Card 3
**Front:** Define "key management personnel" (KMP) under HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 9.

**Back:**
- Persons having authority and responsibility for **planning, directing and controlling** the activities of the entity, directly or indirectly;
- **Including any director (whether executive or otherwise)** of that entity.
- KMP of the **parent** of the reporting entity also counts.

💬 白話：所有董事都係 KMP — non-executive、independent director 都計，唔好以為得 CEO/CFO。母公司嘅 KMP 都係子公司嘅 related party。

### Card 4
**Front:** State the THREE exclusions in HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 11 — parties that are NOT related parties.

**Back:**
1. Two entities simply because they have a director or KMP member **in common** (or KMP of one has significant influence over the other);
2. **Two joint venturers** simply because they share joint control of a joint venture;
3. A **customer, supplier, franchisor, distributor or general agent** with significant business volume, by virtue of economic dependence alone.

💬 白話：記住三個「唔係」— 共同董事、共同控制 JV 嘅拍檔、大客大供應商。考試 MCQ 十居其九會放一個 economic dependence distractor。

### Card 5
**Front:** What parent-subsidiary relationship disclosures does HKAS 24 (Revised) paras. 13 and 16 require, and do they depend on transactions occurring?

**Back:**
- Disclose parent–subsidiary relationships **irrespective of whether transactions have occurred**.
- Disclose the **name of the parent** and, if different, the **ultimate controlling party**.
- If the parent / ultimate parent does not produce consolidated FS available for public use, disclose the **next most senior parent** that does.

💬 白話：呢個係「關係披露」，有冇交易都要寫。母公司唔係最終控制人就要連 ultimate controlling party 個名都披露；冇 public consolidated FS 就要搵上一層有嘅 parent。

### Card 6
**Front:** List the MINIMUM disclosures required by HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 18 when related party transactions have occurred.

**Back:**
- Nature of the related party relationship;
- Amount of the transactions;
- Amount of outstanding balances (including commitments), their **terms and conditions** (incl. whether secured, nature of consideration for settlement) and **guarantees given or received**;
- **Provisions for doubtful debts** related to outstanding balances;
- **Bad/doubtful debt expense** recognised in the period re amounts due from related parties.

💬 白話：記「關係＋金額＋結餘條款＋擔保＋壞賬」五步。免息、無抵押呢啲「唔正常」條款正正係要披露嘅重點。

### Card 7
**Front:** List the FIVE categories of KMP compensation disclosure required by HKAS 24 (Revised) para. 17. Is per-individual disclosure required?

**Back:**
1. Short-term employee benefits;
2. Post-employment benefits;
3. Other long-term benefits;
4. Termination benefits;
5. Share-based payment.
- Disclosed **in total and by category** — disclosure of compensation of **each individual** KMP is **NOT** required by HKAS 24 (Revised).

💬 白話：口訣「短、退、長、散、股」。個別董事薪酬係 Companies Ordinance s.383 嘅事，HKAS 24 只要總數分五類。

### Card 8
**Front:** True or False: (i) Arm's length transactions between related parties need not be disclosed. (ii) A director who resigned mid-year is not a related party for that year. Explain.

**Back:**
- (i) **False** — all transactions between related parties must be disclosed, whether on arm's length or non-arm's length terms.
- (ii) **False** — related parties may change from period to period; a person who was KMP for **part of the period** is still a related party for that period.

💬 白話：兩個經典伏位 — 市價交易一樣要披露；年中離任嘅 director 嗰年都仲係 related party。見到 "arm's length so no disclosure" 即刻知係陷阱。

## 本章急救詞彙

| EN Term | 繁中白話 | English Model Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Related party | 關聯方／關連人士（個人或公司） | A related party is a person or entity that is related to the reporting entity preparing its financial statements. |
| Related party transaction | 關聯方交易（唔使有作價） | A related party transaction is a transfer of resources, services or obligations between related parties regardless of whether a price is charged. |
| Key management personnel (KMP) | 主要管理人員（包所有董事） | Key management personnel include any director, whether executive or otherwise, of the entity. |
| Close members of the family | 密切家庭成員（配偶仔女受養人） | The spouse of a director is a close member of the family and therefore a related party of the entity. |
| Arm's length transaction | 公平／獨立市價交易 | Even transactions conducted on an arm's length basis must be disclosed if they occur between related parties. |
| Ultimate controlling party | 最終控制方 | The subsidiary must disclose the name of its parent and, if different, the ultimate controlling party. |
| Economic dependence | 經濟依賴（≠ related party） | A major customer is not a related party merely because of the entity's economic dependence on that customer. |
| Outstanding balances (including commitments) | 未結算餘額（包括承擔） | The entity must disclose the amount of outstanding balances with related parties, their terms and conditions, and any guarantees given or received. |
