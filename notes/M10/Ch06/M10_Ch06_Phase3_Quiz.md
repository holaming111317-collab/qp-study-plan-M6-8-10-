**Q3.** Derek, the sole proprietor of a Hong Kong trading firm, agreed at the request of an old classmate to receive HK$8 million into his company's bank account and to remit the same amount the following day to an offshore account, keeping HK$50,000 as a "handling fee". Derek found the arrangement strange and suspected the money might be connected with illegal gambling, but he asked no questions and carried out the transfers. At trial, the prosecution is **unable to trace or prove the actual origin** of the HK$8 million. Which of the following statements best reflects the law?

A. Derek cannot be convicted of money laundering because the prosecution must prove that the HK$8 million was in fact the proceeds of an identified predicate offence.
B. Derek can be convicted under s.25(1) of the OSCO (Cap. 455) if he had reasonable grounds to believe that the money represented the proceeds of an indictable offence; the prosecution need not prove that the money was in fact such proceeds.
C. Derek cannot be convicted because a person who merely processes one single transfer at a friend's request lacks the necessary mens rea.
D. Derek can only be prosecuted under s.25(1) of the DTROPO (Cap. 405), and since no drugs are involved, no money laundering offence is available.

**Q4.** Marco is the purchasing manager of a private furniture retailer, Homer Living Ltd. A timber supplier, Forest Way Ltd, transfers an interest-free "loan" of HK$200,000, repayable "when convenient", into the bank account of Marco's wife. Two weeks later, Marco awards Forest Way Ltd a one-year supply contract. Marco has never disclosed the loan to Homer Living Ltd, whose internal policy requires prior written approval from the Board for any benefit from suppliers. Which of the following best analyses Marco's position under the Prevention of Bribery Ordinance (Cap. 201)?

A. No offence — a loan is a commercial arrangement and does not amount to an "advantage" under s.2 of the POBO.
B. An offence under s.9(3) of the POBO — Marco deceived his principal by means of a false receipt, account or other document.
C. An offence under s.9(1) of the POBO — Marco, as an agent, accepted an advantage without lawful authority or reasonable excuse as an inducement for an act in relation to his principal's affairs.
D. An offence under s.4 of the POBO — Marco solicited or accepted an advantage as a public servant.

**Q5.** Peggy is the compliance manager of a money service operator in Hong Kong. During a routine review, she discovers that a long-standing customer has been making monthly remittances to an overseas organisation which was added last week to the list of designated terrorists under the United Nations (Anti-Terrorism Measures) Ordinance (Cap. 575) ('UNATMO'). Peggy now suspects that the remitted funds are terrorist property. Afraid of losing the customer, she refuses to process the latest remittance, but sends the customer a WhatsApp message saying: "Your beneficiary is now a listed terrorist group — you'd better route future transfers under a different name." She makes no report to any authority, and **no suspicious transaction report has been filed by anyone** in respect of these remittances. Which of the following best analyses Peggy's criminal exposure?

A. Peggy has committed tipping-off under s.12(5) of the UNATMO by warning the customer, and this is the only offence she faces.
B. Peggy has committed the offence of failing to disclose her knowledge or suspicion of terrorist property to an authorised officer as soon as practicable, contrary to s.12(1) of the UNATMO (maximum penalty under s.14(5): 3 months' imprisonment and a fine); tipping-off under s.12(5) does not arise because it requires knowledge or suspicion that a disclosure has already been made, and none has been made.
C. Peggy has committed no offence, because the remittances might have had legitimate humanitarian purposes and suspicion alone never creates any duty.
D. Peggy has committed the offence under s.7 of the UNATMO of providing property for terrorist acts, because her employer's remittance channel was used by the customer.
