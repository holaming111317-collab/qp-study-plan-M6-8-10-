# M10 Chapter 11: Company Capital and Financing Companies — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. **Compare and contrast** the different classes of share capital and the different rights held by shareholders and debenture holders.
2. **Analyse** the legal implications of raising loan capital, including types of debentures, company charges and the registration of charges under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) ("CO").
3. **Apply** the common law rules on priority between competing fixed and floating charges in the event of receivership and winding-up.
4. **Explain and apply** the doctrine of capital maintenance, including reduction of capital, redemption/buy-back of shares, financial assistance and distributions of dividends.
5. **Advise on** the procedures for the issue, transfer and variation of class rights of shares.

> 以下所有 "s." 條文引用，除特別註明外，均指 Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)。

### 必背考點

#### A. Debt Financing vs Equity Financing（法律層面 4 大分別）
- **Repayment obligation**：debt 有固定還款義務（principal + interest），與公司業績無關；equity 無固定還款期，capital 被鎖定，只在 liquidation、permitted reduction of capital 或 share re-purchase 時退回 → share capital 又稱 **risk capital**。
- **Control**：equity investor 成為 member 有股東權；debt investor 仍是 outsider（loan documents 可給予有限控制，如 nominee director 或委任 receiver）。
- **Costs**：debt financing 一般成本較低。
- **Gearing ratio** = total debt / total equity，反映 credit worthiness。
- 公司越過章程借款上限，貸款**仍然有效**（ss.116–117）。

#### B. Debentures（Ordinances + 分類）
- **Definition**：s.307 — debenture 包括 bonds 及任何其他 debt securities，不論是否構成對公司資產的 charge。
- **Register of debenture holders**：發行 a series of debentures 或 debenture stock 時須備存（s.308(2) 列明內容：holder 姓名地址職業、持有數額、登記及終止日期）；**single debenture（如普通銀行貸款）不適用**。開放查閱：members 及 debenture holders 免費每日至少 2 小時，其他人付費（s.310）。
- **Allotment registration**：allotment of debentures / debenture stock 須於配發後 **1 個月內**向 Companies Registry 登記（s.316）——不涵蓋 single debenture。
- **Transfer**：必須有 instrument of transfer 方可登記（s.320）；登記須於 instrument 交付後 **2 個月內**完成（s.321）；**無 stamp duty**；死亡/破產/法律施行而轉移（transmission）不需 instrument（s.320(2)）。
- **Private company** 不得邀請公眾認購其 debentures 或 shares（s.11(1)）。
- **Types**：(1) redeemable / irredeemable（s.327 容許 irredeemable；s.328 容許 reissue redeemed debentures）；(2) convertible — 發行可換股 debenture 須先獲 members 批准（s.140(1)(b)）；(3) debenture stock（可拆細發行予不同認購人，各持有人 rank equally）；(4) bearer debenture（交付文件即轉讓）。

#### C. Types of Security
- **Mortgage**：legal title 轉移予 mortgagee；mortgagor 保留 **equity of redemption**；只有協議無轉讓 = equitable mortgage。Mortgagee 於 default 時可 (1) 接管及出售（普通法 + Conveyancing and Property Ordinance (Cap. 219) s.51 & Schedule 4，只適用於土地）；(2) **foreclosure**（消滅 equity of redemption 及債務）。
- **Charge**：**不轉移 title**，chargee 只取得 equitable proprietary interest；default 時向法院申請 power of sale（可明示或依普通法產生）。
- **Pledge**：possessory security，轉移 possession 而非 ownership；**pledgee 無 foreclosure 權**。
- **Guarantee** = personal security（與 real security 相對）。

#### D. Fixed vs Floating Charges（核心考點）
- **Re Yorkshire Woolcombers Association**（Romer LJ）三大 floating charge 特徵：
  1. charge 針對一**類** present and future 資產；
  2. 該類資產在 ordinary course of business 中不時變動；
  3. 在 chargee 採取行動前，公司可在日常業務中自由處理該類資產。
- **Crystallisation**（floating → fixed）觸發事件：
  - 法院作出 compulsory winding-up order（**僅提交 petition 或委任 provisional liquidator 不算**）；
  - 公司通過 voluntary winding-up resolution；
  - chargee 委任 receiver（不影響其他 chargee 的 floating charges）；
  - 雙方約定事件，如 default、其他 floating charge crystallised、chargee 發出轉換通知。
- **Automatic crystallisation clause**：對後設 chargee 的效力取決於其是否 **notice**；因 s.334 要求登記設立 charge 的 instrument，已登記 floating charge 可引發 **constructive notice**。**Negative pledge clause** 違反常觸發自動結晶（Cox v Dublin City Distillery (No. 2) (1915) IR 345）。
- **Romalpa clause / retention of title clause**：Aluminium Industrie Vaassen BV v Romalpa Aluminium Ltd (1976) WLR 676 — 賣方保留貨物 title 直至付清價款，可及於製成品或出售收益，效果等同 floating charge。

#### E. Registration of Charges（伏位密集）
- **Specified charges（s.334 清單）**：(1) uncalled share capital；(2) 如由自然人簽立須登記為 bill of sale 的文書所設/證明的 charge；(3) land 或其權益；(4) book debts；(5) goodwill 或 intellectual property；(6) floating charge。就登記而言 charge **包括 mortgage**（s.333(1)）；pledge、lien **不需登記**。
- **程序（s.335(1)）**：向 Registrar 交付 (1) specified form 的 particulars 陳述 + (2) 設立/證明 charge 文書的 certified copy。
- **期限**：charge 設立後 **1 個月內**（由設立翌日起計）（s.335(5)(a)）。
- **後果**：逾期不登記 → charge 對 **liquidator 及 creditors 無效（void）**（s.337(4)），但在 chargee 與公司之間**仍然有效**，對 unsecured creditor 及買方亦然；公司及其 responsible persons 負**刑事責任**（s.337(2)）。
- **延期**：法院可因 accidental / inadvertence / sufficient cause、不損害 creditors/members 且 just and equitable 而延長期限（s.346(3)）；獲准延期則無刑事責任（s.346(4)）。
- **公司自身 register of charges**：範圍更闊——**所有** fixed 及 floating charges 均須登記（s.352(2)）；不登記不影響效力但有刑事責任（s.352(3)）；members 及 creditors 付費可查閱（s.355）。
- **Non-Hong Kong companies**：就其在香港財產設立的 specified charges 同樣須登記，程序相同（s.336）；不登記同樣 void + 刑事責任（s.337）。

#### F. Priority Between Competing Charges（普通法規則；登記本身不賦予優先權）
- **Real property（土地）**：另須按 Land Registration Ordinance (Cap. 128) ("LRO") 登記；規則見 LRO ss.2A, 3, 5 — 基本機制：1 個月內登記 → first in time 優先；其一逾期 → 如期登記者優先；兩者均逾期 → 按登記日期（s.3(1)）；第一 charge 未登記而第二 chargee 是 bona fide purchaser for value without notice → 第一 charge 對其 void（s.3(2)）；均未登記 → 普通法。
- **Personal property**：
  1. **Fixed vs Fixed**：first in time prevails（Re Weniger's Policy (1910) 2 Ch 291 — 另有 further advance 時須查詢有否收到其他 charge 的 notice，否則優先權被押後）。
  2. **Floating vs Floating**：一般 first in time；但第一份 floating charge 保留權力可設立優先的後續 floating charge（Re Benjamin Cope & Sons Ltd (1914) 1 Ch 800 — 訂明 "rank pari passu" 亦不能推翻先設立者的優先）；可用 **subordination agreement** 調整。
  3. **Fixed vs Floating**：**fixed 一般優先**（Wheatley v Silkstone and Haigh Moor Coal Co (1885) 29 Ch 715）；但若 floating charge 在後設 fixed charge **之前已 crystallised**，則結晶後的 charge 優先。
- **Debts / choses in action**：以 **notice** 定優先——第一個向 debtor 發出 charge 通知的 chargee 優先。
- **Winding-up 分配次序**：fixed chargee 取 proceeds（扣除變現成本、receiver 報酬等）→ 餘額給 general creditors → members；floating chargee 之前還要先滿足 **preferential creditors**（僱員欠款、政府法定債項）。Unsecured creditor 排最後，資產不足時按 **pari passu** 比例分配。

#### G. Share Capital
- **Authorised capital 已廢除**——無上限。
- 類型：**issued capital**（已發行 × 發行價）；**paid-up / unpaid**；**called-up**（paid-up + 已催繳未付）/**uncalled**。
- **Class rights**：同類股份權利須完全一致（s.178(1)）；variation 受 ss.176–184 規管：
  - 默認規則（章程無規定時）：holders 至少 **75% total voting rights 書面同意**（s.180(1)）**或**在 **class meeting** 通過 **special resolution**（s.180(3)）；
  - variation 後 14 日內書面通知該類股東（s.181）；1 個月內向 Registrar 提交通知（含 statement of capital）及決議副本（s.184）；
  - 持不少於 **10%** 該類投票權的持有人可向法院申請 disallow variation；法院信納 variation **unfairly prejudice** 申請人所代表的 members 時可推翻（s.182(1), (5)）。
- **White v Bristol Aeroplane Co Ltd (1953) Ch 65 (CA)**：發行與現有股份 rank pari passu 的新股，只影響既有股東權利的 **enjoyment**，**不是 variation of class rights**，無需 class consent。
- **Issue of shares**：創辦成員股份視為於註冊日發行（s.112）；其後發行 = **allotment**，章程無限制則數量不限（s.85(2)）；**non-pro rata allotment** 須 members 在 general meeting 事先批准，**ordinary resolution 即可**（ss.140–141）；程序：subscription contract → (non-pro rata 時股東批准) → board 批准及發股證 → **2 個月內**登記入 register of members（s.143；登記才成為 member，s.2）→ **1 個月內**向 Registrar 提交 return of allotment（s.142）。
- **Transfer of shares**：sale & purchase contract → instrument of transfer 連同舊股證交公司批准（s.150）→ contract notes + **ad valorem stamp duty 各 0.1%**（以代價或股份價值較高者計，Stamp Duty Ordinance (Cap. 117) s.19(1)）→ 董事批准登記並更新 register of members（s.627）→ 註銷舊證發新證（s.155）。**Transmission**（死亡/破產/法院 vesting order）屬法律施行轉移。
- **Register of members**（s.627 內容）；可電子形式（s.655）；須存於 registered office 或香港其他地方（s.628）；前成員記錄保存 **10 年**（s.627(5)）。**Share certificate** 在無相反證據時是 title 的證明（s.137），但真正 title 以 register of members 為準（s.2）。

#### H. Doctrine of Capital Maintenance 及其四大禁制與例外
普通法原則：share capital 不得退回股東（winding-up 除外），因為 creditors 依賴公司已繳資本作為信用基礎。四大禁制：
1. **Reduction of share capital** → 只可循兩途：(a) **special resolution + solvency statement**（Sub-division 2 of Division 3 of Part 5）；(b) **special resolution 經法院確認**（Sub-division 3 of Division 3 of Part 5；s.226）。Solvency statement 由**全體董事**作出（s.220）；solvency test 見 s.205（能償還 12 個月內到期債務）。法院考慮：股東獲公平對待、方案已妥善解釋、creditors 利益受保障、有可辨識目的。**Re Fok Ying Tung Ming Yuan Development Co Ltd (2016) 2 HKLRD 292**：法院以資產負債表上的 share capital 為準；公司有巨額 accumulated loss 卻描述為無虧損屬誤導，且核數師發出 disclaimer → 拒絕確認。
2. **Self-acquisition of shares** → s.267 禁止；例外：
   - **Redeemable shares**：s.234(1) 容許發行（章程可禁止/限制，s.234(2)）；**不得只有 redeemable shares**（s.234(3)）；只限 **fully paid** 股份方可 redeem/buy back（s.268）；redemption 後股份視為註銷（s.269(1)）；資金來源：distributable profits / 為此目的新發行股份所得 / 依 Sub-division 6 of Division 4 of Part 5 動用 capital。
   - **Buy-back（unlisted company）**：合約須事先經 **special resolution** 批准（s.244）。若以 **share capital** 支付：special resolution 批准 + **付款須在決議後第 5 至第 7 週之間**（s.258）；董事作出 solvency statement 並向全體 members 提供，**special resolution 須在 solvency statement 日期後 15 日內通過**（s.259）。
3. **Financial assistance for acquisition of shares** → 禁止公司**直接或間接**資助他人收購本公司股份（收購前或當時），包括**子公司資助收購控股公司股份**（s.275(1)）。"Financial assistance" 定義廣：gift、guarantee/security/indemnity/release/waiver、loan、或令 net assets 重大減少的其他資助（s.274(1)）。違反有**刑事責任**（s.275(4)），但交易本身**不因此當然無效**（s.276，仍可因董事違反 fiduciary duties 等其他理由被撤銷）。**例外（ss.277–289）**：
   - 全體董事作 solvency statement + (i) 資助 ≤ **5% paid-up capital**，或 (ii) **全體股東一致同意**，或 (iii) **ordinary resolution** 通過（ss.283–285）；
   - **principal purpose exception**：資助並非交易主要目的，只是附帶於公司某更大目的，且真誠為公司利益而給予（s.278）；
   - 法院命令。
   - **Ricco (Int'l) Co Ltd v Uni-Harvest Int'l Ltd (2017) 6 HKC 487**：須區分 "a purpose" 與 "the reason why a purpose was formed"——業務擴展只是配股的 **reason**，不是可开脫的 larger **purpose**；10 年延誤本身（無 prejudice）不阻礙救濟。
   - **Carney v Herbert (1985) AC 301**：子公司 mortgage 構成非法 financial assistance，但非法部分可 **sever**——personal guarantee 仍然可執行。
4. **Dividends other than out of profits** → distribution 只可從 **profits available for distribution** 支付（s.297(1)）= accumulated realised profits − accumulated realised losses（s.297(2)），即**自成立以來累計**，往年虧損須先彌補。須參考最近期財務報表（s.302；中期報表亦可，ss.302、305）。違法分派：董事/核數師可能 breach of duty；**member 明知構成違反的事實**（不論是否知悉屬違法）須退還（s.301）。GM 宣派的股息**不得超過董事建議的數額**（Model Articles Art. 91(1)）；董事可派 interim dividends（Art. 91(2)）；可以 bonus shares / capitalisation of profits 方式支付（Arts. 77, 79）。

#### I. IRAC Application Points（答題框架示範）
- **Charge 優先權題**：**Issue** — 哪個 creditor 優先受償？**Rule** — 先查是否 specified charge（s.334）及有否在 1 個月內登記（s.335(5)(a)）；未登記 → s.337(4) void against liquidator/creditors；再按財產類型套用普通法（fixed vs floating：Wheatley；先結晶者優先；自動結晶條款視乎 notice）。**Application** — 逐項對應事實（設立日期、登記日期、結晶事件）。**Conclusion** — 排定受償次序。
- **Buy-back / reduction 題**：**Issue** — 回購/減資是否有效？**Rule** — s.267 禁制 → s.244 + ss.258–259 程序（時限！）或 s.234 redeemable shares；reduction 則 s.220 solvency statement 或 s.226 法院確認 + Re Fok Ying Tung 的 balance sheet 原則。**Application** — 逐步核對 solvency statement 日期、special resolution 日期、付款週數。**Conclusion** — 有效/無效及補救。
- **Financial assistance 題**：**Issue** — 是否構成 prohibited financial assistance？**Rule** — s.275(1) + s.274(1) 廣義定義；例外 s.278 / ss.283–285；Ricco 的 purpose vs reason 區分。**Application** — 資助形式（guarantee/loan/gift）、目的、董事 solvency statement、批准方式。**Conclusion** — 刑事責任（s.275(4)）但交易不當然無效（s.276）。
- **Class rights 題**：**Issue** — 是否 variation of class rights？**Rule** — s.178(1)、s.180；White v Bristol Aeroplane 的 rights vs enjoyment 區分。**Application** — 權利本身有否改變，還是僅商業上被攤薄。**Conclusion** — 如需 variation：75% 書面同意或 class meeting special resolution；10% 持有人可入稟（s.182）。

### 易混淆概念對比

**表 1：Fixed Charge vs Floating Charge**

| 比較點 | Fixed Charge | Floating Charge |
|---|---|---|
| 附著的資產 | 特定、可識別的資產（ascertained and definite property） | 一類 present and future、不時變動的資產（Re Yorkshire Woolcombers 三特徵） |
| 公司可否自由處理資產 | 不可以 | 可以，只要在 ordinary course of business 內（直至 crystallisation） |
| 優先權 | 一般優先於 floating charge（Wheatley v Silkstone） | 遜於後設的 fixed charge，除非先行 crystallised |
| 靈活性（對借款人） | 低；只能就現有資產設立 | 高；可涵蓋將來取得的資產 |
| Crystallisation | 不適用（本來就是 fixed） | winding-up order、自願清盤決議、委任 receiver、約定事件（如 default） |
| 考試伏位（繁中） | 點樣分辨：睇公司係咪仍然可以自由買賣啲資產——唔得就係 fixed | 最常考伏位：「呈交 winding-up petition 即結晶」係**錯**——要法院作出 order 先結晶；委任 provisional liquidator 都唔算 |

**表 2：Variation of Class Rights vs 只影響 Enjoyment of Rights**

| 比較點 | Variation of Class Rights | Affecting Enjoyment of Rights Only |
|---|---|---|
| 定義 | 股份**附著的權利本身**改變（如取消固定股息、改投票權） | 權利本身不變，只係商業上被攤薄/削弱（如發行 pari passu 新股） |
| 權威案例 | — | White v Bristol Aeroplane Co Ltd (1953) Ch 65 (CA) |
| 所需同意 | 75% 書面同意（s.180(1)）或 class meeting special resolution（s.180(3)） | 無需 class consent（仍可能受其他規則管，如 non-pro rata allotment 須 members 批准，ss.140–141） |
| 不滿股東補救 | ≥10% 該類投票權持有人可入稟申請 disallow（s.182(1)），法院以 unfair prejudice 為準（s.182(5)） | 無 s.182 補救（或考慮其他如 unfair prejudice petition，屬 Ch.10） |
| 考試伏位（繁中） | 點樣分辨：問自己「張股票上面寫嘅權利改咗未？」改咗先係 variation | 最常見陷阱：考生見「dilution」就答 variation——其實 dilution 只影響 enjoyment，跟 White v Bristol Aeroplane 唔使 class consent |

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** State the four legal differences between debt financing and equity financing.
**Back:**
- Repayment obligation: debt = fixed amount (principal + interest), independent of business performance; equity = no fixed repayment, capital locked up except on liquidation / permitted reduction / buy-back (risk capital)
- Control: equity investors become members with shareholder rights; debt investors remain outsiders
- Costs: debt generally cheaper
- Gearing ratio = total debt / total equity; used by lenders to assess borrowing capacity
💬 記住口訣「還錢、控制、成本、槓桿」。Equity 最關鍵一句：capital 鎖死，業績好先至有股息，所以叫 risk capital；債權人唔使理公司賺唔賺錢，到期就要還。

### Card 2
**Front:** What is a "debenture" under the CO, and what are the four types of debentures?
**Back:**
- s.307 CO: includes bonds and any other debt securities of the company, whether or not constituting a charge on the company's assets
- Four types: (1) redeemable / irredeemable (ss.327–328); (2) convertible (members' approval required — s.140(1)(b)); (3) debenture stock (certificates for fractions of the whole, holders rank equally); (4) bearer debentures (transfer by mere delivery)
- Registration: allotment of debentures/stock within 1 month (s.316); transfer registered within 2 months of lodgment (s.321); no stamp duty on debenture transfers
💬 Debenture 即係公司出嘅「借據文件」。考試最常考定義（s.307）同埋時間限制：allotment 一個月、transfer 登記兩個月。Single debenture（普通銀行貸款）唔使登記，呢個係伏位。

### Card 3
**Front:** List Romer LJ's three characteristics of a floating charge and the events of crystallisation.
**Back:**
- Re Yorkshire Woolcombers Association: (1) charge on a class of assets, present and future; (2) the class changes from time to time in the ordinary course of business; (3) until the chargee takes a step, the company may carry on business and deal with the assets in the ordinary way
- Crystallisation: (1) court order for compulsory winding-up (NOT mere petition / provisional liquidator); (2) resolution for voluntary winding-up; (3) appointment of receiver by the chargee; (4) agreed events (default, crystallisation of other floating charges, notice to convert)
💬 三特徵記「一類資產、不時變、自由處理」。結晶事件最易中伏：petition 未結晶，order 先結晶。結晶後 floating 變 fixed，公司唔可以再自由賣啲資產。

### Card 4
**Front:** What must be delivered to the Registrar to register a charge, by when, and what are the consequences of non-registration?
**Back:**
- Deliver (s.335(1)): (1) statement of particulars in specified form; (2) certified copy of the instrument creating/evidencing the charge
- Deadline: within one month of creation, starting the day after creation (s.335(5)(a))
- Non-registration: charge VOID against the liquidator and creditors (s.337(4)) — but remains valid between chargee and company; criminal offence for company and responsible persons (s.337(2))
- Court may extend time if failure was accidental / inadvertence / sufficient cause, not prejudicial and just and equitable (s.346(3)); extension removes criminal liability (s.346(4))
💬 大伏位：「void」唔係對所有人無效——只係對 liquidator 同其他 creditors 無效，對公司本身仍然有效。仲有 chargee 可以自己登記（s.335(3)），費用可向公司追返（s.335(7)）。

### Card 5
**Front:** State the common law priority rules for competing charges over personal property, with authorities.
**Back:**
- Fixed vs fixed: first in time prevails (Re Weniger's Policy (1910) 2 Ch 291 — further advance postponed if chargee failed to enquire about notice of intervening charge)
- Floating vs floating: first in time, unless the first charge reserved power to create a later charge with priority; pari passu wording does not displace earlier priority (Re Benjamin Cope & Sons Ltd (1914) 1 Ch 800); subordination agreement can regulate priorities
- Fixed vs floating: fixed generally prevails (Wheatley v Silkstone and Haigh Moor Coal Co (1885) 29 Ch 715); BUT an earlier floating charge that crystallised before the later fixed charge has priority
- Charges over debts/choses in action: priority by notice — first chargee to notify the debtor wins
💬 一句總結：「fixed 大過 floating，先結晶當 fixed 計」。記住 Wheatley：floating charge 唔阻止公司之後再畀人 fixed charge，所以 floating chargee 通常會加 negative pledge + automatic crystallisation clause 自保。

### Card 6
**Front:** What is the default procedure for varying class rights, and what did White v Bristol Aeroplane decide?
**Back:**
- Same class = rights uniform in all respects (s.178(1)); variation governed by ss.176–184
- Default (articles silent): written consent of holders of ≥75% of total voting rights of the class (s.180(1)) OR special resolution at a class meeting (s.180(3))
- Notice to class shareholders within 14 days (s.181); filing with Registrar within 1 month (s.184); ≥10% of class voting rights may apply to court to disallow for unfair prejudice (s.182(1), (5))
- White v Bristol Aeroplane Co Ltd (1953) Ch 65 (CA): issuing new shares ranking pari passu with existing shares affects only the ENJOYMENT of rights, not the rights themselves — NOT a variation, no class consent needed
💬 考試見「發新股攤薄咗我嘅投票權」九成係 White v Bristol Aeroplane——唔係 variation。Variation 係指改變權利本身，例如取消 preference dividend。10% 入稟門檻同 14 日通知都係數字伏位。

### Card 7
**Front:** Explain the doctrine of capital maintenance and the four transactions it generally prohibits, with their statutory gateways.
**Back:**
- Common law: share capital may not be returned to members except on winding-up — creditors rely on paid-up capital as the fund of credit
- Four prohibited transactions + gateways:
  1. Reduction of capital → special resolution + solvency statement by ALL directors (s.220; solvency test s.205) OR special resolution confirmed by court (s.226; Re Fok Ying Tung Ming Yuan Development Co Ltd (2016) 2 HKLRD 292)
  2. Self-acquisition → prohibited (s.267); redeemable shares (s.234, fully paid only s.268, cancelled s.269(1)); buy-back by unlisted company with contract pre-approved by special resolution (s.244); if funded out of capital: payment 5–7 weeks after SR (s.258), SR within 15 days of solvency statement (s.259)
  3. Financial assistance → prohibited directly or indirectly, incl. subsidiary for holding company (s.275(1)); exceptions ss.277–289
  4. Dividends → only out of profits available for distribution (s.297(1)–(2))
💬 背誦框架：「一個原則、四條禁令、每條禁令一條生路」。時限數字最易考：buy-back 用 capital 支付 = 5–7 週內付款、SR 要喺 solvency statement 後 15 日內通過。

### Card 8
**Front:** What is the rule against financial assistance for acquisition of shares, its exceptions and the leading cases?
**Back:**
- Prohibition: company must not give financial assistance directly or indirectly for acquiring its own shares (before or at acquisition); extends to subsidiary assisting acquisition of holding company's shares (s.275(1))
- Definition (s.274(1)): gift; guarantee/security/indemnity/release/waiver; loan; other assistance reducing net assets by a material extent
- Consequences: criminal liability (s.275(4)); transaction NOT invalid per se (s.276)
- Exceptions (ss.277–289): solvency statement by ALL directors + (≤5% of paid-up capital / unanimous shareholder approval / ordinary resolution) (ss.283–285); principal purpose exception — assistance incidental to some larger purpose, given in good faith in the company's interest (s.278); court order
- Ricco (Int'l) Co Ltd v Uni-Harvest Int'l Ltd (2017) 6 HKC 487: distinguish "a purpose" from "the reason why a purpose was formed" — business expansion was merely a reason, exception failed
- Carney v Herbert (1985) AC 301: illegal mortgage severable; personal guarantee enforceable
💬 Ricco 係答題關鍵：「幫公司擴張業務」只係 reason 唔係 purpose，唔好以為講句「為公司好」就過到 s.278。仲要記住違規係刑事，但交易本身唔會自動無效。
