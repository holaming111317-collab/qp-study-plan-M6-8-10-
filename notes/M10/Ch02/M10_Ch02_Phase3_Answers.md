# M10 Chapter 2: Principles of Contract and Consumer Law in Hong Kong (Part 1) — Phase 3 Model Answers

### Q1 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):**
Issue: whether the window display constituted an offer capable of acceptance by Marcus. Rule: a shop-window display of goods with a price tag is an invitation to treat, not an offer (Fisher v Bell (1961) 1 QB 394; Pharmaceutical Society of Great Britain v Boots Cash Chemists (Southern) Ltd (1953) 1 QB 401). The customer makes the offer when presenting the goods (or the price) to the shop, which the shop is free to accept or reject. Application: the display of the Rolex with the HK$88,000 tag was an invitation to treat; Marcus's tender of HK$88,000 was his offer. Golden Hour Jewellery Ltd never accepted it. Conclusion: no contract was formed and the shop is not bound to sell at the displayed price.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
呢題係經典「櫥窗陳列 = invitation to treat」考點。Option A 係最常見錯誤：Carlill 嘅例外只適用於懸賞式 unilateral contract（承諾任何人完成指定行動即有獎賞），一個標價牌唔係向全世界出嘅懸賞。Option C 將 Thornton v Shoe Lane Parking 嘅「standing offer」亂套——嗰個係針對自動售票機（無法議價嘅機器），櫥窗展示背後有店主可以議價，所以唔係 standing offer。Option D 係混淆視聽：unilateral mistake as to terms 嘅前提係已經有合約（Hartog v Colin and Shields），呢度根本連 offer 都未成立，唔使講 mistake。記住答呢類題要先問「邊個先出 offer」——喺舖頭情境永遠係客人出 offer。

**Rule Check:** Fisher v Bell (1961) 1 QB 394; Pharmaceutical Society of Great Britain v Boots Cash Chemists (Southern) Ltd (1953) 1 QB 401

**Common Trap（繁中）:**
見到「標錯價」就急住答 mistake——其實第一層 issue 係 offer vs invitation to treat；陳列品從來唔係 offer，後面所有 mistake 分析都係多餘。另外唔好一見「advertisement/display」就諗 Carlill；Carlill 只限 reward/unilateral contract。

### Q2 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):**
Under s.7(1) of the Control of Exemption Clauses Ordinance (Cap.71), a person cannot, by reference to any contract term, exclude or restrict his/her business liability for death or personal injury resulting from negligence. The prohibition is absolute — the reasonableness test does not apply. By contrast, s.7(2) permits exclusion of liability for OTHER loss or damage caused by negligence (e.g. property damage, financial loss) only if the term satisfies the reasonableness test in s.3(1). Section 8 deals with liability arising in contract against a consumer or a party on the other's written standard terms (reasonableness required), and s.5(1) of the UCO (Cap.458) deals with unconscionable consumer contracts generally, not negligence liability.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
呢題考 pinpoint section knowledge。CECO 嘅結構要背熟：s.7 管疏忽責任，(1) 死傷 = 絕對唔可以豁免；(2) 其他損失 = 過 reasonableness test 先得。Option A (s.7(2)) 係「可以豁免但要合理」——掉轉咗。Option B (s.8(1)) 係違約責任（consumer/standard terms），唔係疏忽致死傷。Option D 係 UCO，佢管 unconscionable terms 整體，而且舉證門檻高、唔係「絕對禁止」。考試伏位：題目關鍵字係 "IMPOSSIBLE, in any circumstances"——CECO 入面絕對禁止嘅就只有 s.7(1) 同 s.11(1)（SOGO s.14 業權承諾）呢兩類。

**Rule Check:** Control of Exemption Clauses Ordinance (Cap.71) s.7(1), s.7(2), s.8(1); Unconscionable Contracts Ordinance (Cap.458) s.5(1)

**Common Trap（繁中）:**
s.7(1) 同 s.7(2) 掉亂——死傷永遠唔得豁免，財物損失可以先要過合理性測試。背誦口訣：「死傷絕對，財損合理」。

### Q3 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):**
Issue: whether Daniel's day-3 email destroyed Karen's offer. Rule: acceptance must mirror the offer exactly; a counter-offer destroys the original offer (Hyde v Wrench (1840) 3 Beav 334), but a mere request for information — an enquiry about the flexibility of the terms — leaves the offer intact (Stevenson, Jacques & Co v McLean (1880) 5 QBD 346). Application: Daniel's day-3 email ("would you consider HK$320,000…?") was framed as an enquiry, not a definite varied proposal capable of immediate acceptance; it is therefore a request for information and Karen's offer of HK$350,000 remained open. Karen had stipulated the offer would remain open for 14 days, so it had not lapsed by day 9. Daniel's day-9 email was an unconditional, mirror-image acceptance, communicated to Karen. Conclusion: a binding contract was formed on day 9 at HK$350,000; Karen must sell.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
呢題係 mirror-image rule 嘅 classic twist。分辨 counter-offer 同 request for information 要睇措辭：「Would you consider…?」係探問彈性，唔係一個可以即時 accept 嘅確定提議——如果 Daniel 寫「I will pay HK$320,000 in six instalments」，咁先係 counter-offer（Hyde v Wrench）。Option C 唔得：Karen 自己講明 offer 開放 14 日，day 9 遠未過期，冇 lapse of time 問題（Ramsgate 係講冇指明期限先按合理時間計）。Option D 錯：silence 唔係 acceptance，acceptance 必須 communicated（雖然可以有 acceptance by conduct，但 Karen 完全冇任何行為）。

**Rule Check:** Stevenson, Jacques & Co v McLean (1880) 5 QBD 346; Hyde v Wrench (1840) 3 Beav 334

**Common Trap（繁中）:**
見到個回覆提咗另一個價錢就即刻答「counter-offer destroyed the offer」——呢個正正係考官預設嘅錯誤。一定要逐字分析個回覆：係確定性建議定係查詢？考試伏位第二層：就算係 counter-offer，原本 offer 只係 destroyed，唔係話 offeror 之後可以默認接受。

### Q4 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):**
Issue: whether Sparky's completion of work it was already contractually obliged to perform is good consideration for BrightBuild's promise of an extra HK$150,000. Rule: performance of an existing contractual duty owed to the promisor is generally no consideration (Stilk v Myrick (1809) 2 Camp 317), but under Williams v Roffey Bros & Nicholls (Contractors) Ltd (1991) 1 QB 1 (applied in Hong Kong in UBC (Construction) Ltd v Sung Foo Kee Ltd (1993) 2 HKLR 207), the promise is enforceable where: (1) A has contracted to do work for B; (2) before completion, B has reason to doubt A will complete; (3) B promises extra payment for on-time performance; (4) B thereby obtains a practical benefit or obviates a disbenefit; (5) there is no economic duress or fraud by A; and (6) the benefit is capable of being consideration. Application: all criteria are met — BrightBuild doubted timely completion, promised the extra HK$150,000, and obtained practical benefits: avoiding the HK$500,000 late-completion penalty under the main contract and avoiding the trouble and expense of engaging a replacement subcontractor; there was no duress or fraud. Conclusion: the promise is supported by consideration and Sparky can enforce the HK$150,000.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
呢題係「promises to pay more」嘅 standard scenario。Option A 係半對：Stilk v Myrick 係 general rule，但 Williams v Roffey 係公認嘅 refined exception，答 A 即係忽略咗 practical benefit。Option C 係張冠李戴：Pinnel's Case 係「promises to accept LESS」（還少咗債）嘅 rule，唔適用於「俾多啲」，而且 Sparky 係完全履行唔係 partial payment。Option D 係 Exam Lens 特別警告嘅陷阱：promissory estoppel 係 shield not a sword（Combe v Combe (1951) 2 KB 215）——Sparky 係原告拎嚟告人，estoppel 即刻死。六項 criteria 要逐條對落事實，特別係 (4) practical benefit（避咗 penalty clause + 避咗搵替工嘅麻煩）同 (5) 無 duress/fraud。

**Rule Check:** Williams v Roffey Bros & Nicholls (Contractors) Ltd (1991) 1 QB 1; Stilk v Myrick (1809) 2 Camp 317; Combe v Combe (1951) 2 KB 215

**Common Trap（繁中）:**
兩大陷阱：(1) 將 Williams v Roffey 嘅 practical benefit 套用落「收少啲」嘅債務減免——唔得，Re Selectmove Ltd (1995) 1 WLR 474 話明 Foakes v Beer 繼續管住 part-payment；(2) 原告身份用 estoppel。答題口訣：「俾多用 Roffey，收少用 Foakes，告人唔好用 estoppel」。

### Q5 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):**
Issue: whether Clause 12 validly excludes FitZone's liability for (a) Ms. Wong's personal injury and (b) her damaged smartphone. Rule: assuming incorporation (the clause was in a signed document — L'Estrange v E Graucob Ltd (1934) 2 KB 394) and clear wording, statutory control applies. Under s.7(1) of the Control of Exemption Clauses Ordinance (Cap.71), business liability for death or personal injury resulting from negligence can NEVER be excluded or restricted. Under s.7(2), liability for other loss or damage (e.g. property damage) resulting from negligence may be excluded only if the term satisfies the reasonableness test in s.3(1) — the term must be fair and reasonable to include having regard to circumstances known or contemplated when the contract was made, and the burden of proving reasonableness rests on FitZone, with reference to the Schedule 2 guidelines (relative bargaining strength, inducement, knowledge of the term, etc.). Application: FitZone's negligent failure to maintain the treadmill caused the fractured wrist — Clause 12 is wholly ineffective as to that claim regardless of reasonableness. As to the smartphone (property damage), the clause is effective only if FitZone discharges its burden of proving reasonableness; against a consumer signing non-negotiable standard terms, this may be difficult. Conclusion: Ms. Wong can recover for her injury in full; recovery for the smartphone depends on the reasonableness test.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
呢題係 exemption clause 三步曲嘅全面應用：(1) incorporation——簽咗名表面上有约束，(2) construction——「howsoever caused, including negligence」字眼夠闊，(3) statutory validity——先至係勝負關鍵。Option A 係典型「簽咗就係咁」陷阱：L'Estrange 只係解決 incorporation，完全唔過問 CECO 嘅 statutory control。Option B 矯枉過正：CECO 唔係一刀切禁止所有 negligence 免責——財物損失過到 reasonableness test 就得（s.7(2)）。Option D 係錯嘅法律工具：UCO (Cap.458) 管嘅係 exemption clause 以外嘅 unconscionable terms；exemption clause 嘅主戰場係 CECO，而且 UCO 嘅「revise or alter」權力從未用過、門檻極高。答題次序好重要：personal injury 先答 s.7(1)（絕對無效），財物先答 s.7(2) + s.3(1)（要過合理性測試，舉證責任喺 FitZone）。

**Rule Check:** Control of Exemption Clauses Ordinance (Cap.71) s.7(1), s.7(2), s.3(1), Sch.2; L'Estrange v E Graucob Ltd (1934) 2 KB 394

**Common Trap（繁中）:**
三大伏位：(1) 以為簽名就大晒，忽略 CECO 嘅 statutory override；(2) 以為 negligence 免責條款一律無效——只有死傷係絕對無效；(3) 記住 reasonableness 係以簽約當刻衡量（唔係 breach 當刻），而且舉證責任喺依賴條款嘅一方。涉及 misrepresentation 嘅免責條款就要用 s.4 of the Misrepresentation Ordinance (Cap.284) + CECO s.3(1)，唔好用錯條文。
