# M10 Chapter 1: Legal System and Regulatory Bodies in Hong Kong — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. Describe the constitutional framework of the HKSAR and the role of the Basic Law and the HK National Security Law (HKNSL).
2. Identify and explain the sources of Hong Kong law: common law, rules of equity, ordinances, subordinate legislation and Chinese customary law.
3. Apply the jurisdictional limits of Hong Kong courts and tribunals to select the correct forum for a given dispute.
4. Explain the doctrine of precedent (stare decisis) and analyse when a court may depart from a precedent.
5. Distinguish the literal, golden and mischief rules of statutory interpretation and apply them to a scenario.
6. Describe the roles, duties and powers of the HKMA, SFC, HKEX, AFRC and IA in relation to the work of an accountant.

#### B. Leading Cases（full citation，全部出自 LP）

| 案件 | 確立原則 |
|---|---|
| **Tong Ying Kit v HKSAR [2020] HKCFI 2133** | 首宗 HKNSL 案件；CFI 確認香港法院應以 **common law approach**（而非 civil law approach）詮釋 HKNSL，與詮釋 Basic Law 的方法一致 |
| **Re Wong Choi Ho & Another (1969) HKLR 391** | 香港適用的 Chinese customary law = 1843 年起在香港沿用的 **Qing Law** 及習俗 |
| **Grant v Australian Knitting Mills Ltd (1936) AC 85** | 製造商對消費者負 duty of care（縱無合約關係）；內衣殘留化學品致皮炎 |
| **Griffiths v Peter Conway (1939) 1 All ER 685** | **Distinguishing** 示範：原告皮膚異常敏感屬 material fact 差異，法院拒跟隨 Grant 案；duty 不及於正常範圍以外的人 |
| **London and North Eastern Railway v Berriman (1946) AC 278** | **Literal rule**：死者當時在 oiling 路軌（routine maintenance），不屬 "relaying or repairing"，遺孀不獲賠償 — 即使結果不公亦照字面解釋 |
| **Adler v George (1964) 2 QB 7** | **Golden rule**：被告在禁區「內」阻礙執勤人員；字面 "in the vicinity of" 會令區內犯案反而無罪，屬荒謬，故讀作 "in or in the vicinity of" |
| **Heydon's Case (1584) 76 ER 637** | **Mischief rule** 四問：立法前法律為何？舊法未涵蓋的 mischief/defect 為何？立法提供何種 remedy？remedy 的真正原因為何？ |
| **Smith v Hughes (1960) 2 All ER 859** | Mischief rule 應用：妓女在屋內敲窗招徠街上行人，仍屬 "solicit in a street" — 法案目的係令行人免受滋擾 |

#### C. IRAC Application Points（考試套用框架）

**1. Court jurisdiction 選擇題（Ch.1 最常考應用點）**
- **Issue**: 案件應由哪個法院/審裁處審理？
- **Rule**: Small Claims Tribunal ≤ **HK$75,000**（不可律師代表，可放棄超額部分遷就）；District Court 合約申索 ≤ **HK$3 million**（收回土地 ≤ HK$7m；年租/應課差餉租值 ≤ HK$320,000；刑事最高監禁 7 年，無陪審團）；CFI civil > HK$3 million（unlimited jurisdiction）；Labour Tribunal：至少一名申索人申索額 > HK$8,000 或申索人 > 10 人；所有刑事案件首訊必經 Magistrates' Courts（一般最高判監 2 年 + 罰款 HK$100,000）。
- **Application**: 將 scenario 金額/案件性質對照門檻。
- **Conclusion**: 指明 forum + 理由（金額、可否聘律師、是否刑事）。

**2. 法院何時可偏離 precedent**
- **Rule**: 只有 ratio decidendi 具約束力；obiter dicta 僅 persuasive。可偏離的情況：(1) **distinguished**（material facts 不同）；(2) **overruled**（上級法院在**另一案件**推翻下級法院確立的先例）；(3) **reversed**（上級法院在**同一案件**上訴中推翻下級判決）；(4) 先前陳述屬 obiter dicta；(5) reasoning divergent and unclear；(6) **per incuriam**（法官漏考慮相關案例/法例）。Vertical：CFI 受 CA/CFA 約束；DC/Magistrates 受 CFI 約束。Horizontal：同級法院互不約束（CFI 不約束另一 CFI）。
- **Trap**: overruling 與 reversing 的分別在於「不同案件」定「同一案件上訴」；下級法院**不能** overrule 上級法院先例，只能 distinguish。

**3. Statutory interpretation 三步判斷**
- **Rule**: Literal rule → 字面清楚即照用，即使不公；Golden rule → 字面導致 absurd result 時採最少荒謬的解釋；Mischief rule → 探究立法意圖壓制 mischief（Cap. 1 s.19 要求香港法院採用）。
- **Application**: 先問字面是否清楚 → 再問字面結果是否荒謬 → 最後問立法要杜絕的 mischief 是甚麼。
- **Trap**: ejusdem generis（具體詞 + 概括詞 → 概括詞限於同類）與 contextual rule（整條條例一齊讀）係**輔助規則**，唔係三大規則之一。

**4. Subordinate legislation 控制**
- **Rule**: 由 parent/enabling ordinance 授權制定；**Negative vetting**（毋須明文）：刊憲後於立法會下次會議席上提交，立法會可在 **28 日**內以決議修訂或延長審議期，一般刊憲日生效；**Positive vetting**：parent ordinance 規定須立法會批准，議員動議，立法會可 approve / reject / amend。
- **Trap**: subordinate legislation 超越 parent ordinance 權限 = **ultra vires**，可經 judicial review 推翻；立法會對毋須審議的附屬法例無權廢除或修訂。

**5. Equity 補救**
- **Rule**: Common law 補救只有 monetary damages；equity 補救係 court orders（injunction、specific performance），屬**酌情**授予，原告 not entitled as of right；common law 與 equity 衝突時 **equity prevails**；equity 承認新權利（如 trust 受益人權利），common law 不承認。
- **Application**: 標的物獨一無二（unique property）→ damages 不足 → 可求 specific performance，但由法院酌情決定。

**6. Judicial review**
- **Rule**: 針對政府/法定機構決定，理由限於 **illegality, unreasonableness, procedural impropriety**；申請人須有 sufficient interest；法院**不會**判斷決定本身對錯；敗訴須付對方訟費；Basic Law Art. 64 政府須守法。

### 易混淆概念對比

**對比一：Common Law vs Rules of Equity**

| | Common Law | Rules of Equity |
|---|---|---|
| 來源 | 法官在案件判決中創造（case law），嚴格跟 precedent | 為紓緩 common law 嚴苛而發展，追求 fairness and natural justice，法官酌情 |
| 補救 | **只有 monetary damages**（金錢賠償） | **Decrees / court orders**：injunction、specific performance |
| 權利 | 不承認新權利（如 trust 受益人權利） | 承認新權利（trust 受益人可向 trustee 主張權利） |
| 取得補救 | 權利確立即可獲 damages | **酌情**授予，not as of right；damages 足夠時不授予 |
| 衝突時 | — | **Equity prevails** |

📌 **點樣分辨**：題目問「賠償金」→ common law；問「強制履行/禁制令」→ equity。**考試伏位**：買家想強制賣家完成獨特物業交易，答案唔係「一定獲批 specific performance」，而係「法院酌情，damages 不足先會考慮」。

**對比二：Arbitration vs Mediation（vs Conciliation）**

| | Arbitration | Mediation | Conciliation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 性質 | **Adjudicative**：仲裁員作出裁決 | **Non-adjudicative**：調解員協助雙方自行達成和解 | Conciliator 角色比 mediator 更被動，可作 evaluator/intervener 提建議 |
| 結果約束力 | Award **final and binding**；極少情況可上訴（除 obvious error of law） | 無裁決； settlement 由雙方自願達成 | 建議無約束力，雙方可不跟隨 |
| 程序 | 毋須正式聆訊、證據規則較寬鬆、閉門進行 | 受 Mediation Ordinance (Cap. 620) 規管；通訊**保密**（s.8(2) 例外） | 多用於勞資/歧視爭議，預防糾紛擴大 |
| 費用/場地 | 雙方須付仲裁員酬金及場地費 | 較訴訟便宜快捷 | 較訴訟便宜快捷 |

📌 **點樣分辨**：關鍵字 "final and binding decision by a third party" = arbitration；"assist parties to reach settlement themselves, no decision imposed" = mediation。**考試伏位**：題目若講「上訴仲裁裁決」→ 一般唔得（除非 obvious error of law）；若講「調解保密可隨便向法庭披露」→ 錯，要符合 Cap. 620 s.8(2) 例外。

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** Which articles of the Basic Law preserve the pre-1997 sources of Hong Kong law, and what are those sources?

**Back:**
- Article 8: laws previously in force — common law, rules of equity, ordinances, subordinate legislation and customary law — are maintained, except those contravening the Basic Law and subject to amendment by the HKSAR legislature.
- Article 18: laws in force = the Basic Law + Article 8 laws + laws enacted by the HKSAR legislature.
- Article 11: the Basic Law is above local laws enacted by Hong Kong.

💬 白話：97 前嘅法律（普通法、衡平法、條例、附屬法例、習慣法）過渡照用，前提係唔好同 Basic Law 有衝突；Art. 8 講保留、Art. 18 講而家嘅法律體系組成。

### Card 2
**Front:** In Tong Ying Kit v HKSAR [2020] HKCFI 2133, which interpretive approach did the Court of First Instance confirm for the HKNSL, and why?

**Back:**
- HK courts should apply the **common law approach** (not civil law approach) to interpret the HKNSL.
- Reasoning: the Basic Law, also a national law enacted by the SCNPC, is construed using the common law approach; no valid basis to adopt a second approach for the HKNSL.
- Under HKNSL Art. 62 local laws (incl. common law) apply unless inconsistent with the HKNSL; Art. 65 vests interpretation power in the SCNPC.

💬 白話：HKNSL 雖然係全國性法律，但香港法官照用普通法方法去詮釋，因為 Basic Law 都係咁做，保持一貫。

### Card 3
**Front:** State the civil monetary jurisdictional limits of the Small Claims Tribunal, the District Court and the Court of First Instance.

**Back:**
- Small Claims Tribunal (Cap. 338): claims **not exceeding HK$75,000**; informal; **no legal representation**.
- District Court: contract claims up to **HK$3 million**; recovery of land up to **HK$7 million**; annual rent/ratable value not exceeding **HK$320,000**.
- Court of First Instance: **unlimited** civil jurisdiction; hears claims **over HK$3 million**.

💬 白話：記住三條數：7.5 萬（小額錢債）、300 萬（區院合約）、超過 300 萬去原訟庭。考試好鍾意畀個金額叫你揀法院。

### Card 4
**Front:** What is the difference between overruling and reversing a decision under the doctrine of precedent?

**Back:**
- **Overruling**: a higher court, in a **different (later) case**, declares that a precedent set by a lower court in a previous case is no longer binding and substitutes a different rule.
- **Reversing**: a higher court, **on appeal in the same case**, reaches a decision opposite to that of the lower court.
- Only the **ratio decidendi** binds; **obiter dicta** are persuasive only.

💬 白話：Overrule = 上級法院喺**另一單案**度推翻舊先例；Reverse = **同一單案**上訴時改判。考試最常伏位就係混淆呢兩個，記住「同一案定另一案」。

### Card 5
**Front:** List six grounds on which a court may depart from a precedent.

**Back:**
1. The case is **distinguished** (material facts differ — e.g. Griffiths v Peter Conway (1939) 1 All ER 685).
2. The precedent has been **overruled** by a higher court.
3. The decision has been **reversed** on appeal.
4. The statements relied on were only **obiter dicta**.
5. The reasoning of the previous case was **divergent and unclear**.
6. The decision was given **per incuriam** (failure to consider relevant case law/legislation).

💬 白話：口訣「D-O-R-O-D-P」：Distinguish、Overrule、Reverse、Obiter、Divergent、Per incuriam。Horizontal stare decisis：同級法院（如 CFI vs CFI）互不約束。

### Card 6
**Front:** Match each rule of statutory interpretation to its leading case: literal rule, golden rule, mischief rule.

**Back:**
- **Literal rule** — London and North Eastern Railway v Berriman (1946) AC 278: "oiling" tracks ≠ "relaying or repairing"; widow denied compensation despite harsh result.
- **Golden rule** — Adler v George (1964) 2 QB 7: "in the vicinity of" read as "in or in the vicinity of" to avoid absurdity.
- **Mischief rule** — Heydon's Case (1584) 76 ER 637 (four questions); applied in Smith v Hughes (1960) 2 All ER 859; adopted for HK ordinances by s.19, Interpretation and General Clauses Ordinance (Cap. 1).

💬 白話：Literal = 照字面，唔公平都照；Golden = 字面荒謬就修正；Mischief = 問立法原意想杜絕咩弊端。香港考試記住 Cap. 1 s.19 指定用 mischief rule。

### Card 7
**Front:** Distinguish the negative vetting procedure from the positive vetting procedure for subsidiary legislation.

**Back:**
- **Negative vetting**: no express provision needed in the enabling ordinance; subsidiary legislation is laid on the table at the next LegCo sitting after gazettal; LegCo may amend it by resolution within **28 days** or extend the scrutiny period; effective on gazette date unless otherwise provided.
- **Positive vetting**: the parent ordinance requires LegCo **approval**; a public officer moves a motion; LegCo may **approve, reject or amend**; amended legislation deemed effective on gazette date.

💬 白話：Negative = 刊憲先生效，立法會「唔出聲就過關」、28 日內可改；Positive = 要立法會主動批准先生效。名字「negative/positive」指立法會參與程度。

### Card 8
**Front:** Which five external regulatory bodies should an accountant know, and what is the key statute/role of each?

**Back:**
- **HKMA** — Banking Ordinance (Cap. 155): authorisation/supervision of the three types of authorised institutions; currency and banking stability.
- **SFC** — Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571): licenses 10 types of regulated activities; CPAs exempt from Type 4/5/6/9 licences if services are wholly incidental to CPA practice.
- **HKEX** — frontline regulator of listing matters; administers and enforces the Listing Rules.
- **AFRC** (formerly FRC, from 1 Oct 2022) — Financial Reporting Council Ordinance (Cap. 588) as amended by FRC (Amendment) Ordinance 2021: registers PIE auditors/practice units, issues practising certificates, inspects, investigates, disciplines; oversees HKICPA.
- **IA** — Insurance Ordinance (Cap. 41): regulates insurers and intermediaries; protects policyholders.

💬 白話：銀行搵 HKMA、證券期貨搵 SFC、上市搵 HKEX、會計師搵 AFRC、保險搵 IA。記住 2022 年 10 月起 FRC 改名 AFRC 並接管 HKICPA 大部分監管權。

## 本章急救詞彙

| EN Term | 繁中白話 | English Model Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| stare decisis | 「跟住舊判決行」— 下級法院要跟上級法院先例 | Under stare decisis, the District Court is bound by the decisions of the Court of First Instance. |
| ratio decidendi | 判決理由 — 判案所依據的法律原則，先有約束力 | Only the ratio decidendi of a case becomes a binding precedent for later cases. |
| obiter dicta | 「順便講嘅話」— 法官非必要的法律意見，只具說服力 | Statements made by the way are obiter dicta and are persuasive but not binding. |
| per incuriam | 「搞錯咗」— 法官漏咗考慮相關法例或案例而作嘅判決，可不跟隨 | A decision given per incuriam need not be followed because the judge failed to consider the relevant legislation. |
| subordinate legislation | 附屬法例 — 立法會經 parent ordinance 授權其他機構訂立嘅規則 | The Board of Inland Revenue may make rules as subordinate legislation under s.85 of the Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112). |
| ultra vires | 越權 — 附屬法例超越母條例授權範圍 | Subordinate legislation made beyond the limits of the parent ordinance is ultra vires and may be challenged by judicial review. |
| specific performance | 強制履行令 — equity 補救，法院命令違約方履行合約，酌情授予 | The buyer may seek an order of specific performance where damages are inadequate because the property is unique. |
| judicial review | 司法覆核 — 法院審查政府/公共機構決定嘅合法性，唔審對錯 | The court will grant leave for judicial review only if the application has a realistic prospect of success. |
