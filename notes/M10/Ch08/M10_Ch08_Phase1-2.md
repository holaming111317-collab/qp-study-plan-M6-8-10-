# M10 Chapter 8: Incorporation in Hong Kong — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives

1. **Apply** the doctrine of separate legal personality (*Salomon v A. Salomon and Co Ltd*) and the concept of limited liability to scenario facts.
2. **Analyse** the differences between companies, sole traders, partnerships and joint ventures in formation, legal status, management and liability.
3. **Identify** the five types of companies that may be formed under s.66 of the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) and the s.11 requirements of a private company.
4. **Illustrate and apply** the incorporation procedure under ss.67–73 CO, including the incorporation form, articles of association and certificate of incorporation.
5. **Apply** the rules on the articles as a statutory contract (s.86), alteration of articles (s.88), objects clauses and the reformed ultra vires doctrine (ss.115–116).

#### A. Relevant Ordinances（Cap. number + section numbers）

**1. 核心概念：Separate Legal Entity & Limited Liability**
- **s.2 CO (Cap. 622)**：company 定義 —— formed and registered under the Companies Ordinance。
- **s.115 CO**：A company has the **capacity, rights, powers and privileges of a natural person of full age**；s.115(2) 明確公司可 acquire, hold and dispose of land。公司可以同自己股東訂立合約（Salomon 賣生意俾自己公司）。
- **s.66 CO**：五種可成立公司 —— (1) public company limited by shares; (2) private company limited by shares; (3) public unlimited company with share capital; (4) private unlimited company with share capital; (5) company limited by guarantee without share capital。
- **s.8 CO**：company limited by shares —— members' liability limited by the articles **to any amount unpaid on the shares held**（已繳足 = 零額外責任）。
- **s.9 CO**：company limited by guarantee —— **no share capital** + liability limited to the amount members undertake to contribute **on winding up**（慈善/非牟利常用；**不屬 private 或 public**，見 ss.11(1)(b)、12(b)）。
- **s.10 CO**：unlimited company —— no limit on members' liability；注意成員係 **severally liable**（各自為自己份額負責），同 partnership 嘅 joint / joint and several liability 唔同。
- **s.73 CO**：由 certificate of incorporation 日期起，公司可行使 incorporated company 職能，並有 **perpetual succession**（s.73(2)）；founder members 於該日成為 members。
- ⚠️ **Incorporation ≠ limited liability**：limited liability 取決於公司類型（s.66），唔係成立就自動有。

**2. Private vs Public Companies**
- **s.11(1)(a) CO**：private company 三個條件（**全部寫喺 articles 度**，缺一不可）：
  (i) restrict a member's right to **transfer shares**；
  (ii) limit the number of members to **50**；
  (iii) prohibit any **invitation to the public** to subscribe for shares or debentures。
  （s.11(1)(b)：唔可以係 company limited by guarantee。）
- **s.12 CO**：public company = **not a private company** + not a company limited by guarantee。Articles 達唔到 s.11(1)(a) 任何一項 = public。
- 只有 public company 可先可以上市（**Listing Rule 8.03**）。
- Private company 監管較鬆：s.359 reporting exemption + s.380(7) 豁免部分財務報表要求。

**3. 成立程序（Formation）**
- **s.67(1) CO**：any **one or more persons** 可成立公司 —— (a) sign the articles + (b) deliver to the Registrar (i) an **incorporation form** in specified form + (ii) a copy of the articles。**s.67(2)**：只可為 **lawful purpose** 成立。
- **s.68 + Schedule 2 CO**：incorporation form 內容 —— 公司名、registered office 地址、limited/unlimited、private/public、founder members 姓名地址、directors 資料（自然人須 18 歲以上並 consent）、company secretary 資料、股份及股本細節（Sch. 2 ss.1–5, 7–8）。
- **s.71(1) CO**：Registrar 發出 **certificate of incorporation**。
- **s.72 CO**：certificate 係 **conclusive evidence** 已符合註冊要求。
- 代替方案：**shelf company**（已註冊但未營運嘅空殼公司，買入後轉股、換董事及秘書、改名同 registered office）。

**4. Articles of Association（公司憲制文件）**
- **s.75 CO**：公司**必須有** articles；memorandum of association 已喺新 CO 下**廢除**，articles 係唯一 constitutional document。
- **ss.76–77**：articles 須以英文或中文印刷、分條連續編號。
- **ss.81–85**：articles 必載 —— s.81 公司名；s.83(1)/(2) limited/unlimited liability 聲明；s.84 limited by shares 須述明 liability limited to unpaid amount（limited by guarantee 須述明成員承諾出資額）；s.85 有股本公司須載 Sch. 2 s.8 資料；**s.82(2) objects clause 屬 optional**（例外：s.82(1) 依 s.103 申請刪除「Limited」字樣嘅公司必須載明 objects）。
- **ss.78–79**：model articles 由 Financial Secretary 訂明（**Companies (Model Articles) Notice (Cap. 622H)**，Sch. 1 public / Sch. 2 private / Sch. 3 guarantee），公司可採用或修改。
- **s.86 CO**：articles 一經註冊，have effect as a **contract under seal** (i) between the company and each member 及 (ii) between a member and each other member；s.86(2) —— enforceable by the company against each member / by a member against the company / by a member against each other member。
  - ⚠️ **Directors 唔可以**憑 s.86(2) 強制執行 articles（除非以 member 身份）；third parties 亦無權 —— **s.3(2)(g) Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Ordinance (Cap. 623)** 明確排除。
- **Alteration**：**s.87(1)** 可修改；**s.88(2)** 須 **special resolution**（≥75% 多數，s.564(1)）；例外：**s.88(3)** 修改最高發行股數只須 ordinary resolution（simple majority，s.563(1)）；**s.88(5)** 修改後 **15 日內**向 Registrar 交付通知 + 經 officer 核證嘅修改後 articles，違反屬罪行（s.88(6)，Level 3 fine HK$10,000 + 每日 HK$300）；**s.88(4)** articles 自己加嘅額外修改條件無效；**s.92(1)** 不得藉修改強迫現有成員認購更多股份或增加其出資責任（除非書面同意）。
- **Objects / Ultra Vires 改革**：**s.115** 賦予自然人能力 → 公司 capacity 不再受 objects 限制；**s.116(1)** 如 articles 載有 objects，公司不得做未獲授權嘅 act；**s.116(3)** members 可入稟 **restrain** 公司違反 s.116(1)；**s.116(4)** 不得針對履行因違反而產生嘅法律義務嘅行為提出（即已簽約就 too late）；**s.116(5)** 單純違反 s.116(1) **唔會令行為無效**。違反後果 = 追董事責任（breach of directors' duties）。

**5. 其他成立及持續要求**
- **Company name**：**s.100(1)** 不得同已註冊名稱相同 / 構成罪行 / 冒犯或違反公眾利益；**s.100(2)** 不得令人以為同中央人民政府或特區政府有關連；**s.101 + Companies (Words and Expressions in Company Names) Order (Cap. 622A)**（如 "trust"、"savings"、"chamber of commerce"）；**s.102** limited company 英文名最後一字必須係 "**Limited**"、中文名最後四字必須係「有限公司」；**s.103** Registrar 可特許刪除「Limited」（慈善類公司）。公司名須**全英文或全中文**，不得中英混合（CR Guideline para. 3）。
- **Registered office**：**s.658(1)** 必須喺香港設 registered office；**s.827** 文件送達 registered office 即視為送達公司（**即使公司已搬走或不知情** —— Ho Kwok Wah）；無 registered office 屬罪行（s.658(5)，Level 5 fine HK$50,000 + 每日 HK$1,000）。
- **Business registration**：**s.5 BRO (Cap. 310)** 開業後 **1 個月內**向稅局登記；**s.5A BRO** 可同 incorporation 一併辦理（deemed application）。商業登記證須喺營業地點當眼處展示。
- **Common seal**：新 CO 下**唔再必需**；如有，須為金屬印並清晰刻有公司名（s.124，違反 Level 3 fine）。
- **Registers**：**s.627** register of members（2 個月內登記、成員離開後保存 10 年）；**s.641** register of directors；**ss.648/650** register of company secretaries；須存放於 registered office，15 日內通知 Registrar（ss.628, 641(3)–(5), 648(3)–(5)）；違反 = Level 4 fine HK$25,000 + 每日 HK$700；members 免費查閱（ss.631, 642, 649）。
- **Register of Significant Controllers (SCR)**：**Part 12 Division 2A（ss.653A–653ZK）+ Schedules 5A–5C**，2018 年 3 月 1 日生效（Companies (Amendment) Ordinance 2018，回應 FATF 反洗錢建議）。
  - Significant control = 直接或間接持有 **>25%** 已發行股份 / 投票權；或委任/罷免**過半數董事**嘅權利；或對公司有 **significant influence or control**；或對符合上述條件嘅信託/商號有控制（Sch. 5A）。
  - **即使無 significant controller 都要備存 register**；詳情確認後 **7 日內**登記（s.653J(1)–(2)）；存放於 registered office（s.653M(1)）；只供 register 上人士及 **law enforcement officers**（警察、CR、海關、金管局、稅局、ICAC、SFC 等）查閱 —— **唔係公開查閱**。
  - 須委任至少一名 **designated representative**（s.653Z）：香港居民 + 董事/僱員/成員，或會計/法律專業人士或 TCSP 持牌人。
  - **Listed companies 豁免**（由 SFO 披露制度涵蓋）；經上市公司持有嘅人亦排除於 registrable person 之外。
  - 違反 = Level 4 fine + 每日 HK$700（ss.653H(4), 653I(3), 653J(3)）。實際檢控案例：*HKSAR v Best Time Holdings Limited*（罰 HK$8,000）、*HKSAR v Haihua Shipping Service Co., Limited*（罰 HK$10,000）。
- **Members**：s.67(1) 一人即可成立（one-person company）；s.629(1) 單一成員公司須喺 register 註明。
- **Directors**：private company 至少 **1 名**（s.454(1)）；public company 及 company limited by guarantee 至少 **2 名**（s.453(2)）；自然人董事須 **18 歲以上**（Sch. 2 s.4）。
- **Company Secretary**：**s.474(1)** 必須有；**s.474(4)** 自然人須**通常居於香港**，法人團體須喺香港有 registered office/place of business；**s.475(1)** 董事可兼任，**但 s.475(2) 只有一名董事嘅 private company 唔可以**由該唯一董事兼任；**s.475(3)** 亦不得委任以該唯一董事為唯一董事嘅法人團體；**s.479(1)** 凡規定由「董事 + 公司秘書」做嘅事，**唔可以由同一人以雙重身份完成**（例如 s.127(3)(b) 簽立文件）；辭職：**s.477 + s.652(2)**，15 日內交 Form ND4，違反 Level 4 fine；若秘書有理由相信公司唔會交通知，秘書自己須交（s.474(3)）。上市公司秘書須具學術/專業資格（**Listing Rule 3.28**：HKICS 會員、律師、大律師或 CPA）。
- **Dormant companies**：**s.5 CO** —— 通過 **special resolution** 宣佈 dormant 並交付 Registrar；豁免 financial statements、directors' reports、AGM、annual returns、核數師委任（ss.477, 661, 663）；進行任何 **accounting transaction**（s.5(5)(b)，即 s.373 須入帳嘅交易，法定費用除外）即**自動失去** dormant 狀態，知情嘅 member/director 就該交易嘅債項負**個人責任**；自願恢復：special resolution 表明有意進行 accounting transaction 並交付 Registrar（s.5(5)(a)）；**public company 及 s.5(7) 所列受規管機構**（銀行、保險、SFC 持牌法團、強積金受託人及其控股公司、過去 5 年曾屬該類別者）**不得** dormant。
- **De-registration**：**s.750(1)** 六個條件 —— (1) all members agree；(2) 未開業或申請前 **3 個月**無營業；(3) 無未清償債務；(4) 非任何法律程序一方；(5) 無位於香港嘅不動產；(6) 如為控股公司，子公司亦無香港不動產。
- **Non-Hong Kong companies**：**s.2** 定義 = 在外地成立但在香港 establish a **place of business**；**s.774** place of business 包括 **share transfer office / share registration office**；**s.776** 設立營業地點後 **1 個月內**向 Registrar 註冊；Part 16 只適用於**已註冊**嘅非香港公司，但 **Part 14（unfair prejudice remedies）經 s.722** 適用於註冊及未註冊非香港公司（法院是否行使管轄權視乎同香港有否 sufficient connection）；**Cap. 622M** 要求非香港公司展示名稱、註冊地及有限責任狀況（違反 level 3 fine）。
- **監管機構**：**Registrar of Companies / Companies Registry**（s.27 保存紀錄、接受註冊、可剔除公司、可就 filing offences 檢控，但**無調查權**）；**Official Receiver**（s.75 Bankruptcy Ordinance (Cap. 6) 委任；s.194 CWUMPO 無私人清盤人時出任清盤人；s.204 CWUMPO 監督私人清盤人）。
- **其他相關法例**：**CWUMPO (Cap. 32)** —— 舊 CO 保留條文：winding-up/insolvency、directors disqualification (Part IVA)、prospectuses (Part II)；**BRO (Cap. 310)**；**Transfer of Businesses (Protection of Creditors) Ordinance (Cap. 49)** —— s.3 買方承擔賣方業務全部債務、s.4 + s.5(3) 以 Gazette + 兩份中文 + 一份英文報章刊登轉讓通知可免責（轉讓日前 1–4 個月刊登，通知於刊登後 1 個月屆滿時完成）；**Partnership Ordinance (Cap. 38)** —— s.3(1) partnership 定義、s.7 partner binds the firm、s.11/12 joint liability、s.13/14 joint and several liability、s.26 權利平等、s.30 full disclosure、s.31(1) duty to account、s.32 duty not to compete、ss.34–37 dissolution；**Limited Partnerships Ordinance (Cap. 37)** —— general partner 無限責任、limited partner 以出資額為限但**不得參與管理**；**Limited Partnership Fund Ordinance (Cap. 637)** —— 2020-08-31 生效，投資基金工具，limited partner 無 fiduciary duty（s.26(2)）、無法律人格（s.15(b)）；**SFO (Cap. 571)** 及 **Listing Rules**（非成文法）。

#### B. Leading Cases（full citation）

| Case | Principle（一句記住） |
|---|---|
| **Salomon v A. Salomon and Co Ltd (1897) AC 22** (HL) | 公司一經註冊即係 **separate legal person**，即使實際由一人操控亦然；Mr Salomon 毋須為公司債務負責。Separate legal entity 嘅基石案例。 |
| **Farrar v Farrars Limited (1888) 40 Ch. D. 395** | 股東將物業賣俾自己做股東嘅公司，**唔係**「賣俾自己」—— 交易不可僅因此撤銷。印證公司同股東係兩個人。 |
| **Ashbury Railway Carriage and Iron Co Ltd v Riche (1875) LR 7 HL 653** | 舊 **ultra vires** 原則：超出 objects clause 嘅合約 **void**（貸款起鐵路超出「造賣火車卡」嘅 objects）。⚠️ 已被 ss.115–116 改革，考試用嚟對比新舊制度。 |
| **Ng Kin Kenneth v HK Football Association Ltd (1994) 1 HKC 734** | Articles 係 statutory contract，但**條款寫法決定可強制執行嘅範圍** —— 涉案仲裁條款只涵蓋 members 之間嘅爭議，唔涵蓋 company vs member。 |
| **Eley v Positive Government Security Life Assurance Co Ltd (1875) 1 Ex D 88**（LP footnote 2 引用） | 股東只可以 **member 身份**按 articles 起訴，唔可以以第三方/其他身份起訴。 |
| **Kam Leung Sui Kwan v Kam Kwan Lai and Others (2015) 18 HKCFAR 501** (CFA) | 「Place of business」= 公司**經營或有意經營業務嘅地點**；holding company 純行政職能、喺香港開 board meetings **唔足以**構成香港 place of business（但資產全在香港可構成 winding-up 管轄嘅 sufficient connection）。 |
| **Ho Kwok Wah v Group Jewellery Arts Ltd and Others (2000) 3 HKC 595** (CA) | 傳票送達公司 **registered office** 即屬有效送達，**即使公司其實已搬走** —— 判決非不當取得。Registered office 嘅法律後果唔可以hea。 |
| **HKSAR v Best Time Holdings Limited**；**HKSAR v Haihua Shipping Service Co., Limited** | SCR（s.653M(1)）及各 registers 存放要求嘅實際刑事檢控案例，罰款 HK$8,000 / HK$10,000。 |
| （Partnership 相關）**Law v Law (1905)** | Partner 出售權益時未 full disclosure → 對方可終止交易（s.30 PO）。 |
| **Bentley v Craven (1853)** | Partner 低買高賣俾自己 firm → 須 account for 全部利潤（s.31(1) PO duty to account）。 |

#### C. IRAC Application Points（答題框架）

1. **Separate legal entity 題**：
   - **Issue**：X 是否要為公司債務/行為負責？
   - **Rule**：Salomon + s.115 CO；公司同 members/officers 係不同法律主體。
   - **Application**：分清「以個人身份簽」定「以公司身份簽」—— 若 X **親自**做合約一方（即使係股東/董事），X 個人負責；若只有公司係合約一方，members 唔使負責（limited by shares 最多賠 unpaid amount，s.8(1)）。
   - **Conclusion**：明確寫邊個負責、邊個唔使負責。
2. **Private vs public 題**：逐項對照 s.11(1)(a)(i)–(iii) 三個 articles 要求 —— **缺一即 public**（s.12(a)）。注意「限制 100 名成員」≠ private（上限係 **50**）。
3. **Objects clause / ultra vires 題**：
   - 有 objects clause → s.116(1) 公司**不應**做未授權行為；
   - 但 s.115 畀咗 capacity + s.116(5) 行為**唔會無效**（唔再係 Ashbury 嘅 void）；
   - members 可依 s.116(3) 申請禁制，**但** s.116(4) —— 已簽約要履行嘅話 too late；
   - 補救方向：告違約董事 breach of directors' duties。
4. **Non-HK company 註冊題**：問三件事 —— (1) 有冇 place of business（Kam Leung Sui Kwan：純開 board meetings 唔夠）？(2) 有冇 share transfer/registration office（s.774 自動中招）？(3) 有 → s.776 一個月內註冊。
5. **SCR 題**：逐步 trace 持股鏈 —— 直接持股 >25% 嘅 entity（且係 member）= registrable legal entity；透過 **majority stake chain** 間接持股嘅自然人 = registrable person；中間層唔係下層公司嘅 member 就唔係 registrable legal entity。
6. **Company secretary 題**：檢查 (1) 係咪 one-director private company（s.475(2) 禁兼任）；(2) 有冇同一個人以 director + secretary 雙重身份做嘢（s.479(1) 禁止）；(3) 自然人係咪通常居港（s.474(4)）。

### 易混淆概念對比

**表一：Private Company vs Public Company（CO ss.11–12）**

| 比較點 | Private Company (s.11) | Public Company (s.12) | 點樣分辨／考試伏位（繁中） |
|---|---|---|---|
| Share transfer | Articles 必須 **restrict** 轉讓（通常 directors 可拒絕登記，Model Articles Sch. 2 Art. 2 + Art. 64，或 pre-emption 條款） | 無限制要求 | 伏位：articles 唔寫限制 = public，唔使問股東人數 |
| Number of members | Articles 上限 **50** | 無上限 | 伏位：「上限 100 人」唔符合 s.11(1)(a)(ii) —— 必須 ≤50 先算 private |
| Public subscription | Articles 必須 **prohibit** 邀請公眾認購 shares/debentures | 可以邀請公眾 | 伏位：喺港鐵站公開招股嘅公司一定係 public |
| Company limited by guarantee | 排除（s.11(1)(b)） | 排除（s.12(b)） | Guarantee company 唔係 private 亦唔係 public —— 獨立類別 |
| 監管負擔 | 較輕（s.359 reporting exemption、s.380(7) 豁免） | 較重；上市須為 public（**LR 8.03**） | 考試鍾意問「點解大部分香港公司係 private」 |
| 最少董事人數 | **1**（s.454(1)） | **2**（s.453(2)） | 同 guarantee company 一齊記：public/guarantee = 2 |

**表二：Limited by Shares vs Limited by Guarantee vs Unlimited（CO ss.8–10）**

| 比較點 | Limited by Shares (s.8) | Limited by Guarantee (s.9) | Unlimited (s.10) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Share capital | 有 | **無** | 有 |
| 成員責任上限 | 持股 **unpaid amount**（繳足 = 零） | Articles 訂明嘅 **guarantee 金額**，清盤時先要掏 | **無上限**（清盤時全數負責） |
| 責任性質 | Limited | Limited（winding-up 先觸發） | 各成員 **severally liable**（⚠️ 唔係 joint and several，呢點同 partnership 唔同） |
| 典型用途 | 一般商業營運 | 慈善、非牟利組織（無需集資） | 罕見（責任太大） |
| 考試伏位（繁中） | 記住 Illustrative Example：繳咗一半錢 = 清盤時最多補返未繳嗰半 | 「冇股本」→ 唔可以發股集資；唔屬 private/public | 伏位：成員係 several 而唔係 joint and several；同 partnership 對比題常考 |

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** What is the doctrine of separate legal entity, and which case established it?

**Back:**
- A company at law is a **different person** from its founders/members, even if effectively operated by one person.
- Established by the House of Lords in **Salomon v A. Salomon and Co Ltd (1897) AC 22**.
- Consequences: the company incurs its own obligations/liabilities; members are not liable for company debts; the company can contract even with its own members.
- Statutory basis: **s.115 CO** — capacity, rights, powers and privileges of a natural person of full age.

💬 白話解釋：公司一註冊就係一個「獨立嘅人」，老闆欠嘅唔係公司欠嘅，公司欠嘅亦唔關股東事。Salomon 個案入面就算成間公司實質得佢一個人搞，法庭都照樣認公司係獨立法人。

### Card 2
**Front:** State the five types of companies that may be formed under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) and the relevant section.

**Back:** (s.66 CO)
1. Public companies limited by shares
2. Private companies limited by shares
3. Public unlimited companies with share capital
4. Private unlimited companies with share capital
5. Companies limited by guarantee without share capital

💬 白話解釋：記法 = 兩條軸交叉 —— 第一條軸「private 定 public」，第二條軸「limited by shares 定 unlimited」，再加一個獨立嘅「guarantee（冇股本）」。揀邊種就睇你想唔想限責任、想唔想向公眾集資。

### Card 3
**Front:** What are the three article requirements for a company to qualify as a private company under s.11(1)(a) CO?

**Back:**
1. Articles **restrict a member's right to transfer shares**;
2. Articles **limit the number of members to 50**;
3. Articles **prohibit any invitation to the public** to subscribe for shares or debentures.
- Failing any one → the company is a **public company** under s.12(a).
- A company limited by guarantee can be neither (ss.11(1)(b), 12(b)).

💬 白話解釋：三個條件全部要寫入 articles，缺一不可。考試伏位：上限寫「100 人」唔合格（要 ≤50）；寫咗可以公開招股都唔合格。達唔到任何一條自動變 public。

### Card 4
**Front:** Describe the incorporation procedure and the legal effect of the certificate of incorporation (ss.67–73 CO).

**Back:**
- **s.67(1)**: one or more persons (a) sign the articles and (b) deliver to the Registrar an **incorporation form** + a copy of the articles; s.67(2): **lawful purpose** only.
- Incorporation form contents: **s.68 + Schedule 2** (name, registered office, company type, founder members, directors, company secretary, share capital details).
- **s.71(1)**: Registrar issues a **certificate of incorporation**.
- **s.72**: certificate is **conclusive evidence** that registration requirements are complied with.
- **s.73**: from the date of incorporation, the company can exercise its functions and has **perpetual succession**; founder members become members.

💬 白話解釋：成立公司 = 簽 articles + 交 incorporation form 俾公司註冊處。張證書一出就係「鐵證」，冇人可推翻註冊係有效嘅；由證書日期起公司就有「永久延續」—— 股東換人都唔會影響公司存在。

### Card 5
**Front:** What is the legal effect of a company's articles under s.86 CO, and who may enforce them?

**Back:**
- Once registered, articles have effect as a **contract under seal**: (i) between the **company and each member**; and (ii) between **a member and each other member** (s.86(1)).
- Enforceable: by the company against each member; by a member against the company; by a member against each other member (s.86(2)).
- **Directors cannot enforce** the articles as directors (only in capacity as members); third parties are excluded — **s.3(2)(g) Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Ordinance (Cap. 623)**.
- Cases: *Ng Kin Kenneth v HK Football Association Ltd (1994) 1 HKC 734* (drafting limits scope of enforceability); *Eley v Positive Government Security Life Assurance Co Ltd (1875) 1 Ex D 88* (sue only in member capacity).

💬 白話解釋：Articles 係一份「法定合約」，但唔係乜人都告得入 —— 公司告股東得、股東告公司得、股東告股東得；董事同外人就冇份。仲要睇條款點寫，寫得窄就只涵蓋部分爭議（Ng Kin Kenneth）。

### Card 6
**Front:** How may a company alter its articles, and what statutory restrictions apply? (ss.87–92 CO)

**Back:**
- **s.88(2)**: alteration only by **special resolution** (≥75% majority, s.564(1)); exception — **s.88(3)**: altering the **maximum number of shares** the company may issue needs only an **ordinary resolution** (s.563(1)).
- **s.88(5)**: deliver notice of alteration + certified copy of altered articles to the Registrar within **15 days**; offence otherwise (s.88(6): Level 3 fine HK$10,000 + HK$300/day).
- **s.88(4)**: extra conditions written into the articles themselves are **invalid**.
- **s.92(1)**: cannot alter articles to bind an existing member to take more shares or increase his liability to pay money to the company (without consent).

💬 白話解釋：改 articles 一定要 special resolution（75%），改完 15 日內要報註冊處。兩個伏位：(1) 公司唔可以自己喺 articles 加難度（例如要 90%）；(2) 唔可以藉改章程逼現有股東加碼出錢。

### Card 7
**Front:** How has the ultra vires doctrine been reformed in Hong Kong? (ss.115–116 CO)

**Back:**
- Old law: acts outside the objects clause were **ultra vires and void** — *Ashbury Railway Carriage and Iron Co Ltd v Riche (1875) LR 7 HL 653*.
- Now: memorandum abolished; objects clause **optional** (s.82(2)); **s.115** gives the company capacity of a natural person.
- **s.116(1)**: if objects are stated, the company **must not** do unauthorised acts — but **s.116(5)**: the act is **not invalid** merely because it breaches s.116(1).
- **s.116(3)**: members may bring proceedings to **restrain** the company; **s.116(4)**: no restraint against acts fulfilling legal obligations already arising from the breach.
- Real consequence: liability of the **director** who caused the breach (directors' duties).

💬 白話解釋：舊制超經營範圍 = 合約無效，好辣。新制下合約照樣有效（保護交易對手），但公司「唔應該做」，股東可以喺公司做之前申請禁制；一做咗就追唔返，只可以追究簽約董事嘅責任。

### Card 8
**Front:** What are the rules on appointing a company secretary, and the prohibition on dual capacity? (ss.474–479 CO)

**Back:**
- **s.474(1)**: every company must have a company secretary; **s.474(4)**: a natural person secretary must **ordinarily reside in Hong Kong**; a body corporate must have its registered office/place of business in Hong Kong.
- **s.475(1)**: a director may also be company secretary — **but s.475(2)**: a private company with **only one director** cannot have that sole director as secretary; **s.475(3)**: nor a body corporate whose sole director is that director.
- **s.479(1)**: where something must be done by **both a director and the company secretary**, it is **not satisfied** by the same person acting in both capacities (e.g. execution of documents under s.127(3)(b)).
- Resignation: company must notify Registrar within **15 days** (Form ND4, s.652(2)); if the company will not, the resigning secretary must deliver the notice himself (s.474(3)).
- Listed companies: secretary must have specified qualifications — **Listing Rule 3.28**.

💬 白話解釋：一人公司嘅唯一董事唔可以自己做埋秘書 —— 因為好多法定動作要「一個董事 + 一個秘書」兩個人做，用嚟互相制衡。Bruce 想一人分飾兩角簽約？s.479 話唔得。
