# M10 Chapter 8: Incorporation in Hong Kong — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: C

**Model Answer (EN):**
- **Issue:** Whether Oscar, as sole shareholder and director, is personally liable for the debts of Oasis Trading Limited on its winding-up.
- **Rule:** Under the doctrine of separate legal entity established in *Salomon v A. Salomon and Co Ltd (1897) AC 22*, a company at law is a different person from its members, even if it is effectively operated by one person. Under s.115 of the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), the company has the capacity, rights, powers and privileges of a natural person and incurs obligations and liabilities of its own. As the company is limited by shares, Oscar's liability as a member is limited to any amount unpaid on his shares (s.8(1) CO) — here nil, as his shares are fully paid.
- **Application:** The debts were incurred by the company, a separate legal person. Neither Oscar's status as sole shareholder nor as sole director makes him liable for the company's obligations; directors and employees are not generally personally liable for company debts.
- **Conclusion:** Oscar is not personally liable. The creditor's claim against Oscar must fail.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
呢題考 Salomon 原則嘅直接應用。A 錯 —— 「一人操控」唔會令股東負責，Salomon 正正係一人實際控制嘅案例，House of Lords 都判佢唔使負責。B 錯 —— 董事管理公司唔等於個人承擔公司債務；董事/僱員一般以合約/代理關係為本，唔會為公司債務負責。D 錯 —— joint and several liability 係 partnership（s.13/s.14 PO）嘅概念，唔適用於股東同公司。C 啱 —— 公司係獨立法人，Oscar 已繳足股款，連 s.8(1) 嘅 unpaid amount 都冇，責任為零。

**Rule Check:** *Salomon v A. Salomon and Co Ltd (1897) AC 22*; Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) ss.115, 8(1)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 考生一見「sole shareholder + sole director」就以為可以 lift the veil 或「其實係同一個人」。記住：lifting the corporate veil 係例外（Chapter 10 先詳講），本章預設答案係 Salomon —— 冇特別事實（如 fraud、sham）就唔好亂掀面紗。

### Q2 — Answer: 見 Model Answer 六個條件

**Model Answer (EN):**
Under **s.750(1)** of the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), an application for deregistration by the company, or a director or member of the company, may only be made if ALL of the following conditions are satisfied:
1. All members of the company agree to the deregistration;
2. The company has not commenced operation or business, or has not been in operation or carried on business during the **3 months** immediately before the application;
3. The company has no outstanding liabilities;
4. The company is not a party to any legal proceedings;
5. The company's assets do not consist of any immovable property situated in Hong Kong; and
6. If the company is a holding company, none of its subsidiary's assets consist of any immovable property situated in Hong Kong.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
純 recall 題，考 s.750(1) 六個條件。記憶框架：「人（全體同意）、業（3 個月無營業）、債（無負債）、訟（無官司）、樓（自己冇香港物業）、仔（子公司都冇香港物業）」。伏位：(1) 唔係「6 個月」係「3 個月」；(2) immovable property 限於「situated in Hong Kong」；(3) 第 6 條只係控股公司先適用。呢啲係 open-book 都應該即刻搵到嘅基本盤。

**Rule Check:** Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) s.750(1)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 唔好同 **dormant company（s.5）** 撈亂 —— dormant 係「冬眠」保留公司，deregistration 係「解散除名」；亦唔好同 striking-off / liquidation 搞混（panelists 喺 J24 Q5(c) 就批評考生將 deregistration 誤答成 liquidation）。

### Q3 — Answer: C

**Model Answer (EN):**
- **Issue:** Whether the yacht chartering contract is void for being outside the company's objects, and whether the members can restrain its performance.
- **Rule:** Under s.115 of the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), a company has the capacity of a natural person of full age, so its capacity is not restricted by its stated objects. Under s.116(1), where objects are stated in the articles, the company must not do any act it is not authorised to do; under s.116(3), members may bring proceedings to restrain such an act. However, s.116(4) provides that no such proceedings may be brought in respect of an act done in fulfilment of a legal obligation arising from the breach, and s.116(5) provides that an act is **not invalid** merely because it breaches s.116(1).
- **Application:** Operating a yacht chartering service is outside the stated object of retail sale of marine equipment, so the company should not have entered the contract (s.116(1)). But the contract has already been signed: restraining performance would prevent fulfilment of a legal obligation arising from the breach, which s.116(4) prohibits, and s.116(5) saves the contract from invalidity.
- **Conclusion:** The contract is valid and binding. The members' remedy lies against Derek personally for breach of his duties as a director in causing the company to act beyond its objects.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
呢題係新舊 ultra vires 制度嘅對比應用。A 錯 —— Ashbury 嘅「void」舊原則已被新 CO 改革，s.116(5) 明確行為唔會單因違反 objects 而無效。B 錯 —— s.116(3) 禁制令只適用於「未做嘅行為」；合約已簽，履行係因違反而產生嘅法律義務，s.116(4) 禁止針對呢類行為提出禁制（「too late」情況）。D 錯 —— 前半啱（合約有效），但後半錯，理由同 B。C 全對 —— 仲要記得補一句追究 Derek 嘅董事責任，呢個先係完整 exam-style 答案。答呢類題要用 IRAC 四步，唔好齋背條文。

**Rule Check:** Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) ss.115, 116(1), 116(3), 116(4), 116(5); cf. *Ashbury Railway Carriage and Iron Co Ltd v Riche (1875) LR 7 HL 653* (old law)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 最大陷阱係背咗 Ashbury 就直接答「void」—— 新 CO 下 objects clause 違反**唔會**令交易無效。第二個陷阱係以為 members 隨時都可以禁制 —— 記住 s.116(4) 嘅「too late」規則：已簽約要履行就禁唔到。

### Q4 — Answer: C

**Model Answer (EN):**
- Arrangement (1) — **Not permissible.** Under s.475(2) of the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), a private company having only one director must not have as its company secretary that sole director.
- Arrangement (2) — **Not permissible.** Under s.475(3), such a company also must not appoint as company secretary a body corporate whose sole director is that same director.
- Arrangement (3) — **Permissible.** A natural person may be appointed company secretary provided he/she ordinarily resides in Hong Kong (s.474(4)); Gary satisfies this requirement. (Note: s.475(1) allows a director to be company secretary only where the company has more than one director — not applicable here, but Gary is an independent appointee.)
- Arrangement (4) — **Not permissible.** A body corporate appointed as company secretary must have its registered office or place of business in Hong Kong (s.474(4)); Global Admin Inc. has neither.

Therefore only arrangement (3) is permissible.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
逐個 arrangement 對條文就唔會錯。(1) 撞正 s.475(2) —— 一人董事嘅 private company，唯一董事唔可以兼任秘書，因為好多法定動作要「董事 + 秘書」兩個人互相制衡（s.479(1) 嘅精神）。(2) 係 (1) 嘅「迂迴版」，s.475(3) 專登封住呢個漏洞 —— 用間自己全資、自己做唯一董事嘅公司做秘書都唔得。(4) 考 s.474(4)：法人秘書嘅 registered office 或 place of business 必須喺香港，New York 公司冇香港據點 = 唔得。只有 (3) Gary 符合「自然人 + 通常居港」。

**Rule Check:** Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) ss.474(1), 474(4), 475(1), 475(2), 475(3), 479(1)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 考生成日只記得 s.475(1)「董事可以兼任秘書」就揀 A —— 必須記住但書：**only-one-director 嘅 private company 例外**。另一伏位係 (2)：以為搵間公司做秘書就繞過限制，其實 s.475(3) 已經封死。

### Q5 — Answer: D

**Model Answer (EN):**
- **Beta Limited** must be entered as a **registrable legal entity**: it is a member of Coral Limited and directly holds 30% of its shares (more than the 25% threshold), giving it significant control (Part 1, Schedule 5A CO).
- **Alpha Limited** must **not** be entered: although it has significant control over Coral Limited through the chain, it is **not a member** of Coral Limited, and a registrable legal entity must be a member of the applicable company.
- **Ms Ho** must be entered as a **registrable person**: she holds a majority stake in Alpha Limited, which holds a majority stake in Beta Limited, which in turn holds the shares in Coral Limited. She therefore holds 30% of Coral Limited's shares **indirectly** through a chain of majority stakes, exceeding the 25% threshold (Schedule 5A CO).

Therefore the register must contain Ms Ho and Beta Limited.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
呢題考 LP Apply and Analyse 5 嘅同款持股鏈分析。三步走：(1) 直接層 —— Beta 直接持 30% > 25%，又係 Coral 嘅 member → registrable legal entity。(2) 中間層 —— Alpha 雖然透過控制 Beta 而有 significant control，但佢**唔係 Coral 嘅股東**，做唔成 registrable legal entity（記住：entity 必須係 member）。(3) 自然人層 —— Ms Ho 透過「majority stake chain」（70% → 60% → 30%）間接持有 Coral 30%，超過 25% → registrable person。A 錯在漏咗 Ms Ho；B 錯在漏咗 Beta；C 錯在將 Alpha 當成 registrable entity。

**Rule Check:** Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) Part 12, Division 2A (ss.653A–653ZK); Schedule 5A (significant control, majority stake chain); cf. LP Apply and Analyse 5

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 兩個伏位：(1) 以為成條鏈嘅公司都要入 register —— 其實中間層唔係目標公司嘅 member 就唔入得；(2) 以為自然人要「直接」持股先算 —— Schedule 5A 嘅 majority stake chain 令間接持股都計，而且 nominee 持股會當係 beneficiary 持有。記住 register 唔係公開畀公眾查，只有 register 上人士同執法機關可查（唔好答「公眾可查閱」）。
