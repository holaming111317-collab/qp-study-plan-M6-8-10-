# M10 Chapter 13: Receivership, Liquidation and Winding-up — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: D
**Model Answer (EN):** Under s.177 of the Companies (Winding Up and Miscellaneous Provisions) Ordinance (Cap.32), the six grounds on which the court may make a winding-up order are: (1) the company has by special resolution resolved to be wound up by the court; (2) the company has not commenced business within a year of incorporation or has suspended business for a whole year (with no intention of recommencing); (3) the company has no members; (4) the company is unable to pay its debts; (5) an event specified in the articles for dissolution has occurred; and (6) it is just and equitable to wind up the company (Ebrahimi v Westbourne Galleries Ltd (1973) AC 360). A "net liability position for two consecutive financial years" is not a statutory ground. It may, however, be *evidence* supporting ground (4) under the balance sheet test (Re Wah Nam Group Ltd (unreported, 26 July 2000) HCCW 166/2000), but it is not itself a free-standing ground.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題係純 recall，考你背唔背到 s.177 六大 grounds。Distractor 設計：D 係最常見伏位 — 學生會將「balance sheet test 下資不抵債」同「s.177 法定 ground」混淆。資產負債表連續兩年淨負債只係證明 unable to pay debts 嘅**證據方法**，唔係獨立 ground。A、B、C 都係 s.177 明寫嘅 ground（B 記住仲要證明無意復業）。考試伏位：默寫 grounds 時唔好漏咗 ground (5)（articles 指定事件），亦要分清 s.177（grounds）同 s.178(1)（deemed inability to pay debts 嘅三種情況）。

**Rule Check:** Companies (Winding Up and Miscellaneous Provisions) Ordinance (Cap.32) s.177; s.178(1); Re Wah Nam Group Ltd (unreported, 26 July 2000) HCCW 166/2000; Ebrahimi v Westbourne Galleries Ltd (1973) AC 360

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 將 insolvency tests（cash flow / balance sheet）當成獨立清盤 ground — 佢哋只係證明 ground (4) 嘅工具；答案字眼要精準："unable to pay its debts" 先係 ground。

### Q2 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** A members' voluntary winding-up is only available where the company is solvent. The directors must issue a certificate of solvency under s.233 of the CWUMPO, having made full enquiry into the company's affairs, stating their opinion that the company will be able to pay its debts in full within **12 months** of the commencement of the winding-up. The certificate must be issued within the **five weeks preceding** the members' resolution for winding-up, and the certificate and resolution must be filed with the Registrar within 15 days after the resolution (s.117(1)). Option A is wrong: the 14-day creditors' meeting requirement (s.241(1)) applies to a *creditors'* voluntary winding-up. Option C is wrong: in a members' voluntary winding-up the liquidator is appointed by the members by ordinary resolution (s.235(1)); the creditors' nomination priority (s.242) applies only to creditors' voluntary winding-up. Option D misstates both the solvency period (12, not 24 months) and the timing of the certificate (before, not after, the resolution).

**解題思路（繁中）:** 分界線係 solvency：有 certificate of solvency (s.233) = members' VWU；冇 (s.233(4)) = creditors' VWU。三個必背數字：12 個月還債期、resolution 前 5 星期內發證明、15 日內交 Registrar。A 同 C 都係 creditors' VWU 嘅程序（s.241 十四日內開債權人會議、s.242 債權人提名優先），故意搬過嚟做 distractor — 考試最鍾意考「邊條程序屬邊種清盤」。D 玩數字+時間方向（after vs preceding）。

**Rule Check:** Companies (Winding Up and Miscellaneous Provisions) Ordinance (Cap.32) ss.233, 233(4), 235(1), 241(1), 242; filing per s.117(1) as cited in the LP

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 以為 voluntary winding-up 一定使開 creditors' meeting — 只有 creditors' VWU 先要；members' VWU 只有當 liquidator 認為還唔切債時先要召開 (s.237A(1))，屆時更可轉軚做 compulsory。

### Q3 — Answer: D
**Model Answer (EN):** **Issue:** Whether transactions (i) and (ii) are liable to be invalidated as unfair preferences. **Rule:** Under ss.266 and 266B of the CWUMPO (importing ss.50(3)/(4) and 51(1)/(2) of the Bankruptcy Ordinance (Cap.6)), a transaction is voidable as an unfair preference if: (1) it puts a creditor/surety/guarantor in a better position than on a winding-up; (2) it was made within six months before the commencement of the winding-up, or two years if the person preferred is an associate (ss.265B–265C); (3) the company was influenced by a desire to prefer; and (4) the company was insolvent at the time or made insolvent by it. The winding-up is deemed to commence at the presentation of the petition, i.e. 1 June (s.184(2)). **Application:** Transaction (ii) (1 April) falls within six months of 1 June; granting security for a pre-existing unsecured debt is a classic unfair preference, and the desire to prefer is evident from the company's response to the bank's pressure (cf. Liquidator of Almond Ltd v Cheong Chan Kao (2019) HKCFI 634). Transaction (i) (1 March) is more than six months before commencement, but the managing director's wife is an associate, so the two-year period applies; early repayment of a not-yet-due unsecured loan to an associate puts her in a better position, and a desire to prefer is readily inferred. **Conclusion:** Both transactions can be challenged by the liquidator.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題考三個嘢疊埋一齊：(1) **relation-back** — commencement 計 petition presentation (1 June)，唔係 order 日 (15 Aug) (s.184(2))，A 就係中咗呢個伏；(2) **6 個月 vs 2 年** — associate（董事配偶，ss.265B–265C）用兩年窗口，所以 3 月嘅還款都捉得返；B 錯喺以為 (ii) 過期（4 月 → 1 June 得兩個月，明明喺六個月內）；(3) **granting security for pre-existing debt** 係 LP 明寫嘅 unfair preference 例子，而 security for *new* loan 先唔算 — C 搞錯方向。答題要逐個 element 對返事實：better position + desire + insolvency + timing。

**Rule Check:** CWUMPO (Cap.32) ss.184(2), 266, 266A, 266B, 265B–265C; Bankruptcy Ordinance (Cap.6) ss.50(3)–(4), 51(1)–(2); Liquidator of Almond Ltd v Cheong Chan Kao (2019) HKCFI 634

**Common Trap（繁中）:** (1) 用 winding-up order 日期計時限；(2) 唔記得 associate 窗口係兩年；(3) 以為「還錢俾自己人」係正常還債 — 未到期就還 + associate + insolvent = 近乎必中 unfair preference。

### Q4 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** **Issue:** Whether Raymond, a privately appointed receiver, breached his duties in selling the charged building. **Rule:** A privately appointed receiver acts as agent of the debtor company but is "the only non-fiduciary agent" — his primary duty is owed to the appointor debenture holder, and he is not a trustee of the power of sale (Super Master Holdings Ltd v Chung Nam Securities Ltd (unreported, 26 March 2004) HCA 4573/2003). However, he owes an equitable duty of good faith and a duty to take reasonable care to obtain the best price reasonably obtainable. A sale to a party in which the mortgagee/appointor is interested is not absolutely prohibited, but the seller must show good faith and that reasonable precautions were taken, including taking expert advice on the method of sale and the reserve price (Tse Kwong Lam v Wong Chit Sen (1983) 3 All ER 54). Ignoring a known higher bidder is a breach of good faith (Forsyth v Blundell (1973) 129 CLR 477). **Application:** Raymond sold without any valuation or advertisement, to a party connected with his appointor, for HK$5–6 million below market, while ignoring the indicated HK$26 million bidder. He cannot discharge his burden of showing good faith and reasonable precautions. **Conclusion:** Raymond is likely in breach of his duties; Jade Properties Ltd may challenge the sale.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 本題核心係 private receiver duty 嘅精確定位。A 係最常見錯誤 — receiver 雖然首要向 appointor 負責，但唔係冇底線：good faith + reasonable care 係硬要求。C 係觀念錯：private receiver 係公司 agent 但**唔係 fiduciary**（LP 原話 "the only non-fiduciary agent"），佢可以喺利益衝突時優先 appointor，所以用 fiduciary duty 分析係揀錯 rule。D 搬咗 fraudulent trading 嘅 actual dishonesty 標準過嚟 — receiver duty breach 唔使證不誠實，Tse Kwong Lam 案都冇 dishonesty，只係低於 reasonable care 標準。記住口訣：可以賣俾自己人，但要證明 good faith + reasonable precautions（expert advice on method & reserve）。

**Rule Check:** Tse Kwong Lam v Wong Chit Sen (1983) 3 All ER 54; Forsyth v Blundell (1973) 129 CLR 477; Super Master Holdings Ltd v Chung Nam Securities Ltd (unreported, 26 March 2004) HCA 4573/2003; CWUMPO (Cap.32) s.298A

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 誤以為 receiver 對公司負 full fiduciary duties（C 選項）；又或者以為「幫債權人收返錢」就免責（A 選項）。另一伏位：receiver 對第三方合同負 personal liability 但有 statutory indemnity (s.298A(2)) — 唔好同 breach of duty 嘅責任撈埋。

### Q5 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** **Issue:** Whether Daniel is liable for fraudulent trading under s.275(1) of the CWUMPO. **Rule:** Fraudulent trading requires: (1) the carrying on of the company's business; (2) with intent to defraud creditors or for a fraudulent purpose; and (3) the defendant being knowingly a party. **Actual dishonesty** must be established: a person is dishonest if he acts in a way he knows to be dishonest by the standards of reasonable and honest people. Crucially, continuing to trade where there is no reasonable prospect of creditors being paid is **not by itself** fraudulent; something more — e.g. misrepresentation to creditors or dishonest intent for personal advantage — is needed (Aktieselskabet Dansk Skibsfinansiering v Brothers (2000) 3 HKCFAR 70, (2000) 1 HKC 511). **Application:** Daniel honestly relied on a signed letter of intent after weeks of negotiation; the injection failed only because the fund withdrew, an event outside his control. His honest belief that the company could trade through is inconsistent with fraud, even if objectively the prospect of survival was low. **Conclusion:** Daniel is not liable for fraudulent trading. (Had the elements been made out, he would face personal liability for the company's debts without limit and criminal liability of up to five years' imprisonment.)

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題係 hard tier，直擊考評報告批評嘅弱點 — 學生見「資不抵債仲繼續落單」就即刻答 fraudulent trading。A 係呢個錯誤嘅化身：Dansk v Brothers 明講 "no reasonable prospect" 本身**唔等於** fraud，客觀上存活率極低都唔否定 honest belief。B 錯在將 s.275(1) 講成 strict liability — 佢係要主觀欺詐意圖嘅嚴重指控（所以有刑事後果，最高監禁 5 年）。D 係無中生有 — creditor protection / chain of causation 喺 s.275 完全唔係 defence。正確答法係行足 IRAC：先講三個 element + dishonesty test，再 apply honest belief（letter of intent = 合理依據），conclusion 斬釘截鐵話唔成立。

**Rule Check:** Companies (Winding Up and Miscellaneous Provisions) Ordinance (Cap.32) s.275(1); Aktieselskabet Dansk Skibsfinansiering v Brothers (2000) 3 HKCFAR 70, (2000) 1 HKC 511

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 「繼續交易 + 冇合理還款希望 = fraud」係錯嘅推論 — 仲要有 misrepresentation、dishonest personal gain 等加碼事實先會推論到 fraud。答題時記住 civil + criminal 雙重後果，conclusion 先至有說服力。
