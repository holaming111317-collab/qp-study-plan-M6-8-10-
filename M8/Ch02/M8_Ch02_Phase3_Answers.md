# M8 Chapter 2: Legal Liability and Corporate Governance — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** Under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), Part on 'Auditor's Liability' (Subdivision 5, subsection 3), any provision — whether in the company's articles, a contract or otherwise — that exempts an auditor of the company (or of an associated company) from, or indemnifies the auditor against, any liability for negligence, default, breach of duty or breach of trust in relation to the performance of the auditor's duties is **void**. However, the same subsection does **not** prevent a company from taking out and maintaining **insurance** for an auditor against such liability, including liability incurred in defending civil or criminal proceedings. It also does not prevent indemnifying an auditor who successfully defends proceedings, is acquitted, or is granted relief by the Court under ss.903 or 904. Therefore the articles clause is void, but the insurance purchase is permitted.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題考 CO 對「豁免核數師責任條款」嘅處理。核心規則好清晰：豁免／彌償條款 **void（無效）**，但 **買保險係允許嘅**。A 錯因為 clause 唔可能 valid；C 錯因為保險係明文允許；D 錯因為就算股東特別決議通過，條款依然 void——法例直接令呢類條款無效，唔係程序問題。記住口訣：「**豁免 = void；保險 = OK**」。

**Rule Check:** Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), 'Auditor's Liability' provisions (Subdivision 5, subsection 3); relief under ss.903–904 (as cited in LP Ch. 2, s.2.1.3)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 學生常誤以為「股東批准就可以豁免核數師責任」——錯，條款本身 void，任何批准都救唔返。亦有人反轉記成「保險都唔准買」，其實法例只係禁止 company 喺條款上免除/彌償責任，唔禁止投保。

### Q2 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** Pearl Delta Bank is a **third-party primary beneficiary**: it was specifically identified to the auditor before the report was issued, and the auditor knew the audited financial statements would be used by the bank for the particular purpose of the loan decision. Under common law (per the plaintiff/liability framework in Exhibit 2.1 of the LP), the minimum basis for an auditor's liability to a third-party primary beneficiary is **ordinary negligence**, with the burden of proof on the plaintiff being misstated financial statements or erroneous advice. The misstatements here would have been detected by a reasonably careful audit, so ordinary negligence is established.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 關鍵係辨認原告類別。Pearl Delta Bank 喺 audit report 簽發前已 **書面表明身份同用途**（loan application），auditor 知情——呢啲係 **identified / primary beneficiary** 嘅特徵，所以最低責任基礎係 **ordinary negligence**。A 錯：fraud 需要 auditor 有 intent to deceive，題目冇；C 錯：「foreseen beneficiary」嘅最低基礎係 gross negligence，但本案銀行唔止 foreseen——佢係明確 identified，屬 primary beneficiary；D 錯：雖然銀行同 auditor 無 privity of contract，但 common law 下 third-party liability 唔需要 privity，auditor 對 identified third parties 有 assumed responsibility。香港法下 third-party liability 範圍雖然 limited，但正正就係限於呢類 identified and particular third parties。

**Rule Check:** LP Ch. 2 Exhibit 2.1 (plaintiff classes: primary beneficiary → ordinary negligence, burden of proof = misstated financial statements or erroneous advice); auditor's assumed responsibility to identified third parties under Hong Kong common law (LP s.2.1.1/2.1.2.1)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 最大陷阱係將「foreseen」同「identified/primary beneficiary」撈亂——題目話「in writing before the report is signed」就係要話你知佢係 primary beneficiary。另外唔好以為「冇合約關係就一定唔使賠」。

### Q3 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** Kwok & Co's conduct amounts to **gross negligence**: it arises from a lack of duty of care indicative of **reckless behaviour**, and the LP expressly gives the failure to follow the HKSAs when carrying out the audit as an example of gross negligence. Deliberately omitting all substantive procedures on the revenue cycle and disregarding mandatory HKSA requirements is more egregious than a mere failure to exercise reasonable care. It is not fraud because there was no intentional misrepresentation or intent to deceive; fraud requires an intentional act involving deception (HKSA 240).

**解題思路（繁中）:** 分辨三層責任：(1) **Ordinary negligence** = 冇盡合理謹慎（例如證據唔夠）；(2) **Gross negligence** = reckless，LP 明講「failure to follow the HKSAs when carrying out the audit」就係例子——本案 audit manager **刻意省略所有 substantive procedures 兼唔跟 HKSAs**，正中此例；(3) **Fraud** = 必須有 **intentional deception**，題目講明「no intention to deceive」所以排除 A。B 錯在於程度：純粹慳時間而全面唔跟準則，已超越 ordinary negligence 嘅「唔小心」。D 錯：gross negligence 唔需要 intent。

**Rule Check:** LP Ch. 2 s.2.1.2.1 (ordinary negligence vs gross negligence elements; gross negligence = reckless behaviour, e.g. failure to follow the HKSAs); HKSA 240 para. 11(a) definition of fraud

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 見到「deliberately not performing procedures」好多學生會揀 fraud——記住 fraud 嘅核心係 **deception / intent to obtain unjust advantage**，唔係懶或 reckless。冇 intent to deceive → 頂多 gross negligence。

### Q4 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** The proposed audit committee does not comply with the Listing Rules. An audit committee of a Hong Kong listed company must comprise **non-executive directors only**, have **at least three members**, and include at least **one independent non-executive director** with appropriate professional qualifications or accounting or related financial management expertise. Appointing the Chief Financial Officer — an executive director — as chair breaches the 'non-executive directors only' requirement, regardless of the CFO's accounting qualification.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 三條規則逐個 check：① NEDs only — CFO 係 executive director，**違規**；② ≥3 members — 符合；③ ≥1 INED with accounting expertise — 有一位 INED 會計專家，符合。所以唯一錯處係 CFO 坐入 committee，答案 B。A 錯：人數同專才達標唔可以抵消 NEDs-only 要求；C 錯：Listing Rules 要求 **at least three**，唔係 five；D 係精心陷阱：「comply or explain」適用於 CG Code 嘅 **Code Provisions**（例如 chairman/CEO 分設），但 audit committee 嘅組成係 **Listing Rules 硬性要求**（must comprise NEDs only），唔可以靠披露理由就算合規。

**Rule Check:** Hong Kong Listing Rules / CG Code — audit committee composition (non-executive directors only; minimum three members; at least one INED with professional/accounting qualifications), as stated in LP Ch. 2 s.2.2.5

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 兩個伏位：(1) 以為 CFO 係會計專才就可以做 audit committee 主席——身份（NED only）同資格（accounting expertise）係兩個獨立要求；(2) 亂用 'comply or explain' 當萬能擋箭牌——佢只適用於 Code Provisions 層面，Listing Rules 嘅硬性組成規定唔適用。

### Q5 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** Under the Hong Kong Corporate Governance Code (Appendix 14 to the Listing Rules), the roles of **chairman and chief executive should be separated**. All listed companies are required to separate the roles; if they choose to combine them, they must give their **reasons** for doing so in accordance with Appendix 14 (the 'comply or explain' approach). The board should properly communicate its decision and rationale to shareholders. Separation is favoured because it strengthens board independence and reduces potential conflicts of interest and concentration of power.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題考 CG Code 嘅性質：佢係 **principles-based, non-statutory** 文件，行 'comply or explain'。Chairman/CEO 分設係 Code 要求，但唔係絕對禁令——合併可以，**必須解釋理由**。A 錯：呢個係 CG Code / Listing Rules 嘅事，唔係 CO (Cap. 622) 嘅刑事式禁令，兩個規管來源唔好撈亂；C 錯：chairman/CEO 分設屬 **Code Provision** 層級（comply or explain），唔係 Recommended Best Practice，所以唔可以「唔使披露」；D 錯：SFC 唔負責審批呢類公司治理安排。

**Rule Check:** Hong Kong Corporate Governance Code, Appendix 14 to the Listing Rules — separation of chairman and chief executive roles with reasons required if combined (LP Ch. 2 s.2.2.4, 'Chairman and Chief Executive')

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 最常見錯誤係將 CG Code 當成法例（揀 A）。記住層級：**CO (Cap. 622) = statutory**；**Listing Rules = 硬性上市規定**；**CG Code = comply or explain**。題目問 CG Code 義務時，答案通常係「照做，否則解釋」而唔係「禁止」。
