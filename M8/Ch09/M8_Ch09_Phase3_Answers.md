# M8 Chapter 9: Auditor's Reporting — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** A **qualified opinion** is appropriate. The auditor has obtained sufficient appropriate audit evidence and concluded that the financial statements are materially misstated (repairs of HK$3.8 million wrongly capitalised, a departure from HKFRSs), but the misstatement is confined to specific balances (PPE and repairs expense) and is therefore **material but not pervasive**. Under the HKSA 705 (Revised) decision matrix, a material-but-not-pervasive misstatement results in a qualified ("except for") opinion. The Basis for Qualified Opinion paragraph would describe the matter and quantify its financial effects.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 兩步定位法：第一步問「有冇證據？」— 有，auditor 已經量化咗個錯誤，所以排除 disclaimer（D）。第二步問「pervasive 唔 pervasive？」— 題目講明 confined to PPE and related expense、唔影響 FS as a whole，所以唔係 adverse（C），只係 qualified。A 錯因為 EoM 係用嚟指向 FS 已披露嘅事項，而且 unmodified opinion 喺有 material misstatement 又拒絕調整嘅情況下根本唔可以出。考試伏位：見到管理層「refuses to adjust」加「confined to specific balances」就係標準 qualified 劇本。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 705 (Revised) paras. 4, 7 and A1 (decision matrix); HKSA 700 (Revised) for unmodified conditions.

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 考生見金額「material」就衝動揀 adverse — 記住 adverse 要同時 material **AND** pervasive。另一伏位係以為「管理層拒絕調整」等於 scope limitation → 揀 disclaimer；其實 auditor 已攞到足夠證據，呢個係 misstatement 而唔係 inability to obtain evidence。

### Q2 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** A **disclaimer of opinion** is appropriate. The auditor is **unable to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence** over cash sales representing the majority of transactions, and concludes the possible effects of undetected misstatements could be **both material and pervasive**. Under HKSA 705 (Revised) paras. 9–10 and the para. A1 decision matrix, inability to obtain evidence with material and pervasive possible effects leads to a disclaimer — essentially no opinion is expressed ("We do not express an opinion…"). The fire is a circumstance beyond the entity's control, one of the three recognised sources of inability to obtain evidence.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 關鍵字係「unable to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence」+「material and pervasive」→ 直接對應矩陣右下角 = disclaimer。A 錯：scope limitation 唔係管理層造成唔代表可以降格做 qualified — pervasiveness 先係決定因素，70% 收入無憑無據已經係 pervasive。B 錯：adverse 係「有證據證明 FS 錯」先用得；而家係「唔知啲數啱唔啱」，性質完全不同。D 錯：unmodified opinion 要求 FS as a whole 無 material misstatement 且證據充分，明顯不成立；OM paragraph 亦唔係用嚟交代咁重大嘅取證失敗。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 705 (Revised) paras. 4, 9–10, A1; LP §9.5.3 (sources of inability: circumstances beyond entity's control).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 最常犯錯係將「records destroyed」當成 FS 一定錯 → 揀 adverse。記住口訣：**有證據先可以 adverse；無證據 + pervasive = disclaimer**。另外 disclaimer 唔係「soft option」— 佢代表 auditor 完全無法結論。

### Q3 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** **B**. An Other Matter paragraph refers to a matter **other than those presented or disclosed in the financial statements** that, in the auditor's judgement, is relevant to users' understanding of **the audit, the auditor's responsibilities or the auditor's report** (HKSA 706 (Revised)). The fact that the prior year's financial statements were audited by another auditor is not a matter disclosed in the current-year financial statements and is a classic OM trigger listed in the LP. Options A and D describe matters already disclosed in the financial statements → Emphasis of Matter. Option C belongs in the separate "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern" section (HKSA 570 (Revised)).

**解題思路（繁中）:** 分辨口訣：「件事喺唔喺 FS 入面？」喺 → EoM（A、D）；唔喺 → OM（B，上年由另一間 firm 審係關於 *the audit* 嘅資訊，唔會寫喺 FS）。C 係最危險 distractor：going concern material uncertainty 披露足夠時有自己專屬嘅 MURGC section，唔係 EoM 亦唔係 OM。呢條直接打中 Jun 2025 panelist 批評嘅「OM 與 EoM/other information 調亂」弱點。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 706 (Revised) (definitions of EoM and OM paragraphs); HKSA 570 (Revised) (MURGC section).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 唔好以為「重要」嘅事項自動入 EoM — EoM 嘅前提係事項**已經喺 FS 披露**且 fundamental to users' understanding。OM 講嘅係關於審計本身/核數師責任/報告嘅事項，唔係 FS 內容。

### Q4 — Answer: D
**Model Answer (EN):** **D**. HKSA 701 defines KAMs as those matters that, **in the auditor's professional judgement, were of most significance** in the audit of the financial statements of the current period. The introductory language in the KAM section must state that the matters were addressed in the context of the audit of the financial statements as a whole and that **the auditor does not provide a separate opinion** on them. A is wrong: KAM communication is **mandatory for listed entities only**, voluntary otherwise. B is wrong: no separate opinion is given on individual KAMs. C is wrong: a matter giving rise to a modified opinion (or an MURGC) is by nature a KAM, but it is **not repeated** as a separate KAM — the KAM introductory paragraph cross-refers to the Basis for Qualified/Adverse Opinion or MURGC section instead.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 四個選項分別測四個 KAM 核心點：適用範圍（listed only）、唔提供 separate opinion、同 modified opinion/MURGC 嘅 cross-reference 規則、同埋定義本身。A 係「所有公司都要」嘅常見誇大；B 違反「no separate opinion」原則；C 係技術性最強嘅 distractor — 引致 qualified 嘅事項本質上係 KAM，但 HKSA 701 唔准重複描述，只可以 cross-refer。答 D 要講到「professional judgement + most significance + current period」三個關鍵字。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 701 paras. 9–11 (definition, determining and communicating KAMs); LP Key Learning Point — KAMs required for listed entities only.

**Common Trap（繁中）:** Panelist report 明確話考生「did not put enough effort into studying KAM」。最易錯係 C — 以為 modified opinion 嘅事項要照樣列入 KAM section；其實要避免重複，KAM intro 會寫 "In addition to the matter described in the Basis for Qualified Opinion… section, we have determined…"。

### Q5 — Answer: A
**Model Answer (EN):** **A**. Where (i) the going concern basis of accounting is appropriate, (ii) a material uncertainty exists, and (iii) that uncertainty is **adequately disclosed** in the financial statements, the auditor expresses an **unmodified opinion** and includes a separate **"Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern"** section drawing attention to the relevant note disclosures (HKSA 570 (Revised)). The opinion is **not** modified. B is wrong: an adequately disclosed going concern material uncertainty is reported in the dedicated MURGC section, not an Emphasis of Matter paragraph. C is wrong: the mere existence of a material uncertainty does not modify the opinion when disclosure is adequate. D is wrong: an adverse opinion applies only where the going concern basis itself is inappropriate but has been used.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 跟 Exhibit 9.7 決策樹行：GC basis appropriate? YES → adequate disclosure? YES → unmodified opinion + MURGC paragraph。四個選項對應四個可能 outcome，考你識唔識分：披露不足先會 qualified/adverse（C 嘅陷阱）；GC basis 本身唔適用先會 adverse（D 嘅陷阱）；MURGC 有專屬 section 唔係 EoM（B 嘅陷阱 — LP Knowledge Check Q18 同款概念，但場景全新）。記住「going concern 有 uncertainty ≠ opinion 要 modified」。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 570 (Revised) Going Concern; LP §9.6.3 and Exhibit 9.7; Apply and Analyse 4 (unmodified opinion + MURGC).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 考生見「連續虧損 + 巨額貸款到期」就條件反射揀 adverse 或 qualified。考試要逐個條件拆：basis 啱唔啱？disclosure 夠唔夠？evidence 攞唔攞到？三條問題嘅答案組合先決定 outcome，唔係單憑「公司財困」判斷。
