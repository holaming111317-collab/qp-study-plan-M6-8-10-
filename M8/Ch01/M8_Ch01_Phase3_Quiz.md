**Question 1 (MCQ)**

Which of the following correctly describes a review engagement?

A. It provides reasonable assurance expressed in a positive form, because the practitioner performs the full range of audit procedures.
B. It provides no assurance, because the practitioner only reports factual findings agreed with the client.
C. It provides limited assurance expressed in a negative form, based primarily on enquiry and analytical procedures.
D. It provides limited assurance expressed in a positive form, because the practitioner still expresses an opinion on the subject matter.

---

**Question 2 (Scenario MCQ)**

Harbour Logistics Limited is negotiating a HK$40 million bank loan. The bank asks for independent verification of the company's monthly inventory turnover figures. Harbour's management engages a CPA firm to perform a fixed list of procedures that management and the bank have agreed in advance (e.g. recompute turnover ratios, agree figures to the inventory ledger) and to report only the factual results of those procedures. The CPA firm will not express any opinion or conclusion.

Which of the following best describes this engagement?

A. An audit engagement conducted in accordance with HKSAs, providing reasonable assurance.
B. A review engagement conducted in accordance with HKSREs, providing limited assurance.
C. An agreed-upon procedures engagement under HKSRS 4400 (Revised), providing no assurance.
D. An assurance engagement under HKSAE 3000 (Revised), providing either reasonable or limited assurance at the practitioner's discretion.

---

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

A regulator requires Everline Limited, a licensed gas supplier, to engage an auditor to report on whether the company has complied with specific pipeline safety regulations during the year. Management does NOT make any written assertion about the company's compliance; the auditor measures and evaluates the company's compliance against the regulations directly, and reports the outcome together with the auditor's conclusion.

Under the Hong Kong Framework for Assurance Engagements (Amended), this engagement is:

A. An attest engagement, because the auditor is attesting to the company's compliance.
B. A non-assurance engagement, because the subject matter is non-financial information.
C. A limited assurance engagement, because the subject matter is compliance rather than financial statements.
D. A direct reporting engagement, because the auditor measures the subject matter against the criteria without a written assertion from the responsible party.

**Question 4 (Scenario MCQ)**

Vertex & Co, a CPA firm, completed a major project last year for Larkspur Limited, designing and implementing a new accounting and internal control system that now generates all of the company's accounting records and financial statements. Larkspur has now invited Vertex & Co to accept appointment as its external auditor for the current year.

Accepting the audit appointment would most directly create which category of threat to the fundamental ethical principles?

A. Self-review threat.
B. Self-interest threat.
C. Advocacy threat.
D. Intimidation threat.
