# M8 Chapter 3: Audit Planning I — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. Explain the four stages of an audit and the objective of audit planning under HKSA 300 *Planning an Audit of Financial Statements*.
2. Identify and assess the risks of material misstatement at the financial statement level and the assertion level in accordance with HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) *Identifying and Assessing the Risks of Material Misstatement*.
3. Describe the five components of the system of internal control and evaluate control activities relevant to the audit.
4. Distinguish the seven audit methodologies (risk-based, top-down, system-based, systems audit, balance sheet approach, transaction cycle approach, directional testing) and their advantages/disadvantages.
5. Apply the auditor's responsibilities relating to fraud under HKSA 240, including fraud risk factors and audit responses.

### 必背考點

#### A. Planning & Risk Assessment（核心框架）

**Four stages of an audit（LP §3.1）**
1. **Risk assessment stage** — understand the client, its environment; identify risks that could cause material misstatement.
2. **Risk response stage** — devise responses: tests of control + substantive procedures.
3. **Evidence-gathering stage** — 'detailed audit fieldwork'; gather sufficient appropriate audit evidence.
4. **Reporting stage** — review evidence, form opinion, issue auditor's report per **HKSA 700 (Revised)**.

**HKSA 300 — objective & benefits**
- Objective: plan the audit so that it will be performed in an **effective manner**（LP 引述 HKSA 300）。
- **HKSA 300 para. 2** — benefits of adequate planning: (i) devote appropriate attention to important areas; (ii) identify and resolve potential problems on a timely basis; (iii) properly organize and manage the engagement effectively and efficiently; (iv) assist selection of engagement team members with appropriate capabilities/competence and assignment of work; (v) facilitate direction, supervision and review; (vi) assist coordination of work done by component auditors and experts.
- Audit planning is a **continuous process** — the plan may need revision (e.g. new fraud risk factors found during fieldwork); all changes must be documented with reasons.

**Audit strategy vs audit plan**
- Audit strategy = high-level plan of how the audit will be tackled; audit plan = detailed operational plan of how the strategy is achieved (nature, timing, extent of procedures).

**Risk assessment procedures（HKSA 315 (Revised 2019)）**
- Performed through: **inquiries of management; analytical procedures; observation and inspection**.
- HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) requires the auditor to **'stand back'** after risk assessment to evaluate whether all risks of material misstatement have been identified.
- Understanding required at three levels: **entity level** (operations, ownership/governance, investments, structure & financing — HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) App. 1 para. 5), **industry level** (market & competition, cyclical/seasonal activity, product technology, energy supply/cost — para. A68) and **economy level** (recession, interest rates, currency fluctuations — para. A70).

#### B. Risk Model & Materiality 相關定義（本章範圍）

- **Audit risk** (Glossary of Terms / HKSA 200): the risk that the auditor expresses an **inappropriate audit opinion** when the financial statements are materially misstated. Audit risk = function of **risks of material misstatement** × **detection risk**.
- **Risk of material misstatement (RoMM)** = **inherent risk** × **control risk** (exists *before* the audit starts):
  - *Inherent risk* — susceptibility of an assertion to a misstatement that could be material, **before consideration of any related controls**.
  - *Control risk* — risk that a material misstatement will **not be prevented, or detected and corrected, on a timely basis by the entity's internal control**.
- **Detection risk** (**HKSA 200.13(e)**): the risk that the auditor's procedures will **not detect** a misstatement that exists and could be material. → **唯一受 auditor 控制嘅 risk**；detection risk 與 substantive procedures 嘅 extent 成反比。
- **Spectrum of inherent risk** (HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) / HKSA 540 (Revised)): the degree to which inherent risk varies; the higher on the spectrum, the **more persuasive** the audit evidence needed.
- **Significant risk** (**HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para. 12(l)**): an identified RoMM (i) for which inherent risk is assessed **close to the upper end of the spectrum**, or (ii) treated as significant per other HKSAs. Consider: fraud risk; recent significant economic/accounting developments; complexity; related-party transactions; subjectivity/measurement uncertainty; unusual transactions outside normal course of business.
- **Misstatement** (**HKSA 450.4(a)**): a difference between the reported amount/classification/presentation/disclosure of an item and what is required under the applicable financial reporting framework. Can arise from **error or fraud**.
- **Business risk** (**HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para. 12(b)**): a risk resulting from significant conditions, events, circumstances, actions or inactions that could adversely affect the entity's ability to achieve its objectives and execute its strategies. Business risk is **broader than** RoMM but includes it → auditor uses a **business risk approach** (look at the business in its entirety; evaluate risks; consider impact on the FS).

#### C. Assertions（HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para. A190）

**Assertions about classes of transactions and events, and related disclosures (para. A190(a)):**
- **Occurrence** — transactions/events recorded have occurred and pertain to the entity.
- **Completeness** — all transactions/events that should have been recorded have been recorded.
- **Accuracy** — amounts/data recorded appropriately.
- **Cut-off** — recorded in the correct accounting period.
- **Classification** — recorded in the proper accounts.
- **Presentation** — appropriately aggregated/disaggregated and clearly described.

**Assertions about account balances, and related disclosures at period end (para. A190(b)):**
- **Existence** — assets/liabilities/equity exist.
- **Rights and obligations** — entity holds/controls rights to assets; liabilities are obligations of the entity.
- **Completeness** — all assets/liabilities/equity that should have been recorded have been recorded.
- **Accuracy, valuation and allocation** — included at appropriate amounts; valuation/allocation adjustments appropriately recorded.
- **Classification** — recorded in the proper accounts.
- **Presentation** — appropriately aggregated/disaggregated; disclosures relevant and understandable.

**Directional testing（方向性測試）**: assets & expenses tested for **overstatement** (vouch from records → source documents = existence/occurrence); liabilities & income tested for **understatement** (trace from source documents → records = completeness).

#### D. Procedures & Evidence

**HKSA 500 Audit Evidence** — auditor must obtain **sufficient appropriate** audit evidence:
- *Sufficiency* = **quantity** of evidence; *Appropriateness* = **quality** (relevance and reliability).
- Types of audit procedures recognised by HKSA 500: **inspection; observation; external confirmation; recalculation; reperformance; analytical procedures; inquiry**.

**Tests of control vs substantive procedures**
- Tests of control — test the **operating effectiveness** of the client's internal controls; not designed to detect material misstatement; do not focus on a monetary amount.
- Substantive procedures — designed to **detect material misstatement** in the FS (tests of detail + substantive analytical procedures).
- **Key rule**: the auditor can **never eliminate substantive procedures entirely** — inherent limitations of internal control (human error; ineffective controls; collusion; management override; management judgement on extent of controls).

**Five components of the system of internal control (HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para. 12(m)):**
1. **Control environment** — governance/management functions + attitudes, awareness, actions of TCWG and management; sets the 'tone at the top' (integrity & ethical values; effectiveness of controls cannot rise above the integrity of the people who create/administer/monitor them).
2. **The entity's risk assessment process** — how management identifies/manages business risks.
3. **The entity's process to monitor the system of internal control** — management assessing effectiveness of controls over time (ongoing or separate evaluations; e.g. timeliness/accuracy of bank reconciliations).
4. **The information system and communication** — procedures/records to initiate, record, process and report transactions.
5. **Control activities** — **authorisation and approvals; reconciliations; verifications; physical or logical controls; segregation of duties** (HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para. 15 examples).

**Significant deficiency in internal control** → must be communicated to TCWG per **HKSA 265 Communicating Deficiencies in Internal Control to Those Charged with Governance and Management**.

**Engagement letter (HKSA 210)**
- Objective: accept/continue only when preconditions present + common understanding of terms.
- **Preconditions (HKSA 210.6)**: (1) acceptable financial reporting framework; (2) management acknowledges responsibility for (a) preparation of FS, (b) necessary internal control, (c) providing auditor access to all information + additional information + unrestricted access to persons. If preconditions absent → **decline or resign** (unless required by law/regulation).
- Contents: objective & scope; auditor's responsibilities; management's responsibilities (under Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) directors prepare FS showing true and fair view); applicable financial reporting framework; expected form/content of reports; statement that the report may differ from expected form/content.
- Legally binding contract; sent to **new clients before work starts**; reviewed **each year** for existing clients (reissue only on change of circumstances).

**Audit documentation (HKSA 230)**
- **HKSA 230.2**: documentation provides evidence of (1) the basis for the auditor's conclusion on achieving the overall objectives; (2) that the audit was planned and performed per HKSAs and legal/regulatory requirements.
- **HKSA 230.8**: sufficient to enable an **experienced auditor with no previous connection** to the audit to understand nature/timing/extent of procedures, results and evidence obtained, and significant matters/judgements.

#### E. Ethics / Fraud（HKSA 240）

- **Primary responsibility for prevention and detection of fraud rests with management and TCWG** — NOT the auditor.
- Auditor's objectives (**HKSA 240.10**): (1) identify and assess RoMM due to fraud; (2) obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence via designed responses; (3) respond appropriately to fraud/suspected fraud identified.
- **Fraud** = intentional act using deception for unjust/illegal advantage. Two types: **fraudulent financial reporting** (deliberately misstating FS, e.g. fictitious sales) and **misappropriation of assets** (theft, e.g. fictitious supplier/ghost employee). Fraud vs error distinction = **intentional or unintentional**.
- **Fraud risk factors** (HKSA 240.11(b)): events/conditions indicating incentive/pressure or opportunity — e.g. profit-related bonus pressure; loan covenants; dominant manager; missing/doctored documents (fraudulent financial reporting); weak controls, high-value inventory/cash, disgruntled employees, employees not taking leave (misappropriation). A fraud risk factor ≠ proof of fraud.
- Risk assessment procedures (**HKSA 240.17–18**): inquiries of management / internal audit / TCWG about actual/suspected/alleged fraud; evaluate unusual analytical relationships; consider other information; evaluate fraud risk factors.
- Responses: written representations (**HKSA 580**); test journal entries/adjustments near year-end; test accounting estimates for bias; **unpredictable procedures**; assign experienced staff.
- **Professional scepticism** must be maintained throughout, regardless of past experience of management's honesty/integrity.
- Fraud discovered → communicate on a timely basis to appropriate level of management; if fraud involves management → **TCWG**; doubts over TCWG integrity → **seek legal advice**; legal duty to report to third parties (e.g. ICAC, Police, SFC, HKMA) overrides confidentiality.

#### F. Report Modifications Overview（HKSA 700 (Revised)，詳見 Ch9）

| Opinion type | When used |
|---|---|
| **Unmodified opinion** | FS free from material misstatement; prepared per applicable framework (best opinion). |
| **Qualified 'except for' opinion** | Material **but not pervasive** misstatement, or material-but-not-pervasive scope limitation. |
| **Adverse opinion** | Misstatement is **material AND pervasive** (FS do not give true and fair view). |
| **Disclaimer of opinion** | Unable to obtain sufficient appropriate evidence **and** possible effects could be **pervasive** (no opinion expressed). |

- **Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern (MURGC) paragraph** (HKSA 570 (Revised)): used ONLY when the material uncertainty is **adequately disclosed**; inadequate disclosure → modified opinion instead (MURGC inappropriate).
- **Key Audit Matters (HKSA 701)**: mandatory for **listed entities**; matters of **most significance** in the auditor's professional judgement, selected from matters communicated to TCWG (HKSA 701.8, 701.9(a)–(c)). Non-listed entities may include KAM voluntarily.

### 易混淆概念對比

**表 1：Audit Risk / Business Risk / Audit Failure / Business Failure**

| Term | Meaning (EN) | 點樣分辨／考試伏位 |
|---|---|---|
| **Audit risk** | Risk that the auditor expresses an **inappropriate opinion** when FS are materially misstated = RoMM × detection risk | 係「auditor 講錯嘢」嘅風險；只有 **detection risk** 係 auditor 控制到。伏位：唔好寫成「FS 有 misstatement 嘅風險」（嗰個係 RoMM）。 |
| **Business risk** | Risk from conditions/events/circumstances/actions/inactions adversely affecting the entity's ability to achieve **objectives and strategies** (HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para. 12(b)) | 係「間公司」面對嘅風險，**broader than RoMM**（包含但唔等於）。external factors（利率、匯率、競爭）＝business risk；資本化錯誤、cut-off 錯誤＝audit/FS risk。 |
| **Audit failure** | Auditor's report incorrectly states FS are correct when they contain errors/false statements | 係 audit risk **真正發生咗**嘅結果；通常因 poor planning、無經驗員工、procedures 唔足、deadline 太趕。後果：罰款、HKICPA 紀律處分、禁做 audit。 |
| **Business failure** | Entity runs out of cash → liquidation/bankruptcy（包括 profitable 公司 **over-trading**） | 公司倒閉**唔等於**auditor 有錯；考生常混淆——business failure 之後 users 先會話 auditor negligence（嗰啲先係 audit failure）。 |

**表 2：Tests of Control vs Substantive Procedures**

| | **Tests of control** | **Substantive procedures** |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose (EN) | Test the **operating effectiveness** of internal controls | **Detect material misstatements** in the FS |
| Focus | The controls that generate the financial reports — **no monetary amount** | Transactions, balances, disclosures (monetary amounts) |
| When used | When auditor **plans to rely** on internal controls | **Always required** for material balances — can never be eliminated entirely |
| Effect on the other | Effective controls → **reduce** substantive procedures | Weak/absent controls → **extend** substantive procedures |
| 點樣分辨／考試伏位 | 見到「檢查 control 有冇運作」（如觀察授權步驟、reperform bank reconciliation）＝ToC | 見到「搵錯數」（confirm receivables、recalculate depreciation、cut-off testing）＝substantive。Jun 2023 考官報告：考生唔明 **control risk 低 → substantive 少**呢個 inverse relationship。 |

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** What are the four stages of an audit, and what happens in each?

**Back:**
- **Risk assessment stage** — understand the client and its environment; identify risks that could cause material misstatement.
- **Risk response stage** — devise responses: tests of control and substantive procedures.
- **Evidence-gathering stage** — 'detailed fieldwork': gather sufficient appropriate audit evidence.
- **Reporting stage** — review evidence, conclude, form opinion and issue the auditor's report (HKSA 700 (Revised)).

💬 記住順序：「**評 → 應 → 證 → 報**」。Planning 唔係做一次就算，係 continuous process，有新發現（例如 fraud risk factors）就要修訂 plan 兼 document 低原因。

### Card 2
**Front:** Define audit risk and its components. Which component is controllable by the auditor?

**Back:**
- **Audit risk** = risk the auditor expresses an **inappropriate opinion** when FS are materially misstated (Glossary of Terms; HKSA 200).
- Audit risk = **risk of material misstatement × detection risk**.
- RoMM = **inherent risk × control risk** (both exist before the audit; auditor cannot control them, only assess them).
- **Detection risk** (HKSA 200.13(e)) = risk the auditor's procedures fail to detect a material misstatement → **the only risk under the auditor's control**.

💬 考官伏位：RoMM 係「未 audit 之前已存在」，auditor 唔可以降低佢；auditor 只可以做多啲/好啲 procedures 去壓低 detection risk。Control risk 低 → substantive testing 可以少啲（inverse relationship）。

### Card 3
**Front:** List the benefits of adequate audit planning per HKSA 300 para. 2.

**Back:**
- Devote appropriate attention to important areas of the audit.
- Identify and resolve potential problems on a timely basis.
- Properly organize and manage the engagement effectively and efficiently.
- Assist selection of team members with appropriate capabilities/competence and assignment of work.
- Facilitate direction, supervision and review of the team's work.
- Assist coordination of work by component auditors and experts.

💬 六點全背，「List」題常客。注意：「save costs」同「reduce substantive testing」**唔係** HKSA 300.2 嘅 benefit（嗰啲係有效內控嘅效果）——MCQ 經常放呢兩個做 distractor。

### Card 4
**Front:** What are the preconditions for an audit under HKSA 210, and what must the auditor do if they are not present?

**Back:**
1. Determine the **financial reporting framework** is acceptable.
2. Obtain management's agreement that it acknowledges responsibility for:
   - (a) preparation of the FS per the applicable framework;
   - (b) **internal control** necessary to prepare FS free from material misstatement;
   - (c) providing the auditor with **all relevant information + additional information + unrestricted access to persons**.
- If preconditions not present → **decline or resign**, unless required by law/regulation to continue.

💬 Engagement letter 係 legally binding contract；新客開工前必發，舊客**每年 review**（唔係每年必發新信——有情況改變如換管理層、改 reporting framework 先需要更新）。呢個「每年發 vs 每年 review」係常見陷阱。

### Card 5
**Front:** List the five components of the system of internal control (HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para. 12(m)) and the five examples of control activities.

**Back:**
- Five components: **Control environment; Entity's risk assessment process; Process to monitor the system of internal control; Information system and communication; Control activities**.
- Control activities examples: **Authorisation and approvals; Reconciliations; Verifications; Physical or logical controls; Segregation of duties**.

💬 分層記法：五大 components 係「層級」，control activities 係第五層入面嘅具體動作。常見混淆：「commitment to integrity」屬 **control environment**；「segregation of duties」屬 **control activities**；「management 定期 review controls」屬 **monitoring**。仲有：內控再好都**唔可以唔做 substantive procedures**（inherent limitations：human error、collusion、management override 等）。

### Card 6
**Front:** State the assertions for (a) classes of transactions and events and (b) account balances per HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para. A190.

**Back:**
- (a) Transactions/events (para. A190(a)): **Occurrence, Completeness, Accuracy, Cut-off, Classification, Presentation**.
- (b) Account balances (para. A190(b)): **Existence, Rights and obligations, Completeness, Accuracy/valuation/allocation, Classification, Presentation**.

💬 分辨口訣：transactions 用 **OCCAC-P**（有 occurrence 同 cut-off）；balances 用 **ERCAP**（有 existence 同 rights & obligations，**冇** occurrence/cut-off）。Directional testing：由 ledger 查到 source documents（vouching）＝ existence/occurrence（overstatement）；由 source documents 追入 ledger（tracing）＝ completeness（understatement）。

### Card 7
**Front:** What is a 'significant risk' and what factors indicate one?

**Back:**
- Definition (HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) para. 12(l)): identified RoMM where inherent risk is assessed **close to the upper end of the spectrum of inherent risk**, or treated as significant per other HKSAs.
- Indicators: risk of **fraud**; recent significant economic/accounting developments; **complex** transactions; significant **related-party** transactions; high **subjectivity / measurement uncertainty**; **unusual** transactions outside normal course of business.
- If significant risk identified → must obtain understanding of relevant **controls** over it; if management has no controls over it → **significant deficiency** → report to TCWG (HKSA 265).

💬 Spectrum of inherent risk 越高 → evidence 要越 **persuasive**。考試鍾意問「邊樣係 significant risk」：新會計準則首年採用、重大 judgment/estimate（如 WIP 估值由無經驗員工做）都係典型答案。

### Card 8
**Front:** Who is responsible for preventing and detecting fraud, and what are the auditor's objectives and responses under HKSA 240?

**Back:**
- **Prevention/detection of fraud = management + TCWG** (primary responsibility).
- Auditor's objectives (HKSA 240.10): identify/assess RoMM due to fraud; obtain sufficient appropriate evidence via responses; respond appropriately to fraud identified.
- Responses: written representations (HKSA 580); test **journal entries** near year-end; review **accounting estimates** for bias; **unpredictable** procedures; experienced staff.
- Fraud found → tell appropriate level of management; fraud involving management → **TCWG**; doubts over TCWG integrity → **legal advice**; statutory reporting (ICAC, Police, SFC etc.) overrides confidentiality.

💬 考生常錯：以為 auditor 負責 prevent/detect fraud——錯！Auditor 只負責 obtain **reasonable assurance** FS 無 material misstatement（whether due to fraud or error），並且無論過往經驗都要 keep **professional scepticism**。Fraud vs error 嘅分別係 **intentional vs unintentional**。
