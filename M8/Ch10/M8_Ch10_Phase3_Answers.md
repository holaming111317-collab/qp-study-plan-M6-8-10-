# M8 Chapter 10: Group Audits — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: B

**Model Answer (EN):** Pearl Delta Warehousing Limited is of individual financial significance to the group: at 22% of group total assets and 19% of group revenue, it exceeds the 15% benchmark adopted as indicative of financial significance. Under HKSA 600 (Revised), for a component that is financially significant, the group engagement team, or a component auditor on its behalf, is required to perform a full audit of the financial information of the component using component materiality (i.e. a materiality level lower than group materiality).

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題考「size-driven significant component → 必做 full audit」。Component 兩個指標（22% assets、19% revenue）都超過 15% benchmark，所以係 financially significant。Distractors：A（analytical procedures at group level）係 **non-significant** components 嘅做法；C（specified procedures only）係 **risk-driven** significant component 嘅其中一個選項，但呢度 driver 係 size 唔係 risk；D（review consolidation package）根本唔係 HKSA 600 (Revised) 下對 significant component 嘅足夠回應。考試伏位：見到 「no specific risks identified」 唔好被誤導以為唔使做嘢 — size 本身已經足以觸發 full audit。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 600 (Revised) paras. 26–27 (per LP); 15% benchmark per HKSA 600 (Revised) para. A6 (as cited in LP).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 學生常混淆三類 component 嘅所需工作。記口訣：Size → full audit（用 component materiality）；Risk → full audit / audit of specific balances / specified procedures；Non-significant → group-level analytical procedures。

### Q2 — Answer: C

**Model Answer (EN):** Component materiality is determined by the group engagement team for each component on which audit work is performed, and it must be lower than group materiality (it cannot exceed group materiality). The level of component materiality must be communicated to the component auditor, typically in the group audit instructions, so that the component auditor performs work and reports against that threshold.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題考「邊個定 component materiality + 上限係幾多」。A 錯：component materiality 唔係 component auditor 自己定嘅 — LP 明確指出 materiality levels 由 group auditor 決定，component auditor 只係按指示應用。B 錯：component materiality **唔可以高過** group materiality（即使 component 有 local statutory audit，按較低嘅 local materiality 做都唔會造成問題，因為佢仍然要按 group 指示匯報）。D 錯：component materiality 係 group auditor → component auditor 溝通清單入面嘅明確項目，必須傳達。考試伏位：選項用「closest to operations」「unpredictability」等聽落合理嘅包裝嚟掩飾錯誤嘅責任歸屬。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 600 (Revised) — LP 10.3.1 (materiality levels determined by group engagement team; component materiality cannot be higher than group materiality; communicated to component auditor).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 以為「component auditor 最熟 component，所以佢定 materiality」。錯 — materiality 設定係 group engagement partner 責任鏈嘅一部分，因為 group opinion 係佢孭。

### Q3 — Answer: C

**Model Answer (EN):** Under HKSA 600 (Revised), the group auditor must obtain an understanding of: (1) whether the component auditor will comply with ethical requirements, particularly independence (including Hong Kong requirements where the component auditor is based outside Hong Kong); (2) the component auditor's professional competence; (3) whether the group auditor will be able to be involved in the component auditor's work as necessary to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence; and (4) whether the component auditor operates in a regulatory environment that actively oversees auditors. The profitability or fee income of the component auditor's firm is not among these required matters.

**解題思路（繁中）:** Negation stem（NOT）題，考四項 required understanding 嘅背誦同辨認。A、B、D 都係 HKSA 600 (Revised) 明列嘅事項（ethics/independence、competence、regulatory environment），加埋「能否參與 component auditor 工作」就係四項。C 係唯一唔關事嘅 — 一間 component auditor firm 賺唔賺錢同佢能否勝任 group audit 無直接關係，HKSA 600 (Revised) 冇呢個要求。呢類 NOT 題一定要逐個選項對返個 list，唔好靠感覺。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 600 (Revised) paras. 19–20 (as cited in LP).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 學生容易因為「fee」「profitability」聽落似商業考慮而誤以為係 acceptance 考慮嘅一部分。留意：呢度問嘅係 **using the work of a component auditor** 嘅 understanding，唔係 client acceptance；兩者嘅考慮因素清單唔同，唔好撈亂。

### Q4 — Answer: See model answer

**Model Answer (EN):** A component is identified as a significant component if either of the following criteria is met:
1. It is of **individual financial significance** to the group (e.g. a 15% benchmark of group assets, profit or revenue may be applied as indicative of financial significance, subject to professional judgement and documentation of its appropriateness); **or**
2. Due to its **specific nature or circumstances**, it is likely to include **significant risks of material misstatement** of the group financial statements (e.g. components involving impairment estimates, complex financial instruments, taxation provisions or contingencies).

For **non-significant components**, the group auditor is required to perform **analytical procedures at group level** covering those components, to corroborate the conclusion that there are no significant risks in them. If no additional risks are identified, the group auditor documents the belief that no further procedures are needed; if the analytical procedures indicate a possible risk of material misstatement, the group auditor documents the nature, timing and extent of further procedures to address the identified risks.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 短答題，兩部分都要答齊先攞滿分。第一部分係 significant component 嘅定義 — 關鍵字係 **「individual financial significance」** 同 **「likely to include significant risks of material misstatement due to specific nature or circumstances」**，兩個 criteria 係「或」唔係「和」，答少一個會失分。第二部分伏位最大：好多學生答「non-significant components 唔使做程序」— 呢個係零分答案；HKSA 600 (Revised) 明確要求 group-level analytical procedures，而且要 **document** 結論同埋（如有需要）進一步程序嘅 nature, timing and extent。15% benchmark 可以提，但要記住佢只係 indicative，仲要 professional judgement。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 600 (Revised) para. 9(m) (definition of significant component); paras. A5–A6 (15% benchmark); paras. 28–29 (non-significant components) — as cited in LP.

**Common Trap（繁中）:** (1) 將兩個 criteria 寫成必須同時符合；(2) 以為 15% 係硬性法定門檻；(3) 以為 non-significant = 唔使做任何嘢。三個都係典型失分位。

### Q5 — Answer: C

**Model Answer (EN):** The proposal is not acceptable. Sole responsibility for the auditor's opinion on the group financial statements rests with the group engagement partner (and the group auditor), including for components audited by other firms. The group auditor must not simply rely on the component auditors' opinions; the group engagement team must obtain an appropriate understanding of the work performed by component auditors and be satisfied that sufficient appropriate audit evidence has been obtained. Under HKSA 220 (Revised), the group engagement partner is responsible for the direction, supervision, performance and review of the work and for the adequacy of the audit documentation supporting the opinion on the consolidated financial statements.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題考 group audit 嘅核心原則：**冇得 divided responsibility**。Group engagement partner 對成個 group opinion 負全責，包括嗰三間由無關連 component auditors 審嘅子公司。Distractors：A 錯 — 披露 component auditors 嘅名唔會轉移責任；B 錯 — 責任冇得因 component 細而豁免，insignificant components 都要做 group-level analytical procedures；D 錯 — audit committee 同意都改變唔到 HKSA 600 (Revised) 嘅要求。考試伏位：「披露就冇事」係最常見嘅錯誤直覺 — 記住 group auditor 唔可以靠 disclose 嚟卸責。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 600 (Revised) (sole responsibility of group auditor; LP 10.2.3 and Summary); HKSA 220 (Revised) Quality Management for an Audit of Financial Statements (group engagement partner's responsibilities, LP 10.3.1).

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 學生會將「referencing another auditor's report」當成可以接受嘅做法（某些司法管轄區歷史上容許 division of responsibility）。喺 HKSA 600 (Revised) 下：唔得 — sole responsibility 係絕對嘅，所以 group auditor 先要咁多工夫去評估同參與 component auditor 嘅工作。
