# M8 Chapter 10: Group Audits — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. Explain the concept of a group audit and the objectives of the auditor under HKSA 600 (Revised), including the involvement of component auditors.
2. Distinguish significant components from non-significant components and determine the type of audit work required for each.
3. Describe the characteristics and responsibilities of component auditors and how the group auditor evaluates and uses their work.
4. Explain the responsibilities of the group engagement partner and the required communications between the group engagement team and component auditors.

### 必背考點

#### A. Objectives & Overall Responsibility（點解要有 group audit framework）
- Reference standard: **HKSA 600 (Revised) Special Considerations – Audits of Group Financial Statements (Including the Work of Component Auditors)**.
- Auditor's objectives in a group audit:
  1. Determine **whether they can act as the auditor of the group financial statements**; and
  2. If so: (a) **communicate clearly with component auditors** about the scope and timing of their work and their findings; (b) **obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence** regarding the financial information of the components and the **consolidation process** to express an opinion on the group financial statements.
- **Ultimate responsibility for the opinion on the consolidated financial statements rests solely with the group engagement partner / group auditor** — even for components audited by other firms. The group auditor **cannot simply rely on the component auditor's opinion** that the component's financial statements are free from material misstatement; an appropriate level of understanding of the component auditor's work is required.
- Per **HKSA 220 (Revised) Quality Management for an Audit of Financial Statements**, the group engagement partner is responsible for the **direction, supervision, performance and review** of the work, the adequacy of audit documentation, and being satisfied that sufficient appropriate audit evidence has been obtained.

#### B. Significant vs Non-significant Components（分類 → 決定做咩工作）
- Determination of significant components is the **responsibility of the group auditor** (HKSA 600.26–27). A component is significant if it is:
  1. Of **individual financial significance** to the group (driver = **SIZE**); **or**
  2. Likely to include **significant risks of material misstatement** of the group financial statements due to its **specific nature or circumstances** (driver = **RISK**) — HKSA 600.9(m).
- **15% benchmark**: HKSA 600 (Revised) indicates 15% of a chosen benchmark (e.g. group assets, profit, revenue) *might* be chosen as indicative of financial significance — professional judgement still required; higher/lower percentages may be appropriate; **document the appropriateness** if 15% is applied (HKSA 600(A5), 600(A6)). LP also states financially significant components are *prima facie* those comprising more than 15% of sales, net income, assets, liabilities or cash flows.
- Risk indicators (non-financial significance): estimates associated with **impairments**, **inventory impairments**, **taxation provisions**; complex areas such as **financial instruments**, **contingencies**, **subsequent events**.

#### C. Audit Procedures — Work Required per Component Category
| Component category | Required work (HKSA 600 (Revised)) |
|---|---|
| **Financially significant** (size) | **Full audit** of the component's financial information using **component materiality** (performed by group engagement team or component auditor) (HKSA 600.26–27) |
| **Significant due to risk** | One or more of: (1) audit of the component's financial information using component materiality; (2) audit of one or more **account balances, classes of transactions or disclosures** relating to the likely significant risks; (3) **specified audit procedures** relating to the likely significant risks |
| **Non-significant** | **Analytical procedures at group level** to corroborate the conclusion that there are no significant risks; document the belief if no additional risks identified; if analytical procedures indicate possible risk of material misstatement → document the **nature, timing and extent** of further procedures to address the identified risks (HKSA 600.28–29) |

#### D. Evidence — Understanding & Evaluating Component Auditors
- Before using the work of a component auditor, the group auditor must obtain an understanding of (HKSA 600.19–20):
  1. Whether the component auditor will comply with **ethical and independence standards** (HKICPA Code of Ethics for Professional Accountants) — even if based outside Hong Kong;
  2. The **professional competence** of the component auditor (incl. specialist skills, understanding of HKFRSs, and the auditing/quality management standards under which they operate);
  3. Whether the group auditor **will be able to be involved** in the component auditor's work as necessary to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence;
  4. Whether the component auditor operates in a **regulatory environment** that actively oversees auditors.
  - The assessment must be **formally documented**; if the group engagement partner has concerns over (1)–(3), the group engagement team should **perform the audit of the components themselves**.
- Component materiality: set by the **group engagement team**; **cannot exceed (must be lower than) group materiality**; communicated to the component auditor.
- Evaluating the component auditor's work (HKSA 600.42–45): obtain consent to **be involved** and to **access information**; request confirmation on **unrestricted access to / copies of working papers** (or reasons if legally impossible); consider **visits** for significant components (triggers: working papers cannot leave jurisdiction, size/specific risks, doubt about diligence, change of auditors, recent acquisition/expected disposal, internal audit findings, prior-period issues, audit adjustments noted, changes in local management/scope).
- Working paper review by group auditor covers: component audit planning memorandum, significant risks & fraud responses, detailed work programmes, specialists, materiality thresholds applied, tests of control (incl. group-wide controls in group instructions), substantive procedures, judgements/estimates & management bias, related parties, litigation, contingencies, going concern, consolidation package, roll-forward procedures, management representation letter, final analytical procedures, auditor's opinion consistency.

#### E. Communication（group ↔ component 雙向溝通）
- Group engagement team → component auditor (group audit instructions): work to be performed; form and content of communications back; confirmation of cooperation; **ethical/independence requirements** (group and component); **component materiality**; **identified significant risks** of material misstatement of the group financial statements (fraud or error); related parties; non-compliance with laws and regulations; material weaknesses in internal control; relevant accounting, auditing and reporting requirements; communications with TCWG.
- Component auditor → group auditor (summary memorandum / group audit questionnaire, HKSA 600.41): confirmation of compliance with **HKICPA Code of Ethics** and with group requirements; **scope of work** and any variations from instructions (communicated *prior* to variation); **fraud / non-compliance with laws and regulations** (fraud → communicate **immediately**) and indicators of **management bias**; significant risks and responses; **KAMs** (listed parent); **corrected and uncorrected misstatements**; **significant deficiencies in internal control** (report at point of discovery); related-party transactions; subsequent events procedures; items for the **group letter of representation**; matters for group TCWG; contingencies and commitments; overall findings/conclusions/**opinion**.
- Potential **modification of the component auditor's opinion** must be raised with the group auditor **as soon as possible**; the group auditor determines whether it is reflected at the consolidated level.
- If the component auditor cannot meet its responsibilities, the group auditor may need to **replace** the component auditor.

### 易混淆概念對比

**表 1 — Component 分類與所需工作（最常考伏位）**

| | Financially significant component | Significant component (risk-based) | Non-significant component |
|---|---|---|---|
| Driver | Size（e.g. >15% of a group benchmark） | Specific nature/circumstances → significant risk of material misstatement | Immaterial to group as a whole; low risk profile |
| Who decides | Group auditor（專業判斷＋文件記錄） | Group auditor | Group auditor |
| Required work | **Full audit** using component materiality | Full audit / audit of specific balances / specified procedures（三選一或以上） | **Group-level analytical procedures** only；如有風險跡象先再做進一步程序 |
| 點樣分辨／考試伏位 | 見到 「X% of group assets/revenue」 諗 size driver — 必做 full audit，唔係淨做 review | 金額細都可以係 significant（e.g. 有 complex financial instruments、impairment estimates）— 唔好以為細就唔使做嘢 | 伏位：「non-significant = 唔使做任何程序」係錯 — LP 明確要求 analytical procedures at group level 並 document 結論 |

**表 2 — Group Engagement Partner vs Component Auditor（責任邊界）**

| | Group engagement partner / team | Component auditor |
|---|---|---|
| Opinion responsibility | **Sole responsibility** for the opinion on the group financial statements — 不可 divided responsibility | Reports to group auditor in the form agreed in group audit instructions；may have separate local statutory reporting responsibilities |
| Materiality | **Determines** group materiality and component materiality（component ≤ group，實務上較低） | Applies the materiality instructed；component materiality 唔係佢自己定 |
| Engagement letter | Issues **group** engagement letter to group management/TCWG | Issues engagement letter to **component** management（如有 local engagement） |
| Work evaluation | Evaluates component auditor's competence, independence, work（review working papers / visits）| Follows group instructions；maintains open communication；flags issues（fraud、opinion modification）**immediately/as soon as possible** |
| 點樣分辨／考試伏位 | 伏位：以為可以喺 group auditor's report 度話「某啲子公司係其他核數師審嘅」嚟卸責 — LP 明言 group auditor has sole responsibility，唔可以 simply rely on component auditor's opinion | 伏位：component auditor 發現 fraud 或者要 modify opinion，唔可以等到出 report 先講 — 要即時/盡快通知 group auditor |

## Phase 2 閃卡（8 cards）

### Card 1
**Front:** What are the objectives of the auditor in a group audit under HKSA 600 (Revised)?

**Back:**
- Determine whether they can act as the auditor of the group financial statements;
- If acting: communicate clearly with component auditors about the scope and timing of their work and their findings;
- Obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence regarding the financial information of the components and the consolidation process to express an opinion on the group financial statements.

💬 白話解：第一步問「我做得唔做得 group auditor」；做得就要同 component auditors 講清楚做咩、幾時做，同埋攞夠 evidence（包括 consolidation process），先可以出 group opinion。

### Card 2
**Front:** How is a "significant component" defined under HKSA 600 (Revised), and what benchmark is commonly used?

**Back:**
- A component is significant if it is (1) of individual financial significance to the group, OR (2) likely to include significant risks of material misstatement of the group financial statements due to its specific nature or circumstances (HKSA 600.9(m));
- 15% of a chosen benchmark (e.g. group assets or profit) may be used as indicative of financial significance — but professional judgement is required and the appropriateness should be documented (HKSA 600(A6)).

💬 白話解：「大」或者「高危」其中一樣就係 significant component。15% 只係參考門檻，唔係硬性規定，用咗要寫低點解合理。

### Card 3
**Front:** What audit work is required for (a) a financially significant component, (b) a component significant due to risk, and (c) a non-significant component?

**Back:**
- (a) Full audit of the component's financial information using component materiality;
- (b) One or more of: full audit using component materiality; audit of specific account balances/classes of transactions/disclosures relating to the significant risks; specified audit procedures relating to the significant risks;
- (c) Analytical procedures at group level; if these indicate possible risk of material misstatement, document the nature, timing and extent of further procedures.

💬 白話解：記口訣「Size → full audit；Risk → full/partial/specified 三揀；細粒 → group-level analytical procedures」。Non-significant ≠ 唔做嘢。

### Card 4
**Front:** What four matters must the group auditor understand about a component auditor before using their work?

**Back:** (HKSA 600.19–20)
- Compliance with ethical and independence requirements (HKICPA Code of Ethics), even if outside Hong Kong;
- Professional competence (incl. specialist skills and sufficient understanding of HKFRSs);
- Whether the group auditor can be involved in the component auditor's work as necessary;
- Whether the component auditor operates in a regulatory environment that actively oversees auditors.

💬 白話解：四樣嘢 — 操守、能力、俾唔俾我參與、佢嗰邊有冇人監管。Assessment 要正式 document；有疑慮就自己做返嗰個 component。

### Card 5
**Front:** Who sets component materiality, and what is the rule about its level relative to group materiality?

**Back:**
- Component materiality is determined by the **group engagement team**, not the component auditor;
- It must be **lower than (cannot exceed) group materiality**;
- It must be communicated to the component auditor in the group audit instructions.

💬 白話解：Component materiality 係 group auditor 定嘅，而且一定要低過 group materiality — 咁先可以捕獲到喺 component 層面細、但加埋會影響 group 嘅 misstatements。

### Card 6
**Front:** List at least five matters a component auditor normally reports to the group auditor in the summary memorandum / group audit questionnaire.

**Back:** (HKSA 600.41 — any five)
- Compliance with the HKICPA Code of Ethics and with the group auditor's requirements;
- Scope of work performed, including variations from group instructions;
- Fraud or non-compliance with laws and regulations (fraud communicated immediately); indicators of management bias;
- Significant risks affecting the consolidated financial statements and responses;
- Corrected and uncorrected misstatements;
- Significant deficiencies in internal control (reported at point of discovery);
- Related-party transactions; subsequent events procedures; items for the group letter of representation; KAMs (listed parent); overall findings/conclusions/opinion.

💬 白話解：記得三個「即刻講」：fraud → immediately；internal control deficiencies → at point of discovery；可能 modify opinion → as soon as possible。其餘就喺 memorandum/questionnaire 度confirm。

### Card 7
**Front:** Can the group engagement partner divide responsibility for the group audit opinion with component auditors?

**Back:**
- No. Sole responsibility for the opinion on the group financial statements rests with the group engagement partner, including components audited by others;
- The group auditor must not simply rely on the component auditor's opinion — an appropriate level of understanding of the work undertaken is required;
- Per HKSA 220 (Revised), the partner is responsible for direction, supervision, performance and review, and for being satisfied that sufficient appropriate audit evidence has been obtained.

💬 白話解：考試常見伏位 — group auditor 唔可以喺 report 度寫「子公司係人哋審」嚟卸責。個 opinion 係你一個人孭，所以你要了解同評估 component auditor 做咗啲咩。

### Card 8
**Front:** What factors should the group auditor consider in deciding whether to visit a component auditor?

**Back:**
- Working papers cannot be moved out of the jurisdiction;
- Size or specific risks of the component;
- Doubt about the diligence/capability of the component auditor (face-to-face discussion needed);
- Change of group or component auditors; recent acquisition or expected disposal of the component;
- Work/conclusions of internal auditors; prior-period issues; audit adjustments noted by the component auditor;
- Changes in local management or the size and scope of the component.
- Better practice: experienced team member (partner/manager) visits; clear purpose; visit **before** the component auditor's close-out meeting with component management.

💬 白話解：Visit 嘅觸發點都係圍繞「風險高咗」或者「信任度低咗」。時間上記住：要喺 component close-out meeting 之前去，先嚟得切將發現嘅問題放入去。
