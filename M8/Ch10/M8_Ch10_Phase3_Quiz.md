# M8 Chapter 10: Group Audits — Phase 3 Quiz

**Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as `1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. …` — 超時如實申報**

**Q1.** Meridian Logistics Holdings Limited ("Meridian") is a Hong Kong-listed group with a 31 December year-end. Its group engagement team has adopted 15% of group total assets as the benchmark indicative of financial significance. One component, Pearl Delta Warehousing Limited, contributes 22% of the group's total assets and 19% of group revenue; its operations are routine and no specific risks of material misstatement have been identified. Under HKSA 600 (Revised), what type of work must be performed on Pearl Delta Warehousing Limited?

A. Analytical procedures performed at group level only
B. An audit of the component's financial information using component materiality (a full audit)
C. Specified audit procedures relating to likely significant risks of material misstatement only
D. A review of the component's consolidation package with no further audit work

**Q2.** Which of the following statements about component materiality is correct under HKSA 600 (Revised)?

A. Component materiality is determined by the component auditor, who is closest to the component's operations
B. Component materiality may exceed group materiality where the component is audited to local statutory requirements
C. Component materiality is determined by the group engagement team and must be lower than group materiality
D. Component materiality need not be communicated to the component auditor, to preserve audit unpredictability

**Q3.** Under HKSA 600 (Revised), which of the following is **NOT** a matter about which the group auditor is required to obtain an understanding before using the work of a component auditor?

A. Whether the component auditor will comply with ethical requirements, particularly independence, relevant to the group audit
B. The professional competence of the component auditor
C. The profitability and fee income of the component auditor's firm
D. Whether the component auditor operates in a regulatory environment that actively oversees auditors

**Q4.** State the **TWO** criteria under which a component is identified as a significant component of a group under HKSA 600 (Revised), and state the audit work required for components that are **non-significant**.

**Q5.** Harbour Crane Group Limited has ten subsidiaries, three of which are audited by component auditors unrelated to the group auditor, Pine & Co. In drafting the auditor's report on the consolidated financial statements, the group engagement partner proposes to include a statement that Pine & Co's opinion is based in part on the reports of the other auditors, so that responsibility for those components is shared with the component auditors. Which of the following best describes the position under HKSA 600 (Revised)?

A. The proposal is acceptable provided the component auditors' names are disclosed in the auditor's report
B. The proposal is acceptable only for components that are not financially significant to the group
C. The proposal is not acceptable — the group engagement partner takes sole responsibility for the opinion on the group financial statements and cannot divide that responsibility with component auditors
D. The proposal is acceptable if the group's audit committee gives written consent to the division of responsibility
