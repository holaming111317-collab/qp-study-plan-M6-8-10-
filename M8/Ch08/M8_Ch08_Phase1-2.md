# M8 Chapter 8: Major Actions During the Audit Completion — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. Illustrate the major actions performed during the completion phase of an audit, including the final overall review of financial statements and final analytical procedures under HKSA 520.
2. Explain the going concern assumption, the auditor's objectives and requirements under HKSA 570 (Revised), and the implications for the auditor's report.
3. Illustrate the review of contingent liabilities, commitments and subsequent events, distinguishing adjusting from non-adjusting events under HKSA 560 and HKAS 10.
4. Evaluate misstatements under HKSA 450, including the concept of "clearly trivial" and the effect of uncorrected misstatements on the auditor's opinion.
5. Describe the purpose, content and dating of the written representation letter under HKSA 580 and the matters communicated to those charged with governance under HKSA 260 (Revised).

### 必背考點

#### A. Evidence（完成階段嘅證據評估）
- **Sufficient appropriate audit evidence**（HKSA 500 Audit Evidence）：
  - **Audit evidence**（para. 5(c)）：information used by the auditor in arriving at the conclusions on which the auditor's opinion is based — 包括 accounting records 同 other sources。
  - **Sufficiency**（para. 5(f)）：the measure of the **quantity** of audit evidence；受 risk of material misstatement 評估同 evidence quality 影響。
  - **Appropriateness**（para. 5(b)）：the measure of the **quality** of audit evidence — 即 **relevance and reliability**。
- 證據來源：**external evidence**（confirmations、expert reports、analysts' reports、benchmarking data）一般比 internal evidence 更可靠；internal evidence 靠 data 之間嘅 corroboration。
- 完成階段評估證據時要確認：工作符合 HKSAs 同香港 legal/regulatory requirements；planning 階段識別嘅 risks 已被 address；procedures 結果能 support opinion；significant matters 已處理同 documented；consultations 已完成同落實。
- **HKSA 220 (Revised) Quality Management for an Audit of Financial Statements**：engagement partner 負責 direction、supervision 同 review engagement team 嘅工作，確保取得 sufficient appropriate audit evidence。（LP 指明 HKSA 220 (Revised) 於 April 2021 發出、15 December 2022 生效，屬 examinable document。）

#### B. Ethics（完成階段嘅道德要求）
- **Code of Ethics (COE) Section 300** — 五類 threats（對 engagement quality reviewer 同其他 appropriate reviewers 同樣適用）：
  1. **Self-interest**（直接財務利益、lowballing fee、利用 confidential information 圖利）
  2. **Self-review**（review 自己之前做嘅工作，例如曾做 engagement partner 再做 EQR）
  3. **Advocacy**（代表 client 打官司、lobbying）
  4. **Familiarity**（family member 係 client director、long association、兩位 partner 互相做對方嘅 EQR）
  5. **Intimidation**（被威脅解僱、被逼同意 inappropriate accounting treatment、收禮後被要脅）
- Safeguards（COE para. 300.8 A2）：加派人手時間、由非團隊成員做 appropriate reviewer、用不同 partners/teams 處理 non-assurance services、involve another firm、披露 referral fees/commissions。
- Threat 唔可以 mitigate 到 acceptably low level → engagement quality review 必須由另一人執行。
- 完成階段 deadline 逼近時唔准走捷徑：professional competence、due care、professional behaviour（COE Section 110, subsections 113 and 115）。
- Management/TCWG 施壓唔改 opinion = **intimidation threat**；auditor 必須 stand its ground（integrity、objectivity — COE Section 110, subsections 111–115）。

#### C. Procedures（完成階段嘅核心程序）
**1. Final overall review of financial statements**
- 將 draft financial statements cross-reference 返去 audit file（先 aggregated 再 disaggregated totals）。
- 檢查 disclosures 嘅 completeness 同 accuracy，確保符合 HKFRSs 同 **Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)**；通常用 disclosure checklist。
- Quantitative disclosures：segment reporting、related-party transaction amounts、associates/JV summarised financial information；Qualitative disclosures：significant accounting policies、critical accounting estimates、going concern basis、contingent liability circumstances。
- 留意 management 提供 excessive disclosure 會模糊重點。
- **Final analytical procedures（HKSA 520 Analytical Procedures, para. 6）**：near the end of the audit，協助形成 overall conclusion — financial statements as a whole 是否與 auditor 嘅 understanding 一致。如發現之前未識別嘅 risks of material misstatement，必須 revise risk assessment 同 modify planned audit responses。

**2. Going concern（HKSA 570 (Revised) Going Concern）**
- Management 嘅責任：**HKAS 1 Presentation of Financial Statements**（LP margin: HKAS 1.25–26）要求 management assess going concern；CO (Cap. 622) 喺若干情況下要求 directors 作 solvency statement。呢個 assessment **唔係** 保證 entity 未來 12 個月存續。
- Auditor 嘅三大 objectives（HKSA 570 (Revised)）：取得關於 going concern basis 是否 appropriate 嘅 sufficient appropriate evidence；conclude 是否存在 **material uncertainty**；按 HKSA 570 (Revised) 形成 opinion。
- **四個 key requirements**（HKSA 570.9–16）：
  1. Risk assessment procedures（連結 HKSA 315 (Revised 2019) 同 HKSA 330）
  2. Evaluating management's assessment（management 同 auditor 嘅 assessment 都要涵蓋 **at least 12 months from the date of the financial statements**；budgets/forecasts、creditor 書面承諾 12 個月內唔追數、related parties support、進一步融資能力；auditor 要取 going concern written representation）
  3. Period beyond management's assessment（HKSA 570.12–14 — inquire of management）
  4. Additional audit procedures（如有 events/conditions cast significant doubt）
- **Warning signs**：Financial（net current liabilities、total liabilities > total assets、negative operating cash flows、operating losses、COD terms、unusual offshore financing、pending litigation、loan covenant breaches）；Operational（long lead times on asset sales、significant debt due and payable、creditor days 大幅變動、supply chain issues、loss of major customers）；Other（economic/environmental disasters、changes in laws/regulation、non-insurable events）。

**3. Contingent liabilities and commitments**
- 適用 standards：**HKSA 540 (Revised)**（contingent liabilities 多數涉及 accounting estimate）、**HKAS 37 Provisions, Contingent Liabilities and Contingent Assets**、commitments 通常基於 contractual obligations。
- 主要 assertions：valuation（estimates reasonable per HKFRS）+ **completeness**（所有 material contingent liabilities 已識別、計量、披露）；commitments 程序目標係 **completeness and accuracy**。
- Contingent liabilities 程序：
  - 按 **HKSA 505 External Confirmations** 向 legal counsel 同 banks 發 confirmations（pending litigation list + progress、warranties/guarantees claims、bank guarantees）
  - Examine Board minutes（有冇批准 guarantees）
  - Review environmental reviews 同 industry practices（如 mining 'make good' clauses）
  - Review product warranty arrangements
- Commitments 程序：確認 leasing commitments 按 **HKFRS 16 Leases** 入賬；search 已簽約嘅 future capital expenditure 同 licensing commitments。

**4. Subsequent events（HKSA 560 Subsequent Events；HKAS 10 Events After the Reporting Period）**
- **Adjusting events**：provide further evidence of conditions that **existed at the end of the financial period** → 要調整 financial statements。
- **Non-adjusting events**：conditions that **arose after** period end → 唔調整，但 material 嘅話要 note disclosure。（香港上市公司最常見披露：post-period-end dividend declaration。）
- 三個時段嘅責任：
  1. **Date of FS → date of auditor's report**：主動責任（"active duty"）— 必須做 procedures，盡量接近 report date：understand management 點識別 events、enquiry of management/Board、read Board/management minutes、review post-period-end management accounts、跟進 legal counsel（regulators 期望 follow-up 喺 opinion date 前最多 7 日內做）（HKSA 560.6–8）。
  2. **After auditor's report date → before FS issued**：被動責任（"passive duty"）— **no obligation** 做 procedures；但若得知會改變 opinion 嘅事實 → 討論是否 amend FS；management 修改 → 做 procedures + 出新 report + **EoM or OM paragraph**；management 拒絕 → modify opinion per **HKSA 705 (Revised)**；如已用 original report 發出 → take action to prevent reliance，必要時 seek legal advice（HKSA 560.10–13）。
  3. **After FS issued**：同上處理；視乎時間可能喺下年度 FS 或（listed）interim FS 修正（HKSA 560.14–17）。

**5. Evaluation of misstatements（HKSA 450 Evaluation of Misstatements Identified during the Audit）**
- **Misstatement 定義**（para. 3）：reported amount / classification / presentation / disclosure 與 applicable financial reporting framework 要求之間嘅差異；可源自 error or fraud。
- **Accumulate 所有 misstatements，except those that are clearly trivial**（para. 5）。**"Clearly trivial" ≠ "not material"**（para. A2）— clearly trivial 係 wholly different (smaller) order of magnitude，個別或合計都 clearly of no consequence；有疑問就當唔係 clearly trivial。
- 三類 unrecorded misstatements：
  - **Factual** — 無爭議，金額/披露明確錯誤。
  - **Projected** — 由 sample 發現嘅 misstatements 推算落成個 population。
  - **Judgemental** — auditor 唔同意 management 嘅 accounting estimates/policies judgements（例如 provisions、percentage of completion）。
- Prior-year uncorrected misstatements 要 **carried forward**；如果累積效應 + 本年 misstatements 令本期 FS materially misstated → 要求調整。
- Qualitative factors 可令低於 materiality 嘅 misstatements 變成 material（HKSA 450.10–11 相關討論）：影響 regulatory compliance、debt covenants、隱藏 earnings trends 轉變、影響已公佈 forecasts、影響 ratios、classification errors。
- Uncorrected misstatements 個別或合計 material → 盡快通知 management/TCWG 並 **request correction**；唔改 → 一般會 modified opinion（詳見 Chapter 9）。

**6. Written representations（HKSA 580 Written Representations）**
- 又名 management representation letter / rep. letter。
- 三大 objectives（HKSA 580.6）：取得 management（及適當時 TCWG）確認已履行 FS 編製責任同提供資料完整性嘅 written representations；用以 support 其他 audit evidence（或被其他 HKSAs 要求時）；對有冇提供 representations 作適當回應。
- Written representations **係 necessary audit evidence，但 internally generated，單獨唔構成 sufficient appropriate audit evidence**，亦唔可以代替其他 procedures，唔影響 auditor 獲取其他 evidence 嘅 nature。
- Dating：**as close as possible to, but NOT after, the date of the auditor's report**；實務上通常與 auditor's report 同一日簽；要涵蓋 report 提及嘅所有 periods；auditor 唔應同意 management 改動 letter。
- 常見內容：management 承認按 HKFRS 編製 FS 嘅責任、books/records/minutes 完整提供、無 unrecorded transactions、internal control 設計責任、liens/encumbrances 已披露、estimates 假設合理、related parties 已披露、subsequent events 已調整/披露、uncorrected misstatements 個別及合計 immaterial（清單附於 letter）。
- CO (Cap. 622) 公司額外 representations：**s.380**（true and fair view）、妥善 accounting records、**s.383** + **Cap. 622G**（directors' emoluments notes）、**ss.390、543(2)、Schedule 5、s.452(3)**（directors' report）。
- 其他 HKSAs 嘅 written representation 要求（HKSA 580 Appendix 1）：
  - HKSA 240 para. 40（fraud：internal control 責任、fraud risk assessment 結果、已知/懷疑 fraud 已披露）
  - HKSA 250 (Revised) para. 17（non-compliance with laws and regulations 已披露）
  - HKSA 450 para. 14（uncorrected misstatements immaterial + 附清單）
  - HKSA 501 para. 12（litigation and claims 已披露及按 framework 處理）
  - HKSA 540 (Revised) para. 37（estimates 嘅 methods、significant assumptions、data 係 appropriate）
  - HKSA 550 para. 26（related parties 身份同交易已披露及入賬）
  - HKSA 560 para. 9（所有需調整/披露嘅 subsequent events 已處理）
  - HKSA 570 (Revised) para. 16(e)（management plans for future actions 及 feasibility）
  - HKSA 710 para. 9（all periods + prior-period restatement）
  - HKSA 720 (Revised) para. 13(c)（final version of other information documents 會提供）

**7. Communication with those charged with governance（HKSA 260 (Revised)；HKSA 265 para. 9）**
- TCWG = persons entrusted with the **supervision, control and direction** of an entity；溝通對象係最高層（executive/non-executive directors、Audit Committee），可在 engagement letter 釐清。
- 目的：**effective two-way communication** — 交代 auditor 責任、planned scope/timing；從 TCWG 取得 audit 相關資料；及時提供 significant observations（包括 fraud）。
- 常見溝通事項（HKSA 260.14–17）：audit scope/approach 及 limitations、重大會計政策及變更、listed companies 嘅 **key audit matters**、重大 audit adjustments、威脅 going concern 嘅事項、與 management 嘅 disagreements、預期嘅 report modifications、internal control material weaknesses、independence threats 及 safeguards、business risks、written representations 內容、改善 internal control 嘅 recommendations。
- Documentation/timing（HKSA 260.23 一帶）：要 **timely**、喺 management 批准 FS 前溝通；**即使冇事項都要發出 communication**（聲明無 significant findings）；可以口頭，但必須喺 working papers 書面記錄；屬 two-way dialogue，management 有權回應。

**8. Related parties（HKSA 550 Related Parties；披露框架 HKAS 24 (Revised)）**
- Related party 定義（HKSA 550.10）：(a) framework 定義嘅 related party；或 (b) 對 reporting entity 有 control/significant influence 嘅 person/entity、被 entity control/significantly influence 嘅 entity、common control 下嘅 entity（common controlling ownership、owners 係 close family、common key management）。State 共同控制嘅 entities **唔係** related parties，除非有重大交易/資源共享。
- **Related party transaction = transfer of resources, services or obligations between a reporting entity and a related party, regardless of whether a price is charged.**
- Close family members：children、spouse/domestic partner、spouse 嘅 children、dependents。
- 考試重點：auditors 常犯錯誤係只當 related parties 做 disclosure issue，而冇回應 fraud/error 導致嘅 risk of material misstatement。

#### D. Assertions（本章程序對應嘅 assertions）
- Contingent liabilities：**completeness**（所有 material items 已識別）+ valuation（estimates reasonable per HKFRS）。
- Commitments：**completeness and accuracy**。
- FS disclosures：completeness and accuracy（HKFRS + Cap. 622 compliance）。

#### E. Report modifications（完成階段嘅報告影響；詳情見 Chapter 9）
- Going concern（HKSA 570 (Revised)；LP margin: 570.17–18, 570.21–23）：
  | 情況 | 報告結果 |
  |---|---|
  | GC basis appropriate + material uncertainty exists + **adequate disclosure** | **Unmodified opinion** + separate section "**Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern**"（opinion 唔因呢件事 modified） |
  | GC basis appropriate + material uncertainty exists + **inadequate disclosure** | **Qualified or adverse opinion** per **HKSA 705 (Revised)**；Basis for Qualified (Adverse) Opinion 要講明 material uncertainty 未充份披露 |
  | Management 用 GC basis 但 auditor 認為 **inappropriate** | **Adverse opinion** |
  | Events/conditions 存在但 **no material uncertainty** | 評估 disclosure 是否 adequate（按 framework） |
  | Management 唔願意 make/extend assessment | Consider implications for the auditor's report |
- Uncorrected misstatements material 且 management 唔更正 → generally a form of **modified opinion**。
- Report date 後、FS 發出前 FS 被修改 → 新 report + **EoM/OM paragraph** 提示用家。

### 易混淆概念對比

**對比 1：Adjusting events vs Non-adjusting events（HKAS 10 / HKSA 560）**

| 項目 | Adjusting events | Non-adjusting events |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | Provide further evidence of conditions that **existed at** the end of the financial period | Evidence of conditions that **arose after** the end of the financial period |
| Treatment | **Adjust** the financial statements | **No adjustment**; disclose in notes if material |
| 例子（LP 情境） | Board 喺年結前已討論關廠並完成 impairment/provision 評估，年結後先正式拍板 → 要調整 | 年結後先有買家簽 deed of intent 買廠 → note disclosure；HK 上市公司最常見：post-year-end dividend declared |
| 點樣分辨 | 問自己：「呢個 **condition** 喺 balance sheet date 存唔存在？」存在 → adjusting | Condition 係年結後先出現 → non-adjusting，但 material 就必須披露 |
| 考試伏位 | 唔好睇「decision 幾時做」，要睇「condition 幾時存在」——年結後先決定唔代表係 non-adjusting | Dividend declared after year-end 係典型 non-adjusting，唔好誤當 adjusting 入賬 |

**對比 2：Contingent liability vs Commitment**

| 項目 | Contingent liability | Commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | An **existing liability** (actual or asserted) for which the recognition criteria **cannot yet be met** — confirmation depends on an uncertain future event | An agreement **equally and proportionately unperformed** by both parties; relates to a future transaction (e.g. capex, JV infrastructure) |
| 入賬/披露 | Disclosed in notes until outflow becomes **probable** and amount can be reliably estimated（HKAS 37） | Until one party performs, 唔符合 liability 定義；按披露要求（如 HKFRS 16 leases、capital commitments）處理 |
| Audit 性質 | 多數涉及 accounting estimate → **HKSA 540 (Revised)** 適用；較難 conclude | 基於 contractual obligations，較 straightforward |
| 主要程序 | HKSA 505 external confirmations to legal counsel/banks；Board minutes；environmental reviews；warranty arrangements | 確認 HKFRS 16 處理；search contracted future capex 及 licensing commitments |
| 考試伏位 | 「Straightforward / contractual」嘅描述其實係講 commitment，唔係 contingent liability；contingent liabilities 亦唔係「in all cases an accounting estimate」 | 唔好將兩者 disclosures 混為一談；commitments 目標 assertion 係 completeness and accuracy |

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** Under HKSA 500 Audit Evidence, define "sufficiency" and "appropriateness" of audit evidence (with paragraph references).

**Back:**
- Sufficiency (para. 5(f)): the measure of the **quantity** of audit evidence; affected by the auditor's assessment of the risks of material misstatement and by the quality of the evidence.
- Appropriateness (para. 5(b)): the measure of the **quality** of audit evidence — its **relevance and reliability** in supporting the conclusions on which the opinion is based.

💬 記法：Sufficiency = 數量（要幾多「水」）；Appropriateness = 質素（水乾唔乾淨，即 relevance + reliability）。兩者互相影響：質素高，需要嘅數量可以少啲。考試好鍾意將兩個定義調轉做 distractor。

### Card 2
**Front:** List the FOUR key requirements for the auditor in a going concern assessment under HKSA 570 (Revised).

**Back:**
1. Risk assessment procedures and related activities (linked to HKSA 315 (Revised 2019)).
2. Evaluating management's assessment — must cover **at least 12 months from the date of the financial statements**; auditor covers the same period.
3. Period beyond management's assessment — inquire of management about events/conditions beyond that period.
4. Additional audit procedures where events or conditions casting significant doubt are identified.

💬 記得「12 months 係由 date of the financial statements（reporting date）起計，唔係由 report date 起計」。Management 嘅 assessment 係佢哋嘅責任（HKAS 1），auditor 唔可以照單全收，一定要攞 sufficient appropriate evidence 去 evaluate。

### Card 3
**Front:** Distinguish adjusting from non-adjusting subsequent events and state the required treatment of each.

**Back:**
- **Adjusting events**: provide further evidence of conditions that existed at the end of the financial period → **adjust** the financial statements.
- **Non-adjusting events**: conditions that arose after the period end → **no adjustment**; if material, disclose in the notes.
- Key test: did the **condition** exist at the reporting date?

💬 白話：問「件事嘅根源喺年結日存唔存在？」存在 → 調數；唔存在 → 淨披露。最常見陷阱：個 decision 年結後先落，但 condition 年結前已存在 —— 仍然係 adjusting。

### Card 4
**Front:** Describe the auditor's responsibilities for subsequent events in each of the three time periods under HKSA 560.

**Back:**
1. **Date of FS → date of auditor's report** ("active duty"): perform procedures (enquiry, read minutes, review post-year-end management accounts, follow up legal counsel) as close to the report date as possible (HKSA 560.6–8).
2. **After report date → before FS issued** ("passive duty"): no obligation to perform procedures; if a fact that would have amended the opinion becomes known → discuss amendment; if FS amended → new report + EoM/OM paragraph; if management refuses → modify opinion per HKSA 705 (Revised) (HKSA 560.10–13).
3. **After FS issued**: same approach; correction may fall into the following year's FS or interim FS for listed entities (HKSA 560.14–17).

💬 記住分界線：auditor's report 一簽，主動責任就完。之後係「冇責任搵，但知道咗就要郁」。Regulators 期望 legal counsel follow-up 喺 opinion date 前最多 7 日做。

### Card 5
**Front:** Under HKSA 450, what does "clearly trivial" mean, and what are the three types of unrecorded misstatements?

**Back:**
- The auditor must accumulate all misstatements **except those that are clearly trivial** (para. 5). "Clearly trivial" is **not the same as "not material"** (para. A2): a wholly different (smaller) order of magnitude, clearly of no consequence individually or in aggregate. Any doubt → not clearly trivial.
- Three types: **Factual** (no doubt, amount/disclosure wrong); **Projected** (projection of sample misstatements to the population); **Judgemental** (auditor disagrees with management's estimates/policy judgements).

💬 「Clearly trivial < not material」係超高頻考點：低於 materiality 唔代表可以唔記錄，一定要達到 clearly trivial 先可以 skip。三類 misstatements 要識分 —— auditor 計返嚟嘅 impairment 差異通常係 judgemental；由 sample 推算嘅係 projected。

### Card 6
**Front:** State the dating requirement of the written representation letter and explain its status as audit evidence under HKSA 580.

**Back:**
- Dated **as near as practicable to, but not after, the date of the auditor's report**; in practice signed on the same date as the auditor's report; must cover **all periods** referred to in the report.
- Written representations are **necessary audit evidence** but, being internally generated, **do not on their own provide sufficient appropriate audit evidence**; they are **not a substitute** for other audit procedures, and do not change the nature of other evidence obtained.
- The auditor should not agree to management's proposed changes to the letter.

💬 三大考試伏位：(1) 日期唔可以遲過 report date；(2) rep letter 唔可以代替實質程序 —— 例如攞咗 impairment 嘅 representation 都仲要做 procedures；(3) management 想改 letter 內容 → 唔應同意。

### Card 7
**Front:** Summarise the auditor's report implications when a material uncertainty related to going concern exists (HKSA 570 (Revised)).

**Back:**
- GC basis appropriate + material uncertainty + **adequate disclosure** → **Unmodified opinion** + separate section "**Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern**" (opinion NOT modified on this matter).
- GC basis appropriate + material uncertainty + **inadequate disclosure** → **Qualified or adverse opinion** per HKSA 705 (Revised), with the matter stated in the Basis for Qualified (Adverse) Opinion section.
- Use of GC basis judged **inappropriate** → **Adverse opinion**.
- Management unwilling to make/extend its assessment → consider report implications.

💬 最容易考嘅決策樹：有冇 material uncertainty？Basis 啱唔啱？Disclosure 夠唔夠？記住 adequate disclosure 嘅情況 opinion 係 **unmodified**，只係加一個獨立 section —— 唔係 qualified、唔係 EoM paragraph。

### Card 8
**Front:** List the audit procedures normally performed to determine the completeness and accuracy of contingent liabilities, and name the standards engaged.

**Back:**
- External confirmation to **legal counsel and banks** per **HKSA 505** (pending litigation list/progress; claims such as warranties and guarantees with counsel's view on probability and HKD outcome; bank guarantees).
- Examine **minutes of the Board of Directors** (e.g. guarantees approved against loans).
- Examine **environmental reviews** and their likely outcomes.
- Consider **industry practices** (e.g. mining 'make good' restoration clauses).
- Review **product warranty arrangements**.
- Standards: HKSA 540 (Revised) (estimates), HKAS 37 (framework), HKSA 505 (confirmations).

💬 呢啲係「list-type」長問答常客。答題時記得每個 procedure 都要講埋「搵緊乜」：例如 legal confirmation 係攞 pending litigation 清單同 counsel 對 probability/金額嘅意見。

| Adjusting event | 年結日已存在嘅狀況，期後有新證據 → 要調整賬目 | The liquidation of a major customer whose financial difficulty existed at year-end is an adjusting event. |
| Non-adjusting event | 年結後先出現嘅新狀況 → 唔調賬，但可能要披露 | A dividend declared after the reporting period is a non-adjusting event disclosed in the notes if material. |
| Clearly trivial | 微到完全唔使理嘅錯誤，遠低於 materiality | Misstatements that are clearly trivial are not accumulated under HKSA 450 paragraph 5. |
| Uncorrected misstatements |  Auditor 搵到但 management 唔肯改嘅錯誤 | Material uncorrected misstatements must be communicated to management and those charged with governance, with a request for correction. |
| Written representation letter | 管理層親筆簽嘅確認信，係必要但唔足夠嘅證據 | The written representation letter is dated as near as practicable to, but not after, the date of the auditor's report. |
| Those charged with governance (TCWG) | 負責監督公司嘅人，通常係董事會同 audit committee | The auditor communicates significant findings, including any expected modification to the auditor's report, with those charged with governance on a timely basis. |
