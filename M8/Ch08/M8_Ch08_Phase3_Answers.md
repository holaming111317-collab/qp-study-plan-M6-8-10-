# M8 Chapter 8: Major Actions During the Audit Completion — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** Under HKSA 500 Audit Evidence, appropriateness is the measure of the **quality** of audit evidence — that is, its relevance and its reliability in providing support for the conclusions on which the auditor's opinion is based (para. 5(b)). Sufficiency is the measure of the **quantity** of audit evidence, and the quantity needed is affected by **both** the auditor's assessment of the risks of material misstatement **and** the quality of the audit evidence (para. 5(f)).

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題係 LP 最愛考嘅「調轉定義」陷阱。C 係 HKSA 500 para. 5(b) 嘅原文意思。A 錯：佢將 appropriateness 嘅定義（quality = relevance + reliability）擺咗喺 sufficiency 度。B 錯：同 A 相反，將 sufficiency 嘅 quantity 定義擺咗喺 appropriateness 度。D 錯得比較微妙：sufficiency 嘅確受 risk assessment 影響，但 LP 明確講 quantity **also** affected by the **quality** of the audit evidence —— 個 "only" 令佢變錯。記住口訣：sufficiency = 量，appropriateness = 質（relevance + reliability），兩者有互動關係。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 500 Audit Evidence, paras. 5(b) and 5(f)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 考試 MCQ 經常將 sufficiency 同 appropriateness 嘅定義互相對調做 distractor；仲有留意 "only" 呢類絕對字眼 —— D 嘅錯正正係忽略咗 quality 都會影響所需嘅 quantity。

### Q2 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** Reperforming the year-end bank reconciliation is a procedure directed at the audit of **cash/bank balances**, not at identifying or verifying contingent liabilities. The procedures normally undertaken for the completeness and accuracy of contingent liabilities are: external confirmations to legal counsel and banks under HKSA 505 External Confirmations (covering pending/imminent litigation, claims such as warranties and guarantees with counsel's view on probability and outcome, and bank guarantees); examination of Board of Directors' minutes; examination of environmental reviews; consideration of industry practices (e.g. 'make good' restoration clauses); and review of product warranty arrangements.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題係 negation stem（"NOT"），考你分唔分到 procedures 嘅 **目的/方向**。A、B、D 都係 LP 8.4.2 列出嘅 contingent liabilities 程序。C 聽落好合理（係真實審計程序），但佢係 **bank balances** 嘅 substantive procedure，同 contingent liabilities 嘅 completeness 完全冇邏輯關連 —— 呢個就係「logical connection between evidence and the assertions being audited」嘅概念。做 negation 題要逐個 option 問：「呢個 procedure 搵到嘅嘢，係咪關 contingent liabilities 事？」

**Rule Check:** HKSA 505 External Confirmations; HKSA 540 (Revised) Auditing Accounting Estimates and Related Disclosures; HKAS 37 Provisions, Contingent Liabilities and Contingent Assets（LP Ch. 8, section 8.4.2）

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 唔好見到「聽落專業」嘅程序就揀 —— distractor 往往係真實存在但放錯地方的程序。答题时紧扣题干的 assertion（completeness of contingent liabilities）。

### Q3 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** Under HKAS 10 Events After the Reporting Period and HKSA 560 Subsequent Events: (1) The customer's liquidation is an **adjusting event** — the severe liquidity problems existed since October 20X1, i.e. the condition existed at the end of the reporting period, and the post-year-end liquidation merely provides further evidence of that condition. The trade receivable (and any expected credit loss allowance) must be adjusted in the 31 December 20X1 financial statements. (2) The declaration of a special dividend is a **non-adjusting event** — the condition arose after the period end; dividends declared after the reporting period are the most common subsequent-event disclosure in Hong Kong and require **note disclosure if material**, not adjustment.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 判斷關鍵唔係「件事幾時發生」，而係「個 **condition** 喺 balance sheet date 存唔存在」。Customer 早喺 Oct 20X1 已有嚴重 liquidity problems → condition 存在於年結日 → adjusting，要調整 receivable/ECL。Dividend 係 Board 年結後先宣佈、唔依附任何年結前狀況 → non-adjusting，material 就 note disclosure。A 錯：漏咗 dividend 嘅披露責任。C 錯：兩個 event 嘅處理完全倒轉。D 錯：「發生喺年結後」唔等於「唔使理」，adjusting event 一樣係年結後發生。

**Rule Check:** HKAS 10 Events After the Reporting Period; HKSA 560 Subsequent Events (procedures per paras. 6–8)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 最大陷阱係用「decision/event date」取代「condition date」做判斷。Debtor 年結後破产清算係典型 adjusting event（because evidence of impairment existed at year-end）；dividend declared after year-end 係典型 non-adjusting event —— 兩個 pattern 都要背熟。

### Q4 — Answer: A
**Model Answer (EN):** Where the auditor concludes that management's use of the going concern basis is appropriate but a material uncertainty exists, and the financial statements **adequately disclose** the principal events or conditions and management's plans, the auditor expresses an **unmodified opinion** and includes a separate section under the heading "**Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern**", which draws attention to the disclosure note and states that these events or conditions indicate that a material uncertainty exists that may cast significant doubt on the entity's ability to continue as a going concern, and that **the auditor's opinion is not modified in respect of this matter**. Only if disclosure is inadequate would a qualified or adverse opinion under HKSA 705 (Revised) be appropriate.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題係 going concern 決策樹嘅核心分支：(1) GC basis appropriate？(2) material uncertainty 存唔存在？(3) disclosure 夠唔夠？三條都「是」→ **unmodified + MURGC section**。B 錯：qualified opinion 只喺 disclosure **inadequate** 時先用（per HKSA 705 (Revised)）。C 錯：adverse opinion 有兩種情況 —— 用 GC basis 本身 inappropriate，或者 inadequate disclosure 嚴重到 pervasive；淨係「material uncertainty 存在」唔會直接變 adverse。D 錯：adequate disclosure 唔代表可以唔提 —— MURGC section 係 mandatory。記住 MURGC section 唔係 EoM paragraph，而且 opinion 明確「not modified in respect of this matter」。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 570 (Revised) Going Concern (reporting conclusions; LP cites HKSA 570.17–18 and 570.21–23); HKSA 705 (Revised) for inadequate disclosure

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 學生最常犯兩個錯：(1) 一見 going concern 問題就以為要 modify opinion —— 其實 adequate disclosure + appropriate basis = unmodified；(2) 將 MURGC section 當成 Emphasis of Matter paragraph —— 佢係一個 **separate section with a prescribed heading**，唔係 EoM。

### Q5 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** Under HKSA 580 Written Representations, the written representation letter should be dated **as near as practicable to, but not after, the date of the auditor's report**, and must cover all financial statements and period(s) referred to in the auditor's report (in practice it is usually signed on the same date as the auditor's report). Written representations are **necessary audit evidence**, but because they are internally generated, they **do not on their own provide sufficient appropriate audit evidence** for the matters they cover and are **not a substitute** for other audit procedures. The auditor should **not** agree to changes management wishes to make to the letter.

**解題思路（繁中）:** C 一次過講啱兩個核心要求：日期 + 證據地位。A 錯：日期唔可以 **after** report date —— 呢個係硬性規則，唔係「越早越好」嘅彈性安排。B 錯：rep letter 係 internally generated，單獨唔構成 sufficient appropriate evidence；例如攞咗 impairment 嘅 written representation，auditor 仲係要做 procedures 驗證 impairment 夠唔夠。D 錯：LP 明確講 auditor should **not** agree to changes management may wish to make to the letter，否則佢唔可以當作 audit evidence 嘅一部分。呢三個伏位係 written representations 嘅「三大常考」。

**Rule Check:** HKSA 580 Written Representations (objectives per para. 6; list of HKSAs requiring representations per Appendix 1)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 「唔遲過 report date」同「唔係證據替代品」兩點經常分開考。留意 management 想改 letter 字眼係 red flag —— 考試會扮成「合理要求」做 distractor，但標準答案係拒絕。
