# M8 Chapter 5: Sampling — Phase 3 Quiz

**Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as `1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. …` — 超時如實申報**

**Question 1 (MCQ)**

During the audit of Harbourline Trading Ltd, the audit senior selects a sample of 60 purchase invoices and inspects each one for evidence of authorisation by the purchasing director, as required by the company's internal control policy. Which of the following statements BEST describes this test?

A. It is a substantive test of details which provides direct evidence over the valuation of purchases.
B. It is a test of control using attribute sampling, which provides indirect evidence over the financial statements.
C. It is a substantive analytical procedure performed to identify plausible relationships in the purchases data.
D. It is a test of details of balances which provides evidence over the completeness of trade payables.

**Question 2 (MCQ)**

Which of the following is an example of NON-SAMPLING risk?

A. The auditor concludes that controls are more effective than they actually are because the deviation rate in the sample is lower than the actual deviation rate in the population.
B. The auditor concludes that a material misstatement does not exist because the sample selected was too small to be representative of the population.
C. The auditor fails to detect a misstatement because an inappropriate audit procedure was applied to the items selected for testing.
D. The auditor concludes that controls are less effective than they actually are, resulting in unnecessary additional substantive procedures.

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

The audit manager of Brightway Electronics Ltd wishes to reduce the sample size for the test of details of trade receivables, while keeping sampling risk at an acceptable level. Which of the following changes would, other things being equal, achieve this?

A. Increasing the auditor's assessment of the risk of material misstatement of trade receivables.
B. Increasing the amount of misstatement the auditor expects to find in the population.
C. Making more use of other substantive procedures directed at the same assertion.
D. Decreasing tolerable misstatement for the trade receivables population.

**Question 4 (Calculation / short answer)**

You are the audit senior of Manley Garments Ltd. The trade receivables population totals HK$48,000,000, from which you have selected a sample of balances totalling HK$6,000,000 for confirmation testing (a test of details). Your testing identifies overstatement errors totalling HK$360,000 in the sample. None of the errors is anomalous. Tolerable misstatement for trade receivables has been set at HK$2,500,000.

Required:
(a) Calculate the projected misstatement for the trade receivables population, showing your workings.
(b) State, with reasons based on HKSA 530, whether the sample provides a reasonable basis for conclusions about the population, and identify ONE appropriate course of action available to the auditor if it does not.

**Question 5 (Calculation / short answer)**

You are testing the operating effectiveness of a control at Kwun Tong Logistics Ltd which requires every purchase invoice over HK$20,000 to be authorised by the finance director. You select a sample of 200 purchase invoices and find that 8 of them were not authorised. Your sampling parameters are: sampling risk 3%, tolerable rate of deviation 8%.

Required:
(a) Calculate the sample deviation rate and the upper deviation rate, showing your workings.
(b) State, with reasons, whether the auditor can place reliance on this control.
