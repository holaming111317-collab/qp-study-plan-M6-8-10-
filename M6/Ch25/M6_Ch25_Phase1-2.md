# M6 Chapter 25: Joint Arrangements — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. Define joint control and assess whether it exists using the two-step approach (collective control + unanimous consent).
2. Define a joint arrangement and identify its key characteristics (contractual arrangement binding two or more parties, shared control).
3. Classify a joint arrangement as a joint operation or a joint venture by assessing the parties' rights and obligations (structure, legal form, contractual terms, other facts and circumstances).
4. Account for an interest in a joint venture using the equity method, and account for an interest in a joint operation by recognising shares of assets, liabilities, revenues and expenses.
5. Prepare the presentation and disclosure in respect of interests in joint ventures and joint operations (HKAS 1 / HKFRS 12 / HKAS 24).

### 必背考點

#### 1. Recognition — Is it a joint arrangement? (HKFRS 11)

**Definition of a joint arrangement (HKFRS 11.4–6)**
- An arrangement of which **two or more parties have joint control**; the parties are **bound by a contractual arrangement**, and that contractual arrangement gives two or more of those parties joint control.
- HKFRS 11 applies to **all** parties to a joint arrangement — not just those with joint control (a mere participant must still consider HKFRS 11).

**Joint control (HKFRS 11.7)** = the **contractually agreed sharing of control**, which exists **only when decisions about the relevant activities require the unanimous consent of the parties sharing control**. Two-step assessment:
1. **Collective control (HKFRS 11.8, B5):** two or more parties must **act together** to direct the relevant activities; a party that can only *block* (but not direct) decisions does **not** control.
2. **Unanimous consent (HKFRS 11.9, B6, B9, B10):** each party sharing control can **prevent** any other party (or group) from making unilateral decisions about relevant activities.

Key fine points:
- **More than one combination** of agreeing parties can reach the required voting threshold → the contract **must specify which combination must agree unanimously**, otherwise there is **no joint arrangement** (HKFRS 11.B8).
- Unanimous consent over **protective rights only** → that investor does **not** have joint control (HKFRS 11.9, B6, B9, B10).
- Unanimous consent is **not** needed on every decision (e.g. dispute-resolution mechanisms do not prevent joint control) (HKFRS 11.9, B9–B10).
- Not all parties need joint control — some may be pure participants/investors (HKFRS 11.7, 11).
- **Reassess** when facts and circumstances change — a party may gain or cease to have joint control (HKFRS 11.12–13).

#### 2. Recognition — Classification: joint operation vs joint venture (HKFRS 11.14–16)

| Classification | Rights and obligations of the jointly controlling parties | Parties are called |
|---|---|---|
| **Joint operation** | Rights to the **assets**, and obligations for the **liabilities**, relating to the arrangement | Joint operators |
| **Joint venture** | Rights to the **net assets** of the arrangement | Joint venturers |

Classification uses a **substance-over-form** approach — the **detail** of the arrangement drives classification (HKFRS 11.15–16). Assessment sequence (Exhibit 25.2 decision tree, HKFRS 11.17, B15–B16, B21, B24, B33):

1. **Not structured through a separate vehicle** → can **only** be a **joint operation**. ("Separate vehicle" = a separately identifiable financial structure, including separate legal entities or entities recognised by statute, regardless of legal personality — HKFRS 11 Appendix A.)
2. Separate vehicle → does the **legal form** give the parties rights to **net assets** (e.g. a limited company normally does)? If **No** → joint operation.
3. If Yes → do the **terms of the contractual arrangement** override the legal form, giving parties rights to assets/obligations for liabilities in specified proportions? If **Yes** → joint operation (HKFRS 11.B25–B26, Exhibit 25.3).
4. If No → **other facts and circumstances**: is the arrangement designed so that (a) its activities **primarily aim to provide the parties with output**, **and** (b) it **depends on the parties on a continuous basis for settling its liabilities**? If **both Yes** → joint operation (HKFRS 11.B31–32). Otherwise → **joint venture**.

#### 3. Measurement — Accounting by each party (consolidated / equity-accounted FS)

**Joint operator (HKFRS 11.20, 23)** — recognise, in relation to its interest in the joint operation:
- its **assets**, including its **share of any assets held jointly**;
- its **liabilities**, including its **share of any liabilities incurred jointly**;
- its **revenue from the sale of its share of the output** arising from the joint operation;
- its **share of the revenue from the sale of the output by the joint operation**; and
- its **expenses**, including its **share of any expenses incurred jointly**.

Each element is recognised/measured under the **HKFRS applicable to that element** (HKFRS 11.23). A joint operator has **rights to an asset** (or share thereof), **not a "right to use"** — e.g. machinery used in a joint operation is accounted for under **HKAS 16**, not HKAS 38 (HKFRS 11.BC39). The share follows the **contractual rights/obligations**, which **may differ from the ownership interest**; **proportionate consolidation is not permitted**.

**Participant in a joint operation without joint control (HKFRS 11.23):**
- If it has rights to assets / obligations for liabilities → account for those shares **the same way as a joint operator**.
- If not → apply the HKFRS applicable to its interest (e.g. **HKFRS 9** for a loan).

**Joint venturer (HKFRS 11.24; HKAS 28.3, 28.27)** — apply the **equity method**:
- Interest initially recognised at **cost**, adjusted thereafter for the post-acquisition change in the venturer's share of **net assets** (HKAS 28.3).
- P&L includes the venturer's **share of profit or loss**; OCI includes its **share of OCI** of the JV (HKAS 28.3).
- The venturer's **ownership interest** (not its "share of" individual assets/liabilities) drives the accounting — opposite of a joint operation (HKFRS 11.24, BC41).
- **Unrealised gains/losses on upstream transactions** (JV → venturer) are eliminated in applying the equity method (e.g. Dr Share of profit of joint venture / Cr Inventory).
- JVs are often newly formed vehicles, so implicit goodwill/discount on acquisition and FV adjustments may be less common (but still possible).

**Participant in a joint venture without joint control (HKFRS 11.25):**
- Financial asset → **HKFRS 9**; or
- Associate-type interest (significant influence) → **HKAS 28** (equity method may apply).

#### 4. Measurement — Separate financial statements (HKFRS 11.26–27; HKAS 27.6, 27.10)
- Separate FS may be presented **only where the investor has investments in subsidiaries and/or associates and joint venture entities**, and only **in addition to** consolidated/equity-accounted FS. HKFRSs **do not require** separate FS.
- **Joint venturer** in separate FS — accounting **policy choice** (HKAS 27.10): **at cost**, or **per HKFRS 9 (fair value)**, or **equity method**. The choice must be applied **consistently to all joint ventures** (not arrangement-by-arrangement), but may differ from the policy chosen for associates.
- **Joint operator** in separate FS → accounts for its share of assets/liabilities/revenue/expenses **exactly as in consolidated FS** (HKFRS 11.26–27) — the cost/HKFRS 9/equity-method choice does **not** apply.
- A party to only a joint operation (no subsidiary/associate/JV) presents **"individual" (company) financial statements**, not "separate financial statements".

#### 5. Disclosure requirements

**Presentation (HKAS 1.54, 82, 82A)** — face of the FS must show:
- the carrying amount of **all investments accounted for using the equity method** (JVs may be grouped with associates in one line item);
- the share of **profit or loss** of equity-accounted JVs (and associates); and
- the share of **OCI** of equity-accounted JVs (and associates), split between items that **will / will not** be reclassified to P&L.
- Amounts relating to **joint operations are NOT separately reported**.

**Disclosure (HKFRS 12.1, 12.7, 12.20, 12.21–23; HKAS 24.17)** — objective: enable users to evaluate the **nature of, and risks associated with**, the interests and their effects on financial position, performance and cash flows:
- **Significant judgements and assumptions** in determining whether an interest is a joint arrangement and its type (JO vs JV) when structured through a separate vehicle — disclose any **changes** in assessment (HKFRS 12.7);
- **Nature, extent and financial effects** of interests in joint arrangements, incl. particulars (name, activity, place of business, ownership interest) (HKFRS 12.20);
- **Summarised financial information** for each **material** JV (based on the JV's HKFRS-compliant FS, adjusted for policy differences and equity-method adjustments such as FV adjustments, but **NOT adjusted for intercompany transactions** with the venturer), plus a **reconciliation** to the carrying amount (HKFRS 12.21–23);
- **Significant restrictions** on the JV's ability to transfer funds; **unrecognised share of losses**; **commitments**; and **contingent liabilities** relating to JVs (HKFRS 12.21–23);
- **HKAS 24.17**: nature of the related party relationship and transactions/balances (incl. commitments) with JV partners;
- **No separate quantitative disclosure required for joint operations** (qualitative general information suffices, subject to the HKFRS 12 objective).

### 易混淆概念對比

#### 對比一：Joint Operation vs Joint Venture

| | **Joint Operation** | **Joint Venture** |
|---|---|---|
| Rights / obligations | Rights to **assets**; obligations for **liabilities** (HKFRS 11.14–15) | Rights to **net assets** only (HKFRS 11.14, 16) |
| Party's name | Joint operator | Joint venturer |
| Accounting basis | "Share of" each asset/liability/revenue/expense per **contractual share** (may differ from ownership %) — HKFRS 11.20 | **Ownership interest** in net assets via **equity method** — HKFRS 11.24, HKAS 28 |
| Balance sheet effect | Gross-up: own line-by-line shares of assets/liabilities | One line: "Investment in joint venture" (equity accounted) |
| P&L effect | Share of revenue and expenses line-by-line | One line: "Share of profit of joint venture" |
| Separate vehicle? | Not structured through a separate vehicle → **must** be JO; but a separate vehicle can **still** be a JO if legal form/contract/facts give rights to assets & liabilities | Requires a separate vehicle whose legal form gives rights to net assets, not overridden by contract/facts |
| Separate FS | Same accounting as consolidated FS (share of A/L/R/E) | Policy choice: cost / HKFRS 9 / equity method (HKAS 27.10) |
| Presentation | Amounts **not separately reported** | Separate line items with associates (HKAS 1.54, 82, 82A) |

💬 **點樣分辨：** 唔好睇個名（"joint venture company" 都未必係 JV），要問「我直接擁有資產/孭住負債，定只係擁有淨資產嘅權益？」**考試伏位：** (1) 有 separate vehicle ≠ 一定係 JV——若 output 全部賣俾股東、靠股東持續找數，照樣係 JO（HKFRS 11.B31–32）；(2) JO 份額跟合約權利義務，唔係跟持股比例（60/40 權利 vs 50/50 持股 → 用 60/40）；(3) proportionate consolidation 已禁止，JO 唔係按比例合併條線咁簡單，係逐項認自己份額。

#### 對比二：Control vs Joint Control vs Significant Influence

| | **Control** (HKFRS 10) | **Joint control** (HKFRS 11.7) | **Significant influence** (HKAS 28) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Definition | Power over relevant activities + exposure to variable returns + ability to affect returns | Contractually agreed sharing of control; decisions on relevant activities need **unanimous consent** of parties sharing control | Power to **participate** in financial & operating policy decisions, but not control or joint control |
| Unilateral power? | One party can decide alone | No single party can decide alone; each can **block** the others | Cannot decide alone, cannot block; influence only |
| Contractual arrangement required? | No | **Yes** — essential | No |
| Resulting accounting | Consolidation (subsidiary) | Joint operation (line-by-line shares) or joint venture (equity method) | Associate → equity method |
| Blocking rights only | Not control | Not joint control if rights are **protective only** | May indicate influence |

💬 **點樣分辨：** Control = 我一個人話事；Joint control = 冇人單獨話事，相關活動要**一致同意**；Significant influence = 有份參與但話唔到事。**考試伏位：** (1) voting threshold 可以由多於一個組合達成（如 D+E 或 D+F 都得）→ 合約必須指明邊個組合要一致同意，否則**唔係 joint arrangement**（HKFRS 11.B8）；(2) 只能「否決/保護性權利」≠ joint control；(3) 唔需要所有投資者都有 joint control——可以有純 participant。

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** Define "joint control" under HKFRS 11 and state the two conditions that must both be present.
**Back:**
- Joint control = the contractually agreed sharing of control of an arrangement (HKFRS 11.7).
- Exists only when decisions about the **relevant activities** require the **unanimous consent** of the parties sharing control.
- Two conditions: (1) **collective control** — parties must act together to direct relevant activities (HKFRS 11.8); (2) **unanimous consent** — each party can prevent unilateral decisions by the others (HKFRS 11.9).
💬 白話：joint control 即係「合約講明一齊話事」——首先大家夾埋先控制到（collective），其次決策要一致同意，任何一方都可以否決其他人單獨行事。

### Card 2
**Front:** An arrangement's decisions require 75% of voting rights. The threshold can be met by more than one combination of parties. What extra term is needed for the arrangement to be a joint arrangement, and why?
**Back:**
- The contractual arrangement must **specify which parties (or combination of parties) must agree unanimously** on decisions about the relevant activities (HKFRS 11.B8).
- Reason: if multiple combinations can reach the threshold, no single combination is *required* to act unanimously — so unanimous consent (and thus joint control) is not demonstrated.
💬 白話：如果「A+B 得、A+C 都得」，即係冇一個組合係必須嘅，咁就冇「一致同意」可言——合約要白紙黑字寫明邊幾方必須一齊同意，先算 joint arrangement。

### Card 3
**Front:** State the classification rule when a joint arrangement is NOT structured through a separate vehicle, and define "separate vehicle".
**Back:**
- Not structured through a separate vehicle → the arrangement is **always a joint operation** (HKFRS 11.16).
- "Separate vehicle" = a separately identifiable financial structure, including separate legal entities or entities recognised by statute, **regardless of whether they have legal personality** (e.g. a company, a branch) (HKFRS 11 Appendix A).
💬 白話：冇獨立載體 → 實係 joint operation，無得拗。有獨立公司都唔代表係 JV，仲要過埋 legal form、contract terms、facts & circumstances 三關。

### Card 4
**Front:** Even where a separate vehicle's legal form confers rights to net assets, which "other facts and circumstances" can still lead to joint operation classification?
**Back:**
Both of the following must hold (HKFRS 11.B31–32):
- The arrangement's activities **primarily aim to provide the parties with output** (the parties are the vehicle's primary source of cash flows); **and**
- The arrangement **depends on the parties on a continuous basis for settling its liabilities**.
💬 白話：間車係為股東而設（output 全部俾股東、break-even 定價、唔准賣俾第三者），兼要股東持續泵水找數 → 經濟實質係股東自己擁有資產孭住負債 → joint operation。

### Card 5
**Front:** List the five elements a joint operator recognises in relation to its interest in a joint operation (HKFRS 11.20).
**Back:**
1. Its **assets**, including its share of any assets held jointly;
2. Its **liabilities**, including its share of any liabilities incurred jointly;
3. Its **revenue from the sale of its share of the output** arising from the joint operation;
4. Its **share of the revenue from the sale of the output by the joint operation**; and
5. Its **expenses**, including its share of any expenses incurred jointly.
💬 白話：記口訣「A-L-R-R-E」：自己嘅資產負債、賣自己 output 嘅收入、JO 賣 output 我嘅份額、自己嘅開支。每項用返相關 HKFRS 計量（機器用 HKAS 16，唔係 HKAS 38）。

### Card 6
**Front:** How does a joint venturer account for its interest in a joint venture, and how does the equity-accounted carrying amount roll forward?
**Back:**
- Apply the **equity method** (HKFRS 11.24; HKAS 28.3).
- Carrying amount = **Cost** + share of JV's **adjusted net profit** + share of JV's **OCI** − **dividends received** ± other changes in net assets.
- Adjust JV profit for unrealised gains/losses on upstream/downstream transactions and FV-adjustment depreciation before taking the ownership % share.
💬 白話：opening cost 起錶，加應佔利潤（記得調整 unrealised gain），減股息。股息係收回投資，唔係收入——Dr Cash / Cr Investment in JV。

### Card 7
**Front:** A participant in a joint arrangement does NOT have joint control. How does it account for its interest (joint operation vs joint venture)?
**Back:**
- **Joint operation:** if it has rights to assets / obligations for liabilities → recognise its share of assets, liabilities, revenues, expenses **same as a joint operator** (HKFRS 11.23); otherwise apply the HKFRS applicable to the interest (e.g. HKFRS 9).
- **Joint venture:** financial asset → **HKFRS 9**; if significant influence → **HKAS 28** equity method (HKFRS 11.25).
💬 白話：冇 joint control 唔代表唔使理 HKFRS 11。JO 嘅 participant 有實質資產負債權利就照樣逐項認份額；JV 嘅 participant 就當普通投資（HKFRS 9）或聯營（HKAS 28）處理。

### Card 8
**Front:** What are the accounting policy options for a joint venturer's investment in a JV in its separate financial statements, and what consistency rule applies?
**Back:**
- Options (HKAS 27.10): **at cost**, **in accordance with HKFRS 9** (fair value), or **equity method**.
- The same policy must be applied to **all joint ventures** (not an arrangement-by-arrangement choice), but it may differ from the policy used for **associates**.
- HKFRSs **do not require** separate FS; a joint operator in separate FS accounts for its share of A/L/R/E same as consolidated (HKFRS 11.26–27).
💬 白話：separate FS 入面 JV 有三個選擇（cost / HKFRS 9 / equity method），揀咗就要全部 JV 一致用，但可以同 associates 唔同 policy。記住：JO 冇呢個選擇。
