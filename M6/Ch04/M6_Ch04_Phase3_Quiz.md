**Q1.** Ploverhill Limited manufactures solid-wood dining tables in its Dongguan factory. In accordance with HKAS 2, which of the following costs should be **included** in the cost of Ploverhill's inventories?

A. Storage costs of finished tables held in the company's showroom warehouse
B. Import duties and freight inwards incurred on shipments of timber raw materials
C. Abnormal amounts of timber wasted during a machine malfunction
D. Commissions paid to sales staff on contracts signed with retailers

**Q2.** Dragonkiln Limited manufactures ceramic dinnerware. Its budgeted fixed production overhead for the year is HK$1,152,000 and the normal capacity of its production facility is 9,600 units per year. In April 20X7, an equipment breakdown meant only 600 units were produced (normal monthly output is 800 units). In accordance with HKAS 2, what amount of fixed production overhead should be recognised as an **expense** (i.e. unallocated overhead) in April 20X7?

A. HK$0
B. HK$24,000
C. HK$72,000
D. HK$96,000

**Q3.** Regatta Yachts Limited builds racing yachts, each constructed to an individual customer's design and specification; no two yachts are interchangeable. In accordance with HKAS 2, which cost formula should Regatta use to assign costs to its yacht inventories?

A. First-in, first-out (FIFO)
B. Weighted average cost
C. Specific identification
D. Last-in, first-out (LIFO)

**Q4.** Summit Outdoor Limited sells two product lines. At its year end, the following information is available:

| Product line | Units on hand | Cost per unit (HK$) | Estimated selling price per unit (HK$) | Estimated costs of completion per unit (HK$) | Estimated costs to make the sale per unit (HK$) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Down jackets | 200 | 850 | 880 | – | 40 |
| Trail shoes | 100 | 420 | 450 | 50 | 10 |

In accordance with HKAS 2, what is the **total write-down of inventories** that Summit Outdoor should recognise as an expense in the period?

A. HK$0
B. HK$2,000
C. HK$5,000
D. HK$14,000

**Q5.** Lantern House Limited holds a line of decorative lamps with a cost of HK$300,000. At 31 December 20X4, the lamps were written down to their net realisable value of HK$260,000. At 31 December 20X5, there is clear evidence that the NRV of the lamps has recovered to HK$295,000. In accordance with HKAS 2, which journal entry should Lantern House record at 31 December 20X5?

A. Dr Inventories HK$35,000 / Cr Loss on write-down of inventories HK$35,000
B. Dr Inventories HK$40,000 / Cr Loss on write-down of inventories HK$40,000
C. Dr Inventories HK$35,000 / Cr Revaluation surplus HK$35,000
D. Dr Loss on write-down of inventories HK$35,000 / Cr Inventories HK$35,000
