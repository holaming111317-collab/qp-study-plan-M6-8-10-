# M6 Chapter 20: Financial Statements Introduction — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives (3–5, English, action verbs)
1. Explain the basis of preparation and presentation of general purpose financial statements under **HKAS 1 Presentation of Financial Statements**, including the eight general features (true and fair view, going concern, accrual basis, materiality, offsetting, frequency of reporting, comparative information, consistency of presentation).
2. Identify the components of a **complete set of financial statements** under HKAS 1 and state when a **third statement of financial position** is required.
3. Apply the **current / non-current classification** rules for assets and liabilities, including operating-cycle and refinancing considerations.
4. Prepare and present a **statement of financial position**, a **statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income** (single or two-statement approach; expenses by nature or by function) and a **statement of changes in equity** in accordance with HKAS 1.
5. Distinguish items reported in **profit or loss**, **other comprehensive income** (with/without subsequent reclassification), the **statement of changes in equity** and the **notes**.

### 必背考點

#### A. Recognition（元素何時入帳 — 與 Framework 連繫）
- **Accrual basis (HKAS 1 para. 27)**: FS 以 accrual basis 編製（cash flow information 除外）；income, expenses, assets, liabilities, equity 於滿足 Framework 中各元素的 definition 及 recognition criteria 時確認（詳見 Chapter 1）。
- **Going concern (HKAS 1)**: FS 一般假設 entity 在 foreseeable future 持續經營（going concern assumption）。
- **All-inclusive approach (HKAS 1 para. 88)**: 期內所有 income 及 expense items 必須計入 profit or loss，除非另一 HKFRS 要求或准許計入 **other comprehensive income (OCI)**。
- **Profit or loss (HKAS 1 para. 7)**: total of income less expenses, **excluding** OCI items。
- **OCI (HKAS 1 para. 7)**: items of income and expense (including reclassification adjustments) **not recognised in profit or loss** as required or permitted by other HKFRSs（例：revaluation surplus 變動 under HKAS 16 para. 39；fair value gains/losses on investments in equity instruments）。
- **Total comprehensive income (HKAS 1 para. 7)**: profit or loss + OCI = change in equity during a period **other than** transactions with owners in their capacity as owners。

#### B. Measurement（計量基礎 — 本章以披露為主）
- HKAS 1 本身不訂立計量規則；recognition/measurement 由其他 HKFRSs 訂明（e.g. HKAS 16 PPE、HKAS 2 Inventories、HKAS 38 Intangible Assets）。
- **Notes 必須披露 measurement basis (or bases)** used in preparing the FS（historical cost、current cost、fair value 等），因為這對用家分析有重大影響（HKAS 1 para. 117 相關；HKAS 16 亦要求披露 measurement bases）。
- **Judgements (HKAS 1 para. 122)**: management 在應用會計政策時作出、對 FS 確認金額有最重大影響的 judgements 必須披露。
- **Offsetting (HKAS 1 paras. 32–35)**: assets/liabilities、income/expenses **不得對銷**，除非某 HKFRS 要求或准許（例：HKAS 32 在符合條件時准許 financial asset/liability 對銷）。**Measuring assets net of valuation allowances（如 trade receivables 減 provision for expected credit loss）不是 offsetting**。同一交易或同類交易的 gains/losses 可 net（如出售非流動資產的 gain/loss；外匯收益虧損；HKAS 37 provision 開支與第三方補償），但 **material** 的 gains/losses 必須分開列報。

#### C. Disclosure / Presentation requirements（本章核心）

**C1. Complete set of financial statements（HKAS 1 para. 10）**
- statement of financial position as at the end of the period
- statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income for the period
- statement of changes in equity for the period
- statement of cash flows for the period（HKAS 7 — 詳見 Chapter 21）
- notes, comprising **material accounting policy information** and other explanatory information
- comparative information in respect of the preceding period
- a **third statement of financial position** as at the beginning of the preceding period when there is retrospective application of an accounting policy / retrospective restatement / reclassification **with a material effect** on that SOFP (HKAS 1 paras. 40A–40C)；**related notes for this third SOFP are not required**。例：year-end 31 Dec 20X9 → 三份 SOFP 日期為 31 Dec 20X9、31 Dec 20X8、**1 Jan 20X8**。

**C2. True and fair view / compliance（HKAS 1 paras. 15, 16, 23）**
- 遵從 HKFRSs（必要時加 additional disclosures）即 presumed to give a **true and fair view**。
- 遵從 HKFRSs 的 entity 須在 notes 作出 **explicit and unreserved statement of compliance** (para. 16)。
- 極罕有情況下遵從某 HKFRS 會誤導而監管框架又不准偏離：須披露 (i) 該 HKFRS 名稱、要求性質及管理層認為遵從會誤導的原因；(ii) 每期為達致 true and fair view 所需對各項目的調整 (para. 23)。

**C3. Consistency (HKAS 1 paras. 45–46)**
- Presentation/classification 須逐期一致，除非：(i) significant change in operations；(ii) 改變能提供更 relevant 資訊；或 (iii) 另一 HKFRS 要求。改變時 comparatives 須 reclassify。

**C4. Materiality and aggregation (HKAS 1 paras. 29–31; Framework para. 2.11)**
- 'Material' = information 若 omitted, misstated or **obscured** 可合理預期影響 primary users 的決定；以 FS as a whole 評估；HKICPA **不能**設定 uniform quantitative threshold。
- 每個 material class of similar items 須分開列報；dissimilar items 除非 immaterial 否則分開列報；immaterial 金額可 aggregate。
- HKFRS 指定的 disclosure 若 resulting information **not material**，可不作披露（但可自願加 additional disclosure）。

**C5. Comparative information (HKAS 1 paras. 38, 38A, 38B, 41, 42)**
- 最少列報 preceding period 的所有金額（即最少兩年）；narrative/descriptive comparatives 若 relevant 亦須提供（例：未解決官司的進展）。
- 改變 presentation/classification 時，comparatives 須 reclassify，並披露 reclassification 的 **nature, amount and reason**。
- HKAS 8 的 accounting policy 變更及 error 更正亦須調整 comparatives（Chapter 13）。

**C6. Identification & reporting period (HKAS 1 paras. 49–51, 36–37)**
- FS 須 clearly identified 並與同一文件內其他資料區分；prominently display：entity 名稱（及改名）、individual entity 或 group、reporting period 結算日/涵蓋期間、presentation currency（HKAS 21 定義）、level of rounding。
- Complete set of FS **at least annually**；可採用 52-week period。改變 year-end 致期間長短於一年：須披露原因及 comparatives 不完全可比的事實。

**C7. Statement of financial position（HKAS 1 paras. 54–58, 60–70, 77–79）**
- **HKAS 1 不指定 order or format**（para. 57；Implementation Guidance 只提供一個可接受格式；可採 Assets = Equity + Liabilities 或 Assets − Liabilities = Equity）。
- Minimum line items (para. 54)：PPE; investment property; intangible assets; financial assets; investments accounted for using the equity method; inventories; trade and other receivables; cash and cash equivalents; assets/disposal groups held for sale (HKFRS 5); trade and other payables; provisions; financial liabilities; current tax liabilities/assets (HKAS 12); deferred tax liabilities/assets; liabilities in disposal groups held for sale; non-controlling interests (within equity); issued capital and reserves attributable to owners of the parent。
- Additional items/headings/subtotals 當 relevant to understanding (para. 55)，按 nature and liquidity of assets、function of assets、amounts/nature/timing of liabilities 評估。
- **Current asset (para. 66)**：(a) 預期在 normal operating cycle 內變現/出售/消耗；(b) 主要持作 trading；(c) 預期於報告期後 12 個月內變現；(d) cash or cash equivalent（HKAS 7 定義）除非受限於報告期後至少 12 個月不得兌換或用於償債。其他全部 = non-current。
- **Current liability (para. 69)**：(a) 預期在 normal operating cycle 內結清；(b) 主要持作 trading；(c) 於報告期後 12 個月內到期結清；(d) **於報告期末沒有權利**將結清遞延至報告期後至少 12 個月。其他全部 = non-current。
- **Operating cycle (para. 68)**：由購入資產加工到變現為 cash/cash equivalents 的時間；通常 12 個月內但可更長。Operating cycle > 12 個月時（如物業發展 18 個月），inventories/trade payables 仍可全數列 current，但須就每個 line item 額外披露預期 **超過 12 個月**後收回/結清的金額 (para. 61)。
- 金融機構等無清晰 operating cycle 的 entity 可按 liquidity order 列報 (para. 63)。
- Share capital 每類別須披露 (para. 79(a))：authorised 股數；issued（fully/partly paid）股數；par value per share 或無面值；期初至期末股數 reconciliation。另須描述每項 reserve 的 nature and purpose (para. 79(b))。Line items 須再作 subclassification（paras. 77–78），如 PPE 按 HKAS 16 para. 37 分類（land、machinery 等，通常於 notes 披露）。

**C8. Statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income（HKAS 1 paras. 10A, 81A–82A, 85, 90–93, 97–105）**
- **Presentation choice (para. 10A)**：(i) single statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income（profit or loss section 直接接 OCI section）；或 (ii) two statements — separate statement of profit or loss 緊接 statement of comprehensive income（後者以 profit or loss 數字開始）。
- **Profit or loss section minimum line items (para. 82)**：revenue；gains/losses on derecognition of financial assets at amortised cost；finance costs；impairment losses (including reversals)；share of profit or loss of associates and JVs (equity method)；tax expense；a single amount for the total of discontinued operations。須另列 profit or loss、total OCI 及 comprehensive income for the period (para. 81A)，並分配 profit or loss 及 total comprehensive income 予 owners of the parent 及 non-controlling interests (para. 81B)。
- **Material items (paras. 97–98)**：性質/金額重大的 income/expense 須單獨披露，例：write-down of inventories to NRV 或 PPE to recoverable amount（及 reversals）；restructuring 及 provision reversals；disposals of PPE / investments；discontinued operations；litigation settlements。
- **Expense analysis (paras. 99, 102–103, 105)**：按 **nature**（depreciation、employee benefits、raw materials used 等；簡單，不用分攤）或 **function / 'cost of sales'**（cost of sales、distribution costs、administrative expenses；較 relevant 但需 arbitrary allocations）分類 — 選能提供 reliable and more relevant information 者。
- **OCI section (paras. 82A, 90–91)**：分開列示 (a) items **that will not be reclassified** subsequently to profit or loss；(b) items **that may be reclassified subsequently** when specific conditions are met；並單獨列示 share of OCI of associates/JVs。每項 OCI 的 income tax 須在 statement 或 notes 披露；OCI items 可 **net of tax** 或 **before tax（連一個合計稅額）** 列報。
- **Reclassification adjustments (paras. 92–93, 96)**：過往在 OCI 確認的 'unrealised' 金額其後 'recycled' 至本期 P&L，須在相關 OCI component 中扣除以避免 double counting。**Revaluation surplus 不產生 reclassification adjustments** — 永不 recycle 至 P&L；資產 derecognised 時 surplus 可直接轉撥（transfer directly）於 equity 內。

**C9. Statement of changes in equity（HKAS 1 paras. 106–110）**
- 功能：reconcile 每個 equity component 期初至期末的 carrying amount。Total equity 須與 SOFP 的 total equity 相符。
- 必須包括 (para. 106)：(a) total comprehensive income for the period（分開 owners of the parent / NCI）；(b) 每個 component 按 HKAS 8 的 retrospective application/restatement 影響（調整 opening retained earnings，除非準則要求調整其他 component；policy changes 與 error corrections 分開披露）；(d) 每個 component 的 reconciliation，分開列示 profit or loss、OCI、transactions with owners（contributions by / distributions to owners；不導致 loss of control 的 subsidiary ownership 變動）。
- Components of equity (para. 108)：each class of share capital、each class of OCI 的累計結餘（如 revaluation surplus）、retained earnings。
- **Dividends (para. 107)**：已確認分派予 owners 的股息及每股金額須在 SOCIE 或 notes 披露 — **不得**列入 SPLOCI（屬 owner transaction，非 expense）。
- SOCIE 公式：Opening total equity + total comprehensive income −/+ transactions with shareholders（share issues、dividends）= Closing total equity。

#### D. Key Journal Entries（本章示例中出現、必須識做）
1. **Upward revaluation of PPE（首次、未沖回減值）— gain to OCI**（稅前）：
   - Dr Property, plant and equipment (asset) 120,000 / Cr Revaluation surplus (OCI → equity) 120,000
2. **Deferred tax on revaluation gain**（HKAS 12，稅率 16.5% → 120,000 × 16.5% = 19,800）：
   - Dr Revaluation surplus (equity) 19,800 / Cr Deferred tax liability 19,800
   - 淨效應：revaluation surplus 增加 100,200（OCI net of tax）。
3. **Dividend declared（owner transaction，唔係 expense）**：
   - Dr Retained earnings 160,000 / Cr Dividend payable 160,000（ordinary；preference 同理）
4. **Transfer to general reserve（equity 內部轉撥，total equity 不變）**：
   - Dr Retained earnings 20,000 / Cr General reserve 20,000
5. **Issue of shares for cash**：
   - Dr Cash 200,000 / Cr Share capital 200,000

### 易混淆概念對比

**Table 1 — Profit or Loss vs Other Comprehensive Income (OCI)**

| | Profit or Loss | Other Comprehensive Income |
|---|---|---|
| Definition (HKAS 1 para. 7) | Income less expenses, excluding OCI items | Income/expense items (incl. reclassification adjustments) NOT recognised in P&L as required/permitted by other HKFRSs |
| Examples | Revenue, cost of sales, finance costs, tax expense, impairment losses, share of profit of associates | Changes in revaluation surplus (HKAS 16 para. 39); FV gains/losses on equity instruments |
| Recycled to P&L later? | N/A | 視乎類別：'may be reclassified' vs 'will not be reclassified'（para. 82A）— **revaluation surplus 永不 recycle**（para. 96） |
| 點樣分辨 / 考試伏位 | HKAS 1 採 all-inclusive approach（para. 88）：預設所有 income/expense 入 P&L | 伏位：考生誤以為 revaluation gain 入 P&L，或誤將 revaluation surplus 於出售時 recycle 入 P&L（正確做法係直接喺 equity 內轉撥） |

**Table 2 — Statement of Changes in Equity vs Notes to the Financial Statements**

| | Statement of Changes in Equity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 內容 (HKAS 1) | TCI；每個 equity component 期初/期末 reconciliation；HKAS 8 retrospective adjustments；dividends；share issues；equity 內部轉撥 (paras. 106–108) | Statement of compliance with HKFRSs；material accounting policy information (para. 117)；supporting information；other disclosures e.g. contingent liabilities (paras. 112–114) |
| Dividends 放邊度 | SOCIE 或 notes（para. 107）— **絕對唔係** SPLOCI | 可作替代披露位置 |
| Accumulated depreciation / PPE 分類 | 唔會出現（SOCIE 只顯示 equity components） | PPE by class (HKAS 16 para. 37)、accumulated depreciation 等 subclassification 喺 notes (paras. 77–78) |
| 點樣分辨 / 考試伏位 | 凡係 owner transaction 或 equity component 變動 → SOCIE；total equity 要對返 SOFP | 伏位：考生將 dividends declared 放入 SPLOCI 當 expense；或將 prior period error 對 opening retained earnings 嘅調整遺漏喺 SOCIE |

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** According to HKAS 1, what are the components of a complete set of financial statements?

**Back:**
- Statement of financial position (end of period)
- Statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income
- Statement of changes in equity
- Statement of cash flows (HKAS 7)
- Notes (material accounting policy information + other explanatory information)
- Comparative information for the preceding period
- A third SOFP at the beginning of the preceding period (only when retrospective application / restatement / reclassification with material effect)

💬 記口訣「四表一註加比較」：SOFP、SPLOCI、SOCIE、SOCF 四張主表，加 notes、comparatives，特殊情況（追溯調整/重列/重新分類且有重大影響）要加多份期初 SOFP。Statement of cash flows 嘅詳細內容喺 Chapter 21。

### Card 2
**Front:** Under HKAS 1, when must an entity present a third statement of financial position, and are related notes required?

**Back:**
- Required when the entity (i) applies an accounting policy retrospectively; (ii) makes a retrospective restatement; or (iii) reclassifies items — AND there is a material effect on the SOFP at the beginning of the preceding period (HKAS 1 paras. 40A–40B)
- Present SOFPs at: end of current period; end of preceding period; beginning of preceding period
- Related notes for the third (opening) SOFP are NOT required (para. 40C)

💬 三個觸發點：追溯會計政策、追溯重列錯誤、重新分類，仲要有 material effect。例：20X9 年結 → 31/12/20X9、31/12/20X8、1/1/20X8 三份 SOFP，最早嗰份唔使附 notes。考試常問日期點計。

### Card 3
**Front:** State the HKAS 1 criteria for classifying an asset as current.

**Back:** An asset is current when (HKAS 1 para. 66):
- Expected to be realised / sold / consumed in the normal operating cycle; OR
- Held primarily for trading; OR
- Expected to be realised within 12 months after the reporting period; OR
- Cash or cash equivalent, unless restricted from being exchanged or used to settle a liability for at least 12 months after the reporting period
- All other assets → non-current

💬 關鍵係 operating cycle 可以長過 12 個月（例如物業發展商 18 個月 cycle），咁樣 inventory 就算 15 個月後先賣出都係 current。但受限唔用得嘅 cash 超過 12 個月就變 non-current — 呢個係常見伏位。

### Card 4
**Front:** State the HKAS 1 criteria for classifying a liability as current.

**Back:** A liability is current when (HKAS 1 para. 69):
- Expected to be settled in the normal operating cycle; OR
- Held primarily for trading; OR
- Due to be settled within 12 months after the reporting period; OR
- The entity does NOT have the right at the end of the reporting period to defer settlement for at least 12 months after the reporting period
- All other liabilities → non-current

💬 同 asset 規則鏡像對稱。特別留意 (d)：判斷時點係「報告期末」有冇遞延權利。Trade payables 就算 12 個月後先找數，只要屬 normal operating cycle 一部分都照列 current (para. 70)。

### Card 5
**Front:** What is the HKAS 1 rule on offsetting, and what is NOT regarded as offsetting?

**Back:**
- Assets/liabilities and income/expenses shall NOT be offset unless required or permitted by an HKFRS (HKAS 1 para. 32; e.g. HKAS 32 permits offsetting financial assets/liabilities when conditions are met)
- NOT offsetting: measuring assets net of valuation allowances (e.g. trade receivables less provision for expected credit loss)
- Netting permitted for gains/losses from the same or similar transactions (e.g. disposal of non-current assets; FX gains/losses) — but material gains/losses must be reported separately

💬 白話：「唔准玩對沖式隱藏」— 對銷會令用家睇唔到交易全貌同現金流。但「receivables 減 ECL provision」只係計量唔係對銷。記住：auditor/director 批准都唔構成對銷理由，一定要 HKFRS 准許。

### Card 6
**Front:** Which items must be presented in the profit or loss section under HKAS 1 para. 82?

**Back:**
- Revenue
- Gains/losses on derecognition of financial assets measured at amortised cost
- Finance costs
- Impairment losses (including reversals)
- Share of profit or loss of associates and joint ventures (equity method)
- Tax expense
- Single amount for the total of discontinued operations
- Plus: profit or loss, total OCI, total comprehensive income (para. 81A); allocation to owners of the parent / NCI (para. 81B)

💬 伏位：rent expense、audit fees、profit before tax 都**唔係** para. 82 指定嘅 mandatory line items（PBT 係常見但非強制）。記住「R-F-I-A-T-D」主軸再加 discontinued operations 單一數字。

### Card 7
**Front:** Distinguish the 'nature of expense' method from the 'function of expense' method under HKAS 1.

**Back:**
- Nature of expense method: expenses aggregated by nature — e.g. depreciation, employee benefits, raw materials used, advertising; simple, no allocation needed (HKAS 1 paras. 99, 102)
- Function of expense ('cost of sales') method: expenses classified by function — e.g. cost of sales, distribution costs, administrative expenses; more relevant but requires arbitrary allocations and judgement (para. 103)
- Choose whichever provides reliable and more relevant information, based on historical/industry factors and nature of the entity (para. 105)

💬 分辨口訣：nature 問「洗咗喺乜嘢上面」（折舊、人工）；function 問「邊個部門洗」（銷售成本、行政費）。題目見到 'distribution costs / administrative expenses' = function；見到 'depreciation / employee benefits' 逐項列 = nature。

### Card 8
**Front:** What must be included in a statement of changes in equity under HKAS 1 para. 106, and where are dividends disclosed?

**Back:**
- Total comprehensive income for the period (split: owners of the parent / NCI)
- Effects of retrospective application / restatement per HKAS 8 for each component
- Reconciliation of each component of equity (share capital, each OCI reserve, retained earnings) between beginning and end of period, showing separately: profit or loss; OCI; transactions with owners (contributions, distributions, changes in ownership without loss of control)
- Dividends recognised as distributions to owners + amount per share: in SOCIE or in the notes (para. 107) — NEVER in SPLOCI
- Total equity must agree with the SOFP

💬 SOCIE 係「期初 → 期末」嘅對帳表：開頭結餘 + TCI +/− owner transactions（發股、派息）= 期末結餘。最大伏位：dividends declared 唔係 expense，唔准放入 profit or loss！

## 本章急救詞彙

| EN Term | 繁中白話 | English Model Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Complete set of financial statements | 完整財務報表組合：四張主表 + notes + 比較資料（特殊情況加期初 SOFP） | A complete set of financial statements comprises a statement of financial position, a statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income, a statement of changes in equity, a statement of cash flows, notes and comparative information. |
| Going concern | 持續經營假設：預設公司 foreseeable future 繼續營運 | Financial statements are normally prepared on the assumption that the entity is a going concern and will continue in operation for the foreseeable future. |
| Materiality | 重要性：資料若遺漏、錯報或被遮蔽會影響使用者決定；無統一百分比門檻 | Information is material if omitting, misstating or obscuring it could reasonably be expected to influence decisions that the primary users of general purpose financial statements make. |
| Offsetting | 對銷：資產/負債、收入/費用唔准互相抵銷，除非 HKFRS 准許 | Assets and liabilities shall not be offset unless required or permitted by an HKFRS. |
| Operating cycle | 營運週期：由入貨加工到收返現金嘅時間，通常 12 個月但可以更長 | The operating cycle of an entity is the time between the acquisition of assets for processing and their realisation in cash or cash equivalents. |
| Other comprehensive income (OCI) | 其他全面收益：HKFRS 指定唔入損益嘅收入/費用項目，如重估盈餘變動 | Other comprehensive income comprises items of income and expense that are not recognised in profit or loss as required or permitted by other HKFRSs. |
| Reclassification adjustment | 重新分類調整：以前喺 OCI 嘅金額「回收」返入本期損益，避免雙重計算 | Reclassification adjustments are amounts reclassified to profit or loss in the current period that were recognised in other comprehensive income in the current or previous periods. |
| Revaluation surplus | 重估盈餘：資產重估增值喺 OCI 確認並累積於權益，永不 recycle 入損益 | The revaluation surplus is recognised in other comprehensive income and is not reclassified to profit or loss in subsequent periods. |
