# M6 Chapter 12: Non-current Assets Held for Sale and Discontinued Operations — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):**
B is correct. Under HKFRS 5, a non-current asset is classified as held for sale only if it is available for immediate sale in its present condition (subject only to usual and customary terms) **and** the sale is highly probable (paras. 6–7). For the sale to be highly probable, HKFRS 5 para. 8 requires, inter alia, an active programme to locate a buyer to have been initiated and the asset to be actively marketed at a price reasonable in relation to its current fair value. Lantern fails both: no programme to locate a buyer exists, and the asking price (40% above fair value) is not reasonable. Therefore the warehouse cannot be classified as held for sale.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- 兩大條件要**同時**符合：available for immediate sale（Lantern 過咗呢關）＋ sale highly probable（Lantern 過唔到）。
- 伏位一：board approval 只係 highly probable 六項準則之一（management committed to a plan），**唔係充分條件** —— 所以 A 同 C 錯。
- 伏位二：開價高過 FV 四成即係 "not actively marketed at a price reasonable in relation to current fair value"，呢個係 HKFRS 5 para. 8 明文嘅準則，唔係常識題。
- D 錯因為 HKFRS 5 從未要求已簽 binding sale agreement —— 分類基準係 criteria met 之日，唔係簽約日（LP Illustrative Example 2：Board 批准 + 啟動程序當日已分類）。
- 考試伏位：題目刻意畀晒「board 批准」「即刻賣得」呢啲糖衣，考你記唔記得 highly probable 係**六項準則全體**嘅測試。

**Rule Check:** HKFRS 5 paras. 6, 7, 8

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 見到「board 已批准＋可即時出售」就心急揀「must classify」；記住 "highly probable" 係 significantly more likely than probable，要逐項 para. 8 準則核對，尤其 pricing 同 active programme 兩項最常畀人抽走。

### Q2 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):**
C is correct. The measurement requirements of HKFRS 5 do not apply to non-current assets accounted for under the fair value model of HKAS 40 *Investment Property* (HKFRS 5 para. 5). In the three-step approach for disposal groups, such assets must first be remeasured in accordance with their own standard (HKAS 40, i.e. to fair value) before the disposal group's total carrying amount is compared with its fair value less costs to sell. Goodwill, PPE and intangible assets are all **within** the measurement scope of HKFRS 5 and are therefore measured at the lower of carrying amount and FVLCS as part of the group test.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- 口訣「**金融、稅、投資物業、保險**」：HKFRS 9 financial assets、HKAS 12 deferred tax assets、HKAS 40 fair-value-model investment property、HKFRS 17 insurance assets 全部豁免 HKFRS 5 計量。
- A、B、D 全部屬於 HKFRS 5 measurement scope：goodwill 更加係 impairment 分配嘅**第一順位**；PPE 同 intangibles 係 pro-rata 分配對象。
- 留意題目問嘅唔係「邊個唔使減值」，而係「邊個要**先用自己準則 remeasure**」—— 即係 disposal group 三步法嘅 Step 1。豁免資產唔係唔郁，係要按 HKAS 40 先重估至 FV，之後先埋單比較。
- 考試伏位：如果 investment property 用 **cost model** 就唔豁免——題目明寫 fair value model 先係答案。

**Rule Check:** HKFRS 5 para. 5；HKFRS 5 disposal-group three-step approach（LP Section 12.2.2, paras. 18–19）

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 以為「豁免 = 完全唔理」而跳過 remeasurement step；又或者見 goodwill 係特殊資產就以為佢豁免——goodwill 恰恰相反，係減值分配嘅頭號吸收者。HKFRS 5 measurement 連續多年被 panelist 點名為弱項，呢個 scope 分界位務必背熟。

### Q3 — Answer: (a) HK$2,200,000; impairment loss HK$200,000 (b) see journal below (c) Nil
**Model Answer (EN):**

(a) On classification as held for sale, the machine is measured at the **lower of carrying amount and fair value less costs to sell** (HKFRS 5 para. 15):
- Carrying amount: HK$2,400,000
- Fair value less costs to sell: HK$2,300,000 − HK$100,000 = **HK$2,200,000**
- Lower = HK$2,200,000 → **impairment loss of HK$200,000** (HK$2,400,000 − HK$2,200,000) recognised in profit or loss.

(b) Journal entry on 1 October 20X6:

```
Dr  Impairment loss (profit or loss)        200,000
    Cr  Machine (non-current asset held for sale)      200,000
```

(Reclassification entry, if presented separately: Dr Non-current asset held for sale 2,400,000 / Cr Property, plant and equipment 2,400,000, followed by the write-down above.)

(c) **Nil.** An entity shall not depreciate a non-current asset while it is classified as held for sale (HKFRS 5 para. 25). Depreciation ceases from the classification date of 1 October 20X6, so no depreciation is charged for October–December 20X6.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- (a) 留意 FVLCS 要**扣 costs to sell**：HK$2,300,000 − HK$100,000 = HK$2,200,000。好多人唔記得扣 HK$100,000，就會答 HK$2,300,000（無減值）—— 呢個係 panelist 年年鬧嘅 HKFRS 5 measurement 弱點。Costs to sell 只包 incremental costs directly attributable to disposal（legal fees、commissions），**唔包** finance costs 同 income tax（HKFRS 5 App A）。
- (b) Journal 要平衡：Dr impairment loss HK$200,000 / Cr machine HK$200,000。若題目要求先重列，再做多一組 reclassification entry（Dr NCA HFS / Cr PPE，各 HK$2.4m）。
- (c) 分類日起**停止折舊**：10–12 月三個月嘅 HK$20,000/月 一仙都唔計。題目畀 monthly depreciation 係誘你繼續計 HK$60,000 —— 錯。
- 考試伏位：「lower of」測試同「停折舊」係孖生兄弟，一觸發分類兩樣同時生效；唔好以為「賣唔出先停折舊」。

**Rule Check:** HKFRS 5 paras. 15, 25; HKFRS 5 App A (definitions of fair value and costs to sell)

**Common Trap（繁中）:** (1) 忘記扣 costs to sell，用裸 fair value 比較；(2) 繼續計折舊至出售日；(3) 將 costs to sell 當 expense 入 P&L 而唔係扣 FVLCS —— 三個錯誤都係真實考生常犯。

### Q4 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):**
B is correct. A discontinued operation is a component of an entity that has been disposed of **or is classified as held for sale**, and that represents a separate major line of business or geographical area of operations, is part of a single co-ordinated plan to dispose of such a line/area, or is a subsidiary acquired exclusively with a view to resale (HKFRS 5 para. 32). Falcon's entire European operations constitute a component (a CGU with clearly distinguishable operations and cash flows) representing a separate major geographical area, and it has been classified as held for sale — both limbs are satisfied.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- 兩層測試：(1) 係咪 component of an entity（operations & cash flows clearly distinguishable；held for use 時曾為 CGU）；(2) disposed of **或** classified as HFS，再加大三選一條件（major line/geographical area、co-ordinated plan、subsidiary acquired for resale）。
- A 錯：一間細分店唔代表 separate major line of business or geographical area —— 佢係 HFS，但**唔係** discontinued operation，佢嘅 remeasurement gain/loss 留喺 continuing operations（HKFRS 5 para. 37）。
- C 錯：用到報廢 = to be abandoned，carrying amount 透過 continuing use 收回，**永不**分類 HFS（HKFRS 5 para. 13），自然唔係 discontinued operation。
- D 錯：equity-method associate 唔係 component 被出售或分類 HFS；「accounted for using the equity method」從來唔係 discontinued operation 嘅條件。
- 考試伏位：「未賣出」唔緊要 —— classified as held for sale 已經足夠（para. 32 明確）；但純粹 HFS ≠ 自動 discontinued，仲要過 major line/geographical 嗰關。

**Rule Check:** HKFRS 5 paras. 31, 32; HKFRS 5 paras. 13, 37

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 兩個方向都會錯：(1) 以為一定要已經賣咗先係 discontinued operation；(2) 以為任何 held for sale 資產都係 discontinued operation。正確理解：HFS 係必要條件之一，但仲要係 separate major line of business / geographical area。

### Q5 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):**
B is correct. HKFRS 5 paras. 21–22: an entity shall recognise a gain for any subsequent increase in fair value less costs to sell, **but not in excess of the cumulative impairment loss** previously recognised (under HKFRS 5 or previously under HKAS 36). Workings:
- 30 June 20X6: impairment loss recognised = HK$1,800,000 − HK$1,500,000 = HK$300,000 (carrying amount written down to HK$1,500,000)
- 31 December 20X6: increase in FVLCS = HK$1,950,000 − HK$1,500,000 = HK$450,000
- Gain recognised = **HK$300,000** (capped at the cumulative impairment loss); carrying amount restored to HK$1,800,000. The remaining HK$150,000 of the increase is NOT recognised.

Journal entry: Dr Non-current asset held for sale HK$300,000 / Cr Gain on reversal of impairment loss (profit or loss) HK$300,000.

**解題思路（繁中）:**
- Reversal 上限 = 累計 impairment loss（呢度 HK$300,000）。呢個 cap 概念同 HKAS 36 一脈相承，「撇咗幾多，最多賺返幾多」。
- A (HK$450,000) 錯：呢個係 FVLCS 全數升幅，忽視咗 reversal ceiling —— 考試最常見錯誤。
- C (HK$150,000) 錯：呢個係「新 FVLCS 減返原本 carrying amount」（1,950,000 − 1,800,000），即係**唔應該確認**嗰部分，調轉咗嚟答。
- D (HK$0) 錯：HKFRS 5 明確容許 reversal（paras. 21–22），唔係一撇永撇；一撇永撇嘅係 **goodwill**（HKAS 36 下 goodwill impairment 永不 reversal），唔好撈亂。
- 考試伏位：題目個 FVLCS 升到高過原本 carrying amount（1,950,000 > 1,800,000）係刻意設計 —— 測你知唔知 cap 嘅存在。

**Rule Check:** HKFRS 5 paras. 21–22（reversal capped at cumulative impairment loss; no reversal to goodwill per HKAS 36）

**Common Trap（繁中）:** 混淆兩個 cap：HKFRS 5 reversal cap = 累計 impairment（HK$300,000），唔係原本 carrying amount 以上嘅全部升幅，亦唔係零。另外記住 goodwill 永遠唔 reversal —— 如果 disposal group 有 goodwill，reversal 只分畀其他屬 HKFRS 5 範圍嘅資產。
