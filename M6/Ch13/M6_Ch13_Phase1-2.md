# M6 Chapter 13: Accounting Policies, Changes in Accounting Estimates and Errors — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. **Distinguish** between accounting policies, accounting estimates and prior period errors in accordance with HKAS 8.
2. **Apply** the criteria in HKAS 8 for selecting and changing accounting policies, including mandatory and voluntary changes.
3. **Account for** a change in accounting policy retrospectively, a change in accounting estimate prospectively, and a material prior period error by retrospective restatement.
4. **Compute** the effect of accounting changes and error corrections on profit or loss, carrying amounts, tax and retained earnings.
5. **Prepare** the required note disclosures for changes in accounting policies, changes in accounting estimates and corrections of prior period errors.

### 必背考點

#### A. Recognition / Classification — 點樣分三類更改

**Definitions (HKAS 8 para. 5)**
- **Accounting policies**: the specific principles, bases, conventions, rules and practices applied by an entity in preparing and presenting financial statements. Example: choosing FIFO or weighted average cost formula under HKAS 2; cost model vs revaluation model under HKAS 16.
- **Accounting estimates**: monetary amounts in financial statements that are subject to measurement uncertainty (HKAS 8 para. 32A). Examples: loss allowance for expected credit losses; net realisable value of inventory; warranty provision; fair value of an asset or liability; depreciation expense.
- **Prior period errors**: omissions from, and misstatements in, the entity's financial statements for one or more prior periods arising from a **failure to use, or misuse of, reliable information** that (a) was available when those financial statements were authorised for issue, and (b) could reasonably be expected to have been obtained and taken into account. Examples: mathematical mistakes, mistakes in applying accounting policies, oversights or misinterpretations of facts, fraud.

**When may an accounting policy be changed? (HKAS 8 para. 14)** — Only if the change:
- is **required by a HKFRS** (mandatory); or
- results in the financial statements providing **reliable and more relevant information** (voluntary).

**NOT changes in accounting policy (HKAS 8 para. 16):**
- applying a policy for transactions/events/conditions that **differ in substance** from those previously occurring; and
- applying a **new** policy for transactions/events/conditions that **did not occur previously or were immaterial** (e.g. previously immaterial leases becoming material → apply HKFRS 16 going forward).

**Special case (HKAS 8 para. 17):** first-time adoption of the revaluation model under HKAS 16 / HKAS 38 is a change in accounting policy, BUT it is dealt with as a **revaluation under HKAS 16 / HKAS 38** — HKAS 8 paras. 19–31 do **not** apply.

**Estimate vs policy boundary rules:**
- A change in the **measurement basis** applied is a change in accounting policy, NOT a change in estimate (HKAS 8 para. 35; e.g. FIFO → weighted average).
- If it is **difficult to distinguish** a policy change from an estimate change → treat as a **change in accounting estimate** (HKAS 8 para. 35).
- Revising an estimate because of new information / more experience is NOT a correction of an error (HKAS 8 paras. 32–34): an estimate uses information available **at the time**; an error is failure to use / misuse of information that **was available**.

**Materiality (HKAS 8 paras. 5, 41; HKAS 1 definition):**
- HKFRS accounting policies need not be applied when the effect is **immaterial**.
- Financial statements do **not** comply with HKFRSs if they contain **material errors**.
- **Material** prior period errors must be corrected retrospectively in the **first set of financial statements authorised for issue after discovery**.

#### B. Measurement / Accounting Treatment — Retrospective vs Prospective

**Change in accounting policy (HKAS 8 paras. 19–24):**
- Mandatory change with transitional provisions in the HKFRS → follow those **transitional provisions** (retrospective or prospective as specified) (HKAS 8 para. 19(a)).
- Mandatory change **without** transitional provisions → apply **retrospectively** (HKAS 8 para. 19(b)).
- Voluntary change → apply **retrospectively** (HKAS 8 para. 19(b)).
- **Retrospective application** (HKAS 8 paras. 5, 22): apply the new policy as if it had **always been applied**; adjust (a) the **opening balance of each affected component of equity** (e.g. retained earnings) for the **earliest prior period presented**, and (b) the **other comparative amounts** disclosed for each prior period presented.
- Limitation: retrospective application only **to the extent practicable** (HKAS 8 paras. 23–24).

**Change in accounting estimate (HKAS 8 paras. 36–38):**
- Recognise **prospectively** — apply from the date of the change; never adjust prior periods.
- Effect goes to **profit or loss** in: (a) the **period of change only**, if it affects only that period (e.g. annual warranty / ECL allowance); or (b) the **period of change and future periods**, if it affects both (e.g. revised useful life → depreciation for current + remaining years).
- To the extent the change affects assets/liabilities/equity, adjust the **carrying amount** of the related item in the period of change (HKAS 8 para. 37).

**Correction of prior period error (HKAS 8 paras. 42–46):**
- Correct by **retrospective restatement** in the first set of financial statements issued after discovery:
  - restate the **comparative amounts** for the prior period(s) presented in which the error occurred; or
  - if the error occurred **before** the earliest prior period presented → restate the **opening balances of assets, liabilities and equity** for the earliest prior period presented.
- Purpose: current period financial statements are presented **as if the error never occurred**.
- Remember the **tax effect**: restate income tax expense/payable at the applicable rate (LP examples use HK profits tax rate 16.5%).

#### C. Disclosure Requirements

**Change in accounting policy — initial application of a HKFRS (HKAS 8 para. 28):**
- the **title** of the HKFRS;
- that the change is made in accordance with its **transitional provisions** (if any);
- the **nature** of the change;
- a **description of the transitional provisions** and those that might affect future periods (if applicable);
- for the **current period and each prior period presented**, to the extent practicable, the amount of the adjustment for **each financial statement line item affected** and (if HKAS 33 applies) for **basic and diluted EPS**;
- the amount of the adjustment relating to **periods before those presented**, to the extent practicable.

**New HKFRS issued but not yet effective (HKAS 8 para. 30):** disclose that fact + known or reasonably estimable information relevant to assessing the possible impact on initial application.

**Change in accounting policy — voluntary (HKAS 8 para. 29):**
- the **nature** of the change;
- the **reasons** why the new policy provides reliable and more relevant information;
- the same line-item / EPS adjustment amounts as above (current + each prior period presented; periods before those presented), to the extent practicable.

**Change in accounting estimate (HKAS 8 para. 39):**
- disclose the **nature and amount** of the change that has an effect in the current period or is expected to have an effect in future periods (if practicable to quantify the future effect).

**Correction of prior period error (HKAS 8 para. 49):**
- the **nature** of the prior period error;
- for **each prior period presented**, to the extent practicable, the amount of the correction for **each financial statement line item affected** and (if HKAS 33 applies) for **basic and diluted EPS**;
- the amount of the correction **at the beginning of the earliest prior period presented**.

**Timing rule for all three:** disclosures are given in the period of change / period the error is corrected; subsequent periods **need not repeat** the disclosures.

### 易混淆概念對比

**Table 1: Change in Accounting Policy vs Change in Accounting Estimate vs Prior Period Error**

| | Change in Accounting Policy | Change in Accounting Estimate | Prior Period Error |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nature | Change in principles, bases, conventions, rules, practices (e.g. FIFO → weighted average; cost model → fair value model) | Revision of a monetary amount subject to measurement uncertainty (e.g. useful life, warranty %, ECL allowance) | Omission/misstatement from failure to use or misuse of reliable info available at the time (maths mistakes, fraud, oversight) |
| Trigger | Required by HKFRS, or voluntary to give reliable + more relevant info (HKAS 8.14) | New information, change in circumstances, more experience (HKAS 8.32–34) | Discovered after prior FS authorised for issue |
| Treatment | Retrospective application (subject to transitional provisions / practicability) (HKAS 8.19, 22) | Prospective recognition in P&L — current period, or current + future periods (HKAS 8.36–38) | Retrospective restatement of comparatives / opening balances (HKAS 8.42) |
| Adjust opening retained earnings of earliest prior period presented? | Yes (cumulative pre-comparative effect) | **No** — never touch prior periods | Yes, if error occurred before earliest prior period presented |
| Disclosure | HKAS 8.28 / 8.29 (nature, title of HKFRS or reasons, line-item amounts) | HKAS 8.39 (nature and amount) | HKAS 8.49 (nature, line-item corrections, opening balance correction) |
| 點樣分辨 / 考試伏位 | 睇下係咪改「量度基礎/原則」：改 measurement basis = policy change（HKAS 8.35）。唔好見到「depreciation」就以為係 policy | Useful life、residual value、warranty %、ECL 呢啲 estimate revision 一律 prospective；考生最常犯錯係 restate 咗 comparatives（Jun 2025 panel report 指考生識計數但唔識講 prospective treatment） | 關鍵：資料當時「已經存在兼可合理取得」但冇用到 → error；如果係新資料先令估計改變 → estimate change，唔係 error |

**Table 2: Mandatory Change vs Voluntary Change in Accounting Policy**

| | Mandatory (initial application of a HKFRS) | Voluntary |
|---|---|---|
| Condition | Required by a HKFRS (HKAS 8.14(a)) | Provides reliable and more relevant information (HKAS 8.14(b)) |
| Treatment | Follow the HKFRS's **transitional provisions** if any; if none → retrospective (HKAS 8.19) | Retrospective application (HKAS 8.19(b)), to the extent practicable (HKAS 8.23–24) |
| Example | HKFRS 16 first adoption for former operating leases (transitional provisions HKFRS 16.C5–C10) | FIFO → weighted average; cost model → fair value model for investment property (HKAS 40) |
| Unique disclosure items | Title of HKFRS; description of transitional provisions; those affecting future periods (HKAS 8.28) | Reasons why the new policy gives reliable and more relevant information (HKAS 8.29) |
| Common disclosure items | Line-item + EPS adjustment amounts for current and each prior period presented; amounts for periods before those presented | Same |
| 考試伏位 | MCQ 成日問「all changes in policy 必須披露乜」：答案係 **每個受影響 line item 嘅調整金額**（title of HKFRS / reasons 只係各自類型先要）；記住特殊位：首次用 revaluation model 雖然係 policy change，但跟 HKAS 16/38 做 revaluation 處理，HKAS 8 paras. 19–31 唔適用 | 自願改 policy 一定要講得出點解「more relevant」；答 disclosure 題時 voluntary 冇 transitional provisions 可以寫，唔好照抄 mandatory 嘅 disclosure list |

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** How does HKAS 8 define "accounting policies", and give two examples of policy choices.

**Back:**
- Specific principles, bases, conventions, rules and practices applied by an entity in preparing and presenting financial statements (HKAS 8 para. 5).
- Examples: FIFO vs weighted average cost formula (HKAS 2); cost model vs revaluation model (HKAS 16 / HKAS 38); cost model vs fair value model for investment property (HKAS 40).

💬 白話解：Accounting policy 即係公司揀嘅「會計原則同做法」，例如存貨用 FIFO 定 weighted average。呢個係「揀邊把尺」嘅問題，唔係「把尺度出嚟幾多」嘅問題。

### Card 2
**Front:** Under HKAS 8 para. 14, when is an entity permitted to change an accounting policy?

**Back:**
Only if the change:
- is required by a HKFRS; or
- results in the financial statements providing reliable and more relevant information about the effects of transactions, other events or conditions on the entity's financial position, financial performance or cash flows.

💬 白話解：改 policy 唔可以話改就改——一係準則逼你改，一係你要證明改咗之後資料更相關可靠。隨意改嚟「調節盈利」係唔得嘅。

### Card 3
**Front:** Which two situations are NOT changes in accounting policy under HKAS 8 para. 16?

**Back:**
- Applying an accounting policy for transactions/events/conditions that differ in substance from those previously occurring.
- Applying a new accounting policy for transactions/events/conditions that did not occur previously or were immaterial (e.g. previously immaterial leases becoming material → apply HKFRS 16).

💬 白話解：新嘢第一次出現、或者以前唔重要依家先重要，咁樣用新政策唔算「改政策」，所以唔使 retrospective restate。考試鍾意用呢個做 distractor。

### Card 4
**Front:** Explain the mechanics of retrospective application of a change in accounting policy (HKAS 8 paras. 22–24).

**Back:**
- Apply the new policy as if it had always been applied.
- Adjust the opening balance of each affected component of equity (e.g. retained earnings) for the earliest prior period presented.
- Adjust the other comparative amounts disclosed for each prior period presented.
- Apply retrospectively only to the extent practicable.

💬 白話解：即係「扮成一向都係咁做」——最早呈報嗰年嘅期初 retained earnings 要調整，之後每年 comparative 都要重報。計唔到（impracticable）先可以豁免。

### Card 5
**Front:** How is a change in accounting estimate recognised, and how does the treatment differ between a one-period effect and a multi-period effect (HKAS 8 paras. 36–38)?

**Back:**
- Recognised prospectively from the date of the change; prior periods are never adjusted.
- Affects current period only (e.g. annual warranty or ECL allowance) → include in profit or loss of the period of change.
- Affects current and future periods (e.g. revised useful life) → include in profit or loss of the period of change and each future period (via revised depreciation).
- Related asset/liability/equity carrying amounts are adjusted in the period of change.

💬 白話解：估計改咗就「由今日起計」，唔准返轉頭改舊數。Warranty 呢類一年一估嘅只影響今年；useful life 呢類長命估計就影響今年加埋以後每年嘅折舊。

### Card 6
**Front:** A change in the measurement basis applied — policy change or estimate change? What if the two are difficult to distinguish?

**Back:**
- A change in the measurement basis applied is a change in an accounting policy, NOT a change in an accounting estimate (HKAS 8 para. 35) — e.g. FIFO → weighted average.
- If it is difficult to distinguish a change in accounting policy from a change in accounting estimate, the change is treated as a change in an accounting estimate.

💬 白話解：換「把尺」（measurement basis）= 改 policy；把尺度嘅「讀數」改咗 = 改 estimate。真係分唔到？HKAS 8 叫你當佢係 estimate change，即 prospective 處理。

### Card 7
**Front:** Define a prior period error and state how a material one is corrected (HKAS 8 paras. 5, 41–42).

**Back:**
- Omissions/misstatements in prior period financial statements from failure to use, or misuse of, reliable information that was available and could reasonably have been obtained when those statements were authorised for issue (e.g. mathematical mistakes, mistakes in applying policies, oversights, misinterpretation of facts, fraud).
- Material prior period errors are corrected retrospectively in the first set of financial statements authorised for issue after discovery: restate comparatives for the period(s) in which the error occurred, or restate opening balances of assets, liabilities and equity for the earliest prior period presented if the error occurred earlier.

💬 白話解：Error 同 estimate change 嘅分界在於「資料當時存唔存在」：當時已經有但你冇用/用錯 = error，要 retrospective restate；新資料先至改估計 = estimate change，prospective。

### Card 8
**Front:** List the disclosure requirements when correcting a material prior period error (HKAS 8 para. 49).

**Back:**
- The nature of the prior period error.
- For each prior period presented, to the extent practicable, the amount of the correction for each financial statement line item affected, and for basic and diluted EPS if HKAS 33 applies.
- The amount of the correction at the beginning of the earliest prior period presented.
- Disclose in the period the error is corrected; subsequent periods need not repeat.

💬 白話解：Error disclosure 三寶：「咩錯」（nature）、「每個受影響項目改咗幾多」（line-item amounts）、「最早嗰年期初改咗幾多」。只係發現嗰年披露一次，之後唔使重複。
