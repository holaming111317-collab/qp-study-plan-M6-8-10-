**Question 1**

In accordance with HKAS 24 (Revised) *Related Party Disclosures*, which of the following parties is **NOT** a related party of Falcon Limited, a Hong Kong trading company?

A. Kestrel Limited, a fellow subsidiary of Falcon Limited (both are wholly owned by the same parent company)
B. Ms. Wong, the spouse of Falcon Limited's chief executive officer
C. Merlin Limited, a company whose only connection with Falcon Limited is that the finance director of Falcon Limited also serves as a non-executive director of Merlin Limited
D. Osprey Limited, a joint venture of Falcon Limited over which Falcon Limited shares joint control with an unrelated investor

**Question 2**

A. The wife of Marble Limited's chief financial officer

C. Harbour Bank, which provides Marble Limited's mortgage financing in the ordinary course of its banking business

**Question 3**

Mango Limited owns 80% of the issued shares of Papaya Limited. Papaya Limited owns 30% of the issued shares of Lychee Limited and has significant influence over Lychee Limited. Mango Limited and Durian Limited each hold 50% of Rambutan Limited and share joint control of Rambutan Limited; Mango Limited and Durian Limited are otherwise unrelated.

In accordance with HKAS 24 (Revised), which of the following sets correctly identifies the related parties of **Papaya Limited**?

A. Mango Limited and Lychee Limited only
B. Mango Limited, Lychee Limited and Rambutan Limited
C. Mango Limited, Lychee Limited, Rambutan Limited and Durian Limited
D. Mango Limited only

**Question 4**

The board of directors of Otter Limited comprises the only key management personnel of the company. For the year ended 31 December 20X6, the following amounts were paid or granted:

| Item | HK$ |
|---|---|
| Directors' salaries | 2,400,000 |
| Directors' performance bonuses | 600,000 |
| Employer's pension contributions for directors | 180,000 |
| Termination payment to a retiring director | 250,000 |
| Fair value of share options granted to directors | 320,000 |
| Salaries of sales staff (not key management personnel) | 1,900,000 |
| Dividends paid to directors on their personal shareholdings | 90,000 |

In accordance with HKAS 24 (Revised), what is the **total key management personnel compensation** that Otter Limited must disclose for the year ended 31 December 20X6?

A. HK$3,430,000
B. HK$3,840,000
C. HK$5,650,000
D. HK$3,750,000

**Question 5**

B. The terms and conditions of the balance, including whether it is secured and the nature of the consideration to be provided in settlement
