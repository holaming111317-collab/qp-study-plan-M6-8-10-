# M6 Chapter 11: Impairment of Assets — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** Goodwill acquired in a business combination must be tested for impairment annually (through the CGU to which it is allocated), irrespective of whether any indication of impairment exists. The same annual requirement applies to intangible assets with an indefinite useful life and intangible assets not yet available for use. All other assets — including the delivery truck, the 15-year licence and the owner-occupied building — are tested only when an indication of impairment exists (assessed at the end of each reporting period).

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題考 HKAS 36.10 三類「年年必測」資產：indefinite-life intangibles、not-yet-available-for-use intangibles、goodwill。A、C、D 都係有明確年期嘅資產，只係有 impairment indication 先要測（HKAS 36.9）。記住 goodwill 唔可以單獨測，要經佢所屬 CGU 測。
**Rule Check:** HKAS 36.10; HKAS 36.9
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 見到 licence 就以為係 intangible 所以要年測——關鍵係 **indefinite useful life**；15-year licence 係 finite-life，要攤銷，唔使年年測。反向問法（"which does NOT require annual testing"）係真實考試常見包裝，睇清楚問題方向先好揀。

### Q2 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):**
Workings:
- Fair value less costs of disposal = HK$300,000 − HK$15,000 = **HK$285,000**
- Value in use = 120,000 × 0.926 + 110,000 × 0.857 + 100,000 × 0.794 = 111,120 + 94,270 + 79,400 = **HK$284,790**
- Recoverable amount = **higher** of FVLCD and VIU = HK$285,000
- Impairment loss = Carrying amount HK$350,000 − Recoverable amount HK$285,000 = **HK$65,000**, recognised immediately in profit or loss (cost model).

**解題思路（繁中）:** 三步曲：(1) FVLCD 記得扣 costs of disposal（285,000）；(2) VIU 要折現（284,790）；(3) recoverable amount 取**高者**——呢度 FVLCD 高少少，所以用 285,000。Distractor 解構：A（20,000）= 冇折現，直接用 330,000 總現金流當 VIU；B（50,000）= 忘記扣 disposal costs，用咗 fair value 300,000；D（65,210）= 用咗「lower of」攞咗 VIU 284,790，呢個係考官報告點名嘅真實錯誤。
**Rule Check:** HKAS 36.6; HKAS 36.59–60
**Common Trap（繁中）:** Recoverable amount 係 **higher of**，唔係 lower of——Jun 2023 panelists' report 特別點名呢個錯。另外 FVLCD 一定要扣 costs of disposal，唔好用 fair value 直接比較。

### Q3 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):**
An impairment loss on a revalued asset is treated as a revaluation decrease: it is recognised in OCI against the revaluation surplus **for that same asset** to the extent of the surplus balance (HK$30,000), and the excess (HK$80,000 − HK$30,000 = HK$50,000) is recognised in profit or loss.

| Account | Dr (HK$) | Cr (HK$) |
|---|---|---|
| Revaluation surplus (OCI) | 30,000 | |
| Impairment loss (P&L) | 50,000 | |
| Machine | | 80,000 |
| **Total** | **80,000** | **80,000** |

**解題思路（繁中）:** 重估模式資產減值 = downward revaluation：先衝同一資產嘅 revaluation surplus（上限係 surplus 餘額 30,000），多餘 50,000 先落 P&L。A 錯在全部落 P&L，無視咗 surplus；B 錯在全部落 OCI，surplus 唔夠 cover 嘅部分唔可以再扣其他儲備；D 嘅 Dr/Cr 方向完全倒轉，仲要虛增 surplus，係 nonsense。
**Rule Check:** HKAS 36.60–61
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 兩個伏位：(1) 以為 revalued asset 減值全部入 OCI——surplus 係有上限嘅，excess 必須落 P&L；(2) 忘記 surplus 必須係 **that same asset** 嘅 surplus，唔可以挪用其他資產嘅 surplus 抵銷。

### Q4 — Answer: A
**Model Answer (EN):**
Workings:
- Carrying amount of CGU = 200,000 + 500,000 + 300,000 = HK$1,000,000
- Impairment loss = HK$1,000,000 − HK$700,000 = HK$300,000
- Step 1: write off goodwill first → HK$200,000 (goodwill reduced to nil)
- Step 2: remaining HK$100,000 allocated pro rata to other assets by carrying amount:
  - Bottling machine = 500,000 / (500,000 + 300,000) × 100,000 = **HK$62,500**
  - Factory building = 300,000 / 800,000 × 100,000 = HK$37,500
- Floor check (HKAS 36.105): neither asset has a measurable FVLCD or VIU, so no floor constraint binds.

**解題思路（繁中）:** CGU 撇減次序係「goodwill 先死、其餘 pro rata」。Distractor 解構：B（50,000）= pro rata 嘅分母錯咗，用咗 1,000,000（連 goodwill）去攤剩餘嘅 100,000；C（150,000）= 完全冇先撇 goodwill，將成個 300,000 按 500/1,000 攤分；D（100,000）= 誤以為 goodwill 撇完之後嘅餘額全部落最大嗰件資產。LP Knowledge Check Q6/Q7 正正考呢兩個錯法。
**Rule Check:** HKAS 36.104; HKAS 36.105
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 最常見錯誤：冇將 goodwill 先撇到零就開始 pro rata。第二個伏位：pro rata 用 **carrying amount** 做權重，唔係 cost。第三：如果某資產有自己嘅 FVLCD/VIU，記住 floor 規則——唔可以撇低過 highest of FVLCD / VIU / zero，多咗嘅要再攤俾其他資產。

### Q5 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):**
Workings:
- Original depreciation = HK$1,800,000 ÷ 9 = HK$200,000 per year
- Carrying amount at 31 December 20X4 = 1,800,000 − (4 × 200,000) = HK$1,000,000
- Impairment loss 20X4 = 1,000,000 − 700,000 = HK$300,000; revised carrying amount = HK$700,000
- Revised depreciation 20X5 = 700,000 ÷ 5 remaining years = HK$140,000
- Carrying amount at 31 December 20X5 = 700,000 − 140,000 = HK$560,000
- Carrying amount had NO impairment occurred = 1,800,000 − (5 × 200,000) = HK$800,000 (the ceiling)
- Recoverable amount HK$950,000 exceeds the ceiling; reversal capped at 800,000 − 560,000 = **HK$240,000**, recognised in profit or loss (cost model).

**解題思路（繁中）:** Reversal 三條數要齊：(1) 而家賬面值（560,000）——記住減值後要用**新**折舊率 140,000；(2) recoverable amount（950,000）；(3) 上限 = 「冇減值世界」折舊後賬面值（800,000）。Reversal = min(RA, ceiling) − 現賬面值。Distractor 解構：A（100,000）= 忘記 20X5 要折舊，直接用 700,000 做賬面值；C（300,000）= 將當年嘅 impairment loss 全數撥返，衝穿咗上限；D（390,000）= 用 RA − CA（950,000 − 560,000），完全冇理會 ceiling。
**Rule Check:** HKAS 36.117; HKAS 36.119; HKAS 36.63
**Common Trap（繁中）:** Reversal 上限係「冇減值嘅折舊後賬面值」，**唔係** recoverable amount——RA 高過上限嘅部分永遠冇得入賬。另外減值之後一定要重計折舊（HKAS 36.63），再用重計後嘅賬面值去計 reversal。Jun 2023 考官明言考生「no awareness of the ceiling on impairment reversal」，呢題係必操題型。
