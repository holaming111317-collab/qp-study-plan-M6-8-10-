### Q1 (1 mark)

In accordance with HKAS 28 (2011) *Investments in Associates and Joint Ventures*, which of the following statements is correct?

A. Heron Limited holds 26% of the voting power of Wader Limited. Because its holding exceeds 20%, Wader Limited must be accounted for as an associate even though an administrator has been appointed to manage Wader Limited's affairs.

B. Significant influence is the power to direct the activities of another entity that significantly affect the investor's returns.

C. Kingfisher Limited holds 18% of the voting power of Tern Limited. Kingfisher Limited has the contractual right to appoint two of Tern Limited's seven directors and supplies the essential software platform on which Tern Limited's operations depend. Kingfisher Limited may have significant influence over Tern Limited.

D. Sandpiper Limited holds 30% of the voting power of Plover Limited and has the right to appoint six of the eight directors on Plover Limited's board. Plover Limited is an associate of Sandpiper Limited.

### Q2 (1 mark)

In accordance with HKAS 28, how should Avocet Limited account for the dividend received in the financial statements in which the investment is equity accounted?

### Q3 (1 mark)
