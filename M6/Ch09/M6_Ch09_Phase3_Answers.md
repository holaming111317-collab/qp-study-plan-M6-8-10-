# M6 Chapter 9: Intangible Assets — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):**
A ferry operating licence **purchased from another company for HK$1,500,000** is a separately acquired intangible asset. It is identifiable (it arises from contractual/legal rights and is separable), the entity controls the benefits, and the agreed purchase price demonstrates both probable future economic benefits and reliable measurement of cost. It is recognised at cost (HKAS 38.21, 38.24, 38.27).
- **A (incorrect):** an internally generated customer list is explicitly prohibited from recognition (HKAS 38.63) — the expenditure cannot be distinguished from the cost of developing the business as a whole.
- **C (incorrect):** training expenditure fails the **control** criterion — the entity cannot prevent trained staff from leaving (HKAS 38.13, 38.15–16); it is expensed.
- **D (incorrect):** a self-built reputation is **internally generated goodwill**, which shall not be recognised (HKAS 38.48–49); it is not an identifiable resource measurable reliably at cost.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢題考「三大屬性＋禁止清單」。正解 B 係 separately acquired：有價有交易，兩個 recognition tests 自動過。A 係內部產生客戶名單（HKAS 38.63 明令禁止）；C 培訓支出過唔到 control test（員工隨時走）；D 聲譽 = internally generated goodwill，永遠唔入賬。記住 goodwill 只可以喺 business combination「買返嚟」先確認（HKFRS 3）。
**Rule Check:** HKAS 38.8, 38.12–13, 38.21, 38.24, 38.27, 38.48–49, 38.63
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 以為「花咗錢、有價值」就係資產——training 同 reputation 都創造價值，但分別敗喺 control 同 identifiability/reliable measurement。考試見到 internally generated brands / customer lists / mastheads / publishing titles 即刻排除。

### Q2 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):**
**The design, construction and testing of a pre-production prototype** is a development-phase activity — it is the *application* of research findings to a plan or design for a new or substantially improved product before commercial production or use (HKAS 38.8).
- **A (incorrect):** the search for and evaluation of alternatives for materials/compounds is a **research** activity (HKAS 38.8).
- **B (incorrect):** activities aimed at obtaining **new scientific knowledge** fall within the definition of research.
- **D (incorrect):** the formulation, evaluation and final selection of **possible applications of research findings** is a research activity (HKAS 38.8).

**解題思路（繁中）:** Research = 搵新知識、搵 alternatives、評估同揀選研究方向；Development = 將研究成果「落地」——做 prototype、design tools/jigs/moulds、pilot plant（非商業規模）。呢題四個選項全部係 HKAS 38.8 嘅標準例子，考你有冇背熟邊啲歸 research、邊啲歸 development。
**Rule Check:** HKAS 38.8 (definitions of research and development)
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 「evaluation and final selection of applications of research findings」聽落好後期、似 development，其實 LP 明確歸入 research。仲有 pilot plant 要「not of an economically feasible scale for commercial production」先係 development activity。

### Q3 — Answer: C (HK$1,937,500)
**Model Answer (EN):**
Workings:
- Cost of separately acquired licence = purchase price 1,800,000 + legal fees (directly attributable) 120,000 + non-refundable purchase tax 80,000 = **HK$2,000,000** (HKAS 38.27–28).
- Useful life = **shorter of** legal term (10 years) and expected benefit period (8 years) = **8 years** (HKAS 38.94).
- Residual value = **nil** (no third-party commitment; no active market) (HKAS 38.100).
- Amortisation for 3 months (1 Oct – 31 Dec 20X5), straight-line (pattern not determinable): 2,000,000 / 8 × 3/12 = **HK$62,500** (HKAS 38.97).
- Carrying amount = 2,000,000 − 62,500 = **HK$1,937,500** (cost model, HKAS 38.74).

Distractor analysis:
- **A (HK$1,743,750):** wrongly excludes legal fees and the non-refundable tax from cost (1,800,000 − 1,800,000/8 × 3/12 = 1,800,000 − 56,250).
- **B (HK$1,750,000):** wrongly charges a **full year** of amortisation (2,000,000 − 250,000) despite the 1 October acquisition.
- **D (HK$1,950,000):** wrongly uses the **10-year legal life** (2,000,000 − 2,000,000/10 × 3/12 = 2,000,000 − 50,000).

**解題思路（繁中）:** 三步曲：①砌 cost——purchase price＋directly attributable costs（legal fees 入；non-refundable tax 入）；②揀 useful life——合約/法律年期同預期受益期 **取短**（8 年）；③攤銷要 **pro-rate**（年中買入，月攤），residual value 預設零。三個 distractor 分別對應三個典型錯誤：漏咗直接歸屬成本、唔 pro-rate、用錯 legal life。
**Rule Check:** HKAS 38.27–28, 38.74, 38.94, 38.97, 38.100
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 考試最鍾意「legal life 長過經濟效益期」嘅伏位——記住 contractual/legal rights 產生嘅無形資產，useful life 係 **shorter of** 兩者。另外 intangible asset 嘅 residual value **預設係零**，唔好自己加返個殘值。

### Q4 — Answer: A
**Model Answer (EN):**
Development-phase expenditure is capitalised because **all six** HKAS 38.57 criteria were demonstrated:

| | Dr HK$ | Cr HK$ |
|---|---:|---:|
| Intangible asset — capitalised development costs | 480,000 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cash | | 480,000 |

Amortisation commences when the asset is **available for use** (1 July 20X5), straight-line over 4 years, nil residual value: 480,000 / 4 × 6/12 = **HK$60,000** (HKAS 38.97):

| | Dr HK$ | Cr HK$ |
|---|---:|---:|
| Amortisation expense (P&L) | 60,000 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Accumulated amortisation | | 60,000 |

- **B (incorrect):** charges a **full year** of amortisation (120,000) even though the software was available for use for only 6 months.
- **C (incorrect):** expenses the development costs — that is the **research-phase** treatment (HKAS 38.54); once all HKAS 38.57 criteria are met, capitalisation is required.
- **D (incorrect):** no amortisation — that treatment applies only to **indefinite-life** intangibles (HKAS 38.107); a 4-year useful life is finite.

**解題思路（繁中）:** 兩張分錄：①資本化（Dr Intangible / Cr Cash）——六個條件齊就必須 capitalise，唔係選擇；②攤銷由 **available for use** 日期起計，年中可用就 pro-rate（6/12 = 60,000）。B 係全年攤銷錯誤；C 將 development 當 research 費用化；D 錯用 indefinite-life 處理。分錄題記住：amortisation expense 入 P&L，credit 落 **Accumulated amortisation**。
**Rule Check:** HKAS 38.57, 38.97, 38.99, 38.100, 38.107
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 攤銷起點係「available for use」（可以按管理層意圖運作）嗰日，唔係年頭、唔係真正開始賣嗰日。年中可用 → 按月 pro-rate，係 QP 最常見嘅計分位。

### Q5 — Answer: A (HK$760,000)
**Model Answer (EN):**
Workings:
- **Capitalised:** only the **prototype design/construction/testing** costs incurred **from the date all six HKAS 38.57 criteria were first met** (1 May 20X6) = **HK$800,000**.
- **Expensed as incurred:**
  - Market research into demand → **research phase** (obtaining new knowledge/evaluating applications) → expense (HKAS 38.54);
  - Staff training → fails control/identifiability → expense (HKAS 38.13; training costs are not eligible, HKAS 38.29);
  - Advertising/launch campaign → promotional expenditure → expense.
- Amortisation from date **available for use** (1 Oct 20X6) to year end: 800,000 / 5 × 3/12 = **HK$40,000** (HKAS 38.97).
- Carrying amount = 800,000 − 40,000 = **HK$760,000**.

Distractor analysis:
- **B (HK$800,000):** capitalised correctly but **no amortisation charged** (wrongly treated as indefinite life).
- **C (HK$855,000):** wrongly capitalises **training costs** as well (900,000 − 900,000/5 × 3/12 = 900,000 − 45,000).
- **D (HK$1,235,000):** wrongly capitalises the **research-phase market research** too ((800,000 + 500,000) − 1,300,000/5 × 3/12 = 1,300,000 − 65,000).

**解題思路（繁中）:** 呢類 multi-item 題目要逐項過篩：market research（研究需求）係 research phase → expense；prototype 喺六條件達成後先 capitalise；training 同 advertising 無論點都係 expense（training 過唔到 control，advertising 係推廣支出）。資本化之後仲要記得由 available-for-use 日起 **pro-rate 攤銷 3 個月**。四個選項分別對應：正確、唔攤銷、連 training 都 capitalise、連 research 都 capitalise。
**Rule Check:** HKAS 38.54, 38.57, 38.13, 38.29, 38.97
**Common Trap（繁中）:** ①研究階段同開發階段分唔清——「market research into demand」係典型 research；②以為 training/advertising 係「令產品用得返」嘅直接成本——佢哋唔係 directly attributable 嘅 preparation costs，要費用化；③ capitalise 咗就唔記得攤銷。Jun 2025 panel report 特別點名考生對 internally generated brands 同 training costs 處理薄弱，呢啲正係出題熱點。
