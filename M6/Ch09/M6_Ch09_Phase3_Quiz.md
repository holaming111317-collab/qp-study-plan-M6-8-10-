**Question 3**

The licence has a legal term of 10 years, but management expects the licence to generate economic benefits for only 8 years. There is no active market for the licence and no third party has committed to purchase it. Coral Ferry Limited adopts the cost model, amortises on a straight-line basis (monthly) and has a 31 December year end.

In accordance with HKAS 38, what is the carrying amount of the licence in Coral Ferry Limited's statement of financial position as at 31 December 20X5?

A. HK$1,743,750

B. HK$1,750,000

C. HK$1,937,500

D. HK$1,950,000

**Question 5**

In accordance with HKAS 38, what is the **carrying amount** of the intangible asset in Phoenix MedTech Limited's statement of financial position as at 31 December 20X6?
