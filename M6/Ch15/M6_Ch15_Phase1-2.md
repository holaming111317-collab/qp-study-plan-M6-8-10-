# M6 Chapter 15: Provisions, Contingent Liabilities and Contingent Assets — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives

1. **Distinguish** provisions from other liabilities (trade payables, accruals) and from contingent liabilities in accordance with HKAS 37.
2. **Apply** the recognition criteria in HKAS 37 para. 14 to determine whether a provision, a contingent liability, or no liability arises.
3. **Measure** provisions at the best estimate of the expenditure required to settle the obligation, applying the expected value method and discounting to present value where the time value of money is material.
4. **Determine** the accounting treatment for future operating losses, onerous contracts, restructuring costs, and decommissioning/restoration costs.
5. **Prepare** the note disclosures for provisions, contingent liabilities and contingent assets required by HKAS 37 paras. 84–86 and 89.

### 必背考點

#### A. Recognition（確認）

**Provision — HKAS 37.14：三個條件必須全部符合**
1. **Present obligation (legal or constructive) as a result of a past event** — the obligating event (HKAS 37.17: the entity has *no realistic alternative* to settling the obligation)
2. **Probable outflow of resources embodying economic benefits** — "probable" = *more likely than not* (>50%) (HKAS 37.23)
3. **Reliable estimate** of the amount of the obligation can be made

- **Legal obligation**（HKAS 37.15–16）：源自 contract、legislation 或 other operation of law（例：warranty agreement、legal requirement to clean up contaminated land）
- **Constructive obligation**（HKAS 37.15–16）：established pattern of past practice / published policies + valid expectation created in other parties（例：retail store published refund policy with no legal requirement）
- **罕有情況（lawsuit）**（HKAS 37.15–16）：judgement at the end of the reporting period based on all available evidence (incl. expert opinions) — *more likely than not* a present obligation exists → condition 1 satisfied
- **Board / management decision**（HKAS 37.20）：只有在 communicated to those affected in a sufficiently specific manner 先至係 obligating event；未公佈前仍可撤回 → no provision
- **Environmental damage, no legal requirement**（HKAS 37.21）：即時無 obligation；除非 law enacted (retrospectively) 或 entity publicly accepts responsibility（→ constructive obligation）
- **Proposed new law**（HKAS 37.22）：present obligation 只喺 enactment is *virtually certain* 時出現
- **Intention to incur future expenditure**（HKAS 37.19）：NOT a past event — entity can avoid it by future actions → no provision（例：regular overhaul policy）

**Probability thresholds — liabilities（HKAS 37.23, 27–28）**

| Likelihood of outflow | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Probable (>50%, more likely than not) + reliable estimate | Recognise **provision** |
| Probable but cannot be reliably measured | **Contingent liability** — disclose (rare) |
| Possible (not probable, but more than remote) | **Contingent liability** — disclose in notes |
| Remote | Do nothing — no recognition, no disclosure |

**Contingent liability（HKAS 37.10）** = (a) possible obligation confirmed only by uncertain future events not wholly within entity's control; 或 (b) present obligation failing recognition because outflow not probable (10(b)(i)) 或 cannot be reliably measured (10(b)(ii))。永遠 **not recognised**（HKAS 37.27）；disclose unless remote（HKAS 37.28）。

**Contingent asset（HKAS 37.10, 31–34）**

| Likelihood of inflow | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Virtually certain | **Recognise asset**（no longer contingent — HKAS 37.33） |
| Probable (but not virtually certain) | Disclose **contingent asset** in notes（HKAS 37.34） |
| Not probable | No recognition, no disclosure（HKAS 37.31） |

⚠️ **Threshold asymmetry**：liabilities 喺 *probable* 時已經要入賬；assets 要等到 *virtually certain* 先入賬。呢個係防範提前確認收入嘅 prudence 設計，考試必考。

**Specific applications — Recognition**
- **Future operating losses**（HKAS 37.63）：**never** recognise a provision — no obligating event, management has realistic alternatives. 但係可能係 impairment indicator → HKAS 36（HKAS 37.65）
- **Onerous contracts**（HKAS 37.66）：unavoidable costs of meeting obligations > economic benefits expected → present obligation **recognised as a provision**
- **Restructuring**（HKAS 37.70, 72）：recognise only when BOTH (a) **detailed formal plan** (business concerned, locations, employees, expenditures, implementation timing — HKAS 37.72(a)) AND (b) **valid expectation raised** in those affected by starting implementation OR announcing main features (HKAS 37.72(b)–73). Board approval alone is **NOT** enough.
- **Decommissioning / restoration**（HKAS 37 + HKAS 16）：recognise provision at best estimate; PV included in the **cost of PPE**（not expensed）

#### B. Measurement（計量）

- **Best estimate**（HKAS 37.36–37）：the amount the entity would *rationally pay to settle* the obligation at the end of the reporting period or *to transfer it to a third party*
- **Large population of similar obligations**（HKAS 37.39）：**expected value** method — weight all possible outcomes by probabilities; if each outcome equally likely, use the mid-point
- **Single obligation**（HKAS 37.40）：**most likely outcome** may be best estimate, BUT must consider other possible outcomes — if other outcomes are mostly higher (lower), best estimate is a higher (lower) amount
- **Discounting**（HKAS 37.45–46）：where the effect of the time value of money is **material**, provision = **present value** of expected expenditures. LP 實務指引：settlement > 12 months after reporting date → discount
- **Unwinding of discount**（HKAS 37.60）：increase in provision due to passage of time → **interest expense**（a borrowing cost），NOT added to PPE
- **Onerous contract measurement**（HKAS 37.68–68A）：unavoidable costs = **lower of** (i) cost of fulfilling the contract AND (ii) compensation/penalties from failure to fulfil。Cost of fulfilling = incremental costs (direct labour, materials) + allocation of other directly related costs (e.g. depreciation on PPE used)
- **Restructuring measurement**（HKAS 37.80–84）：
  - **Include**: only **direct expenditures** necessarily entailed by the restructuring AND not associated with ongoing activities（e.g. redundancy payments, lease termination costs）
  - **Exclude**: retraining/relocating **continuing** staff; marketing; investment in new systems and distribution networks; future operating losses (unless onerous contract); gains on expected disposal of assets

#### C. Disclosure（披露）

**Provisions — for each class**（HKAS 37.84–85）：
| Para | Disclosure |
|---|---|
| 84(a) | Carrying amount at beginning and end of period |
| 84(b) | Additional provisions made, incl. increases to existing provisions |
| 84(c) | Amounts used (charged against the provision) during the period |
| 84(d) | Unused amounts reversed during the period |
| 84(e) | Increase in discounted amount from passage of time / change in discount rate |
| 85(a) | Brief description of nature of obligation + expected timing of outflows |
| 85(b) | Uncertainties about amount/timing; major assumptions on future events |
| 85(c) | Expected reimbursements + any asset recognised for them |

- Comparative information **not required** for para. 84 items

**Contingent liabilities**（HKAS 37.86, disclose unless remote）：brief description of nature + where practicable (a) estimate of financial effect, (b) uncertainties re amount/timing, (c) possibility of reimbursement

**Contingent assets**（HKAS 37.89, disclose when probable）：brief description of nature + where practicable an estimate of financial effect

**通用豁免**：impracticable to disclose → disclose that fact (HKAS 37.91)；disclosure would *seriously prejudice* entity's position in a dispute → disclose general nature of dispute + fact and reason for non-disclosure (HKAS 37.92)

### 易混淆概念對比

**Table 1: Provision vs Contingent Liability（probability thresholds）**

| | Provision | Contingent Liability |
|---|---|---|
| Nature of obligation | **Present** obligation from past obligating event | **Possible** obligation; 或 present obligation 但唔符合 recognition criteria |
| Probability of outflow | Probable (more likely than not, >50%) | Not probable (但 more than remote)；或 outflow probable 但 cannot be reliably measured |
| Recognised in SFP? | **Yes** — recognised as liability | **No** — never recognised (HKAS 37.27) |
| Disclosure | Full movement reconciliation per class (HKAS 37.84–85) | Notes disclosure **unless remote** (HKAS 37.28, 86) |
| 點樣分辨 | 問自己：係咪 present obligation + probable + reliable estimate？三個 YES 先入賬 | 任何一個 criteria fail（但仍 possible / more than remote）→ 只披露；**remote** → 咩都唔使做 |
| 考試伏位 | Lawsuit 判斷：lawyers 話 *likely to lose* → provision；*unlikely (but not remote)* → contingent liability 披露；*remote* → 唔披露 | 唔好見到 "present obligation" 就入賬 — 仲要 check probable + reliable estimate！可靠估計做唔到都係 contingent liability |

**Table 2: Contingent Liability vs Contingent Asset（threshold asymmetry）**

| | Contingent Liability | Contingent Asset |
|---|---|---|
| Definition (HKAS 37.10) | Possible **obligation** from past events, confirmed by uncertain future events outside entity's control | Possible **asset** from past events, confirmed by uncertain future events outside entity's control |
| Recognised in SFP? | Never | Never — **除非 inflow 係 virtually certain** → 變成真正的 asset 入賬 (HKAS 37.33) |
| Disclosure threshold | Disclose unless outflow is **remote** (HKAS 37.28) | Disclose only when inflow is **probable** (HKAS 37.34) |
| Recognition threshold | 升到 **probable** + reliably measurable → 變 provision 入賬 (HKAS 37.30) | 升到 **virtually certain** → 變 asset (receivable) 入賬 (HKAS 37.33) |
| 點樣分辨 | Liability 門檻低啲：more than remote 就要披露 | Asset 門檻高好多：probable 只係披露；virtually certain 先入賬 — 防範確認可能永遠收唔到嘅 income |
| 考試伏位 | "Remote" 無定義，靠 professional judgement — 題目俾 lawyers' advice 字眼要捉實 | 最經典錯誤：probable inflow 就急住 recognise receivable — 錯！**probable ≠ virtually certain**，只可以披露 |

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** Define a "provision" under HKAS 37 and explain how it differs from other liabilities such as trade payables and accruals.

**Back:**
- A provision is a **liability of uncertain timing or amount** (HKAS 37.10/37.11)
- Trade payables: goods/services received and invoiced or formally agreed — low uncertainty
- Accruals: goods/services received but not yet invoiced/formally agreed — medium uncertainty
- Provisions: amount and/or timing of settlement is genuinely uncertain (e.g. warranty claims) — high uncertainty → presented separately from payables and accruals in the SFP

💬 白話解釋：三樣都係負債，分別只在於「唔確定性」有幾高。收咗貨等找數（trade payable）同埋未收單嘅 accrual 金額時間都好肯定；provision 係你知道遲早要俾錢，但唔知幾時俾、俾幾多，所以要獨立一欄披露。

### Card 2
**Front:** State the THREE recognition criteria for a provision under HKAS 37.14. Must all of them be met?

**Back:**
- (1) A **present obligation** (legal or constructive) as a result of a **past event** (obligating event)
- (2) It is **probable** (more likely than not, >50%) that an outflow of resources embodying economic benefits will be required to settle the obligation (HKAS 37.23)
- (3) A **reliable estimate** can be made of the amount of the obligation
- **ALL THREE must be met**; if any fails → no provision (but a contingent liability may exist)

💬 白話解釋：記口訣「 obligation–probable–estimate」三環扣。少一個都唔可以入賬，最多做 contingent liability 喺 notes 披露。

### Card 3
**Front:** Distinguish a legal obligation from a constructive obligation under HKAS 37, giving one example of each.

**Back:**
- **Legal obligation**: arises from a contract, legislation, or other operation of law — e.g. warranty agreement issued at time of sale; legislative requirement to clean up contaminated land (HKAS 37.15–16)
- **Constructive obligation**: arises from an **established pattern of past practice or published policies** indicating the entity will accept certain responsibilities, **AND** the entity has created a **valid expectation** in other parties that it will discharge them — e.g. retail store with published refund policy despite no legal requirement

💬 白話解釋：Legal = 白紙黑字法律合約迫你做；constructive = 你一直以來嘅做法或公開承諾令到人哋合理預期你會做，你冇 realistic alternative 甩身。兩者都符合 provision 第一個條件。

### Card 4
**Front:** What is a contingent liability under HKAS 37.10, and what is its accounting treatment (HKAS 37.27–28)?

**Back:**
- Definition: (a) a **possible obligation** from past events, confirmed only by occurrence/non-occurrence of uncertain future events not wholly within the entity's control; OR (b) a **present obligation** not recognised because outflow is not probable or amount cannot be reliably measured
- Treatment: **never recognised** in the financial statements (HKAS 37.27)
- **Disclose** in the notes **unless the possibility of outflow is remote** (HKAS 37.28); if remote → no disclosure at all

💬 白話解釋：Contingent liability 永遠唔上 SFP。問自己 outflow 係咪 remote：唔係 remote 就要喺 notes 講（nature、financial effect、uncertainties）；remote 就當冇事發生。

### Card 5
**Front:** State the accounting treatment of a contingent asset at each likelihood level of inflow (HKAS 37.31–34).

**Back:**
- **Virtually certain** → inflow is no longer contingent: **recognise the asset** (e.g. receivable) in the SFP (HKAS 37.33)
- **Probable** (but not virtually certain) → **do not recognise**; **disclose** contingent asset in notes — brief description of nature + where practicable estimate of financial effect (HKAS 37.34, 89)
- **Not probable** → **no recognition, no disclosure** (HKAS 37.31)

💬 白話解釋：記住資產門檻高過負債：負債 probable 就入賬；資產要 virtually certain 先入賬，probable 只係披露。原因係避免確認可能永遠收唔到嘅 income。

### Card 6
**Front:** How is the "best estimate" of a provision measured under HKAS 37.36–40 for (a) a large population of similar obligations and (b) a single obligation?

**Back:**
- Best estimate = amount the entity would **rationally pay to settle** the obligation at the end of the reporting period or to transfer it to a third party (HKAS 37.36–37)
- (a) **Large population** (e.g. product warranties): **expected value** — weight all possible outcomes by probabilities; mid-point if outcomes equally likely (HKAS 37.39)
- (b) **Single obligation** (e.g. rectifying a fault in one building): **most likely outcome**, but consider other possible outcomes — if they are mostly higher (lower), best estimate is a higher (lower) amount (HKAS 37.40)

💬 白話解釋：一大堆同類嘢（賣咗十萬件貨嘅 warranty）用期望值 probability-weighted；單一事件（一幢樓維修）用最可能結果，但其他結果普遍偏高時要向上調。

### Card 7
**Front:** When must a provision be discounted to present value, and how is the subsequent unwinding of the discount accounted for (HKAS 37.45–46, 60)?

**Back:**
- Discount when the effect of the **time value of money is material** — provision = **present value** of expenditures expected to settle the obligation (HKAS 37.45–46). LP working guide: settlement more than **12 months** after reporting date → discount
- The increase in the provision due to the **passage of time** (unwinding) is recognised as **interest expense** (a borrowing cost) in P&L (HKAS 37.60): Dr Interest expense / Cr Provision
- For decommissioning provisions, the initial PV is capitalised into the **cost of PPE** under HKAS 16 — but unwinding is **never** added to PPE

💬 白話解釋：十年後先使嘅 500 萬唔值今日嘅 500 萬，所以要折現。之後每年個 provision 會「滾大」返向面值，滾大嗰部分入 interest expense，唔係加落 PPE 成本。

### Card 8
**Front:** State the recognition criteria (HKAS 37.72) and measurement rule (HKAS 37.80–84) for a restructuring provision.

**Back:**
- Recognition — BOTH required: (a) a **detailed formal plan** (business/part concerned, principal locations, employees to be compensated, expenditures, implementation timing); AND (b) a **valid expectation raised** in those affected — by starting to implement the plan or announcing its main features (HKAS 37.72–73). Board approval alone is **not** sufficient
- Measurement — include only **direct expenditures** necessarily entailed by the restructuring and not associated with ongoing activities (e.g. redundancy payments, lease termination costs)
- **Exclude**: retraining/relocating continuing staff; marketing; new systems and distribution networks; future operating losses (unless onerous contract); gains on expected disposal of assets

💬 白話解釋：董事會齋拍板唔算數 — 要公佈咗俾受影響嘅人知，令佢哋有 valid expectation 先可以確認。計數只計「直接、必要、同持續經營無關」嘅開支；retraining、marketing、新系統呢啲係將來生意嘅嘢，全部唔計。
