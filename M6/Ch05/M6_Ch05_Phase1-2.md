# M6 Chapter 5: Property, Plant and Equipment — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. **Apply** the recognition criteria of HKAS 16 to initial and subsequent expenditure on property, plant and equipment (PPE).
2. **Determine** the cost of an item of PPE at initial recognition, including directly attributable costs and dismantling/restoration obligations.
3. **Account for** PPE after recognition under both the cost model and the revaluation model, including depreciation, impairment and revaluation journal entries.
4. **Account for** the derecognition of PPE, including computation of the gain or loss on disposal and the treatment of any related revaluation surplus.
5. **Prepare** the disclosures required by HKAS 16 for each class of PPE.

### 必背考點

#### A. Recognition（確認）
- **HKAS 16.6**：PPE = tangible items held for use in production/supply of goods or services, for rental to others, or for administrative purposes, **expected to be used during more than one period**。
- **HKAS 16.7**：確認條件（兩個都要滿足）：
  1. it is **probable** that future economic benefits will flow to the entity; and
  2. the **cost can be measured reliably**。
- 唔符合條件 → 即時 **expense**（實務上好多公司設 monetary threshold，低過門檻直接費用化）。
- **HKAS 16.12**：day-to-day servicing / repairs and maintenance → **profit or loss as incurred**（只係維持原有 capacity，唔係提升）。
- Subsequent costs：**提升 capacity／延長 useful life／提高產出質量或數量** → capitalise；**只係維持** → expense。Component approach：重大部件（如飛機引擎）當獨立資產逐件折舊及重置資本化。

#### B. Measurement at Recognition（初始計量）
- **HKAS 16.15**：initially measured at **cost**。
- **HKAS 16.16**：Cost 包括三大元素：
  1. **Purchase price**（含 import duties 及 non-refundable purchase taxes，扣除 trade discounts 及 rebates）；
  2. **Directly attributable costs**（HKAS 16.17）：employee benefits from construction/acquisition、site preparation (16.17(b))、initial delivery and handling、installation and assembly、testing costs、professional fees；qualifying asset 嘅 borrowing costs 按 **HKAS 23** 資本化；
  3. **Dismantling/removal/restoration obligations**（HKAS 16.16(c) 及 16.18）：initial estimate 按 **HKAS 37** 計量，**capitalise 入資產成本**（Dr Asset / Cr Provision），唔係即時費用化。
- **HKAS 16.19–20**：**不得**資本化嘅成本：opening new facility (16.19(a))、introducing new product（廣告）、conducting business in new location、staff training、initial operating losses、abnormal wastage、administration and general overheads、relocation/reorganisation costs、閒置或未達產能時嘅成本。
- **HKAS 16.20A**：資產未 ready 前測試產出嘅樣本，**proceeds 及成本都入 profit or loss**（唔准扣減資產成本）。
- **HKAS 16.23**：cost = **cash price equivalent**；deferred payment 超出正常信貸期，差額作 interest（除非按 HKAS 23 資本化）。
- Asset exchange：以**取得資產嘅 fair value** 計量（等於付出代價 FV + 現金）；差額認 gain/loss。
- Self-constructed asset（HKAS 16.22）：原則同外購一樣。

#### C. Measurement After Recognition（後續計量）
- **HKAS 16.29**：accounting policy choice — **cost model 或 revaluation model**，按 **class**（同類同性質同用途，HKAS 16.37）一致應用。

**Cost model（HKAS 16.30）**：cost − accumulated depreciation − accumulated impairment losses。

**Revaluation model（HKAS 16.31）**：**fair value at revaluation date** − subsequent accumulated depreciation − subsequent impairment losses；只適用於 FV 能可靠計量嘅資產。
- **HKAS 16.36**：重估一項 → **成個 class 都要重估**（唔准揀嚟重估）。
- **HKAS 16.38**：revaluations 要 with **sufficient regularity**，確保 carrying amount 與 FV 無重大差異。
- **HKAS 16.39**：重估**增值** → credit **OCI（revaluation surplus）**；例外：如屬逆轉同一資產先前喺 P&L 確認嘅減值 → 嗰部分入 **P&L**。
- **HKAS 16.40**：重估**減值** → **P&L**；例外：先用盡同一資產 revaluation surplus 嘅 credit balance（debit OCI），**超出部分先入 P&L**。
- **HKAS 16.35**：重估調整兩法（policy choice）：
  - **Net method**：eliminate accumulated depreciation against gross carrying amount，再將 carrying amount 調至 FV；
  - **Gross method**：gross carrying amount 按比例重列，accumulated depreciation 調整至等於 gross 與 revalued carrying amount 之差。
- **HKAS 16.41**：revaluation surplus 可**直接**轉 retained earnings（**唔經 P&L**）：(i) 隨資產使用，按「重估後折舊 − 原成本折舊」之差逐期轉；及/或 (ii) derecognition 時全數轉。
- Impairment（HKAS 16.63 → **HKAS 36**）：carrying amount > recoverable amount 即減值；revalued asset 嘅 impairment loss 當 **revaluation decrease** 處理（HKAS 16.39–40 規則）；reversal 當 revaluation increase。

#### D. Depreciation（折舊）
- **HKAS 16.6, 16.50**：depreciation = systematic allocation of **depreciable amount**（cost − residual value）over **useful life**。
- **HKAS 16.43–47**：每個成本重大嘅 component **分開折舊**。
- **HKAS 16.48–49**：depreciation 入 P&L，除非計入另一資產成本（如生產設備折舊 → Dr Inventories / Cr Accumulated depreciation）。
- **HKAS 16.55**：**開始**折舊 = 資產處於 location and condition necessary to be capable of operating as intended（**ready for use 就開始，唔係開始用先折**）；**停止** = earlier of held for sale（HKFRS 5）或 derecognised；閒置資產**繼續折舊**（usage method 下 charge 可為零）。
- **HKAS 16.56–57**：useful life 考慮 expected usage、wear and tear、technical/commercial obsolescence、legal limits；useful life 可以**短過** economic life（entity-specific）。
- **HKAS 16.58–59**：freehold land 一般**唔折舊**（無限 useful life）；但 quarries、landfill 等有限壽命土地要折；含 dismantling/restoration 成本嘅部分要折。
- **HKAS 16.62**：三個認可方法：**straight-line / diminishing balance / units of production**；**HKAS 16.62A：禁止 revenue-based depreciation**。
- **HKAS 16.51, 60–61**：residual value、useful life、depreciation method **至少每年年結檢討**；改變屬 **change in accounting estimate（HKAS 8）→ 前瞻性（prospective）處理，唔准追朔調整**。
- Diminishing balance 伏位：rate 乘以 **carrying amount（唔扣 residual value）**；residual value 只作為下限（floor）。

#### E. Derecognition（終止確認）
- **HKAS 16.67**：disposed of **或** no future economic benefits expected → derecognise。
- **HKAS 16.71**：Gain/loss = **net disposal proceeds − carrying amount**（carrying amount 計到出售日，記得計返至出售日嘅折舊）。
- **HKAS 16.68**：gain/loss 入 **P&L**，但 gains **唔准列入 revenue**（列入 other income）。
- Revalued asset 出售：gain/loss 按 **revalued carrying amount** 計；revaluation surplus 直接轉 retained earnings（HKAS 16.41），**唔 recycle 經 P&L**。

#### F. Disclosure（披露）
- **HKAS 16.73**：每個 class 披露 — measurement basis、depreciation methods、useful lives or depreciation rates、gross carrying amount 及 accumulated depreciation（連 impairment）期初期末、**carrying amount 期初到期末 reconciliation**（additions、disposals、business combinations acquisitions、revaluations、impairment losses/reversals、depreciation）。
- **HKAS 16.77**（revalued assets 額外披露）：effective date of revaluation、whether an independent valuer was involved、**如用 cost model 會係幾多** (16.77(e))、revaluation surplus 變動及分配限制、HKFRS 13 嘅 fair value hierarchy 資料。
- 另加 HKAS 36（impairment）、HKAS 8（estimate changes）、HKAS 1（estimation uncertainty）嘅相關披露。

### 易混淆概念對比

#### 對比一：Cost Model vs Revaluation Model（HKAS 16.30 vs 16.31）

| 比較點 | Cost Model (HKAS 16.30) | Revaluation Model (HKAS 16.31) |
|---|---|---|
| Carrying amount | Cost − accumulated depreciation − accumulated impairment losses | FV at revaluation date − subsequent accumulated depreciation − subsequent impairment losses |
| 適用範圍 | 任何 PPE | 只限 FV 能**可靠計量**嘅資產 |
| 政策一致性 | 按 class 一致應用（16.29） | 重估一項 → **成個 class 都要重估**（16.36）；regularity 要求（16.38） |
| 價值上升 | 唔認（無 upward adjustment） | Credit **OCI — revaluation surplus**（16.39） |
| 價值下跌 | Impairment → **P&L**（HKAS 36） | 先扣 revaluation surplus（OCI），超出部分 → **P&L**（16.40） |
| Impairment 是否適用 | 適用 | **一樣適用**，但當 revaluation decrease 處理 |

💬 **點樣分辨／考試伏位**：睇題目 policy 講明用邊個 model；revaluation model 唔代表唔使折舊、唔使減值（兩者都照做）。最常伏位：**減值時忘記先扣盡 surplus**；或將 revaluation increase 錯誤入 P&L。

#### 對比二：Capitalise（直接歸屬成本）vs Expense（唔准資本化）

| 比較點 | Capitalise（HKAS 16.16–17） | Expense（HKAS 16.19–20） |
|---|---|---|
| 原則 | 令資產達到 **location and condition necessary for intended use** 所必需 | 資產 ready 後發生，或屬經營/推廣性質 |
| 例子 | Purchase price（扣 discount）、import duties、**non-refundable taxes（如 stamp duty）**、site preparation、delivery & handling、installation、testing、professional fees、dismantling obligation、qualifying borrowing costs | **Refundable taxes（如 VAT）**、opening ceremony、staff training、advertising、initial operating losses、abnormal wastage、general admin overheads、recurring operating costs（如年度保險、年檢費） |
| Subsequent costs | 提升 capacity / 延長 life / 提高產出 → capitalise（component replacement 都計） | Repairs & maintenance（只恢復原狀）→ expense（16.12） |
| Testing 產出樣本 | Testing 成本本身資本化 | **賣樣本 proceeds 入 P&L**（16.20A），唔准扣資產成本 |

💬 **點樣分辨／考試伏位**：問自己「呢筆錢係咪令資產 **ready for use** 所必需？」——係就 capitalise。考試常混：**VAT（refundable → expense）vs stamp duty（non-refundable → capitalise）**；**transit insurance（資本化）vs annual insurance（費用化）**；**initial testing（資本化）vs opening function（費用化）**。

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** Under HKAS 16.7, what are the two criteria for recognising the cost of an item of PPE as an asset?

**Back:**
- It is **probable** that future economic benefits associated with the item will flow to the entity; and
- The **cost of the item can be measured reliably**.
- Failing items → expense when incurred (HKAS 16.7).

💬 兩個條件缺一不可：「好大機會有將來利益流入」＋「成本可靠計量」。記唔齊兩個條件係常見扣分位。

### Card 2
**Front:** List the three elements of cost of an item of PPE under HKAS 16.16, and name four examples of directly attributable costs under HKAS 16.17.

**Back:**
- Elements: (1) purchase price (incl. import duties, non-refundable taxes, net of trade discounts/rebates); (2) directly attributable costs; (3) initial estimate of dismantling/removal/site restoration obligations.
- Directly attributable examples: site preparation; initial delivery and handling; installation and assembly; testing costs; professional fees; employee benefits from construction/acquisition.

💬 口訣：「買價＋搞掂佢用到嘅直接成本＋拆遷復原責任」。VAT 可退 → 唔計；stamp duty 唔可退 → 計。

### Card 3
**Front:** Which costs must be EXCLUDED from the cost of PPE under HKAS 16.19–20?

**Back:**
- Costs of opening a new facility; introducing a new product/service (advertising); conducting business in a new location or with a new class of customer (incl. staff training);
- Administration and other general overheads; abnormal wastage;
- Costs incurred while the asset is idle or operating below full capacity; initial operating losses; relocation/reorganisation costs.
- Proceeds from selling items produced during testing → P&L (HKAS 16.20A).

💬 見到「開幕、培訓、廣告、搬遷、閒置」呢啲字眼，一律費用化。

### Card 4
**Front:** State the measurement rules for the cost model (HKAS 16.30) and the revaluation model (HKAS 16.31), and the class rule in HKAS 16.36.

**Back:**
- Cost model: cost **less accumulated depreciation and accumulated impairment losses**.
- Revaluation model: **fair value at the revaluation date** less subsequent accumulated depreciation and subsequent impairment losses; only where FV can be measured reliably; revaluations with sufficient regularity (16.38).
- HKAS 16.36: if one item is revalued, the **entire class** must be revalued.

💬 揀咗 revaluation model 唔可以「逐件揀嚟估」——成個 class 一齊重估，但唔同 class 可以用唔同 model。

### Card 5
**Front:** How are revaluation increases and decreases accounted for under HKAS 16.39 and 16.40?

**Back:**
- **Increase** → credit **OCI (revaluation surplus)**; EXCEPT recognise in **P&L** to the extent it reverses a previous revaluation **decrease** of the same asset recognised in P&L (16.39).
- **Decrease** → **P&L**; EXCEPT debit **OCI** to the extent of any existing credit balance in the revaluation surplus for that asset; excess → P&L (16.40).
- Entries decided **asset-by-asset**, though revaluation is whole-of-class.

💬 口訣：「升 → OCI；跌 → P&L；逆轉優先，跟返上次條路」。跌落嚟先食盡 surplus，多出嚟先碰 P&L。

### Card 6
**Front:** When does depreciation commence and cease under HKAS 16.55? What are the three permitted methods and the prohibition in HKAS 16.62A?

**Back:**
- Commences: when the asset is in the **location and condition necessary to be capable of operating** as intended by management (ready for use, even if not yet used).
- Ceases: at the **earlier** of classification as held for sale (HKFRS 5) and derecognition; idle assets keep depreciating (charge can be zero under usage method).
- Methods: **straight-line; diminishing balance; units of production** (16.62).
- **Revenue-based** depreciation is **not appropriate** (16.62A).

💬 「Ready for use 即開始折」係大伏位——唔係「開始用」先折。Diminishing balance 唔扣 residual value（佢只係下限）。

### Card 7
**Front:** How is a change in residual value, useful life or depreciation method accounted for?

**Back:**
- Review residual value, useful life and method **at least at each financial year-end** (HKAS 16.51).
- Treat as a **change in accounting estimate under HKAS 8** → apply **prospectively** (no cumulative catch-up): allocate the revised depreciable amount over the **remaining** useful life from the date of re-estimate.

💬 千祈唔好返轉頭改舊數。新折舊 = 改估嗰刻嘅 carrying amount ÷ 剩餘 useful life。

### Card 8
**Front:** State the derecognition rules for PPE under HKAS 16.67–72, including the treatment of gains and of revaluation surplus on disposal.

**Back:**
- Derecognise on **disposal** or when **no future economic benefits** are expected from use or disposal (16.67).
- Gain/loss = **net disposal proceeds − carrying amount** (carrying amount updated for depreciation to disposal date) → **P&L** (16.71).
- Gains are **NOT classified as revenue** (16.68) — include in other income.
- Revaluation surplus on a disposed revalued asset → transferred **directly to retained earnings**, not through P&L (16.41).

💬 賣 PPE 嘅 gain 唔係 revenue！仲有 surplus 轉 RE 係 equity 內部轉賬，永遠唔經 P&L。

## 本章急救詞彙

| EN Term | 繁中白話 | English Model Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Carrying amount | 賬面值（成本減累計折舊同減值） | The plant's carrying amount is its cost of HK$100m less accumulated depreciation of HK$20m and accumulated impairment losses of HK$5m. |
| Depreciable amount | 應折舊金額（成本減剩餘價值） | The depreciable amount of HK$20,000 (cost HK$25,000 less residual value HK$5,000) is allocated over five years. |
| Residual value | 剩餘價值：假設資產而家已經到咗退役年齡同狀態，而家賣到幾多錢（扣埋出售成本） | The residual value is the estimated amount currently obtainable from disposal if the asset were already of the age and condition expected at the end of its useful life. |
| Useful life vs Economic life | Useful life 係「我公司預計用幾耐」（entity-specific，可以短啲）；economic life 係「件資產本身經濟上用得幾耐」 | Because CDE replaces executive vehicles every four years, the vehicles' useful life (4 years) is shorter than their economic life (7–10 years). |
| Revaluation surplus | 重估盈餘：重估增值入 OCI 累積喺 equity 嘅儲備 | The revaluation increase of HK$40,000 is credited to the revaluation surplus in other comprehensive income. |
| Component approach | 部件法：重大部件當獨立資產，各自折舊同重置資本化 | Under the component approach, the aircraft engines and seats are depreciated separately from the airframe. |
| Derecognition | 終止確認：出售或無將來利益時，將資產賬面值移出賬簿 | The carrying amount of the plant is derecognised on sale, and the resulting gain of HK$25,000 is recognised in profit or loss. |
| Accumulated depreciation | 累計折舊：contra asset（資產減項），唔係負債 | Accumulated depreciation is a contra asset deducted from the gross carrying amount, not a liability. |
