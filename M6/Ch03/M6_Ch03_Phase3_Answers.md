# M6 Chapter 3: Revenue Recognition — Phase 3 Answers

### Q1 — Answer: A
**Model Answer (EN):** The contract contains ONE performance obligation: the combined commercial kitchen. Although the ovens, refrigeration units and worktops may each be capable of being distinct (the customer could benefit from them with other readily available resources), they are not distinct **within the context of the contract**: KitchKraft provides a significant service of integrating and substantially modifying the components into the combined output for which the customer contracted. Non-distinct goods/services are combined until a distinct bundle is identified.
**解題思路（繁中）:** 「Distinct」要過兩關：capable of being distinct **兼** distinct within the context of the contract。呢題啲零件過到第一關，但 KitchKraft 提供重大 integration 及 customisation 服務，令承諾喺合約語境下**唔係 separately identifiable**，所以第二關過唔到 → 合成一個 performance obligation。Distractor B 只考慮第一關，漏咗 context test；C 錯將「合約提到嘅每項嘢」都當獨立 PO（installation 係 integration 嘅一部分，並非獨立承諾）；D 嘅「goods and services are always separately identifiable」係絕對化錯誤陳述。**考試伏位：** 見到「significant integration / customisation」關鍵字，即刻諗住合併計。
**Rule Check:** HKFRS 15 paras. 27–29 (distinct criteria and indicators of not being separately identifiable)
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 以為「可以獨立賣」就等於「separate performance obligation」— 必須同時通過「distinct within the context of the contract」測試；顯著整合服務（significant integration service）係最常見嘅合併原因。

### Q2 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):** TixHub is an **agent**: the organiser is primarily responsible for fulfilment (staging the concert), TixHub bears no inventory risk, and TixHub has no pricing discretion (the organiser sets the HK$500 price). TixHub therefore does not control the service before it is transferred to the customer and recognises revenue only at the **net** commission amount: HK$500 − HK$460 = **HK$40** per ticket.
**解題思路（繁中）:** Principal vs agent 睇三個指標：邊個主要負責履行、邊個有存貨風險、邊個有定價權 — 三個都指向 organiser，所以 TixHub 係 agent，只認 commission HK$40。Distractor A 係 principal 先會用嘅 gross 做法，會**誇大 revenue**；B 係 remittance 金額，同收入定義無關；D 錯在混淆「唔係 principal」同「冇收入」— agent 照樣認 commission revenue。**考試伏位：** agent 負責收錢（collect on behalf）唔影響判斷；「net profit 一樣」唔代表 revenue presentation 冇分別。
**Rule Check:** HKFRS 15 paras. B34, B37 (principal–agent considerations; indicators of control)
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 見到平台收足 HK$500 就以為 revenue 係 gross 數 — agent 永遠只認淨佣金；錯誤 gross up 會同時谷大 revenue 同 expense。

### Q3 — Answer: C
**Model Answer (EN):**
Workings — allocation on relative stand-alone selling prices:
| Performance obligation | SSP | Allocation of HK$8,100 |
|---|---|---|
| Exercise bike | HK$7,200 | 7,200 / 9,000 × 8,100 = **HK$6,480** |
| Maintenance plan (1 yr) | HK$1,800 | 1,800 / 9,000 × 8,100 = **HK$1,620** |
| Total | HK$9,000 | HK$8,100 |

- Bike: point in time — HK$6,480 recognised on delivery (1 January 20X5).
- Maintenance: over time (customer simultaneously receives and consumes the benefits) — HK$1,620 / 12 = HK$135 per month.
- January 20X5 revenue = 6,480 + 135 = **HK$6,615**.
**解題思路（繁中）:** 先按 relative SSP 分攤個 bundle discount（HK$900 discount 按比例攤），再判斷每個 PO 嘅 timing：bike 係 point in time 即日認；maintenance 係 over time 每月認 1/12。Distractor A 將成個 HK$8,100 直線攤 12 個月，冇分 PO；B 只認咗 bike，漏咗一個月 maintenance；D 用 SSP 原價（7,200 + 1,800/12 = 7,350）而冇分攤 discount。**考試伏位：** 分攤係用 **relative SSP 比例**落 transaction price，唔係直接用 SSP 認收入。
**Rule Check:** HKFRS 15 paras. 73–77 (allocation based on relative stand-alone selling prices); para. 81 (discount allocation); paras. 35–38 (over time vs point in time)
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 忘記 bundle discount 要攤落每個 PO；或者將整個合約金額當一個 PO 直線攤分 — 凡係混合「即日交貨 + 長期服務」嘅合約，一定要拆開計 timing。

### Q4 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** The performance obligation is satisfied **over time**: the asset has no alternative use to BuildSpan AND BuildSpan has an enforceable right to payment for performance completed to date (third criterion satisfied). Using the input method:
- Progress = costs incurred ÷ total expected costs = 1,200,000 / 4,800,000 = 25%
- Revenue for the year = 25% × HK$6,000,000 = **HK$1,500,000**
**解題思路（繁中）:** 先過 over-time 測試：第三個條件「no alternative use **AND** enforceable right to payment」兩者齊全 → over time。再用 input method：cost-to-cost 25% × 合約價。Distractor A 直接當 costs incurred 係 revenue（利潤歸零，唔合理）；C 係 30%（錯用 1,200/4,000 或憑空加成）；D 係按時間直線攤（9 個月 / 總期）— 但題目講明用 input method 按成本計進度。**考試伏位：** input method 分母係 **total expected costs**（已發生 + 預計將發生），唔係已發生成本或合約價。
**Rule Check:** HKFRS 15 paras. 35–37 (over-time criteria, incl. no alternative use + enforceable right to payment); para. B15 (input methods)
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 「No alternative use」同「enforceable right to payment」係 **AND** 關係 — 只中一個唔算 over time；計 progress 時記得用累計成本對總預期成本，唔好用時間比例代替題目指定嘅 input method。

### Q5 — Answer: B
**Model Answer (EN):** Cash received before performance creates a **contract liability**. The service is satisfied over time (customer simultaneously receives and consumes the benefits). By 30 June 20X5, 4 of 12 months have elapsed: 36,000 × 4/12 = HK$12,000 of revenue is recognised.
```
1 March 20X5
Dr Cash                            36,000
    Cr Contract liability                    36,000

30 June 20X5
Dr Contract liability              12,000
    Cr Service revenue                       12,000
```
Remaining contract liability at year-end = HK$24,000 (8 months of service still to be provided).
**解題思路（繁中）:** 「先收錢、後做嘢」→ **contract liability**（唔係 revenue，唔係 contract asset）。之後按時間推移（output method — time elapsed）逐步將 liability 轉做 revenue：4/12 × 36,000 = 12,000。Distractor A 收款即全數認收入，違反 over-time 原則；C 用咗 **contract asset**（方向完全倒轉 — asset 係「做咗嘢未收錢」先至用）兼收款日先認收入；D 年結時全數 36,000 認收入，冇按 4/12 計算。**考試伏位：** contract asset vs contract liability 方向：perform first + right conditional → asset；cash first + obligation outstanding → liability。
**Rule Check:** HKFRS 15 para. 35 (over time — simultaneous receipt and consumption of benefits); para. 106 / Appendix A (contract liability: obligation to transfer goods/services for which consideration has been received)
**Common Trap（繁中）:** 混淆 contract asset 同 contract liability 嘅方向；以及年結時「懶方便」全數認收入而唔按時間比例（4/12）攤分 — 記住 mid-year 起約一定要 pro-rate。
