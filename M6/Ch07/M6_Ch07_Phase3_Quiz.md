**Q1.** On 1 March 20X9, Lantern Logistics Limited (Lantern) enters into a five-year contract with Victoria Storage Limited (Victoria) for the use of a specified floor area (Unit 8) in Victoria's warehouse in Kwai Chung. The contract states that Victoria may, at any time and without Lantern's consent, relocate Lantern's goods to another comparable unit in the same warehouse complex. Victoria frequently exercises this right in practice, because it can lease premium ground-floor units to other customers at significantly higher rentals, and the cost of moving Lantern's goods (which are stored on movable pallets) is minimal.

In accordance with HKFRS 16, which of the following statements is correct?

A. The contract contains a lease because Unit 8 is explicitly specified in the contract.
B. The contract contains a lease because Lantern obtains substantially all of the economic benefits from using the storage space.
C. The contract does not contain a lease because Victoria has a substantive substitution right, so there is no identified asset.
D. The contract does not contain a lease because Lantern does not have the right to operate the warehouse.

**Q2.** On 1 April 20X9, Harbour Retail Limited (Harbour Retail) enters into a lease of a shop unit with a four-year non-cancellable period and an option to extend the lease for a further two years at a rental set at 30% below the prevailing market rate. At the commencement date, Harbour Retail has just completed significant leasehold improvements to the shop unit at its own expense, which are expected to generate economic benefits over the next seven years and can only be enjoyed through continued tenancy of the unit.

In accordance with HKFRS 16, what is the lease term at the commencement date?

A. Four years, because only the non-cancellable period of a lease is ever included in the lease term.
B. Six years, because Harbour Retail is reasonably certain to exercise the extension option.
C. The lease term cannot be determined until Harbour Retail actually exercises the extension option.
D. Seven years, because the leasehold improvements are expected to generate benefits over seven years.

**Q3.** On 1 January 20X9, Bamboo Manufacturing Limited (Bamboo) enters into a four-year lease of a machine with Peak Equipment Limited. Annual lease payments of HK$80,000 are payable **in arrears** on 31 December each year. In addition, the contract requires a variable payment each year equal to 2% of Bamboo's annual sales; Bamboo estimates this variable payment will be approximately HK$10,000 per year. Bamboo guarantees that the residual value of the machine at the end of the lease term will be at least HK$20,000; Bamboo expects it will need to pay HK$5,000 under this residual value guarantee. The interest rate implicit in the lease is 6% per annum and is known to Bamboo. (Round your answer to the nearest HK$.)

In accordance with HKFRS 16, what is the initial measurement of the lease liability at 1 January 20X9?

A. HK$293,051
B. HK$281,169
C. HK$325,000
D. HK$315,820

**Q4.** On 1 January 20X9, Coral Trading Limited (Coral) enters into a lease of office premises. The lease liability is initially measured at HK$500,000. Annual lease payments of HK$120,000 are payable **in arrears** on 31 December each year. The interest rate implicit in the lease is 5% per annum. Coral's reporting date is 31 December.

In accordance with HKFRS 16, which journal entry should Coral record on 31 December 20X9 in respect of the lease payment?

A. Dr Lease liability HK$120,000; Cr Cash HK$120,000
B. Dr Interest expense HK$25,000; Dr Lease liability HK$95,000; Cr Cash HK$120,000
C. Dr Interest expense HK$25,000; Cr Lease liability HK$25,000
D. Dr Right-of-use asset HK$95,000; Dr Interest expense HK$25,000; Cr Cash HK$120,000

**Q5.** On 1 January 20X9, Dragon Motors Limited (Dragon Motors), a dealer of commercial vehicles, enters into a finance lease of a delivery van with Sunrise Catering Limited. The fair value of the van is HK$400,000 and its carrying amount in Dragon Motors' inventory is HK$320,000. The present value of the lease payments accruing to Dragon Motors, discounted at the market rate of interest, is HK$380,000. There is no unguaranteed residual value. Costs incurred by Dragon Motors in obtaining the lease amount to HK$8,000.

In accordance with HKFRS 16, which of the following correctly states the amounts Dragon Motors should recognise in profit or loss at the commencement date (excluding the subsequent finance income)?

A. Revenue HK$380,000; cost of goods sold HK$320,000; selling profit HK$60,000; lease costs of HK$8,000 expensed
B. Revenue HK$400,000; cost of goods sold HK$320,000; selling profit HK$80,000; lease costs of HK$8,000 included in the net investment in the lease
C. Revenue HK$nil; the lease is purely a financing arrangement, so only finance income is recognised over the lease term
D. Revenue HK$400,000; cost of goods sold HK$320,000; selling profit HK$80,000; lease costs of HK$8,000 expensed
