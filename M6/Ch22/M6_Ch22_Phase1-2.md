# M6 Chapter 22: Business Combinations — Phase 1 速查表 + Phase 2 閃卡

## Phase 1 速查表

### Learning Objectives
1. **Define** a business combination and **explain** the three components of control (power; exposure to variable returns; ability to use power to affect returns) in accordance with HKFRS 3 (Revised) and HKFRS 10.
2. **Apply** the acquisition method: identify the acquirer, determine the acquisition date, and measure the consideration transferred (including deferred and contingent consideration) at acquisition-date fair value.
3. **Account for** acquisition-related costs, distinguishing them from consideration transferred and from debt/equity issue costs under HKFRS 9 / HKAS 32.
4. **Recognise and measure** identifiable assets acquired, liabilities assumed (including contingent liabilities) and any non-controlling interest (NCI), applying the recognition and measurement exceptions.
5. **Calculate** goodwill or gain from a bargain purchase under both the partial goodwill and full goodwill NCI measurement options.

---

### 必背考點

#### A. Recognition 認確認知

**1. Is it a business combination at all?（判斷入口）**
- Business combination = a transaction or other event in which an acquirer **obtains control of one or more businesses** — HKFRS 3(R) App A.
- 兩大條件缺一不可：(i) a **business** must be acquired; (ii) the acquirer must **control** the business after the acquisition.
- A **business** = an integrated set of activities and assets with at least an **input** and a **substantive process** that together significantly contribute to the ability to create outputs — HKFRS 3(R) App A, B7, B8, B12A. Outputs 並非必要（HKFRS 3(R).B9）。
- **Substantive process** = not minor or ancillary; an organised workforce performing a critical function 係 indicator — HKFRS 3(R).B12D。Back-office systems (accounting, billing, payroll) 一般唔算 substantive process（HKFRS 3(R).B11）。
- **Control** 三要素（全部同時存在）— HKFRS 3(R).7, B13; HKFRS 10.6–7:
  1. **Power** over the investee (existing rights giving the **current ability to direct relevant activities**);
  2. **Exposure, or rights, to variable returns** from involvement;
  3. **Ability to use power to affect** the amount of returns.
- Rights 必須係 **substantive**（currently exercisable、能 benefit 持有人），唔係 merely **protective** — HKFRS 10.B22–B24。Deeply out-of-the-money options 唔算 substantive（LP Knowledge Check Q3）。
- **De facto control**：持股 <50% 都可以有 power，如果其餘股權分散、唔會集結投票 — HKFRS 10.B38–B46。

**2. Recognition principle at acquisition date** — HKFRS 3(R).10, 12:
- Acquirer 須 **separately from goodwill** 確認：all identifiable assets acquired、liabilities assumed、any NCI。
- 條件：項目須 meet the definition of an asset/liability at acquisition date，且係 business combination 嘅一部分（唔係 separate transaction，例如報酬員工未來服務嘅安排 — HKFRS 3(R).52）。
- 收購後可能確認 acquiree **從未確認**嘅 intangible assets（如 internally developed brands、customer lists），只要 identifiable：
  - **Contractual-legal criterion** 或 **separability criterion**（capable of being separated and sold/transferred/licensed/rented/exchanged）— HKFRS 3(R).B31–B34; HKAS 38.12。
  - 不考慮 acquirer 是否有意出售（intention irrelevant），只看是否 *capable* — HKFRS 3(R).B33。
- **Assembled workforce 永遠唔可以單獨確認**（無 control、無 contractual/legal right、唔 separable）→ 價值 subsumed into goodwill — HKFRS 3(R).B37–B38。

**3. Recognition exceptions**（LP 要求掌握嘅三個）— Exhibit 22.4:
| Exception | HKFRS 3 (Revised) requirement | Ref |
|---|---|---|
| **Contingent liabilities** | Acquiree 按 HKAS 37 唔確認，但 acquirer 須確認若屬 **present obligation** arising from past events 且 FV reliably measurable，以 acquisition-date FV 計量（possible obligation 則不確認）| HKFRS 3(R).22–23 |
| **Leases (acquiree as lessee)** | 按 HKFRS 16 確認 lease liability（= PV of remaining lease payments as if a new lease）及 right-of-use asset（同額，± favourable/unfavourable terms 調整）| HKFRS 3(R).28A–28B |
| **NCA / disposal groups held for sale** | 按 HKFRS 5 計量 | HKFRS 3(R).31 |

#### B. Measurement 計量

**Acquisition method 五步驟** — HKFRS 3(R).5:
1. Identify the acquirer（付現/發股/促成合併、規模較大一方通常係 acquirer — HKFRS 3(R).6, B14–B17）；
2. Determine the acquisition date = **date acquirer obtains control**（一般係 closing date，可早可遲於 closing，睇 facts）— HKFRS 3(R).8–9；
3. Determine the **consideration transferred**；
4. Recognise & measure identifiable assets/liabilities/NCI；
5. Recognise & measure **goodwill or gain on bargain purchase**。

**Consideration transferred** — HKFRS 3(R).37（sum of acquisition-date fair values of）:
- assets transferred by the acquirer;
- liabilities incurred by the acquirer to former owners;
- equity interests issued by the acquirer。
- **Share consideration**：以 **acquisition date** 嘅 fair value（上市股=當日報價），無論股份幾時發行 — HKFRS 3(R).37。若 acquirer 非上市而 acquiree 上市，可用 acquiree 股價計量（HKFRS 3(R).B14 方向，LP §22.3.3.1）。
- **Deferred consideration**：以 acquisition-date FV（即折現值）計量；**unwinding of discount = finance cost in P&L (HKFRS 9)，唔屬於 business combination**。
- **Contingent consideration**（如 earn-out）：以 acquisition-date **estimated fair value** 計入 consideration — HKFRS 3(R).39。**日後 contingency 落實後，差額唔會回頭調整 goodwill**（settlement difference 唔屬 business combination accounting）。
- **Acquisition-related costs**（finder's fees、due diligence、legal、valuation、internal acquisition department 成本）：**expensed as incurred，唔入 consideration、唔入 goodwill、唔入 parent's separate FS 嘅 investment cost** — HKFRS 3(R).59。例外：**debt/equity issue costs** 按 HKFRS 9 / HKAS 32 處理（share issue costs = deduction from equity，e.g. against share capital/share premium）。

**Identifiable assets & liabilities**：以 **acquisition-date fair values** 計量 — HKFRS 3(R).18（HKFRS 13 定 FV）。FV uplift on PPE、unrecognised intangibles、contingent liabilities 全部要計入 identifiable net assets。

**NCI measurement options** — HKFRS 3(R).19（**acquisition-by-acquisition basis** 逐單選擇）:
- **Fair value（full goodwill method）**；或
- **Proportionate share of the acquiree's identifiable net assets（partial goodwill method）**。
- NCI at FV ≠ 股數 × 股價，亦 ≠ proportionate share of net assets（可能含 lack-of-control discount）— HKFRS 3(R).B44–B45。

**Goodwill** — HKFRS 3(R).32:
```
Goodwill = FV of consideration transferred
         + amount recognised for NCI
         (+ FV of previously held equity interest — step acquisition, out of scope)
         − FV of identifiable net assets acquired at acquisition date
```
Goodwill = residual asset，代表未單獨識別資產（synergies、assembled workforce、potential new contracts）嘅未來經濟利益 — HKFRS 3(R) App A。

**Gain on bargain purchase** — HKFRS 3(R).34–36:
- 當 identifiable net assets > consideration + NCI 時，**先 reassess**：(1) 是否已識別晒所有 assets/liabilities；(2) 計量是否反映 acquisition date 所有可得資料；
- 確認無誤後，**gain 即時計入 profit or loss（acquisition date），歸屬 acquirer**。

#### C. Disclosure / Reporting requirements 披露與報告
- Business combination 以 **equity instruments** 達成 → 形成 parent–subsidiary group，parent 須編製 **consolidated financial statements**（assets/liabilities/equity/income/expenses/cash flows 以 single economic entity 呈列）— **Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) s.379(2)**；HKFRS 10.4, App A。
- 以 **net assets acquisition** 達成 → 無 subsidiary，資產負債直接併入 acquirer 財報，**唔叫 consolidated FS**。
- HKFRS 10 豁免：intermediate parent（符合條件）及 **investment entity** 可免編 consolidated FS（細節 out of scope）。
- ⚠️ 本章 LP 未展開 HKFRS 3 嘅逐段 disclosure 清單（屬 Ch 23 consolidation 範圍），考試層面掌握上列「邊個要編 consolidated FS」即可。

#### D. Key Journal Entries（全部平衡）

**JE1 — Parent's separate books: acquisition of shares (cash + shares + contingent consideration)**
```
Dr  Investment in subsidiary                     X   (= total consideration transferred)
    Cr  Cash                                                        X   (cash paid)
    Cr  Share capital                                               X   (nominal value of shares issued)
    Cr  Share premium                                               X   (FV of shares issued − nominal)
    Cr  Contingent consideration liability                          X   (acquisition-date FV)
```
**JE2 — Acquisition-related costs（expensed）**
```
Dr  Administrative / professional fees expense (P&L)   X
    Cr  Cash                                                          X
```
**JE3 — Share issue costs（HKAS 32: deduction from equity）**
```
Dr  Share premium / share capital (equity)   X
    Cr  Cash                                             X
```
**JE4 — Deferred consideration unwinding（HKFRS 9 finance cost）**
```
Dr  Finance cost (P&L)                    X
    Cr  Deferred consideration liability                 X
```
**JE5 — Consolidation of identifiable net assets + goodwill（consolidation worksheet 概念）**
```
Dr  Identifiable assets acquired (at FV)        X
Dr  Goodwill (residual)                          X
    Cr  Liabilities assumed (at FV)                            X
    Cr  Consideration transferred (cash/shares/liabilities)    X
    Cr  Non-controlling interest (equity)                      X
```
如為 bargain purchase：唔借 goodwill，改為 `Cr Gain on bargain purchase (P&L)`。

### 易混淆概念對比

**表 1：Full goodwill method vs Partial goodwill method（HKFRS 3(R).19, 32）**

| 項目 | Full goodwill method | Partial goodwill method |
|---|---|---|
| NCI 計量 | NCI at **fair value** | NCI at **proportionate share of identifiable net assets** |
| Goodwill 包含 | Acquirer + NCI 雙方應佔 goodwill | 只包含 acquirer 應佔 goodwill |
| Goodwill 金額 | 較**高** | 較**低**（差額 = NCI 應佔 goodwill）|
| 政策選擇 | 逐單收購選擇（acquisition-by-acquisition）| 同左 |
| 點樣分辨（繁中） | 題目畀「FV of NCI」或「NCI at fair value」就用呢招 | 題目講「proportionate share of net assets」就用呢招 |
| 考試伏位 | 用 NCI 股數 × 股價當 FV（錯：可能有 lack-of-control discount）；FV of NCI 唔係 net assets × NCI% | 計 goodwill 時忘記加 NCI；或將 NCI% 用錯母公司持股比例（要用 100% − parent%）|

**表 2：Deferred consideration vs Contingent consideration**

| 項目 | Deferred consideration | Contingent consideration |
|---|---|---|
| 性質 | 金額**確定**，只係**遲啲找數** | 金額**取決於未來事件**（earn-out、業績目標）|
| Acquisition-date 計量 | **折現值**（PV at acquisition date）| **Estimated fair value**（反映預期達成機率）— HKFRS 3(R).39 |
| 後續處理 | Unwinding of discount → **finance cost in P&L (HKFRS 9)**；唔影響 goodwill | Contingency 落實後差額 **唔調整 goodwill**；與 business combination 分開處理 |
| 點樣分辨（繁中）| 題目寫「payable in 12/24 months」+ 畀 discount rate → deferred | 題目寫「if revenue/earning target met…」+ 畀 fair value → contingent |
| 考試伏位 | 用 face value 唔折現；或將 unwinding 當 acquisition cost | 用最終實付金額取代 acquisition-date FV 重計 goodwill（錯）；或將 FV 當成 expense |

## Phase 2 閃卡

### Card 1
**Front:** Define "business combination" and state the two features a transaction must have to be one (HKFRS 3 (Revised)).

**Back:**
- A transaction or other event in which an acquirer **obtains control of one or more businesses** (HKFRS 3(R) App A).
- Feature 1: a **business** must be acquired (at least an input + a substantive process capable of contributing to outputs).
- Feature 2: the acquirer must **control** the business after the acquisition.

💬 白話解釋：兩個條件要齊——買嘅嘢要係一盤「生意」（有 input + 實質 process），而且買完你要**控制**到佢。淨係買部機器或者買少少股份唔夠控制權，就唔係 business combination。

### Card 2
**Front:** State the three components of control under HKFRS 10.6–7 / HKFRS 3(R).7.

**Back:**
1. **Power** over the investee — existing rights giving the **current** ability to direct the **relevant activities**;
2. **Exposure, or rights, to variable returns** from involvement with the investee;
3. The **ability to use that power to affect** the amount of the investor's returns.

💬 白話解釋：記「權、利、用權影響利」。三個要**同時**存在；仲要留意係 *current* ability，唔係 future；direct 嘅係 *relevant activities*，唔係所有 operation。持股過半通常有 power，但唔係必要條件（de facto control）。

### Card 3
**Front:** List the five steps of the acquisition method (HKFRS 3(R).5).

**Back:**
1. Identify the **acquirer**;
2. Determine the **acquisition date** (date control is obtained — HKFRS 3(R).8–9);
3. Determine the **consideration transferred** (acquisition-date FV — HKFRS 3(R).37);
4. Recognise and measure **identifiable assets acquired, liabilities assumed and any NCI** (HKFRS 3(R).10, 18, 19);
5. Recognise and measure **goodwill or a gain from a bargain purchase** (HKFRS 3(R).32, 34–36).

💬 白話解釋：口訣「人、日、價、產、誉」——邊個買、幾時控制、畀幾多、執到咩資產負債同 NCI、最後計 goodwill 定 bargain purchase gain。每一步都有對應段數，答題時逐步引。

### Card 4
**Front:** How is share consideration measured, and which date's share price is used (HKFRS 3(R).37)?

**Back:**
- Shares issued by the acquirer are measured at **fair value on the acquisition date**, regardless of when the shares are actually issued.
- For listed shares, FV = **quoted share price on the acquisition date** (NOT the agreement/signing date, NOT the settlement date if control passed earlier/later).
- If the acquirer is unlisted but the acquiree is listed, use the acquisition-date FV of the **acquiree's** shares.

💬 白話解釋：伏位之王！簽約日、交收日嘅股價都係陷阱，**唯有取得控制權嗰日**嘅股價先啱。記住 acquisition date = control passes 嘅日子。

### Card 5
**Front:** How are acquisition-related costs accounted for under HKFRS 3(R).59, and what is the exception?

**Back:**
- Acquisition-related costs (finder's fees, due diligence, legal, valuation, internal M&A department costs) are **expensed in profit or loss as incurred**.
- They are **excluded from**: consideration transferred, goodwill calculation, and the cost of investment in subsidiary in separate FS.
- **Exception**: costs of issuing **debt or equity securities** are dealt with under HKFRS 9 / HKAS 32 — share issue costs are a **deduction from equity** (e.g. against share premium).

💬 白話解釋：請律師、做 due diligence 嘅錢全部即時 expense，唔准加落收購成本度「扮大」goodwill。唯獨**發股/發債嘅發行費**例外——發股費用扣 equity，唔落 P&L，亦唔入 consideration。

### Card 6
**Front:** State the two measurement options for non-controlling interest at the acquisition date (HKFRS 3(R).19) and their effect on goodwill.

**Back:**
- Option 1: **Fair value** → **full goodwill** method (goodwill includes NCI's share);
- Option 2: **Proportionate share of the acquiree's identifiable net assets** → **partial goodwill** method (only acquirer's goodwill recognised);
- Choice made on an **acquisition-by-acquisition** basis;
- FV of NCI may include a **lack-of-control discount**, so it need not equal share price × shares or NCI% × net assets (HKFRS 3(R).B44–B45).

💬 白話解釋：同一單收購，NCI 用 FV 計，goodwill 就大啲（full）；用 net assets 比例計，goodwill 就細啲（partial）。考試會指明用邊個，睇清楚先好落筆；題目冇講就唔好自己創第三個方法。

### Card 7
**Front:** What must an acquirer do before recognising a gain on a bargain purchase, and where is the gain recognised (HKFRS 3(R).34–36)?

**Back:**
1. **Reassess** whether all assets acquired and liabilities assumed have been correctly identified;
2. **Review** the measurement procedures to ensure amounts reflect all information available at the acquisition date;
3. Then recognise the resulting gain **in profit or loss at the acquisition date**, attributed to the acquirer.

💬 白話解釋：「執到平貨」唔可以即刻開心入賬——因為正常冇人會平賣，所以 HKFRS 3 強制你先 double-check 有冇睇漏資產負債、估值有冇錯，確認真係平先可以將 gain 落 P&L。**永遠唔係 credit 去 equity 或者負 goodwill**。

### Card 8
**Front:** Why can an assembled workforce never be recognised as a separate intangible asset in a business combination, and where does its value go?

**Back:**
- Fails the definition of an asset: the acquirer usually has **insufficient control** over the future economic benefits (employees can leave);
- Fails the **contractual-legal criterion**: the "workforce" as a unit does not arise from contractual or legal rights;
- Fails the **separability criterion**: it cannot be sold/transferred separately from the business;
- → Its value is **subsumed into goodwill** (HKFRS 3(R).B37–B38).

💬 白話解釋：員工會辭職，你控制唔實；「成個團隊」又唔係合約權利，亦拆唔散嚟賣。所以 workforce 永遠冇得單獨確認——佢嘅價值全部跌落 goodwill。對照：trademark、customer list 通常 separable，可以單獨確認。
