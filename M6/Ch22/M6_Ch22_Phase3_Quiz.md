**Q1.** Heron Limited is considering four transactions. In accordance with HKFRS 3 (Revised) *Business Combinations*, which of the following transactions should be accounted for as a **business combination**?

A. Heron Limited purchases a single second-hand delivery truck from a car dealer for use in its logistics operations.

B. Heron Limited purchases 100% of the share capital of a company incorporated two weeks ago that has not yet commenced any activities and holds no assets other than the cash subscribed for its shares.

C. Heron Limited purchases all of the net assets of an operating bakery business, including the ovens and premises, and takes over the employment contracts of the bakery's trained workforce whose production processes are critical to producing the bakery's output.

D. Heron Limited purchases 25% of the ordinary share capital of an operating confectionery company, over which it gains significant influence but no ability to direct the relevant activities.

**Q2.** On 1 March 20X5, Falcon Limited signed an agreement to acquire 100% of the share capital of Wren Limited. Control of Wren Limited passed to Falcon Limited on 31 March 20X5. The consideration comprised:

- Cash of HK$3,000,000 paid at settlement;
- 200,000 ordinary shares of Falcon Limited (quoted share price: HK$14 on 1 March 20X5; HK$16 on 31 March 20X5);
- A further cash payment of HK$500,000 if Wren Limited's revenue exceeds an agreed target within 12 months of acquisition; the fair value of this contingent consideration at the acquisition date is assessed as HK$350,000.

Falcon Limited also paid legal fees of HK$90,000 in connection with the acquisition and share issue costs of HK$30,000.

In accordance with HKFRS 3 (Revised), what is the **consideration transferred**?

A. HK$6,150,000
B. HK$6,550,000
C. HK$6,670,000
D. HK$6,700,000

**Q3.** Otter Limited acquired 80% of the share capital of Seal Limited on 30 June 20X5. Seal Limited is defending a lawsuit brought by a former supplier. The claim arises from a past breach of contract, but Seal Limited's lawyers consider an outflow of resources is not probable, so Seal Limited has disclosed (but not recognised) the claim as a contingent liability in its own financial statements. Otter Limited determines that the obligation is a present obligation arising from past events and that its fair value at the acquisition date can be reliably measured at HK$180,000.

In accordance with HKFRS 3 (Revised), how should Otter Limited account for this contingent liability in accounting for the business combination?

A. Do not recognise it, because HKAS 37 prohibits the recognition of contingent liabilities.
B. Disclose it only, consistent with Seal Limited's own accounting treatment.
C. Recognise it as an assumed liability at its acquisition-date fair value of HK$180,000.
D. Recognise it only if and when an outflow of resources becomes probable after the acquisition date.

**Q4.** On 1 July 20X5, Panther Limited acquired 75% of the share capital of Leopard Limited for cash consideration of HK$9,000,000. At the acquisition date:

- The carrying amount of Leopard Limited's recognised net assets was HK$7,200,000;
- Leopard Limited owned an unrecognised trademark with a fair value of HK$600,000;
- The fair value of Leopard Limited's property, plant and equipment exceeded its carrying amount by HK$400,000;
- A contingent liability (a present obligation whose fair value is reliably measurable) of HK$200,000 existed but was not recognised by Leopard Limited;
- The fair values of all other recognised assets and liabilities equalled their carrying amounts.

Panther Limited measures the non-controlling interest in Leopard Limited at **fair value**. The fair value of the non-controlling interest at the acquisition date is HK$2,600,000.

In accordance with HKFRS 3 (Revised), calculate the **goodwill** arising on the acquisition. State your answer in HK$.

Kestrel Limited also paid legal fees of HK$80,000 relating to the acquisition.

Which set of journal entries correctly records these transactions in Kestrel Limited's **separate (unconsolidated) financial statements** in accordance with HKFRS 3 (Revised) and HKAS 32?

A. Dr Investment in subsidiary 6,880,000; Cr Cash 4,080,000; Cr Share capital 2,500,000; Cr Contingent consideration liability 300,000

B. Dr Investment in subsidiary 6,800,000; Cr Cash 4,000,000; Cr Share capital 100,000; Cr Share premium 2,400,000; Cr Contingent consideration liability 300,000; **and** Dr Administrative expenses 80,000; Cr Cash 80,000

C. Dr Investment in subsidiary 6,500,000; Cr Cash 4,000,000; Cr Share capital 100,000; Cr Share premium 2,400,000; **and** Dr Administrative expenses 80,000; Cr Cash 80,000
