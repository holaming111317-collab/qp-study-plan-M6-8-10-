# M6 Chapter 14: Events after the Reporting Period — Phase 3 Quiz

**Rules: Closed-book · 15 minutes · 5 questions · Submit as `1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … 5. …` — 超時如實申報**

---

**Q1.** Harbour Lighting Limited (Harbour Lighting) prepares financial statements for the year ended 31 December 20X7. Management completes the draft financial statements on 18 February 20X8 and circulates them to the audit committee. The board of directors reviews and authorises the financial statements for issue on 12 March 20X8. The financial statements are uploaded to the company's website on 20 March 20X8, filed with the Companies Registry on 2 April 20X8, and approved by shareholders at the annual general meeting on 28 May 20X8.

In accordance with HKAS 10, within which period must Harbour Lighting identify events after the reporting period?

A. Between 31 December 20X7 and 18 February 20X8
B. Between 31 December 20X7 and 12 March 20X8
C. Between 31 December 20X7 and 2 April 20X8
D. Between 31 December 20X7 and 28 May 20X8

---

**Q2.** Velvet Curtain Limited (Velvet Curtain), a retailer of home furnishings, has a reporting period ended 30 September 20X6. Its financial statements were authorised for issue on 15 December 20X6. In accordance with HKAS 10, which of the following events is an **adjusting** event after the reporting period?

A. On 2 November 20X6, Velvet Curtain acquired 100% of a competitor, increasing its market share by 15%.
B. On 20 October 20X6, Velvet Curtain's internal audit team discovered that the sales department had deliberately overstated revenue for the year ended 30 September 20X6.
C. On 8 November 20X6, Velvet Curtain announced a plan to discontinue its loss-making curtain-dyeing operation.
D. On 25 November 20X6, Velvet Curtain issued 5,000,000 new ordinary shares to the public.

**Q3.** Marble Fountain Limited (Marble Fountain) has a reporting period ended 31 December 20X7, and its financial statements were authorised for issue on 20 March 20X8. On 5 March 20X8, the board declared a final dividend of HK$0.80 per share on the 3,000,000 ordinary shares in issue. In accordance with HKAS 10 and HKAS 1, how should Marble Fountain account for the dividend in its financial statements for the year ended 31 December 20X7?

A. Recognise a dividend payable of HK$2,400,000 as a liability at 31 December 20X7, because the declaration occurred before the financial statements were authorised for issue.
B. Recognise a dividend payable of HK$2,400,000 as a deduction from retained earnings in the statement of changes in equity for the year ended 31 December 20X7.
C. Make no adjustment, but disclose in the notes the total dividend of HK$2,400,000 and the amount per share of HK$0.80.
D. Make no adjustment and no disclosure, because the dividend was declared after the end of the reporting period.

**Q4.** Copper Kettle Limited (Copper Kettle), a manufacturer of cookware, has a reporting period ended 30 June 20X8. Its financial statements are expected to be authorised for issue on 25 September 20X8. At 30 June 20X8, Copper Kettle holds 4,000 units of a discontinued saucepan model in finished goods inventory, recorded at a total cost of HK$960,000. On 20 July 20X8, in a clearance sale, Copper Kettle sold the entire batch of 4,000 units for HK$180 per unit, incurring selling costs of HK$24,000 in total. The clearance price was driven by a competitor's product launch in May 20X8, which had already sharply reduced demand for the model before 30 June 20X8.

In accordance with HKAS 10:

(a) State whether this is an adjusting or non-adjusting event, and calculate the amount of any write-down required in the financial statements for the year ended 30 June 20X8. Show your workings.

(b) Prepare the journal entry (Dr/Cr) as at 30 June 20X8 to record any adjustment.

**Q5.** Coral Island Limited (Coral Island) operates a single food-processing plant, its only revenue-generating asset, and has a reporting period ended 31 March 20X8. On 10 April 20X8, before the financial statements were authorised for issue, a typhoon completely destroyed the plant. The damage was not covered by insurance. Coral Island was unable to obtain financing to rebuild, and on 30 April 20X8 management resolved to liquidate the company. In accordance with HKAS 10, how should Coral Island prepare its financial statements for the year ended 31 March 20X8?

A. Continue to prepare the financial statements on a going concern basis, making no adjustment, and disclose the typhoon as a material non-adjusting event with an estimate of its financial effect.
B. Adjust the carrying amount of the plant to its recoverable amount of nil within the 31 March 20X8 financial statements, and retain the going concern basis for all other items.
C. Not prepare the financial statements on a going concern basis, because the pervasive effect of the loss requires a fundamental change in the basis of accounting rather than adjustments to amounts recognised, with the related disclosures required by HKAS 1.
D. Prepare the financial statements on a going concern basis because the typhoon occurred after the end of the reporting period, and recognise the destruction of the plant in the financial statements for the year ending 31 March 20X9 with no disclosure in the 31 March 20X8 financial statements.
